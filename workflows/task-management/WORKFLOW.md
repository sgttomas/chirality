# Task Management

Keep significant concerns from being lost when they have no suitable executable
home. The development loop discovers and performs its own work. Task Management
preserves concerns for disposition and allocation; its register is not an
implementation backlog, dependency graph or deliverable memory.

WORKING_ITEMS owns the invocation and register writes; TASK may perform bounded
inspection and return findings without delegating or writing register rows.
Select this workflow with the invoking loop, purpose and scope. No separate Task
Management init prompt or retired TASK_MANAGEMENT role is needed.

## Choose the invocation

- **Bounded intake:** the loop supplies particular concerns and their evidence.
  Examine only those concerns and relevant existing records after the required
  federation preflight. Do not expand this into a general harvest or a review of
  every deferred item.
- **Legacy-source retirement:** the human names an existing source population
  and authorizes its one-time examination and retirement. Account for every
  supplied entry and current additions, prepare grouped treatments with explicit
  exceptions, and apply actual human decisions. Examination is permitted even
  when an entry is ordinary future work; it does not make the entry eligible for
  register promotion.
- **Register review:** the human selects triage, harvest, staleness, closure-echo,
  deferral review, row maintenance or resolution of specified ruled items. A
  generational sweep is one explicitly requested combination of these modes.

A loop may authorize bounded intake under its own stated conditions, including
needed consequences of a substantive PR or its final documentation/governance
closeout. Timing alone never makes a concern eligible. No unrelated
act depends on a Task Management invocation, service or new register row.
An intake is not permission to promote, dispose, assign or execute its concern.

## Method

1. Establish the invoking loop, supplied concern, requested review or named
   retirement population, existing authority and exact write boundary. Read the [contract](resources/contract.md).
2. Perform the invocation-local federation preflight, stating coverage and limits.
3. Assess the supplied concerns, selected register or retirement population using the
   [method](resources/method.md). Separate ordinary executable work from genuine
   allocation/disposition problems; prepare concise recommendations for the human.
4. Record only the human's actual promotions and dispositions. Route authorized
   resolution to its owning work; preserve concerns still awaiting a decision.
5. Return the disposition and outstanding consequences to the caller. Preserve
   the result once in the Task Management home, linked from the caller's graph
   or closeout. Do not create a separate Task Management receipt or a MEMORY
   work list. An App/Piping development loop's one final receipt links this
   outcome when the invocation affected that undertaking.

If required inputs or federation coverage are unavailable, return the precise
limit with the retained concerns. Do not claim global absence or closure from
partial evidence, and do not block unrelated development. An open intake does
not discharge an unmet requirement in the originating undertaking.
