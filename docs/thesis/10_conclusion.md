# Chapter 10 — Conclusion

---

## 10.1 Research Question

The original thesis asked: *How can systems engineering principles be applied to
govern LLM-based agent systems such that their outputs can be relied upon in
regulated professional engineering practice?*

Chapter 1 makes the revised question explicit: how can an application environment
support useful agent work and informed, accountable human reliance while its
mechanisms change with capabilities, circumstances and experience?

The answer developed here is a framework for supporting accountable work
and a revisable family of implementations. Systems engineering contributes
useful distinctions, interfaces, feedback, configuration control and bounded
verification. Their presence does not certify professional reliance. The
person must still establish what the work means, what its grounds support
and what they will stand behind in the applicable practice.

The revised central proposition is:

> Chirality supports purposeful work by making relevant objects and
> relationships intelligible, the grounds and limits of claims inspectable,
> action and correction practicable, and consequential commitments
> attributable. The mechanisms that provide these conditions remain open
> to revision through use.

---

## 10.2 Contributions

The case makes five connected contributions. These are proposed and
implemented contributions, not a claim of demonstrated novelty over all
prior work or of universal effectiveness.

**1. A conceptual account of accountable human–agent work.** Ontology asks
what participants are dealing with; epistemology asks what supports their
claims and understanding; praxeology asks how work becomes usable through
action and correction; axiology asks what matters and who answers for
consequential choices. The questions recur at different scales without
prescribing identical schemas, documents or organizational structures. No
pillar uniquely determines the architecture or licenses neglect of the others.

Within this account, agents can investigate, infer, plan, compare, propose,
check and act within granted authority. Judgment, in the thesis's
philosophical sense, is the person's situated and committed, world-involving
engagement with meaning, value and responsibility [CITE:Smith2019]. That
engagement shapes work throughout; it is not exhausted by a final approval
or its record. Improved reckoning can change delegation, supervision and
verification without itself transferring accountable judgment.

**2. A separation of claim types.** Normative commitments establish adopted
values and responsibility boundaries. Design hypotheses propose mechanisms
that may serve them. Empirical findings report observations and their
limits. The separation prevents philosophical coherence from being treated
as proof of an architecture, or implementation from being treated as proof
of professional adequacy. It also makes contrary evidence useful rather
than a threat to the entire framework.

**3. An architectural pattern for inspectable work.** The revised repository
keeps commitments, design and conditions in their current authoritative
artifacts, derives relationships through tools, and uses existing change
history. It distinguishes presence from suitability, test evidence from
acceptance, and records from the acts they represent. Routine duplicated
status and receipts are unnecessary where these sources serve the work.
Records with an actual reliance, use or recovery function remain justified.

App v4 serves as the workflow-authoring exemplar. SWBPIPE tests the pattern
against numerical meaning and professional domain objects. Common validated
operations are intended to support different human and agent entry points.
The pattern is implemented in parts and under continuing development. Its
cost-effective transfer across domains and to another professional remains
to be demonstrated.

**4. A professional-practice interpretation.** The regulatory discussion
contributes a situated interpretation of supervision, review and reliance,
and of possible supporting mechanisms. It does not establish regulatory
endorsement or confer professional authentication on a software record.
The scope and limits of that interpretation accompany the argument.

**5. An evolving implementation case.** The history shows scaffolding
introduced to address real difficulties, followed by revisions as
capabilities and understanding changed. October's reset removed duties and
their enforcement together while retaining important distinctions and
responsibilities. That is evidence against the universal necessity of some
original mechanisms. It is not a controlled estimate of productivity gain
or proof that the surviving arrangement is optimal.

## 10.3 The Central Insight

Useful agent-assisted professional work needs both productive capability and
an intelligible basis for appropriate reliance. A result that is correct but
misapplied can fail its purpose; a well-documented result can still be wrong;
and a process that consumes attention without improving understanding can
undermine the judgment it was intended to support.

The architecture must therefore be assessed as part of practice. Its controls
have costs in attention, delay, maintainability and user agency, as well as
benefits in detection, recovery and attribution. Their adequacy depends on
the consequences and detectability of failure in the actual undertaking.

This yields a more precise claim than a universal prescription of gates or
invariants: accountable work requires relevant distinctions and continuing
responsibilities, but there are multiple ways to support them. Their adequacy
must be examined through the work, including the costs they impose and the
failures they permit.

## 10.4 Implications for Practice

**For people directing agents:** establish purpose and authority, make the
current work discoverable, and retain responsibility for integration and
reserved decisions. Use roles and methods as resources. Their existence does
not require a separate agent or approval step at every boundary. Verification
should detect consequential failures rather than merely reproduce process
conformance.

**For application developers:** expose meaningful objects and operations,
keep validation faithful across entry points, and make actual outcomes and
uncertainty inspectable. Demonstrate recovery and refusal behaviour where
it matters. A shared catalog or protocol is useful only to the extent that
its receiving applications preserve those meanings.

**For professional organizations and regulators:** the relevant questions
include competence, grounds for reliance, attribution and the scope of actual
review. Software can support their examination. This case supplies no
substitute for the applicable professional requirements or assessment of a
particular deployment.

**For researchers:** distinguish normative commitments, design hypotheses
and empirical findings. Examine total effort for comparable usable results,
consequential escaped errors, calibration of confidence, interruption
recovery and transfer to other practitioners. Activity counts alone cannot
establish those outcomes.

---

## 10.5 Closing

Agents extend reckoning. People in the present framework retain judgment:
the situated engagement through which the question takes shape, evidence
becomes meaningful and someone commits to what they will stand behind.
That distinction supports substantial agent initiative. It does not require
read-only coordination, a fixed hierarchy, immutable write-scope doctrine or
an administrative record at every step.

The repository's evolution is therefore part of the thesis's result. The
App provides an environment for developing useful human–agent interaction;
SWBPIPE brings the approach into a domain with demanding numerical and
professional consequences. Successive implementations can be derived anew
from current requirements and accumulated evidence, provided they retain
learning rather than merely repeat the same discoveries. A PRD guides that
process; it does not mechanically determine its result.

The broader promise remains a proposition to examine: useful applications
in which capable agents can participate while the work's meaning, evidence
and human responsibility remain intelligible. Another practitioner, another
domain and sustained use will test that proposition more strongly than
further elaboration of the architecture alone.

The philosophical account does not settle the possibilities of future
artificial knowers. Appendix D's existential conjecture and configurational
analogy remain non-foundational. Neither is needed to assess the present
allocation of responsibility or the observed mechanisms.

Chirality must apply its own standards to itself. Its claims remain bounded,
its evidence remains open to examination, and its means remain revisable.
