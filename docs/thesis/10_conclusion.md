# Chapter 10 — Conclusion

---

## 10.1 Research Question

Capability becomes useful through a working relationship among the person, the
purpose, the subject and its consequences. Agents extend what can be
investigated and done, and the environment makes context and action accessible.
Human judgment shapes what matters and what the person will stand behind.

Root supports that relationship by making the means of work available to be
used and revised. Roles preserve responsibility, artifacts sustain shared attention,
methods carry learning, and tools make selected actions repeatable. Their value
lies in these contributions.

---

## 10.2 Contributions

Current artifacts and derived relationships are meant to reduce the effort of
piecing together what governs a change. Whether they do so at an acceptable total
cost remains an empirical question. Continuing ownership gives responsibility for
repair and integration a home. Roles make those responsibilities explicit without
requiring a separate agent for each one. Authorization comes from the applicable
assignment and governing commitments. The host supplies capabilities and enforces
permissions on the paths it controls, but it does not grant authority merely by
making an operation available. The person retains judgment throughout the work
[CITE:Smith2019].

Examination must reach the actual result. Correct source, valid
representation and successful integration support different claims. A shared
correction has to reach its consumers; announcing it is not
enough. Existing evidence can be reused where its grounds remain applicable.

The means themselves require examination. Chapter 8 supplies a longitudinal
case in which the method produced working software and carried selected product
meanings through redevelopment. It traces abstraction, accepted commitments,
deliverable ownership, implementation and native use, including functional
extensions and repairs. [Appendix E](appendix_e_development_history.md) preserves
the development history. Reliability, usefulness and costs in further settings
remain subjects for continued examination.

---

## 10.3 The Central Insight

The means of work should help participants stay with the work. A representation
that aids attention can also divert it. A role that preserves responsibility
can become a compulsory handoff. A check that exposes error can become a ritual
repeated after its evidence is already sufficient. The same mechanism can serve
or frustrate the work under different conditions.

This makes revisability of the means central to the argument. Root must
preserve the purpose of a distinction while allowing its representation to
change. It must keep responsibility for integration while allowing different
staffing. It must support accountable judgment while giving agents room for
substantial reckoning and action. A fixed orchestration cannot settle those
relationships once, for all future work.

The four perspectives provide a way to examine the tension. Ontology asks what
the work concerns; epistemology asks what understanding and evidence support it;
praxeology asks how it becomes effective action; axiology asks what warrants that
action and its costs. They stay connected within the particular work.
Their value is that they expose partial accounts and support correction, not
that they prove a universal architecture.

---

## 10.4 Implications for Practice

Root's form follows a claim about learning: the environment should preserve what
experience has made useful without turning its present arrangement into a
permanent prescription. A role, method or tool carries learning forward only if
later participants can understand its purpose, examine its limits and change it
within authority. A growing inventory alone does not establish progress.

The person's attention is part of the system's finite capacity. Procedures that
consume that attention have ethical and practical costs, especially when the
person remains responsible for reliance. Removing a redundant control can
therefore support accountability, but only where the relevant distinctions and
grounds remain available; efficiency cannot turn a missing condition into an
established result.

Deriving an application poses the same question at a larger scale. An earlier
implementation contains both valuable learning and contingent design choices.
A product requirements document (PRD) can help distinguish current commitments
from inherited mechanisms, but it cannot replace the interpretation of use,
failure and changing capability. A new application is a substantive advance
when it preserves what matters while improving how that is realised, rather than
merely reproducing a familiar structure.

The observed case establishes useful production and temporal continuity.
Comparisons of total effort and reliability, and further practitioners' use,
would establish different claims. Their evidence must concern usable results,
consequential errors and recovery; activity counts and internal consistency alone
cannot supply those comparisons. Human steering remains part of every such
undertaking, giving purpose to the work and informing its course.

---

## 10.5 Closing

Root, the App and SWBPIPE express the approach at different scales. Root develops
the means of work. The App makes method authoring and agent participation usable.
SWBPIPE brings them into a practice with physical objects, numerical consequences
and professional commitments. Each must earn its usefulness in that setting.

In the examined App redevelopment, useful behavior and governing distinctions
were recovered from the predecessor, expressed in an accepted successor basis,
allocated to deliverables, and realized with extensions in the rebuilt
application. The meaning of workflows, revisions, trials, execution and human
acts remained intelligible while their implementation evolved. Recorded findings
also changed the product and its development method.

This is substantial longitudinal case evidence for the framework's practical
claim. Development-method transfer to SWBPIPE and selected external numerical
comparisons add a distinct domain case. Complete embedded-agent integration,
comparative advantage and professional suitability retain their own evidence
questions. The person's purpose, judgment and steering are constitutive of the
demonstrated arrangement.

The continuing task is to improve the means without letting them consume the
work. Their grounds remain open to examination, their costs matter, and their
form must answer to the work. The [conclusion](CONCLUSION.md) returns to
this relationship between shared methods and the application cases.
