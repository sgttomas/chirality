# Writing for professional readers

Use this when revising the documents written for people: the course series in
`docs/human-team-courses/` and the thesis collection in `docs/thesis/`,
the independent [App thesis](https://github.com/sgttomas/chirality-app/tree/main/docs/thesis) and
the independent [SWBPIPE thesis](https://github.com/sgttomas/SWBPIPE/tree/main/docs/thesis).

It does not apply to Appendix D in any thesis, to the manuals written for
agents, or to the SWBPIPE Engineering Reader and Open Manual.

## The reader

A professional: an engineer, manager, reviewer or instructor deciding whether to
learn, teach, use or rely on this work. They read carefully but are not
specialists in philosophy or software. They need to see what is claimed, why it
matters to their work, and where its limits are.

## Rules

1. **Lead with the point.** The first sentence of a paragraph says what the
   paragraph establishes. Supporting detail follows.
2. **Say who does what.** Prefer a person, an agent or a document as the subject
   ("the person decides") over an abstraction ("the decision remains reserved").
3. **Connect the reasoning.** When sentences depend on each other, join them
   with *because*, *so*, *but* or *then* instead of leaving a run of fragments.
4. **Use the concrete word.** Replace an abstract noun with the thing it refers
   to when a plainer word carries the same meaning: *the work* or *the project*
   for *the undertaking*, *reasons* or *evidence* for *grounds*, *matters* for
   *is consequential*. Keep a term from the course or thesis glossary when it is
   used in its defined sense, including *judgment* and *reckoning*.
5. **Keep every claim at its strength.** Do not add, drop, strengthen or soften a
   claim, qualification, example, citation, number or link. A limit stated in
   the source stays stated.
6. **Keep headings.** Heading text forms the anchors that other documents link
   to. Change a heading only together with every link to it.
7. **Edit only what needs it.** A clear paragraph stays as written. The size of
   an edit follows the passage, not a target.
8. **Punctuation and spelling.** Use no em dashes in revised text; use a colon,
   comma, parentheses or a new sentence. Keep the spelling the document already
   uses.

## Checks for each revision

- Citations, links, numbers and headings are unchanged unless the revision says
  otherwise.
- Course reading editions are regenerated and current with their sources
  (`docs/human-team-courses/MAINTAINING.md`).
- The chirality.ai thesis import and link audit pass on the revision.
- A separate reviewer compares old and new text for changes in meaning, claim
  strength or lost qualifications. The editor resolves every finding.
