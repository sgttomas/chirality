## Proposed canonical position

1. **Information is an externalizable substrate.** It may be recorded,
   transmitted, reproduced, compared, and identified independently of any
   particular knower's present act of knowing.
2. **Knowledge is a situated achievement of a knower.** Knowledge belongs to
   the knower, not to information considered by itself. A knower may know
   wrongly, incompletely, or provisionally; what the knower knows may later be
   revised.
3. **Identical information permits configurational multiplicity.** The same
   information may occasion different knowledge for different knowers, or for
   one knower in different contexts, for different purposes, or at different
   times. Categorization schemes and semantic lenses scaffold interpretation;
   they do not exhaust in advance what a knower may know in the information.
4. **Authentication is relational and attributable.** Authentication is an
   accountable actor's scoped, purpose-specific, SHA-bound act concerning
   identified information. It confers accountable-reliance status within
   that stated scope and purpose. It neither creates knowledge, guarantees
   correctness, nor establishes metaphysical truth.
5. **The accountability gap is the primary chirality of knowledge.** The
   permanent non-identity between externalizable information and accountable
   knowing is the sole primary sense in which the project describes knowledge
   as chiral. The operational missing-warrant state `Gap` remains a separate,
   remediable condition within the project's six epistemic primitives.
6. **The operative term is “configurational multiplicity.”** Chapter 3 uses
   this term for the multiple knowings that identical information may
   occasion. Quantum superposition may appear only as a bounded,
   non-foundational analogy in Appendix D. The appendix must state that the
   analogy is not a physical explanation, not a formal model of knowledge,
   and not a warrant for the architecture.

## Exclusions and limits

The proposed ruling does **not** authorize or assert:

- geometric precision or exact geometric correspondence;
- a universal account of duality, or treatment of every duality as chiral;
- claim/warrant, meaning/commitment, content/accountability, the former
  four-pillar pairs, or other pairs as independently chiral structures;
- a factive requirement under which only true or correct knowing counts as
  knowledge;
- quantum mechanics as the foundation, cause, or formal model of the
  epistemic account;
- authentication as observation, collapse, transformation of information
  into knowledge, creation of knowledge, or establishment of truth;
- recurrence of one chiral duality at every project level; or
- any change to public APIs, types, invariants, lifecycle transitions,
  schemas, runtime behavior, agent behavior, or professional authority.
