Verdict, two axes. As a mechanics kernel and governance artifact, the engine is genuinely mature: ~104k LOC of disciplined Rust, real numerical methods, 926 test attributes, 64 hand-calc-witnessed benchmark cases, and a boundary discipline (no allowables, no defaults, everything user-entered with provenance) that is the product's point. As a validated analysis tool, it is deliberately not mature: every tolerance policy is TBD, all 66 validation-manual cases sit at DRAFT_EVIDENCE, 100 of 101 deliverables are IN_PROGRESS, and professional reliance is affirmatively withheld (D-61 CLOSED_WITH_RELIANCE_HOLD). Under the 2026-07-31 owner intent of record — a design tool built to a standard for a limited purpose, with judgment and validation external — the second axis is not a defect list; it is the intended posture.

  • Rust, product name OpenPipeStress, inside projects/chirality-piping/ (workspace openpipestress-workspace). ~104,300 LOC across 63 .rs files in 37 crates; 926 #[test] attributes. Per-crate builds by explicit --manifest-path.
  • Solver kernel (core/solver/, ~15,600 LOC, 9 crates): frame_kernel, straight_pipe, curved_bend, linear_supports, nonlinear_supports, nonlinear_integration, sparse_direct, diagnostics, performance_harness.

  • Method quality is real, not scaffolding: RCM ordering with in-repo skyline LDLT and a dense Gauss parity oracle (core/solver/sparse_direct/README.md); Castigliano flexibility formulation for curved bends with closed-form arc integrals, no quadrature (core/solver/curved_bend/README.md).

2 · Feature coverage

Implemented

  • Linear statics: 3D 12-DOF Euler–Bernoulli frame, direction-cosine transforms, BC reduction, dense + sparse parity.
  • All eight load categories (core/loads/primitive_loads/src/lib.rs:14-52): Weight, Pressure, Thermal, ImposedDisplacement, Hydrotest, Wind, Seismic (equivalent-static), Occasional.
  • Wind incl. subspan exposure: user-entered pressure, shape factor, exposed diameters; partial-span lever-rule distribution with blocking diagnostics; hand-calc witness validation/hand_calcs/mechanics/tp_pmm_p3_subspan_wind_exposure.md.
  • Pressure thrust incl. expansion-joint effective area and a curved-bend arc fixture; thermal axial EAαΔT plus curved-bend/expansion-loop thermal bending.
  • Supports: linear families (Anchor, Guide, LineStop, VerticalSupport, Spring, ImposedDisplacement); nonlinear one-way, gap, lift-off, friction via state-switched active set (DEC-067); constant-effort supports present.
  • DEC-077 material discipline implemented as ruled: per-load-case modulus basis; linear interpolation of user-entered E and α only between strictly bracketing adjacent points, both source-point IDs and method recorded; blocks (never extrapolates) at range edges. Shear modulus G deliberately not temperature-indexed — routed to D-45, the register's single AWAITING_RULING row.
  • Mill-tolerance effective wall; stress recovery for axial, bending, torsional shear, thin-wall pressure membrane, mechanics-only stress range.

Not implemented — and declared, not hidden

  • No dynamics of any kind (spectrum, modal, harmonic, time history): zero substantive hits repo-wide; FR-024 de-scoped by owner ruling D-12 (O-B).
  • No SIF/flexibility tables, allowables, or code combinations — by design; flexibility factors are user-entered opaque validated numbers. This is the design-tool boundary enforced in code.
  • No von Mises/Tresca/principal/SRSS; no Bourdon effect; no shear deformation (Euler–Bernoulli only); no spring-hanger sizing; friction path-history (anti-chatter) unresolved, gated on a future ruling.
