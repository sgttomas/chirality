Suggested wording:

> The structures also connect across successive undertakings. Attention and decomposition organize an intent into a tree; relationships among its units inform production dependencies; production generates observations, proposals, failures and decisions that enter the network of records; renewed attention draws on that history when interpreting or revising the work. This conceptual recurrence is not an instruction to run `LOOP_INIT` or any universal execution loop. Human direction shapes the purpose and consequential changes, while acceptance for reliance follows the applicable authority and scope. Recording a proposal or failed result preserves evidence without making it accepted.

This preserves phase-sensitive entry, allows learning from unsuccessful work, and avoids implying that only accepted material may enter memory. The revised candidate should receive the proposed narrow backcheck.
