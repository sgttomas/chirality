07 · The hinge

Authority remained human; closeout conformance needs a precise account

The charter’s deepest claim is that authority moves only through attributable human acts. The closing-day direction satisfies that claim: the owner deliberately delegated acceptance of later recommendations within one named plan. The assessment must not confuse that acceptable delegation with two narrower procedural questions: self-merge under D-8 and approval identity under K-AUTH-2.

On 2026-07-28 the owner directed, in session: “Finish out your plan now … with self merge of PRs and auto approve for owners rulings … with your recommendation standing as what I approved.” That sentence — quoted verbatim across the resulting records — supplied plan-bounded umbrella authority for later Agent 0 recommendations, including the App hold posture, the 51-contract repin, the APP-HOLD-1 release, the PEC SOW reconciliation, the PEC-HOLD-001 release, the Piping current-effect acceptance, Root Receipts 58–59, and the terminal COMPLETE_FOR_ARCHITECTURE_REMEDIATION verdict, whose own evaluation package states that its recommendation becomes the owner-approved disposition when the package merges. PRs #394–#408 then merged in roughly three and a half hours. The speed is context for review, not evidence that the owner act was invalid.

HINGE-1 · accepted owner delegation

The direction is a real, attributable, per-plan-bounded human act. Letting Agent 0’s later recommendations stand as approved is an acceptable delegation choice under the Root’s A1+B2 posture. Authority remained with the owner; the agent supplied judgment and execution within the delegated envelope.

HINGE-2 · confirmed D-8 conflict at this basis

The delegated self-merge permission conflicted with the accepted Root commitment that Git closeout uses human-gated PRs and never self-merges. That is a procedural-conformance finding, not an authority finding. The owner has since authorized changes around this conflict in another session; those bytes should be reviewed before this assessment recommends any further correction.

HINGE-3 · SHA-binding review

K-AUTH-2 requires approvals to bind to a specific Git SHA and expressly admits more than one approval vehicle. The remaining question is therefore factual: for each recommendation accepted under the umbrella direction, did the durable record bind the owner act to the resulting bytes? Review that tranche inventory before deciding whether any backfill is required.

Why precision still matters at the next phase. This assessment established the accepted paper boundary but did not test its operational enforcement. When later rulings authorize runtime/ changes, releases, or professional reliance, an umbrella delegation should state its scope, SHA-binding mechanism, merge authority, expiry, and exclusions. That preserves efficient owner delegation without making procedural exceptions implicit.
