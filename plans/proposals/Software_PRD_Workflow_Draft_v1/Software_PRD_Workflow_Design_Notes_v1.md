# software-prd: design and review record

Version 1 · 21 September 2026 · HELPS_HUMANS design contribution

## What is being proposed

A reusable workflow named `software-prd` develops a software product basis from shared intent, examines it, and returns a human-accepted PRD for FEED. It applies from a feature to a coordinated product or platform. It excludes maintenance, repair, issue execution, and stand-alone data repair or analysis. A ticket can supply intent for a genuine feature project; an API, library, or reusable data-processing service can be a product without having screens.

This is a new design requested by Ryan Tufts. Its choices are proposals for his review. The existing DBM method and manual account supply background and transferable practice; they do not prove that this new workflow will work or constitute its acceptance.

The package contains three files: `WORKFLOW.md`, `resources/product-questions.md`, and `resources/records-and-review.md`. The core method is readable without a machinery of schemas, generated maps, or required helper tools. The supporting resources are loaded at the relevant stages. The complete package accompanies this record.

## Interpretation of the human's request

The user asks for a software-development method of its own, designed through HELPS_HUMANS and the supplied create-workflow method. The aim is a usable project basis, rather than a software rebranding of the DOMAIN-derived DBM publication pipeline. Data intake and triage should examine relevant existing material while remaining proportionate to a feature or a much larger development project. The manual should then explain this specific method, preserving the material the user already found useful.

The design retains the user's established course: Conceptual produces alignment and the accepted PRD; FEED performs decomposition and setup; execution definition forms the DAG toward 30%; detailed work develops toward 60%; contributions culminate toward 90%; delivery follows toward 100%. It retains considerable early human judgment, agent reckoning, the four fixed roles, and the distinction between product commitments and the records through which participants understand them.

## Principal choices and their reasons

### Two grouped human checkpoints

Checkpoint A confirms product direction and the basis for authoring. It groups the outcome, boundaries, constraints, material source limits, consequential questions, and the proposed writing and review approach. A direction already given in the actual conversation can satisfy it. It does not demand approval of every source, heading, or draft paragraph.

Checkpoint B concerns the complete reviewed PRD, the exact content set, its next use, and the treatment of remaining work. The human accepts or returns the actual document. Earlier alignment cannot substitute for this later examination because writing may introduce interpretations the person has not seen.

The two checkpoints are a new proposal, not an existing Chirality rule inferred from the DBM workflow. They reduce seven publication decisions to the two distinct forms of human commitment this method needs. Consequential design questions can still return between them. Uncertainty and routine mechanical repair do not automatically create new prompts.

### Product formation rather than republishing accepted knowledge

The method can receive conversation, existing specifications, observations, prototypes, code inspection, interface information, and new design proposals. It must distinguish their contributions. A proposed capability is legitimate design work; calling it an already demonstrated capability would be a different claim.

The DBM publisher consumes an accepted DOMAIN root and publishes its admitted current content. Requiring that whole arrangement for an initial software PRD would either exclude new projects or put project preparation before its own requirements. This workflow therefore has no DOMAIN root, publication-admission closure, semantic matrix, or DBM helper dependency.

### Proportionate intake with a reason for every material treatment

A small feature can record a few sources in the authoring record. A larger product can use an inventory and linked registers. Sources are examined for actual identity, relevant authority, quality, currency, applicability, and conflicts. Triage determines what can support current drafting and what needs investigation or a decision. An unreadable source blocks the reliance that needs it, not every unrelated paragraph.

The method keeps source constraints in the body where they affect product meaning. It does not turn every source into normative content by placing it in a manifest. It also rejects a common error in source-based generation: converting absence of material into a product exclusion or treating a suggested approach as the owner's decision.

### Domain-appropriate content without a universal form

The optional question guide addresses users and consumers, product state, data, interfaces, operating conditions, quality, failures, permissions, verification, validation, and delivery. It includes headless and multi-system products so that the method does not assume the editor example or a consumer application. Applicability to each subject must be established from the actual product.

Larger products may have a coherent overview and normative annexes. The accepted set is explicit, with common obligations in identifiable homes. This is document organisation, not premature FEED decomposition. The method does not generate package IDs, Scope Items, Context Envelopes, or a DAG.

### An independently examined candidate

A competent human or a fresh-context agent that did not author the candidate examines it against relevant original directions and sources. Checks are proportioned to the possible failure. A brief feature still needs a separate examination; it need not receive a large review packet. Where no such review can be obtained, the method returns a review-pending draft rather than relabelling self-checks as independence.

This is a proposed default within the new method. It is compatible with the supplied loop's separate review of a changed candidate, but the method does not assume a Git host or import repository-specific commands. Human PRD acceptance remains distinct from an agent review and from any repository merge decision. No actual independent review of this new workflow has been performed in the present authoring session.

### A small record with exact acceptance identity

Two mutable working files are enough for a small run: the candidate PRD and its authoring record. Normative content, source evidence, human directions, and navigation retain different roles. Larger records may be split where useful.

The exact candidate set is retained before checkpoint B. The decision is recorded separately from the bytes it identifies, avoiding an acceptance flag or a manifest that changes its own hash. An accepted predecessor remains available while a successor is drafted. Changed bytes need affected review and a corresponding human decision; unrelated accepted decisions are preserved.

### End at the boundary of PRD work

The handoff supplies an exact input to `software-decomp` with constraints and unresolved work. Its consumer still makes its own three grouped decisions. Producing a PRD creates no new authority to run FEED, edit accepted decomposition, or implement the product. Existing human direction may already authorise the next undertaking; the workflow does not invent another permission request where that direction exists.

Later changes to an accepted PRD are within the method's revision capability. Changes that also affect accepted decomposition become a `scope-change` handoff. Neither an edited PRD nor its acceptance claims that downstream propagation has occurred.

## What was retained and what was not transferred from DBM

Retained: examination of source roles; understanding before document structure; substantive content in the body; explicit uncertainty and supersession; bounded contributions with integration ownership; post-authoring examination; candidate-specific human acceptance.

Not transferred: a prerequisite DOMAIN root; seven mandatory publication decisions; separate mandatory schema, rules, section-map, and authorisation records; knowledge-artifact selectors; required publication or review helper tools; a formal concordance substrate; mandatory section delegation; facility-specific supersession machinery; publication registration or lifecycle acts.

The six review finding categories are retained as useful diagnostic aids, without a new fixed severity system, automatic acceptance score, or additional per-finding human gate. Optional semantic processing remains outside the required path.

## Validation and limitations

The saved files are read back, their metadata and package-name grammar checked, internal resource links resolved, and resource containment and ordinary-file status inspected. A local structural checker and its result accompany the bundle. It is not a Chirality runtime validator. The supplied archives do not contain the named workflow-metadata and index validators, so no pass from those tools is claimed.

`WALKTHROUGH.md` examines representative use, non-applicable work, failures, revision, and interruption against the saved method. These are analytical tabletop cases, not real workflow runs, user acceptances, executed child agents, or empirical evidence of successful reuse. No product was built and no PRD was accepted during this design task.

Package inspection is distinct from registration, observed catalog discovery, independent review, and successful reuse. The last four remain unperformed. The manuscript now has a concrete proposed method to explain, but its prose is not authority to mark that method accepted.

## Questions for human review

The principal design question is whether the two-checkpoint arrangement puts human attention in the right places, with existing alignment allowed to satisfy checkpoint A. A second inspectable choice is the separate-review requirement even for a small feature, scaled in depth but not silently omitted. The package proposes both directly so that the human can retain, narrow, or revise them.

No answer is manufactured. Method registration, actual PRD production, and later FEED execution remain separate undertakings after this return.
