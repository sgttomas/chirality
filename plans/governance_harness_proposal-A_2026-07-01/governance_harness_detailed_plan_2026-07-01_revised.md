# Chirality Governance Harness Detailed Plan — Revised Practitioner Bench Architecture

## 0. Executive verdict

Build the **practitioner bench tool** version of the governance harness.
Do not build a database-owned control plane, autonomous coordinator, lifecycle-transition system, or general project-management layer as the first move.

The harness should help a human practitioner and bounded agents answer five questions:

1. What is the current state of the relevant project, domain, or governance root?
2. What is the next bounded tranche of work?
3. What files and authorities define the permissible work fence?
4. What evidence exists, is missing, stale, contradictory, or out of scope?
5. What human decision is required before any claim becomes binding?

The harness is therefore a **compiler of existing governed truth**, not a creator of new governed truth. It reads project files, produces generated views, prepares candidate tranche briefs, runs objective checks, and stops at human judgment boundaries.

## 1. Authority and adoption boundary

This revised plan is itself only planning material. It does not supersede governed project files, root governance documents, decision records, approval SHAs, validation evidence, git history, or practitioner judgment.

Before implementation is treated as governance-enforcing, add the following human decision records.

| Decision record | Required ruling | Why it is first-order |
|---|---|---|
| `D-GH-001_substrate_authority.md` | Git/versioned authored files are the sole authority. Harness outputs are generated views or candidate briefs only. SQLite, if later introduced, is rebuildable projection/cache only. | Closes the conflict between a heavier control-plane proposal and the practitioner-bench direction. |
| `D-GH-002_verifier_severity.md` | Define `BLOCK`, `REVIEW`, `WARN`, `INFO`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, who may override each, and what exit codes mean. | Prevents the harness from over-blocking judgmental work or under-reporting objective violations. |
| `D-GH-003_pilot_scope.md` | MVP pilot scope is `chirality-app-dev`, `chirality-piping`, and `_DomainEngines` read-only status/self-check only. | Keeps the first release narrow and testable. |
| `D-GH-004_human_actor_identity.md` | Define what counts as a human-authored approval/ruling and what metadata must bind it to content. | Prevents agent-authored prose from masquerading as approval. |
| `D-GH-005_minimal_governance_basis.md` | Ratify the minimal governance basis needed by the harness: source-of-truth rule, human authority rule, generated-output rule, path containment, approval-SHA binding, evidence/provenance requirement, and verifier severities. | Root `docs/{DIRECTIVE,CONTRACT,SPEC,TYPES,PLAN}.md` are draft/pending in the archive. The harness needs a ratified minimum basis before it claims enforcement. |
| `D-GH-006_domain_profile_current_truth.md` | Resolve whether `open_pipe_stress` is currently `DRAFT`, `VALIDATED`, `ADOPTED`, or historically draft but currently adopted. | The archive contains contradictory current-state signals that a harness must not silently normalize. |

Until these rulings exist, all harness findings should be labeled **advisory**, except purely local technical checks such as path containment, source-file existence, and generated-output labeling.

## 2. Design principles

### 2.1 Files remain the reliance boundary

The harness reads authored files and git history. It may write generated reports, JSON projections, and candidate briefs, but those outputs are not authority unless a human separately adopts them in the governed record.

A generated file must carry this header:

```text
GENERATED VIEW — NOT AUTHORITY
Sources cited inline.
Regenerate from project files.
Do not edit this file as a source of truth.
Structural checks do not constitute approval, issue, authentication, professional review, or acceptance of residual risk.
```

### 2.2 No hidden authority store

A database may be useful later, but only as a projection:

- rebuildable from git-tracked files and evidence artifacts;
- safe to delete and regenerate;
- not required to interpret approvals;
- not the only place status, lifecycle, or decisions exist;
- labeled cache/projection, not governance authority.

### 2.3 Status is multidimensional

The harness must not emit one global green badge. It should keep these dimensions separate:

- lifecycle state;
- evidence completeness;
- reference/hash resolution;
- approval basis and approval SHA status;
- staleness/drift status;
- scope conformance;
- human decision state;
- warrant/authentication state;
- domain profile/adoption status where applicable.

A deliverable may be structurally complete and still unauthenticated. Evidence may be present and still insufficient. A domain profile may be validated by schema and still require a human adoption ruling.

### 2.4 Mechanical checks can block; judgmental checks must surface

The harness may fail a command or CI job for objective defects such as out-of-scope writes, missing required source files, malformed JSON, unresolved references, or generated reports lacking disclaimers.

It must not autonomously approve, issue, seal, certify, authenticate, accept residual risk, or decide professional sufficiency.

### 2.5 Historical record and current truth must be distinguishable

The archive contains legitimate historical material and also conflicting current-state claims. The harness must distinguish:

- immutable historical snapshots;
- current authoritative state;
- generated summaries;
- stale prose that contradicts later ruled facts;
- draft/root governance material that has not been ratified.

The harness must surface current-truth conflicts rather than picking the newest or most convenient claim.

## 4. The harness architecture

The harness has five layers.

```text
Human authority
  ↓ adopts, rules, approves, rejects
Authored files and git history
  ↓ read by adapters
Harness projections and checks
  ↓ emit generated views / findings / candidate briefs
Bounded agent work
  ↓ scoped execution + evidence
Human review and governed update
```

### 4.1 Layer 1 — Authority files

The authority layer is not created by the harness. It consists of existing authored artifacts, such as:

- root governance documents;
- project-local status files;
- project-local plans and coordination files;
- decision records and ruling files;
- dependency registers and DAG artifacts;
- domain profiles and validation reports;
- run/evidence artifacts;
- git commit history and approval SHAs.

The harness may report contradictions within this layer. It does not silently resolve them.

### 4.2 Layer 2 — Adapters

Do not start with one universal governance schema. Start with small adapters that read real project shapes.

| Adapter | Reads | Emits |
|---|---|---|
| `root_governance_adapter` | `README.md`, root docs, framework/professional docs, plan files | Governance-basis status, draft/ratification warnings, source path model, generated-output rules. |
| `app_dev_adapter` | Local coordination/status/plans/validation surfaces in `projects/chirality-app-dev` when available | Current queue posture, deliverable state counts, active tranche candidates, validation commands, caveats. |
| `piping_adapter` | Local PRD/DAG/status/decision/validation surfaces in `projects/chirality-piping` when available | PRD stage, DAG context, deliverable state distribution, validation surfaces, unresolved decisions. |
| `domain_engines_adapter` | `_DomainEngines/**`, profiles, validation reports, published rulings, latest pointer | Domain profile status, integration level, protected/write surfaces, validation posture, contradictions. |
| `git_adapter` | Local git status, commit graph, diff ranges, object existence | Approval SHA reachability, changed paths, evidence age checks, diff scope. |

Adapters return sourced facts, not unsourced summaries.

```json
{
  "fact_id": "domain.open_pipe_stress.profile_status",
  "value": "ADOPTED",
  "source_path": "_DomainEngines/profiles/open_pipe_stress.DRAFT.yaml",
  "source_hint": "domain_profile.profile_status",
  "authority_status": "conflicted_current_truth",
  "caveat": "File header says DRAFT/NOT adopted while field says ADOPTED."
}
```

### 4.3 Layer 3 — Commands

The MVP command surface should answer practitioner questions directly.

| Command | Purpose | Writes | Authority boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| `chirality-harness status --project app-dev` | Summarize active queue, status counts, validation posture, unresolved decisions, dirty git state. | Markdown/JSON generated view only. | Read-only relative to governed files. |
| `chirality-harness status --project piping` | Summarize current plan/PRD stage, DAG pointer, status distribution, decision register state, validation surfaces. | Markdown/JSON generated view only. | Read-only relative to governed files. |
| `chirality-harness status --domain-engines` | Summarize domain profiles, integration level, validation reports, protected paths, open issues, contradictions. | Markdown/JSON generated view only. | Read-only relative to governed files. |
| `chirality-harness self-check --root .` | Check the governance corpus for contradictions, stale fields, missing disclaimers, absolute paths, unresolved references, draft/root-basis issues. | Findings report only. | May block objective hygiene failures; does not approve fixes. |
| `chirality-harness brief --project <name> --tranche <id>` | Generate a candidate tranche brief from a practitioner-selected active work item. | Candidate brief JSON/Markdown only. | Candidate until human adopted. |
| `chirality-harness scope-check --brief <brief.json> --diff <range>` | Compare changed paths against brief read/write/evidence scopes. | Findings report only. | May block objective path violations. |
| `chirality-harness evidence-check --brief <brief.json>` | Check whether required validations, artifacts, skip reasons, and evidence records are present. | Findings report only. | Reports completeness, not sufficiency. |
| `chirality-harness closeout-digest --brief <brief.json> --diff <range>` | Summarize changed files, evidence, skipped checks, caveats, and human questions for a closeout review. | Generated digest only. | Does not transition lifecycle. |

### 4.4 Layer 4 — Generated views

Generated files should live in an obviously non-authoritative location, such as:

```text
<working-root>/_HarnessViews/
<working-root>/_HarnessBriefs/
<working-root>/_HarnessReports/
```

or, if a project already has a coordination output root:

```text
<working-root>/_Coordination/HarnessGenerated/
```

Every generated output must include:

- generation timestamp;
- command used;
- repo root and working root;
- git commit SHA or dirty-state caveat;
- source files used;
- authority disclaimer;
- structural-check disclaimer;
- machine-readable finding list when applicable.

### 4.5 Layer 5 — Human decision points

The harness should explicitly surface decisions that require a human. Examples:

- “Human ruling required: profile status conflict.”
- “Human ruling required: root governance basis remains draft.”
- “Human approval required: lifecycle transition from `CHECKING` to `ISSUED`.”
- “Human review required: evidence exists, but sufficiency is professional judgment.”
- “Human review required: upstream dependency changed after approval SHA.”

## 5. Tranche model

The core work object is the **tranche**, not the task, dashboard row, database event, or agent run.

A tranche is a bounded intervention against project-local context. It should be small enough for scope containment and evidence review.

### 5.1 Candidate tranche brief fields

```yaml
schema: chirality-harness-tranche-brief/v1
brief_id: BRIEF-YYYYMMDD-<slug>
status: CANDIDATE # CANDIDATE | HUMAN_ADOPTED | SUPERSEDED
project: chirality-piping
tranche_id: <project-local-id>
objective: <bounded objective>
source_basis:
  - path: <repo-relative-path>
    expected_sha_or_hash: <optional>
read_scope:
  - <repo-relative-glob>
write_scope:
  - <repo-relative-glob>
prohibited_paths:
  - <repo-relative-glob>
expected_outputs:
  - <repo-relative-path-or-glob>
required_validations:
  - id: <validation-id>
    command: <command>
    evidence_path: <path>
skip_policy:
  required_reason: true
stop_conditions:
  - <objective condition requiring stop>
human_decision_points:
  - <question requiring practitioner call>
authority_notice: GENERATED CANDIDATE — NOT AUTHORITY UNTIL HUMAN ADOPTED
```

### 5.2 Tranche adoption

A generated brief is not binding until a human adopts it according to the future `D-GH-004` identity/approval rule. The harness can record that a brief appears to have been adopted only by reading the governed adoption artifact.

## 6. Finding model and severity taxonomy

Use a small common finding schema across checks.

```json
{
  "finding_id": "GH-SCOPE-001",
  "severity": "BLOCK",
  "title": "Out-of-scope write",
  "description": "Changed path is outside the adopted write scope.",
  "source_paths": ["BRIEF.json", "git diff --name-only"],
  "affected_paths": ["projects/chirality-piping/core/..."],
  "human_action": "Either revert the change or obtain a revised human-adopted brief.",
  "machine_action": "fail"
}
```

| Severity | Meaning | Machine behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `BLOCK` | Objective violation. The harness can safely fail the command or CI job. | Exit nonzero. |
| `REVIEW` | Material issue requiring human judgment. | Exit zero by default unless strict mode is requested. |
| `WARN` | Non-blocking inconsistency, hygiene issue, or stale advisory. | Exit zero. |
| `INFO` | Contextual fact useful for review. | Exit zero. |
| `NOT_APPLICABLE` | Check intentionally skipped because preconditions do not apply. | Exit zero and report reason. |

Initial `BLOCK` examples:

- out-of-scope write;
- path traversal or write-scope escape;
- required source file missing;
- adopted/issued claim with `Ruling SHA: TBD` or missing approval basis;
- generated report missing required disclaimer;
- protected domain path modified by a non-domain tool;
- malformed required validation report.

Initial `REVIEW` examples:

- conflicting profile status across current-truth files;
- root governance docs draft while downstream docs treat them as binding;
- evidence present but sufficiency unclear;
- stale open issue contradicted by later validation evidence;
- upstream dependency changed after approval SHA.

## 7. Required self-checks

`chirality-harness self-check --root .` is a first-class MVP command, not a later nicety.

Initial checks:

| Check | Detects | Suggested severity |
|---|---|---|
| `status_header_body_conflict` | Header/frontmatter says draft/proposal/TBD while body claims ruled/adopted/issued. | `REVIEW`, `BLOCK` if used as authority. |
| `profile_status_conflict` | Filename/header/YAML status/index/latest/validation disagree. | `REVIEW`. |
| `stale_open_issue` | Open-issue prose is contradicted by later validation or ruling artifacts. | `REVIEW`. |
| `absolute_path_leak` | Machine-local absolute paths in instruction, coordination, plan, profile, validation, or generated files where forbidden. | `WARN` or `BLOCK` depending on file class. |
| `generated_disclaimer_missing` | Generated report lacks non-authority disclaimer. | `BLOCK`. |
| `source_ref_unresolved` | Cited source path does not exist in checkout/archive. | `BLOCK` for current authority, `WARN` for historical planning. |
| `approval_sha_unreachable` | Approval/ruling SHA is absent, `TBD`, or unreachable in git. | `BLOCK` for claims of issued/adopted authority; `REVIEW` otherwise. |
| `draft_basis_used_as_binding` | Draft root governance docs are treated as ratified authority. | `REVIEW` until minimal basis is ratified. |

The archive should be converted into adversarial fixtures for these checks.

## 8. Domain engine integration in MVP

The domain-engine area should be read-only in MVP.

The harness may:

- read `_DomainEngines/DOMAIN_ENGINE_INDEX.md`;
- read `_DomainEngines/_LATEST.md`;
- read `_DomainEngines/RULINGS_PUBLISHED.md`;
- read `_DomainEngines/_DECISIONS/**`;
- read profiles and validation reports;
- report protected paths and proposal paths;
- report status contradictions;
- check that generated OperationProposal artifacts, if present, are in allowed proposal paths;
- check that protected domain truth was not touched by an unauthorized diff.

The harness must not:

- apply domain operations;
- mutate protected engine truth;
- decide that validation equals professional correctness;
- advance `DRAFT → VALIDATED → ADOPTED`;
- claim code compliance, engineering approval, certification, sealing, or construction readiness.

## 9. Roadmap

### Phase 0 — Governance basis and plan classification

Deliverables:

- create `docs/governance_harness/PLAN_INDEX.md`;
- create `D-GH-001` through `D-GH-006` decision records as drafts for human ruling;
- mark the earlier control-plane/event-ledger assessment as historical or deferred for MVP;
- update this revised plan as the current MVP candidate;
- document root governance draft status in every generated report footer.

Acceptance criteria:

- no plan ambiguity remains about whether SQLite is authority;
- no generated output claims authority;
- human-ruling requirements are explicit.

### Phase 1 — Read-only inventory and status

Deliverables:

- harness charter under `tools/practitioner_harness/README.md`;
- adapter skeletons for root governance, app-dev, piping, domain engines, and git;
- `status` command emitting Markdown and JSON;
- generated-output disclaimer enforcement.

Acceptance criteria:

- status generation modifies no authority files;
- every material claim cites source paths;
- reports distinguish lifecycle, evidence, approval basis, staleness, scope, and human decision state.

### Phase 2 — Self-consistency checks

Deliverables:

- `self-check` command;
- checks for profile status conflict, stale open issues, TBD/ruling conflicts, absolute path leakage, missing generated disclaimers, unresolved references;
- fixture tests based on `_DomainEngines` contradictions.

Acceptance criteria:

- the OpenPipeStress draft/adopted contradiction fixture is detected;
- `RULINGS_PUBLISHED` contradictory current-state claims are detected;
- `D-T0-*` header/body ruling conflicts are detected;
- validation absolute-path leakage is detected.

### Phase 3 — Candidate tranche briefs

Deliverables:

- `brief` command;
- tranche brief JSON/Markdown schema;
- read/write/prohibited path model;
- human-stop-condition rendering.

Acceptance criteria:

- generated briefs are clearly candidate/non-authoritative;
- brief contains source basis, read scope, write scope, validation commands, evidence targets, prohibited paths, and human decision points;
- no lifecycle transition or approval claim is created.

### Phase 4 — Scope and evidence checks

Deliverables:

- `scope-check` against a diff range;
- `evidence-check` against required validations and artifacts;
- robust path containment tests covering absolute paths, `..`, symlinks, case weirdness, and generated-output locations.

Acceptance criteria:

- out-of-scope writes fail as `BLOCK`;
- protected domain paths touched by unauthorized work fail as `BLOCK`;
- missing evidence reports completeness gaps without approving sufficiency;
- evidence older than changed files is flagged.

### Phase 5 — Optional cache and CI integration

Only after file projections are stable and query pain is real:

- add a rebuildable local index/cache;
- add CI wrappers for objective `BLOCK` findings;
- keep all authoritative lifecycle and decision state in authored files.

## 10. Cuts and deferrals

The following are intentionally not MVP work:

- universal obligations register;
- full RAIL/action/risk/change register system;
- SQLite governance store as operational authority;
- leases and coordinator process;
- autonomous lifecycle transitions;
- agent-dispatch scheduler;
- broad document-control model;
- generalized event ledger;
- green dashboard UI;
- MCP/domain-engine write integration;
- automatic issue/approval/closeout claims.

These can be revisited after the read-only bench tool earns trust.

## 12. Final guardrail

The harness is successful when it makes the human decision smaller, clearer, and better evidenced.

It fails if it becomes a cleaner-looking second source of truth.
