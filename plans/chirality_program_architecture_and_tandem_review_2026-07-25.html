01 · Program thesis

One continuing program; several distinct products and situated forms

“Program” names the continuing human-governed enterprise. “Root” names its canonical generative product. App, PEC, shared runtime, resource governance, domain applications, and domain engines occupy distinct positions within that program; convergence does not erase their ownership boundaries.

The Chirality Program is the continuing human-governed program through which Chirality Root, shared capabilities, situated applications, and domain forms are developed, evaluated, and brought into governed convergence.
Program

The longitudinal enterprise

Holds continuity of human intention, the product family, developmental inquiry, accumulated evidence, and decisions about what should remain situated or converge toward Root.

Root

The canonical generative product

Owns the common governing form: normative basis, reusable instruction and method resources, shared runtime substrate, developmental machinery, evidence conventions, and safe self-application.

Products and situated forms

Where the common form becomes usable or particular

App and PEC provide distinct reusable product capabilities. Piping, future domain applications, domains, and working roots specialize the common form according to product purpose and professional circumstances.

The monorepo is not identical to the Program. It is the canonical record and principal development environment. The Program also includes the human inquiry, judgments, uses, and continuity that no directory tree can perform by itself.

02 · Architecture

A situated application is a declared composition, not a leaf in a hierarchy

The owner’s graph is best read as a capability wheel. A domain application sits where Root, the reusable work surface, PEC, resource governance, runtime execution, and domain truth are composed. The edges are first-class contracts, not accidental integration code.

Constraint and accepted capability Execution or consumption Evidence, finding, or candidate Human judgment
What the wheel preserves

Reciprocity without automatic authority transfer

Situated applications consume common capabilities and return evidence. Root, App, PEC, runtime, and resource monitoring also need direct contracts with one another. Feedback may move in every direction; authority moves only through attributable human acts and accepted records.

What the wheel corrects

No simple “platform above, app below” hierarchy

Piping or another domain product is a complete situated environment with its own UI, engine, truth, users, and agent hierarchy. It is neither a plugin nor an Agent 2. It composes Root-governed capabilities while retaining domain ownership.

03 · Ownership and relationship contracts

Every shared function needs one semantic owner

Code location, product ownership, client integration, and professional authority are different facts. The review must prevent temporary migration arrangements or implementation convenience from turning into permanent divided ownership.

04 · Assertion status

Keep accepted architecture separate from emerging program insight

This document deliberately records confidence and authority status. A useful interpretation must not silently become a requirement merely because it is coherent.

Accepted basis Root is the canonical human-governed application environment and generative operating form; human judgment is the hinge; Root serves situated variants; domain engines own domain truth. Missing or contradictory decomposition/SOW coverage is a conformance finding.
Accepted basis The root runtime/ executable substrate is part of the Root operative product; its operational state is not project truth or authority. Runtime ownership must land at Root; consumer products cannot redefine it silently.
Clarified framing The Chirality App has two useful identities: standalone general application and reference/reusable human–agent work surface. Test whether App PRD/SOWs support this without claiming it is already governed in exact terms.
Clarified framing A situated domain application is a composition of shared capabilities, its own UI/engine, governed working state, and human judgment. Look for an application-integration home; do not force one topology from the metaphor.

Candidate capability PEC is generally available for complex work and resource governance provides budgets, usage, cost, and forecast monitoring. PEC’s present product contract governs what exists; resource governance needs separate definition before becoming mandatory scope.
Open design question Physical bundling versus logical composition: embedded resources, companion process, sidecar, shared installation, or compatible client. Require declared compatibility and one runtime owner; avoid duplicative daemon or governance forks.

05 · Reviewer guidance

Use this as a challenge set, never as an additional accepted basis

This synthesis may help reviewers see relationships and failure modes that are easy to miss when reading one product at a time. It must not decide their findings in advance. Each reviewer first orients from the exact frozen repository basis, forms an independent account, and only then uses this document to challenge that account.

Orient

Begin from governed records

Establish the accepted PRD, decomposition, registers, ScopeOfWork files, decisions, and snapshots. Where this synthesis differs, the governed record controls and the difference is itself useful review evidence.

Challenge

Probe without prescribing

Use the architecture and questions to test omissions, contradictions, ambiguous ownership, hidden dependencies, and cross-product consequences. Do not assume the suggested answer is the correct one.

Route

Keep new claims out of findings

A review may expose a candidate requirement, but it cannot create one. Route genuinely new commitments through the owning PRD, governance act, or scope-change workflow with human acceptance.

Permissible treatment follows assertion status

Assertion status What the reviewer may conclude What the reviewer must not do
Accepted basis Return an evidence-linked conformance finding when accepted intent is missing, contradicted, or left without executable coverage. Replace the accepted meaning with this document’s preferred interpretation.
Clarified framing Return a concordance question, ambiguity, architectural risk, or recommendation for owner consideration. Fail a product merely because it does not instantiate the clarified framing.
Candidate architecture Return an evidence-linked proposal or owner-decision request when the candidate would resolve a demonstrated problem. Count absence as a scope gap or introduce the candidate through decomposition or ScopeOfWork prose.
Open design question Preserve viable alternatives, identify decision criteria, and show what downstream work each alternative affects. Close the question through reviewer preference or implementation convenience.
Common ownership test: for each shared function, identify its semantic owner, producers, consumers, authoritative record, compatibility obligation, fallback behavior, and routed change path. “Shared” describes use; it does not authorize divided ownership.

Architectural challenges worth testing

Ownership and authority
  • Has Root become the default owner merely because a function is shared?
  • Does every shared function have one semantic owner and one accepted record?
  • Has generic infrastructure absorbed domain truth or professional authority?
  • Can governed work remain intelligible and recoverable without PEC?
  • Has a machine gate, runtime service, or reviewer preference acquired human authority?
Composition and change
  • Is App’s standalone-product role confused with its possible reusable-work-surface role?
  • Has logical composition been mistaken for mandatory physical bundling?
  • Are compatibility, semantic parity, migration, rollback, and degraded behavior owned?
  • Has a product commitment appeared only in decomposition or ScopeOfWork prose?
  • Are both reviewers independently testing the basis, or reproducing this synthesis?
Question, do not command. “App shall provide the reusable work surface for every domain application” would create scope. Instead ask whether the accepted basis assigns ownership of such a surface and, if the scopes rely on one without an owner or contract, classify the result as a conformance gap, unresolved design question, or proposed PRD change.

06 · Tandem review charter

Two independent reviewers; one frozen basis; no consensus shortcut

A formal review should run under EVALUATION as the read-only Agent 1 manager. It dispatches two sealed, independent Agent 2 reviewers over the same complete corpus. Their first-pass reports remain separate and immutable; reciprocal challenge begins only after both returns are frozen.

Reviewer A · vertical lens

Authority, intent, and traceability

Tests status and provenance; PRD commitment → objective → SOW → package → deliverable trace; human gates; non-goals; falsifiers; accepted-versus-proposed discipline; stable IDs; and whether the decomposition preserved rather than replaced owner intent.

Reviewer B · horizontal lens

Architecture, boundaries, and adversarial concordance

Tests Root/App/PEC/runtime/domain ownership; producer, consumer, fallback, and degraded-mode relations; duplicated truth; circular dependency; self-authorization; cross-product gaps; and scope that has migrated to the wrong product.

Both reviewers cover Root, App, and PEC. Dividing products between reviewers would create coverage without independence. The lenses differ; the reviewed corpus does not. Both reviewers orient independently from the frozen repository basis before consulting this synthesis. They may then use it as a common challenge set, but neither may treat it as accepted evidence.

Showing the complete cross-product review set.

Root

Shared runtime ownership

Does one Root package own runtime protocol, daemon, clients, sessions, tools, delegation, credentials, adapters, compatibility, security, migration, and release evidence?

Cross-product

Producer, consumer, and fallback

For every shared function, are the semantic owner, consuming products, accepted basis, fallback, and routed-change obligations explicit?

Root

Four-category coverage without folder mimicry

Are normative, operative, developmental, and evidence functions covered or reasonedly deferred without simply creating four packages named after the categories?

Cross-product

Application composition boundary

Does the corpus provide a lawful home for reusable work-surface and application-integration contracts, or reveal a genuine PRD gap that decomposition may not invent?

App

Standalone product and reusable work surface

Can the App remain a coherent standalone product while its human–agent surface is reused as embedded, companion, or sidecar capability without exporting incidental presentation assumptions?

App

Faithful human/agent mediation

Do UI and direct-agent paths preserve prerequisites, authority, state effects, provenance, interruption, recovery, errors, and outcomes without either surface bypassing governance?

App ↔ Root

Runtime client, not runtime authority

Are App deliverables limited to client integration, presentation, packaging, and conformance rather than retaining permanent semantic ownership of the shared Root runtime?

PEC

Coordination without authority

Does PEC remain advisory, explainable, rebuildable, and file-fallback-safe—never a ruling surface, system of record, dispatcher, lock manager, or Git actor?

PEC ↔ environment

Complexity-dependent availability

Is PEC optional for correctness and available where concurrency warrants it, with declared identities and graceful absence rather than silent mandatory coupling?

All

Human judgment remains the hinge

Do validators, runtime state, UI controls, coordination projections, cost thresholds, and agent capability remain evidence or objective gates rather than substitutes for acceptance, reliance, iteration, or release judgment?

All

Domain truth remains situated

Do Root, App, runtime, and PEC govern work around domain engines without absorbing protected domain state, deterministic domain acts, or professional authority?

Deliverable SOW

Executable and warranted scope

Does each SOW trace to accepted product scope, have one primary owner, name interfaces and evidence, fit its context envelope, and avoid introducing new product commitments through implementation prose?

Formal two-pass protocol

  1. Freeze the review manifest. Record exact SHAs or exact bytes for Root, App, and PEC PRDs; accepted decompositions; SOWs; controlling decisions; companion registers; and the review question set. Mutable pointers are navigation only.
  2. Seal two independent briefs. Both reviewers receive the same complete corpus, scope, evidence rules, output schema, read-only boundary, and decision criteria. They do not receive one another’s reasoning or drafts.
  3. Run independent pass 1. Reviewer A applies the vertical authority/trace lens. Reviewer B applies the horizontal boundary/adversarial lens. Both produce full coverage and boundary matrices, not selected anecdotes.
  4. Validate returns deterministically. EVALUATION checks schema, evidence anchors, basis currency, coverage, stable IDs, duplicate ownership, and missing outputs before either report enters fan-in.
  5. Run reciprocal challenge. After pass-1 returns are frozen, each reviewer receives the other’s report and marks every high-severity finding—and a recorded sample of lower findings—as CONFIRM, REFUTE, NARROW, or ADD-MISSING-EVIDENCE.
  6. Fan in without averaging. EVALUATION classifies each issue as AGREED, RESOLVED_BY_EVIDENCE, STANDING_DIVERGENCE, SHARED_BLIND_SPOT_RISK, or STALE_INPUT. Agreement is not proof; disagreement is not failure.
  7. Return consequential decisions to the human. Agent 0 presents unresolved authority, scope, risk, ownership, and acceptance questions. The review itself edits no PRD, decomposition, or SOW.
  8. Route the smallest governed action. Accepted corrections go to the owning instrument: Root governance or PRD, Root decomposition/SCOPE_CHANGE, App PRD/SCOPE_CHANGE, PEC PRD/SCOPE_CHANGE, deliverable remediation, or derivative regeneration.
  9. Re-review only the affected basis. Preserve prior findings and decisions, update the manifest to the new accepted bytes, and rerun impacted trace and boundary rows.

Review closure is not product acceptance. Close the review only when every admitted PRD commitment has a trace disposition, every cross-product function has a declared truth and authority owner plus fallback, every high-severity disagreement is adjudicated or explicitly held, and the exact reviewed basis is recorded. The human still decides what to accept.

07 · Recommended sequence

Use the current pause to preserve the right order

The architecture synthesis should guide review, not pre-empt the Root decomposition or silently broaden the paused App parity work.

1 Root SOWs land

Freeze the accepted Root PRD, decomposition, registers, and deliverable scopes.

2 Tandem Root audit

Test runtime ownership, four-category coverage, application integration, evidence, and human-gated self-application.

3 Route true gaps

Distinguish decomposition repair from a genuine Root PRD amendment; do not smuggle new architecture through SOW prose.

4 Rebaseline App and PEC

Then resume App semantic-parity SCA planning and audit App/PEC against the accepted Root ownership model.

Immediate audit expectations
  • Every PRD commitment and objective has coverage or a reasoned deferral.
  • The shared runtime contract has one Root owner.
  • App, PEC, CLI, and domain products remain consumers or situated owners.
  • Human evaluation, iteration, and release judgments remain distinct.
  • No new requirement appears only in decomposition or SOW prose.
  • Dependencies and acceptance evidence make each deliverable executable.
Questions intentionally held open
  • Whether the reusable work surface is a Root requirement, an App capability, or both under different ownership.
  • The exact authority and schema of an application environment profile.
  • When PEC is available, recommended, or required by complexity.
  • The product home and authority boundary of resource governance.
  • Logical composition versus physical bundling and update cadence.
  • Which cross-client conformance proofs are release requirements.
How findings become governed work: observation → evidence-linked finding → tandem challenge → EVALUATION fan-in → human disposition → owning instrument or workflow → accepted change → affected-scope rerun. No reviewer preference enters product scope without that route.
