# W1 Population Verification

## Verdict

Independent read-only review of all 32 active `ScopeOfWork.md` contracts confirms an exact semantic-repair population of **11 contracts**:

`DEL-00-01`, `DEL-03-06`, `DEL-04-01`, `DEL-04-02`, `DEL-04-03`, `DEL-08-01`, `DEL-08-03`, `DEL-08-04`, `DEL-10-01`, `DEL-10-10`, and `DEL-10-11`.

The ten contracts forecast by D-PEC-69 are confirmed. Execution-time extraction adds **DEL-04-03** because CLM-016 does more than preserve DEL-10-12's canonical label: in a list of adjacent acts and owners it calls DEL-10-12's function “harness poll-adoption measurement.” That is a live allocation of the superseded harness-owned uptake model. Current SOW-060 instead measures candidate-consumer enablement and enabled-consumer use against consumer-owned opportunities; it creates no receiving-loop conformance criterion. CLM-017 and AX-011 inherit the same adjacent-act framing, so DEL-04-03 requires a bounded semantic repair while its citation-stamping act remains unchanged.

The other 21 contracts do not require semantic repair in this window. Their exact row-by-row dispositions are in `W1_POPULATION_VERIFICATION.csv`.

## Basis and method

The review compared every active contract against:

- adopted PRD v2.2, especially PEC-K-03, PEC-K-11, §§5, 8, 11, 12, 13, and 15;
- `SOFTWARE_DECOMP.md` revision 1.3, especially C3, C15, OI-011, and SOW-041/060/085/088;
- SCA-003's owner-ruled exact semantic postures and no-op boundaries;
- D-PEC-69's expected ten-contract population and its execution-time population-extraction rule.

Each contract was searched and read for retired ADR-014 live/allocation claims; harness polling, session-start, mode-transition, injection, and cadence assertions; consumer duty or conformance; old SOW-060/SOW-085/falsification wording; and quotations from directly affected current sources. Selection required a current incompatible meaning or an operative allocation, not merely a matching token.

Common revision-1.2 provenance and embedded `OPEN` lifecycle text were not treated as independent population selectors. D-PEC-69 makes basis/lifecycle correction part of the bounded whole-contract replacement for contracts already selected by the SCA-003 semantic change; it does not authorize converting a global housekeeping issue into a 32-contract semantic wave.

## Why the controls remain out

The notable control hits were dispositioned as follows:

- `DEL-00-03` exactly reproduces the still-present PRD v2.2 §13 sentence that calls ADR-002 and ADR-014 live. It does not translate that sentence into an operative contract duty; the seed's operative source is the accepted decomposition, whose SOW-088 is corrected. The contradiction is in the current PRD source itself and must be resolved by PRD authority, not silently rewritten as a SOW repair.
- `DEL-01-04` uses the former §11 heading and SOW-085 grounding only to establish self-observability as an instrumentation substrate. Its actual logging duties remain compatible with observable system-and-use behavior and impose no consumer contact or conformance.
- `DEL-03-03` quotes the former SOW-060/SOW-085 limb annotation to establish that parity count is not itself the falsification limb. Current SOW-060 still supports the clause together with SOW-085; the contract imposes no external duty, so its operative producer-attribution meaning remains compatible.
- `DEL-03-04` refers to unchanged OBJ-006 and practitioner-harness parity behavior, not harness polling or PEC injection.
- `DEL-10-02` quotes only the unchanged kill-test consequence of the falsification clause. `DEL-01-05`, `DEL-10-02`, and `DEL-10-03` use “fault injection” solely as a test method.
- The remaining controls contain none of the affected semantics, or use “consumer” only for ordinary dependency/interface relationships that expressly create no downstream obligation.

## Evidence comparison and discrepancies

The independent population agrees with all completed per-contract W1 discoveries reviewed. The current aggregate `W1_LEDGER_VALIDATION.json` is not a complete population artifact: it records nine deliverables and predates later W1 evidence. At verification time, `DEL-10-11` had validation/checklist/boundary/consistency files but no claim ledger or notes, so its detailed discovery return remains incomplete. These conditions do **not** block freezing the 11-contract population, but they do block treating the current aggregate W1 ledger as final discovery fan-in.

One non-blocking source-governance discrepancy remains: PRD v2.2 §13 still says “live postures (ADR-002, ADR-014)” even though D-PEC-67/-68, PRD §15, SCA-003, and SOW-088 revision 1.3 make ADR-014 historical lineage only. This review does not amend or silently supersede PRD text. Contract repair can proceed from the ruled SCA-003/D-PEC-69 basis, but the PRD contradiction should be routed to its owning authority.

## Blockers

- **Population freeze:** none; exact population is the 11 contracts listed above.
- **W1 evidence fan-in:** incomplete until `DEL-10-11` claim-ledger/notes evidence is present and the aggregate W1 census/candidate/validation artifacts are regenerated over all 11 selected contracts.
- **Source governance:** PRD §13's ADR-014 sentence is internally inconsistent with the adopted historical-lineage posture; it is not a blocker to this population decision because the controlling owner rulings and SCA-003 postimage are explicit.
