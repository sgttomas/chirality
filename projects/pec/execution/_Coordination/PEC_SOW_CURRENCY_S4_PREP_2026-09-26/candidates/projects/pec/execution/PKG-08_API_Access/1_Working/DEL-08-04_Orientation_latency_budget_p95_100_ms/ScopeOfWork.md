## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Production proceeds in the order upstream-contract survey → boundary and
vantage declaration → corpus and conditions declaration → harness and stand-in
inventory → measurement runs → budget record → tests. The upstream-contract
survey comes first because the three predecessors on the measured path are at
`INITIALIZED` and supply obligations rather than artifacts, so a harness written against an
imagined entry point, envelope, or socket would violate REQ-006 before any code
existed. The boundary and vantage are declared before any timing is taken
rather than inferred from a finished harness, because REQ-003 makes the
measured act's extent a premise of the measurement: a boundary reverse-engineered
from whatever the harness happened to time would document an accident and
would silently apportion a budget no accepted source apportions. The corpus and
conditions are declared before the runs for the same reason, because a
percentile whose sample was chosen after the numbers were seen is a selection,
not a measurement.

All work is bounded to this deliverable folder, its test workspace within the
`PKG-08` test surface, and the budget record path. This contract authorizes no
register, decomposition, PRD, or upstream-deliverable edit; it neither defines
nor reshapes the return it times, the envelope that carries it, the transport
that serves it, or the corpus it reads. Tests implement the verification
methods below and create no scope or acceptance criteria.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — `OBJ-001` governs this deliverable's reason for existing: orientation for any loop is a sub-second query, not a session-length prose derivation. Every other deliverable mapped to that objective builds the answer; this one is the only place where the claim that the answer arrives fast enough is put to a number. That is why REQ-002 through REQ-005 are stated as obligations on the record rather than as good practice: a latency figure is the most portable and the most easily decontextualized artifact this project will produce, and it will outlive the run that produced it.
- **AX-002** — The bound is stated and is therefore carried, not set. `PEC-API-002` fixes ≤100 ms at p95 in the accepted sources, so this contract's discipline runs opposite to the one its `PKG-03` counterpart needs: there is no numeric gap to route to an owner, and the temptation here is the reverse one — to soften a stated number into a target when a measurement is inconvenient, or to tighten it into an implied service-level promise the sources do not make. REQ-001 forbids both directions, and REQ-009 keeps the disposition of a failing measurement a recorded fact rather than a verdict this deliverable issues.
- **AX-003** — A measurement conditions its own claim, and the conditioning is the part that disappears first. `PEC-K-08` requires that "Every status, verdict, and warning carries rule ID, threshold, and contributing cited sources. Drill-down never dead-ends", and a p95 with no corpus, no boundary, no sample, and no composition state is precisely a verdict whose drill-down dead-ends. REQ-002 through REQ-005 exist so that each recorded figure carries the four things that determine what it is a figure *about*, and the prohibitions on aggregation exist because averaging across conditions is how a conditioned claim becomes an unconditioned one without anyone deciding that it should.
- **AX-004** — Measuring three contracts imposes nothing on any of them. `[E-P51]`, `[E-N12]`, and `[E-P52]` all run *into* this deliverable, and each predecessor's own contract already records this one as the measurer and disclaims the budget in its own voice (CLM-007, CLM-008, CLM-009). CON-003 and CON-004 record gaps in the upstream record and assign work in neither place: a measurement that started specifying how a return should be composed, what an envelope should contain, or how a token should be issued would have left this deliverable's scope in exactly the way the `PKG-10` instrument contract warns against for its own case.
- **AX-005** — A stand-in is not the thing. The measured path does not exist, and the honest form of that fact is not to wait but to time a stand-in and say so. REQ-006 permits the stand-in and forbids the elision, because the failure mode is silent and one-directional: a stand-in number recorded without its label is indistinguishable from a real-surface measurement six months later, and it will be quoted as one. The same reasoning is why the stand-in inventory belongs in the budget record rather than in a test comment.
- **AX-006** — Adjacency on a path is not an allocation of the budget. The serializer sits on the measured path and the socket carries it, and neither fact makes any part of the ≤100 ms theirs to hold or this contract's to assign. Revision 1.6 gives the path a second budget of a different kind: `PEC-API-006`'s response-size budgets belong to `DEL-08-03` under `SOW-098`, their numbers are confirmed at Phase 1, and the register calls them distinct from the latency budget (CLM-008). Supplying either from here — by apportioning milliseconds, by declaring what a compact or budgeted response costs, or by treating a response size as a latency allowance — would answer an upstream question with a number this contract has no source for. This deliverable owns the latency budget; it does not own, and does not define, the properties of the things that spend it.
- **AX-007** — `PEC-K-10` content-minimal governs what a measurement may retain. A latency harness reads a corpus of governed prose and has every incentive to keep payloads around for debugging, which is the one place file content could enter through a deliverable whose outputs are numbers. REQ-011 binds the capture side directly rather than relying on the ingest guard, because the suite's retention is outside any path that guard covers.
- **AX-008** — Read-only means the measured corpus is unchanged by having been measured. `PEC-K-06` observation-not-participation and the graceful-absence posture make PEC's own state deletable; a performance suite that mutated the corpus to make a run reproducible would corrupt the subject of every subsequent measurement and would do so invisibly, since the artifact of the corruption is a faster number. REQ-010 states the boundary as a checkable property with a before-and-after hash rather than as a discipline.

Token cost and wall-clock latency are different metrics over different subjects. Reading a `DECLARED` stratum as a stronger claim on this contract's content than its own text supports would be the same error in the opposite direction from ignoring it.

Provenance is not authority in either direction: the `DECLARED` edge is not thereby a stronger obligation on content, and the three `PROPOSAL` edges are not thereby weaker ones. The one annotation from that set this contract touches at all is the `C-08` standing-node set, cited at CLM-005 and CON-006 with the owner's confirmation of its exclusion and without a cadence conclusion drawn from it.
