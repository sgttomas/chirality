## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

The requirements below state what future production must satisfy. Nothing in
this section asserts that a harness, a gate record or a test exists, or that
any condition has been evaluated.

- **REQ-001** — For each evaluation the harness shall bind exactly one release candidate and the scope it serves, as identified by the release act that invokes it (TBD-002), and shall evaluate, for that candidate and scope, the five conditions the PRD §12 paragraph names (CLM-002), each as a separately named condition: harness parity clean or explained; complete coverage statements under seeded unparseable and stale feeds; the reliance envelope on every response; passing parser fixture suites; and the kill test. It shall add no condition that paragraph does not name and omit none. Where the evidence for a condition covers less than the declared scope, the condition shall be recorded as not passed, naming the uncovered part.
- **REQ-002** — The parity condition shall be evaluated over the parity result that the practitioner-harness parity diff of `DEL-03-04` (`SOW-020`, `PEC-RCN-005`) produced for that candidate and scope. It shall pass only where the result reports no difference, or where every difference it reports carries a recorded DriftFinding disposition explaining it; a difference without a recorded disposition fails the condition. The harness shall check that each disposition is recorded and bound to its difference; it shall judge no explanation, and it shall itself record, dispose of or explain no difference (TBD-005).
- **REQ-003** — The coverage condition shall be evaluated over the coverage-honesty evidence of `DEL-04-05` (`SOW-009`, `PEC-ORI-006`) produced under seeded unparseable feeds and seeded stale feeds for that candidate and scope. It shall pass only where both seeded kinds are present and every seeded case yields an explicit statement of the measurement limitation, with none silently omitted. The gate record shall name the seeded cases evaluated (TBD-006). The harness shall author no seed.
- **REQ-004** — The envelope condition shall be evaluated over evidence, produced for that candidate and scope, that responses carry the reliance envelope of `PEC-ORI-007`, which `DEL-04-03` covers under `SOW-097`: the pin (examined-through SHA), per-feed coverage and freshness with any stated limitation, the trust tier of each claim, and the file-fallback signal whenever PEC is absent, degraded or failing its own checks. It shall pass only where the evidence covers every response surface in the declared scope and shows all four elements on each response it covers; a response surface in scope without evidence, or a response lacking an element, fails the condition (TBD-007).
- **REQ-005** — The parser-fixture condition shall be evaluated over the results, for that candidate, of the fixture suites of every `PKG-02` deliverable that an `ACTIVE` `EXECUTION` row of this deliverable's `Dependencies.csv` names at evaluation time; at `125cfacc1` those are the eight rows `[E-P92]` through `[E-P99]` (CLM-006). It shall pass only where each such suite ran against that candidate and passed. The gate record shall list each suite it composed together with the dependency row that names it. Whether the `DEL-02-07` suite is among them follows that register and is not decided in this contract (CON-002).
- **REQ-006** — The kill-test condition shall be evaluated over the verdict that the kill test of `DEL-10-02` (`SOW-055`, `PEC-K-01`) returned for that exact candidate. It shall pass only on an explicit passing verdict for that candidate; a blocking verdict, a verdict for another candidate, or a kill test that was not executed for that candidate fails the condition.
- **REQ-007** — The harness shall compose evidence and implement none of the behaviours that produce it. It shall obtain each evidence item only through an interface or a recorded result that the producing mechanism exposes (TBD-003), and it shall read no internal state, private module or store of another deliverable, per the envelope note "composes other packages' evidence and consumes no internals (as DEL-10-02)" (CLM-005).
- **REQ-008** — Every evidence item shall be bound to the identity of the exact candidate under evaluation; an item bound to another candidate, or bound to none, counts as absent. A gate record shall bind one candidate only, and no record, passed or not, shall be carried forward as evidence for another candidate or a later release: the gate is evaluated afresh for each release that advertises operational reliance, per the register's "re-proved at each such release" (CLM-005) and the PRD's "The gate is re-proved at each release that advertises operational reliance" (CLM-002).
- **REQ-009** — The gate record shall state for each condition exactly one outcome — passed, not passed, or not evaluated with a stated reason — and one overall verdict, which is passed only when all five conditions passed and is otherwise not passed. Absent evidence, an unimplemented producing mechanism, an unreadable evidence item and a harness error shall each yield not passed or not evaluated, never passed, per the project rule that "absent implementations are unmet gates, never fabricated passes" (CLM-011). No not-passed or not-evaluated outcome shall be presented, summarized or rendered as passed.
- **REQ-010** — The gate record shall carry the candidate identity, the declared scope, for each condition the references to its evidence (the producing deliverable, the path or interface, and the SHA or hash that identifies each evidence item), the outcome and its reason, the overall verdict, and the identity and version of the harness that produced it. It shall be content-minimal per `PEC-K-10` — paths, counts, SHAs, states and hashes, never file or diff content — so that no field of the record can hold source text, diff text or prose copied from the evidence.
- **REQ-011** — Evaluation shall be deterministic: identical evidence items for an identical candidate and scope under an identical harness version shall yield identical per-condition outcomes and an identical verdict.
- **REQ-012** — The gate record shall declare itself evidence for a release act. It shall make no statement that any release advertises operational reliance; it shall not be presented as an advertisement, a release, an acceptance, a ruling, a lifecycle transition or a merge opinion; and it is never citable as authority, per `PEC-K-02` and the `D-PEC-90` concordance (CLM-011). A passed verdict meets the PRD §12 precondition for advertising; it does not itself advertise, and it grants nothing.
- **REQ-013** — The harness shall be invocable by a release act against a named candidate and declared scope without modification to the harness, and shall report explicitly, as a not-evaluated record, any invocation that lacks a candidate identity or a declared scope. It shall cut, tag or publish no release, set or withhold no advertisement of operational reliance, decide nothing about whether a release advertises, and create no release process or pipeline; this contract reads those acts as the human owner's (CLM-010, CON-004).
- **REQ-014** — The harness and its records shall create no consumer duty: no consumer, harness, agent or loop shall be required to query PEC, read a gate record or check a verdict, and no record shall be pushed, injected or scheduled into any consumer, per the PRD §12 sentence "The gate creates no consumer duty" and `PEC-K-03` and `PEC-K-11`. ## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Production proceeds in the order gate-record form → condition definitions over
evidence interfaces → candidate binding and verdict rules → harness assembly →
tests. The record form comes first so that no condition is shaped around
whatever evidence happens to be available, and each condition is written
against the register and PRD text before any evidence interface is chosen.
All work is bounded to this deliverable folder, the `PKG-10` source and the
test paths a ruled packet names; this contract authorizes no register,
decomposition, PRD, upstream-deliverable, release or lifecycle edit, and no v2
source write by itself. Because the upstream deliverables are at `INITIALIZED` at `125cfacc1` (CLM-007)
and their contracts do not yet oblige all of the evidence (CON-003), the
harness's own verification uses synthetic evidence labelled as such (REQ-018). Tests implement the verification methods
below and create no scope.

- **VER-001** — Execute the harness for a named candidate and scope over synthetic evidence for all five conditions; assert that exactly the five named conditions appear in the record, each under its own name; then withhold evidence for part of the declared scope and assert a not-passed outcome naming that part.
- **VER-002** — Execute the parity condition over synthetic parity results with no difference, with differences that all carry recorded dispositions, and with one difference lacking a disposition; assert pass, pass and fail; inspect the harness for any path that writes a difference, finding or disposition or returns a judgement of an explanation.
- **VER-003** — Execute the coverage condition over synthetic seeded-case evidence in which every case yields a limitation, in which one case is silently omitted, and in which one seeded kind is missing; assert pass, fail and fail and that the record names the cases; inspect the harness for any seeding path.
- **VER-004** — Execute the envelope condition over synthetic response evidence covering every in-scope surface with all four elements, over evidence in which one response lacks each element in turn, and over evidence missing one in-scope surface; assert pass, and fail in every other case.
- **VER-005** — Execute the parser-fixture condition over a register fixture with the eight rows and synthetic suite results; assert the composed set and the row listing; make one suite fail and one not run and assert fail; add and retire a row in the register fixture and assert the composed set changes with the harness unchanged.
- **VER-006** — Execute the kill-test condition over synthetic verdicts: passing for the candidate, blocking, passing for another candidate, and not executed; assert pass only for the first.
- **VER-007** — Inspect the harness source and import graph for parity, limitation, envelope, parsing or kill-test logic and for any import of, or read from, another deliverable's internal modules, state or store; trace every evidence input to an exposed interface or a recorded result.
- **VER-008** — Execute the harness with evidence bound to another candidate and with unbound evidence and assert both are treated as absent; present a passed record of one candidate as evidence for another and assert it is refused; run two evaluations and assert two records.
- **VER-009** — Execute over synthetic cases for each failure mode — absent evidence, unimplemented mechanism, unreadable item, harness error — and assert not passed or not evaluated with a reason; assert the verdict rule over all combinations of five condition outcomes; inspect every summary and rendering path for display of a non-passing outcome as passed.
- **VER-010** — Inspect the record schema and a sample record for each REQ-010 field; run a record over evidence carrying distinctive text and assert none of it appears; review every field's type for capacity to hold file content, diff content or prose.
- **VER-011** — Run two evaluations over identical evidence, candidate, scope and harness version and compare the records' outcomes and verdicts.
- **VER-012** — Inspect the record schema and sample records for the evidence-for-a-release-act declaration and search them for advertising statements and claims of authority, acceptance, release, ruling, lifecycle transition or merge opinion.
- **VER-013** — Invoke the harness against a named candidate and scope with no modification; invoke it without a candidate and without a scope and assert not-evaluated records; inspect the source for release, tag, publish, advertisement or pipeline-creation paths.
- **VER-014** — Inspect the harness for any path that requires, pushes, injects or schedules consumption; compare PRD §12 before and after a harness run and inspect the harness for any restatement of a phase exit test; inspect sample records for the distinction between gate verdicts and phase exit verdicts.
- **VER-015** — Inspect the harness's call surface for each excluded act of REQ-015.
- **VER-016** — Inspect the harness for any write to a `Dependencies.csv`, a `_DEPENDENCIES.md` or a `_STATUS.md`, and for any path that records a `C-08` classification.
- **VER-017** — Hash the composed evidence and a governed-file sample before and after a harness run and assert byte-identity; record every write the run performs and assert that each is a gate record at the TBD-004 location.
- **VER-018** — Run the `PKG-10` test suite and confirm that each of VER-001 through VER-017 has a corresponding executing test, that no test asserts a criterion absent from this contract, and that every record produced from synthetic evidence carries the synthetic label and is not presented as a real candidate's gate record.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — `PEC-K-02` files govern. A gate record is evidence about a release candidate; no ruling, acceptance, lifecycle transition, merge or release rests on it as authority (CLM-011), which is why REQ-012 makes the record say so.
- **AX-002** — An instrument is not the behaviour it tests (`DL-6`). Composing evidence keeps each behaviour, and each of its acceptance criteria, with the deliverable that owns it (CLM-009); a gate that re-implemented parity, limitation honesty, the envelope, a parser or the kill test would be testing itself (REQ-007, REQ-015).
- **AX-003** — Absence is an unmet gate. The project rule "absent implementations are unmet gates, never fabricated passes" (CLM-011) is why REQ-009 fails closed and why synthetic evidence never stands in for a real candidate's (REQ-018).
- **AX-004** — Each release proves itself. "Re-proved at each such release" (CLM-005, CLM-002) means no passed record carries forward, which is why REQ-008 binds every item and record to one exact candidate.
- **AX-005** — The gate is necessary, not sufficient. PRD §12 says no release advertises operational reliance until its gates prove the five conditions; it does not say that passing advertises. Whether a release advertises remains a release act this contract does not perform (REQ-012, REQ-013, CON-004).
- **AX-006** — Graceful absence and consumer-owned use. The gate creates no consumer duty (CLM-002); a release that has not passed advertises none, and consumers keep reading the files (`PEC-K-01`, `PEC-K-03`, `PEC-K-11`; REQ-014). Without a passed gate record nothing changes for any consumer except that operational reliance is not available from that release.
- **AX-007** — Neutrality on the `C-08` classification. The question is the owner's and is not answered at `125cfacc1` (CLM-012, CON-001). Writing every requirement so that it holds under either answer keeps this contract true whichever way the owner rules, and keeps the classification out of production choices (REQ-016).
- **AX-008** — The edges cited are `[E-P88]` through `[E-P91]` (`DERIVED`) and `[E-P92]` through `[E-P99]` (`PROPOSAL`), all upstream and all seeded under `D-PEC-101`. Stratum is provenance, not authority. `RequiredMaturity` `INITIALIZED` makes the upstream *contracts* the reliable inputs, which is why CON-003 is recorded.
- **AX-009** — `PEC-K-10` content-minimal. The gate record carries references, outcomes and hashes, never the content of the evidence it composes (REQ-010).
- **AX-010** — Unknowns stay marked. TBD-001 through TBD-007 and CON-001 through CON-004 are recorded rather than resolved by inference.
- **AX-011** — `C-04` `PHASE_PRECEDENCE` and `C-10` `STRATUM_RULE` are register-wide, non-gating constraints recorded in this deliverable's `_DEPENDENCIES.md` Run Notes. Blocker output under `FULL_GRAPH` at threshold `INITIALIZED` is advisory visibility only and never work assignment.
- **AX-012** — This contract is lifecycle-neutral. `_STATUS.md` remains the sole lifecycle authority and is untouched by the run that authored this document; the deliverable is at `OPEN` and nothing has been built.
