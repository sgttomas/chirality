## Direction of record

Operational reliance on PEC data is PEC product direction: an agent, directly
or through its harness, may take a PEC record-tier claim as true as of the
examined-through commit the response carries and act on it without
re-reading the cited source, within the proposal's bounds. Authority is
unchanged: rulings, acceptance, lifecycle transitions, merges and citations
in governed records rest on the file-native record, and PEC-K-01 and PEC-K-02
stand. Reliance begins at a PEC release whose gates prove the conditions the
proposal names, not now.

calls. This steers the later amendment's PRD §8 access text. It changes no
access class now; the change is made by that later amendment.
