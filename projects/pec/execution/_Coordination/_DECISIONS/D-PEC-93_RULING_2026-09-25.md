## Clarifications from the PR #913 review (proposal bytes unchanged)

The independent review of the proposal (PASS, no blocking finding;
`returns/F17_REVIEW_D93_PROPOSAL.md`) reproduced the option-A result exactly
and raised these points. They bind the act as follows and enlarge nothing.

- **Partial failure (N1).** The generator creates the two folders and their
  status and scaffold files before its main writes, and runs the fileset check
  after them, so a failure at those steps can leave untracked files. The act
  therefore runs in an isolated worktree; any failure discards that worktree
  (removing untracked files) and returns to HELP_HUMAN, with nothing merged.
- **Same-day reproduction (N2).** The generator refuses unless the local date
  equals `--act-date`. The act runs on the date it records, and the verifier's
  generator reproduction runs on that same local date against a fresh export of
  the preimage commit. If that is not possible, the verifier compares the
  written files with the act's recorded postimage hashes and the proposal's
  slot rule instead, and says which method it used. No clock or time-zone
  manipulation is used.
- **Rollback (N6).** Before merge, rollback is discarding the act's branch and
  worktree. After merge, rollback is a revert PR of the act (a new commit
  restoring the preimages and removing the created files), as for earlier
  slices; it is not a history reset. This is distinct from SCA-005's
  non-destructive retirement rule, which governs retired deliverables and
  stays in force.
- **Later dependency-extract runs (N5).** The four new EXECUTION rows survive a
  later `dependency-extract` UPDATE only if that deliverable's sources then
  show the edge; this is recorded as a known property, not a defect.
