keep the files current "as a standing duty", and the answer was yes.

## Standing clause (HELP_HUMAN interpretation of the direction)

1. **Who.** The agent acting as HELP_HUMAN for the PEC loop may edit
   `projects/pec/docs/STATUS.md` and `projects/pec/README.md` without a
   further per-tranche clause. The owner answered a question addressed to
   HELP_HUMAN ("Do you want me to keep…"); no other role gains the grant.
2. **What.** Present-current maintenance only: current basis, gates, owner
   decisions made, active undertakings, pointers, and the census they
   summarise. Paragraphs that stop describing the current state are
   labelled historical, not deleted.
3. **Truthfulness.** Neither file may assert an acceptance, ruling, lifecycle
   state or merge that has not occurred on the shared basis, or present a
   proposal as a ruling. Owner acts are cited to their file-native record.
4. **Trace (verification).** Each change is named in that undertaking's loop
   receipt and reviewed with the PR that carries it.
5. **Not granted.** No other `projects/pec` surface, no change to `AGENTS.md`
   fences or F-PEC-1..4, no `v2/**` or decomposition write, and no authority
   derived from either file: both remain derivative, and files of record
   govern where they differ.
6. **Duration.** Standing until the owner revokes or narrows it. Whether it
   carries into a migrated PEC loop is decided at that migration
   (`D-PEC-86` §3 I-7).
7. **Rollback.** Any single change reverts with its PR hunk. The clause itself
   ends on the owner's revocation, after which the files return to the
   per-tranche rule.
