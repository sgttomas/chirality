1. **Who.** The agent acting as HELP_HUMAN for the PEC loop, or the loop's
   closeout stage acting under it, may edit `projects/pec/docs/STATUS.md` and
   `projects/pec/README.md` without a further per-tranche clause.
2. **What.** Present-current maintenance only: current basis, gates, owner
   decisions made, active undertakings, pointers, and the census they
   summarise. Paragraphs that stop describing the current state are
   labelled historical, not deleted.
3. **Truthfulness.** Neither file may assert an acceptance, ruling, lifecycle
   state or merge that has not occurred on the shared basis, or present a
   proposal as a ruling. Owner acts are cited to their file-native record.
4. **Trace.** Each change is named in that undertaking's loop receipt (or
   central receipt after the PEC loop migrates) and reviewed with the PR
   that carries it.
5. **Not granted.** No other `projects/pec` surface, no change to `AGENTS.md`
   fences or F-PEC-1..4, no `v2/**` or decomposition write, and no authority
   derived from either file: both remain derivative, and files of record

6. **Duration.** Standing until the owner revokes or narrows it. It survives
   the PEC loop's later migration to the shared development-loop method.
