## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Production proceeds in the order upstream-contract survey → limitation-source
and trigger map → rendering path → non-omission pressure cases → provenance
carry-through → tests, because the trigger map is the acceptance surface of
everything after it and because the trigger set itself diverges between this
deliverable's scope item and its predecessors' obligations (CON-004). The
upstream-contract survey comes first because all three predecessors are at
`INITIALIZED` and supply obligations rather than artifacts, so a rendering path
written against an imagined limitation record or an assumed aggregate surface
would violate REQ-004 before any code existed. The non-omission pressure cases
are built before provenance carry-through rather than after it, because a
component exercised only over small fixtures acquires caps, thresholds, and
compaction behaviours that REQ-003 forbids, and those are cheapest to exclude
before the shape sets. All work is bounded to this deliverable folder and the
`PKG-04` service-core source it names; this contract authorizes no register,
decomposition, PRD, or upstream-deliverable edit, and it neither defines nor
reshapes the parsers that report limits, the record tier that holds them, the
return they qualify, the stamp they accompany, the transport that serializes
them, or the surfaces that render or measure them. Tests implement the

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — `OBJ-001` governs: orientation for any loop is a sub-second query with per-claim citations, not a session-length prose derivation. A query is only worth substituting for a derivation if what it does not know is visible in the answer. This deliverable is the one place in `PKG-04` whose entire content is that visibility, which is why every requirement here is about what may not be left out rather than about what is produced.
- **AX-002** — `PEC-ORI-006` is the whole of this deliverable's normative source, and its second clause is the operative one. That clause, "silent omission is prohibited", is a prohibition on a behaviour that is invisible by construction: a response missing a limitation looks exactly like a response that had none. That asymmetry is why REQ-001 and REQ-003 are stated as checkable non-omission obligations under adversarial pressure rather than as a disposition to be honest.
- **AX-003** — `PRD.md` §7.3 carries coverage honesty forward as pattern, not code — "a figure the records don't support is absent and said to be absent" — and the ledger's `Notes` cell records that lineage. This contract treats §7.3 as the posture behind `PEC-ORI-006` and derives no obligation from it that `PEC-ORI-006` does not state, because §7.3's own framing is machinery carried as pattern rather than as a requirement (CLM-003).
- **AX-004** — `PEC-K-04` staleness-is-a-comparison is why REQ-004 forbids this component from deriving a staleness verdict. Staleness is a structural comparison the consumer performs against what the stamp carries; a staleness judgment originated in the reporting layer would look identical to a reported one and would mean something entirely different — an opinion presented in the slot reserved for a fact.
- **AX-005** — `PEC-K-01` graceful absence cuts inward here as it does upstream. PEC exists to be deletable, so the ordinary case includes a record tier that has never been reconciled and feeds that were never read. A limitation reporter that only worked when the inventory was complete would fail exactly in the case that most needs it, which is why REQ-008 applies coverage honesty to this component's own coverage rather than only to the feeds it reports on.
- **AX-006** — `PEC-K-10` content-minimal binds this deliverable at a tempting seam. A statement that a feed was unparseable is more useful with the offending text attached, and `PEC-K-10` is unconditional — "never file or diff content". REQ-006 states the boundary as a checkable property of every rendered statement rather than as a discipline, because the helpful excerpt is precisely the thing an implementer would add.
- **AX-007** — Compactness never buys silence. `PEC-API-004` states "Responses are compact, machine-first, and citation-bearing", and the scope item carrying it, `SOW-043`, is `DEL-08-03`'s (CLM-014, CLM-016); the two obligations meet in the same payload, and only one of them is a prohibition.
