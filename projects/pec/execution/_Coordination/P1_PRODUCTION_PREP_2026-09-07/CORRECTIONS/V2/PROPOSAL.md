DEL-01-03 CON-001 leaves extraction of state tokens from prose unresolved. The proposed guard accepts only explicitly typed, source-cited field classes and enumerated known states; it must not authorize free-form strings merely because their key is `state`, `path` or `metadata`, nor accept caller-defined permissive policy as a bypass.
