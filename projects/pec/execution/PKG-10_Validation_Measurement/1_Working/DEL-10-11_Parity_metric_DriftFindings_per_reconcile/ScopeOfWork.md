## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Production proceeds in the order input-source declaration → inclusion rule →
denominator → reporting period and series shape → figure derivation with
state binding → limitation carry-through → explainability wiring, because the
counting rule is what every figure is checked against, and because a count
written before the inclusion rule and denominator are declared would settle
CON-001 and CON-002 silently in the derivation. The parity findings are read,
never produced, and the practitioner harness is not touched at all (CLM-015,
REQ-003). All work is bounded to this deliverable's artifact and the surfaces it
reads; this contract authorizes no register, decomposition, PRD, harness, or
upstream-deliverable edit. The verification methods below are stated as
inspections and exercises over fixture inputs, because at `INITIALIZED` no measured
population exists; tests, where production writes them, implement these methods
and create no scope or acceptance criteria.

- **VER-001** — Known-population exercise: derive the report over a fixture corpus of reconciles whose parity-finding population is known by construction; assert one count per reconcile equal to the known population; include a fixture reconcile whose findings are withheld and assert no figure appears for it; and inspect every figure in the output for an estimation, extrapolation, or imputation step, asserting none.
- **VER-002** — Counting-rule inspection: read the declared counting rule as an artifact and assert it states the input source, the inclusion rule, the denominator, the reporting period and series shape, and each adopted operational reading; then re-derive each figure by hand from the stated rule and assert equality, and attempt to produce a figure under a rule absent from the record and assert refusal.
- **VER-003** — Boundary inspection: inspect this deliverable's source, procedure, and call surface for parity-comparison, reconcile, successive-snapshot drift-classification, harness read/invoke/configure, DriftFinding mutation, and sibling-metric computation paths, asserting each absent; and hash the fixture finding population before and after a derivation run, asserting byte-identity.
- **VER-004** — Read-only boundary exercise: hash the fixture source tree, governed files, registers, lifecycle files, and stored findings before and after a full derivation and assert byte-identity; capture every filesystem write performed during the run and assert each resolves to the report's declared output location.
- **VER-005** — State-binding exercise: derive over a fixture corpus in which each reconcile carries a distinct examined-through SHA and one carries none; assert the SHA appears on every figure, that the SHA-less case yields a stated limitation and no count, and that no figure claims a state later than the one it counted.
- **VER-006** — Limitation carry-through: derive over fixture cases in which the input is unavailable, incomplete or not retained, unreadable for one reconcile, indistinguishable between producers under the declared inclusion rule, and accompanied by upstream-stated coverage limitations; assert per case that the affected reconcile and the reason are named on the report and that no such case reports a complete count.
- **VER-007** — Explainability inspection: for every figure produced over the fixture set, assert the presence of its derivation rule, its inclusion rule, its denominator, and its contributing cited sources, and follow each citation through to the counted findings and reconciles, asserting no dead end.
- **VER-008** — Content-minimal inspection: derive over a content-dense fixture population and dump every field of the report, asserting field by field that none carries file or diff content.
- **VER-009** — Determinism: derive twice over the same unchanged finding population, the same unchanged reconcile set, and the same declared counting rule, and assert identical figures and identical stated limitations.
- **VER-010** — Non-authority inspection: search the report and its derivation surface for thresholds, targets, acceptable ranges, pass/fail or gate verdicts, falsification determinations, and adoption/ruling/direction/disposition records, asserting each absent; and assert the report carries its own non-authority statement.
- **VER-011** — Upstream-binding inspection: review this contract, the source, and the fixtures for any assertion that an upstream parity tool, finding, comparison, or output exists, asserting none; assert every input reference resolves to an obligation stated in the upstream accepted contract; and review for any requested field, schema change, or act imposed on `DEL-03-04`, asserting none.
- **VER-012** — Dependency, egress, and absence exercise: inspect any service-core component's dependency manifest and import graph for third-party runtime dependencies and external network calls, asserting none; run a fixture reconcile and a representative governed workflow with this report absent, unproduced, and unread, asserting each completes unblocked; and re-run the `DEL-01-05` zero-dependency and locality enforcement once that deliverable is available, without discharging it here.
- **VER-013** — Unsettled-reading inspection: cross-check every operational reading appearing in the derivation against the declared counting rule, asserting each is declared and marked unsettled with its open record named; and search this deliverable's artifacts for any statement resolving the input-selection rule, the denominator, the metric's meaning or arming, its gating role, the measured population's bounds, or the input's retention, asserting none.
