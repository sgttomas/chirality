## Separate bounded lead — four identities grouped at each position

**Ordering premise is valid.** `modelIndex.ts` adds nodes in `model.nodes` order; `viewportSelection.ts` walks `index.treeOrder` for box hits; `selectionState.ts` preserves those hits and makes the final hit primary. PipeViewport passes the ordered keys to labelPolicy, which attempts primary/hover/current-row before the remaining selected keys. Thus adjacent records per group genuinely change greedy occupancy order compared with separate A/B populations. A deterministic ID spelling by itself is insufficient: supply actual adjacent model-array records, and retain the observed ordered selection. The final group's last identity still moves to the front as primary; a later pointer hover must not silently alter the claimed order.

**Single-group capacity is also valid, within its declared geometry.** At the middle of the previously measured603×679 canvas, four real co-located Node identities with measured58×32 labels and the existing small node pick box can occupy the four second-ring diagonal positions. Their centers relative to the anchor are `(95,-56)`, `(95,56)`, `(-95,-56)`, `(-95,56)`. Their rectangles do not overlap one another or the own-node pick envelope. First-ring positions remain blocked by that envelope. This is independent arithmetic for an isolated group, not a browser observation or a general four-label guarantee near other geometry/edges.
