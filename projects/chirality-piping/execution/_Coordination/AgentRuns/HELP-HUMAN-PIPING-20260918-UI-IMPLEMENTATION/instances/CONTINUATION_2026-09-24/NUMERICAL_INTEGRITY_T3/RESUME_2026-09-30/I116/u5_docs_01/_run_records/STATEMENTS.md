**Why "contract", not "profile".** U3's T2 text says "pressure is solved only on the exact straight-pressure profile". Two product profiles solve exact-contract pressure: `exact_straight_pressure_v2` (model 0.3.0) and `resolved_straight_load_state_v1` (model 0.4.0, which "reuses this exact straight route unchanged", `pressure_runtime.rs` `is_exact`). "Contract" is true of both. The product text is not wrong; it reads "profile" loosely.

**Not stated, on purpose.** The documents do not describe what the exact contract computes: wall and effective axial force, axial membrane and Lamé surface stresses, closed-end cap transfer, straight members only. That standing is the contract's own, and the accepted records I found (`HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260914-RESULT-COMPATIBILITY-PRESSURE/instances/ROOT/FINAL_ACCEPTANCE_V1.md`, the private kernel; `CONTINUATION_2026-09-24/ENGINE_INTEGRATION/RETURN.md`, the runtime join in #905) each disclaim engineering acceptance. T4's planned capability is not anticipated.
