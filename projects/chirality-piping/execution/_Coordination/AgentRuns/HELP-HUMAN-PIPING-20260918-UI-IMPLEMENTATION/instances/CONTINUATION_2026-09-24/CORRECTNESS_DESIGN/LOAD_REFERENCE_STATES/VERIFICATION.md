# Independent examples and negative controls

`verify_reference_states.py` is a standard-library independent analytical calculation. It imports no product, fixture generator or existing solver. Its output is recorded in `_run_records/analytical_examples.json`. It checks the proposed mathematical contract, not any production implementation. All engineering numbers below are invented test inputs, not library records. Decimal/Fraction examples preserve exact rational comparisons; the logarithmic expansion control uses Decimal.exp. Pi appears symbolically where appropriate.

1. **Boundary coupling:** two axial bars in series, each 1 m, area 0.001 m², E respectively 200 and 100 GPa. Root UX=0.0001 m, far end UX=0, transverse modes suppressed. Middle UX=1/15000 m; root/far-end support forces ±20000/3 N. Omitting K_fc u_c returns zero middle motion and fails the original free equation. Repeat node/member permutation, both modes, unit conversion and an all-prescribed single-bar case. This extends existing numerical R04; preserve that case's original oracle too.
2. **Prescribed rotation:** Euler–Bernoulli beam length 2 m, EI=2000 N*m². Root RZ=0.001 rad, all other root/end DOFs prescribed zero: support-on-member [Fy_i,Mz_i,Fy_j,Mz_j]=[3,4,-3,2] in N/N*m. Check total force and moment about the root. With a free tip instead, root rigid rotation gives tip UY=L*theta, tip RZ=theta and zero internal actions to first order. This distinguishes actual rotation from a translated force or incorrectly length-normalized angle.
3. **Same material, different element temperatures:** two parallel axial bars, same user material ID, same 1 m length/0.001 m² area, cold E=200 GPa and zero thermal strain, hot E=100 GPa and explicitly prescribed strain 0.001. A common unloaded free end moves 1/3000 m; hot/cold forces are -200000/3 and +200000/3 N. A material-ID-only override produces the wrong movement even if a residual check passes its wrongly assembled matrix. Block a mixed-basis result superposition as a new equilibrium state; a direct resolved physical solve remains valid.
4. **Thermal datum conversion:** T_m=20°C, T_install=50°C, T=150°C, alpha_sec at installation 12e-6/K and at operating 16e-6/K. Actual free extension ratio is `(1+16e-6*130)/(1+12e-6*30)-1 = 43/25009`; alpha_hot*(150-50)=0.0016 is a deliberately wrong control. Equivalent degC/K absolute and temperature-interval inputs give identical normalized mechanics. With a constant-alpha interval and fixed explicit material, alpha=1e-5/K, deltaT=80K gives exactly 0.0008 without requiring an ambient temperature.
5. **Coefficient-definition discrimination:** over 100 K, coefficient changes linearly from 10e-6 to 20e-6. Integral per datum length gives dilation 0.0015; current-length logarithmic derivative gives exp(0.0015)-1. Evaluating final alpha times the interval gives 0.002 and must fail either reference. Check reversed interval, split interval composition, exact tabulated endpoints, missing bracket, duplicate T, datum outside coverage and nonpositive free length. Test malformed units and unknown expansion definitions at typed boundaries.
6. **Cold-spring sign, composition and reversibility:** L=10 m, cut short 0.002 m, area0.001 m², cold E=200 GPa, installation T20°C. Both ends fixed gives N=+40000 N cold. With mean constant engineering alpha=1e-5/K referred to20°C, hot T100°C and E150 GPa, epsilon_star=0.00059984 and N=-89976 N. Returning to the cold state recovers +40000 N without accumulating a second cut. Releasing axial far end yields u=-0.002 m at installation and zero wall force. Opposite sign gives cut-long compression; c>=L is invalid. Mutation controls flip the cut sign, apply mismatch as both eigenstrain and anchor movement, apply it twice, or incorrectly retain cold E for the hot case. Compare no-fit baseline and explicit strain-based fit route under its declared approximation; do not label these exact finite-strain rod results.
7. **Hydrotest pressure + weight:** 2 m horizontal cantilever, OD0.1 m, wall0.01 m, explicit pipe density7000 kg/m³, full test-fluid density900 kg/m³, gravity -10 m/s² in Y, no insulation, uniform p2 MPa, transferring end closures with one axial datum. Mass/length=(6.3+1.44)pi kg/m; q=-77.4pi N/m. Root Fy=+154.8pi N and Mz=+154.8pi N*m, pressure wall tension=3200pi N and pressure-only root axial action0. Pressure extension/stress follows the existing independently checked pressure oracle with explicit E/nu. Change fluid to100 kg/m³: q=-64.6pi, so retaining the old generated q must be stale. Check refresh stable IDs/manual data, failed queued refresh after state edit, explicit manual preservation, omitted insulation versus zero-density insulation and pressure-only edit not invalidating an unconsumed mass input.
8. **Locked support replaces the device:** simple one-DOF elastic structure stiffness1000 N/m, spring hanger500 N/m, selected upward reference force100 N, external load -1000 N. Active spring gives u=-0.6m, hanger+400 N and structure support+600 N. Lock at0 replaces the hanger action: displacement0, total locked-support assembly action+1000 N, structure support0. No extra+100 N is added to that total. If the spring retains +100 N internally at u=0, the locking pin carries +900 N; the equivalent rigid support alone does not resolve this partition. Lock at a bound nonzero predecessor uses that exact value, not zero. Release returns the original device definition, and inactive state removes its action. These values are intentionally large algebraic witnesses, not a small-deflection piping suitability example; build a scaled native counterpart.
9. **Persistent preload once:** structure k1000, device force+100, weight -1000 and an independent external action+200. Physical combined RHS=-700, u=-0.7. Separately solved W total=-900 and additional-action total=+300 sum to -600 (u=-0.6), wrongly duplicating the+100. The resolved source ledger must expose the distinction. Two independently identified real forces with equal numbers both remain; labels or numeric equality never deduplicate them. Signed total-state subtraction can cancel a shared linear preload without becoming a new equilibrium state.
10. **History negative control:** an elastic tangential spring k1000 N/m in series with an ideal friction slider of limit1 N, driven support displacement0→0.003→0. At the peak slip is0.002 m and force+1 N; on reversal at final0 the slip is0.001 m and force-1 N. Direct virgin equilibrium at final0 has force0. This independently derived elementary return-map example proves that the final load/position and contact label alone cannot reconstruct history. It does not qualify the current Piping friction law. Reject absent/incompatible predecessor internal state for a route claiming this behavior; conserve trial versus committed state on interruption.

11. **Hydrostatic load path and local force recovery:** [HYDROSTATIC_CONTROL.md](HYDROSTATIC_CONTROL.md) independently derives a vertical closed column. Actual cap forces alone have net downward fluid weight. Adding the full axial contents weight again doubles the reaction; using uniform top pressure plus distributed weight can pass total balance while giving wrong wall-force distribution and extension. Choosing uniform average pressure can also match total extension while retaining the wrong station wall forces. Check bottom/middle/top wall and effective actions, not just one global total. Until the pressure-field adapter owns this formulation, the full-fluid uniform-pressure route must reject nonzero axial gravity projection.
