# T4-I1: the exact pressure contract and its extension surface

**Purpose.** T4's plan must extend pressure, now analysable only under `2.0.0/exact_straight_pressure_v2` (straight pipe, linear supports), to bends, joints and fittings, element by element. The plan needs an exact map of what the contract computes today, where it refuses, and what a first end-to-end extension (pressure on one curved bend) touches.

**Questions.**
1. **What the contract computes.** The authored input it reads (contract id, pressure primitive, material E/ν, wall and mill-tolerance data, any corrosion allowance), where it is validated, and the exact composition in PP (`pressure_runtime.rs`, `pressure_exact.rs` and `pressure_exact/`, `pressure_sum.rs`, `pressure_material.rs`, `annulus_geometry.rs`, `membrane_publication_range.rs`): which pressure effects enter the solve (closed-end thrust, Poisson/Bourdon axial strain, anything else), in what form (nodal loads, initial strain, element load vector), in what arithmetic, and which stresses are published (hoop, longitudinal membrane, others), with their result fields and identities.
2. **Every refusal** the exact contract makes: code, site, trigger (bends and curved elements, joints, fittings/components, nonlinear and constant-effort supports, combinations, `equivalent_static`, missing E/ν, the zero primitive, anything else). For each, what the narrowest admitting change would touch.
3. **Routes and readers.** Which routes accept the contract (ordinary `preview-physics-1`, `physics-1`, T3's retained precision B3b, the headless runner, the desktop) and how the pressure outputs reach the Rust, Python and TypeScript readers and the desktop result views. What T3 expects from T4 for retained-precision widening ("element by element", T3-F2b), as far as the code and T3's records state it.
4. **Load states (T1).** How pressure relates to T1's load and reference states (`load-reference-1`, `load-reference-source-1`): is pressure per case or per state; where hydrotest pressure is refused (M21).
5. **The first path.** A concrete touch-point list (file, function, what changes) for admitting pressure on a single curved bend under the exact contract, from authored input through validation, solve, published outputs, readers and desktop display. Flag anything that looks like a premise likely to invalidate later work.
