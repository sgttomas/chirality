# Independent near-incompressible two-span append

ROOT requested this connected control before grouped repair tests. It is additional reference evidence; existing six-test source and all earlier freezes are unchanged at this point.

Use exactly representable ri=1/16 m in both members. Member 1 has OD=5/32 m, wall=1/64 m, E=200 GPa, L=3 m, and nu bits 3fdfffffffffffff. Member 2 has OD=3/16 m, wall=1/32 m, E=100 GPa, L=3 m, and nu bits 3fdffffffffffffe. Both are admissible and immediately below .5. Their (1-2nu) values are exactly 2^-53 and 2^-52.

The source annular areas are 9*pi/4096 and 5*pi/1024 m². With p=2 MPa, common P=(15625/2)*pi N. Root is anchored, the far end is free, both physical closures transfer and no thermal/mechanical loads are applied. Each span therefore has Nw=P, S=0 and extension (1-2nu_i)*P*Li/(Ei*Asi).

Independent Fraction algebra gives:

    d1 = 1/(18750*2^53) m
    d2 = 3/(62500*2^52) m
    u_middle=d1; u_tip=d1+d2; d2/d1=9/5.

The intended global axial RHS is [-(1-2nu1)P, 2(nu2-nu1)P, (1-2nu2)P]. The interior term is small and nonzero. Stabilizing only terminal cap/eigen cancellation is insufficient if the interior near-equal Poisson terms are still subtracted after rounding.

The two displacement increments are comparable, so recovering d2 from u_tip-u_middle does not subtract an increment of scale1e-20 from a displacement of scale1e-10. This deliberately isolates assembly coefficients and member-specific nu. Member reversal and region-terminal order reversal preserve physical displacements and tension-positive wall/effective forces. Both solver modes are required.
