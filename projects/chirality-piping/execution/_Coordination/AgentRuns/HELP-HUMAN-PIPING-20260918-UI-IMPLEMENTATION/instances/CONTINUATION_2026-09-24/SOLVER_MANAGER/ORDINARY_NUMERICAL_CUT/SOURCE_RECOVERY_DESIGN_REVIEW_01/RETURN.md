## Independent checks and conclusions

1. **Direct source solve and affine recovery are correct (§§2–3).** Subtracting every retained `K_fc*u_c` term gives `h`. For each symmetric block `[[a,b],[b,c]]`, `a>0` and `Δ=ac−b²>0` prove positive definiteness and give `u1=(c*h1−b*h2)/Δ`, `u2=(a*h2−b*h1)/Δ`. An affine row has prescribed offset `β+Σ_c ℓ_c*u_c`, and each free block contributes its combined numerator over that block's determinant. Combining different block ratios exactly includes every participating block. Wholly prescribed systems still have meaningful forces/reactions. No projection of `u` is mathematically required before evaluating a force. No inverse certificate is required by these identities.

2. **Device action and ideal reaction remain separate (§3).** With spring stiffness included in `K` and reference-movement loading included in `f`, `K*u−f` is the ideal-constraint action and is exactly zero on each original free row. A particular ground spring instead exerts `−s*k*(sᵀu−g)`. Multiple devices on one DOF retain distinct identities and actions. A spring on a prescribed DOF is not subsumed into its ideal reaction. The proposed signed rotational device output is necessary: current product support resultants are translational vectors and cannot observe the N05 root torque.

3. **All member and station terms are required (§§3,5,7).** The 12 node-on-element components are `K_L*T*E*u−f_equiv,L`, with existing thermal/pressure corrections included once in the affine offset. Nodal point loading is not automatically a member fixed-end load. Current straight recovery first subtracts distributed equivalents, then applies axial-effect corrections; the design accurately identifies that sequence. Current section recovery combines end shear with distance and load lever moments, then negates all six components into the product's j-side convention. Thus station rows and endpoint section cuts must compose the unprojected force functionals, including the shear-times-distance terms even in the nodal-only tranche. Feeding projected end forces into the old station path would not fulfill the design.

4. **Transform and coefficient scope are sound (§2).** Exact algebra over a retained local `K_L,T` recipe does not automatically equal separately rounded global stiffness. The required source comparison closes that gap. An actual signed-permutation transform with matching coefficients is sufficient; testing the computed source matrices avoids coordinate or fixture exceptions. Physical rotation tests must use the actual proper orientation and its corresponding translation/rotation blocks. Generic DOF sign/permutation tests alone do not validate a physical orientation convention. Unsupported arbitrary rotations or an exact nonzero connected free block larger than two must reject. Tiny couplings are edges; a zero load does not license dropping their DOFs.

5. **N06 must be reachable independently of ordinary success (§5).** For `[[a+k,−a],[−a,a]]`, `Δ=a*k>0` yields root `T/k`, tip `T/k+T/a`, member end actions `−T,+T`, and spring action `−T`. Rounded assembly can have determinant zero while this source system is positive. A route reached only after successful `assembly.solve(...)?` cannot implement N06 recovery. The design explicitly requires restructuring that selection while preserving the ordinary rejection as an ordinary-attempt fact. Both requested modes may select the same stronger source method; this does not prove successful dense/sparse factors or their parity.

6. **Positivity, projection and resource boundaries are appropriately separate (§§2,4).** Exact source minors establish bounded represented-system positivity, while each final ratio needs its own unchanged relative `1e−9` projection check. Exact zero must be proved from the ratio; a small projected value or interval crossing zero is insufficient. Existing helper limits and arithmetic hypotheses remain relevant, including finite nearest-rounding/FMA/gradual-underflow and explicit range rejection. RET-01's sizing/comparison lesson applies to recovery descriptors, dynamic strings and replay. A shared decreasing parent budget is required because the existing per-call projection caps are not a whole-attempt bound. The feasibility of completing the required product cases within those limits still needs execution evidence.
