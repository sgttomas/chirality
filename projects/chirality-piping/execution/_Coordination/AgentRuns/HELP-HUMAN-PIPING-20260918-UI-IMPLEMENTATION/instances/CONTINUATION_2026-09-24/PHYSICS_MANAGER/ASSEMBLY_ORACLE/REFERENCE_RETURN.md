## Independent derivation

Solve radial traction with sigma_r=C-B/r², sigma_h=C+B/r², sigma_r(ri)=-p and sigma_r(ro)=0. It gives C=p*ri²/(ro²-ri²)=P/As and sigma_r+sigma_h=2P/As. Isotropic axial compliance then yields

    Nw=E*As*(epsilon-alpha*DeltaT)+2*nu*P
    S=Nw-P; P=p*pi*ri².

For a local member, q_wall=K*d-f_mechanical_equivalent-f_eigen, with f_eigen=[-EAs*epsilon0,+EAs*epsilon0], epsilon0=alpha*DeltaT-2nuP/EAs. Physical terminal cap loads belong to the applied ledger and must not be subtracted from q_wall again. Support reactions require subtraction of the actual cap load; they cannot be inferred from S alone.
