# Dialogue-Centred Concept Rubric

> **NON_AUTHORITATIVE — CONCEPT EVIDENCE**

| Criterion | Question | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| Dialogue centrality | Does the real human–agent dialogue remain the obvious, durable centre of work? | 20 |
| Artifact collaboration | Can artifacts be introduced, inspected, compared, revised, and returned without severing the conversation? | 20 |
| Context legibility | Can the user see exactly which project/artifact references are available to the agent without inventing a shared-intent object? | 10 |
| Supervisory visibility | Can current status, evidence, and—where proposed—recorded parent–child hierarchy be understood without a graph editor? | 10 |
| Runtime truthfulness | Does every represented state derive from existing canonical records/events? | 10 |
| Task continuity | Can the six accepted journeys proceed without repeated context loss or turn unmounting? | 10 |
| Professional calm | Does the interface avoid feed spectacle, command-centre density, and agent-taxonomy-first navigation? | 5 |
| Accessibility | Are dialogue, artifacts, hierarchy, focus modes, resizers, and activity operable without pointer or spatial inference? | 10 |
| Migration economy | Can existing routes, APIs, SSE, providers, drafts, and state migrate safely? | 5 |

## Disqualifiers

- Dialogue becomes a secondary drawer, dock, or route.
- The interface claims a canonical “shared intent” field.
- Selecting an artifact silently transmits its contents to an agent.
- Agent hierarchy includes inferred or editable relationships.
- A focused artifact unmounts or loses an active turn.
- The concept requires runtime/API expansion.
- Existing compatibility or authority boundaries are broken.

## Common requirements

- Centre the existing dialogue, composer, permission requests, streaming state,
  and interruption control.
- Make artifact inclusion explicit and visible.
- Keep raw operational detail subordinate to dialogue and work products.
- Treat local-model intent summarization, if ever explored, as optional,
  rebuildable, and non-authoritative.
- Preserve a separate route to deeper Agent Room supervision.
