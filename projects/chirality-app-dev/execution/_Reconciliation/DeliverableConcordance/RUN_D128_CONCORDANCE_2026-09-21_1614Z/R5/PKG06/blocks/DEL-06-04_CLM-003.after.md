### CLM-003 — Attributes

Write/edit operations must respect their authorized targets, applicable denials, governed instruction-root boundary and protected domain content. Exact edit preconditions and safe provenance remain required. The retained path-control contract includes outside-root and symlink-write rejection; its implementation in legacy hooks is not evidence that the Codex path enforces those checks.

D-GOV-43 item 4 makes the actual host sandbox a user choice. Full access does not authorize otherwise forbidden writes. No sandbox label alone proves protection of an in-project instruction file, domain path or symlink target. Under D-APP-131 P-13, record the selected policy and preserve unverified live enforcement as a gap.

Verification hooks: `frontend/src/__tests__/lib/chirality-hooks.test.ts` for retained containment, symlink and exact-edit checks; `projects/chirality-runtime/tests/codex-supervisor.test.ts` for policy transport. The latter is not a path-denial proof.
