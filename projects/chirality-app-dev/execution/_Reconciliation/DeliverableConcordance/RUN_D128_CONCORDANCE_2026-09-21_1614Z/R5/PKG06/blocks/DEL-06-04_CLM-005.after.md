### CLM-005 — Construction

A governed write/edit must satisfy its applicable permission and target constraints before mutation, reject stale exact-edit preconditions, preserve safe provenance and avoid partial replacement where atomic writing is practical. Verification must distinguish outside-root, instruction-root, protected-domain and symlink targets, missing files, stale edits and allowed writes; MCP transport cannot supply an exception to the same obligations.

Legacy helper paths, SDK hook phases, diff algorithms and event field shapes are implementation evidence. The named checks are `frontend/src/__tests__/lib/chirality-hooks.test.ts` (denials and exact edits) and `frontend/src/__tests__/lib/atomic-write.test.ts` (replacement and failure cleanup). Live policy transmission is checked separately in `projects/chirality-runtime/tests/codex-supervisor.test.ts`. D-GOV-43 retires the fixed-policy mechanism; D-APP-131 retains unmet stable controls and requires truthful disclosure of host-conditioned enforcement. These references do not establish a live path-control pass.
