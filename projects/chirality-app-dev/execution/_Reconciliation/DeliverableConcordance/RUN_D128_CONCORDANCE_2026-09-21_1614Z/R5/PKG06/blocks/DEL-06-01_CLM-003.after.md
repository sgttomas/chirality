### CLM-003 — Attributes

Permission policy must identify the session, turn where applicable, requested action, active host policy, decision, reason and source. Applicable denials and human gates must not be bypassed by a tool being available or named in an allow list. Prompt text is not technical enforcement.

On the live Codex path, the user selects Codex approval and sandbox policy, including an explicit Full access choice; the App must display and record that policy (D-GOV-43 items 4 and 9; D-APP-131). Full access changes host capability, not governed write authority or human accountability. The former SDK mode names, callback vocabulary and overlay record fields remain compatibility evidence, not proof that the live path runs that overlay.

Verification hooks: `projects/chirality-runtime/tests/app-owned-composition.test.ts` (declined approval and permission evidence), `projects/chirality-runtime/tests/codex-supervisor.test.ts` (selected policy and server-request responses), and the legacy `frontend/src/__tests__/lib/permission-overlay.test.ts` for its retained module contract. These subjects are distinct; missing live enforcement or evidence remains open.
