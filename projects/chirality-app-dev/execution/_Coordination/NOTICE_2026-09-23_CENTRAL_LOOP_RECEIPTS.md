The owner clarified that recoverable central run evidence for an App/Piping
development undertaking is one loop receipt, kept at
`execution/_Coordination/AgentRuns/<RunID>/RECEIPT.md`. Its concise account of
the result, checks, limits and source pointers supplies the final PR message;
affected deliverable `MEMORY.md` entries link that receipt. The work graph
continues to carry execution. Decisions, Task Management dispositions and raw
evidence stay at their owning sources.

prospectively when integrated. They replace the contrary prospective statement
in the preserved 2026-09-22 local-work-graph notice; they do not rewrite old
receipts, backfill closed undertakings or change a live graph's scope. The owner
retains authorship of the manual/guide update. PEC's receipt and work-selection
arrangement is outside this tranche.
