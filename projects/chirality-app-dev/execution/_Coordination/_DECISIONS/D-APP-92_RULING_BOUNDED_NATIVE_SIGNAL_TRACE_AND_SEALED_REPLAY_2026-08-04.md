## Selected semantics

Option A is selected exactly as the packet proposes. It authorizes one
diagnostic-only tranche in an interactive macOS GUI session against isolated
runtime state. The tranche reconstructs the exact source-aligned
uninstrumented helper candidate, binds its source/package/identity/topology/
dependency evidence, seals the replay inputs and timing sequence, and uses
out-of-process native/Electron signal tracing without changing App product
bytes or adding callback logging.

The diagnostic evidence must bind exact PIDs and time and distinguish, where
the selected tracing capability can do so, OS signal delivery, libuv/JS
callback entry, Electron `before-quit`, teardown entry, and Root-stop
call/settlement. Raw evidence and limitations are frozen; generated/runtime
residue is removed; every product/config/test byte is restored.
