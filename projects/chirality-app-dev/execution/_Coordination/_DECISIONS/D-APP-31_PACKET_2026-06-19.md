This packet asks how OPERATIVE (Type-2) matrix cells and deliverable rows behave on the loop-first portal, given Type-2 cannot boot at `/chat` and D-APP-27 ruled no direct-chat brief. It blocks the OPERATIVE-cell behaviour in tranche 28c. It does not reopen D-APP-27 or relax subagent governance.

## Decision Question

On the loop-first portal, how do OPERATIVE (Type-2) matrix cells and deliverable rows behave — keep a route hop to the retained `/pipeline`, open the `/pipeline` form as an in-place tertiary form, or something else?

## Grounding (current state)

- OPERATIVE cells target `/pipeline?category=…` (Type-2 task work) and deliverable rows target `/pipeline?category=TASK&scopeKey=…` (`src/components/portal/agent-matrix.tsx:104`; targets in `src/lib/portal/agent-matrix-cells.ts`). OPERATIVE cells are marked `*` (operative, route to `/pipeline` only — never `/workbench`).
- Type-2 cannot boot as a top-level chat persona: `assertDirectChatPersona` (`src/lib/harness/agent-roster.ts:98`) rejects a Type-2 at session-create when the section is `CHAT` (D-APP-24).
- D-APP-27 RULED Option C — Type-2 remain excluded from direct chat and NO new INIT-TASK / task-init / brief contract is introduced.
- `/pipeline` (`src/app/pipeline/pipeline-client.tsx`) is the existing governed OPERATIVE task-launch surface; it stays — tranche 28d demotes it to a sidebar-reachable tertiary form (NOT deleted).

## Options

| Option | Ruling | What it permits | What remains denied |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Keep a route hop to the retained `/pipeline` (interim default) | OPERATIVE/deliverable cells keep navigating to the still-present `/pipeline` route; nothing deleted; defers any in-place Type-2 surfacing. This is the plan interim for 28c. | In-place loop-first surfacing of Type-2 launches until 28d. |
| B | Open the `/pipeline` form as an in-place tertiary form | OPERATIVE cells open the governed `/pipeline` launch form in the sidebar/tertiary region (loop-first-consistent); same governed launch, surfaced in place. Depends on the 28d tertiary-form affordance (D-APP-32). | A route-as-main hop; (no governance change — it is the same governed pipeline launch). |
| C | Require a Type-2 brief at launch | A structured Type-2 brief before launch. | This would REOPEN D-APP-27 (ruled Option C / no brief) — not in scope here. |
| D | Custom | Human supplies alternate handling. | Anything not named. |

## Recommendation

**Option A for tranche 28c, with B once the 28d tertiary-form affordance (D-APP-32) lands.** A keeps the governed, working `/pipeline` route as the OPERATIVE launch (nothing deleted) while 28c focuses on the Type-0/Type-1 in-place launch; B then surfaces that same governed launch in place for loop-first consistency, with no governance change. C is out of scope — it would reopen the ruled-deferred D-APP-27; pick it only via a fresh D-APP-27 reconsideration, not here.

## Required Constraints If A (or B) Is Approved

- Do NOT reopen D-APP-27: introduce no new task-init / brief contract; Type-2 never boots as a top-level chat persona (`assertDirectChatPersona` stands).
- Reuse the existing governed `/pipeline` launch; do not fork a parallel Type-2 launch path.
- Nothing deleted: `/pipeline` + deliverable-row deep links stay reachable.
- `npm run typecheck` + matrix-cells / pipeline / gate tests green.
