# D-APP-42 Ruling - Tool Result Artifact Metadata, Checksum, and Retention Policy

**Status:** RULED
**Date:** 2026-06-21
**Decision ID:** D-APP-42
**Ruling basis:** owner directive, 2026-06-21: proceed according to the long-term consistency
recommendation.

## Ruling

SELECT: D-APP-42 **Option A - minimal audit policy with SHA-256 and session-lifetime retention**.

Persisted tool-result artifacts must include `sha256` for the exact stored artifact bytes after
redaction and truncation. Returned metadata must include `toolName` and optional `turnId` where those
inputs are available, existing redaction/truncation flags, byte counts, and relative path.

Retention is session-lifetime only: artifacts remain with the session folder and are deleted only by
explicit session/artifact deletion or a later ruled cleanup path. No TTL, quota purge, independent
retention daemon, or release-quality retention claim is authorized. Output thresholds, preview length,
and artifact naming remain unchanged for this slice.
