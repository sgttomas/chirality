## Ruling

Fence **F1** is reconciled from a **categorical hard-deny** of provider/network expansion beyond the
Anthropic path into an **owner-permitted, default-closed configuration**: non-Anthropic providers and
private-data-to-provider are permitted **only** under an explicit owner provider/residency configuration.
Absent that configuration, the Anthropic key-aware loopback default stands, the app does not enforce
privacy/residency, and the harness **does not auto-egress** (default-closed). Provider expansion remains
human/owner-gated — never silent or agent-initiated.

This authorizes the doc edits applied in this tranche (the 8 canonical edits — D-APP-39 fence #1 note,
`docs/BOUNDARY_REVIEW_CHECKLISTS.md`, `docs/PLAN.md` §6.3 + §11, `docs/PRD.md` FR-089 + FR-125,
`docs/CONTRACT.md` K-ENGINE-3 — plus the forward-reference in the active queue
`plans/PLAN_2026-06-21_inspection_orphan_remediation.md`).

## Scope of the ruling (what it does NOT grant)

- **Only F1.** Fences **F2** (release/distribution), **F3** (R7 domain-engine implementation), **F4**
  (`CHECKING→ISSUED`), and **K-AUTH** (truthful attribution) are unchanged.
- **Posture only — no implementation.** Lifting the categorical fence does not build non-Anthropic routing
  or private-data egress. Any real non-Anthropic backend still must pass the **K-ENGINE-2**
  engine-conformance gate and respect the **F2** release fence. Do not infer broader implementation
  approval than this text grants.
- **No K-ENGINE-6 reversal.** Provider-adapter generality was already the strategic architecture
  (`SCA-APP-001`); F1 was the shipping fence, not the strategy.
