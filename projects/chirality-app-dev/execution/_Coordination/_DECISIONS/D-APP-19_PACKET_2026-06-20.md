## Recommendation

Choose **Option B (accept staged)**. The plan is well-grounded and the per-deliverable human gate
is real control, so opening the phase is low-risk — but a 53-deliverable program with a
never-exercised issuance mechanism benefits from one explicit checkpoint: prove the
`IN_PROGRESS -> CHECKING -> ISSUED` flow (with `approvalSha`), the Evidence template, and the
G1-G6 fixes on the lowest-risk pilot set, then a quick re-gate authorizes the waves. This is real
forward progress, not a half-measure, and it matches the project's incremental, gated cadence.

**Option A** is defensible if you would rather authorize the whole walk now and rely on the
per-deliverable gates as the control (nothing issues without your SHA regardless).

**Option C** fits if you want the project fully evidence-ready and reconciled but are not ready to
begin issuing anything.

In all of A/B/C, the embedded sub-rulings (REF-006, AMD-01, PKG-10 basis) still come to you
separately as `ISS-01` reaches them.
