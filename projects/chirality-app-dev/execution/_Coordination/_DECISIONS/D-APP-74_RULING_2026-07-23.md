## Ruled product direction

- Actual human-agent dialogue is the primary workspace.
- The primary live transcript and composer remain mounted across in-shell
  presentation changes.
- Provenance-bearing references, proposals, diffs, validation results,
  permissions, returns, and evidence may appear inline and open into focused
  artifact views that return to their dialogue anchor.
- Visible artifacts are not automatically next-turn model context. The
  composer exposes the exact context references included in the next turn.
- The right Coordination Panel toggles between Work and Agents.
- Work renders only explicitly admitted project or runtime records and keeps
  governed project records, human-approved bases, agent proposals, and
  ephemeral runtime state visibly distinct.
- Agents renders recorded sessions and exact canonical parentage. Selecting a
  recorded session may replace the central region's visible content with a
  clearly labelled read-only replay lens.
- A replay lens does not resume, switch, merge with, or mutate the primary live
  dialogue. Drafts, attachments, context, permissions, interruption state, and
  session identity never transfer implicitly.

## Projection and authority boundary

The Work/Agents Coordination Panel is a rebuildable projection over admitted
project and runtime records. Rendering, grouping, linking, filtering,
selecting, or locally annotating a plan, task, status, assignment, session, or
relationship does not create project truth, approve a plan, assign authority,
transition lifecycle, authenticate a human act, or change runtime state. The
admitted source remains controlling.

Missing optional fields render as absent or unknown. Stale or contradictory
sources remain provenance-labelled. Conversational prose is not silently
converted into a structured plan or task. Runtime completion is not
deliverable acceptance. Parentage and cross-links require exact recorded
identifiers.
