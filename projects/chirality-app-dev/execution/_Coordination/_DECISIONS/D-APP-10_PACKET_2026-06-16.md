child turns, require a narrower custom step, or continue holding R5.

## Recommendation

Approve Option A or Option B.

Rationale: `R5-BRIDGE-001` proves the non-executable bridge and hard-deny gates. The next
lowest-risk work is lifecycle evidence and bounded artifact-reference semantics without
child execution. Option C should be reserved for a later ruling after non-executable
lifecycle/artifact evidence exists, unless the human explicitly accepts the combined
implementation risk.
