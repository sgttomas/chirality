## Options

- **O-A (recommended): scoped interpretation.** The recorded invalidity
  assertion is scoped to dependency-extraction evidence: `_SEMANTIC.md` is
  not admissible as evidence for dependency rows. Consumption of
  `_SEMANTIC.md` as the input to semantic lensing is outside that scope and
  consistent with it. On ruling: one dated clarification addendum citing
  D-APP-62 is appended to `_DEPENDENCIES.md` in each of the 10 named
  deliverables; the remaining 41 unqualified assertions are governed by the
  register row without file changes.
- **O-B: genuine conflict.** The two stances conflict; the owner directs
  which run's stance governs and what remediation follows (scope to be
  specified in the ruling).
- **O-C: defer.** No interpretation recorded; the literal contradiction
  stands.
