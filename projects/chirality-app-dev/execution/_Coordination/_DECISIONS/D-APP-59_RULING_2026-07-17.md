## S1 — Standing decision-latitude delegation (ADOPTED)

The owner adopts, as a standing direction for the chirality-app-dev work
loop, the delegation of agent decision latitude phrased as: *decide ensuring
consistency and coherence in ontology, epistemology, praxeology, and axiology
of this project*.

**Reach.** The standing delegation covers:

1. design-shaped judgments — selection among fence-respecting means,
   conventions, locations, schema identities, naming, and structure;
2. evidence-against-criteria judgments — whether recorded evidence satisfies
   an explicitly stated criterion (e.g., a dependency row's Statement);
3. hold-as-truthful-gap judgments — recording that an item correctly awaits a
   future owner act rather than manufacturing closure.

**Limits (not reachable by this or any delegation).** The delegation never
reaches:

1. reliance and acceptance acts (K-CLAIM-1; K-DOMAIN-3) — accepting evidence,
   proposals, or outputs as relied-upon truth;
2. accountability assignments — naming a ResponsibleParty or evidence owner
   is a human self-binding act;
3. lifecycle issuance and transitions through ruled gates (F-APP-4 and the
   `Checking Approval SHA` discipline);
4. the hard fences F-APP-1 through F-APP-5 and the fresh-ruling stops in the
   standing workplan;
5. creation or acceptance of new authorized scope (e.g., a PKG-10 amendment)
   — a direction-shaped owner act;
6. adoption, ruling, and direction themselves (K-AUTH-1; D-GOV-04).

**Attribution.** Every judgment exercised under this delegation is recorded
as the agent's decision under owner-delegated latitude — never as an owner
selection — with the exercising receipt naming the artifact that carries the
rationale. Truthful attribution remains the one firm limit of the
decision-latitude model. Any uncertainty about whether an item falls inside
the reach is itself returned to the owner (consequential-amendment rule,
`AGENTS.md`).

**Precedents incorporated as worked examples:** the D-APP-58 location/schema
selection (`D-APP-58_RULING_2026-07-16.md`) and the DEP-10-04-004 amendment
judgment (loop Receipt-56).
