# D-APP-19 Ruling - Open the Deliverable-Inspection Phase (Issuance Deferred)

**Status:** RULED
**Date:** 2026-06-20
**Decision ID:** D-APP-19

SELECT: D-APP-19 **Option D (Custom)** — open a deliverable-**inspection** phase, not the issuance
lifecycle. Per owner directive (2026-06-20), the project will move all deliverables into `CHECKING`
and inspect each at issuance-gate rigor to **form new evidence on how to proceed developing the
application**, while **evaluating whether the issuance-gate process is the one to keep**. Issuance
(`CHECKING -> ISSUED`) is **deferred** — the owner does not expect to close out deliverables yet.

This supersedes the packet's issuance-oriented Options A/B/C. The governing plan is
`plans/PLAN_2026-06-20_deliverable_inspection_and_development_evidence.md`, which transposes the
still-valid materials of `plans/PLAN_2026-06-18_deliverable_issuance_and_evidence_consolidation.md`
(current-state map, prerequisites P1-P5, code/scope gaps G1-G6). The issuance program is retained as
the eventual follow-on, to be reconsidered after the inspection and the gate-process evaluation
conclude.

## Authorized scope

- Move all 53 deliverables `IN_PROGRESS -> CHECKING` as the inspection-entry checkpoint (the human
  authorizes the batch; see acknowledgements).
- Per-deliverable inspection producing evidence of true state vs spec + a forward development
  recommendation; a synthesized, dependency-ordered development roadmap.
- Evaluate the per-deliverable `approvalSha` human-gate process and recommend keep / modify / replace.
- A bounded control-plane truth-fix (the stale PKG-00 `CYCLIC` record -> acyclic).

## Owner acknowledgements (binding on execution)

1. **One blessed `approvalSha`.** Every `IN_PROGRESS -> CHECKING` transition binds a single
   owner-confirmed git SHA naming the reviewed content (the commit `HEAD` at execution). The
   implementing agent applies the transition mechanically and must never mint a SHA or claim
   approval — the owner's directive + this ruling is the human authorship (K-AUTH-1), and the SHA
   binds the reviewed content (K-AUTH-2). The owner confirms the SHA before the batch move.
2. **`CHECKING` here means "admitted to inspection," not "reviewed-and-approved-for-issue."**
   53 deliverables in `CHECKING` is not 53 issuance approvals. `ISSUED` remains a separate, deferred,
   per-deliverable human gate.
3. **`CHECKING` is one-way in code** (the transition engine rejects backward moves); reverting a
   deliverable requires a manual human `_STATUS.md` amend. The owner accepts this before the batch.

## Required constraints

- No `CHECKING -> ISSUED` transition under this ruling.
- No code development: the genuine gaps (G1-G6) are roadmap **outputs**, not built here; only the
  bounded PKG-00 control-plane truth-fix is applied.
- This ruling does **not** decide REF-006 (PRD-hash), AMD-01 (UI render-test bar), or the PKG-10
  doc-only acceptance basis; each remains a separate later ruling, informed by the inspection.
- All `docs/PLAN.md` §11 fences stay closed (R7 / domain-engine, remote MCP, non-Anthropic
  providers, release signing/notarization/publication, professional-reliance claims); no
  provider-default change.
- Inspection records (Assessments) are review artifacts added to deliverable folders; they do not
  alter the substantive content the `CHECKING` SHA bound. Any substantive change would require
  re-confirming the binding.
