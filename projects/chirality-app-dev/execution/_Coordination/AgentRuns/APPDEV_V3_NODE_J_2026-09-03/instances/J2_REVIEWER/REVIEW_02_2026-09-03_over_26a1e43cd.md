## Findings

### J2-F1 — MAJOR — The replacement identity can collide and is not bound atomically to the signal

File: `projects/chirality-app-dev/execution/PKG-09_Validation_Packaging_Security_and_Release/1_Working/DEL-09-01_Section_8_Harness_Validation_Preservation/Evidence/Node_H_Section8_Preservation_2026-09-03/rerun-section8-local.sh`

Locations: lines 104–125 and 182–200.

`read_process_identity` hashes `ps -o lstart= -o uid=` plus the executable path. On this host, `lstart` has one-second resolution. Two different same-uid `/bin/sleep` processes launched in the same second produced the same identity:

```text
pid1=2770
pid2=2771
identity1=04029d312f15940a37327a5d87d7af4b39be849f659ec6f1e4ab3aa2e73603b2
identity2=04029d312f15940a37327a5d87d7af4b39be849f659ec6f1e4ab3aa2e73603b2
equal=yes
```

Therefore start/uid/executable is not a unique process identity. If a recorded PID is reused by the same executable under the same uid within that timestamp bucket, the candidate accepts the foreign process as the original. This directly contradicts the comments at lines 105–107 and the run/evidence claims that stale or reused PIDs are never accepted.

There is also an unavoidable check-to-signal window in the current construction: line 191 reads the identity, line 192 compares it, and line 193 calls a separate `kill(2)` through `send_process_signal`. A process can exit and its PID can be reused between those operations. `test-rerun-section8-cleanup.sh` changes the fake identity before survivor construction; it does not exercise reuse after the final successful identity read and before the signal.

Impact: the teardown method still cannot establish the required guarantee that it will never signal a foreign process. J1-F1 remains open.

Required remediation: use an ownership/signal primitive whose target remains bound to the script-started job for its lifetime (or an OS-backed non-reusable process handle), rather than a second-resolution fingerprint plus a separate PID signal. If PID records remain part of diagnostics, they must not be the authority for an unsafe signal. Add deterministic coverage for same-second/same-executable identity collision and for reuse in the final check-to-signal interval. Preserve merged early/current capture behavior and survivor-only escalation.
