# Software reverse engineering now supports successor product bases

Root amends `chirality-root:bundled:workflow:reverse-engineer-software` to
examine a project's implementation, requirements, scopes of work, decisions,
and evidence as sources of exemplars and lessons for a successor PRD and
supporting documents. A bounded reference account remains a supported outcome.
The package adds `resources/successor-basis.md` and preserves its qualified
identity and specialist navigation placement.

The method now includes unchanged transfer of material designated by the human,
selection of useful patterns with their evidence limits, development of embedded
and standalone product expressions, and feedback from subsequent use. It ends
at the conceptual output selected by the assignment.

This notice concerns Root source under
`ROOT-REVERSE-ENGINEER-SUCCESSOR-20260925`. The App loop decides when to adopt
or package it. Existing selected methods and project contracts retain their
own basis. No App redesign, source re-pin, build, qualification, or release is
performed. The owner's motivating direction is retained in
`execution/_Coordination/REVERSE_ENGINEER_SUCCESSOR_20260925.md` at Root.
