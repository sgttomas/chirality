## What this frames (restatement, non-authoritative)

- Chirality App is a user-facing product in its own right: the general
  instantiation of the Chirality paradigm for knowledge work ("instance
  0"), not merely infrastructure or a harness that delivers other
  applications.
- OpenPipeStress (projects/chirality-piping/) is the first
  domain-specific instantiation of that same paradigm ("instance 1"):
  the working pattern — dialogue-centered work, governed agents,
  deterministic validation, file-native authority, human decision gates
  — carries from instance 0; the subject-matter layer is replaced by the
  piping-design domain.
- This framing resolves the direction of the TM-APP-025 "dual-target"
  question: both targets exist by design, with instance-0 primacy —
  the standalone Chirality App defines the paradigm surface that
  domain instantiations derive from. The ruling itself remains a
  separate owner act.
