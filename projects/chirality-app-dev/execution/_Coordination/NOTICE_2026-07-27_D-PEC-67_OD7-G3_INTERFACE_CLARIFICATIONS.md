# Coordination Notice — D-PEC-67 PEC interface clarifications

**Status:** NON-BINDING NOTICE CANDIDATE
**Receiving loop:** Chirality App

PEC proposes to clarify PEC-K-03 and PEC-K-11 as optional PEC-provided
interfaces: orientation is pull-oriented and labeled; scope and mode are
capabilities; contact and cadence remain receiving-owner choices.

This notice creates no App scope, consumer, polling, injection, cadence, or
implementation requirement. The App loop may adopt, amend, defer, or decline
any receiving-loop implication under its own instruments.
