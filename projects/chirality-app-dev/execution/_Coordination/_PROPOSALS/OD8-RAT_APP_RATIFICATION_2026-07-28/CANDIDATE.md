D-APP-79, D-APP-80, and D-APP-81 — to the owner for fresh per-decision
judgment over the present state each act established. Every act is enumerated,

## What this candidate is not

and self-merges are disclosed procedural exceptions, and they remain
disclosed. This candidate adds fresh per-decision owner judgment over the
already-effective present state. It does not repair, backdate, or extinguish
the historical nonconformance, and it does not change the effective status of
any act: each is already durably effective at its recorded merge.

The candidate also creates no new lifecycle, implementation, scope, contract,
hold, runtime, release, issuance, or professional-reliance effect in any
direction, whether ratified or declined.

## Blanket direction versus per-decision judgment

Across all four acts, the 2026-07-28 blanket direction supplied exactly two
things: permission to self-merge the landing pull requests, and an
instruction to treat the standing agent recommendation as the approved owner
selection while still recording it in the usual manner. It supplied no
per-decision reading of any option, no per-decision acceptance of any exact
manifest, and no per-decision acknowledgment of any disclosed defect.

Everything else in each act — the option chosen, the exact manifest accepted,
the scope limits, and the non-effects — was authored by the agent and merged
under that blanket permission. `D-APP-82` is where per-decision owner
judgment is supplied for the first time, one line at a time.

## Decline semantics

- Each decision is ruled by returning its own verbatim line. Silence on a
  line is not ratification of that line.
- Declining a line has no effect on the other three lines, and no effect on
  the already-effective state of the declined act. It records that the act's
  present state has been reviewed and has not received fresh owner judgment.
- Declining every line leaves the entire App current state exactly as it is
  today, with the disclosed procedural exceptions intact and undisturbed.
- Returning the D-APP-78 selection token is a separate return from the
  D-APP-78 ratification line. Either may be given without the other.
- No line, returned or declined, authorizes any future act.
