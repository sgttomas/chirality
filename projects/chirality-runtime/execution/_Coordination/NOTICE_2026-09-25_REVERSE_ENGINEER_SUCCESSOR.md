# Software reverse engineering now supports successor product bases

Root amends `chirality-root:bundled:workflow:reverse-engineer-software` to
examine a project's implementation, requirements, scopes of work, decisions,
and evidence as sources of exemplars and lessons for a successor PRD and
supporting documents. A bounded reference account remains a supported outcome.
The package adds `resources/successor-basis.md` and preserves its qualified
identity and specialist navigation placement.

Runtime's catalog and packaging consumers can use the revised source through
their existing contracts. Selection and adoption remain with the receiving
loop; this notice does not change an in-flight method basis or perform a
consumer re-pin, runtime change, qualification, or release.

Source and authorization are recorded in tranche
`ROOT-REVERSE-ENGINEER-SUCCESSOR-20260925` and Root's
`execution/_Coordination/REVERSE_ENGINEER_SUCCESSOR_20260925.md`.
