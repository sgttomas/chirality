# Specification — Construction Work Package (DEL-084-03)

Pass: P1_P2 (initial draft + cross-reference sweep)

## Scope

This Construction Work Package (CWP) defines how the PKG-084 Fuel Gas Skid serving the 03-25 facility shall be physically installed, built, inspected, turned over, and tied into the larger facility utility systems. The CWP covers the construction-phase scope and deliverables associated with SOW-0095, SOW-0096, SOW-0097, and SOW-0098 (source: DELIVERABLE_REGISTER row 326).

**Includes:**
- Module/skid receipt, set, and mechanical hookup of the fuel gas skid.
- Field interconnecting piping for LP fuel-gas headers serving listed users (source: `3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` line 463).
- Tie-ins to the shared 04-25 utility building infrastructure (source: `3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` lines 452, 706).
- Civil/structural support, electrical/instrument home-run cabling and terminations, area lighting, fire/gas detection, drain/vent tie-ins as applicable at skid boundary (source: `3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` line 619).
- Inspection, commissioning support, and turnover documentation.

**Excludes (unless explicitly added):**
- Offsite pipeline scope beyond facility tie-in flanges (source: `3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` line 70).
- Third-party LACT and similar excluded equipment (source: `3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` line 69).
- Skid vendor shop fabrication (covered by the vendor package, not the CWP).
