# Specification: DEL-048-03 — Construction Work Package

## Scope

This specification governs the EPC Integrator's Construction Work Package (CWP) for PKG-048 (Inlet / Sales Compressors) at the 03-25 West Doe Compressor Station and Liquids Hub. It defines what the CWP must contain so that the package can be physically installed, built, inspected, turned over, and tied into the larger facility systems (Source: _CONTEXT.md Scope).

In scope:
- Construction management, civil/structural, mechanical, piping, electrical, and instrumentation execution scope as enumerated in the DBM Construction Scope Summary (Source: 3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md SEC-03).
- Receipt, offloading, and field setting of modularized inlet compressor packages KM-2150/KM-2250 and the TEG dehydration package; reassembly of three-piece modules into self-framing buildings (Source: 3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md SEC-05).
- ISBL/OSBL interconnecting piping; mechanical hookups; installation of shipped-loose instruments and valves; home-run cabling and terminations; pipe supports (Source: 3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md SEC-03).
- Installation tie-ins at the plant inlet boundary (first aboveground flange within lease boundary) and at the sour-gas export interface to the 04-25 inlet gathering system (Source: 3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md SEC-04).
- Construction interface management and mechanical-completion turnover to commissioning.

Out of scope (excluded unless explicitly assigned):
- Third-party LACT equipment scope beyond facility-side power/tie-in interfaces (Source: 3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md SEC-03).
- Upstream and product pipelines outside facility tie-ins (Source: 3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md SEC-03).
- Local 03-25 instrument-air compression (superseded by 04-25 supply per SCA-006) (Source: 3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md SEC-03).
