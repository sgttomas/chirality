# Guidance — DEL-067-03 Construction Work Package (Tanks, Sour Water (API 650) 4-25)

## Purpose

The Construction Work Package (CWP) exists so the EPC Integrator can physically install the vendor-supplied pair of API 650 modified atmospheric sour-water / produced-water tanks (expected tags TK-9010-1 and TK-9020-1) into the WBS 01 04-25 Deepcut facility, prove all interfaces are correctly tied in, and hand a mechanically complete, sour-service-rated, containment-ready system to commissioning with controlled open-item evidence. The deliverable is a Gate 5 EPC-anchored production unit. Source: `_CONTEXT.md` (Scope, Notes); `DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.csv` DEL-067-03.

## Principles

1. **Vendor designs; EPC integrates and constructs.** The CWP must not redesign the vendor tanks. It plans, executes, inspects, and documents the integration and installation of a vendor-engineered package. Source: `PACKAGE_REGISTER.csv` PKG-067 ResponsibilityModel; `OBJECTIVE_REGISTER.csv` OBJ-004.
2. **Interface fidelity over interface convenience.** Every applicable interface in `INTERFACE_REGISTER.csv` for PKG-067 (9 rows) must appear as a planned tie-in with a turnover record, even when the tie-in is small. Source: `INTERFACE_REGISTER.csv` PKG-067.
3. **Source-grounded, not narrative-grounded.** Construction decisions trace to authoritative basis (DBM-Deepcut sections, vendor package documents in DEL-067-04 and DEL-067-05, package datasheet in DEL-067-02, API 650). Decomposition narrative is routing — not authority. ASSUMPTION elevated from skill authority hierarchy.
4. **Sour-service rigor is mandatory.** The tanks store sour-water / produced-water (SOW-0226). Materials traceability (PMI), weld hardness control, internal-coating QA, hydrostatic test discipline per API 650, and code/standard compliance are not optional and must be visible in the turnover package. Source: `SCOPE_LEDGER.csv` SOW-0226, SOW-0227; `OBJECTIVE_REGISTER.csv` OBJ-009.
5. **Containment is part of construction, not a separate concern.** Two storage tanks in sour-water service create environmental release risk; the CWP must treat secondary containment, drainage, and spill response as construction-critical, with explicit walkdowns and acceptance records. Source: `INTERFACE_REGISTER.csv` PKG-067 (Drain/Containment, Grading/Site Drainage/Spill Containment); `OBJECTIVE_REGISTER.csv` OBJ-007, OBJ-009.
6. **Closeout discipline.** Open items at mechanical completion are tracked and explicitly dispositioned, not deferred silently — and the SOW-0228 confirmation list (service naming, tags, source headers, design SG, heating/insulation extent, VRU/blanket applicability, 3-25 basis applicability) must be resolved before mechanical completion. Source: `OBJECTIVE_REGISTER.csv` OBJ-010; `SCOPE_LEDGER.csv` SOW-0228.

## Considerations

- **SOW-0228 open items dominate construction readiness.** The package source explicitly defers service naming, tank count, tags, source headers, design SG, heating/insulation extent, VRU/blanket applicability, and 3-25 basis applicability. Many of these are not optional construction inputs — design SG affects foundation loading and hydrostatic test plan; tank count affects civil layout and containment volume; VRU/blanket applicability affects relief-path design and tie-in. The CWP cannot reach release-for-construction without these resolved. Source: `SCOPE_LEDGER.csv` SOW-0228.
- **Field-erected vs. shop-fabricated.** API 650 tanks of typical produced-water size are usually field-erected. Whether these tanks are field-erected or shop-fabricated drives crane/lift planning, weld-out sequence, coating sequence (interior coating typically applied after weld-out and hydrotest), and overall schedule. Specific vendor erection method: TBD (location TBD — DEL-067-04 vendor scope).
- **Sequencing with civil and containment.** PKG-067 carries Structural/Foundations/Supports (IFC-2815BE772E), Grading/Site Drainage/Spill Containment (IFC-E6F955CD2C), and Drain/Containment (IFC-C5A70E8F73). Foundation and containment readiness is a hard prerequisite to tank erection. The CWP must sequence civil → foundation acceptance → tank erection → containment closure → tie-in → hydrotest → coating (if interior) → instrumentation/insulation → mechanical completion. ASSUMPTION (construction-discipline convention).
- **Coating sequence.** Internal coating per SOW-0227 is typically applied after hydrostatic test (water test) and after all internal welding is complete and surfaces are blasted to standard. Sequencing the coating window between hydrotest dry-out and instrumentation/insulation closure is a critical-path consideration. ASSUMPTION (coating practice); specific coating-system spec TBD (location TBD).
- **PVRV/EPRV and blanket-gas integration.** SOW-0227 lists PVRV/EPRV protection and LP fuel-gas blanket as included features. PVRV/EPRV set-pressure verification, blanket-gas leak-test, and relief-path destination (atmospheric vent vs. LP flare/vent header) are construction-critical. Specific relief destination: TBD (location TBD).
- **VRU integration.** SOW-0227 lists "VRU suction/header connection as applicable". Applicability and the receiving VRU package identity are open (SOW-0228). The CWP must coordinate with the owning VRU/compressor package if applicable, or document a "not-applicable" decision with provenance.
- **EHT applicability.** "External insulation and electric heating where required" (SOW-0227) — the *where required* extent is TBD per SOW-0228. Construction cannot finalize EHT circuit layout, power-feed sizing, or commissioning plan without this resolved. Note: `INTERFACE_REGISTER.csv` does not carry a discrete EHT row; if EHT is required, the power-feed tie-in must be planned against the facility electrical scope. ASSUMPTION; see Conflict Table CFT-001.
- **Cathodic Protection.** PKG-067 uniquely (within its interface set) carries a Cathodic Protection interface (IFC-A3C5E09CBC), consistent with buried/contact-with-earth surfaces (tank bottoms on ring-wall or oil-sand pad). CP design, anode placement, and reference-cell testing must be planned in the CWP. Specific CP system type: TBD (location TBD).
- **Relief / Flare / Vent interface present.** Unlike many sibling EPC packages, PKG-067 explicitly carries a Relief/Flare/Vent interface row (IFC-44474541F7), consistent with the PVRV/EPRV protection in SOW-0227. The CWP must plan relief-path tie-in and acceptance.
- **Fire & Gas absence in interface register.** No discrete F&G interface row for PKG-067. Area-wide F&G coverage of the tank pad is assumed to come from the facility F&G package; verify tank pad is within an existing detector coverage envelope. ASSUMPTION; see Conflict Table CFT-002.
- **Utility Piping absence in interface register.** PKG-067 does not carry a discrete Utility Piping row; blanket-gas supply and VRU suction connections are treated either as Process Piping or Relief/Flare/Vent ties. Confirm with DEL-067-02 before tie-in lock-in. ASSUMPTION; see Conflict Table CFT-002.
- **Vendor-document availability** drives construction readiness. The CWP cannot finalize before DEL-067-05 supplies the required vendor documentation (certified drawings, API 650 tank data report, weld maps, coating spec, recommended inspection plans, PVRV/EPRV data). ASSUMPTION based on OBJ-010 closure conditions.

## Trade-offs

- **Pre-fab vs. stick-build of tie-in piping (sour service):** pre-fab improves quality and schedule but locks in dimensions before field reality is known. Pre-fab is generally preferred for sour-service piping because shop-controlled welding improves PMI and hardness compliance — but trade-off rationale beyond this general principle: TBD (source not deliverable-local).
- **Vendor-attended installation / erection vs. EPC-only crew:** vendor presence reduces installation risk and warranty exposure but costs more. For field-erected API 650 tanks, vendor-attended erection is common practice. Trade-off rationale: TBD.
- **Hydrostatic test water source and disposal:** hydrostatic test of two large tanks consumes significant water and may produce sour-contact residue depending on test sequencing relative to internal-coating application. Specific water source / disposal plan: TBD (location TBD).
- **Interior coating system selection:** coating system (e.g., novolac epoxy, phenolic, glass-flake) drives surface prep, application weather/humidity envelope, cure time, and inspection regime. Trade-off rationale: TBD (coating-spec not deliverable-local).
- **Heating method selection (EHT vs. steam tracing vs. none) where required:** EHT is simplest where small electrical loads are acceptable; steam tracing requires a steam utility tie-in not currently in PKG-067's interface set. EHT is implicitly indicated by SOW-0227 wording. Trade-off rationale beyond this: TBD.
