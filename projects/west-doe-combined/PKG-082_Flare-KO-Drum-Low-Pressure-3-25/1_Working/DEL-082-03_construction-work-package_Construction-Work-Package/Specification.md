# Specification — DEL-082-03 Construction Work Package (LP Flare KO Drum, 3-25)

## Scope

This Construction Work Package (CWP) specifies how PKG-082 — Flare KO Drum (Low Pressure) 3-25 — shall be physically installed, built, inspected, turned over, and tied into the larger 03-25 facility systems by the EPC Integrator. The package centers on the LP flare KO drum V-3900-2 and its transfer pump P-3900-2, the 508 mm (20 inch) LP relief header tie-ins, and the interface to the shared HP/Cryo and LP dual flare stack (SourcePath: `_Sources/DBM-Comp_and_Liquids/3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md`, SectionRef: SEC-07 Flare and Blowdown, lines 497–501).

In scope:
- Construction execution of the LP flare KO drum vessel V-3900-2 and pump P-3900-2 mechanical setting.
- LP relief header construction and tie-ins to TEG regeneration, VRU, and compressor seal-pot services (DBM SEC-07, line 499).
- LP flare stack interface tie-in within the 03-25 boundary of the shared HP/Cryo and LP dual flare stack (DBM SEC-07, line 499; SEC-01, line 56).
- Construction interface and turnover checklist; installation and tie-in workface plan (per _CONTEXT.md anticipated artifacts).

Excluded:
- LP flare stack mechanical scope where allocated to 04-25 or to a shared-interface package (DBM SEC-01 line 56: shared-interface system, allocation is an open interface item).
- Detailed design of the vessel and pump (design is upstream).
- Operations procedures post-turnover.
