# Guidance — DEL-063-03 Construction Work Package (Tanks, DSO (API 650))

> Directional guidance for executing the Construction Work Package (CWP) for `PKG-063 Tanks, DSO (API 650)`. Source-anchored to `_Sources/DBM-Comp_and_Liquids/3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` (caustic mercaptan treating + DSO service). The package-requirements DOCX (`_Sources/26020-Package_Requirements.docx` heading 18) is cited by `_CONTEXT.md` but is not available as accessible plain text in `_REFERENCES.md`; specific demarcation language from that slice is `TBD`. Inferences are marked `ASSUMPTION`.

## Purpose

The CWP exists because a DSO (disulphide oil) tank built to API 650 is a field-erected, multi-discipline construction artifact whose installation, internal coating, tie-in piping, EHT, instrumentation, F&G, and structural access work crosses many trades on a cold-climate site (−40 °C minimum ambient per DBM line 145). DSO is the sulphur-bearing by-product of the non-regenerative caustic mercaptan treating package (DBM line 389) and is captured in the caustic system's tank list (DBM line 400). Without a deliberate CWP, no party owns the workface plan that converts a delivered/erected tank into an installed, tested, and turned-over piece of facility equipment with safe handling of a sulphur-bearing hydrocarbon.

## Principles

1. **API 650 governs the tank; the project specs govern the surroundings.** API 650 (Modified — analogue per DBM line 421) sets shell, bottom, roof, anchorage, and hydrostatic test rules. Piping, EHT, electrical, instrumentation, F&G, foundations, and containment around the tank follow project specifications, not API 650.
2. **DSO service is not produced-water service.** The DBM specifies Devchem 253 internal coating for the produced-water tank family (line 421) and material/coating notes for caustic tanks (line 402). DSO is a sulphur-bearing hydrocarbon by-product; do not silently inherit produced-water or caustic coating selections — coating choice for DSO must be confirmed in detailed design and recorded as a Conflict Table entry until resolved.
3. **Cold-climate winterization is structural, not optional.** The −40 °C site basis (DBM line 145) drives EHT, instrument heating, insulation, drain freeze protection, and material toughness. The CWP must treat winterization as a first-class workface activity.
4. **Build for turnover.** Every construction activity should generate the records required by `R-CWP-16`–`R-CWP-20`. Records collected at the activity are cheap; records reconstructed at turnover are not.
5. **Maintain interface evidence.** The package row in `_Sources/26020-Packages_Interfaces_4_export.xlsx` (row 90 per `_CONTEXT.md`) is the canonical interface-level evidence for this package. The Datasheet's interface applicability is `ASSUMPTION` until that row is excerpted and reconciled; the CWP should not silently re-baseline these interfaces.
6. **Containment first.** A sulphur-bearing hydrocarbon tank without functioning bund and spill drainage at the time of first liquid is a safety and environmental risk. Bund/drain completion must precede hydrostatic-test water disposal and especially first DSO introduction.

## Considerations

- **Tank standard slice not in `_REFERENCES.md`.** The DSO tank datasheet (vendor or EPC), the tank capacity, and the specific API 650 Modified clauses are not in `_REFERENCES.md`. The CWP cannot fix anchor-bolt patterns, plate thickness, or hydrotest water volume until those inputs arrive. Sequence the workface plan with explicit "vendor input" gates.
- **Foundation depends on geotechnical report.** DBM line 700 requires foundations be designed for the final geotechnical report. Ringwall vs. pile-supported vs. slab is a function of soil bearing, frost, and settlement tolerance — none of which are in `_REFERENCES.md` for this deliverable.
- **Drain header rating constraint.** Hydrocarbon liquid drain header is part of the declared drain systems and 300# ANSI is the minimum drain-header rating (DBM line 493). The tank drain interface must respect this.
- **DSO transfer pumps.** The DBM lists "DSO pumps, 2 × 100 %" (line 577). Whether these pumps are package-internal to the tank scope or a separate package is `TBD` — confirm in the package-requirements slice and adjust workface boundaries accordingly.
- **Vent disposition.** Spent caustic tank vents through a flame arrestor to the incinerator header (DBM line 402). Whether DSO tank shares incinerator-header venting, has a dedicated arrestor/stack, or is fuel-gas blanketed is `TBD`. Vent piping installation is a workface item that depends on this answer.
- **F&G near tankage.** DBM line 838 names tankage and caustic/DSO systems explicitly as F&G placement governance areas. Expect LEL, H2S, methyl mercaptan, and fire detectors in this scope. Detector quantity/placement is design-output (TBD pending detailed design per DBM); the CWP must reserve installation capacity even before final detector lists.
- **Permitting context.** BCER permit `100120203` covers the liquids hub; further amendments are required for the truck rack and sour-water treating scope (DBM line 872). If DSO is trucked out, the truck-rack permit dependency may affect first-fill timing — confirm.

## Trade-offs

- **Shop-fabricated vs. field-erected tank.** Smaller-capacity DSO tanks may be shop-fabricated and trucked in; field erection is required above shipping-size limits. Shop fabrication compresses field welding/NDE; field erection gives larger volume but stretches the construction window. Tank size (TBD) drives this trade-off.
- **Internal coating in shop vs. at site.** Shop-applied internal coating gives better surface preparation control; site-applied coating allows post-erection coating but is sensitive to weather. Cold-climate site arguments favour shop coating where feasible.
- **Hydrotest water source and disposal.** API 650 hydrotest water volume can be substantial. Source (potable, raw water, treated produced water — but produced water re-use is constrained by chlorides/coating risk) and disposal route both matter. Hydrotest water cannot be released to environment without treatment.
- **Single MC at package level vs. multi-stage MC by sub-system.** Single MC is simpler administratively but delays sub-system handovers. Multi-stage MC enables commissioning to start on electrical/instrumentation early but increases punch-list and re-walk overhead.

## Examples

- Drain rating example: The DSO tank low-point drain piping that connects into the hydrocarbon liquid drain system is bounded by the 300# ANSI minimum drain-header rating stated in DBM line 493. The CWP weld map and material take-off for the drain tie-in MUST honour this — not the lower flange class that might otherwise be assumed for "atmospheric tank drain".
- Source-grounded scope demarcation example: The CWP cannot reuse the SGBC-style "By others" list (e.g. `DEL-049-03`) verbatim because the analogous list from `_Sources/26020-Package_Requirements.docx` heading 18 has not been excerpted into `_REFERENCES.md`.
