# Guidance: DEL-038-06_epc-vendor-package-review-and-acceptance

## Purpose

Explain how the EPC Integrator should perform vendor package review and acceptance for `PKG-038` (600V ELECTRICAL BUILDING (820-1)) without overstating what the accessible source set actually authorizes, and how to record acceptance evidence that downstream activities (construction handover, turnover to operations) can rely upon.

## Principles

- **Authority hierarchy is upstream-fixed.** The accepted EPC Scope of Work (`DEL-038-01`), the `DEL-038-02` Package Datasheet, and the `DEL-038-03` Construction Work Package are the acceptance basis. The vendor package is the subject of review, not its own authority.
- **Responsibility split is non-negotiable.** Per `PACKAGE_REGISTER.csv` row `PKG-038`, the Package Vendor owns package engineering, package design, vendor documentation, and the physical equipment package; the EPC Integrator owns facility-level integration. Review comments shall not redesign the vendor package or accept vendor scope drift into EPC scope.
- **Source gaps are not silently filled.** Where the EPC Scope of Work, Construction Work Package, or vendor data is not accessible at acceptance-criteria fidelity, the acceptance criteria are recorded as `TBD` with the missing reference noted — not invented.
- **Interface evidence is the minimum acceptance unit.** Each of the twelve declared package interfaces (Utility Piping; Drain / Containment; Electrical Power; Grounding / Bonding; Area / Exterior Lighting; I&C / Control Cabling; Communications / Network; Building HVAC / Services; Fire & Gas / Safety Systems; Maintenance Access; Grading / Site Drainage / Spill Containment; Structural / Foundations / Supports) must have an explicit disposition before acceptance is asserted.
- **Status discipline.** This deliverable updates only its own `_STATUS.md` (safe rule). It does not change the Package Datasheet's status or any other deliverable's metadata.

## Considerations

- The accessible source set in `_REFERENCES.md` is a decomposition basis, not a vendor data package. Reviewers should expect the bulk of acceptance criteria to depend on the EPC SoW and the accepted Package Datasheet (`DEL-038-02`) rather than the DBM, and should treat the DBM electrical slices as background design basis.
- The package is an electrical *building* — i.e., a prefabricated/site-built enclosure housing 600V distribution equipment. The vendor scope therefore typically spans building structural shell, HVAC, lighting, fire & gas, and the internal electrical lineup; this is why the interface list in `INTERFACE_REGISTER.csv` is broad (twelve interfaces) compared to a single-equipment package.
- The package name "600V ELECTRICAL BUILDING (820-1)" identifies voltage class and a building-tag suffix only; the actual building configuration (dimensions, layout, HVAC capacity, fire & gas configuration, internal lineup, distribution buses, MCCs, panelboards, control voltage, metering, arc-flash classification, structural and foundation loads) remains vendor-defined and is `TBD` at the package-decomposition level until vendor data is received and accepted.
- 600V-class distribution acceptance typically depends on a project-specific short-circuit and protective-coordination study; the EPC SoW determines whether the study is vendor-supplied, EPC-supplied, or jointly produced, and reviewers should confirm that allocation rather than assuming it.
- Arc-flash hazard analysis and labeling (IEEE 1584 / CSA Z462) are commonly required for 600V distribution acceptance; reviewers should confirm the EPC SoW for the specific compliance basis rather than asserting limits not source-supported.
- Test/inspection categories (FAT, SAT, insulation, breaker timing/operation, grounding/bonding, lighting verification, fire & gas integration, HVAC commissioning) are reasonable expectations for a vendor-supplied electrical building, but the specific acceptance criteria belong to the EPC SoW and the Package Datasheet; reviewers should not assert acceptance criteria that are not source-supported.
- Turnover categories (mechanical-complete, energization-readiness, handover-to-operations) align with typical EPC construction workflow, but the authoritative gates belong to the Construction Work Package (`DEL-038-03`); until that document is accessible at acceptance-criteria fidelity the categories serve as placeholders.
- `PKG-038` is the second of two 600V Electrical Building packages on the project (the companion package is `PKG-034` "600V ELECTRICAL BUILDING (820-2)"). The two are decomposed as distinct packages and shall not be conflated; review evidence is package-scoped.

## Trade-offs

- **Accept partial vs. hold for completeness.** Where vendor documentation is partial, EPC Integrator may accept-with-comments and carry an explicit punch list rather than rejecting wholesale; the trade-off is schedule continuity vs. acceptance-evidence completeness. Either choice must be recorded in the review log.
- **Re-test vs. accept by analysis.** Where FAT/SAT cannot be re-performed, acceptance by certified vendor records and on-site inspection is acceptable when the EPC SoW permits; otherwise the item remains `TBD` and is surfaced for human ruling.
- **Strict interface verification vs. construction sequencing.** Strict verification of all twelve package interfaces before energization protects integration integrity; deferring some verification (typically Communications integration, Fire & Gas final integration, and final protective-relay settings) to commissioning may be necessary for sequencing reasons but must be explicit, not implicit.
