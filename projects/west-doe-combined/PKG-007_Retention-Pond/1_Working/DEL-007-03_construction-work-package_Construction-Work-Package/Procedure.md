# Procedure: DEL-007-03 Construction Work Package

## Purpose

Define the bounded procedure for producing and using the PKG-007 Retention Pond Construction Work Package so that construction, inspection, interface verification, and turnover are traceable to accepted source evidence.

## Prerequisites

- Accepted Gate 7 decomposition snapshot is available.
- Deliverable-local `_CONTEXT.md`, `_REFERENCES.md`, `_DEPENDENCIES.md`, and `_STATUS.md` are available.
- Accessible source basis includes the Gate 7 registers and `_Sources/DBM-Comp_and_Liquids/3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md`.
- Declared upstream dependencies: none declared during PREPARATION.
- Current blocker threshold: INITIALIZED, advisory only, and limited to declared dependency edges.
- Required detailed-design inputs must be either received or carried as open items: hydrology update, drainage design, retention pond sizing, retention pond location, grade/survey surface, plot plan/civil layout coordination, geotechnical report, and regulatory review.

## Steps

1. Confirm identity and scope.
   - Verify DEL-007-03, PKG-007, Retention Pond, WBS 02, discipline Civil, responsible party EPC Integrator, and SOW-0007 against `_CONTEXT.md` and the Gate 7 registers.

2. Build the CWP artifact index.
   - Include construction work package, installation and tie-in workface plan, and construction interface and turnover checklist.

3. Establish source-controlled constraints.
   - Record the NBCC 2020 Dawson Creek IDF proxy rainfall basis.
   - Record that final hydrology inputs are not closed.
   - Record that final pond sizing, pond location, detailed drainage design, grade surface/survey information, and geotechnical inputs are TBD unless supplied by later approved documents.

4. Develop the installation and tie-in workface plan.
   - Identify construction sequence, access, temporary works, tie-ins, inspection points, and turnover boundaries.
   - Include the Drain / Containment interface.
   - Include the Grading / Site Drainage / Spill Containment interface.
   - Mark exact routing, locations, elevations, slopes, and discharge or pump-out criteria as TBD where the source basis does not provide values.

5. Develop surface-water and drainage construction controls.
   - Plan temporary and final works to prevent uncontrolled offsite discharge.
   - Protect process areas and preserve construction and operations access.
   - Segregate process-contaminated drainage from surface-water discharge and route it to the appropriate drain or containment system.

6. Define inspection and hold points.
   - Include checks for interface status, grading/drainage readiness, containment boundaries, erosion/sediment controls where applicable, and turnover prerequisites.
   - Include hold points for unresolved hydrology, geotechnical, layout, and regulatory inputs.

7. Prepare the construction interface and turnover checklist.
   - Include interface owner/status fields.
   - Include required evidence for tie-in, inspection, deficiency closure, as-built or redline capture where applicable, and final handoff.

8. Verify open items before construction release or final turnover.
   - Confirm whether hydrology, drainage design, pond location/capacity, grade surface, geotechnical, and regulatory items are closed.
   - If not closed, record each item as a blocker, constraint, or accepted carry-forward according to project governance.

## Verification

| Check | Acceptance criterion | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Identity check | Deliverable, package, WBS, discipline, and scope item match accepted registers. | CWP cover/check sheet |
| Artifact completeness | CWP, workface plan, and interface/turnover checklist are present. | Artifact index |
| Interface coverage | Drain / Containment and Grading / Site Drainage / Spill Containment are included. | Interface verification log |
| Source limitation handling | Missing pond dimensions, final hydrology, location, and drainage details remain `TBD` or open items. | Assumptions/TBD register |
| Surface-water control | CWP prevents uncontrolled offsite discharge and protects process areas/access. | Civil/environmental inspection record |
| Contaminated drainage segregation | Process-contaminated drainage is not routed to surface-water discharge. | Drainage segregation inspection record |
| Turnover readiness | Inspection, deficiency, interface, and open-item records are complete or explicitly deferred. | Turnover checklist |

## Records

- Construction Work Package.
- Installation and tie-in workface plan.
- Construction interface and turnover checklist.
- Interface verification log.
- Inspection and hold-point records.
- Assumptions/TBD/open-item register.
- Regulatory review/open-item record.
- Construction turnover record.
