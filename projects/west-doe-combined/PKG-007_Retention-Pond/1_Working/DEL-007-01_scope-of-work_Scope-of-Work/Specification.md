# Specification: DEL-007-01_scope-of-work — Scope of Work

## Scope

This deliverable defines the EPC scope of work for PKG-007 Retention Pond. It shall identify the package basis, WBS/workbook identity, package function, source references, boundaries, whole-facility integration narrative, tagged/package identity list, and responsibility assignment record. Source: `_CONTEXT.md`; Gate 7 `DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.csv` row 26; Gate 7 `ARTIFACT_REGISTER.csv` rows 86-89.

The scope includes the workbook-defined Civil package "Retention Pond" under WBS 02 with CoA tracking number 26020-02-42-007. Source: `_Sources/26020-Packages_Interfaces_4_export.xlsx` worksheet row 8; Gate 7 `PACKAGE_REGISTER.csv` row 8.

Package-specific exclusions are `TBD`; no package-specific exclusions are stated in the accessible source materials. Source: Gate 7 `PACKAGE_REGISTER.csv` row 8.
