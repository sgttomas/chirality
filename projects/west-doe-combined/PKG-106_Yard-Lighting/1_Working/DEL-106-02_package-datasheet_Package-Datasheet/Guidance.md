## Principles

1. **Source-grounded handoff.** The Datasheet must capture what the locally accessible source set actually says about yard / area exterior lighting; it must not invent values, photometric levels, or quantities that are not in the source slices. Source: skill `four-documents` Method, Step 4; `OBJECTIVE_REGISTER.csv` OBJ-005 derived-source-intent.
2. **Vendor-owned design, EPC-owned integration.** The Datasheet declares what the EPC Integrator hands to the Package Vendor; it does not pre-empt vendor design choices that the vendor is responsible for under OBJ-004 / `PACKAGE_REGISTER.csv` row 12 Responsibility model.
3. **Interface facts are evidence, not requirements engineering.** The three "X"-marked interfaces for workbook row 12 (Electrical Power; Grounding/Bonding; Area/Exterior Lighting) are carried as datasheet evidence per `_CONTEXT.md` Notes. They identify *that* an interface exists, not the complete interface engineering. Source: `_CONTEXT.md` Notes; `ARTIFACT_REGISTER.csv` rows 170-172.
4. **LED-only lighting basis.** The Deepcut DBM establishes LED as the facility-wide lighting technology choice; this is both an electrical-demand and a regulatory light-pollution principle. Source: `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Lighting and Receptacles (lines 3029, 3035); §Energy and Emissions Design Considerations (line 2183).
5. **Light-pollution control is a regulatory driver, not a preference.** Downward illumination, prohibition of horizontally aimed floodlights, photocell/switch control, and mast-pole setback are framed as the regulatory response, not as optional best practice. Source: `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Lighting and Receptacles (line 3035).
6. **Code, classification, and area suitability come before fixture selection.** Fixtures must be suitable for the area classification in which they are installed; CEC compliance and area-classification sealing govern the raceway approach. Source: `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Lighting and Receptacles (line 3029); §Cable, Wire, and Raceways (line 3025).

- **Lack of per-package narrative.** `26020-Package_Requirements.docx` contains per-package basic-scope blocks for many tagged equipment packages but does NOT contain a "Yard Lighting" entry. The Yard Lighting scope is established by workbook row 12 plus the facility-wide electrical and lighting-and-receptacles basis in the DBMs. Treat the absence as a real gap, not as silence-equals-no-requirement. Source: full-text scan of `26020-Package_Requirements.docx`; `_REFERENCES.md` Missing/Deferred References.
- **Two DBMs apply.** Both the 04-25 Deepcut DBM and the 03-25 Comp_and_Liquids DBM carry parallel SEC-12 Electrical Basis content; values are consistent across the two for system voltages, lighting source technology, and the LV/120-208 V split. Source: `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Power System (lines 2937-2939); `DBM-Comp_and_Liquids/3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` §System Voltages (lines 734-736).
- **Mast vs. building-mounted lighting.** DBM language about "building lighting" and emergency-light counts is building-centric; for pure outdoor mast/pole yard lighting, building-code emergency-light requirements may not directly apply. Treat building-anchored requirements as ASSUMPTION when extending to outdoor mast lighting. Source: `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Lighting and Receptacles (line 3031).
- **Photometric design is vendor scope.** Specific illuminance, uniformity, glare, and pole spacing are not stated in the accessible source slices; these are appropriate vendor-design responsibilities under OBJ-004, with the EPC Integrator reviewing against light-pollution and area-classification constraints.
- **Construction tie-in placement.** The Comp_and_Liquids DBM Construction Responsibility table and Construction Scope Summary place "area lighting" in the field-construction scope; integrate construction-execution direction in `DEL-106-03_construction-work-package`, not here. Source: `DBM-Comp_and_Liquids/3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` §Construction Scope Summary (line 75); `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Construction Responsibility (line 120).

The CoA digit "01" agrees with the Deepcut (04-25, Site 01) DBM as primary discipline source. Recorded as ASSUMPTION for the codification interpretation.

## Trade-offs

- **Conductor sizing vs. RDC placement.** The DBM prefers conductors not larger than #10 AWG, achieved by locating RDCs closer to lighting loads. This trades distribution-equipment count and footprint against conductor cost and voltage-drop margin. Source: `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Cable, Wire, and Raceways (line 3001).
- **Photocell vs. switch control.** Both are accepted control methods for light-pollution minimization; photocell control automates dusk-to-dawn operation while switch control depends on operator discipline. The DBM authorizes either. Source: `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Lighting and Receptacles (line 3035).
- **Tray cable vs. rigid conduit.** Outdoor distribution favors armored tray cable (`TECK 90`/`ACWU`/`ACIC`); rigid conduit is favored for shop-fabricated building lighting. Yard lighting selection depends on whether fixtures are pole-mounted (tray/direct burial to pole base, ASSUMPTION) or building-mounted (rigid conduit). Source: `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Cable, Wire, and Raceways (lines 3001, 3025).
- **Emergency lighting scope.** Including emergency/UPS-backed lighting in the yard package widens vendor scope and the UPS load; restricting it to building-mounted fixtures (per building code) narrows it. The DBM language is building-centric. Source: `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` §Lighting and Receptacles (line 3031).
