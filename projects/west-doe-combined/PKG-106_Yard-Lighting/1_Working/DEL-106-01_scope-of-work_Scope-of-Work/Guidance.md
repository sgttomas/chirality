# Guidance — DEL-106-01 Scope of Work (PKG-106 Yard Lighting)

## Purpose

This Scope of Work is the EPC Integrator's anchoring statement of *what the PKG-106 Yard Lighting package is and how it integrates into the facility*. It is the upstream deliverable that the Package Datasheet (`DEL-106-02`), Construction Work Package (`DEL-106-03`), vendor production unit (`DEL-106-04`), vendor document turnover (`DEL-106-05`), and EPC review/acceptance (`DEL-106-06`) all consume (per `DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.csv`). Getting identity, function, boundary, responsibility, conditions, and interface set correct here prevents downstream rework when the vendor receives the technical handoff and when site construction integrates yard lighting with the facility electrical and earthing systems.

Source: `_CONTEXT.md`; `DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.csv`; package note "Mandatory Gate 5 EPC anchor deliverable defined by user instruction".

## Principles

1. **Vendor owns the package; EPC owns the integration.** Package engineering, design, vendor documentation, and equipment supply belong to the Package Vendor. Interfaces, tie-ins, constructability, procurement/construction coordination, and facility-level integration belong to the EPC Integrator. This split is the controlling responsibility model for `PKG-106`. Source: `PACKAGE_REGISTER.csv` ResponsibilityModel.
2. **Source-anchored content; no invention.** Every non-trivial statement should cite Workbook Packages row 12, `DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` SEC-12 Lighting and Receptacles, `DBM-Comp_and_Liquids/3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` SEC-12, or another locally accessible source slice. Where a value is not in an accessible slice, mark `TBD` with `location TBD` rather than inferring it. Source: SKILL `four-documents` source-grounding rule.
3. **Interface set is exhaustive at the package boundary.** The 3 interface types in `INTERFACE_REGISTER.csv` for `PKG-106` (Electrical Power; Grounding / Bonding; Area / Exterior Lighting) form the integration surface. Omitting any will create a hidden EPC scope item downstream. Source: `INTERFACE_REGISTER.csv`.
4. **LED-only, hazardous-area-suitable, light-pollution-controlled.** Per DBM-Deepcut SEC-12, all yard lighting is LED, fixtures must be suitable for the area classification in which they are installed, and regulatory light-pollution requirements are addressed through downcast floodlights, prohibition of horizontally aimed floodlights, photocell/switch control, and mast pole placement away from the pad edge. These are non-negotiable design drivers, not preferences. Source: DBM-Deepcut SEC-12 lines 3029, 3031, 3035.
5. **WBS placement is TBD.** PKG-106 has no workbook WBS assignment (column blank). The CoA tracking number `26020-01-30-001` suggests facility code 01, but this should not be promoted to a confirmed WBS without an explicit human ruling. Source: `PACKAGE_REGISTER.csv` row PKG-106.

## Considerations

- **Vendor-to-design components.** Specific fixture catalog, wattages, pole heights, mast counts, illuminance (lux) targets per area, and photometric calculations are not present in locally accessible source slices and are vendor-design outputs. The SOW must make clear that detailed sizing belongs to the vendor design package (`DEL-106-04`) rather than being asserted in this SOW.
- **Hazardous-area classification dependency.** Per DBM-Deepcut SEC-12 line 3029, fixtures must be suitable for the area classification in which they are installed. Yard-lighting fixture selection is therefore downstream of the area-classification drawing set, which is not part of this deliverable's source slices. Cross-reference to facility area-classification drawings is required before fixture selection is finalized.
- **Grounding/bonding interface.** The Grounding / Bonding interface (`IFC-DA0D60681B`) ties the yard-lighting system to the facility earth grid. Specific bonding requirements (mast bonding, fixture bonding, surge-protection earth) are not enumerated in source slices and belong to the Package Datasheet (`DEL-106-02`).
- **Electrical Power interface.** The Electrical Power interface (`IFC-6FCF1B30D6`) ties yard lighting to the facility power distribution. DBM-Deepcut SEC-12 line 3029 establishes 120/208 V general-purpose lighting from the nearest power distribution centre; whether yard-lighting feeders are 120/208 V branch circuits, dedicated lighting-panel feeders, or 600 V site distribution to remote pole-mounted transformers is not stated and belongs in `DEL-106-02`.
- **Construction-scope conflict.** DBM-Deepcut SEC-04 (line 120) lists "Area lighting" within the Tourmaline field construction scope. The workbook framing of PKG-106 as a vendor-owned Electrical package therefore conflicts with the field-construction-scope characterization. Captured as `CONF-106-01-01` below.
- **Emergency-lighting overlap.** DBM-Deepcut SEC-12 line 3031 governs emergency lighting "in each building" with battery backup — distinct from exterior yard lighting, but the boundary between "exterior emergency egress lighting at building exits" and "yard lighting" is not source-defined. Captured as `CONF-106-01-02`.
- **Light-pollution regulatory citation.** Source states regulatory light-pollution requirements apply but does not cite specific BCER conditions, municipal bylaws, or IDA guidelines. The SOW should retain the requirement set verbatim and mark the regulatory citation `location TBD`.

## Trade-offs

- **Specificity vs. source coverage.** Locally accessible source slices describe identity, responsibility, interface set, and qualitative design constraints (LED, downcast, hazardous-area-suitable, mast placement), but do not provide fixture catalogs, lux targets, or pole heights. SOW content should be specific where source supports it and explicitly `TBD` (with `location TBD`) elsewhere. Pushing more detail into the SOW than source supports compromises auditability.
- **EPC-authored vs. vendor-authored content.** This SOW is EPC-authored; technical design content (fixture selection, photometrics, pole structural design, fixture wattages) belongs in the Package Datasheet (`DEL-106-02`) and the vendor's engineered equipment package (`DEL-106-04`). Resist drift of vendor design content into this deliverable.
- **Interface enumeration vs. interface design.** Naming the 3 applicable interfaces satisfies the integration narrative requirement; interface *design* (feeder sizes, bonding conductor sizing, control wiring) belongs in the Package Datasheet and downstream EPC discipline deliverables.
- **Verbatim source vs. paraphrase.** Verbatim reproduction maximizes auditability; paraphrasing improves readability but risks subtle drift. Prefer verbatim for the SEC-12 light-pollution and hazardous-area sentences (lines 3029, 3035).

## Examples

Source-grounded statement (good):
> "All yard-lighting luminaires shall be LED type and shall be suitable for the area classification in which they are installed. Regulatory light-pollution requirements shall be addressed by LED lighting, selective minimization of exterior lighting to working areas, downward illumination such as downcast floodlights, prohibition of horizontally aimed floodlights, photocell or switch control to minimize emissions, and locating lighting mast poles away from the pad edge where mast poles are required (DBM-Deepcut SEC-12 lines 3029, 3035)."

Insufficiently grounded statement (avoid):
> "Yard lighting shall provide a minimum maintained illuminance of 20 lux at grade across the process area, with 50 lux at operator walking paths." — no locally accessible source slice supports specific lux targets; mark `TBD` with `location TBD` and defer to vendor photometric design.
