# Guidance — DEL-073-05 Vendor Document Turnover Package (PKG-073)

## Purpose

This guidance frames how the Vendor Document Turnover Package is to be assembled, interpreted, and reviewed for PKG-073 (Amine Treating Unit). It supports `OBJ-001`, `OBJ-003`, `OBJ-004`, `OBJ-005`, `OBJ-006`, `OBJ-007`, `OBJ-008`, `OBJ-009`, `OBJ-010` (objective association is a PACKAGE_HEURISTIC ASSUMPTION inherited from `_CONTEXT.md`) by ensuring the EPC Integrator receives a complete, traceable vendor documentation set sufficient for facility integration, operations, regulatory readiness, and life-cycle support of a sour-service MDEA amine plant.

## Principles

1. **Source-driven document list.** The applicable document classes come directly from `_Sources/26020-Package_Requirements.docx` heading 27 ("Vendor Engineering Deliverables") for the Amine Treating Unit. This skill does not invent new document classes for the PKG-073 turnover, and does not adopt classes from other package templates that heading 27 omits.
2. **No silent superset.** Heading 27 omits "Rotating equipment / compressors", "Rotating equipment / pumps", "Heat transfer equipment" subsections, and `ELE-011` (Motor Starting Study). Do not add these unless detailed design surfaces equipment that warrants them; if added, record the rationale and the source that triggered the addition.
3. **EPC Integrator as acceptor, not author.** Per `_CONTEXT.md` and `DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.csv` row 262, the Package Vendor authors; the EPC Integrator reviews via `DEL-073-06`. Acceptance is a human decision (K-AUTH-1).
4. **Traceability over volume.** The Vendor Document Index (`PRQ-009`) is the canonical contents list; every document submitted must appear in it with current revision and status. Volume alone is not acceptance.
5. **Codes and standards stay sourced.** Where the accessible source set does not cite a specific standard clause, the turnover set states the applicable standard at a category level and leaves clause-level claims to detailed-design documents (`location TBD`).
6. **Sour-service is a first-class concern.** The package process function is removal of H₂S and CO₂ from sour natural gas. Vendor materials, welding, inspection, and PWHT submittals must address sour-service requirements even though clause-level citations are TBD in accessible sources.

## Considerations

- **Mechanical discipline center with broad cross-discipline coverage.** PKG-073 is classified Mechanical (`PACKAGE_REGISTER.csv` row 49, WBS 01), but the ATU heading 27 enumerates Process, Static pressure equipment, Relief/flare, Piping, Drainage, Electrical, I&C, Fire and gas, and Structural document classes. Plan reviewer capacity accordingly; the EPC Integrator's `DEL-073-06` review touches every discipline.
- **Pressure equipment registration is explicit.** `REG-022` Pressure Equipment Registration Package is in the source list — this is uncommon among packages and reflects the multiple pressure vessels typical of an ATU (absorber, regenerator, reflux drum, flash drum). The jurisdiction and registration sequence belong in the PO and must be reflected in `DOC-008`.
- **Relief/flare and blowdown deserve early interface coordination.** ATU regeneration overhead carries acid gas and water; `PRO-017` Flare Load Summary and `PRO-018` Blowdown Study must reconcile with the facility-level relief and flare basis (an interface upstream of vendor authority).
- **Process safety information package required.** `PRO-027` PSI Package is in the source list. This is a regulator-facing artifact and is critical for HAZOP closure and `OBJ-007` (safety/regulatory readiness, per the conservative PACKAGE_HEURISTIC objective association).
- **Interface basis.** Per `PACKAGE_REGISTER.csv` row 49 and heading 27, applicable interface types include Process Piping; Utility Piping; Relief/Flare/Vent; Drain/Containment; Electrical Power; Area/Exterior Lighting; EHT; Grounding/Bonding; I&C/Control Cabling; Fire & Gas / Safety Systems; Maintenance Access; Structural/Foundations/Supports. Heading 27 explicitly lists Cathodic Protection, Communications/Network, Building HVAC/Services, Grading/Drainage/Spill Containment, Product Loading, and Pipeline/Pigging as NOT applicable.
- **Turnover gate.** Turnover records (`PRQ-016`, `MEC-023`, `ELE-030`, `QLT-020`, `QLT-021`, as-builts `PIP-028`/`INS-029`/`PRO-028`) are the critical evidence for `DEL-073-06` acceptance. Plan the FAT/SAT/energization milestones backwards from the EPC Integrator's acceptance gate.

## Trade-offs

- **Modular vendor scope vs. integrated facility behavior.** The ATU is a vendor-supplied package, but its relief, flare load, and PSI directly load facility-level systems. Compress vendor authority where the deliverable is genuinely a vendor responsibility (FAT, material certs, IOM); leave facility-level reconciliation to the EPC Integrator and capture as interface obligations on the Vendor Document Index.
- **Early submittal vs. as-built quality.** Releasing documents early gives the EPC Integrator time to coordinate facility-level integration but creates revisioning churn. `DOC-008` should establish a small number of named milestones (preliminary, certified-for-construction, as-built) rather than continuous trickle.
- **Standalone vendor IOM vs. integrated turnover book.** A combined `PRQ-016` book is easier for operations to use long-term; per-document standalone files are easier for vendor revisioning. Decide upfront and reflect in `DOC-008`.
- **Generic discipline applicability vs. ATU-specific reduction.** The heading 27 list is already curated for the ATU. Resist the temptation to copy the broader package template; conversely, surface any missing class as a Conflict Table entry rather than silently extending.

## Examples

- The source's heading 27 ATU section is the controlling template; sibling packages (e.g., PKG-019 MV VFD) instantiate a different reduction of the same generic template. Use the source heading directly; do not transplant document classes from sibling deliverables.
- DBM line 41 enumerates "selective MDEA amine treating" as one of the facility's emissions-reduction means, validating the ATU's role in `OBJ-007` (regulatory/emissions objectives under the conservative PACKAGE_HEURISTIC association).
