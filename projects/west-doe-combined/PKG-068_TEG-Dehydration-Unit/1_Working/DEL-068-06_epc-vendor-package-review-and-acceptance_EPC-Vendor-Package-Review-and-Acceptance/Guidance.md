# Guidance — DEL-068-06 EPC Vendor Package Review and Acceptance (TEG Dehydration Unit)

## Purpose

This deliverable provides the EPC Integrator's binding evidence that the Package Vendor's engineered package and turnover documentation for the TEG Dehydration Unit (`PKG-068`, one-by-100 percent sour-gas TEG unit downstream of inlet compression at 03-25) satisfy the EPC anchor deliverables — Scope of Work (`DEL-068-01`), Package Datasheet (`DEL-068-02`), and Construction Work Package (`DEL-068-03`) — and are ready for integration into the broader 03-25 / 04-25 process system.

Source: `_Sources/DBM-Comp_and_Liquids/3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md` SEC-05 ("TEG Dehydration Basis", "TEG Package Equipment"); `_Sources/26020-Package_Requirements.docx` heading 23 (location TBD at sub-heading level); `_CONTEXT.md`; `DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.csv`.

## Principles

- **Acceptance is integrator-led, not vendor-self-certified.** `_CONTEXT.md` and the decomposition register assign EPC Integrator as the lead responsible party with Package Vendor input.
- **Evidence is anchored to the source's enumerated artifacts.** The heading 23 "Vendor Engineering Deliverables" list (binary; sub-list TBD) and the SEC-05 "TEG Package Equipment" enumeration are the canonical artifact and equipment sets. Acceptance does not invent new artifact requirements; it confirms the presence and adequacy of those artifacts and that the as-built package matches the enumerated equipment scope.
- **Numeric design values come from vendor submittals reconciled to SEC-05.** SEC-05 supplies the design basis (e.g., gas flow 82.5 MMSCFD; inlet pressures 4,502 / 5,378 / 4,502 / 6,205 kPag; outlet water 4 lb H2O/MMSCF; contactor at least three theoretical stages with Fs not more than 3.0; surge drum 30 min retention at 50 psig). Vendor submittals must reconcile to these values; deviations are flagged, not silently absorbed.
- **No agent or vendor self-certification of binding acceptance.** Binding acceptance sign-off is human-authored per `K-AUTH-1`.
- **Source-grounded TBDs over invention.** Where the binary source slice (heading 23) cannot be read in this drafting run, requirements that depend on it (the enumerated vendor deliverables list, the open-items list, the per-interface flag map for Packages row 97) are carried as `TBD` with `location TBD`, not invented.

## Considerations

- **HP-flare / LP-flare interfaces.** Contactor blowdown is automated to the HP flare; regen overhead routes to the LP flare via LP KO drum `V-3900-2`. Acceptance must confirm both interfaces and cross-reference the respective flare-package deliverables (PKG-054 HP flare KO and the LP flare KO drum package).
- **Flash-gas routing under SCA basis.** Flash gas is pressure-regulated to 04-25 SOC first-stage suction under the current SCA basis. Acceptance must confirm the pressure-regulation hardware, set points, and the cross-facility 03-25 → 04-25 interface acceptance.
- **Makeup tank exclusion from VRU.** SEC-05 explicitly states the TEG makeup tank is fuel-gas blanketed and NOT connected to the VRU. Acceptance must verify this exclusion at walk-down; reviewer should flag any vendor drawing that routes the makeup tank to VRU.
- **Pressure equipment registration.** The TEG package contains multiple coded vessels (contactor, flash drum, surge drum, regen overhead scrubber; potentially still/stripping/reflux/reboiler shells). Acceptance is contingent on registration acceptance by the applicable jurisdictional authority. (ASSUMPTION — authority identity, e.g., ABSA, not stated in available source slices.)
- **Rotating-equipment subset.** TEG pumps (2 x 100 percent, rotary gear or PD, single mechanical seals), regen overhead pumps, and makeup pump form a rotating-equipment sub-scope. Treat their acceptance evidence (FAT, NPSH, seal/lube, motor starting, vibration where applicable) as a dedicated subsection of the acceptance pack rather than mixed with vessel evidence.
- **Filtration and contactor internals.** Source specifies 5 micron full-flow rich-TEG filtration plus a 20 percent carbon/particle slipstream; contactor with at least three theoretical stages, Fs not more than 3.0, and inlet/outlet demisters. Acceptance must confirm internals match these specs.
- **Control-system integration.** Per the DBM control-system architecture, the TEG package PLC / Unit Control System replicates values and alarms to the BPCS; Modbus via Kepware KepserverEX where applicable. Final package data maps, permissive logic, and trip interfaces are resolved during vendor integration — this acceptance pack must close those items.
- **Open-items closure vs. carryover.** Items that cannot be closed before package handoff should be explicitly carried with named owners rather than left silent; this preserves traceability into commissioning. Detector-placement and final trip-list items called out as TBD in SEC-15 / SEC-16 for the TEG package vicinity are typically handled in detailed design / safety studies and should not be re-opened in acceptance unless they bound the package's deliverable boundary.
- **Binary-source recovery.** The `26020-Package_Requirements.docx` heading 23 source slice was not directly readable during initial drafting. Acceptance work should not begin without the heading 23 sub-list extracted; this is the canonical "Vendor Engineering Deliverables" enumeration and the "Scope Notes / Open Items" source for `R-068-06-02` and `R-068-06-12`.

## Trade-offs

- **Depth of vendor re-engineering review vs. timeline.** EPC Integrator review must be deep enough to find configuration mismatches against `DEL-068-02` but cannot substitute for vendor engineering. The default disposition for purely vendor-engineering details with consistent source-grounded inputs is "accepted on evidence" rather than re-derivation.
- **Single acceptance bundle vs. staged acceptance.** A staged approach (engineering acceptance → fabrication acceptance → turnover acceptance) aligns with the artifact maturity progression typical of vendor packages. (ASSUMPTION — staging not mandated in available source slices.)
- **Reconcile-to-vendor vs. reconcile-to-DBM precedence.** Source material (SEC-05) is authoritative over decomposition prose, and vendor submittals are authoritative over the EPC Package Datasheet for as-built behavior. Variances must be flagged and resolved through change control, not silently absorbed into the acceptance pack.

## Examples

The acceptance checklist should mirror the SEC-05 "TEG Package Equipment" grouping (inlet/contactor train; flash drum and downstream filtration; regeneration train including still/stripping/reflux/reboiler/surge drum/regen cooler/regen scrubber/regen overhead pumps; makeup tank and pump; lean/rich exchanger; lean TEG cooler) so reviewers can audit one functional sub-system at a time. Cross-functional checklists (process tie-ins, utility tie-ins, electrical, I&C, structural) cut across the equipment groupings and should be tracked as separate matrices.
