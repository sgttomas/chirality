# Specification — DEL-064-01 Scope of Work (Tanks, Water (API 650) 4-25)

> Normative EPC Integrator Scope of Work for PKG-064 Tanks, Water (API 650) 4-25 (Process Water Storage).
> Pass 1/Pass 2 draft. Requirements are source-grounded where the locally accessible DBM material supports them. Unsupported claims are marked `TBD`; conservative inferences are labeled `ASSUMPTION`.

## Scope

This Scope of Work covers EPC Integrator responsibilities for the PKG-064 Tanks, Water (API 650) 4-25 package at the 04-25 Deep Cut Gas Plant (West Doe Deepcut expansion). The scope includes:

- definition of tagged equipment and package identity for the two atmospheric API 650 process water storage tanks (TK-5317-1 and TK-5318-1);
- package function, source-of-design basis, operating envelope, and battery-limit boundaries for the process water storage service;
- the whole-facility integration narrative tying the process water storage tanks to the caustic NGL contactor water-wash make-up service, the Module 530 amine regeneration module process-water service, the downstream Process Water Transfer Pumps interface, the tank-farm spacing and freeze-protection basis, and the containment-berm interface;
- the responsibility assignment record between EPC Integrator and the Package Vendor for engineering, supply, documentation, and acceptance.

Source: `_CONTEXT.md` Scope; DBM-Deepcut §SEC-05 (Module 530 amine regeneration), §SEC-07 (NGL caustic treating water wash), §SEC-10 (water tank freeze protection), §SEC-16 (Workbook Packages row 102; deliverable rows 83 and 102).

Excluded from this deliverable: the vendor-engineered tank package itself (DEL-064-04), vendor documentation register (DEL-064-05), construction work package (DEL-064-03), vendor package acceptance evidence (DEL-064-06), and the EPC Package Datasheet (DEL-064-02). The Process Water Transfer Pumps (P-5317-1, P-5318-1) are part of Tank Farm Pump Building 2 and are outside PKG-064 package boundary. Containment berms are governed by PKG-006. Source: GATE-07 DELIVERABLE_REGISTER and PACKAGE_REGISTER rows for PKG-064 and PKG-006.

## Requirements

### R-064-01-01 — Package configuration
The package shall consist of two (2) atmospheric API 650 welded steel storage tanks dedicated to process water service, identified as TK-5317-1 and TK-5318-1. Source: DBM-Deepcut §SEC-16 Workbook Packages row 102 ("Tanks, Water (API 650) 2 / PROCESS WATER STORAGE TANK (x2, 4-25)"); deliverable row 102.

### R-064-01-02 — Service and function
The package shall provide bulk atmospheric storage of process water for the 04-25 Deep Cut Gas Plant. The tanks shall be capable of supplying make-up water to the caustic NGL contactor water-wash system during upset operation and shall support the process-water storage and transfer service associated with the Module 530 amine regeneration module/building. Source: DBM-Deepcut §SEC-07 ("Make-up water, if required during upset operation, is supplied from the process water storage tank. Final make-up water routing remains to be confirmed by detailed engineering."); §SEC-05 Module 530 line ("process-water storage and transfer").

### R-064-01-03 — Code basis
Each tank shall be designed, fabricated, inspected, and tested in accordance with API 650 (atmospheric welded steel storage tanks). Edition, addenda, and jurisdictional registration requirements are TBD pending detailed engineering and provincial AB/BC pressure-equipment authority confirmation. Source: DBM-Deepcut §SEC-16 package class "Tanks, Water (API 650) 2"; ASSUMPTION on jurisdiction based on BC site location (DBM-Deepcut §SEC-01).

### R-064-01-04 — Tank capacity
Nominal working volume and total volume per tank are TBD; the accessible DBM source slice does not state a process-water tank capacity. The package shall be sized for the process-water demand of the caustic NGL contactor water-wash make-up service and the Module 530 process-water service, with final capacity confirmed during detailed engineering. Source: capacity not stated in accessible source — `location TBD`.

### R-064-01-05 — Freeze protection
The process water tanks shall be insulated to prevent winter freezing. The specific insulation system (type, thickness, jacketing) and any heat tracing or heater requirements are TBD. Source: DBM-Deepcut §SEC-10 ("water tanks shall be insulated to prevent winter freezing").

### R-064-01-06 — Overpressure and vacuum protection
Each tank shall be fitted with overpressure and vacuum protection consistent with API 650 / API 2000 atmospheric storage practice. Specific PVRV / EPRV sizing and quantities are TBD for the process water tanks; the produced water tanks specify at least one PVRV per tank in DBM-Deepcut §SEC-03 — applicability to the process water tanks is ASSUMPTION pending detailed engineering. Source: §SEC-03 (produced water tank PVRV statement); `location TBD` for PKG-064 specifics.

### R-064-01-07 — Internal coating and corrosion control
Internal coating and external corrosion control requirements for the process water tanks are TBD. The DBM specifies an internal coating (Devchem 253) for produced water tanks in §SEC-03; equivalent specification for process water service is not stated in the accessible source slice. Source: §SEC-03 (produced water coating reference); `location TBD` for PKG-064.

### R-064-01-08 — Spacing and siting
Tank siting within the 04-25 tank farm shall comply with the applicable atmospheric tank spacing rules summarized in DBM-Deepcut §SEC-02, including:

- 2.35 m (7.72 ft) minimum between adjacent atmospheric tanks per NFPA 30 Table 22.4.2.1;
- 80 m (262.5 ft) minimum from atmospheric tanks to the nearest public road per OGAOM Sec. 9.6.15, DPR 48;
- Flare-to-tank and fired-equipment-to-tank spacing per the §SEC-02 spacing table (the 25 m flare-to-atmospheric-produced-water-tank figure may apply by analogy — ASSUMPTION pending discipline review).

Source: DBM-Deepcut §SEC-02 Atmospheric Tank and General Plant Spacing table.

### R-064-01-09 — Containment interface
Secondary containment for the process water tanks shall be coordinated with the project containment-berms package (PKG-006). Containment design, berm height, sizing for spill volume, and drainage are NOT within PKG-064 scope. Source: GATE-07 PACKAGE_REGISTER (PKG-006 entry); not stated as PKG-064 scope in accessible source.

### R-064-01-10 — Downstream transfer interface
The package shall provide outlet nozzle(s) and isolation suitable for connection to the PROCESS WATER TRANSFER PUMPS (P-5317-1, P-5318-1) located in Tank Farm Pump Building 2. The transfer pumps themselves are NOT within PKG-064 scope. Nozzle sizing, elevation, and isolation arrangement are TBD pending detailed engineering. Source: DBM-Deepcut §SEC-16 deliverable row 83 (transfer pump building); `location TBD` for nozzle details.

### R-064-01-11 — Drains and instrumentation
Each tank shall be provided with drain, level, and temperature instrumentation suitable for atmospheric water service. Specific instrumentation tag list, drain header routing, and any sampling provisions are TBD; the drain-system framework is summarized in DBM-Deepcut §SEC-08 and §SEC-10 but specific process-water-tank drain routing is not stated. Source: DBM-Deepcut §SEC-08 (drain framework); `location TBD` for PKG-064 drain specifics.

### R-064-01-12 — Tag list
The EPC Integrator shall maintain a tagged equipment list for PKG-064. Confirmed tags from the accessible source slice are TK-5317-1 and TK-5318-1. Additional ancillary tag numbers (nozzles, valves, instruments) are TBD pending detailed engineering and facility tag-register issuance. Source: DBM-Deepcut §SEC-16 deliverable row 102.

### R-064-01-13 — Battery-limit interfaces
The package shall integrate with the following plant systems at defined battery limits:

- Outlet tie-in to the Process Water Transfer Pumps suction (P-5317-1, P-5318-1, Tank Farm Pump Building 2);
- Make-up / fill source(s) to the tanks (source and routing TBD; see §SEC-07 reference to upset make-up routing remaining open);
- Vent tie-in (atmospheric vents via PVRV/EPRV — design TBD);
- Drain tie-in to the appropriate plant drain header (routing TBD per §SEC-08);
- Tank-farm secondary containment (PKG-006 interface);
- Civil tie-in (foundations, ringwall, access platforms — PKG-064 vendor scope or civil discipline scope to be confirmed in DEL-064-02);
- Electrical and instrumentation tie-ins for any heat tracing, level, and temperature instruments (scope TBD).

Exact tag numbers, line sizes, and isolation point locations are TBD pending detailed engineering. Source: DBM-Deepcut §SEC-05, §SEC-07, §SEC-08, §SEC-10, §SEC-16; ASSUMPTION: battery-limit list is the conservative integration set implied by the source narrative.
