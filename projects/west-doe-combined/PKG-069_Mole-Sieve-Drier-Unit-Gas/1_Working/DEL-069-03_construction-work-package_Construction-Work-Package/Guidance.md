# Guidance: DEL-069-03_construction-work-package

## Purpose

The Construction Work Package exists to convert the accepted Gate 7 package basis for `PKG-069` into a construction-facing deliverable that describes how the Mole Sieve Drier Unit (Gas) is physically installed, built, inspected, turned over, and tied into the larger facility systems. It should make EPC construction and turnover scope explicit while keeping vendor-owned package design with the Package Vendor and explicitly coordinating the construction boundary with the cryogenic unit package per the Gate 6 scope-coupling disposition.

## Principles

- Preserve source spelling and identity. The package name is carried as "Mole Sieve Drier Unit (Gas)" because that is the workbook and Gate 7 register spelling. The DBM uses "molecular sieve" interchangeably; the construction work package should use the workbook spelling as the primary identifier and treat "molecular sieve" as the DBM-side technical equivalent.
- Treat the Construction Work Package as construction-facing only. Do not redefine package design values (cycle times, bed loadings, regeneration temperatures, MRU media life, pressure-class basis); accept the Package Datasheet (`DEL-069-02`) as the technical handoff basis.
- Treat workbook interface `X` facts as construction-facing tie-in scope items, covering all twelve `PKG-069` interfaces.
- Explicitly state the construction boundary with the cryogenic unit package. The Gate 6 disposition couples Gas Mole Sieve scope with the Cryogenic Unit package scope; the construction work package must specify how shared interfaces (regeneration return, dry-out header, dust filter / MRU train, BAHX trip interlock, dried-gas tie-in) are constructed, tested, and turned over without scope gaps or duplication.
- Keep vendor-owned design work with the Package Vendor and facility-level construction integration with the EPC Integrator.
- Use `TBD` for installation plot-plan location, modularization/shipping split, lifting plan, ITPs, hold points, package-specific feeder/conductor/conduit detail, EHT circuit list, foundation/support detail, building HVAC/F&G/communications tie-in detail, adsorbent-loading procedure, mercury-service handling procedure, and detailed turnover-checklist content until a source-supported basis is available.
- Use the DBM construction and process basis only at the level it supports: Tourmaline-assigned construction scope, the multilevel outdoor module placement adjacent to the cryogenic modules, the inlet-coalescer module/building, the regeneration scrubber installed inside a building, the 900# flange basis for molecular sieve system piping, the BAHX 66 degC temperature-protection trip on bed-bring-online, the operator-initiated HMI blowdown configuration (50 psi/min limit), the 3A adsorbent / silica gel protective layer / mercury-service basis, and the requirement for pre-issue alignment to plot plan, equipment list, and construction work package register.

## Considerations

The DBM construction scope (assigned to Tourmaline Oil Corporation) explicitly includes construction management, grading, piling, foundations, field-erected buildings, shipping and off-loading of modules, setting modules and equipment on foundations, mechanical hookup of modules and interconnecting piping, installation of shipped-loose instruments/valves/components, miscellaneous structural supports, home-run cabling, electrical terminations, and area lighting. These activities are directionally applicable to installing `PKG-069`, but the source does not assign quantities, sequences, or package-specific construction steps.

The DBM identifies the molecular-sieve unit as the final gas-phase dehydration step upstream of cryogenic recovery, with three driers (two in adsorption mode, one in standby/regeneration/cooling), dried-gas flow through a dust filter and the mercury recovery unit before entering the cryogenic unit. The construction work package should respect this process layout while preserving the Package Datasheet position that detailed bed sizing, cycle times, and equipment counts come from the vendor and the upstream datasheet.

The DBM explicitly states the molecular sieve system requires 900# flanges (in contrast to the surrounding 600# basis up to the J-T valve/expander outlet isolation). This is a non-trivial construction-facing requirement: bolt-up procedures, gasket selection, material verification, torque values, and pressure-test plans must respect the 900# rating. Mixing piping classes during construction is a known integrity risk that the construction work package should explicitly guard against.

The DBM defines two safety-critical construction-facing items that must be preserved during installation and commissioning: (a) the BAHX temperature-protection trip — when a freshly regenerated bed is brought online, gas temperature must not exceed 66 degC or the facility shuts down; and (b) the blowdown configuration — operator-initiated HMI only, two blowdown valves for regeneration and adsorption loops, 50 psi/min rate limit at maximum inlet pressure, and a regeneration-compressor bypass to prevent reverse rotation. The construction work package should require loop checks, cause-and-effect verification, tag verification, and physical bypass piping confirmation for these items.

Mercury service introduces handling, decontamination, and disposal considerations not present on other vendor-owned mechanical packages. The MRU vessel media is sulphur-impregnated activated carbon with a guaranteed life of at least six years; any field activity that opens MRU-side equipment or potentially mercury-exposed downstream piping (e.g., during dust-filter change-out or piping modifications) must follow project HSE and applicable regulatory handling provisions.

Maintenance access is both an explicit workbook interface fact and a DBM constraint for the multilevel module. Vessel/bed change-out access, MRU media change-out access, dust-filter element change-out access, and inlet-coalescer element change-out access drive the placement of cable trays, conduit, instrument tubing, EHT circuits, and platforms. Detailed clearances remain `TBD` unless issued by detailed design or vendor general arrangement.

Structural/foundation/support requirements depend on the final geotechnical report. The DBM site/environmental section explicitly states soil type and maximum wind speed are TBC pending the geotechnical report, and that the climate basis is Dawson Creek (NBCC 2020 IDF). The construction work package should treat geotechnical acceptance as a hard prerequisite for issue for construction rather than a downstream check.

The twelve interfaces for `PKG-069` include several building-services topics (Building HVAC / Services, Fire & Gas / Safety Systems, Area / Exterior Lighting, Drain / Containment, Relief / Flare / Vent) plus EHT, which is not present on some simpler packages. The construction work package should require explicit coordination of these tie-ins between the package and the corresponding facility scope, without inventing detail that is not in source material.

Construction interface and turnover evidence will be consumed by the downstream EPC Vendor Package Review and Acceptance deliverable (`DEL-069-06`) and complemented by the Vendor Document Turnover Package (`DEL-069-05`). The construction work package should be authored so that its turnover checklist is directly usable by those reviews and so that the construction boundary with the cryogenic unit package is explicit and inspectable.

Bolt-up records shall reflect the 900# rating. Source: `_Sources/DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md`, line 628."
- Acceptable construction work package entry: "Operator-initiated HMI blowdown only; two blowdown valves provided for regeneration and adsorption loops; blowdown rate limited to 50 psi/min at maximum inlet pressure; regeneration compressor bypass shall be physically installed and tagged. Source: `_Sources/DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md`, Molecular-Sieve Equipment, Controls, and Protection (Blowdown)."
- Acceptable source-gap entry: "Lifting plan and set-on-foundation sequence for the multilevel molecular sieve module: TBD. No package-specific source slice available; vendor general arrangement pending."
- Not acceptable without new source: "The molecular sieve module shall be set on a 16 m x 8 m piled foundation at coordinate N+250, E+120, with the regeneration compressor on the south side and inlet coalescer building on the north side." The accessible source set does not establish this location, dimension, or equipment layout.
