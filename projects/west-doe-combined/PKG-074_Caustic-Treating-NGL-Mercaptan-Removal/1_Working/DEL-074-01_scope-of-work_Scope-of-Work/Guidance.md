# Guidance — DEL-074-01 Scope of Work (PKG-074 Caustic Treating, NGL Mercaptan Removal)

> Directional guidance for the EPC Integrator preparing and using the PKG-074 Scope of Work. Anchored to the 04-25 Deepcut DBM SEC-07 (NGL mercaptan treating), SCOPE_LEDGER SOW-0059 through SOW-0062, and PKG-074 objective set.

## Purpose

PKG-074 is a Mandatory Gate 5 EPC anchor deliverable. The Scope of Work establishes the package boundary, function, and responsibility split that downstream PKG-074 deliverables (Package Datasheet, Construction Work Package, vendor engineering, vendor document turnover, and EPC review/acceptance) all depend on. It is the EPC Integrator's first authoritative statement that resolves *what is in the box* and *who owns which surface of the box* for the NGL mercaptan treating unit at the 04-25 Deepcut Gas Plant. (`_CONTEXT.md`; DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.csv; SOW-0059)

## Principles

1. **Source-anchored package definition.** The package identity, equipment categories, and tagged item count come from `26020-Package_Requirements.docx` package heading 28 (referenced) and Workbook Packages row 51. The current-scope process basis comes from `4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md` SEC-07. Where the source defers a value, this Scope of Work carries the deferral rather than invent a number. (DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.csv; 4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md SEC-07)
2. **Vendor-engineered, EPC-integrated.** OBJ-004 governs the responsibility model: Package Vendor owns engineering, design, fabrication, equipment supply, and vendor documentation; EPC Integrator owns facility integration, interface coordination, integration review, and turnover acceptance. The Scope of Work must not assign vendor design work to the EPC Integrator. (OBJ-004; DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.csv DEL-074-04)
3. **Current basis is non-regenerative.** The 04-25 DBM SEC-07 declares the unit as non-regenerative with no active on-site caustic regeneration column. The SCOPE_LEDGER SOW-0062 flags an open conflict with an alternate regenerative basis. The Scope of Work carries non-regenerative as the default while requiring vendor/process-provider confirmation of the governing basis. (4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md SEC-07; SOW-0062; Conflict Table CFT-001)
4. **Caustic-service discipline.** Caustic-containing equipment is installed indoors due to freezing/crystallization risk; aluminum is prohibited in the caustic building; insulation cladding/straps in caustic exposure areas are stainless steel; tank materials/coatings are polymer or other caustic-compatible material. These are non-negotiable material constraints from the source basis. (4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md "NGL Mercaptan Treating Equipment and Utilities")
5. **Interfaces are first-class.** The package consumes feed from the de-ethanizer outlet and produces treated NGL to filtration/water wash/mole-sieve dehydration/storage. It exports vapours (caustic drain drum to SOC first-stage suction; spent caustic and DSO to the 3-25 incinerator), spent caustic and DSO to truck-out, and water wash drain to produced water. These interfaces frame downstream interface-matrix work in DEL-074-02. (4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md SEC-07 and "Incinerator Interface")
6. **Flow down cross-cutting facility requirements.** Safety (OBJ-009), controls and instrumentation (OBJ-006), electrical (OBJ-005), utilities (OBJ-007), civil/structural (OBJ-008), and operability/handoff (OBJ-010) requirements are facility-wide; the Scope of Work points the Package Vendor at them rather than re-derives them.

## Considerations

- **Tagged equipment closure.** SOW-0062 records thirteen tagged items in heading 28 with one duplicated tag requiring reconciliation. The Scope of Work cannot finalize the tag list without the heading 28 slice; this is recorded as REQ-074-01-021 and flagged in the Conflict Table.
- **Process provider selection drives detailed values.** A third-party proprietary process provider supplies the detailed engineering package (e.g., Merichem-type technology is named in the 03-25 DBM for the condensate analogue, but the 04-25 DBM does not name a 04-25 NGL unit provider). Several design values (contactor stages, circulating caustic concentration, water make-up routing, winter vapour pressure cases) are explicitly deferred to that selection. (4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md SEC-07; 3-25 DBM "Condensate Mercaptan Treating" provides analogue context only, not authority for the 04-25 unit)
- **Incinerator is shared with 3-25.** Spent caustic and DSO vapour routing terminates at the incinerator physically located at the 3-25 facility near the flare stacks. Operational responsibility for the shared incinerator and supplemental fuel-gas rate are `TBD`. The Scope of Work should record this as an inter-facility responsibility surface, not a PKG-074-only item. (4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md "Incinerator Interface")
- **Sales-spec coupling.** Treated NGL C3+ product must support sales-product volatile-mercaptan spec of < 175 wppm S. The 04-25 DBM treating performance case (970 ppmw total S at 1 mol% H2S inlet) clears the volatile-mercaptan spec by design but does not change total sulphur limits; the Scope of Work should keep this coupling visible. (4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md "NGL C3+ Product" and NGL sulphur case table)
- **Drain and caustic-service hazards.** Caustic drains, methyl-mercaptan toxicity, and stainless-steel embrittlement concerns are flagged at the facility level (3-25 DBM SEC drains discussion) and are relevant to PKG-074 building, drain, and detection design. The Scope of Work should require the Package Vendor to address these per the facility HAZOP and SIL outcomes.

## Trade-offs

| Trade-off | Discussion | Source / status |
|---|---|---|
| Regenerative vs. non-regenerative caustic basis | Non-regenerative is the current 04-25 DBM basis (no on-site regen column). A regenerative variant in SCA-001/VE-8 would change equipment count, utility loads, DSO handling, and incinerator duty. | SOW-0062; 4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md SEC-07; CFT-001 |
| DSO truck-out vs. blending into C5+ product | Source basis is truck-out; blending into C5+ is permitted only after detailed engineering review. Blending reduces truck-out logistics but raises C5+ quality risk. | 4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md "Disulphide Oil, Spent Caustic, and Waste Amine" |
| Process provider selection now vs. later | Selecting the proprietary process provider earlier locks in contactor staging and caustic chemistry; selecting later preserves vendor competition. | 4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md SEC-07 (provider selection `TBD`) |
| Caustic regeneration equipment line in SOW-0061 | SOW-0061 enumerates "caustic regeneration equipment" as a major included equipment category, while 04-25 DBM SEC-07 states the current basis has no active on-site regeneration column. The Scope of Work must reconcile the literal SOW-0061 list against the DBM current basis. | SOW-0061 vs. 4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md SEC-07; CFT-002 |

## Examples

- The 03-25 condensate analogue (PKG-088) uses a Merichem-or-equivalent non-regenerative caustic treating package for 20,000 bbl/d of C5+ condensate, with similar fresh/spent caustic and DSO truck-out architecture. This is *analogue context only*; the 04-25 NGL unit basis (15,000 bbl/d C3+) governs PKG-074. (3-25_Comp_and_Liquids_DBM.md "Condensate Mercaptan Treating")
