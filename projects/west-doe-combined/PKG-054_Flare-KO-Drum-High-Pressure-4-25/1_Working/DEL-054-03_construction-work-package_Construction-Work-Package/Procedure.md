# Procedure — Construction Work Package (DEL-054-03)

## Purpose

This Procedure defines the field execution and turnover steps for installing **Module 410-1 HP / Cryo Flare KO Drum Module** (housing V-4100-1 HP Flare KO Drum and P-4100-1 HP Flare KO Drum Transfer Pump) at the 04-25 Deepcut facility, tying it into the HP flare relief header, transfer-pump truck-out, and supporting utility/instrument systems, and producing the construction interface and turnover checklist for Gate 5 handoff.

## Prerequisites

1. **Upstream deliverable readiness (DECLARED upstream is currently "None declared", but ASSUMPTION-level dependencies apply):**
   - Civil foundation for Module 410-1 placement: accepted by the relevant civil package (ASSUMPTION — package ID TBD; see _DEPENDENCIES.md note: declared upstreams are empty as of PREPARATION).
   - Shop-fabricated Module 410-1 received on site with vendor data book and as-shipped test records (Package Vendor scope, OBJ-004).
   - Site grading, drainage, and access roads complete enough to permit module delivery and crane set-up (civil scope, OBJ-008).
   - HP relief header upstream and combined HP/cryo header tie-in stubs available at correct elevation and orientation (piping scope).
2. **References available on site:**
   - DBM-Deepcut/4-25_Deepcut_DBM.md (flare systems basis).
   - Module 410-1 vendor data book and certified P&IDs / isometrics.
   - 26020-Package_Requirements.docx package heading 9 (source slice location TBD).
   - This deliverable's Specification.md and Guidance.md.
3. **Permits and plans:**
   - Hot work, confined space, and lift permits per site procedures.
   - Lift plan for Module 410-1 (engineered lift if mass/dimensions warrant — TBD).
   - Workface plan integrating piping, electrical, instrumentation, and insulation crews.
4. **Spacing verification:**
   - Pre-set survey confirming planned module location meets OGAOM Sec. 9.6.15 distances (>=10 m to vegetation and other fire hazards) and BCER radiation envelope criteria.

## Steps

### Phase 1 — Receipt and Pre-Set

1. Receive Module 410-1 on site; verify vendor data book completeness, shipping damage inspection, and matchmark integrity. Document discrepancies.
2. Confirm foundation acceptance from the civil package (anchor bolt survey, elevation, level, grout readiness). Sign foundation acceptance row on the construction interface checklist.
3. Verify spacing of planned set point versus OGAOM 10 m clearances and BCER radiation envelope. Record survey results.

### Phase 2 — Module Set

4. Execute the engineered lift plan for Module 410-1. Capture lift records.
5. Set, align, level, and grout Module 410-1 on its foundation. Record final survey position.
6. Install permanent anchorage per vendor instructions.

### Phase 3 — Process Tie-Ins

7. Complete tie-in of the 508 mm (20 in) HP relief header to V-4100-1 inlet (per certified isometrics and material spec: SA-333 segments per DBM Flare Header and Backpressure Basis).
8. Complete tie-in of V-4100-1 outlet to the combined HP/cryo header (304SS per DBM). Confirm routing preserves the header backpressure basis (R-054-03-05).
9. Complete tie-in of P-4100-1 discharge to the truck-out connection(s).
10. Verify all PSV outlet free-drain orientations entering the HP flare header (no traps or pockets).
11. Pressure test newly fabricated field welds per applicable code and project test plan (TBD test medium and pressures).
12. Perform PMI/MTR verification on a documented sample of installed field-piping materials.

### Phase 4 — Utility, Electrical, and Instrument Tie-Ins

13. Install and energize electric heat trace on HP flare header sections outside heated buildings (excluding PSV outlets that free-drain into the header). Verify continuity and controller setpoints.
14. Complete insulation on heat-traced runs and on equipment per insulation spec.
15. Connect P-4100-1 motor power, control wiring, local instrumentation; verify rotation and protection settings.
16. Connect module instrumentation (level, pressure, temperature, fire/gas as applicable) to the host control system. Perform loop checks.
17. Tie in instrument air, fuel gas pilot/purge, and any other utility services per vendor list (exact services TBD).

### Phase 5 — Inspection, Punch List, and Turnover

18. Walkdown installation against certified P&IDs and isometrics with the EPC Integrator and operations representative. Generate punch list.
19. Resolve A-priority punch items before mechanical completion; track B/C items into commissioning.
20. Compile the **construction interface and turnover checklist** with signed rows for: foundation acceptance, module set, process tie-ins, electrical tie-ins, instrument tie-ins, heat trace, insulation, pressure tests, NDE, lift records, spacing/radiation verification, sour-service safety items (OBJ-009), and as-built redlines.
21. Hand over the completed turnover package (vendor data book + construction records) to the EPC Integrator for Gate 5 / pre-commissioning.
