# Specification — Construction Work Package (DEL-054-03)

## Scope

This Construction Work Package (CWP) defines how the **PKG-054 Flare KO Drum (High Pressure) 4-25** scope is physically installed at the 04-25 Deepcut facility, inspected, turned over, and tied into the larger facility flare and utility systems. The CWP covers field execution of the shop-fabricated **Module 410-1 HP / Cryo Flare KO Drum Module** (housing HP Flare KO Drum **V-4100-1** and transfer pump **P-4100-1**), including foundation setting, mechanical and process tie-ins, electrical and instrument tie-ins, heat tracing and insulation completion, pressure testing, inspection, and pre-commissioning handoff to the EPC Integrator and operations.

### Out of scope (executed by other deliverables/packages)

- Vendor package engineering, design, fabrication, and shop assembly of Module 410-1 (Package Vendor scope per OBJ-004).
- Site civil grading, foundation design, and earthworks (separate civil packages PKG-001..PKG-005 family).
- Detailed relief volume determination and flare system analyzer modeling (DBM-level / detailed engineering scope).
- Construction of the common HP/cryo flare stack (located at 03-25, shared scope; not within PKG-054).
