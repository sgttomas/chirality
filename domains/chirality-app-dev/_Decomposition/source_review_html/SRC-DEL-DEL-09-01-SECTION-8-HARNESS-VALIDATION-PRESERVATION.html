A preservation change should cover this chain, not just the Node scripts. - The current stable summary records eight passing rows. Historical provenance summaries are suitable shape references, while release-significant acceptance needs current evidence for the local checks named by `docs/SPEC.md` Section 19.1 and `docs/CONTRACT.md` K-VALIDATE-1.
