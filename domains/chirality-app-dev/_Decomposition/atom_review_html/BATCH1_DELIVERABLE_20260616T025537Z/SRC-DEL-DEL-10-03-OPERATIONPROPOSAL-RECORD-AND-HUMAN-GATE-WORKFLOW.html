SEC-ED051-0012 Principles

- Treat `OperationProposal` as a future proposal and audit record, not as permission to execute a domain operation by itself. - Keep authoritative domain truth in the domain engine. Chirality governs interaction, proposals, records, review aids, and human gates. - Keep protected domain paths separate from agent-writable proposal paths. - Require deterministic checks before a future operation can be considered review-ready. - Require explicit human acceptance before a future operation is applied. - Preserve professional-boundary language: Chirality must not claim professional approval, code compliance, external validation, or solver truth ownership.
