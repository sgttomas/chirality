Keep expected values and final tolerances as `TBD` until the responsible authority accepts them. 7. Wire accepted cases into the approved regression harness only after source, unit, diagnostic, and tolerance gates are satisfied. 8. Record benchmark outputs as mechanics verification evidence only; do not claim code compliance, certification, sealing, approval, or project-specific professional reliance.
