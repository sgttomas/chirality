**Surface Condenser**

The surface condenser is an integral part of a condensing turbine installation. It is a shell-and-tube type exchanger, usually with a fixed tube sheet (nonremovable tubes). Figure 2-8 shows a mezzanine-mounted turbine exhausting down to its surface condenser and a grade-mounted turbine exhausting up to a grade-mounted surface condenser. In both installations an expansion joint is utilized to minimize forces and stresses on the turbine nozzle.

Also shown are a side elevation of a surface condenser, defining various parts and three types of continuously used relief valves attached to the surface condenser. The exhaust steam system and surface condenser operate on a vacuum and the relief valve is designed to open at the slightest positive pressure above atmospheric pressure. Note that each relief valve has a water seal, a continuous water supply and overflow which must be routed to a drain funnel.

Figure 2-9 diagramatically describes how vacuum is maintained in the surface condenser by continuous removal of air by ejectors.
