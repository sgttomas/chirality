## Heater Instrumentation

Certain minimum instrumentation is necessary to ensure proper check and control of heater performance whether box or vertical type. Figure 3-29 indicates usual instrument connection locations. Item 4, flue gas sample connection, consists of a coupling and valve and must be accessible from ladder or platform.
