**Figure 4-20.** Flow patterns for air cooler piping.

Three flow pattern diagrams are shown for air cooler piping, each depicting tube bundle arrangements with inlet and outlet nozzle connections:

- **For 4 to 6 nozzles** (upper left): A single tube bundle with inlet at top center and outlet at bottom center. Flow enters the inlet header, passes through two tube rows in the bundle, and exits via the outlet header. Header end-caps are shown at both left (C) and right (D) ends.

- **For less than 4 nozzles** (upper right): A single, narrower tube bundle with inlet at upper right and outlet at lower left. Single-pass flow arrangement through the bundle.

- **For more than 6 nozzles** (lower): Two tube bundles placed side by side and connected in parallel, sharing a common inlet header at top center and a common outlet header at bottom center. Flow is split to enter both bundles simultaneously and recombined at the outlet. Header end-caps labeled C and D at each end of each bundle.
