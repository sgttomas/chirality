where $r$ is the mean radius of the pipe, $k_1$ and $k_2$ are the in-plane and out-of-plane bending factors. If there is any portion of the pipe with stiffness $E I_s X$ different from the constant $EI$, the integration of Eq. 5A.8 must be performed in segments, and a correction factor $Q = EI/E_sI_s$ be applied to all $a_i$ and $b_i$.

Using Castigliano's Theorem which states that:

$$\begin{cases} \phi_j = \dfrac{\partial U}{\partial M_j}, \\ \Delta_j = \dfrac{\partial U}{\partial F_j}, \end{cases} \quad j = 1, 2, 3 \tag{5A.10}$$

we obtain from Eqs. 5A.7 and 5A.8 the following equations:

$$\begin{cases} EI\phi_j = \displaystyle\int \sum_i b_i m_i \dfrac{\partial m_i}{\partial M_j} ds \\ \qquad = \displaystyle\int \sum_i b_i [\xi_i \cdot (\overline{M} - \overline{\rho} \times \overline{F})] \dfrac{\partial m_i}{\partial M_j} ds, \\ \\ EI\Delta_j = \displaystyle\int \sum_i \left( a_i f_i \dfrac{\partial f_i}{\partial F_j} + b_i m_i \dfrac{\partial m_i}{\partial F_j} \right) ds \\ \qquad = \displaystyle\int \sum_i \xi_i \cdot \left[ a_i \overline{F} \dfrac{\partial f_i}{\partial F_j} \right. \\ \qquad \left. + b_i (\overline{M} - \overline{\rho} \times \overline{F}) \dfrac{\partial m_i}{\partial F_j} \right] ds, \\ \qquad j = 1, 2, 3, \end{cases} \tag{5A.11}$$

From Eqs. 5A.4 and 5A.7, we see that:

$$\begin{cases} \dfrac{\partial m_i}{\partial M_j} = -\xi_i \cdot \overline{z}_j = \dfrac{\partial f_i}{\partial F_j}, \\ \dfrac{\partial m_i}{\partial F_j} = -\xi_i \cdot (\overline{\rho} \times \overline{z}_j) = (\overline{\rho} \times \xi_i) \cdot \overline{z}_j. \end{cases} \tag{5A.12}$$

Substituting Eq. 5A.12 in Eq. 5A.11 and collecting terms, we get:

$$\begin{cases} EI\phi_j = \displaystyle\sum_k M_k \int \sum_i b_i \alpha_{ik} \alpha_{ik} ds \\ \qquad + \displaystyle\sum_k F_k \int \sum_i b_i \beta_{ik} \beta_{ij} ds, \\ \\ EI\Delta_j = \displaystyle\sum_k M_k \int \sum_i b_i \beta_{ij} \alpha_{ik} ds \\ \qquad + \displaystyle\sum_k F_k \int \sum_i (a_i \alpha_{ik} \alpha_{ij} + b_i \beta_{ik} \beta_{ij}) ds, \\ \qquad j = 1, 2, 3, \end{cases} \tag{5A.13}$$

where

$$\begin{cases} \alpha_{ij} = \xi_i \cdot \overline{z}_j, \\ \beta_{ij} = (\overline{\rho} \times \xi_i) \cdot \overline{z}_j. \end{cases} \tag{5A.14}$$

We introduce the notation:

$$\begin{cases} W_{jk} = \displaystyle\int \sum_i b_i \alpha_{ij} \alpha_{ik} ds, \\ U_{jk} = \displaystyle\int \sum_i (a_i \alpha_{ij} \alpha_{ik} + b_i \beta_{ij} \beta_{ik}) ds, \\ V_{jk} = \displaystyle\int \sum_i b_i \beta_{ij} \alpha_{ik} ds. \end{cases} \tag{5A.15}$$

It may be noted in Eq. 5A.15 that the subscripts $j$ and $k$ can be interchanged for $W_{jk}$ and $U_{jk}$ without affecting the results, but this is not true for $V_{jk}$. In other words, the matrices $|W_{jk}|$, $|U_{jk}|$ are symmetric but $|V_{jk}|$ is not. Thus we have:

$$\begin{cases} W_{jk} = W_{kj}, \quad \text{or} \quad W = W', \\ U_{jk} = U_{kj}, \quad \text{or} \quad U = U', \\ V_{kj} = V'_{jk} = \displaystyle\int \sum_i b_i \beta_{ik} \alpha_{ij} ds. \end{cases} \tag{5A.16}$$

Substituting Eqs. 5A.15 and 5A.16 in Eq. 5A.13, we get:

$$\begin{cases} EI\phi_j = \displaystyle\sum_k M_k W_{jk} + \sum_k F_k V_{jk}, \\ EI\Delta_j = \displaystyle\sum_k M_k V'_{jk} + \sum_k F_k U_{jk}, \end{cases} \quad j = 1, 2, 3$$

or in a detailed form:

$$\begin{cases} EI\phi_1 = W_{11}M_1 + W_{12}M_2 + W_{13}M_3 + V_{11}F_1 \\ \qquad + V_{12}F_2 + V_{13}F_3, \\ EI\phi_2 = W_{21}M_1 + W_{22}M_2 + W_{23}M_3 + V_{21}F_1 \\ \qquad + V_{22}F_2 + V_{23}F_3, \\ EI\phi_3 = W_{31}M_1 + W_{32}M_2 + W_{33}M_3 + V_{31}F_1 \\ \qquad + V_{32}F_2 + V_{33}F_3, \\ EI\Delta_1 = V_{11}M_1 + V_{21}M_2 + V_{31}M_3 + U_{11}F_1 \\ \qquad + U_{12}F_2 + U_{13}F_3, \\ EI\Delta_2 = V_{12}M_1 + V_{22}M_2 + V_{32}M_3 + U_{21}F_1 \\ \qquad + U_{22}F_2 + U_{23}F_3, \\ EI\Delta_3 = V_{13}M_1 + V_{23}M_2 + V_{33}M_3 + U_{31}F_1 \\ \qquad + U_{32}F_2 + U_{33}F_3. \end{cases} \tag{5A.17}$$

Equation 5A.17 can be written simply in the matrix form:

$$EI \begin{vmatrix} \phi_j \\ \Delta_j \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} W_{jk} & V_{jk} \\ V'_{jk} & U_{jk} \end{vmatrix} \begin{vmatrix} M_k \\ F_k \end{vmatrix} = A \begin{vmatrix} M_k \\ F_k \end{vmatrix}, \tag{5A.18}$$
