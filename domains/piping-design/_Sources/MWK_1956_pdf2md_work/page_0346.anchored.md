## C-6. MOMENTS AND FORCES

### Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed

### Thermal Expansion in Plane of Members

![C-6. Moments and Forces — Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed, Thermal Expansion in Plane of Members. Log-log chart of $A_1$, $A_2$, $A_3$, $A_4$ versus $K$ \(ranging from 0.1 to 1.0\), with diagram inserts for $K<1$ and $K>1$ configurations and an axis-convention sketch showing $F_x$, $F_y$, and $M_z$ at the corner.](figures/MWK_1956_p0346_fig01_c-6-moments-and-forces-two-member-system.png)

For $K < 1$:

$$F_{xA} = -F_{xC} = -10^4 A_1 I e / L^2$$
$$F_{yA} = -F_{yC} = +10^4 A_2 I e / L^2$$
$$M_{xA} = +10^4 A_3 I e / L$$
$$M_{xC} = -10^4 A_4 I e / L$$

For $K > 1$:

$$F_{xA} = -F_{xC} = -10^4 A_1 I e / (KL)^2$$
$$F_{yA} = -F_{yC} = +10^4 A_2 I e / (KL)^2$$
$$M_{xA} = 10^4 A_3 I e / KL$$
$$M_{xC} = -10^4 A_4 I e / KL$$

$F$ = Force, lb.
$M$ = Moment, ft-lb.

First subscript denotes *direction*.

Second subscript denotes *location*.

Signs are those of forces or *moments acting on anchors*.

$I$ = Moment of inertia of pipe, in.$^4$.
$e$ = Unit linear thermal expansion, in./ft.
Value of $E$ used = $29 \times 10^6$ psi.
