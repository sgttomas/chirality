entire load on $L$. For a point between $F$ and $A$, only the moments and forces at $A$ apply.

The member $NF$ is assigned to the $z$ plane; hence the bending moment $M_b$ in the plane of the member is an $M_z$ moment. The bending moment transverse to the member, $M'_b$, and the torsional moment, $M_t$, are expressible in terms of $M_x$ and $M_y$ moments. Thus for the point $P(x, y, z)$ located between $N$ and $F$, the bending moment components and the torsional moment assume the following form:

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_b &= M'_z - F_x(y_A - y) + F_y(x_A - x) - w_x L(\bar{y} - y) + w_y L(\bar{x} - x) \\
M'_b &= [M'_y - F_x(x_A - x) + F_z(x_A - x) - w_x L(\bar{x} - x)] \cos \psi \\
&\quad - [M'_x - F_y(x_A - x) + F_z(y_A - y) + w_y L(\bar{y} - y)] \sin \psi \\
M_t &= [M'_x - F_y(x_A - z) + F_z(y_A - y) + w_y L(\bar{y} - y)] \cos \psi \\
&\quad + [M'_y - F_x(x_A - x) + F_z(x_A - x) - w_x L(\bar{x} - x)] \sin \psi
\end{aligned} \tag{A.3}
$$

For another point $(x, y, z)$ between $O'$ and $N$ the relations are:

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_b &= M'_z - F_x(y_A - y) + F_y(x_A - x) - w_x L(\bar{y} - y) + w_y L(\bar{x} - x) \\
M'_b &= [M'_y - F_x(x_A - x) + F_z(x_A - z) + w_x L(\bar{z} - z) - w_z L(\bar{x} - x)] \cos \psi \\
&\quad - [M'_x - F_y(x_A - z) + F_z(y_A - y) - w_y L(\bar{z} - z) + w_z L(\bar{y} - y)] \sin \psi \\
M_t &= [M'_x - F_y(x_A - z) + F_z(y_A - y) - w_y L(\bar{z} - z) + w_z L(\bar{y} - y)] \cos \psi \\
&\quad + [M'_y - F_x(x_A - x) + F_z(x_A - z) + w_x L(\bar{z} - z) - w_z L(\bar{x} - x)] \sin \psi
\end{aligned} \tag{A.3}
$$

The partial derivatives of these moment expressions which are required in the general rotation and displacements formulas (eq. A.2) are:

|---|---|---|
| $\dfrac{\partial M_b}{\partial M'_x} = 0$ | $\dfrac{\partial M'_b}{\partial M'_x} = -\sin \psi$ | $\dfrac{\partial M_t}{\partial M'_x} = +\cos \psi$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial M_b}{\partial M'_y} = 0$ | $\dfrac{\partial M'_b}{\partial M'_y} = +\cos \psi$ | $\dfrac{\partial M_t}{\partial M'_y} = +\sin \psi$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial M_b}{\partial M'_z} = +1$ | $\dfrac{\partial M'_b}{\partial M'_z} = 0$ | $\dfrac{\partial M_t}{\partial M'_z} = 0$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial M_b}{\partial F_x} = -(y_A - y)$ | $\dfrac{\partial M'_b}{\partial F_x} = +(z_A - z) \cos \psi$ | $\dfrac{\partial M_t}{\partial F_x} = +(z_A - z) \sin \psi$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial M_b}{\partial F_y} = +(x_A - x)$ | $\dfrac{\partial M'_b}{\partial F_y} = +(z_A - z) \sin \psi$ | $\dfrac{\partial M_t}{\partial F_y} = -(z_A - z) \cos \psi$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial M_b}{\partial F_z} = 0$ | $\dfrac{\partial M'_b}{\partial F_z} = -(x_A - x) \cos \psi - (y_A - y) \sin \psi$ | $\dfrac{\partial M_t}{\partial F_z} = +(y_A - y) \cos \psi - (x_A - x) \sin \psi$ |
