## 3. The Submatrices of the Shape Coefficient Matrix.

The coefficients $\alpha_{ij}$ and $\beta_{ij}$, being function of direction cosines and the position vector $\bar{p}$, may be easily obtained for favorably oriented straight line pipes or simple bends. If the pipe is skewed with respect to the fixed $x$-system, we shall select a convenient local origin, $\bar{q}$, as shown in Figure 5A.1, Section 3A-2.1, and introduce a local coordinate system with respect to which the given segment of pipe will have the simplest form of shape coefficient matrix. We can then refer the shape coefficient matrix back to the $x$-system by means of suitable matrix operations which will be derived below.

Let the local coordinate system be identified as the $\xi$-system. Using Eqs. 5A.1 and 5A.2, one gets:

$$
\bar{p} \times \bar{\xi}_i = \bar{q} \times \sum_k \xi_{ki}\bar{x}_i + \bar{p} \times \bar{\xi}_i. \quad (5A.20)
$$

Introducing the vector $\bar{u}_r$ defined in the $x$-system as:

$$
\bar{u}_r = \bar{q} \times \bar{x}_r = \sum_k u_{rk}\bar{x}_k, \quad (5A.21)
$$

and the vector $\bar{e}_r$ defined in the $\xi$-system as:

$$
\bar{e}_r = \bar{p} \times \bar{\xi}_r = \sum_k e_{rk}\bar{\lambda}_k, \quad (5A.22)
$$

Equation 5A.20 may be written as:

$$
\bar{p} \times \bar{\xi}_i = \sum_r \xi_{ri}\bar{u}_r + \sum_r e_{ri}\bar{\lambda}_r \quad (5A.23)
$$
$$
= \sum_r \xi_{ri} \sum_k u_{rk}\bar{x}_k + \sum_r e_{ri} \sum_k \lambda_{rk}\bar{x}_k.
$$

Substituting Eqs. 5A.2, 5A.3 and 5A.23 in Eq. 5A.14, we get:

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l} \alpha_{ij} = \sum_r \xi_{ri}\lambda_{rj} \\ \beta_{ij} = \sum_r \xi_{ri}u_{rj} + \sum_r e_{ri}\lambda_{rj}. \end{array}\right. \quad (5A.24)
$$

Equation 5A.15 will then take the following form:

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l} W_{jk} = \sum_s \lambda_{sj} \sum_i \lambda_{sk} \sum_i b_i \int \xi_{li}\xi_{li} \, ds \\ U_{jk} = \sum_s \lambda_{sj} \sum_i \lambda_{sk} \sum_i a_i \int \xi_{li}\xi_{li} \, ds \\ \quad + \sum_s \lambda_{sj} \sum_i u_{sk} \sum_i b_i \int e_{li}e_{li} \, ds \\ \quad + \sum_s u_{sj} \sum_i \lambda_{sk} \sum_i b_i \int \xi_{li}e_{li} \, ds \\ \quad + \sum_s \lambda_{sj} \sum_i u_{sk} \sum_i b_i \int \xi_{li}e_{li} \, ds \\ \quad + \sum_s \lambda_{sj} \sum_i u_{sk} \sum_i b_i \int e_{li}e_{li} \, ds, \\ V_{jk} = \sum_s \lambda_{sj} \sum_i \lambda_{sk} \sum_i b_i \int \xi_{li}\xi_{li} \, ds \\ \quad + \sum_s \lambda_{sj} \sum_i \lambda_{sk} \sum_i b_i \int \xi_{li}e_{li} \, ds. \end{array}\right. \quad (5A.25)
$$

We define:

$$
L = |\lambda_{kj}|, N = |u_{kj}|,
$$
$$
B = |B_{ri}| = \left| \sum_i b_i \int \xi_{li}\xi_{li} \, ds \right|, \quad (5A.26)
$$
$$
C = |C_{ri}| = \left| \sum_i a_i \int \xi_{li}\xi_{li} \, ds + \sum_i b_i \int e_{li}e_{li} \, ds \right|,
$$
$$
D = |D_{ri}| = \left| \sum_i b_i \int \xi_{li}e_{li} \, ds \right|,
$$

each being a $3 \times 3$ matrix. Upon substitution of Eq. 5A.26 in the matrices of Eq. 5A.25, we get:

$$
|W_{jk}| = L'BL,
$$
$$
|U_{jk}| = L'CL + N'BN + N'DL + L'D'N, \quad (5A.27)
$$
$$
|V_{jk}| = L'BN + L'DL.
$$
