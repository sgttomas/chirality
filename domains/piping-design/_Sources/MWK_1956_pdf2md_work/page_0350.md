## C-10. MOMENTS AND FORCES

### Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed

### One Support Displaced Normal to Plane of Members

[FIGURE: C-10. Moments and Forces — Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed, One Support Displaced Normal to Plane of Members. Chart of $\bar{A}_1$, $\bar{A}_2$, $\bar{A}_3$, $\bar{A}_4$, $\bar{A}_5$ versus $K$ for $0 \leq K \leq 1.0$, with accompanying schematic of the two-member system showing supports A, B, C, dimensions L, $K L$, and displacement $\Delta$, plus a moment-diagram sketch.]

$$F_{yA} = -F_{yC} = 10^3 \frac{I \Delta}{L^3} \bar{A}_1$$

$$M_{xA} = -10^2 \frac{I \Delta}{L^2} \bar{A}_2$$

$$M_{zA} = +10^2 \frac{I \Delta}{L^2} \bar{A}_3$$

$$M_{zC} = -10^2 \frac{I \Delta}{L^2} \bar{A}_4$$

$$M_{xC} = -10^2 \frac{I \Delta}{L^2} \bar{A}_5$$

$F$ = Force, lb.

$M$ = Moment, ft-lb.

First subscript denotes *direction*.

Second subscript denotes *location*.

Signs are those of forces or moments *acting* on anchors.

$I$ = Moment of inertia of pipe, in.$^4$

$\Delta$ = Displacement normal to plane.

Value of $E$ used = $29 \times 10^6$ psi.
