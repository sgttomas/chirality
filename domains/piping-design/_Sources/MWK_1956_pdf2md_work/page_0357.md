## C-16. Span vs. Stress, Horizontal Pipe Lines, Uniform Load

[FIGURE: C-16. Span vs. Stress, Horizontal Pipe Lines, Uniform Load]

Formula: $S = 1.2wl^2/Z$

Key:
1. Connect $Z$ with $w$ locating turning point ($A$).
2. Connect ($A$) with $s$ locating $l$.
   Connect ($A$) with $l$ locating $s$.

Example: Given: $Z = 24.5$ in.$^3$, $w = 66$ lb/ft, $S = 2750$ psi.
Result: $l = 29$ ft.
