### C-12. MOMENTS AND FORCES

#### Symmetrical Expansion Loop

![C-12 Symmetrical Expansion Loop - geometry diagram showing guides, leg lengths $L_1$, $L_2$, $L_3$, dimensions $K_2$, $K_3$, and points $A'$, $B'$.](figures/MWK_1956_p0352_fig01_symmetrical-expansion-loop-geometry-diag.png)

![C-12 Force and moment diagram showing $F_B$, $F_A$, and $M_A$ at point B with subscript convention for symmetrical expansion loop.](figures/MWK_1956_p0352_fig02_force-and-moment-diagram-for-symmetrical.png)

$$F_{AY} = -F_{AY'} = 10^3 A_1 f \Delta / L^2$$

$$M_{AB} = -M_{AB} = 10^3 A_2 f \Delta / L^3$$

$I$ = Moment of inertia of pipe, in.<sup>4</sup>
$\Delta$ = Expansion from $A'$ to $B'$, in.
Value of $E$ used = 29 × 10<sup>6</sup> psi.

$F$ = Force, lb.
$M$ = Moment, ft-lb.
First subscript denotes direction.
Second subscript denotes location.
Signs are those of forces or moments *acting on anchors*.
