The following partial derivatives for substitution in the $\delta_x$, $\delta_y$ and $\delta_z$ equations result:

$x$-Plane

|  | $\dfrac{\partial F_x}{\partial}$ | $\dfrac{\partial F'_x}{\partial}$ | $\dfrac{\partial F_n}{\partial}$ |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial F_x}$ | $0$ | $+1$ | $0$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial F_y}$ | $-\sin\psi$ | $0$ | $+\cos\psi$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial F_z}$ | $+\cos\psi$ | $0$ | $+\sin\psi$ |

$y$-Plane

|  | $\dfrac{\partial F_x}{\partial}$ | $\dfrac{\partial F'_x}{\partial}$ | $\dfrac{\partial F_n}{\partial}$ |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial F_x}$ | $+\cos\psi$ | $0$ | $+\sin\psi$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial F_y}$ | $0$ | $+1$ | $0$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial F_z}$ | $\sin\psi$ | $0$ | $+\cos\psi$ |

$z$-Plane

|  | $\dfrac{\partial F_x}{\partial}$ | $\dfrac{\partial F'_x}{\partial}$ | $\dfrac{\partial F_n}{\partial}$ |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial F_x}$ | $-\sin\psi$ | $0$ | $+\cos\psi$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial F_y}$ | $+\cos\psi$ | $0$ | $+\sin\psi$ |
| $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial F_z}$ | $0$ | $+1$ | $0$ |

Substitution of these values into the $\delta_x$, $\delta_y$, and $\delta_z$ equations, plane by plane, produces the following recurring integrals

$$
\begin{aligned}
S &= \int 2.6 Q r_m{}^2 \, dl \\
U &= \int Q r_m{}^2 (2.6 \cos^2\psi + .5 \sin^2\psi) \, dl \\
V &= \int Q r_m{}^2 (2.6 \sin^2\psi + .5 \cos^2\psi) \, dl \\
W &= \int Q r_m{}^2 \, 2.1 \sin\psi \cos\psi \, dl
\end{aligned}
\quad (\text{A.23})
$$

The way these constants are entered into the simultaneous equations is shown in Section 5.10 of Chapter 5.
