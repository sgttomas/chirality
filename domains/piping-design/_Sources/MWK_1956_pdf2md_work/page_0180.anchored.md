Solution of the equations gives the reactions at the free end, the moment $M_x$ referred to the origin. The vertical reaction at the fixed end is the difference between the total load of 29,150 lb and the $F_y$-force of 19,864 lb or 9286 lb.

The moment at any point $N$ of the line is calculated in accordance with the following general formula:

$$M'_{xN} = \sum (M_x + M_{xt}) + \sum F_{xN} y_N - \sum (F_y + F_{yw}) x_N$$

where $\sum F_x$ and $\sum F_y$ are the sum of the reacting forces at the free end obtained from the equation sheet, and any restraint in the line between the free end and point $N$. $\sum M_x$ is the sum of the moment at the free end referred to the origin, obtained from the equation sheet, and the moment at the origin caused by any restraint.
