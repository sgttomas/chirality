$$EI\delta_x =$$

$$- M'_x \int_A^{0'} (z_A - z)(1.3 \cos^2 \psi + k \sin^2 \psi) Q \, dl$$

$$- M'_y \int_A^{0'} (z_A - z)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi Q \, dl$$

$$+ M'_z \int_A^{0'} k(z_A - z) Q \, dl$$

$$- F_x \int_A^{0'} [k(z_A - z)(y_A - y) - (z_A - z)^2 (1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi] Q \, dl$$

$$+ F_y \int_A^{0'} [k(z_A - z)(x_A - x) + (z_A - z)^2 (1.3 \cos^2 \psi + k \sin^2 \psi)] Q \, dl$$

$$- w_z L_z \int_M^{0'} (z_A - z)[(\bar{y} - y)(1.3 \cos^2 \psi + k \sin^2 \psi) - (1.3 - k)(\bar{x} - x) \sin \psi \cos \psi] Q \, dl$$

$$- w_x \int_P^{0'} k(z_A - z)(y' - y) Q \, dl$$

$$+ w_y \int_P^{0'} k(z_A - z)(x' - x) Q \, dl$$
