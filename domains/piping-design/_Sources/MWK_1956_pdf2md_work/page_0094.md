## 4.4 Selected Chart-form Solutions

Special solutions have been frequently presented in the literature by means of a variety of formulas, charts, or tables [1, 2, 3], which are both time-saving and convenient for simple configurations. Each solution applies only to a particular configuration, although the proportions of the legs are permitted to vary. Since the number of variables which may be conveniently handled is limited, these solutions are restricted with regard to the number of legs in the configuration. With judgment and experience, segmenting of more intricate systems permits wider use, although generally at the expense of considerable hazard of error and with little saving in time over a complete solution by a more versatile approach. The selected cases included herein are limited to four which are believed unavailable elsewhere in the form given. These cases, shown in Fig. 4.3, are set up primarily for convenient use in establishing preliminary layouts, and provide directly the dimensions required rather than the stress for a set of assumed dimensions.
