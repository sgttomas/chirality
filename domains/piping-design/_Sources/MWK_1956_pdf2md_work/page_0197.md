where $\Sigma F_x$ is the sum of the reacting forces at the free end, obtained from the equation sheet, and any restraint in the line between the free end and point $N$. $\Sigma M_x$ and $\Sigma M_y$ are the sums of the moments at the free end referred to the origin, obtained from the equation sheet, and the moments at the origin caused by any restraint. $\Sigma F_{yN}$ is the sum of the wind load from the free end to point $N$. $M_{xn}$ and $M_{yn}$ are the sum of the moments at the origin due to $F_{xn}$. $M_{xn}$, $M_{yn}$, and $M_{zn}$ are obtained from Form F'.
