[FIGURE: Fig. A.16 Counterclockwise integration of shape coefficients for weight of circular members in the x-plane.]

For x-plane members taken in the *counterclockwise* direction (Fig. A.16):

$$w_b = -\int_0^{\alpha+\Phi} kQR^4 [\sin\alpha + \phi) + \phi \cos(\alpha+\phi)] d\phi$$

$$= -kQR^4 \{\Phi[\sin(\alpha+\Phi) + \sin(\alpha+\Phi) - \cos\alpha)]\} + 2[\cos(\alpha+\Phi) - \cos\alpha]\}$$

$$w_{ba} = -qw_b + w'_{ba}$$

where $w'_{ba} = +\int_0^{\Phi} kQR^4 [\Phi[\sin\alpha - \sin(\alpha+\phi)] \sin(\alpha+\phi)] d\phi$

$$= -kQR^4 \left\{\frac{\Phi}{2}\left[1 + \frac{\cos 2(\alpha+\Phi)}{2}\right] - \frac{3}{8}[\sin 2(\alpha+\Phi) - \sin 2\alpha] + \sin\alpha[\cos(\alpha+\Phi) - \cos\alpha]\right\}$$

$$w_{bb} = +bw_b + w'_{bb}$$

where $w'_{bb} = +\int_0^{\Phi} kQR^4 [\sin\alpha - \sin(\alpha+\phi) + \phi \cos(\alpha+\phi)] \cos(\alpha+\phi) d\phi$

$$= +kQR^4 \left\{\frac{\Phi}{2}[\Phi + \sin 2(\alpha+\Phi)] + \frac{1}{4}[\cos 2(\alpha+\Phi) - \cos 2\alpha] + \sin\alpha[\sin(\alpha+\Phi) - \sin\alpha]\right\}$$
