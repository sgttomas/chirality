## C-9. LENGTH OF LEG REQUIRED

### Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed

### One Support Displaced Normal to Plane of Members

[FIGURE: C-9. Length of Leg Required - Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed, One Support Displaced Normal to Plane of Members. Diagram showing members AB and BC with leg lengths L and KL, displacement Δ at point C normal to plane of members.]

$L$ = Length of leg $AB$, ft.

$\Delta$ = Displacement normal to plane, in.

Value of $E$ used = $29 \times 10^6$ psi.

$S_A$ = Code allowable stress range $(1.25 S_c + 0.25 S_h)$, psi.

[FIGURE: Chart C-9. Log-log plot of $\frac{L^2 S_A}{10^7 \Delta}$ (vertical axis, range 0.02 to 2.0) versus K (horizontal axis, range 0 to 1.0), with curves for nominal pipe sizes from 1" through 30".]

Multiply $L$ by $K$ to obtain length of leg $BC$ required.
