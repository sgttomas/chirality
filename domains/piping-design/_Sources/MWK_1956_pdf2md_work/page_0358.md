## C-17. Span vs. Natural Frequency and vs. Deflection

### Horizontal Pipe Lines, Uniform Load

[FIGURE: C-17. Span vs. Natural Frequency and vs. Deflection - Horizontal Pipe Lines, Uniform Load. Nomograph with axes: I = Moment of Inertia (IN.4), l = Pipe Span (FT), f_n = Approximate Natural Frequency (cycles per sec), δ = Deflection (IN.), E = Modulus of Elasticity (LB PER SQ IN), T = Temperature (F), w = Total Unit Weight (LB PER FT).]

Formulas: $\delta = 17.1 w l^4 / EI$  $f_n = 3.13/\sqrt{\delta}$

Key:
1. Connect $E$ (or $T$) with $I$ locating turning point ($A$) at intersection on $l$.
2. Connect ($A$) with $w$ locating turning point ($B$) at intersection on $I$.
3. Connect ($B$) with $l$ locating $\delta$ (or $l$).
   Connect ($B$) with $\delta$ (or $f_n$) locating $l$.

Example: Given: $E = 29 \times 10^6$ psi, $I = 2840$ in.$^4$, $w = 320$ lb/ft.
Result: $f_n = 0.76$ cy/sec, $\delta = 0.17$ in.
