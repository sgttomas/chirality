## C-4. Chart for Criterion in Par. 620(a) in Code for Pressure Piping ASA B31.1

![C-4. Chart for Criterion in Par. 620\(a\) in Code for Pressure Piping ASA B31.1](figures/MWK_1956_p0344_fig01_c-4-chart-for-criterion-in-par-620-a-in.png)

$$\frac{U}{D} = \frac{\text{Anchor distance, ft}}{\text{Nominal diameter, in.}}$$

*Analysis of piping is mandatory if*

$$\frac{L}{U} < R'$$

$D$ = Nominal diameter of pipe, in.

$L$ = Developed pipe length, ft.

$Y$ = Resultant of restrained thermal expansion and net linear terminal displacements, in.

$U$ = Anchor distance (length of straight line joining anchors), ft.

$T$ = Temperature, F.

\* The temperature scale may be used instead of $Y/U$ when there is no displacement of the anchor points.

### Basis of Chart

Analysis of piping is mandatory if the following criterion is not satisfied:

$$\frac{DY}{(L-U)^2} \le 0.03$$
