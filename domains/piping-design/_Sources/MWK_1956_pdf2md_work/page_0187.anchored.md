# FLEXIBILITY ANALYSIS BY THE GENERAL ANALYTICAL METHOD

The pipe configuration previously given in Sample Calculation 5.14 is calculated for a wind load of 60 lb/ft in both the $x$- and $z$-directions, and shown as Sample Calculations 5.15 and 5.16 respectively. A stop in the $z$- direction at point $i$ is included. The load constant for this stop equation is calculated on Form $S_z$. Sufficient explanation for the computation is given on the form.

The moments at any point $N$ are computed in accordance with the following general formulas:

For $z$-wind:

$$M'_{xN} = \Sigma M_x + \Sigma M_{xw} + (\Sigma F_y + \Sigma F_{yw})y_N - \Sigma F_y z_N$$

where $\Sigma F_y$, $\Sigma F_y$ are the sums of the reacting forces at the free end (obtained from the answer sheet).

plus all restraint in the line between the free end and point $N$. The sum of the moment at the free end referred to the origin (obtained from the equation sheet), and the moment at the origin caused by any restraint is $\Sigma M_x$. $\Sigma F_{xw}$ is the sum of the wind load from the free end to point $N$. $\Sigma M_{xw}$ is the sum of the moments at the origin due to $\Sigma F_{xw}$. $F_{xw}$ and $M_{xw}$ are obtained from Form $W_x$. The calculation is shown in Form F-1. Since no restraint is included, in the example, $\Sigma M_x$, $\Sigma F_{xw}$ and $\Sigma F_{yw}$ are the moments from the equation sheet.

For $x$-wind:

$$M'_{xN} = \Sigma M_y + \Sigma M_{yw} - (\Sigma F_x + \Sigma F_{xw})y_N$$

$$M'_{yN} = \Sigma M_y + \Sigma M_{yw} + (\Sigma F_z + \Sigma F_{zw})x_N$$
