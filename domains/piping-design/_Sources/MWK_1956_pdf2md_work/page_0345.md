## C-5. LENGTH OF LEG REQUIRED

### Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed, Thermal Expansion in Plane of Members

[FIGURE: C-5. Length of Leg Required — Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed, Thermal Expansion in Plane of Members. Nomograph plotting $LS_A/10^4 e$ versus K with nominal pipe size curves (1", 1½", 2", 2½", 3", 4", 5", 6", 8", 10", 12", 14", 16", 18", 20", 24", 30", 36"). Accompanying sketch shows two-member system ABC with leg L from A to B and leg KL from B to C.]

$L$ = Length of leg $AB$, ft.

$e$ = Unit linear thermal expansion, in./ft.

Value of $E$ used = $29 \times 10^6$ psi.

$S_A$ = Code allowable stress range $(1.25S_c + 0.25S_h)$, psi.

Multiply $L$ by $K$ to obtain length of leg $BC$ required.
