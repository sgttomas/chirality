So far, all the forces and displacements are referred to the common origin. To account for the effects of stops occurring at point 8, it is necessary to modify Eq. 5A.32. Let $\bar{F}^{88}$ be the force (including moment) vector at point 8, but not referred to the origin; and $\bar{a}^{88}$ be the corresponding displacement (including rotation) vector at point 8 with respect to the undeformed position of point 8. We have:

$$\bar{F}^{8} = K_{08} \bar{F}^{88},$$
$$\bar{a}^{88} = K'_{80} \bar{a}^{8},$$

(5A.33)

where,

$$K_{08} = \begin{vmatrix} I & \begin{matrix} 0 & -q_{3} & q_{2} \\ q_{3} & 0 & -q_{1} \\ -q_{2} & q_{1} & 0 \end{matrix} \\ O & I \end{vmatrix}$$

(5A.34)

$$K'_{80} = \begin{vmatrix} I & O \\ \begin{matrix} 0 & -q_{3} & q_{2} \\ q_{3} & 0 & -q_{1} \\ -q_{2} & q_{1} & 0 \end{matrix} & I \end{vmatrix}$$

and $q_{1}, q_{2}, q_{3}$ are the coordinates of point 8. Upon substitution of Eq. 5A.33, Eq. 5A.32 becomes:

$$EI \begin{vmatrix} \bar{a}^{1} \\ \bar{a}^{2} \\ \bar{a}^{3} \\ \bar{a}^{88} \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} A^{1} + A^{2} + A^{8} + A^{P_8} & A^{1} + A^{8} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{2} + A^{7} & A^{1} K_{08} \\ A^{1} + A^{8} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{6} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{7} & A^{1} K_{08} \\ A^{1} + A^{2} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{2} + A^{P_8} & (A^{1} + A^{8}) K_{08} \\ K'_{80} A^{1} & K'_{80} A^{1} & K'_{80} (A^{1} + A^{8}) & K'_{80} (A^{1} + A^{8}) K_{08} \end{vmatrix} \begin{vmatrix} \bar{F}^{8} \\ \bar{F}^{6} \\ \bar{F}^{4} \\ \bar{F}^{88} \end{vmatrix}$$

Since from Eq. 5A.34, $K_{08} K'_{80} = I$, the last equation may be modified as follows:

$$EI \begin{vmatrix} \bar{a}^{1} \\ \bar{a}^{2} \\ \bar{a}^{3} \\ \bar{a}^{88} \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} A^{1} + A^{2} + A^{8} + A^{P_8} & A^{1} + A^{8} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{2} + A^{7} & (A^{1} + A^{8}) K_{08} \\ A^{1} + A^{8} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{6} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{7} & A^{1} K_{08} \\ A^{1} + A^{2} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{7} & A^{1} + A^{2} + A^{P_8} & (A^{1} + A^{8}) K_{08} \\ K'_{80} A^{1} & K'_{80} A^{1} & K'_{80} (A^{1} + A^{8}) & K'_{80} (A^{1} + A^{8}) K_{08} \end{vmatrix} \begin{vmatrix} \bar{F}^{8} \\ \bar{F}^{6} \\ \bar{F}^{4} \\ \bar{F}^{88} \end{vmatrix}$$
