## DERIVATION OF ACOUSTIC VIBRATION FORMULAS

For double action,

$$\omega = 4\pi \frac{N}{60} m \quad (B.45)$$

If then, $V^* =$ volume flow of gas per single stroke per piston, calculated relative to average *tank* density, it is found, by virtue of eqs. B.42 and B.45, that for either case:

$$v_w = 2\pi \frac{1 - p_0/\bar{p}}{1 - \frac{1}{2} p_0/\bar{p}} \frac{V}{V^*} \quad (B.46)$$

Finally, $p_0$ is the pressure immediately at the (effective) orifice. Actually, there is usually some pressure recovery. The latter varies with orifice-to-pipe diameter. A pressure recovery of 50% is assumed. If $(p_0/\bar{p})_a$ is the actual pressure ratio be-

tween tank and piping, then (for 50% pressure recovery):

$$(\bar{p} - p_0)_a = \tfrac{1}{2}(\bar{p} - p_0)$$

where $\bar{p}_a = \bar{p}$, hence,

$$\left(\frac{p_0}{\bar{p}}\right) = 2\left(\frac{p_0}{\bar{p}}\right)_a - 1$$

and

$$\frac{1 - p_0/\bar{p}}{1 - \tfrac{1}{2} p_0/\bar{p}} = \frac{1 - (p_0/\bar{p})_a}{\tfrac{3}{2} - \tfrac{1}{2}(p_0/\bar{p})_a} \quad (B.47)$$

substituting eqs. B.46 and B.47 into eq. B.44, gives the final expression for the attenuation factor A.F.,

$$\text{A.F.} = \sqrt{1 + \left(2\pi \frac{1 - (p_0/\bar{p})_a}{\tfrac{3}{2} - \tfrac{1}{2}(p_0/\bar{p})_a} \frac{V}{V^*}\right)^2} \quad (B.48)$$

The results are shown in Fig. 9.30 (Subsection 9.8b) for $(p_0/\bar{p})_a = 0.99, 0.95, 0.9, 0.8$.
