**Table 5.32 Shape Coefficients for Uniform Loading: General Formulas for Circular Members**

**For *x*-Wind Acting on Members in the *y*-Plane or**
**z-Wind Acting on Members in the *z*-Plane**

*Note:* Signs prefixed to formulas below to be selected as follows:
Use upper signs when arc $\Phi$ lies in the I and/or II quadrant.
Use lower signs when arc $\Phi$ lies in the III and/or IV quadrant.
Split the arc into two members when $\Phi$ lies in I and II or III and IV quadrants.

![Quadrant diagram showing four quadrants labeled III \(top-left\), II \(top-right\), IV \(bottom-left\), I \(bottom-right\)](figures/MWK_1956_p0186_fig01_table-5-32-quadrant-diagram.png)

**Counterclockwise Direction**

$$w_a = \pm QkR^2 \left[ \frac{\Phi}{4}(1 + 2\cos^2 \alpha) + \frac{\sin 2(\alpha + \Phi)}{8} - \cos\alpha \sin(\alpha + \Phi) + \frac{1}{8}\sin 2\alpha \right]$$

$$w'_{aa} = \pm QkR^2 \left[ \frac{1}{2}\sin(\alpha + \Phi) - \sin\alpha \right]$$

$$w'_{ax} = \pm QkR^2 \left\{ \frac{1}{3}[\sin^3(\alpha + \Phi) - \sin^3 \alpha] - \frac{1}{2}[\sin(\alpha + \Phi) - \sin\alpha] + \frac{\cos\alpha}{2}[\Phi + \sin(\alpha + \Phi)\cos(\alpha + \Phi) - \cos\alpha] \right\}$$

**Clockwise Direction**

$$w_a = \mp QkR^2 \left[ \frac{\Phi}{4}(1 + 2\cos^2 \alpha) - \frac{\sin 2(\alpha - \Phi)}{8} + \cos\alpha \sin(\alpha - \Phi) - \frac{1}{8}\sin 2\alpha \right]$$

$$w'_{aa} = \pm QkR^2 \left[ \frac{1}{2}\sin(\alpha - \Phi) - \sin\alpha \right]$$

$$w'_{ax} = \pm QkR^2 \left\{ \frac{1}{3}[\sin^3(\alpha - \Phi) - \sin^3 \alpha] - \frac{1}{2}[\sin(\alpha - \Phi) - \sin\alpha] - \frac{\cos\alpha}{2}[\Phi - \sin(\alpha + \Phi)\cos(\alpha - \Phi) + \sin(\alpha - \Phi)\cos\alpha] \right\}$$

**Table 5.33 Shape Coefficients for Uniform Loading: General Formulas for Circular Members**

**For *z*-Wind Acting on Members in the *x*-Plane or**
**x-Wind Acting on Members in the *y*-Plane**

*Note:* Signs prefixed to formulas below to be selected as follows:
Use upper signs when arc $\Phi$ lies in the I and/or IV quadrant.
Use lower signs when arc $\Phi$ lies in the II and/or III quadrant.
Split the arc into two members when $\Phi$ lies with I and II or III and IV quadrants.

![Quadrant diagram showing four quadrants labeled III \(top-left\), II \(top-right\), IV \(bottom-left\), I \(bottom-right\)](figures/MWK_1956_p0186_fig02_table-5-33-quadrant-diagram.png)

**Counterclockwise Direction**

$$w_a = \mp QkR^2 \left[ \frac{\Phi}{4}(1 + 2\sin^2 \alpha) - \frac{\sin 2(\alpha + \Phi)}{8} + \sin\alpha \cos(\alpha + \Phi) - \frac{1}{8}\sin 2\alpha \right]$$

$$w'_{aa} = \pm QkR^2 \left[ \frac{1}{2}[\sin(\alpha + \Phi) - \sin\alpha] \right]$$

$$w'_{ax} = \pm QkR^2 \left\{ \frac{1}{3}[\cos^3(\alpha + \Phi) - \cos^3 \alpha] - \frac{1}{2}[\cos(\alpha + \Phi) - \cos\alpha] - \frac{\sin\alpha}{2}[\Phi - \sin(\alpha + \Phi)\cos(\alpha + \Phi) + \sin\alpha \cos(\alpha + \Phi)] \right\}$$

**Clockwise Direction**

$$w_a = \mp QkR^2 \left[ \frac{\Phi}{4}(1 + 2\sin^2 \alpha) + \frac{\sin 2(\alpha - \Phi)}{8} - \sin\alpha \cos(\alpha - \Phi) + \frac{1}{8}\sin 2\alpha \right]$$

$$w'_{aa} = \pm QkR^2 \left[ \frac{1}{2}[\sin(\alpha - \Phi) - \sin\alpha] \right]$$

$$w'_{ax} = \pm QkR^2 \left\{ \frac{1}{3}[\cos^3(\alpha - \Phi) - \cos^3 \alpha] - \frac{1}{2}[\cos(\alpha - \Phi) - \cos\alpha] + \frac{\sin\alpha}{2}[\Phi - \sin(\alpha - \Phi)\cos(\alpha - \Phi) - \sin\alpha \cos(\alpha - \Phi)] \right\}$$
