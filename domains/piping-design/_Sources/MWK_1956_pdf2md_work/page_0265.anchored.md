### 9.2e Approximate Natural Frequencies of Pipe Bends with Two Members (Vibration Perpendicular to Plane of Bend).

Consider a pipe bend as shown in Fig. 9.11, having both legs the same in diameter, thickness, and material. The mass distribution is considered to be due only to the weight of the pipes.

For the usual pipe materials, the ratio of bending rigidity to torsional rigidity, $EI/GJ = 1.3 \approx 1$. With this approximation, the general expression for the fundamental natural frequency of vibration perpendicular to the plane of the bend will be of the form

$$f = \alpha \sqrt{\frac{EI}{WL^3}} \quad (9.25)$$

where $f$ is in cps, $E$ is in lb/in.$^2$, $I$ is in in.$^4$, $W$ = total weight (of bend in lb, $L = L_1 + L_2$ = total length of bend in ft, and $\alpha$ is a numerical factor depending upon the ratio $L_1/L_2$ and the angle $\theta$.
