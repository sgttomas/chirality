The shape coefficients of circular members are given below. The signs to be used in the expressions which follow are dependent upon the location of the arc $R\phi$ in a system of quadrants which is oriented from the negative vertical axis as shown in Fig. A.19.

In deriving the shape coefficient formulas for circular members subjected to *in-plane* wind, the case of the member in the *x*-plane acted upon by *x* wind parallel to the *x*-axis is considered first. Figure A.20 shows such a member set up for integration in a *counterclockwise* direction.

![Fig. A.20 Counterclockwise integration of shape coefficients for circular members in the x-plane acted upon by x-wind.](figures/MWK_1956_p0323_fig02_counterclockwise-integration-of-shape-co.png)

$$l' = \int_0^\phi dl' = \pm R [\cos \alpha - \cos (\alpha + \phi)]$$
$$dl' = \sin (\alpha + \phi) R \, d\phi$$
$$x = a + R \sin (\alpha + \phi)$$
$$y = b - R \cos (\alpha + \phi)$$
$$y' = b - \frac{1}{2}R[\cos \alpha + \cos (\alpha + \phi)]$$

$$w_b = \pm \int_0^\phi kQR^3 \frac{[\cos \alpha - \cos (\alpha + \phi)]^2}{2} d\phi$$

$$= \pm kQR^3 \left[ \frac{\Phi}{4} (2 + \cos 2\alpha) + \frac{\sin 2(\alpha + \Phi)}{8} - \cos \alpha \sin(\alpha + \Phi) + \tfrac{3}{8} \sin 2\alpha \right]$$

$$w_{ba} = -aw_b + w'_{ba}$$

where
$$w'_{ba} = \pm \int_0^\Phi kQR^4 \sin(\alpha + \phi) \frac{[\cos \alpha - \cos(\alpha + \phi)]^2}{2} d\phi = \pm kQR^4 \frac{[\cos \alpha - \cos \Phi]^3}{6}$$

$$w_{bb} = +bw_b + w'_{bb}$$

where
$$w'_{bb} = \pm \int_0^\Phi kQR^4 \cos(\alpha + \phi) \frac{[\cos \alpha - \cos(\alpha + \phi)]^2}{2} d\phi$$

$$= \pm kQR^4 \left[ \frac{\sin^3(\alpha + \Phi) - \sin^3 \alpha}{6} - \frac{\sin(\alpha + \Phi) - \sin \alpha}{2} \right.$$

$$\left. + \frac{\cos \alpha}{2} \{\Phi + \sin(\alpha + \Phi)\cos(\alpha + \Phi) - \sin(\alpha + \Phi)\cos \alpha\} \right]$$

The upper signs apply when $\Phi$ lies in the *I* and/or the *II* quadrants; the lower signs apply when $\Phi$ lies in the *III* and/or the *IV* quadrants. When $\Phi$ lies in the *I* and *IV* quadrants or the *II* and *III*, it must be split up into two arcs.

For the counterclockwise integration of circular members in the *y*-plane subjected to *x*-wind, the identical formulas apply.
