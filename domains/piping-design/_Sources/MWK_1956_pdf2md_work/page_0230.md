$\Delta_y$ is similarly defined:

$$\Delta_y = \Delta_{yb} - \Delta_{ya} - ye$$

where $e$ is the unit expansion which applies to the piping between $a$ and $b$. The rotation of joint $a$ in degrees is:

$$\phi_a = \frac{\Delta_x x_b + \Delta_y y_b}{x_b y_a - x_a y_b} \times \frac{180}{\pi}$$

The rotation of joint $b$ in degrees is:

$$\phi_b = \frac{\Delta_x x_a + \Delta_y y_a}{x_b y_a - x_a y_b} \times \frac{180}{\pi}$$

The rotation of joint $ab$ is found from:

$$\phi_{ab} + \phi_a + \phi_b = 0$$

Dimensions and expansion must be in consistent units.
