### NOMENCLATURE

- $A, B$ = METAL AREA, (SQ.IN.)
- $D_i, D_p$ = INSIDE DIAMETER OF FITTINGS, (IN.)
- $b, x$ = INDICATED PRESSURE AREA, (SQ.IN.)
- $b, x$ = INDICATED LENGTHS, (IN.)
- $p$ = DESIGN PRESSURE, AT DESIGN TEMPERATURE, (PSI)
- $S_{HOT}$ = ALLOWABLE STRESS AT DESIGN TEMPERATURE, (PSI)
- $t_b$ = INDICATED METAL THICKNESS, (IN.)
- $t_s$ = AVERAGE METAL THICKNESS OF FLAT SURFACE, (IN.)
- $\alpha, \beta$ = INDICATED ANGLES
