# Derivation of Acoustic Vibration Formulas

IN Chapter 9, the derivations of certain expressions were omitted because of their length. As they are not available in the literature, at least not in the form given herein, they are included in this appendix. Numbered citations below refer to the list of references at the end of Chapter 9.

## B.1 Multiple Resonator of nth Order

General expressions for the natural frequencies of multiple resonator systems can be derived and will be given here for the sake of completeness (see also [24]). Consider Fig. B.1. $V$ (ft), $A$ (ft$^2$), $L$ (ft) are chamber volume, neck area, and neck length respectively. In addition let:

Acoustic conductivity $= \mu = \dfrac{A}{L + \tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{\pi A}}$ (ft);

$C =$ speed of sound in fluid (ft/sec)

Then,

$$f = \frac{C}{2\pi}\sqrt{\eta} \qquad \text{(B.1)}$$

where $\eta$ is a root of the determinantal equation:

$$
\begin{vmatrix}
\dfrac{1}{V_1} - \dfrac{\eta}{\mu_1} \,, & -\dfrac{1}{V_1} \,, & 0 \,, & 0 \,, & \cdots & 0 \,, & 0 \\[6pt]
-\dfrac{1}{V_1} \,, & \dfrac{1}{V_1} + \dfrac{1}{V_2} - \dfrac{\eta}{\mu_2} \,, & -\dfrac{1}{V_2} \,, & 0 \,, & \cdots & 0 \,, & 0 \\[6pt]
0 \,, & -\dfrac{1}{V_2} \,, & \dfrac{1}{V_2} + \dfrac{1}{V_3} - \dfrac{\eta}{\mu_3} \,, & -\dfrac{1}{V_3} \,, & \cdots & 0 \,, & 0 \\[6pt]
0 \,, & 0 \,, & -\dfrac{1}{V_3} \,, & \dfrac{1}{V_3} + \dfrac{1}{V_4} - \dfrac{\eta}{\mu_4} \,, & \cdots & 0 \,, & 0 \\[6pt]
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots \\[6pt]
\dfrac{\mu_{n+1}}{\mu_1 V_n} \,, & \dfrac{\mu_{n+1}}{\mu_2 V_n} \,, & \dfrac{\mu_{n+1}}{\mu_3 V_n} \,, & \dfrac{\mu_{n+1}}{\mu_4 V_n} \,, & \cdots & \dfrac{\mu_{n+1}}{\mu_{n-1} V_n} \,, & \dfrac{\mu_{n+1}}{\mu_n V_n} - \dfrac{\eta}{V_{n-1}} + \dfrac{1}{V_n} - \dfrac{\eta}{V_n}
\end{vmatrix} = 0
$$

This result was derived on the basis of a generalization of the method of derivation given by Rayleigh in the "Theory of Sound," Vol. II [3], for $n = 1, 2$; the $n$ roots of the determinantal equation are all real and positive, corresponding to $n$ physical modes.

For $n = 1$ $V_1 = V$, $(V_2 = \cdots = V_n = \infty)$ is found the following:

$$\dfrac{\mu_{n+1}}{\mu_n V_n} + \dfrac{1}{V_{n-1}} - \dfrac{\eta}{V_n} + 0 + \dfrac{1}{V_1} - \dfrac{\eta}{\mu_1} = 0 \qquad \text{(B.3)}$$

or

$$\eta = \frac{\mu_1 + \mu_2}{V_1}, \qquad f = \frac{C}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{\mu_1 + \mu_2}{V_1}} \qquad \text{(B.4)}$$

and for $\mu_2 = 0$, (2nd neck closed)

$$f = \frac{C}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{\mu_1}{V_1}} \qquad \text{(B.5)}$$

For $n = 2$ the determinantal reduces to:

$$
\begin{vmatrix}
\dfrac{1}{V_1} - \dfrac{\eta}{\mu_1} \,, & -\dfrac{1}{V_1} \\[6pt]
\dfrac{\mu_3}{\mu_1 V_2} - \dfrac{1}{V_1} \,, & \dfrac{\mu_3}{\mu_2 V_2} + \dfrac{1}{V_1} + \dfrac{1}{V_2} - \dfrac{\eta}{\mu_2}
\end{vmatrix} = 0 \qquad \text{(B.6)}$$
