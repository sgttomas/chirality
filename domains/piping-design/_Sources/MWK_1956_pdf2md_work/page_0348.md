### C-8. MOMENTS AND FORCES

#### Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed

#### One Support Displaced in the Direction of the Adjoining Member

[FIGURE: C-8. Moments and Forces - Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed - One Support Displaced in the Direction of the Adjoining Member]

$F_{xA} = -F_{xC} = -10^3 \, I \Delta A_1 / L^2$

$F_{yA} = -F_{yC} = +10^3 \, I \Delta A_2 / L^2$

$M_{zA} = +10^3 \, I \Delta A_3 / L^2$

$M_{zC} = -10^3 \, I \Delta A_4 / L^2$

$F$ = Force, lb.

$M$ = Moment, ft-lb.

First subscript denotes *direction*.

Second subscript denotes *location*.

Signs are those of forces or moments *acting* on anchors.

$I$ = Moment of inertia of pipe, in.$^4$.

$\Delta$ = Displacement in z-direction of end $A$, in.

Value of $E$ used = $29 \times 10^6$ psi.
