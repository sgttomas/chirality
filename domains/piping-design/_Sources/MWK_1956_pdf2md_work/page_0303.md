## HISTORY AND DERIVATION OF PIPING FLEXIBILITY ANALYSIS

From eqs. A.2 and A.3 the following equations are obtained:

$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\theta_x = \;& + M'_x \int_A^{O'} (1.3 \cos^2 \psi + k \sin^2 \psi) Q\, dl \\
& + M'_y \int_A^{O'} (1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi \, Q\, dl \\
& + F_x \int_A^{O'} (z_A - z)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi \, dl \\
& - F_y \int_A^{O'} (z_A - z)(1.3 \cos^2 \psi + k \sin^2 \psi)\, dl \\
& + F_z \int_A^{O'} [(y_A - y)(1.3 \cos^2 \psi + k \sin^2 \psi) - (x_A - x)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi]\, dl \\
& + w_x L \int_N^{O'} (\bar{z} - z)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi \, Q\, dl \\
& - w_y L \int_N^{O'} (\bar{z} - z)(1.3 \cos^2 \psi + k \sin^2 \psi) Q\, dl \\
& + w_z L \int_N^{O'} [(\bar{y} - y)(1.3 \cos^2 \psi + k \sin^2 \psi) - (\bar{z} - z)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi] Q\, dl \\
& + w_z \int_P^{N} [(\bar{y}' - y)(1.3 \cos^2 \psi + k \sin^2 \psi) - (\bar{z}' - z)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi] Q\, dl
\end{aligned}
\tag{A.4}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
EI\theta_y = \;& + M'_x \int_A^{O'} (1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi \, Q\, dl \\
& + M'_y \int_A^{O'} (k \cos^2 \psi + 1.3 \sin^2 \psi) Q\, dl \\
& + F_x \int_A^{O'} (z_A - z)(k \cos^2 \psi + 1.3 \sin^2 \psi)\, dl \\
& - F_y \int_A^{O'} (z_A - z)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi \, dl \\
& - F_z \int_A^{O'} [(x_A - x)(k \cos^2 \psi + 1.3 \sin^2 \psi) - (y_A - y)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi]\, dl \\
& + w_x L \int_N^{O'} (\bar{z} - z)(k \cos^2 \psi + 1.3 \sin^2 \psi) Q\, dl \\
& - w_y L \int_N^{O'} (\bar{z} - z)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi \, Q\, dl \\
& - w_z L \int_N^{O'} [(\bar{x} - x)(k \cos^2 \psi + 1.3 \sin^2 \psi) - (\bar{y} - y)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi] Q\, dl \\
& - w_z \int_P^{N} [(\bar{x}' - x)(k \cos^2 \psi + 1.3 \sin^2 \psi) - (\bar{y}' - y)(1.3 - k) \sin \psi \cos \psi] Q\, dl
\end{aligned}
\tag{A.4}
$$
