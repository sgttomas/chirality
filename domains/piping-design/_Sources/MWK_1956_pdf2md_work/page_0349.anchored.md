## C-9. LENGTH OF LEG REQUIRED

### Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed

### One Support Displaced Normal to Plane of Members

![C-9. Length of Leg Required - Two-Member System, Both Ends Fixed, One Support Displaced Normal to Plane of Members. Diagram showing members AB and BC with leg lengths L and KL, displacement Δ at point C normal to plane of members.](figures/MWK_1956_p0349_fig01_c-9-length-of-leg-required-two-member-sy.png)

$L$ = Length of leg $AB$, ft.

$\Delta$ = Displacement normal to plane, in.

Value of $E$ used = $29 \times 10^6$ psi.

$S_A$ = Code allowable stress range $(1.25 S_c + 0.25 S_h)$, psi.

![Chart C-9. Log-log plot of $\\frac{L^2 S_A}{10^7 \\Delta}$ \(vertical axis, range 0.02 to 2.0\) versus K \(horizontal axis, range 0 to 1.0\), with curves for nominal pipe sizes from 1" through 30".](figures/MWK_1956_p0349_fig02_chart-c-9-log-log-plot-of-l-2-s-a-10-7-d.png)

Multiply $L$ by $K$ to obtain length of leg $BC$ required.
