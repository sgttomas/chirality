## C-11. REQUIRED HEIGHT

### Symmetrical Expansion Loop

![C-11. Required Height - Symmetrical Expansion Loop. Chart plotting $\\frac{L^2 S_A}{10^7 D \\Delta}$ versus $K_2$ with curves for various $K_1$ values.](figures/MWK_1956_p0351_fig01_c-11-required-height-symmetrical-expansi.png)

$L$ = Length from $A$ to $B$, ft.

$L_o = \frac{1}{2}L(1 - K_1)$

$\Delta$ = Expansion from $A'$ to $B'$, in.

$D$ = Outside diameter of pipe, in.

Value of $E$ used = $29 \times 10^6$ psi.

$S_A$ = Code allowable stress range $(1.25 S_c + 0.25 S_h)$, psi.

Multiply $L_o$ by $K_1$ and $K_2$ to obtain dimensions of loop.
