The $x$-coordinate appearing under some of the integral signs in Table A.1 is that of any point of the member to the origin. As the line is assigned to the $y$-plane, $x$ is a constant equal to the distance of this plane from the origin. In Table A.2 the constant $x$ has been replaced by $c_x$ to indicate its true significance. Tables A.1 and A.2 cover only the coefficients for members in the $z$-plane. The corresponding coefficients for members in the $x$-plane or the $y$-plane can be similarly derived or can be readily obtained by cyclic permutation.

In the foregoing derivation the rotations and the deflections have been visualized as taking place at the free end $A$, the end $O'$ being fixed. These rotations and deflections may also be regarded as the differences between deformations at the free end and those at the fixed end and expressed as follows:

[FIGURE: Fig. A.3 Auxiliary coordinate system used in integration of shape coefficients.]

$$\theta_x = \theta_{xA} - \theta_{xO'}$$
$$\theta_y = \theta_{yA} - \theta_{yO'}$$
$$\theta_z = \theta_{zA} - \theta_{zO'}$$
$$\delta^*_x = \delta^*_{xA} - \delta^*_{xO'} = \delta_{xA} + y_A\theta_{zA} - z_A\theta_{yA} - (\delta_{xO'} + y_O'\theta_{zO'} - z_O'\theta_{yO'}) \quad (A.8)$$
$$\delta^*_y = \delta^*_{yA} - \delta^*_{yO'} = \delta_{yA} + z_A\theta_{xA} - x_A\theta_{zA} - (\delta_{yO'} + z_O'\theta_{xO'} - x_O'\theta_{zO'})$$
$$\delta^*_z = \delta^*_{zA} - \delta^*_{zO'} = \delta_{zA} + x_A\theta_{yA} - y_A\theta_{xA} - (\delta_{zO'} + x_O'\theta_{yO'} - y_O'\theta_{xO'})$$

Consider next the pipe line $AB$ which lies in the $y$-plane as shown in Fig. A.2 and which is a continuation of the line $O'A$ of Fig. A.1. Designating the end $A$ fixed and the end $B$ free, the constant terms of the equations would be:

$$\theta_x = \theta_{xB} - \theta_{xA} \qquad \delta^*_x = \delta^*_{xB} - \delta^*_{xA}$$
$$\theta_y = \theta_{yB} - \theta_{yA} \qquad \delta^*_y = \delta^*_{yB} - \delta^*_{yA} \quad (A.9)$$
$$\theta_z = \theta_{zB} - \theta_{zA} \qquad \delta^*_z = \delta^*_{zB} - \delta^*_{zA}$$

For the combined configuration $O'B$, the difference in deformation between the ends $O'$ and $B$ would simply be the sum of the deformations of the partial configurations, $O'A$ and $AB$.

$$\theta_x = \theta_{xA} - \theta_{xO'} + \theta_{xB} - \theta_{xA} = \theta_{xB} - \theta_{xO'}, \text{ etc.} \quad (A.10)$$

Hence, the system of equations expressing the deformation differences between $O'$ and $B$ can be found by *adding the systems of equations for the partial configurations*. In a like manner it can be seen that similar operations may be performed for partial configurations in the $x$-plane.

Addition of the equations consists of adding the coefficients of the respective terms as indicated in Table A.3. The summation coefficients $A_{xx}$, $A_{xy}$, etc. thus determined are coefficients of a system of equations. If the integrals are evaluated for each of three shapes taking dimensions, locations, and orientations as variables, sets of formulas are obtained which permit the various shape coefficients or members to be computed by simple substitution. Shape coefficients for a group of members are simply summations of those for the individual members.

Since the principle of cyclic permutation permits ready transformation of formulas from one plane to another, the integrations for concentrated loads* are presented only for members in the $z$-plane. The integrations are simplified by using an auxiliary coordinate system $x'$, $y'$, as shown in Fig. A.3 which indicates the following relationships:

$$x = a + x'$$
$$y = b + y' \quad (A.11)$$

where $a$ and $b$ are the respective horizontal and vertical coordinates of the midpoint of a straight member and of the radius center of a circular member. The distance of the plane of the member from the origin is designated $c_z$ if the plane is in a $z$-plane, $c_x$ if an $x$-plane, and $c_y$ if a $y$-plane.

*Separate integrations are presented for uniform loads since the axis of load application changes for each case considered.
