## Pumps and Turbines

When locating vertical pumps, the main concern should be accessibility for maintenance. There must be areas above the pump left free of obstruction or overhead piping must have break-out joints or flanges for dismantling. Access must be provided for a small crane or Austin-Western to pull the pump up so it may be taken to the shop for repairs.

### Turbines

Although there are many types of turbines—from the gas-fired turbines which drive huge compressors and generators to the smaller steam-driven turbines used as pump drivers, this section will deal only with the latter.

Figure 1-32 shows the inside construction of a turbine. At the top of the case are two connections. Directly on the center is a tapped hole for an eye bolt used when lifting the turbine. This tap does not penetrate the case. The other connection is for a sentinel valve which is shown and furnished with the turbine.
