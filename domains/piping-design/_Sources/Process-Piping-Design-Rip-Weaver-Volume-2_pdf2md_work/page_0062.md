# CONDENSING TURBINE & SURFACE CONDENSERS

The figure illustrates various arrangements for condensing turbines and surface condensers, including:

**Condensing Turbine — Ground Mounted:**

- Shows a ground-mounted condensing turbine configuration with surface condenser
- Surface condenser is typically located below the turbine exhaust
- Power is normally transmitted to the driven equipment via coupling
- Grade-level arrangement allows for conventional pump installations
- Hotwell pump(s) and condensate extraction pump installations shown
- Supports and anchor points indicated

**Condensing Turbine — Mezzanine Mounted (Elevated):**

- Turbine mounted at mezzanine elevation
- Surface condenser suspended below or located at grade
- Arrangement provides maintenance access from mezzanine level
- Arrow indicates transition between arrangements

**Condensing Turbine — Bracket Mounted (Elevated Up):**

- Turbine mounted on structural bracket at elevated position
- Condenser suspended below turbine exhaust connection
- Compact arrangement suitable for limited floor space

**Surface Condenser Notes:**

1. Drain box: Condensate is collected and pumped by hotwell pump(s) back to system
2. Hotwell: Acts as the liquid collection point at bottom of condenser shell
3. Water box: Cooling water enters and exits through water boxes on each end

**Horizontal Relief Valve** — Detail shown at lower right:
- Nozzle supply connection
- Water supply connection
- Relief valve body
- Bottom drain/panel detail
- Angle stop valve arrangement

**Vertical Relief Valve** — Detail shown at upper right:
- Nozzle connection
- Angle relief valve
- Water supply connection
- Main valve body and connections
