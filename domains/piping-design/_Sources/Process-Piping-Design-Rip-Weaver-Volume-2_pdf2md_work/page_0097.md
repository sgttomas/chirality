The plan view shows a box heater arrangement with the following labeled elements:

- **PIPEWAY** — overhead pipe rack running horizontally across the top of the plan
- Circled item **1** — pipeway connection point at upper right
- Circled item **3** — branch connection at upper left
- **CV** (×2) — control valves on the inlet lines dropping from the pipeway into the heater
- Circled item **5** — heater inlet header at left side
- Circled items **9** and **6** — instruments/connections on the common inlet header
- Circled item **11** — note point with annotation: *PIPING SHALL CLEAR HEATER STEEL*
- **HEATER** — box heater unit shown in plan (rectangular outline with center circle indicating stack or burner location)
- **H** symbols — hanger or support locations along the piping runs flanking the heater
