**TYPICAL BURNER PIPING**
**(COMBINATION FUEL GAS & FUEL OIL FOR VERTICAL HEATERS)**

The figure shows a plan view schematic of typical burner piping for a vertical heater using a combination of fuel gas and fuel oil. Key elements labeled in the drawing include:

- **PIPEWAY** — shown at upper left, with piping connections running to the burner assembly
- **TO B.O. VALVE / PIPING BATTERY** — piping return line from the pipeway
- **FUEL OIL RST. ST.** — Fuel oil reset station
- **FUEL GAS SUPPLY ST.** — Fuel gas supply station
- **ATOMIZING STEAM** — Steam line for fuel oil atomization
- **PILOT GAS** — Pilot gas connection (labeled ②)
- **OBSERVATION DOORS** — Located on the left side of the circular heater plan
- **FUEL OIL / FUEL GAS / ATOMIZING STM.** — Lines entering the burner assembly (labeled on left interior)
- **BURNER — SEE TYPICAL BURNER DETAIL** — Reference note at bottom left
- **BREAKOUT FLANGES MAY BE REQUIRED TO INSTALL HEADER** — Note at lower left
- **PEEP HOLES AND ACCESS OPENING — DO NOT OBSTRUCT** — Note at lower right
- Circled numbered callouts: ①, ②, ③, ④, ⑤, ⑥, ⑦ identifying various components around the burner ring
