# FLAT PERFORATED STRAINERS

FOR 150 LB.-300 LB.-600 LB. FLAT FACE, RAISED FACE AND RING JOINT FLANGES
900 LB.-1500 LB.-2500 LB. AVAILABLE UPON SPECIAL REQUEST.

**RAISED FACE**

Series FP, F-RJ, and F-FF for use with 150#, 300#, and 600# flanges.

**REVOLUTIONARY CONSTRUCTION**

Flange deeply rebated into ring joint gasket under pressure for tremendous strength and prevention of deformation. Smooth, accurate contours . . . no weld "blob" around ring edge.
