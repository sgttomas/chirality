[FIGURE: Figure 2-16a and b. Reciprocating compressor utility flow diagram.]

The figure shows two interconnected utility flow diagrams for a reciprocating compressor system:

**Upper diagram (Gas Engine / Compression Cylinder section):**

Key components and connections include:
- **Compression Cyl.** connected to **Gas Engine**
- **2" Cylinder Vent** — routed to building when req'd
- **4" Crankcase Vent** — routed to building when req'd
- **Expansion Joint 24" Exhaust** — connected to **Exhaust Muffler**
- **24" Air Intake** — connected to **Air Filter** with **Backfire Relief Valve**
- **Final Strainer (When Req'd)** — in line between gas engine and lube oil cooler
- **Lube Oil Filter** — connected to lube oil circuit
- **Lube Oil Cooler** — at base of upper diagram
- Instrumentation shown: temperature indicators (T), pressure gauges (G)

**Lower diagram (Compressor Driver / Jacket Water / Fuel Gas section):**

Key components and connections include:
- **Jacket Wtr. Cooler** — with cooling water in/out (TW, TCV instrumentation)
- **Jacket Water Surge Tank** — with internal overflow
- **Jacket Water Pumps** — (two pumps shown with check valves and isolation valves)
- **Make-Up Water** — feed to jacket water circuit
- **Compressor Driver** — central component with quick opening valve on outlet
- **Fuel Gas Volume Tank** — with PCV (pressure control valve) upstream
- **Fuel Gas** inlet line
- **From Shut Down Sys.** — connected to compressor driver
- **Starting Air** — supplied to compressor driver
- Instrumentation shown: temperature wells (TW), temperature indicators (T), pressure indicators (P), level gauge (LG), pressure control valve (PCV), temperature control valve (TCV)
- Pipe sizes noted include: 1", 5", 6", and others throughout the diagram
