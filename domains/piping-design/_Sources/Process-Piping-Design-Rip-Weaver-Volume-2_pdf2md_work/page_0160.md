Vertical distance = *b* − *d* + *f*

Step No. 2: Determine *L*, the longest distance, *a* + *e* > *c* + *g* > *b* − *d* + *f* therefore *L* = *a* + *e*.

Note: This example assumes *L* to be *a* + *e*. *L* must be the largest of the above three sums.

Step No. 3: Determine minimum *h*, the shortest distance. The sum of legs *b* + *c* + *d* + *f* + *g* must equal or exceed *h*. These are the legs at right angles to *L*.

## Formulas for Z-Shapes

The basic formulas for determining minimum *h* and maximum *L* apply to Z-shaped configurations:

Minimum $h^2 = 0.0025\ D_o L T$

Minimum $h = 0.05\sqrt{D_o L T}$

Maximum $L = \dfrac{400\ (h)^2}{D_o T}$

To test for minimum *h* a test for leg ratio must also be made. Referring to Figure 5-4:

$$\frac{B}{C} \geq 4 \qquad L = B + C$$

Test both minimum *h* and leg B as equal to *A* × $D_o$

*Example:* Z-shaped configuration, see Figure 5-4:

- Pipe = 8" Sch. 40;
- $D_o$ = 8.625" so use 9";
- $T$ = 300° − 70°
- $T$ = 230°F;
- $L$ = 30′-0″

To solve for minimum *h*:

$$h^2 = 0.0025 \times 9 \times 30 \times 230$$
$$= 155.25$$
$$= 12\text{′-}5\tfrac{1}{2}\text{″}$$

Test for minimum *h*:

$h = A \times D_o = 1 \times 9$

= 9′-0″ minimum for *h* and *B*.

Test *B* and *C* length ratio: Assume *B* = 25′-0″, *C* = 5′-0″

$$\frac{B}{C} = \frac{25}{5} = 5 \geq 4$$

so it is satisfactory.

## Formulas for U-Shapes

Formulas for U-shapes differ somewhat from the L and Z shapes. For U-shaped configurations with equal legs *h*, the formulas are noted below. Refer to Figure 5-5.

Minimum $h^2 = 0.0016\ D_o L T$ or

Minimum $h = 0.04\sqrt{D_o L T}$

Maximum $L = \dfrac{625\ (h)^2}{D_o T}$

To test for minimum *h*:

$$h = \frac{A \times D_o}{1.25}$$
