**DECOKING PROCEDURE**

1. AFTER TUBES BECOME HEATED, STEAM IS INJECTED AT CONVECTION INLETS. VALVES #1, 3, 4 & 5 ARE CLOSED. TO START BURNING, STEAM FLOW IS REDUCED; AIR IS INTRODUCED BY OPENING VALVE #4.

2. FOR REVERSE FLOW, VALVES #2, 3 & 6 ARE CLOSED; #1 & 4 ARE OPEN. VALVE #5 IS OPENED ONLY IF REVERSE BURNING IS REQUIRED.

3. WHILE PASS #1 IS BEING DECOKED, STEAM IS INJECTED INTO PASS #2 TO KEEP TUBES COOL.
