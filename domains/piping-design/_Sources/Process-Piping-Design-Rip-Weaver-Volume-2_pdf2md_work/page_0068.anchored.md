- **MINIMUM** — indicating minimum distance for the suction line from the suction source
- **SUCTION SOURCE** — vessel shown at upper right with connection to the compressor suction inlet
- **ALTERNATE** — alternate piping configuration shown with dashed lines
- **SUPPORT** — structural support indicated on the piping
