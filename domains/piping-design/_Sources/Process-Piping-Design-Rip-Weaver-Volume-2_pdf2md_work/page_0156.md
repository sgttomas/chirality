## Cast Iron or Aluminum Turbine Force, Moment and Stress Limitations

Cast iron and aluminum turbines are subject to the same limitations as for steel turbines except that allowable values are much lower. Allowable bending stress becomes *Sh*÷4 and *Sh*÷3 for the two catagories noted in steel turbines and the resultant shear force maximum is limited to 500 pounds. Resultant forces are further limited by the following:

*Individual components*

a. *Fx* ≤ 1.3*w* ≤ 40D
b. *Fy* ≤ 0.6*w* ≤ 35D
c. *Fz* ≤ *w* ≤ 40D

*Resultant components*

a. Algebraic summation of *Frx* ≤ 1.6*w*
b. Algebraic summation of *Fry* ≤ *w*
c. Algebraic summation of *Frz* ≤ 1.6*w*

*Combined resultant*

a. *(Frx²* + *Fry²* + *Frz²)*½ ≤ 2*w*

Using 100% cold spring and calculating using both operating and installed conditions, the total resultant moment imposed on the turbine at any connection must not exceed:

$F \leq (500\ D_c - M) \div 3$

The combined results of forces and moments at the inlet, extraction and exhaust connections, resolved at the action lines of the exhaust connections and shaft must not exceed:

$Fr \leq (250\ D_c - Mr) \div 2$

These components of these resultants must not exceed:

a. *Fxr* ≤ 50 *Dy*,  *Mxr* ≤ 250 *Dy*
b. *Fyr* ≤ 25 *Dy*,  *Myr* ≤ 125 *Dy*
c. *Fzr* ≤ 100 *Dy*,  *Mzr* ≤ 125 *Dy*

## Stress and Force Limitations of Compressors With Steel Nozzles and Casings

At the operating temperature, using the hot modules *E*, resultant bending moments are permissible up to a value which would cause a bending stress of *Sh*÷4 in a connection having a section modulus equal to the connecting piping for the same size where the connection is 4 in. IPS or larger. On smaller size connections a stress of *Sh*÷3 shall be permitted.

The resultant shear force at the flange face and any individual component must not exceed 2000 pounds. The resultant forces and individual components shall be further limited by the following:

*Individual components*

a. *Fx* ≤ 1.3*w* ≤ 130D
b. *Fy* ≤ 0.6*w* ≤ 130D
c. *Fz* ≤ *w* ≤ 160D

*Resultant components*

a. Algebraic summation of *Frx* ≤ 1.6*w*
b. Algebraic summation of *Fry* ≤ *w*
c. Algebraic summation of *Frz* ≤ 1.6*w*

*Combined resultant*

a. *(Frx²* + *Fry²* + *Frz²)*½ ≤ 2*w*

Using 100% cold spring and calculating the operating condition, the total resultant force and moment imposed on the compressor at any connection must not exceed:

$F \leq (925\ D_c - M) \div 3$

The combined resultants of forces and moments of the suction, interstage and discharge connections, resolved at the centerlines of the discharge connection must not exceed:

$Fr \leq (463\ D_c - Mr) \div 2$
