## 2.6.1 Integrity Management of SCE

The SCE list will be turned over to the operating company for assignment of required inspection, testing and maintenance tasks and frequencies, as well as definition of performance standards, in line with the company's standards and criteria. Testing and inspection frequencies and methodologies are generally established through consideration of the following:

- Codes and standards such as the National Board Inspection Code, American Society for Testing and Materials, American Petroleum Institute, National Fire Protection Association, American National Standards Institute, American Society of Mechanical Engineers;
- Factors such as magnitude of risk from malfunction/failure of equipment, intensity of use, operating environment and variety of operations that the equipment is exposed to;
- Failure rate of equipment (published values such as those available in OREDA, as well as historical data once available) which in turn influences inspection and testing frequency;
- For SIFs, testing and inspection frequency is established in accordance with ISA-84.00.01.
