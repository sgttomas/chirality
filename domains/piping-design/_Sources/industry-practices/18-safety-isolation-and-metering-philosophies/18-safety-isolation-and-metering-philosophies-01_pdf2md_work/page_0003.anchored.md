5. Equipment that prevents, detects, controls and mitigates a major accident (see section 2.4 for examples).

## 2.3 Hazard Assessments

For the scope of work, SCE will be identified through the Hazard Identification (HAZID) Study and Hazard and Operability Study/Layer of Protection Analysis (HAZOP/LOPA). Other equipment may be considered SCE by the operating company, as determined through other criticality assessment methodologies implemented external to the Project scope of work.

### 2.3.1 Qualifying events for SCE consideration

For an item to be considered for SCE designation, all credible failure events will be evaluated. Based on these failure events, any that are categorized as a health and safety related ‘major accident’, a term defined by the operating company’s risk criteria, will be considered safety-critical.

## 2.4 SCE Evaluation and Categorization

For events determined to be safety-critical by the Project, per section 2.3, equipment will be evaluated and designated as a SCE based on the two categories that follow:

### 2.4.1 Isolation and Prevention

1. Equipment under this category, often referred to as Independent Protection Layer (IPL), is defined as the ultimate safety-critical device that by engineered design, provides managed control for avoiding a hazardous event leading to a hazardous release of hydrocarbons/ chemicals. The systematic loop analyses of these SCE components should be based on HAZOP/LOPA data.

   a. IPLs are generally specified in the HAZOP/LOPA study, include all equipment from sensing devices to ending devices, and are required to be independent of the initiating event or the action of any other protection layer associated with the scenario.

   b. Examples of IPLs include, but are not limited to: Pressure safety valves (PSVs), safety-instrumented systems (SIS), key control valve loops, critical alarms that require operator intervention, combination IPLs such as a limiting element (restriction orifice/spools, check valves or control valve that limits flow) and a PSV sized for full capacity of restricted flow.

### 2.4.2 Control, Detect and Mitigate

1. This equipment will provide detection, mitigation, and control functions in the event of an uncontrolled release of hydrocarbons/chemicals. The primary intent for these SCEs is to prevent and/or control a catastrophic release of hydrocarbons (which generally reduces severity of event).

   a. Equipment in this category are generally listed in the HAZOP/LOPA study as Safeguards.
