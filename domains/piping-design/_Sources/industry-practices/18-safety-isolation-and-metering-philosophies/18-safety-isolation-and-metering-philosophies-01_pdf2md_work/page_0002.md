# 1 Introduction

Safety critical element (SCE) is identified for the purpose of assuring availability and functionality of equipment that provides the greatest contribution to risk reduction for major accident hazards. The intent of identifying SCE is to facilitate prioritization of inspection, testing, maintenance and performance standards by the operating company.

The intent of this philosophy is not to identify SCE, but rather give a guideline on:

1. What constitutes safety critical, pertinent to the work;

2. What studies, relevant to the Project, will define SCE and;

3. What purpose the resulting SCE list will serve.

## 2 Safety Critical Element Designation

SCE is classified as an equipment or system, whose failure could cause or contribute to a major accident, or whose purpose is to prevent or mitigate the effect of a major accident.

### 2.1 Purpose

SCE is identified for the purpose of assuring reliability, availability and functionality of the equipment that prevents and/or mitigates 'major accident hazards'. Major accident hazard is a term defined by the operating company's risk criteria.

SCE will have prioritized inspection, testing, and maintenance tasks, thus providing the greatest relative contribution to risk reduction for potential major accident events. SCE is an integral component of integrity management. The SCE list generated will be provided to the operating company for incorporation into asset and operations integrity management systems (Maintenance Management Systems, Management of Change, Procedures) to enable items to be flagged, prioritized, tracked and managed appropriately.

### 2.2 Definition of Safety Critical

Safety-critical, as will be considered by the Project, is defined by the following:

4. Pieces of equipment where the consequences of failure could cause or contribute substantially to a major accident event.

    a. Examples of this include a control valve failing closed or a pump failing to operate, resulting in an overpressure condition in upstream equipment. It should be noted that the control valve or the pump will only qualify as an SCE if the extent of the overpressure consequence is defined as a 'major accident' (see section 2.3.1).
