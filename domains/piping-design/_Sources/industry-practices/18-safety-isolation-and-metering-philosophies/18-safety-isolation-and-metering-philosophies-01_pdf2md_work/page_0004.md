b. Examples of these equipment types include, but are not limited to: Active fire and gas systems, ventilation systems with detection/mitigation/control equipment, manually initiated ESD and blowdown systems.

## 2.5 SCE Exclusions

Although they may be discussed during the HAZOP/LOPA, following is a list of equipment categories that are generally not considered SCE:

- Passive mitigation and evacuation systems (i.e., fire and blast walls, egress structures)

- Electrical Systems (i.e., junction boxes)

- Fixed hydrocarbon-containing equipment

  - These equipment types do not actively mitigate or prevent a catastrophic release by their function, and are not designed to alert personnel of an impending or occurred release

  - Their pressure-containment function is only passive in nature; therefore, as a rule, these equipment types not to be classified as SCE include vessels, tanks, piping, and flow lines. Pumps, compressors, and turbines are also included in this category unless they are part of a protective system.

- Occupational Personal Safety Equipment (PPE, SCBA, fall protection gear, eye wash)

It should be noted the above mentioned categories will have maintenance activities through their associated regulatory requirements and/or facility maintenance strategies.

## 2.6 SCE List

A SCE list will be generated by the project upon completion of the HAZOP/LOPA that will identify:

- A description of the equipment/instrument classified as SCE, including type and function;

- Equipment/instrument tag number;

- Corresponding HAZOP/LOPA scenario reference number;

- P&ID reference number;

- Safety Integrity Level (SIL) Requirement for Safety Instrumented Functions (SIF).
