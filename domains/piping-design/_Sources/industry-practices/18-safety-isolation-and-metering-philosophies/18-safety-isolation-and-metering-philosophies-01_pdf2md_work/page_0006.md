Equipment/systems identified in the SCE list should be incorporated into asset and operations integrity management systems, including maintenance management systems, procedures and Management of Change. This facilitates identification of SCE, provides a means to track and ensure completion of required tasks at the required intervals, and provides administrative controls of SCE items to ensure changes to the SCE are not made that would defeat its intended function as a protective device.
