#### 5.8.4 Special Purpose Steam Turbines

a) Special purpose steam turbines shall be as defined in API 612.

b) The maximum allowable forces and moments which may be applied to turbine flanges by the steam piping shall be limited to those values which are acceptable to both Syncrude and the manufacturer.

c) Direct connections between the turbine exhaust and a surface condenser will be made using a packless expansion joint.

d) Turbine nozzle loads shall also be analyzed in the cold condition with the permitted flange misalignment.

#### 5.8.5 Centrifugal and Axial Compressors

a) The maximum allowable forces and moments which may be applied to compressor flanges by the piping shall be limited to the values allowed by API 617.

b) Compressors nozzle loads shall be analyzed in the cold condition with the permitted flange misalignment.

#### 5.8.6 Air-Cooled Heat Exchangers

a) The maximum allowable forces and moments which may be applied to air cooler flanges shall be limited to those values which are acceptable to both Syncrude and the manufacturer. API 661 criteria shall be a minimum requirement unless the allowable loads are provided by the Manufacturer.

b) For multi-bundle air cooled exchangers, thrust blocks between bundles shall be considered in the analysis. Clearances at the end bundles shall be considered to accommodate the maximum thermal expansion calculated.

   Effects of piping weight and friction forces due to thermal expansion shall be included in the evaluation of loads on nozzles.

c) See Specification L-3 for fixed header box location.

#### 5.8.7 Shell and Tube Exchangers

Allowable forces from thermal displacements of piping on equipment shall be limited in accordance with the appropriate codes. See Specification L-3 for fixed end location.
