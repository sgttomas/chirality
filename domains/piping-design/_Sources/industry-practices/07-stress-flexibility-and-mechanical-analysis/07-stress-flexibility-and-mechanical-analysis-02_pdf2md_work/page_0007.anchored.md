d) Shell and tube exchanger piping.

e) Lines subject to excessive settlement of 75 mm (3 in.) or more.

g) All process, regeneration and decoking lines to and from fired heaters and steam generators.

5.4 In addition to requirements for thermal and deadweight analysis, special consideration needs to be given to other loading conditions and design features of piping systems (see para 5.11) in the following categories:

a) Lines NPS 3 and larger subject to significant differential settlement of tanks, vessels, equipment or supports.

b) Lines subjected to mixed phase flow (liquid and vapor) shall be analyzed for vibration problems.

c) Lines subject to external pressure by reason of vacuum or jacketing.

d) Jacketed piping.

e) Small aeration, steam, etc. lines connected to large lines shall be checked for thermal differential and/or surge vibrations.

f) Lines to and from reciprocating pumps and compressors.

g) Lines subject at steam purging. Minimum design temperature shall be 180°C (356°F).

h) Hot lines subjected to cold temperature injection.

i) Lines requiring proprietary expansion devices.

j) Thin walled piping.

k) Hot (operating at over 70°C (200°F)) underground piping.

l) Larger underground cooling water lines and associated small diameter branch connections.

m) In-line pump piping.

n) Refractory lined piping.

o) Lateral stability of long straight runs of piping.
