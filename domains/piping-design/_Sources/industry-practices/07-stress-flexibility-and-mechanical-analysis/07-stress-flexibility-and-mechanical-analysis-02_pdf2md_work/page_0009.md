NOTES:

(1) The 50°C (120°F) temperature is considered the maximum metal temperature resulting from solar radiation with no flow in the line.

(2) Under certain conditions of dual service, the fluid temperature range may be both above 50°C (120°F) and below minimum winter dry bulb temperature.

5.7.2 Lines subject to steam purging shall be designed for a minimum temperature of 180°C (355°F). If it is possible that vessels may be steamed independently of process lines, this factor shall be considered.

5.7.3 Lines which cannot be disconnected during purging shall be designed with sufficient flexibility to accommodate the thermal displacement of the vessel.

5.7.4 Equipment thermal expansion (radial and axial) shall be considered in the flexibility analysis.

5.7.5 The use of cold spring for piping systems which connect to rotating equipment is prohibited.

5.7.6 Piping which connects to equipment shall be supported so as not to overload the nozzles and/or equipment.

5.7.7 Equipment nozzle load analysis shall take friction into account, but no credit may be taken when friction reduces nozzle loads.

5.7.8 Pipe support considerations shall include concurrent anchor loads due to friction and thermal expansion.

5.7.9 Wind loads shall be determined in accordance with the Alberta Building Code.

5.7.10 Design for snow loads is not required except as required by Syncrude.

5.7.11 Design for seismic loads is not required.
