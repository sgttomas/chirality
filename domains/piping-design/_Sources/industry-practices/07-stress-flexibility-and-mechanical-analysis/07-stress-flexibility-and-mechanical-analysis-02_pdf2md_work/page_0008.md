5.5 Complete documentation in Paragraph 5.2 analysis shall mean clear filing of the following.

a) A sketch of the analyzed layouts with nodes, reference orientation and approximate dimensions clearly indicated.

b) Hand calculation, if any.

c) Computer analysis result including maximum stresses, significant loads on equipment nozzles.

d) Data sheets for spring hangers and expansion joints, if any.

e) Indication on sketches of any thermal movement of more than 50 mm (2 in.) and loads of more than 9 kN (2 kips).

5.6 Adequate documentation in paragraph 5.3 shall mean any calculations, notes and comments which confirm that an analysis was performed and adequate consideration was given.

## 5.7 Design Conditions

5.7.1 The Flexability Analysis shall consider the most severe conditions imposed by:

Revised Layout:

- Normal operation e.g. upset condition, regeneration, recycle.
- Other expected conditions, e.g. startup, shutdown, steamout, decoking, emergency shutdown, testing.
- Abnormal conditions, e.g. loss of cooling medium flow, cessation of process flow while line heating facilities remain operative. The analysis shall be for the maximum temperature differential. The effect of minimum and maximum installation and solar temperatures shall be considered in determining the maximum temperature differential.
