SYNCRUDE SPECIFICATION | PIPING MECHANICAL ANALYSIS

## 1.0 OBJECTIVE

This specification provides an analysis guide that will produce safe and reliable pressure piping system design in the plant.

Not all piping systems require formal mechanical analysis as described in this specification. According to ASME B31.3, no formal analysis of adequate flexibility is required in systems which are duplicates of successfully operating installations or replacements without significant change of systems with a satisfactory service record, or which can readily be judged adequate by comparison with previously analyzed systems.

## 2.0 SCOPE

This specification outlines the basic piping design requirements and consideration for thermal flexibility, sustained loads, vibration, fluid or gas flow reactions, environmental factors, and effects on equipment.
