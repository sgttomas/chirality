## 5.0 DESIGN

5.1 Mechanical analysis of piping systems is usually accomplished by using one of the following methods.

   a) Visual inspection based on past successful experience of similar configurations and design conditions as per ASME B31.3, Para. 319.4.1.

   b) Hand calculation using simplified methods such as guided cantilever and expansion loop charts commonly used by the industry.

   c) Computer analysis of simulated piping models.

The choice of analysis method shall be made by either qualified Engineers or under the supervision of qualified Engineers.

5.2 Computer analysis of thermal and deadweight load cases, with complete documentation, is mandatory for, but not limited to, the following piping systems unless paragraph 319.4.1 of ASME B31.3 governs.

   a) All lines with design temperatures over 400°C (750°F).

   b) All horizontal centrifugal pump piping NPS 4 or larger.

   c) All lines to and from reactors.

   d) All lines to and from centrifugal compressors with the exception of auxiliary piping.

   e) All steam lines to and from turbines.

   f) All air-cooled heat exchanger piping systems connecting to exchangers with four or more header box nozzles.

   g) All centrifuge piping NPS 3 or larger.

   h) All lines operating under severe cyclic conditions.

5.3 Thermal and deadweight analysis with adequate documentation, by computer or simplified methods may be used on the following systems.

   a) All lines NPS 16 and larger.

   b) Lines to and from reciprocating pumps and compressors.

   c) Lines NPS 6 and larger at design temperatures over 260°C (500°F) but less than 400°C (750°F).
