#### 5.11.4 High Pressure Drop Control Valves

When high pressure drop conditions across control valves exist, sonic harmonics along with extreme noise levels can be expected. Piping subjected to these conditions shall be designed to ensure that its size and configuration downstream of the valve prevent excessive vibration and noise.
