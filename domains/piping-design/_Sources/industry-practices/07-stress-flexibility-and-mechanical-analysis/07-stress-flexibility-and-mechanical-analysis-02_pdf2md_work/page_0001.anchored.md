**PROPRIETARY DOCUMENT**
**regarding**
**ENGINEERING STANDARDS and PRACTICES**

The proper use of engineering and engineering related practices has a positive influence on Syncrude's safety, environmental, reliability and profitability performance.

Syncrude Standard Practice Instructions capture and reflect current industry practices, legislative requirements and Syncrude experience. Adherence to these standards minimizes risk and increases reliability therefore it is expected that they be followed.

Opportunities for innovation and continuous improvement highlight the need to challenge existing standards and, when beneficial, to deviate from them. To do this, the implications of the change must be clearly understood through proper Change Management. The deviation process for SPIs must be followed and is described in SPI-1: Explanation and Revision of SCL Engineering Standards.
