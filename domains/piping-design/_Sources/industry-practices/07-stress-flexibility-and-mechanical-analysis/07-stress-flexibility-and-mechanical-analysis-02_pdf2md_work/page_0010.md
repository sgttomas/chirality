## 5.8.1 Vertical In-Line Pump

Small vertical in-line pumps, less than 900 kg (2000 lb) may be supported by soil bearing foundations or reinforced concrete slabs where approved by Syncrude. Piping forces shall be determined with the pump considered as a rigid, segment of the piping system.

## 5.8.2 Centrifugal Pumps

a) The maximum forces and moments which may be imposed on the pump nozzles shall be in accordance with API-610. The Contractor shall accept the allowables recommended by the pump Seller only if such allowables exceed the limits specified in API-610.

b) Piping force and moment limitations may be increased by 50 percent for reactions which occur only when a pump is not operating: e.g. the case of the idle pump (installed spare) or a condition during equipment steamout.

c) Use of a more rigid baseplate and support assembly for horizontal pumps shall be evaluated as an alternative to revised piping layout when computations indicate that the combined piping forces moment limitations would be exceeded (See API 610).

d) The effects of piping weight and friction forces due to thermal expansion shall be included in the evaluation of loads on pump nozzles.

e) For design purposes, when using a hot bypass the spare pump piping shall be assumed to be at 50 percent of design temperature of the operating pump.

## 5.8.3 General Purpose Steam Turbines

a) General purpose steam turbines shall be as defined in API 611.

b) The maximum forces and moments which may be imposed on the turbine nozzles shall be in accordance with API 611. The Contractor shall accept the allowables recommended by the turbine Seller only if such allowables exceed the limits specified in API 611.

c) Turbine nozzles loads shall also be analyzed in the cold condition with the permitted flange misalignment.
