End connections shall be socket welded for 1½-inch and smaller and butt-welded for all others.

The finish, unless otherwise specified, for all raised-face gasket contact surfaces shall be per MSS SP-6 and ASME B16.5 or B16.47. For services specifying Raised Face Smooth Finish, a finish between 63 to 125 AARH is acceptable.

All flanges with ring-type joints shall have a flat bottom groove per ASME B16.20, or API 6A, Design Type 6B, as applicable. Groove surface finish shall be as per MSS SP-6 and ASME B16.5 or B16.47.

Where flanges are specified as "jackbolt flanges" they shall be modified with two drilled and tapped jackbolt holes 180 degree apart on the flange bolt circle and centered between adjacent bolt holes. Jackbolts shall be ASTM A320 Grade L7 hex head bolts. Jackbolt diameter shall be a minimum of one-half of the standard stud diameter or 3/4-inch, whichever is greater. Jackbolt length shall be a minimum of two times the flange thickness. Jackbolt tips shall be ground smooth with a rounded end and a radius equal to the bolt diameter. The jackbolt holes shall be tapped to the lesser of the full flange thickness or twice the jackbolt diameter.
