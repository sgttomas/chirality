# A.1: Appendix A – Evaluating Notch Ductility

## A.1.1: Scope

This Appendix gives the requirements for impact testing of low-yield carbon steel pipe, fittings, flanges, and other items covered by the scope of this specification. Impact testing shall be performed when required by this or the applicable ASTM specification, when required by ASME B31.3 Section 323.2.2 (−20 °F minimum design metal temperature [MDMT]), or when indicated on the purchase description. The impact tests shall be Charpy V-Notch Type A in accordance with ASTM A370, except as modified below.

## A.1.3: Specimens

For all items, full-size transverse Charpy impact specimen shall be used except as follows. Flattened specimens are prohibited.

- If full-size specimens cannot be used because of thickness limitation, full thickness sub-size transverse specimen may then be used.
- If above still cannot be met because of product size and/or thickness limitations, full-size longitudinal (for both size and thickness limitations) specimen may then be used for the following sizes/thicknesses:
    - Sizes 4-inch and smaller, all wall thicknesses
    - Size 6-inch wall thicknesses extra-strong (XS) and under
    - Size 8-inch wall thicknesses standard weight and under
- For item actual wall thicknesses equal to 0.40 inch and thicker, full size specimens shall be used.
- The equipment used for the tests shall be calibrated according to ASTM A370 by an independent agency. Upon request from the Company, the Supplier shall provide the Calibration Certificate.

## A.1.4: Number and Location of Specimens

### A-1.4.1 General

The samples may be removed from a prolongation of an original flange, connector, or fitting, where feasible, to prevent destruction of a usable item. All Charpy V-notch specimens shall be the thickest standard size that can be prepared from the item.
