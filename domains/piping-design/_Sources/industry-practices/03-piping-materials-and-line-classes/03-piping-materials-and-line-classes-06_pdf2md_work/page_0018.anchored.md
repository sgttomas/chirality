### 6.3.3 Materials Not Requiring Impact Testing

The following materials do not require impact testing, unless otherwise specified in the applicable ASTM specification or on the Purchase Order:

- Low-yield, non-low temperature pipe, fittings, and flanges with nominal wall thickness less than those requiring impact testing per ASME B31.3, Section 323.2.2 for a Minimum Design Metal Temperature of −20 °F.
- Items for which the ASTM specification waives testing because they are too small to obtain a one-quarter size specimen, such as A333 pipe smaller than ⅛ XXH, ¼ Schedule 160 or 1-inch Schedule 80.

# 7 Mill Inspection Hydrostatic Test

Upon request, test records required by ASTM Standards shall be made available for examination by Company inspector.

# 8 Non-destructive Examination

## 8.1 General

All items, including pipe, shall meet the nondestructive examination (NDE) requirements of the applicable industry specifications, and the further requirements of this specification.

## 8.2 Fittings and Flanges

### 8.2.1 Tests

Each fitting and flange shall be inspected to ensure that tolerances listed in Paragraph 5.3 of this specification are not exceeded. Thickness measurements may be taken by ultrasonic inspection or by micrometer.

For small size fittings and flanges where ultrasonic testing is required but not feasible due to fitting configuration, magnetic particle examination per article 7, Section V of the ASME code may be used, except the yoke method of magnetization may not be employed. Acceptance requirements shall be per Appendix VI, Section VIII, Division I of ASME Code.
