## 6.2 Tensile Test Specimen Locations

### 6.2.1 Fittings

Tensile test specimens may be either transverse or longitudinal. Tensile test specimens shall be taken at the following regions:

- Tangent length (if heat-treated; base and weld).
- Transition zone (base and weld).
- Full formed and or extruded region(s): Locations shall be at both the most compressed and tensioned sections of the fitting base metal and at any welds.

Where fittings are too small to obtain test specimens from the fitting or a prolongation, the tests may be performed on specimens from a test bar that has been subjected to approximately the same working as the actual fittings, and is of the same heat as the actual fittings.

### 6.2.2 Flanges, Connectors, Blinds, and Spacers

The tensile test specimens shall be taken from a rough forged or finished flange or connector by cutting the test specimens from the hub parallel to the axis of flow through the hub. The samples may be taken from a prolongation of the hub to avoid destroying a usable flange or connector.

The tensile tests for blinds and spacers may be taken from the parent plate.

## 6.3 Fracture Toughness Test

### 6.3.1 General

When required by this or the applicable ASTM specification, or when specified on the Purchase Order, the items shall be impact tested in accordance with Appendix A. The toughness values shall not be less than the values listed in Table A-1 of this specification.

### 6.3.2 Materials Requiring Impact Testing

The following low-yield materials require impact testing per Appendix A, whether or not the requirement is listed on the Purchase Order:

- Low-yield, low-temperature materials, all thicknesses, including all blind flanges, slip blinds (blanks), and spacers.
- Low-yield, non-low temperature pipe, fittings, and flanges as specified in ASME B31.3, Section 323.2.2 for a minimum design metal temperature of –20 °F. See Appendix A for more specific details.
