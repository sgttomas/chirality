# 5 Chemical Properties and Test

## 5.1 Composition

The chemical composition of the steel used in the manufacture of pipe, fittings, flanges, and connectors shall fall within the limits of composition of the referenced standard in the purchase description. For A350-LF2, vanadium may not be increased above 0.05%, notwithstanding A350 Table 1, Note D.

## 5.2 Nickel Content

Carbon and low-alloy steel, including weld metal on welded items, shall have nickel content less than 1% per NACE MR-0175.

## 5.3 Acceptance

The analysis report shall include all elements listed in the applicable ASTM standard. If any value falls outside the specified limits, all the items from that heat shall be rejected, or at The Supplier's option, each item may be submitted for testing individually.

The Company reserves the right to make as many tests of the items as he considers necessary to ensure that the final product meets the specification.

# 6 Physical Properties and Test

## 6.1 Tensile Testing

- Low-yield carbon steel pipe and butt-weld fittings, excluding flanges and branch connections such as "weldolets", laterals, etc. shall have actual yield strength no greater than 25,000 psi above the ASTM specified minimum in the finished condition..
  - The ratio of actual yield strength to the ultimate tensile strength shall not exceed 0.85 for low-yield carbon steel in the finished condition.
  - The tensile tests shall be performed following final heat treatment.
- Tensile requirements of low-yield carbon steel items shall be verified by tension tests. All tensile tests shall be made by The Supplier per ASTM Standards as applicable, and the results reported to the Company.
