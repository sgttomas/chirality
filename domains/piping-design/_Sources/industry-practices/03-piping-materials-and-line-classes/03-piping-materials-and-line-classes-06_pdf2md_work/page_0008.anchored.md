| **Term** | **Definition** |
| :--- | :--- |
| | requirements (for flanges beyond the referenced ratings). |
| Connector | The term "connector" shall mean "Grayloc" or other proprietary type mechanical connectors. |
| Forged Fitting | The term "forged fitting" shall mean a billet section forged through heating and controlled compression between a set of forging dies. |
| 3-R | The term "3-R" fitting shall mean a fitting with a centerline radius of three times the nominal pipe diameter. |
| Short Radius Butt-weld Fitting | The terms "short radius butt-weld fitting" and "long radius butt-weld fitting" shall mean a pipe section curved to a centerline radius of one or one and a half times the nominal pipe diameter respectively, by heating and controlled bending. |
| Mandrel-formed Fitting | The term "mandrel-formed fitting" shall mean a pipe section curved to a 3-R radius through heating and controlled bending over a mandrel. |
| Factory Hot Bend | The term "factory hot bend" shall mean a pipe section curved to a 3-R radius through heating and controlled bending without a mandrel. |
| Induction Bent Fitting | The term "induction bent fitting" shall mean a pipe section with one or more bends formed through localized heating and controlled working without a mandrel on a machine specifically designed for that purpose. |
| Fitting | The term "fitting" shall refer any of the above, as well as caps, tees, crosses, y-laterals, reducers, sweepolets and extruded headers, and other proprietary forged or machined pressure containing components. |
| Low Temperature | The term "low temperature" shall mean any item with a rated service temperature lower than -20 °F. |
