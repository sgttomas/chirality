### 8.2.2 Rejection

If any fitting or flange fails to meet the test result requirements, two additional fittings or flanges from the same lot shall be tested. If either fitting or flange fails to meet the specified requirements, the entire lot of fittings or flanges shall be rejected unless subsequent heat treatment and 100 percent retesting shows acceptable results.

## 8.3 Hardness Testing

Testing shall be done in accordance with ASTM A370. Brinnell or Rockwell methods may be used.

Hardness tests shall be done on at least one piece from each heat treatment lot.

Hardness tests shall be performed after all forming and heat treatment. For heat-treated items, the test location may be anywhere on the outside surface. For non-heat treated items, the test location shall be in the most highly worked area accessible. Welded items shall also be tested on the weld seam and the heat affected zone.

Hardness testing shall consist of at least three impressions at each location.

No hardness shall exceed HB 237/HC 22. If any item does not meet the maximum hardness requirement, the entire heat lot shall be hardness tested.

In lieu of providing individual hardness test results, the Vendor may provide written certification that the finished material has been tested per the above, and meets the requirements.

# 9 Workmanship, Visual Inspection, and Repair of Defects

All sharp notches, imperfections, or gouges, shall be removed by grinding. A smooth curved surface shall be maintained and the final thickness after grinding shall not be less than the required minimum thickness. The remaining wall thickness shall be measured by ultrasonic methods.

If the Company Inspector concludes that the defect is excessive in accordance with the above, the item shall be rejected. Defects in pipe encroaching upon the specified minimum thickness shall be cut out.

The repair of injurious defects by welding is permitted only with the prior approval of Company.

Hard spots are not acceptable and shall be removed from the pipe as a cylinder.

# 10 Marking

All items shall be marked in accordance with the applicable ASTM/ASME Material Designations and MSS SP-25 as below. English units such as inches, pounds, etc. are preferred for markings. All markings shall be low stress die stamped (round-nose or interrupted dot stamp), or electrochemically etched, or paint stenciled, or marked with permanent ink.
