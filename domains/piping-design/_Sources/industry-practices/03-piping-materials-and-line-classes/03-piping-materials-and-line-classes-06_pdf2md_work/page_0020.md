The following information shall be marked on each item:

- Manufacturer's name or trademark.
- Material Designation (ASTM specification number and grade)
- Rating Designation (Pressure Class) as applicable
- Temperature rating (if pertinent and with corresponding pressure limit)
- Impact Test Temperature (when required by ASTM specification, or if the test temperature is other than that listed in the applicable specification for that grade)
- Nominal diameter and wall thickness/schedule no.
- Heat treatment (if marking is required by industry specification)
- Heat Lot No.

Items may be marked per minimum ASTM requirements if space limitation is a factor.

For split hot-tap fittings mark the top half on one end and the bottom half on the opposite end.

Cold die stamping shall not be used except on the outside surface of the flange rim or connector clamp assembly or handles of slip blinds and spacers.

If marking is to be done by stamp, low-pressure (blunt-nosed-continuous or blunt-nosed-interrupted- dot die) steel stamps in accordance with MSS SP-25 shall be used. Manufacturer shall insure the depth of impression will not infringe upon the required design minimum wall thickness. Steel stamping on welded construction fittings shall not be on any weld or heat affected zones.

All stenciled, painted, and inked markings shall be protected with clear weather-protective coating.

## 11 Handling and Shipment

Items shall be carefully handled and protected during all phases of manufacture, shipping, and storage. The Supplier is responsible for proper packing and care of materials to ensure the physical specified condition of weld bevels and gasket and threaded mating surfaces are not compromised due to normal shipping conditions.

The inner and outer surfaces of items shall be free of oil, grease, dirt, loose mill scale, and other foreign matter. All items shall be shipped with standard mill coating.

For flange sizes listed in the table below, all flange sealing faces shall be protected by heavy wooden or plastic covers securely fastened in place. A minimum of four bolts or poly plugs shall be required for securing all flange face covers. Flange weld bevels shall have securely fastened bevel protectors or plastic end caps.
