Boxed items, such as small fittings, shall be packed such that the externally threaded connections, sealing surfaces, and weld bevels are protected from damage due to shifting within the box.

The use of unprotected chains shall be prohibited at all times. Protected wire rope may be used to sling pipe less than eight-inch nominal O.D. only if dunnage is used to allow removal of the slings without marking the pipe. No slings may be left in place during transport. All pipe with plastic end protectors shall be loaded with dunnage between rows or bundles to permit unloading with slings. All dunnage shall be secured to prevent contact between the pipe and cargo lashing. For truck and rail transport, protected chains or nylon straps are allowed.

All items shall be palletized or securely fastened to trailers for shipping purposes. Large items and pipe shall be secured so that they do not come in contact with each other.

All railroad transportation of mill run pipe shall be in accordance with API RP 5L1. All marine transportation of mill run pipe shall be in accordance with API RP 5LW. The loading of rail cars, trucks, ships, and/or barges may be checked by Company Inspector at Company option.
