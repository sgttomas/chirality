### 3.1.2 Flanges, Connectors, and Proprietary Fittings

All flange, connector, and proprietary fitting design stresses shall be in accordance with the applicable allowable stresses specified in ASME B31.3. Except as noted otherwise in this Specification or in the purchase description, all low-yield flanges shall be made in accordance with ASME B16.5.

All special flanges, connectors, and proprietary fittings shall be designed to extrapolations of the referenced flange standards. Since these special designs shall be of a "custom" or "special" nature, The Supplier shall submit all design calculations and detailed dimensional drawings for Company review before commencing fabrication or with the quotation.

When the specified minimum yield strength of the flange or connector hub material equals that of the adjoining pipe, the hub design shall be in accordance with ASME B16.5 or B16.47, or MSS SP-44 or API 6A, Design Type 6B, as applicable.

All special flanges and connectors shall be supplied complete with all required gaskets, studs, and nuts. All studs shall be of a quality and strength not less than ASTM A320, Grade L7. All nuts shall be of a quality and strength not less than ASTM A194, Grade 7.
