# 1 General

## 1.1 Scope

This Specification (SPC) sets forth the minimum requirements for the manufacture, testing, and inspection of pipe, fittings, and flanges constructed of low-yield strength carbon steel materials (less than 52,000 psi specified minimum yield strength [SMYS]). This includes seamless and welded low- yield carbon steel pipe, seamless and welded butt-weld, socket-weld, and threaded pipe fittings, and welding flanges (including designated "specialty" flanges and "Grayloc" or other proprietary type mechanical connectors) for low temperature and high temperature services in carbon steel materials.

## 1.2 Alternative Proposal

The Vendor shall quote as specified. The Vendor may propose alternatives to this specification. Alternative quotes shall indicate where the proposal deviates from this specification, the reason for the alternative and the effect on cost, delivery, and performance.
