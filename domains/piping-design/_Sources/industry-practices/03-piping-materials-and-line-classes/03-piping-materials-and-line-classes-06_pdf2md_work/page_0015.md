Fabricated branch fittings made by joining two pieces of pipe (stub-in) are not permitted. Y-laterals may be submitted for approval by the Company on an exception basis.

Mitered type fittings are not permitted.

Circumferential welds within the fittings are prohibited.

The welding of a pup piece to correct fabrication defects or dimensional errors is not permitted on fittings.

Split hot tap fittings shall have no weld seams in the body of the fitting before use.

#### 4.2.3 Welding

Welds shall be made with the addition of filler metal; by welders, welding operators, and welding procedures qualified under provisions of ASME, Section IX. Welds shall be radiographically examined throughout the entire length of each fabricated weld in accordance with Paragraph UW-51 of ASME Section VIII, Div. 1, or ultrasonically examined per Appendix 12 of ASME VIII, Div. 1.

## 4.3 Pipe

All carbon steel pipe shall be seamless or double-submerged arc welded (DSAW) with single longitudinal weld seam.

## 4.4 Galvanizing

When specified, galvanizing shall be hot dip in accordance with ASTM A153. Electro- galvanizing in accordance with ASTM B633 is permitted for unions and small threaded and socket weld fittings only.

## 4.5 Heat Treatment

### 4.5.1 General

All items shall receive the heat treatment prescribed in the referenced standard, except as noted herein. All A-516 material that is cold formed shall be "Normalized" after forming.

### 4.5.2 Fittings

Heat treatment shall be on the entire fitting. Localized heat treatment on welds (of welded fittings) is not permitted. All split hot tap fittings requiring heat treatment shall have both halves heat treated in the same lot. The Supplier shall specify the proposed heat treatment on the quotation.

### 4.5.3 Pipe

Full body normalizing, or quenching and tempering, is acceptable for all pipe.
