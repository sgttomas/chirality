## TABLE No. 1 — CONVERSION FACTORS

### LINEAR MEASURE

- 1 inch (in.) = 2.540 centimeters (cm.)
- 1 foot (ft.) = 0.305 meters (m.)
- 1 yard (yd.) = 0.914 meters (m.)
- 1 mile (mi.) = 1.609 kilometers (km.)

### SQUARE MEASURE

- 1 square-inch (sq. in.) = 6.452 square centimeters (sq. cm.)
- 1 square foot (sq. ft.) = 929.034 square centimeters (sq. cm.)
- 1 square yard (sq. yd.)
- 1 square mile = 2.590 square kilometers (sq. km.)

### CUBIC MEASURE

- 1 cubic inch (cu. in.) = 16.387 cubic centimeters (cu. cm.)
- 1 cubic foot (cu. ft.) = 0.028 cubic meters (cu. m.)
- 1 cubic yard (cu. yd.) = 0.765 cubic meters (cu. m.)

### LIQUID MEASURE

- 1 ounce (oz.) = 29.573 (lbs.)
- 1 quart (qt.) = 0.946 liters (l.)
- 1 gallon (gal.) = 3.785 liters (l.)
- 1 gallon (gal.) U.S. = 0.833 Imperial gal.
- 1 gallon (gal.) = 231 cubic inches
- 1 barrel (bbl.) = 42 gallons (gal.)

### WEIGHT

- 1 ounce (oz.) = 28.350 grams (g.)
- 1 pound (lb.) = 0.454 kilograms (kg.)
- 1 ton (2000 lb.) = 907.200 kilograms (kg.)
- 1 ton (long) = 0.014,097 kilograms (kg.)

### TEMPERATURE

To convert Fahrenheit (F) to Centigrade (C):

$$C = \frac{5}{9}(F - 32)$$

### PRESSURE

- 1 pound per square = 70.307 grams per square inch (psi) centimeter (g. per sq. cm.)
- 1 atmosphere = 14.696 pounds per square inch (psi)
- 1 atmosphere = 760 millimeters of mercury at sea level 0°C (29.92 in. of Hg.)
- 1 atmosphere = 1.033 kilograms per square centimeter (kg. per sq. cm.)

### WEIGHT OF WATER

- 1 cu. ft. of water weighs 62.42 lb.
- 1 gal. at 50°F weighs 8.34 lb.
- 1 cu. ft. at one weight 57.2 lb.
- Water at or maximum density at 39.2°F weighs 62.42 lb. per cu. ft.
- Water at 212°F (boiling) weighs 59.83 lb. per cu. ft.

### WEIGHT OF GASES

- 1 gal. (U.S.) = 8.34 lb. × sp. gr.
- 1 cu. ft. = 62.4 lb. × sp. gr.
- 1 gal. = 0.1337 cu. ft.
- 1 lb. per gal. = 7.48 lb. per cu. ft.

### FLOW

- 1 gpm = 0.134 cu. ft. per min.
- 500 lb. per hr. = 1 gpm at water density of sp. gr. = 1
- 1 gpm = 449.0 gal. per hr. (gph)

### MASS

- 1 lb. (avoird.) = 16 oz.
- 1 stone (U.K.) = 14 lb.
- 1 ton (short) = 2000 lb.
- 1 ton (long) = 2240 lb.

### VOLUME

- 1 gal. (U.S.) = 231 in.³ (U.S.)
- 1 gal. (U.S.) = 0.833 gal. (Brit.)
- 1 cu. in. = 0.004329 (Brit.)
