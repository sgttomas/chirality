## Packing Performance Studies

The previous sections discussed the types of packings that are currently available for use and guidelines for selecting a packing material. This section will discuss how some of these packings have performed in various studies. As more and more types and brands of packing become available, it has become important to determine which packing type performs best in reducing fugitive emissions. This information will help in deciding which packings should be used within a facility.

As regulatory pressure on reducing fugitive emissions increased, so did the research on the leak rates of graphite packings. The newest information on the performance of low-emission valve packings (flexible graphite packings) includes:

- A 1991 Petroleum Environmental Research Forum (PERF) laboratory study (90-05) measuring emissions from 16 packing types, and the effect of aging on the different packing types.
- A 1992 PERF study (91-17) using field data from several petroleum companies to determine the performance of several different kinds of low-emission packing.
- A 1996 PERF study (94-05) evaluating the use of spool type packings alone as a complete packing system.
- Analyses of low-emission packing performance data from the Richmond Refinery based on quarterly screening data.

Each of these studies will be discussed in more detail below.

**Laboratory Emissions Testing (PERF 90-05).** In 1991, under the auspices of the Petroleum Environmental Research Forum (PERF), eight petroleum companies sponsored a research project (90-05) aimed at reducing emissions from valve packing glands [8]. This project focused on measuring the emissions from 16 different commercially available low-emission valve packing types in a controlled laboratory setting. The packings tested were:

–Argo 60006300 –Garlock Style 1312 –Chesterton GTP-I
–Garlock EVSP Simplified –Garlock 127 AFP –Chesterton Style 370
–Garlock 708 and Style 98 –RM Graphaseal –Marlo Style 7311
–Garlock 958 and Style 98 –DuPont Kalrez CRCC –EGC Co. Thermabraid
–Garlock Style 5888 (Teflon) –Slade 3300G
–Garlock Style 1300 –J.Crane 287 I
