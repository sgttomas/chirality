## 1432 Valve Packing for Hand Operated Valves

Tighter emission standards require better packing than what has been used in the past. Minimization of incidents, conservation of resources, and ever tightening controls governing fugitive emissions of volatile organic compound (VOC) streams require that careful attention be given to the selection and installation of packing materials shown to be the most effective in satisfying valve stem sealing requirements. This section covers the types of packings available, how some packings have performed during testing, and guidelines on how to evaluate other packings. Bellows sealed valves and packing cost are also discussed.

### Summary

For new valves in volatile organic compound service (VOC), use the manufacturer standard graphitic packing as long as it meets the general guidelines discussed in "Packing Guidelines from Valve Manufacturers" on page 1400-41. Valves purchased from the recommended manufacturer's listed in Figure 1400-13 should meet these criteria. The packing sets should consist of 2 braided graphite end rings and 3 flexible graphite middle rings. In addition, valves should be tagged with the type of packing so the packing's performance can be tracked through a facilities VOC Inspection and Maintenance program.

For situations where a valve can be disassembled for repacking, flexible graphite die-formed ring packing are recommended (with braided graphite end rings to prevent extrusion). The following packings have done well in research studies:

- Garlock EVSP Simplified
- Garlock 70#

**Repacking in the field.** For repacking valves in the field, braided packing is recommended because it is the easiest to install. Garlock 1303 FEP is the recommended braided packing. Slide 3300G and RM Graphseal have been used at some Chevron facilities, but have had mixed results in research and field tests. Die-formed middle rings can also be used for field installations by cutting the ring and twisting it over the stem. However, be careful not to break apart the rings.
