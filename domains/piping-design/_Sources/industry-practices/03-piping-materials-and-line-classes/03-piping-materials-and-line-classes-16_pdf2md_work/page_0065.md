## 1460 Corrosion of Valve Stems

High levels of salts and other materials are often used in manufacturing mechanical packing. In addition, it is thought that salts can be transferred to the packings during handling and installation. In the presence of water from hydrostatic testing or condensation, those salts go into solution to form an electrolyte, and can cause galvanic corrosion of the metal valve parts. Such corrosion will roughen the stem surface, create leak paths, and increase friction and packing wear. Corrosion is most likely to occur during storage of a packed valve. Galvanic corrosion can be reduced by incorporating a sacrificial material or a passivating inhibitor into the packing materials. Zinc and aluminum powders are used as sacrificial inhibitors (zinc inhibitors are preferred). The passive metallic ones typically are molybdate salts such as MoS₂ or Ba₂Mo.

When PTFE (Teflon) is utilized in service conditions above 450°F it sublimes and gives off hydrogen fluoride which, in the presence of water moisture, forms hydrofluoric acid and can cause stem corrosion. Some graphite packing materials are impregnated with PTFE which can also cause the generation of hydrofluoric acid under service conditions above 450°F. This limitation should be taken into consideration before specifying the use of PTFE packing.

### 1472 Service Limitations

In spite of its sealing advantages in a variety of services, there are a number of service limitations to flexible graphite packings. These are discussed below and are also outlined on Standard Drawing GC-L99771.

Graphite is subject to oxidation in air at temperatures above 850°F. However, the oxidation attack is reduced when a corrosion inhibitor is used. The recommended limit for hot air is 650°F. In steam service, flexible graphite can be used up to 1200°F. In non-oxidizing atmospheres it is limited to 5000°F, but for CO₂ and N₂ service, it is limited to 1600°F. Flexible graphite is usable in the full pH range from 0 to 14, but with the service limits stated below.

Flexible graphite **cannot** be used successfully in the following environments:

- Oxidizing acids: chromic acid, nitric acid, >85% phosphoric acid, sulfuric acid
