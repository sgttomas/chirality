**Summary.** The best performing packings in terms of being able to maintain low emission rates include:

–Garlock EVSP Simplified     –DuPont Kalrez     –Garlock 1303 FEP
–Garlock Teflon               –Argo Composite without Teflon spacers

RM Graphaseal performed well in the PERF 90-05 and 91-17 studies; however, it did not perform well in the PERF 94-05 or Richmond Refinery study. Slade 3300G performed well in the Richmond study but did not perform well in the PERF studies.

Keep in mind that the Teflon or Kalrez packings are not appropriate for use in hydrocarbon service because of their relatively low temperature limit.

These studies serve to illustrate that the performance of packings may vary significantly. The condition of the valve and the packing installation could play a very important role. These two variables could explain the differences in performance between the various studies.

Installing a packing incorrectly or into a valve in poor condition could outweigh any benefits that the new packing could provide. Because of the numerous variables involved, it is difficult to determine how any packing will actually perform, or whether you will see the same performance in your facility as has been seen in these studies. Consequently, it is very important to follow their performance over time and make decisions on future packing selection based on the actual performance in your facility.

## Packing Guidelines from Valve Manufacturers

Valve manufacturers often use their own choice of packing as standard packings in new valves. Specifying a different packing type when purchasing new valves could increase the price significantly since the valve is packed with the standard packing and then repacked before shipping with the specified packing. To avoid this additional cost, and to make sure that valve manufacturers understand Chevron's packing needs, use the following packing description, which outlines the minimum packing requirements [12]:

Gland packing shall consist of three to five die-formed flexible graphite middle rings, with one braided carbon/graphite ring top and bottom. Materials shall conform to the following:

**Braided Carbon/Graphite** - Fibers shall contain 95% carbon by weight minimum (Union Carbide, Polycarbon, or equal approved by purchaser), zinc powder or molybdenum corrosion inhibitor, graphite lubricant, and 100 ppm leachable chlorides maximum, 1000 ppm total sulfur maximum. Ring construction shall be interbraid weave and shall have no more than 5% weight loss upon ignition. No Teflon suspension shall be used in the manufacture of this packing.
