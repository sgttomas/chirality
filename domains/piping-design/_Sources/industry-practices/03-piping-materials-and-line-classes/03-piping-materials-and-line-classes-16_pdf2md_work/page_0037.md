**Richmond Refinery Data Analysis.** In 1994 the Richmond Refinery began an in-house study to determine the best low-emission valve packing types based on the results of the PERF 90-05 project. Four packing types were chosen for evaluation:

–Garlock 708  –RM Graphaseal

–Garlock #9000 EVSP Simplified  –Slade 3300G

Gradually these packings were installed into refinery valves during shutdowns.

Over a period of two years, monitoring data was collected quarterly and analyzed to assess the performance of the packings. A comparison of the emission rate for the packings in Richmond to the emission rates measured in PERF 90-05 is shown in Figure 1400-18. Both the emission rates based on initial screening values, and after two repair attempts (if >500 ppm), are shown. This is done to show how the four packings perform without attention (initial readings), and how they perform compared to regulatory limits (after repair attempts). Except for Slade 3300G, the packings had much higher emission rates (10 to 100 times) than they did in the PERF study. This is probably due to the difference in both the condition of the field valves and service conditions compared to the tightly controlled conditions in the PERF study. The Slade 3300G packing performed much better at the Richmond Refinery than in the PERF study which may be due to better installation or higher compression levels being used. The average emission rates at Richmond for Slade, Garlock 708, and Garlock EVSP are all equivalent to being less than 50 ppm (after repair attempts). The average emission rate for Graphaseal is equivalent to about 2000 ppm. This data only serves as an indication as to whether or not the packing can meet regulatory limits.

The performance of the low-emission valve packings (in% below 100 ppm) at Richmond for 1994-1995 is shown in Figure 1400-19. PERF 91-17 field test data is included for comparison. The data shows that the packings are performing better at the Richmond Refinery than they did at the other refineries providing data in the PERF study, which may be a result of a larger data pool.

To get a better idea of how the packings can reduce fugitive emissions, a comparison of valve performance before new packing installation to valve performance after installation is shown in Figure 1400-20. This shows that Garlock 708, Slade 3300G, and Garlock EVSP Simplified are all able to reduce the emissions from leaking valves by greater than 98%. Graphaseal was only able to reduce emissions by 77% [11].

Overall, an analysis of screening data from valves in the Richmond Refinery has shown that Garlock 708, Garlock EVSP, and Slade 3300G are all effective in reducing fugitive emissions from valves. Graphaseal, although a good performer in the PERF studies, did not perform as well as the other packings in reducing fugitive emissions.
