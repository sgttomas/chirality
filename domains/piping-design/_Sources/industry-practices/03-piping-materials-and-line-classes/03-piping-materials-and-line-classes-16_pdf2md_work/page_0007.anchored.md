By reducing fugitive emissions, it is expected that cost savings can be achieved because of:

- reduced incidents due to releases from valves, including fires, injuries, and spills
- reduced amount of maintenance and valve repair
- less chance of compliance violations and fees
- reduced permit fees
- decreased frequency of required inspection and maintenance
- informed decision making
