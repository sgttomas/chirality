## 1433 Packing Installation for Hand Operated Valves

Installing the packing properly is critical for reducing fugitive emissions from valves. Various aspects of packing installation are covered in this section, including an example of the impact packing procedures may have on emission rates, information on packing configurations, installation procedure, and enhancements for improved performance.

### Summary

A summary of Company recommendations follows:

- All existing packing should be removed carefully, preferably with a water jet, before installing new packing.

- Visually inspect the valve condition to make sure no obvious flaws exist, such as a corroded stem, which would affect the packing performance. A damaged valve may need to be reconditioned or replaced.

- In general, manufacturer guidelines, if available, should be followed when installing packing.

- Train personnel or contractors on installing new packings before large repacking efforts take place. If possible, have packing manufacturers give the training.

- Verify that the correct packing is being installed considering the valve service. Use packing with available information on its ability to control emissions well (vendor, facility, or PERF data).

- For deep stuffing boxes, reduce the depth by using a carbon bushing or central lantern ring.

- While packing, each ring should be compressed 2-3%. When the entire packing set is in place, it should be compressed to about 20-25% less than the original height (30% for spool type packing). Alternatively, a torque wrench can be used to tighten the packing gland if a recommended bolt torque is specified by the manufacturer.

Installing the packing correctly is a critical component to controlling valve emissions, along with having the valve in good condition and using the right packing. The above recommendations will help ensure that the packing is installed correctly.

### Importance of Proper Installation

In addition to making sure the valve is in good condition, and that the appropriate packing type has been chosen for a service, installing the packing correctly also plays a key role in achieving low fugitive emissions. Improper installation could likely outweigh any benefits resulting from installing low emission valve packing. Some data has shown that when personnel unfamiliar with a particular packing type installed that packing into valves, emission increased dramatically. The conclusion was that the emissions increase was likely due improper packing being installed.
