Automatic control valves have different design and packing requirements than hand operated block valves because of the need to maintain precise control over the stem movement. The recommendation for control valves are stated in Figure 1400-4.

| **Key Factor** | **Recommendation** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Automatic Control Valves** | - Valves that are not performing well and that have obvious mechanical defects (such as stem corrosion) should be removed and reconditioned.<br>- Use the valve manufacturer alliance with Fisher-Rosemont to ensure valve quality.<br>- Stem finishes of 4 rms provide the best performance.<br>- Avoid lifting valves by their actuators; instead cradle valves with a non-metallic slings.<br>- Use live-loaded combination packings for VDC control in service where the temperature is less than 450°F.<br>- Use graphite packings and oversized actuators for VDC control for valve in service where the temperature is greater than 450°F. |
