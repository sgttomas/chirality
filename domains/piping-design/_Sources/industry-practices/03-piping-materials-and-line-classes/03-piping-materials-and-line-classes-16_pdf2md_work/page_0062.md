## 1452 Repair Methods

Some of the common, recorded repair methods used are:

- Adjust Packing Gland
- Inject Soft Packing (Orbits, WKM's etc.)
- Repack Valve
- Leak Seal (Furmanite, Kopp)
- Replace Valve or Fitting
- Tighten Flange or fitting
- Renew Gaskets or O-Rings
- Apply Thread Sealant
- Install Plug
- Replace with Upgrade
- Renew Minor Parts
- Rebuild Component, Packing Box
- Adjust Gland & Inject Soft Packing
- Repair in Shop
- Use Cartridge Seal Repair

In general, the first attempt at repair should be a tightening of the packing gland and other fittings as this can usually bring the valve into compliance. If this does not work, try alternative methods. Repacking the valve is an effective option if the valve can be taken out of service. The valve should be reconditioned at the next shutdown if it is obviously in poor condition. Some of these methods are far more time consuming and costly than others and should be used only if other methods of repair fail.

There are some procedures for tightening that can enhance the effectiveness of the repair [14]. Overtightening can damage a flange, gasket or packing and increase leakage. Unequal tightening of bolts that are spaced around a flange can result in higher leakage. Tightening the bolts in the wrong order can create stresses in the gasket that result in higher leakage. Based on these principles, the following protocol is suggested for flanges:

- Tighten the first bolt (selected at random) by one half turn.
- Tighten the bolt 180 degrees from the first bolt by one half turn.
- Tighten the bolt at 90 degrees from the first bolt by one half turn.
- Tighten the bolt at 270 degrees from the first bolt by one half turn.
- If there are more than four bolts, continue to tighten them by a similar procedure that crosses the flange to promote even stresses around the gasket.
- Once all of the bolts are tightened by one half turn, check the emission with a portable instrument.
- If emissions are acceptably low, the repair is finished. If not, then repeat by tightening another one half turn in the same sequence on all bolts.
- Check the emissions and repeat as necessary or until the bolts will not move or are being stripped off, at which point the component would need to be put on the non-repairable list until shutdown.
