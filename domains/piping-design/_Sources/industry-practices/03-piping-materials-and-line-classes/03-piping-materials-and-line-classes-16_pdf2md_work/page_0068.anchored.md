**DuPont Kalrez**

This packing is recommended for new and shop repacked valves, but it is not suitable for field installation because the rings are not split; valve disassembly is required. *Manufacturer plans to determine if split rings can provide a suitable stem seal.*

- Addition of flexible graphite top ring has enabled packing set to pass fire-test requirements.
- Molded solid second, fourth, and sixth rings may be either PTFE Teflon, carbon-filled Teflon, or Zymaxx (carbon-fiber-filled PFA Teflon) as dictated by process temperature range. Molded solid third and fifth rings are Kalrez which is an Elastomeric Teflon.
- Avoid molten or gaseous alkali metals over 212°F. Service life may be reduced in fluids containing diamines, nitric acid, or base phenols.
- Manufacturer highly recommends lubrication with KRYTOX GPL-206 Fluorinated Grease during installation.
