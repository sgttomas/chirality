All repairs shall be completed prior to solution heat treatment.

## 8.2 Visual Inspection

Every pipe shall be visually examined externally and internally when diameter permits, for surface flaws in the body of the pipe. Defect acceptance levels for visual inspection shall be in accordance with this specification.

Illumination levels at all areas where visual inspection is to be carried out shall be a minimum of 33-foot candle (350 lx).

In addition, all mill run pipe shall be subject to inspection by the Company at all stages of the manufacturing and delivery process.

## 8.3 Repair of Defects

Minor cosmetic areas and flaws in the pipe not encroaching upon the required minimum thickness may be removed by grinding, provided a smooth curved surface is maintained and the final thickness after grinding is not less than the required minimum thickness. The remaining wall thickness shall be measured by UT methods. Defects in the pipe encroaching upon the specified minimum thickness shall be cut out.

Dye penetrant inspection (DPI) shall be used to confirm the removal of defects such as laps, scabs and slivers.

The repair of dents shall not be permitted after solution annealing.

The supplier shall maintain continuous supervision of inspection procedures by qualified supervisors directly responsible to management.

In addition to the above, all mill run pipe shall be subject to inspection by the Company at all stages of the manufacturing and delivery process.

All required test inspection shall be made at the place of manufacturer prior to shipment, unless otherwise specified, and shall be conducted so as not to interfere unnecessarily with the manufacturer's operation.

# 9 Traceability

Throughout the manufacturing process, a means of identification shall be used to maintain a strict accountability of all pipes.
