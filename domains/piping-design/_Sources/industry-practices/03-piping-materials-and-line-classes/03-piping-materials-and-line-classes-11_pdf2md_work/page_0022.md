## A.7: Test Temperature

The temperature shall be maintained at 194 °F or the maximum design temperature defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) if this is less than 194 °F. The acceptable tolerance shall be ±9 °F.

## A.8: Test Pieces

Four-point bend test pieces shall be machined from the pipe wall in the circumferential direction. If circumferential specimens cannot be obtained, longitudinal specimens may be used. Test-piece dimensions shall be agreed upon with the Company before commencing testing.

## A.9: Number of Tests

One set of SSC tests per condition (i.e., produced water and/or, if required by the Data Sheet for Line Pipe [22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless], condensing water) per heat; a "set" shall comprise duplicate tests.

## A.10: Applied Stress

The applied stress shall be 100% of the measured (actual) yield strength at the test temperature. The yield strength shall be determined on a round bar test piece (Figure 4 of ASTM A370-94) using the 0.2 percent offset method in ASTM A370. The required displacement shall be verified using suitably strain-gauged samples, in accordance with EFC 17. The use of published formulae (e.g., in ASTM G39) is not acceptable.

As an alternative, the use of a methodology based upon an applied strain may be offered for approval by the Company. Again, the displacement required shall be verified.

## A.11: Test Duration

The test duration shall be a minimum of 720 hours.

## A.12: Failure Appraisal

Total fracture of a test piece during the 720-hour test period shall be considered a failure. To be considered a pass, the test piece shall be free of any signs of fissures and/or cracks after the exposure period.

This shall be assessed through:

- Visual examination of the full length of the outside surfaces of the specimen (at a magnification of x 10).

- Confirmation of any visual indications using DPI.
