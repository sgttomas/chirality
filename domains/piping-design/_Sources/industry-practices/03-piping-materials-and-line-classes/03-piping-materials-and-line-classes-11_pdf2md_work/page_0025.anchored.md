- Prepare a base solution of the required levels of salts in distilled or deionized water.

- Add the organic acid salts and bicarbonate. (Note: in some instances, saturation with CO₂ is required before adding the bicarbonate to prevent precipitation. If this is the case, add AFTER the following step.)

- Introduce the solution into a vessel, fully deaerate using "oxygen free" nitrogen and saturate with a mixture of CO₂/H₂S (adjust composition of mixture to achieve the required partial pressure of H₂S and CO₂ at the test conditions of pressure and temperature).

- Transfer the preconditioned test solution to the vessel containing the 4PB test piece(s). (Note: ensure the test vessel and associated pipework is totally oxygen free by flushing with a suitable gas for a suitable period before transfer of the test solution; also, ensure no contamination with oxygen during solution transfer).

- Continue to condition the test solution with the test gas after transfer.

- Shut the autoclave in and allow the autoclave to come to the necessary pressure to achieve the required partial pressures of H₂S and CO₂ at the test temperature. Heat test solution to the test temperature and check that the necessary test pressure has been achieved on reaching the test temperature.

During the test, the pH may alter, but shall not exceed 0.2 pH units above the required pH. This is achieved through the complete exclusion of oxygen and the maintenance of a sufficient solution volume to test the piece surface area ratio. If the pH cannot be measured under the test conditions, measure the pH at the start and end of the test under ambient pressure/temperature conditions to demonstrate that the pH shift during the test has been less than 0.2 pH units.

During the immersion, specimens shall be kept away from direct contact with the container wall and the other specimens.

## B.6: Solution Volume

This shall be a minimum of 30 ml/cm² of specimen.

## B.7: Test Temperature

The temperature shall be maintained at the maximum design temperature defined on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) within a tolerance of ±9 °F.
