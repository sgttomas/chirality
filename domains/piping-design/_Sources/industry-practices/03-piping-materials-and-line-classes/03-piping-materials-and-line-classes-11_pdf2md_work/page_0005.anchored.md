# 1 General

## 1.1 Scope

This Specification covers the manufacturing, testing, and inspection of seamless and welded 22Cr and 25Cr duplex stainless steel line pipe. This specification supplements the following:

- ASTM A999 – "General Requirements for Alloy and Stainless Steel Pipe"
- ASTM A928 – "Ferritic/Austenitic (Duplex) Stainless Steel Pipe Electric Fusion Welded Addition of Filler Material"
- ASTM A790 – "Seamless and Welded Ferritic/Austenitic Stainless Steel Pipe

The guidance for 22Cr and 25Cr duplex stainless steel line seamless line pipe is based on API Spec 5LC – "Specification for Corrosion-Resistant Alloy (CRA) Line Pipe" and to standards as indicated in Section 2.0 Regulations, Codes and Standards. In the event of an inconsistency, conflict or discrepancy between any of the standards, specification or regulations, the contractor shall follow the most stringent and safest requirement applicable to the project. Any inconsistency critical to the design shall be brought to Company attention for approval.
