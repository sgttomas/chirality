## 3.1 Acceptance

One product analysis shall be performed in accordance with Section 9 of ASTM A790 or A928, and the results reported to the Company.

If the results of either of the product analyses fail, every pipe from the lot shall be tested.

## 3.2 Heat Analysis

Each required analysis of steel shall be made by the steel manufacturer to determine the percentages of the specified elements.

The chemical composition thus determined, or that determined from a product analysis made by the tubular product manufacturer shall conform to the requirement specified. For this requirement, a grade is defined as an alloy describe individually and identified by its own unified numbering system (UNS) designation in a table of chemical requirements within any specification listed within the scope.

## 3.3 Product Analysis

Product analysis requirements and options, if any, shall be contained in the applicable product specification.

Product analysis carried out on sample representative of the full material thickness, shall be performed at a frequency of two pipes per heat. One of these analyses shall be performed on the first pipe rolled from each individual heat of steel.

Product analysis variation shall be as agreed upon in the manufacturing procedure specification.

# 4 Microstructure

## 4.1 Sampling Location and Frequency

One specimen representing each lot of pipe shall be removed from a length of pipe after the final solution annealed heat treatment for microstructural examination. For welded pipe, the specimen shall include the weld metal and Heat Affected Zone (HAZ).
