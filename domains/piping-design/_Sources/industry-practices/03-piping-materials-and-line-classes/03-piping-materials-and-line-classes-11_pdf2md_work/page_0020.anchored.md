# APPENDIX A: Sulfide Stress Cracking Test Procedure

## A.1: Introduction

For 22% and 25% Cr super-duplex stainless-steel materials in which the service is defined as "sour," four-point bend (4PB) SSC tests shall be carried out as part of the production control tests.

Precise details of testing required shall be included in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless). The information to be supplied shall consist of:

- Test solution(s) composition (fully formulated produced water and/or, when the service on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe [22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless] is defined as gas, simulated condensing water).
- Test solution(s) pH (for information purposes only).
- Partial pressure of hydrogen sulfide and carbon dioxide at the test temperature.

The proposed, detailed test procedures shall be supplied to Company for review/approval before the commencement of testing.

## A.2: A.2 Sulfide Stress Cracking Test Method

Four-point bend testing shall be carried out in accordance with the procedures specified in EFC 17 and ASTM G39, together with the additional requirements:

- 4PB tests shall be undertaken such that the inner pipe surface is in tension.
- The inner pipe surface finish shall be "as manufactured," with the exception that the test specimens shall be thoroughly cleaned/degreased before testing.

## A.3: Reagents

The reagents shall be in accordance with those specified in NACE TM0177.

## A.4: Gases

High-purity (99.99%) H₂S/CO₂ gas mixtures shall be used. If possible (this is a requirement when not using a "shut-in" autoclave/vessel), the test solution shall be purged with the gas mixture throughout the test period.

## A.5: Test Solution Preparation

A test solution corresponding to the produced water composition defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) shall be prepared in accordance with a procedure agreed upon with the Company.
