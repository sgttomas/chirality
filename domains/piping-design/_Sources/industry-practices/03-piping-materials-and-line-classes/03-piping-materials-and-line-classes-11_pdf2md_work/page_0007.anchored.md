## 2 Manufacturing

Pipe shall be either seamless or welded. All seamless pipes shall be in accordance with ASTM A790. All welded pipe shall require the addition of a filler metal, and shall be in accordance with ASTM A928, Class 1 or Class 3. Pipe shall be supplied in the solution annealed and water quenched, descaled and pickled condition.

The acceptable pipe manufacturing process for the production of 22% and 25% chromium (Cr) duplex stainless steel seamless line pipe shall be either:

- Blast furnace refining followed by de-carbonization using both the Argon Oxygen (AOD) and Vacuum Oxygen (VOD) processes. If necessary, final adjustment or molten metal analyses and condition shall be carried out using the Ladle Treatment (LT) process. or
- Electric furnace melting and refining followed by AOD (if considered necessary), VOD, and LT.

### 2.1 Longitudinal Seam Welds

All seam welds shall be made using a filler metal.

Welding shall be performed by only qualified welders using Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) qualified in accordance with ASME Section IX. The qualification tests shall be conducted with base plate of the same nominal thickness as will be used for production. The test plate shall meet the requirements of this specification.

The brand name and designation of the weld filler metal and flux shall be included in the WPS and PQR (Procedure Qualification Record). The same filler metal or wire/flux combination used for qualification shall be used for production welding.

### 2.2 Circumferential Welds

Circumferential welding is permitted to provide the lengths requested in the purchase order. PWHT (Post Weld Heat Treatment) of circumferential welds can only be done with prior approval of the Company representative.

### 2.3 Heat Treatment

All pipes must be solution annealed and water quenched within the temperature range requirements of ASTM A790 or A928.

Lubricants shall be removed from the surfaces prior to heat treatment. The Supplier shall submit heat treatment procedures to the Company for approval.
