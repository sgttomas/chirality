# 11 Packaging, Handling, and Shipment

## 11.1 General

If specified in the purchase order, packaging, marking, and loading for shipment shall be in accordance with the procedures of ASTM A700.

Pipe ends and bevels shall be protected using a plastic end cap.

No chains are allowed for any reason.

Only nylon or other suitable nonmetallic materials are acceptable for straps.

## 11.2 Pipe Handling and Storage

A procedure shall be provided that defines the precaution taken during pipe handling and on-site storage to avoid mechanical damage surface contamination of 22% and 25% chromium materials. If there is possibility that iron contamination has occurred, the accessible pipe surface shall be inspected using the Ferricyanide or other agreed method to confirm the absence of contamination.

If responsibility for delivery of the pipe is included, this procedure shall also address loading and protection during transportation.

# 12 Certification

The Supplier shall certify in writing that the pipe meets the requirements of this specification. The purchase order, or attachment to the purchase order, will define the specific documentation requirements. This shall include, but not be limited to:

- List of documents requiring Company comment/approval
- Content list for the electronic manufacturing dossier.
- Certification dossier for each shipment
- Tally list for coating and installation contractor's use.

Certified copies of the material certifications and the results of the tensile, hardness, Charpy test and others shall be furnished to Company.
