- Metallographic examination of any indications confirmed by DPI (at a minimum magnification of x 100).

- If there are no indications, metallographic examination (at a minimum magnification of x 100) of suitably polished longitudinal sections at 1/3 and 2/3 through the width of the four-point bend sample.

## A.13: Data Reporting

Data reporting shall include test piece geometry, test solution and conditions (temperature, gas mixture), pH (initial and final), test duration, test result (failure/pass), and test piece surface appearance after test termination, with photographs where appropriate.
