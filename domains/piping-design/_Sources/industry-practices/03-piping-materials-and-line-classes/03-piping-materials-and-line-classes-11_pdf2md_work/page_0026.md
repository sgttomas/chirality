## B.8: Test Pieces

Four-point bend test pieces shall be machined from the pipe wall in the circumferential direction. If circumferential specimens cannot be obtained, longitudinal specimens may be used. Test-piece dimensions shall be agreed upon with The Company before commencing testing.

## B.9: Number of Tests

One set of SCC tests shall be carried out on the first two heats as part of the Manufacturing Procedure Qualification Test (MPQT); a "set" shall comprise triplicate tests.

## B.10: Applied Stress

The applied stress shall be 100% of the measured (actual) yield strength at the test temperature. The yield strength shall be determined on a round bar test piece (Figure 4 of ASTM A370-94) using the 0.2 percent offset method in ASTM A370. The required displacement shall be verified using suitably strain-gauged samples, in accordance with EFC 17. The use of published formulas (e.g., in ASTM G39) is not acceptable.

As an alternative, the use of a methodology based upon an applied strain may be offered for approval by the Company. Again, the displacement required shall be verified.

## B.11: Test Duration

Test duration shall be a minimum of 720 hours.

## B.12: Failure Appraisal

Total fracture of a test piece during the 720-hour test period shall be considered a failure. To be considered a pass, the test piece shall be free of any signs of fissure and/or cracks after the exposure period.

This may be assessed through:

- Visual examination of the full length of the outside surfaces of the specimen (at a magnification of x 10).
- Confirmation of any visual indications using DPI.
- Metallographic examination of any indications confirmed by DPI (at a minimum magnification of x 100).
- If there are no indications, metallographic examination (at a minimum magnification of x 100) of a suitably polished longitudinal section of the test specimen taken from the middle one-third of the specimen length between the two inner supports.
