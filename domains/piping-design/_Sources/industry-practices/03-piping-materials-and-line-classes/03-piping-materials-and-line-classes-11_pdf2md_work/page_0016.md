Manual ultrasonic reassessment of imperfections shall be carried out with a reference level determined by the signal amplitude obtained from an external N5 notch, without added gain.

Acceptance limits for laminations and inclusion clusters shall be as given in ISO 10124; the pipe body shall comply with Acceptance Level B2, and the pipe ends shall comply with acceptance Level B1.

## 7.4 Dye Particle Inspection of Seamless Pipe

Any appropriate indications from ultrasonic examination, together with the end bevel and the pipe surfaces within 1 inch of the end of each pipe end, shall be inspected using DPI.

- Equipment: The equipment shall be capable of detecting the presence of any defects, such as laminations, in the end bevel.
- Reference Standard: Pipe containing appropriate defects shall be made available, or a suitable field strength indicator shall be proposed so that checks on the efficiency of the DPI procedure and consumables may be made at any time during processing of the order.
- Acceptance Limits: Any inclusion or inclusion cluster greater than 0.12 inch in diameter, or any lamination detected on the end bevel, shall be cause for re-beveling.

Imperfections shall not be repaired by welding.

# 8 Workmanship, Visual Inspection and Repair of Defects

## 8.1 Workmanship

No weld repairs to the body of the pipe shall be permitted after solution heat treatment. Pipe shall NOT contain any dents.

After blasting and pickling, if grinding is necessary to remove surface blemishes or remaining scale, the surface roughness of any ground areas shall be equal to or less than the adjacent pipe metal base.

Cracks, sweats, and leaks shall be clearly marked and the pipe length shall be set aside for inspection by the Company representative. Pipe bevels containing laminations or other discontinuities shall be cut back until imperfections detectable by dye penetrant or ultrasonic inspection have been removed. Sharp-edged imperfections, regardless of depth, shall be considered as defect.

All weld seam repairs shall conform to the requirements of ASTM A928, paragraph 6.2.4. A weld seam repair procedure shall be approved by the Company prior to repair of weld seam defects. The following shall include:

- Type of defect to be repaired and location drawing.
- Defect elimination procedure.
