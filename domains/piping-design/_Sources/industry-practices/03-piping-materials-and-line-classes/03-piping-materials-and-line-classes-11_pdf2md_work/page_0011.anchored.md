## 5.2 Flattening Test

One flattening test shall be run on one specimen from each lot of seamless or welded pipe, as per A999, Section 21.

## 5.3 Guided Bend Test

One guided bend test specimen shall run on the long seam weld from one pipe from each lot of welded pipe as per A928, Section 12.

## 5.4 Hardening Testing

Hardness testing shall be performed in accordance with A790, on the same sections used for the tensile tests.

## 5.5 Impact Testing

Impact testing shall be carried out at the temperature and against the requirements given on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless). Unless otherwise specified on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless), the test temperature shall be the lesser of 32 °F or the pipeline minimum Td.

Unless otherwise specified on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless), the pipeline minimum design temperature (Td), shall be -50 °F.
