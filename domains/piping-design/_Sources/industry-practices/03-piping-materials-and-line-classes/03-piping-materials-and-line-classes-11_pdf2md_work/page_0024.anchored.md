# APPENDIX B: Autoclave Stress Corrosion Cracking Test Procedure

## B.1: Introduction

If the maximum design temperature given on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) is greater than 194 °F, SCC resistance tests, in accordance with the following procedure, shall be conducted as part of the material prequalification process. Precise details of the testing required shall be included in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless). The information to be supplied shall consist of:

- Produced water composition.

- Test temperature (i.e. Maximum Design Temperature given on Data Sheet for Line Pipe [22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless]).

- Partial pressure of carbon dioxide and hydrogen sulfide at the test temperature.

The proposed detailed test procedures shall be supplied to the Company for review/approval before the commencement of testing.

## B.2: Stress Corrosion Cracking Test Method

Four-point bend SCC tests shall be performed in accordance with EFC Publication No. 17 and ASTM G39, together with the following additional requirements:

- 4PB tests shall be undertaken such that the inner pipe surface is in tension.

- The inner pipe surface finish shall be "as manufactured" (with the exception that the test specimens shall be thoroughly cleaned/degreased before testing).

## B.3: Reagents

The reagents shall be high-purity (Analar) grades.

## B.4: Gases

High-purity (99.99%) H₂S/CO₂ gas mixtures and nitrogen gas shall be used.
