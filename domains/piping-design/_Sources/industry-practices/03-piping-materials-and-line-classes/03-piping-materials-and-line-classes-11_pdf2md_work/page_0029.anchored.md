- Introduce the solution into a vessel, fully deaerate, using "oxygen free" nitrogen and saturate with a mixture of CO₂/H₂S (adjust composition of mixture to achieve the required partial pressure of H₂S and CO₂ at the test conditions of pressure and temperature).

- Transfer the pre-conditioned test solution to the vessel containing the four-point bend test- piece(s). (Note: ensure the test vessel and associated pipework is totally oxygen free by flushing with a suitable gas for a suitable period before transfer of the test solution; also, ensure no contamination with oxygen during solution transfer).

- Continue to condition the test solution with the test gas after transfer.

- Shut the autoclave in and allow the autoclave to come to the necessary pressure to achieve the required partial pressures of H₂S and CO₂ at the test temperature. Heat test solution to the test temperature and check that the necessary test pressure has been achieved on reaching the test temperature.

During the test the pH may alter, but shall not exceed 0.2 pH units above the required pH. This is achieved through complete exclusion of oxygen and the maintenance of a sufficient solution volume to test the piece surface area ratio. If the pH cannot be measured under the test conditions, measure the pH at the start and end of the test under ambient pressure/temperature conditions to demonstrate that the pH shift during the test has been less than 0.2 pH units.

## C.6: Solution Volume

This shall be a minimum of 30 ml/cm² of specimen.

## C.7: Test Temperature

The temperature shall be maintained at the maximum design temperature defined on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) within a tolerance of ±9 °F.

## C.8: Test Pieces and Specimen Preparation

The specimens shall be taken from the pipe wall and shall measure a minimum of 2 inches × 1 inch. Specimens shall be ground flat to a 600# paper finish on the outside, the internal surface being maintained in the "as produced" condition. Specimens shall be thoroughly degreased, dried in a nitrogen gas stream, weighed, and finally kept in desiccators before immersion.

## C.9: Number of Tests

One set of pitting corrosion tests shall be carried out on the first two heats as part of the MPQT, a "set" shall comprise triplicate tests.
