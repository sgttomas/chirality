## 4.2 Examination and Assessment of Ferrite Content

The metallographic specimen shall be polished and etched and examined at a minimum magnification of 400 times (x400), shall be carried out on both longitudinal and transverse specimens taken from a minimum of one pipe per heat after final heat treatment. Photomicrographs of all metallography shall be part of the permanent mill report to the Company. The material shall be essentially free from grain boundary carbides, nitrides and intermetallic phases such as sigma.

The ferrite content shall be determined by the manual point counting method in accordance with ASTM E562 or by a computerized quantitative image analysis technique. Use of any other technique requires prior approval of the Company.

The ferrite content shall be measured in the base material for all pipe and in the HAZ and weld metal of welded pipe. The average ferrite content of each region tested shall be 35% to 55%.

## 4.3 Sulfide Stress Cracking Test

Sulfide stress corrosion (SSC) cracking test shall be in accordance with Appendix A. This should be carried out on at least one of the pipes selected from each mechanical testing in accordance with Section 5. The test solutions and other test variables shall be defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless Steel Seamless).

## 4.4 Stress Corrosion Cracking and Pitting Corrosion Test

High-temperature Stress Corrosion Cracking (SCC) and pitting corrosion tests are required to "pre-qualify" the material for the intended application and shall be carried out in accordance with Appendices B and C, respectively, on one pipe selected from each of the first two heats as part of the manufacturing procedure qualification test. The test solution to be used shall be as defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless).

# 5 Mechanical Properties

All mechanical property tests, including Charpy V-Notch (CVN) impact tests, shall be performed after final solution annealed, sizing, and straightening. All test procedures shall be in accordance with the relevant ASTM standard.

## 5.1 Tensile Test

Tensile test shall be in accordance with the tensile properties in the applicable product specification. The tensile test shall be performed on a minimum of two pipes from each heat with test position being representative of the manufacturing process condition.
