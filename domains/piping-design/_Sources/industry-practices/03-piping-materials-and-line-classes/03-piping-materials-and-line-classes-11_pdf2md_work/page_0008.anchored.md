All pipes shall be descaled and pickled after long seam welding and final heat treatment. Welding repairs on the long seam welds cannot be performed after the final heat treatment.

## 2.4 Cold Working

Cold working after final heat treatment is not allowed except for sizing and straightening of pipe. Cold sizing shall not result in more than 1½% reduction in cross-sectional area. Cold bending for straightening shall not exceed 2 degrees. Cold work is not permitted for the purpose of enhancing mechanical properties.

# 3 Chemical Composition

For 22% Cr pipe, the composition of the steel to be used in the manufacture of the pipe shall be according to UNS S31803 or S32205, with an additional requirement that nitrogen content shall be 0.14 to 0.20 weight percent. PRE~W~ shall be a minimum of 33.

For 25% Cr pipe, the composition of the steel to be used in the manufacture of the pipe shall be as per UNS S32750, or S32760, or S39274, or S39277. PRE~W~ shall be a minimum of 40.

A preferred steel composition (heat and product analyses) for the specific material nominated on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) shall be nominated for approval by the Company Representative. The proposed analysis and the tolerances applied to each element shall also be subject to approval by the Company Representative. Alternative grades may be proposed, subject to approval by the Company.
