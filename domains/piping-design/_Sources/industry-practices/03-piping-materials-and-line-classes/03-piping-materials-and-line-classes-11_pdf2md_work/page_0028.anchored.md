## APPENDIX C: Autoclave Corrosion/Pitting Corrosion Test Procedure

### C.1: Introduction

As part of the material prequalification process, pitting corrosion performance tests shall be performed in accordance with the following procedure.

Precise details of testing required shall be included in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless). The information to be supplied shall consist of:

- Produced water composition.
- Test temperature (i.e., maximum design temperature given on Data Sheet for Line Pipe [22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless]).
- Test pressure of carbon dioxide and hydrogen sulfide at the test temperature.

The proposed, detailed test procedures shall be supplied to Company for review/approval before the commencement of testing.

### C.2: Test Method

During the immersion, specimens shall be kept away from direct contact with the autoclave wall and other specimens.

### C.3: Reagents

The reagents shall be high-purity (Analar) grades.

### C.4: Gases

High-purity (99.99%) CO₂/H₂S gas mixtures and nitrogen gas shall be used.

### C.5: Test Solution Preparation

The test solution shall correspond to the produced water composition defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless).

The procedure for solution preparation/conditioning shall be as follows:

- Prepare a base solution of the required levels of salts in distilled or deionized water.
- Add the organic acid salts and bicarbonate (note: in some instances, saturation with CO₂ is required before adding the bicarbonate to prevent precipitation. If this is the case, add AFTER the following step).
