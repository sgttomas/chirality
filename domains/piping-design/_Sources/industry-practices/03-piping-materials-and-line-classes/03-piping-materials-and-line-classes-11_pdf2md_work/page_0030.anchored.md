## C.10: Test Duration

Test duration shall be a minimum of 720 hours.

## C.11: Data Analysis

The specimens shall be photographed and examined immediately after exposure, and their condition shall be noted.

They shall then be mechanically cleaned by scrubbing with a soft brush under running water followed by the application of an appropriate cleaning solution (e.g., inhibited acid) to remove any scale (refer to ASTM Standard G1).

Specimens shall be dried and reweighed to identify the weight loss.

After weighing, the specimens shall be visually examined for corrosion/pitting with the aid of a stereomicroscope at X40.

To be considered acceptable, the test coupons on the prepared outer surface sides and the original pipe inner surface shall be free from:

- Pitting greater than 0.004 inch (0.1 mm) in diameter.
- Localized corrosion.

If pitting does occur, pits that are greater than 0.004-inch (0.1 mm) diameter shall be reported. The report shall include the number, maximum depth (inches [mm]), population per unit area, and equivalent pitting rate (milliliters [ml]/year [mm/year]).

In addition, the test coupons shall have a (weight loss) general corrosion rate equivalent to less than (ml/year [0.1 mm/year]).

## C.12: Data Reporting

As a minimum, data reporting shall include the following:

- Specimen geometry.
- Test solution and conditions (temperature, total pressure, and partial pressure of CO₂ and H₂S).
- pH (initial and final in ambient conditions).
- Specimen surface appearance subsequent to test termination together with appropriate photographs.
- Initial and final weight (milligrams [mg]).
