The minimum hydrostatic test pressure shall be as stated on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) (it is the intention to test at a pressure corresponding to a fiber stress of 95% of the minimum specified yield strength based on the minimum allowable wall thickness).

Hydrostatic testing shall be carried out on the pipe in its final condition and before final inspection.

# 6 Dimensions, Weights and Lengths

## 6.1 Diameter

All outside diameters, wall thicknesses, weights, and tolerances shall conform to ASTM A999. Pipe ends shall be prepared with a single bevel, 30 degrees (+5, -0 degrees), unless otherwise stated.

Pipe diameter shall be measured using circumferential (pi) tape, and out-of-roundness shall be determined by direct measurement. The maximum tolerance for diameter shall be 0.012 inch for all pipes. In Addition, along the whole length of the pipe, the maximum outside diameter (OD) measured at any point shall not exceed the minimum OD, measured by the same technique at the same point, by more than 1% or 0.16 inch whichever is lesser.

The controlling dimensions shall be revised to inside diameter (ID) and tolerance on the ID within 4 inches of the pipe ends shall be ±0.08 inch. The internal out-of-roundness within 4 inches of any pipe end shall not exceed 0.12 inch or 0.75% of the ID whichever is lesser.

The finished pipe shall be reasonably straight. The deviation from straight line shall not exceed 0.10%.

## 6.2 Wall Thickness

The wall thickness tolerance shall be:

- ±12.5% for nominal wall thickness less than 0.4 inch.
- ±10% from nominal wall thickness greater than or equal to 0.4 inch.

Note:

For specific applications, enhanced tolerances on pipe I.D. may require the pipe to be bore machined. Any such requirements will be defined on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless).
