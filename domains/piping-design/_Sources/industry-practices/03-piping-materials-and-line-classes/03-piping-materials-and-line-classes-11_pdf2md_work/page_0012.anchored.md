The energy values for full size, transverse specimens shall be a minimum average of 55.31 ft-lbs with a minimum individual of 40.57 ft-lbs.

If applicable, alternative energy requirements for sub-size or longitudinal tests shall be as defined on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless).

One set of Charpy impact tests in accordance with ASTM A370 (a set consists of three specimens) shall be performed on welded pipe on the weld, and one set on the HAZ from one length of pipe from each lot.

Specimens shall be oriented transverse to the longitudinal axis of the pipe with the notch oriented through the pipe wall thickness so that the crack propagates in the longitudinal direction of the pipe.

For pipe that accommodates 1/2-size (0.394 x 0.197 in) Charpy specimens, the specimens shall be machined from the pipe without flattening. For sizes too small for 1/2-size specimens, Charpy specimens of the full wall thickness shall be machined from flattened blanks to achieve the largest possible specimen size.
