# 7 Nondestructive Testing

## 7.1 General

The full length of each pipe shall be inspected for longitudinal and transverse defects by multi-probe automatic ultrasonic examination equipment.

Nondestructive inspection for acceptance shall be carried out after completion of any cold expansion, heat treatment, and hydrostatic test using equipment, techniques, and procedures approved by the Company Representative.

If flaws are indicated by any of the inspection techniques, Company shall require additional testing to demonstrate the acceptability of the pipes.

Manual ultrasonic inspection shall be used to inspect any portion of the pipe length not covered by all automatic probes.

After end preparation, ultrasonic inspection using compression wave techniques shall be carried out on both ends of each pipe in a full circumferential band at least 2 inches wide to ensure freedom from laminations.

Dye penetrant inspection (DPI) shall be carried out on the finished pipe ends.

## 7.2 Personnel Certification

Personnel carrying out nondestructive inspection shall be experienced in using the equipment specified and shall be qualified to a minimum of American Society for Nondestructive Testing (ASNT) TC-1A Level 2.

The person responsible for the preparation of inspection procedures and supervision of inspection activities shall be qualified to ASNT-TC-1A Level 3.

## 7.3 Ultrasonic and Electromagnetic Inspection of Seamless Pipe

The automatic ultrasonic probe array shall scan the pipe along a helical path with a separation between adjacent probes, which ensures the:

- Detection of longitudinal and transverse defects with 100% of the pipe surface being inspected
- Measurement of wall thickness and detection of pipe body laminations over 100% of the pipe volume.
