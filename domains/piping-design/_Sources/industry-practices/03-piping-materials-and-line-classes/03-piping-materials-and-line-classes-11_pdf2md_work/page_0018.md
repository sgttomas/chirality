Should heat, heat treat lot, or part number traceability be lost during processing, the affected pieces shall be rejected.

Disposition of rejected pipe shall be a matter of agreement between manufacturer and the purchaser.

Pipe that fails in any of the forming operations or in the process of installation and is found to be defective shall be set aside and the manufacturer shall be notified for mutual evaluation of the suitability of the pipe. Disposition of such pipe shall be matter for agreement.

# 10 Marking and Coating

All pipe manufactured in conformance with this specification shall have all required markings in accordance with ASTM A790 or A928, as applicable. In addition, the purchase order (PO) number shall be included in the markings.

Marking materials shall be free of any harmful contaminants such as zinc, lead, chlorides, sulfur, and other low melting point metals using a water insoluble ink.

All die stamping is prohibited.

All pipes shall be shipped without mill coating. The pipe surface shall be free of oil, grease, dirt, loose mill scale, and other foreign matter.

Units used for pipe markings shall be U.S. Customary as defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless).
