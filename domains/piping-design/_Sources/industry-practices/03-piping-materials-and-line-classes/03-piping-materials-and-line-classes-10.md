# 1 General

## 1.1 Scope

This Specification covers the manufacturing, testing, and inspection of duplex stainless steel fittings made from plate or pipe, as well as line blinds and spacers made from forgings or plate, and flanges, forged connectors, and forged fittings for use in gas, oil, and water systems.

# 3 Manufacturing

## 3.1 General

All fittings shall be made in such a manner as to preserve the cross-sectional shape of the fitting, and shall be free from wrinkling, buckling, cracks, or other evidence of mechanical damage. Waves with a width-to-depth ratio of 12 to 1 or greater are acceptable.

Fittings made from pipe containing a longitudinal weld, it shall be positioned as near as practicable to the least distortion axis of the fitting.

Blind flanges, blind spectacles, blind paddles, and spacer paddles may be offered constructed of low temperature CS (-50 °F), with a weld overlay of Incoloy 825 (UNS N08825) or Alloy 20 (UNS N08020), instead of solid duplex. The Manufacturer shall submit welding and fabrication procedures for approval.

For connectors, only the hub pieces and seals must be duplex. The clamps may be -50 °F rated CS, unless otherwise noted.

All items must be solution annealed and water quenched within the temperature range requirements of ASTM A815 or A182.

After final heat treating, all items shall receive a chemical descaling and neutralizing treatment before the final repassivation of the product. Air repassivation is acceptable unless otherwise noted.

## 3.2 Longitudinal Seam Welds

All seam welds shall be made using a filler metal.

Welding shall be performed by only qualified welders using Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) qualified in accordance with ASME Section IX. The qualification tests shall be conducted with base plate of the same nominal thickness as will be used for production. The test plate shall meet the requirements of this specification.

The brand name and designation of the weld filler metal and flux shall be included in the WPS and PQR (Procedure Qualification Record). The same filler metal or wire/flux combination used for qualification shall be used for production welding.

Welding repairs on the long seam welds may not be performed after the final heat treatment.

## 3.3 Cold Working

Cold working after final heat treatment is not allowed except for sizing and straightening of fittings. Cold sizing shall not result in more than 1.5% reduction in cross-sectional area. Cold bending for straightening shall not exceed 2 degrees. Cold work is not permitted for the purpose of enhancing mechanical properties.

# 4 Chemical Composition

## 4.1 Composition

For 22% Cr items, the composition of the steel to be used shall be as per UNS S31803 (F51) or as per UNS S32205 (F60), with an additional requirement that the nitrogen content shall be 0.14 to 0.20 weight percent. PRE (PRE = %Cr + 3.3x%Mo + 16x%N) shall be a minimum of 33.

For 25% Cr items, the composition of the steel to be used shall be as per S32750 (F53), or S32760 (F55), or S39274 (F54), or S39277 (F57). PRE shall be a minimum of 40.

# 5 Microstructure

The ferrite content shall be determined for the base, weld metal and HAZ. The average ferrite content of each region tested shall be between 30% to 65%.

# 6 Mechanical Properties

All mechanical property tests, including CVN impact tests, shall be performed after final solution annealed, sizing and straightening. All test procedures shall be in accordance with the relevant ASTM standard.

Unless otherwise noted, the physical properties for fittings, blinds, and spacers made from pipe or plate shall comply with ASTM A815, while the properties of flanges, connectors, forged fittings and blinds and spacers made from forgings shall comply with ASTM A182.

## 6.1 Tensile Test

Tensile test shall be in accordance with the tensile properties in the applicable product specification.

## 6.2 Hardness Test

Hardness measurements shall be made in accordance with the applicable product standard.

## 6.3 Impact Testing

Two set of Charpy impact tests in accordance with ASTM A370 (a set consists of three specimens) shall be performed on welded fittings on the weld, and one set on the HAZ from one fitting from each lot.

Charpy specimens from the weld shall be machined with the notch along the centerline of the weld and oriented through the wall thickness so that the crack propagates in the longitudinal direction of the fitting. Flattening of the specimens shall be permitted.

For items that accommodate 2/3-size (0.394 x 0.264 in) Charpy specimens, the specimens shall be machined without flattening. For sizes too small for 2/3-size specimens, Charpy specimens of the full wall thickness shall be machined from flattened blanks to achieve the largest possible specimen size.

The Charpy tests shall be performed at -50 °F, regardless of specimen size, and meet the following requirements.

The lateral expansion, in mils (0.001 inch), and the percent shear shall be reported for information.

The Company's Inspector is authorized to permit a retest if there is reason to believe that the failure of a specimen to show the minimum impact value required is due to some fault in its preparation or testing and that it has not provided a true indication of the notch ductility of the material.

If any specimen fails to pass the test, the remainder of the fittings in the heat may, at the option of the Supplier, be submitted individually for tests and acceptance.

# 7   Dimensions, Weights and Lengths

## 7.1   Fittings

The diameter, wall thickness, and grade of the fitting shall be as shown on the PO. Fitting dimensions shall comply with the following:

- All fittings shall have dimensions compliant with ASME B16.9, except tolerances for fittings 16-inch and larger shall comply with MSS SP-75, Table 3.
- Screwed and socket welding fittings shall comply with ASME B16.11.
- Forged 3,000-lb. screwed and socket welding unions shall comply with MSS SP-83.
- When 3R bends are specified, the "A" dimension of Table 2 in ASME B16.9 shall be two times the dimension shown. Straight tangents 2 feet 0 inches long shall be added to both ends of 3R bends, unless otherwise specified.
- Unless otherwise noted, transition pieces shall have lengths equal to ASME B16.9 reducer lengths. When specified, some transition pieces will be required to have drawn outlets (for use with bypass valves).
- Non-standard and proprietary fittings that are not manufactured to an industry standard; such as weldolets, laterals, etc., shall be furnished with the dimensions of the Manufacturer referenced in the purchase description. All other manufacturing considerations shall comply with the design standard referenced in the purchase description.
- All tolerances shall comply with MSS SP-75 or ASME B16.9, as applicable and as further specified herein. For fittings larger than 48 inches nominal OD, the tolerances shall be the same as for 48-inch nominal OD.
- The maximum wall thickness of the completed fittings shall not exceed the specified wall thickness by more than 15% at the tangent pipe (i.e., fitting ends) without prior agreement by the Company. If the minimum ID is more than 3/16 inch smaller than the nominal ID of the adjoining pipe, the ID of the fitting shall be taper bored in accordance with the applicable code.
- All fitting ends shall be beveled for welding according to ASME B16.25, Figures 1, 2a, or 3a, as appropriate (extrapolated as required for sizes larger than 36 inches nominal OD), unless otherwise specified in the PO. Any beveled ends not meeting the dimensions specified shall be machined at no cost to the Company.
- All fittings specified as having controlled roundness throughout shall have a maximum allowable OD out-of-roundness at any point of +1%.

## 7.2 Flanges and Connector

The diameter, wall thickness, and grade of the flanges and connectors shall be as shown on the PO. Dimensions shall comply with the following:

- Except as noted otherwise in this specification, all flanges shall be made in accordance with ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47 or MSS SP-44.

- All connector assemblies shall have three components: a metal seal ring, hubs, and a clamp assembly. The connector hubs shall be made of the same generics material as the piping system they connect. All connectors shall be dimensionally interchangeable with mechanical connectors. The Supplier shall furnish with the quotation a detailed dimensional drawing of all connector assemblies for review.

- All special flanges or connectors in sizes and ratings not covered by ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47, or MSS SP-44 shall be designed to extrapolations of the referenced flange standards. Since these special designs shall be of a custom or special nature, the Vendor shall submit all design calculations and drawings for Company review before commencing fabrication. All special flanges and connectors shall be supplied complete with all required gaskets, studs, and nuts.

- All flanges of ½ - 1½ inches nominal pipe size shall be socket welded type. All flanges of 2-inch nominal pipe size and larger shall be butt welded type.

- All flanges of ASME ratings 150#, 300#, or 600# shall be RF. All flanges of ASME ratings 900#, 1500#, or 2500# shall be RTJ. All flanges of API ratings 2000#, 3000#, 5000#, 10000#, or 20000# shall be RTJ.

- All flanges of 2 - 14 inches nominal pipe size or ASME 1500# and 2500# flange rating shall meet the dimensional requirements of ASME B16.5. All flanges of larger nominal pipe size or ASME 150# through 900# shall meet the dimensional requirements of MSS SP-44, or ASME B16.47 Series A, unless otherwise specified.

- For materials not shown in ASME B16.5 Table 2 or meeting the requirements of MSS SP-44 Section 3, allowable pressure-temperature ratings shall be provided, calculated in accordance with ASME B16.5 Annex D.

- The PO or BOM shall state the wall thickness and SMYS of the pipe to which each flange or connector shall be welded.

- When the SMYS of the flange or connector hub material equals that of the adjoining pipe, the hub design shall be in accordance with ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47, MSS SP-44, or API Spec 6A, Design Type 6B, Material Type 4, as applicable.

- The Supplier shall bevel the hub ends in accordance with ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47, MSS SP-44, or API Spec 6A, Design Type 6B, Material Type 4, as applicable.

- The Supplier shall bore the flange or connector to match the ID of the pipe to which it shall be welded.

- The finish for all RF gasket contact surfaces shall comply with MSS SP-6.

- All flanges with RTJs shall have a flat bottom groove in accordance with ASME B16.20 or API Spec 6A, Design Type 6B, Material Type 4, as applicable.

- All special flange and connector studs shall be of a quality and strength not less than ASTM A320 grade L7 with ASTM A194 grade 7 nuts.

- Where flanges are specified as "jackbolt flanges" they shall be modified with two drilled and tapped jackbolt holes 180 degrees apart on the flange bolt circle and centered between adjacent bolt holes. Jackbolts shall be ASTM A193 grade BBM hex head bolts. Jackbolt diameter shall be a minimum of one-half of the standard stud diameter or ⅝-inch, whichever is greater. Jackbolt length shall be a minimum of two times the flange thickness. Jackbolt tips shall be ground smooth with a rounded end and a radius equal to the bolt diameter. The jackbolt holes shall be tapped to the lesser of the full flange thickness or twice the jackbolt diameter.

- All slip blind and spacer "T" dimensions shall meet the requirements of ASME B16.48.

## 8 Nondestructive Testing

NDT shall be based upon ASME Section V. Each item, NPS 4 and larger, shall be full body, 100% UT inspected for longitudinal and transverse defects after forming and heat treating. Welded items shall have all welds 100% radiographically inspected after heat treating according to ASME Section VIII, UW-51 (e.g., ASTM A815 Class WP-WX).

All weld bevels are to be checked for lamination by UT or PT processes. No lamination is acceptable.

All personnel performing NDT shall be certified in accordance with ASNT SNT-TC-1A. Personnel interpreting or evaluating results of NDT for acceptance or rejection shall be certified to ASNT SNT-TC-1A Level II or Level III.

- Thoroughly investigate all indications of imperfections using UT and visual methods.

- Any imperfection equal to or greater than 10% of the nominal wall thickness is defined as a defect.

- Remove all detected imperfections and defects, provided 90% of the nominal wall remains after removal.

[Oil Search logo]

- All grinds for removal of imperfections shall be checked for remaining wall thickness with a calibrated ultrasonic wall thickness meter. Grinding and cleaning shall be done only with tools that will not leave detrimental deposits on the base metal. Only aluminum oxide or silicon carbide grinding wheels and austenite stainless steel brushes shall be used. These tools shall not have previously been used on any materials other than that to be cleaned.

## 9 Workmanship

- Every item shall be visually examined externally and internally when diameter permits, for surface flaws in the body of the item.

- Minor cosmetic areas and flaws in the item not encroaching on the required minimum thickness may be removed by grinding, provided a smooth curved surface is maintained and the final thickness after grinding is not less than the required minimum thickness. The remaining wall thickness shall be measured by UT methods.

- No repairs by welding are permitted, except for repair of seam welds. All weld seam repairs shall conform to the requirements of ASTM A815. All repairs shall be completed prior to solution heat treatment.

- No repairs of dents are permitted.

- The Vendor shall maintain continuous supervision of his inspection procedures by qualified supervisors directly responsible to management.

- In addition to the above, all millrun items shall be subject to inspection by the Company at all stages of the manufacturing and delivery process.

## 10 Traceability

- Throughout the manufacturing process, a means of identification shall be used to maintain a strict accountability of all items.

- Should heat, heat treat lot, or part number traceability be lost during processing, the affected pieces shall be rejected.

## 11 Marking and Coating

- All items shall be marked in accordance with MSS SP-25.

- In addition to the above, extruded headers shall also include the design pressure and temperature and code.

[Oil Search logo]

- Cold die stamping shall not be used except on the outside surface of the flange rim or connector clamp assembly or handles of slip blinds and spacers. Low stress round nose or interrupted dot stamp shall be used for marking on any other part of the item.

- Marking materials shall be free of any harmful contaminants such as zinc, lead, chlorides, sulfur, and other low melting point metals.

- All fittings shall be shipped without mill coating. The fitting surface shall be free of oil, grease, dirt, loose mill scale, and other foreign matter.

## 12 Packaging, Handling, and Shipment

- Ends and bevels shall be protected using a plastic end cap. Flange sealing faces shall be protected by heavy wooden or plastic covers securely fastened in place.

- No chains are allowed for any reason.

- Only nylon or other suitable nonmetallic materials are acceptable for straps.

## 13 Certification

The Supplier shall certify in writing that the pipe meets the requirements of the latest edition of the applicable ASME or MSS standard and this specification. The purchase order, or attachment to the purchase order, will define the specific documentation requirements. This shall include, but not be limited to:

- List of documents requiring Company's comment/approval

- Content list for the electronic manufacturing dossier.

- Certification dossier for each shipment

- Tally list for coating and installation contractor's use.

Certified copies of the material certifications and the results of the tensile, hardness, Charpy test and others shall be furnished to Company.
