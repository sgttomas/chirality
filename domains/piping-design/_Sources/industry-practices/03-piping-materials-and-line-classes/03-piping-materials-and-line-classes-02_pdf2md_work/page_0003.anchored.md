## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This Practice provides guidelines for selecting *PIP Piping Material Specifications* for various services.

### 1.2 Scope

This Practice lists common process industry fluid services and the corresponding *PIP Piping Material Specifications* which are typically suitable for each service under the conditions listed, if any.
