# 1 General

## 1.1 Scope

This Specification (SPC) provides requirements for plain-end, seamless carbon-steel line pipe manufactured to Product Specification Level 2G (PSL2G) requirements, in addition to the base requirements of API 5L, as modified by the applicable annexes for the Company service.

References to PSL 1 in API 5L are not applicable.

The pipe defined in this SPC is for use in the construction of long-distance, land-based pipelines that have design temperatures between -50 °F and 250 °F, and are suitable for transmission of oil, water, and gas, in Arctic duty and sour service.

This SPC may be used to supply mother pipe for the subsequent manufacture of hot formed bends, for similar applications.

The additional requirements of Annexes G and H apply to all seamless pipe supplied in accordance with this SPC. The definition of "sour" shall also include service (in hydrogen sulfide [H2S] containing fluids) where resistance to hydrogen-induced corrosion (HIC), but not sulfide stress cracking (SSC), is required.

Only material grades from X52 and up to and including X70 shall be applicable.

Subject to the requirements defined within this SPC, the base API 5L specification, API 5L Annex H, and API 5L Annex K being fulfilled, SSC, HIC, or stepwise cracking (SWC) testing of seamless line pipe as defined in H.4.5 or H.4.3 shall not be required, unless defined otherwise as an additional requirement by the Company - Data Sheet for Line Pipe (Seamless).

The requirements of this specification, in conjunction with API 5L, are set to be qualified in accordance with NACE MR0175 Part 2, Clause A.2, for use of carbon steel materials in sour service without additional qualification (per NACE MR0175-2 Clause 7.1.2).

The equipment, techniques, and procedures to be applied for nondestructive inspection shall be subject to approval by the Company Responsible Engineer.

## 1.2 Objective

The objective of this document is to provide requirements for seamless line pipe in accordance with API Specification 5L, Specification for Line Pipe, Forty Fifth Edition (API 5L) and covers the following characteristics:

- Grades from X52 up to X70
- Product Specification Level 2G (PSL2G)

- Outside diameter up to 24 inches

- Maximum thickness of 1.6 inches

References to PSL 1 in API 5L are not applicable.

Where applicable, the respective clause numbers of API 5L are referenced in parentheses next to the paragraph headings for ease of reference.

For applications other than those detailed above, additional requirements will be defined in the Purchase Order.

The following Annexes in API 5L shall apply to pipe supplied in accordance with this SPC:

- For all seamless line pipes:
  - Annex C for treatment of surface imperfections and defects.
  - Annex E for the nondestructive inspection of line pipe for all service conditions, as modified by Annex K for sour service.
  - Annex G for line pipe for gas service.
  - Annex H for line pipe for sour service.
  - Annex K for the additional nondestructive inspection of line pipe for sour service.
- Annex B, as modified by this specification, applies where specified in the Purchase Order.
- The following Reference Standard Annexes from API 5L shall not apply to seamless line pipe supplied in accordance with this SPC:
  - Annex A for welded jointers.
  - Annex D for welded repairs.
  - Annex F for PSL 1 couplings.
  - Annex I for through the flow line (TFL) pipe.
  - Annex J for line pipe for submarine service.

## 1.3 Conformance to this International Standard

This specification states the Company requirements in addition to the referenced codes and standards. If any conflicts arise between this specification and API 5L, they shall be brought to attention to the Company responsible engineer for resolution.

The vendor's Quality Management System (QMS) shall include provision for the quality assurance (QA) of raw materials, intermediate products, pipe manufacture, and final inspections. The QA

plan shall be subject to approval by the Company representative before manufacture. The effectiveness of QMS shall be subject to monitoring by Company and may be audited following an agreed period of notice.

The line pipe shall be made in accordance with the Manufacturing Procedure Specification (MPS), which shall be subject to approval by the Company Responsible Engineer and qualified before contract between the Supplier and the Company.

If required by the Purchase Order, the steel maker and pipe mill shall perform a Manufacturing Procedure Qualification and will be witnessed by an authorized Company representative in accordance with Annex B.

- The qualification will be witnessed by an authorized representative of the Company.
- No change shall be made to a successfully qualified MPS without approval of the Company Responsible Engineer.
- If such approval is granted, Company may require appropriate procedure qualification to be carried out on the new MPS.

Qualification tests may be taken from a preproduction run or the first day production, subject to agreement with the Company Responsible Engineer. Pipe made from a successful preproduction run may be supplied as part of the order. Test-certificates and other formal documents shall use International System of Units (SI) or United States customary (USC) units. Regardless of the actual units of measurement, measuring and testing equipment shall be calibrated and certified as part of the QMS.

## 1.4 Third Party Inspection

- The Company personnel shall have access, at any reasonable time, to the steelmaking, casting, dressing, rolling, pipe making, and other processes under direct control of the manufacture that are connected with the production of a pipe order.
- Manufacturer shall attempt to arrange access to third party suppliers of processes or raw materials, upon request from Company personnel.
- Relevant production records and data on relevant technical investigations shall be made available to the Company Inspector in a timely manner.

# 3 Manufacturing

## 3.1 Process of Manufacture (Clause 8.1)

Pipe shall have plain ends in accordance with API 5L Clause 9.12 or an alternative end preparation as defined in the Purchase Order.

## 3.2 Starting Materials (Clause 8.3)

- Steel shall be fully killed and made in accordance with fine-grain, low-hydrogen practice.
- Steel for all pipes shall be vacuum degassed.
- If scrap other than recycled offcuts from previously produced line pipe steels is used as starting material, the proportion of such scrap shall be stated in the Manufacturing Procedure Specification (MPS), and the chemical analyses shall include determination of additional residual elements.
- If the steel, blooms, billets, and pipe are not processed in an integrated facility, the name and location of each manufacturing plant involved shall be declared in the bid.
- Pipe shall be rolled and/or heat-treated by processes that produce uniform and consistent mechanical properties and microstructure together with high toughness and good weldability.

## 3.3 Bloom and Billet Quality

- Blooms and billets for pipe rolling shall be produced by a continuous casting process that shall include adequate precautions to minimize occurrence of centerline segregation.
- Other casting processes may be offered for consideration by the Company Responsible Engineer.
- Surfaces of the bloom or billet shall be inspected visually for defects. Scarfing shall be carried out, if necessary, to produce an acceptable surface for rolling.

## 3.4 Cold Sizing and Cold Expansion (Clause 8.9)

Seamless line pipe shall not be cold expanded, cold sized, or cold finished.

## 4.2 Elements Analyzed

- Each required analysis shall include the determination of each of the elements listed in Table 1, together with the analysis for other elements whose addition has been approved by the Company Responsible Engineer.

- One of the two product analyses should be performed on the first pipe rolled from each heat of steel.

- The chemical analysis shall be carried out on material representative of the full material thickness.

## 4.3 Chemical Properties Acceptance

Product analysis variation shall be as agreed in the MPS.

The Company Responsible Engineer shall be advised of any deviations from the limits placed on heat and product analyses in the MPS, and further qualification of a modified MPS may be required if the deviations are considered significant.

If any part of the product analysis fails to comply with the requirements in Table 1 and Sections 4.1a thru 4.1d of this SPC, either the whole heat shall be rejected, or the additional testing described below shall be completed:

- Each individual pipe from the rejected heat shall be fully analyzed.
- One sample for recheck analysis shall be taken from each end of the pipe.
- If both analyses from an individual pipe comply with the requirements in Table 1 and Section 4.1a thru 4.1d, that joint shall be accepted.
- If either of the two analyses from an individual pipe fails to comply with the requirements in Table 1 and Section 4.1a thru 4.1d, that joint shall be rejected.
- The manufacturer shall retain the option to reject the rest of the heat at any point of the individual pipe recheck analysis.

## 4.4 Mechanical Properties

### 4.4.1 Mechanical Test Failures and Retesting (Clause 10.2.12.2)

- Test pieces shall be taken from pipe in the final heat-treated condition.
- If any mechanical test specimen fails to comply with the specified requirements, subject to the allowances of API 5L Clause 10.2.3.1 for defective specimens, the additional testing described below shall be completed:
  - Two retests shall be taken from the same pipe, plus an additional test from each of two pipes from the same heat.
  - If all of the individual retests comply with the stated requirements, the whole heat shall be accepted.
  - If any of the additional individual retests fail to comply with the stated requirements, the whole heat shall be rejected, unless every pipe within that heat is tested.
  - All nonconforming pipes shall be permanently rejected.

### 4.4.2 Tensile Properties (Clause 9.3)

- The maximum allowable yield strength shall not exceed the specified minimum value in API 5L Table H.2 by more than 21,700 pounds per square inch (psi) (150 mega-pascals [MPa]).
- For all grades, the pipe body ratio of yield/tensile strength (Rt0.5/Rm) shall be recorded and shall not exceed 0.90.

- Tensile elongation shall be recorded and shall not be less than 22 percent for strip tensile and 18 percent for round bar specimens.

- If additional tensile properties are required to be determined at other than room temperature, the requirements shall be as defined in the Purchase Order.

- Control Tensile Tests

  - Mill control tests carried out in addition to the mandatory tensile testing specified shall comply with the same requirements as compulsory tests.

  - The results of such tests shall be reported to the Company Responsible Engineer.

  - The Company Inspector shall be given the opportunity to witness such tests.

- Transverse tensile testing in accordance with recorded tensile elongation in API 5L Table 20 shall be completed using round bar transverse tensile specimens, if practical.

### 1.1.1 Hardness Testing and Metallographic Examination (Clauses H.4.4, H.7.3.3)

- A hardness survey using a Vickers diamond pyramid hardness tester with a 22.05 lbs load (HV10) shall be carried out on a polished and etched macro section removed from the same sections used for tensile testing. Rockwell C Hardness (HRC) testing is not an acceptable alternative.

- Locations of hardness indentations shall be in accordance with API 5L Annex H, Figure H.1, at each of four quadrant locations around the pipe diameter.

- No individual hardness value shall exceed 230 HV10.

- Pipe that meets the hardness value criteria in Section 4.4.4.c.

- Results that exceed the limits in hardness value shall be reported to the Company Responsible Engineer, and two further sections shall be cut from the same pipe. If both retest specimens meet the hardness limits in hardness value, each length of pipe in the lot is acceptable. If either of the retest specimens gives results over the limit, all pipes from that heat shall stand rejected. The manufacturer may elect to remove a test ring from each length of the rejected lot for hardness testing of each length. Each individual joint that is tested may be accepted or rejected based on the acceptance criteria in hardness value.

## 4.4.3 Charpy V-notch (CVN) Impact Test for PSL2G SMLS Pipe

a) Charpy V-notch impact testing shall be conducted in accordance with API 5L, as modified by this specification.

b) Charpy V-notch impact test temperature shall be –50 °F.

c) Each full-size specimen test shall comply with Table 2. These values are set to satisfy the provisions of Annex G for fracture arrest.

d) The minimum average shear area shall be 85 percent for each test of three specimens.

e) Lateral expansion shall be reported, for information only.

f) These tests shall be carried out on specimens taken from the pipe material in a direction transverse to the pipe axis and notched in the through-thickness direction.

g) Transverse specimens shall be taken from a position that permits the machining of test specimens of the maximum possible standard dimensions.

## 4.5.2 Visual Inspection

### 4.5.2.1 *Internal and external surfaces*

- The external, end bevel, and, where practical, internal surfaces shall be examined visually for surface defects over the full length of each pipe.
- Imperfection acceptance levels for visual inspection shall comply with this SPC.

## 4.5.3 Surface and Volumetric Conditions, Imperfections, and Defects

### 4.5.3.1 *Longitudinal and Transverse Imperfections and Laminations (Annex K)*

- The pipe body shall be ultrasonically inspected for transverse imperfections in accordance with API 5L Annex K.3.4.1.
- Acceptance criteria for laminations shall be in accordance with API 5L Annex K for sour service as modified by this specification.
- The inspection coverage for pipe body laminations shall be 100 percent of the pipe surface.
- Pipe bevels containing laminations or other discontinuities shall be cut back until imperfections detectable by wet magnetic particle or ultrasonic inspection have been removed.

### 4.5.3.2 *Geometric Deviations (Clause 9.10.5)*

Dents that have creases, sharp-edged imperfections, or gouges shall be considered as defects.

- Dents exceeding 1/8 inch depth, including those caused by straightening clamps, shall be considered as defects and dispositioned per API 5L Clauses C.3b) or C.3c).
- Repair of dents shall not be allowed.
- Continuous (cumulative 720 degrees or more) spiral indentations that exceed 1/16-inch depth, shall be considered as defects and shall be dispositioned per API 5L Clause C.2.
- Isolated indentations, if not classified as dents per a. of this section, that exceed the lesser of 1/32-inch or 5% of the wall thickness shall be considered as defects and shall be dispositioned per API 5L Clauses C.3b) or C.3c).

#### 4.5.3.3 Hard Spots (Clause 9.10.6)

Inspectors shall examine all pipe surfaces visually to detect irregularities in the curvature of the pipe that may be evidence of hard spots. Any suspect area shall be examined further with portable hardness testing equipment. Any spot of any size with hardness that exceeds the limit in Section 4.4.3 of this SPC shall be removed from the pipe as a cylinder.

#### 4.5.3.4 Other Surface Imperfections (Clause 9.10.7)

- The external surface of the full length of each pipe shall be inspected by electromagnetic inspection (EMI) automatic equipment in accordance with API 5L Annex K.3.4.3. The EMI equipment shall scan the pipe to ensure detection of longitudinal and transverse defects with 100 percent of the pipe surface being inspected.

- The extent of other surface imperfections identified by ultrasonic or visual inspections shall be determined using wet magnetic particle inspection.

- The acceptance criteria for other surface imperfections shall be in accordance with API 5L Annex H.5 as modified by this specification.

- Sharp-edged imperfections, regardless of depth, shall be considered to be defects.

- The pipe external surface shall be free from loose scale, scabs, scores, and laps as well as tears, raised slivers, sharp notches, scratches, or mechanical damage.

- Defects in the body of the pipe, such as laps, scabs, and slivers, shall be removed by grinding in accordance with API 5L Annex C. Wet magnetic particle inspection shall be used to confirm the removal of these defects.

- The wall thickness of ground areas shall be individually measured using an ultrasonic thickness gauge.

- If more than 10 percent of any pipe outside or inside surface has been ground, the pipe shall be subjected to a 100-percent wall thickness check using the approved automatic ultrasonic equipment.

- The end bevel shall be smooth and free from tool marks.

## 4.6 Dimension Tolerance

### 4.6.1 Diameter Tolerance (Clause 9.11.3.1)

- Tolerances for diameter of pipe body:

- Unless restricted otherwise by API 5L, the maximum positive tolerance for diameter shall be 0.125 inch for all pipe sizes.
- Along the entire length of the pipe, the maximum outside diameter (O.D.), measured at any point, shall not exceed the minimum O.D., measured by the same technique at the same point, by more than 1 percent or 0.16 inch, whichever is less.
- The internal out-of-roundness within 4 inches of the pipe ends shall not exceed 0.25 inch or 1.0 percent of the nominal diameter (I.D.), whichever is less.

#### 4.6.2 Wall Thickness Tolerances (Clause 9.11.3.2)

- For specified wall thickness less than 0.984 inch, the wall thickness at any location on the pipe shall comply with API 5L Clause 9.11.3.2.
- For specified wall thickness 0.984 inch, and greater, the maximum positive tolerance shall be 0.1t or 0.146 inch, whichever is less.
- For specified wall thickness 0.984 inch, and greater, the maximum negative tolerance shall be 0.125 inch.
- The O.D. shall be measured using a circumferential (pi) tape. I.D. and out-of-roundness shall be determined by direct measurement.

#### 4.6.3 Length Tolerance (Clause 9.11.3.3)

All lengths shall be as specified on the Purchase Order within the following tolerances:

- Minimum Length: 0.8 x Nominal
- Maximum Length: Nominal plus 2 feet, not to exceed 41 feet
- Minimum Average Length: Nominal less 2 feet

#### 4.6.4 Straightness Tolerances (Clause 9.11.3.4)

- Total deviation from a straight line over the entire pipe length shall not exceed 0.10%.
- Local deviation over a length of 3 feet at each pipe end shall not exceed 0.125 inch.

## 4.7 Nondestructive Inspection

### 4.7.1 Ultrasonic and Electromagnetic Inspection

#### *4.7.1.1 Ultrasonic and Electromagnetic Inspection Reference Standards (Clause E.5.2)*

- The ultrasonic reference standard for calibration of automatic shear wave technique shall contain four N5 notches of the nominal wall thickness. One longitudinal and one transverse notch shall be machined on the inside surface and one of each on the outside surface.

- The reference standard shall also include suitably positioned reflectors that confirm the effective scan length (dead zone) of the automatic equipment and define the additional length of the pipe that is to be examined by manual ultrasonic as required by API 5L Annex E.3.3.1.

- For pipe wall thicknesses in excess of 0.75 inch, an additional reflector located at mid thickness may be required to assist in the detection of buried longitudinal imperfections. The type and position of the additional reflector shall be subject to agreement by the Company Responsible Engineer.

- The EMI reference standard for calibration shall contain two N5 notches of the nominal wall thickness, with one longitudinal and one transverse notch machined on the outside surface.

#### *4.7.1.2 Instrument Standardization (Clause E.5.3)*

- To minimize inaccuracies in automatic ultrasonic inspection during production, the calibration pipe shall be passed through the system a minimum of 10 times to demonstrate the repeatability of reflector response signals before the beginning of any production run.

- During pipe production, the reference pipe shall be run a minimum of three times at the start and end of each shift and again at approximately mid-shift. No adjustments shall be made before any proving run.

#### *4.7.1.3 Acceptance Limits (Clause E.5.5)*

Any imperfection causing the automatic ultrasonic or electromagnetic systems to alarm shall be investigated by using manual ultrasonic, magnetic particle inspection (MPI), or both, as appropriate. The imperfection shall be classified as a defect, unless one of the following applies:

- Manual ultrasonic reassessment carried out with a reference level determined by the signal amplitude obtained from an external N5 notch without added gain produces an indication that is less than the applicable acceptance criteria defined within this SPC.

- The imperfection causing the indication is a surface imperfection that is not a defect.

- Radiographic inspection shows that the indication results from a slag inclusion or gas pocket/porosity that does not exceed the limits of acceptability defined in API 5L Annex E.4.5.

#### 4.7.2 Magnetic Particle Inspection Reference Standard (Clause E.6.3)

Pipe containing appropriate defects shall be made available, or a suitable field strength indicator shall be proposed such that checks on the efficiency of the magnetic field and magnetic ink may be made at any time during processing of the order.

#### 4.7.3 Residual Magnetism (Clause E.7.6)

No single reading shall exceed 2.5 milliteslas (mT) (25 gauss), as measured with a Hall-effect gaussmeter or equivalent values, if measured with another type of instrument.

## 4.8 Finish of Pipe Ends

  - The wall thickness from the inside diameter of the root face to the pipe O.D. shall not be less than the minimum specified wall thickness.

The procedures shall define the measures being taken to ensure safety of personnel and avoidance of pipe damage during pipe handling.

- Hooks used for handling pipe shall be soft faced, with rubber or plastic. Metal-to-metal contact shall be avoided.

- All pipes with closed plastic end protectors shall be loaded with dunnage between rows or bundles to permit unloading with slings. All dunnage shall be secured to prevent contact between the pipe and cargo lashing. For truck and rail transport, protected chains or nylon straps are allowed.
