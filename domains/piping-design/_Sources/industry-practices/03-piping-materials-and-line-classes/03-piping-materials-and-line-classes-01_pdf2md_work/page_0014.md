**3.9.3** If additional corrosion allowance is required, a separate Piping Class shall be created with a suffix (2) to denote increase in the corrosion allowance [e.g., PAB2)]. A separate Piping Class shall be created with a suffix (1) to denote decrease in the corrosion allowance [e.g., PJB(1)].

**3.9.4** When the Piping Class indicates that wall thickness calculations are required and a minimum pipe schedule is specified, then the selected wall thickness shall not be less than the one specified in the Piping Class.

## 3.10 WALL THICKNESS

**3.10.1** The minimum wall thickness shall be in accordance with ANSI B31.3 Code rules.

**3.10.2** Piping for which wall thickness has been calculated for pressure only shall have its thickness not less than the minimum allowable wall thickness as shown on Table 3.1. Also, see sub-section 3.10.3.

**3.10.3** The **minimum allowable wall thickness** shown in Table 3.1 may be used as the basis for calculation of the required wall thickness for carbon steel components, only within the following pressure, temperature and pipe size limits:

[TABLE: Pressure (max.), Temperature (max.) and Pipe (NPS) size limits]

| Pressure (max.) | | Temperature (max.) | | Pipe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| kPa | (PSI) | C | (F) | (NPS) |
| 2400 | (350) | 260 | (500) | 14 and below |
| 1380 | (200) | 260 | (500) | 16 to 24 |

The **required wall thickness** used to select the pipe schedule shall be the **minimum allowable wall thickness** (from Table 3.1, where applicable) plus the corrosion allowance (from the Piping Class).

**3.10.4** For piping operating outside of the limits in sub-section 3.10.3, or where full stress analysis was done, the minimum wall thickness shall be based on calculations. Calculations shall take into account **all loads** identified in ANSI B31.3.
