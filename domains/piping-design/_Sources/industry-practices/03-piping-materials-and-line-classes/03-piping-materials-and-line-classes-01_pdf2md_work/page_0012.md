**3.7.2** Ball valves shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components – Manufacturing.

**3.7.3** High-performance butterfly valves shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components – Manufacturing.

**stem packing**

**3.7.4** Valve stem packing shall be asbestos or alternative material approved by Shell Engineering Services Division, for temperature range -29 C (-20 F) to 455 C (850 F), in the following services: hydrocarbon fluids, hydrogen, hydrogen sulphide mixture, saturated steam, superheated steam, condensate, oil, water, liquid sulphur, corrosive hydrocarbon, solvents and other general refinery applications **excluding** strong acids and alkaline substances. Refer to Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components – Manufacturing for acceptable manufacturers/designs of stem packing.

For service temperatures up to 705 C (1300 F), use die-formed, flexible graphite rings with braided graphite filament end rings (bull rings).

**3.7.5** When specifying use of "soft-seated" valves, refer to the manufacturer's recommended pressure-temperature rating.

**3.7.6** Manually operated gear drives shall be provided as follows, unless high pressure drop considerations dictate otherwise:
