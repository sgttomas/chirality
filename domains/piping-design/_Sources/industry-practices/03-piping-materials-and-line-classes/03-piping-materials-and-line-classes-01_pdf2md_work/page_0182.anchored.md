## NOTES:

( 1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings.

( 2.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched XXS Min. See sub-section 3.15.

( 3.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.

( 4.) Swages made of barstock conforming to ANSI C-1025 fully killed and stress relieved with rolled ends are acceptable.

( 5.) Use flanges.

( 6.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-175 microinch (3.2 to 4.5 micrometres) AARH.

( 7.) Use 3/4 NPS and 2 NPS for vent and drain service. See sub-section 3.14.

( 8.) Combination trim 13 CR with Stellite seats is acceptable. Only Type 410 SS is permitted.

( 9.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.

(10.) Flexible wedge is acceptable. Gate valves 1 NPS and larger in service above 150 C (300 F) shall have flexible wedge.

(11.) Refer to API 941 ("Nelson" curves) for temperature limit for material at applicable H- -> P.P. The lower temperature limit of either Nelson curves or 205 C (400 F) shall apply.

(12.) Heat treated means in normalized condition. Hardness shall not exceed 187 HB. Forgings shall be stamped (N) to identify heat-treated condition.

(13.) For wall thickness (including C.A.) greater than 3/4" user is to consult with Shell Engineering Services Division regarding heat treatment. See sub-section 3.12.

(14.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.

(15.) When PWHT is required, valve bonnets, covers, gaskets, internals and trim shall be removed and tagged prior to stress relief. Reassemble with new gaskets.

(16.) C1018 CS valve, normalized body is acceptable. The valve body shall be stamped (N) to identify normalized condition.

(17.) Acceptable valves are: HEX HG461, Century CM0 or AGCO MSK. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.

(18.) Refer to Table 5.4 for the Tubing Class.
