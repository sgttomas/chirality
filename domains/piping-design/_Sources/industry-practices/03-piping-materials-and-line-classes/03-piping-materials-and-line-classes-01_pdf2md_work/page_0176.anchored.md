## NOTES:

(1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings.
(2.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched.160 Min. See sub-section 3.15.
(3.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.
(4.) Swages made of barstock conforming to AISI C-1025 fully killed and stress relieved with plain ends are acceptable.
(5.) Use flanges.
(6.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-175 microinch (3.2 to 4.5 micrometres) AARH.
(7.) Use 3/4 NPS for vent and drain service.
(8.) Combination trim 13 CR with Stellite seats is acceptable. Only Type 410 SS is permitted.
(9.) Bolted bonnet may be considered as an alternative for 2 NPS.
(10.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.
(11.) Flexible wedge is acceptable. Gate valves 3 NPS and larger in service above 150 C (300 F) shall have flexible wedge.
(12.) Refer to API 941 ("Nelson" curves) for temperature limit for material at applicable H - 2• P.P. The lower temperature of either Nelson curves or 205 C (400 F) shall apply.
(13.) Heat treated means in normalized condition. Hardness shall not exceed 187 HB. Forgings shall be stamped (N) to identify heat-treated condition.
(14.) For wall thickness (including C.A.) greater than 3/4" user is to consult with Shell Engineering Services Division regarding heat treatment. See sub-section 3.12.
(15.) Use 6000# rating when branches are Sched.160 or heavier. See sub-section 3.15.3.
(16.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.
(17.) Where API 606 valve cannot be used, substitute SW/female thd valve to API 602.
(18.) When PWHT is required, use valves with bolted bonnets and covers. Valve bonnets, covers, gaskets, internals and trim shall be removed and tagged prior to stress relief. Reassemble with new gaskets.
(19.) C1019 CS valve, normalized body is acceptable. The valve body shall be stamped (N) to identify normalized condition.
(20.) Acceptable valves are: HEX HG661, Century CM9 or AGCO M5K. A long body extension may be required where line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.
(21.) Refer to Table 5.5 for the Tubing Class.
