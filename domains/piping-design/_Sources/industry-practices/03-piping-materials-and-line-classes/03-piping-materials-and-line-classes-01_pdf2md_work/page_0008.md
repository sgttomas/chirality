## Pipe Rating

Sched. - Schedule (e.g., Sched. 80)
STD - Standard Wall per ANSI B36.10
XS - Extra-Strong per ANSI B36.10
XXS - Double Extra-Strong per ANSI B36.10

## Connections

SW - Socket Weld (may be spelled out or shortened)
RF - Raised Face
FF - Full Faced
WN - Weld Neck Flange
SO - Slip On Flange
FLG'D - Flanged
THR'D - Threaded
SCR'D - Screwed

## Valve Descriptions

BB - Bolted Bonnet (Gate, Globe)
UB - Union Bonnet
BC - Bolted Cover (Check, Plug)
BG - Bolted Gland (Gate, Globe)
ISNR - Inside Screw, Non-Rising Stem (Gate, Globe)
ISRS - Inside Screw, Rising Stem (Gate, Globe)
OS&Y - Outside Screw and Yoke (Gate, Globe)
TFE - Tetrafluoroethylene
PTFE - Polytetrafluoroethylene
WB - Welded Bonnet (Gate, Globe)
TRIM - Stem, Seat(s), Disc, Wedge, Backseat (Bushing) as applicable to Gate, Globe and Check Valves
13CR - Either solid and/or weld overlayed, ASTM Type 410 SS
HFS - Hard Faced Stellite
