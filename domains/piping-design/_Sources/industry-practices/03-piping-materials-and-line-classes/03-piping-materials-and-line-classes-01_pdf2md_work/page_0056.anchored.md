## NOTES:

( 1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings.

( 2.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched.160 Min. See sub-section 3.15.

( 3.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.

( 4.) Swages made of barstock conforming to AISI C-1025 fully killed and stress relieved with plain ends are acceptable.

( 5.) Normally, use unions for 1-1/2 NPS and under.

( 6.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-250 microinch (3.2 to 6.4 micrometres) AARH.

( 7.) Use 3/4 NPS for vent and drain service.

( 8.) Combination trim 13 CR with Stellite seats is acceptable.

( 9.) Bolted bonnet may be considered as an alternative for 2 NPS.

(10.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.

(11.) Flexible wedge is acceptable. Gate valves 3 NPS and larger in service above 190 C (300 F) shall have flexible wedge.

(12.) Where valves with non-metallic seats, seals, liners, etc. are used, the manufacturer's pressure and temperature ratings shall limit the service of this Class.

(13.) Heat treated means in normalized condition. Hardness shall not exceed 187 HB. Forgings shall be stamped (N) to identify heat-treated condition.

(14.) Use 6000# rating when branches are Sched.160 or heavier. See sub-section 3.15.3.

(15.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.

(16.) These valves shall only be used up to a maximum operating temperature of 150 C (300 F).

(17.) Where API 606 valve cannot be used, substitute SW/female thd valve to API 602.

(18.) CI101 CS valve, normalized body is acceptable. The valve body shall be stamped (N) to identify normalized condition.

(19.) Acceptable valves are: HEX HG461, Century CM9 or AGCO MSK. A loop body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping – Design.

(20.) Refer to Table 5.5 for the Tubing Class.
