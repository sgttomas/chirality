## NOTES:

(1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings.
(2.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched.160 Min. See sub-section 3.15.
(3.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.
(4.) Use flanges.
(5.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-175 microinch (3.2 to 4.5 micrometres) AARH.
(6.) Use 3/4 NPS for vent and drain service.
(7.) Combination trim 13 CR with Stellite seats is acceptable. Only Type 410 SS is permitted.
(8.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.
(9.) Flexible wedge is acceptable. Gate valves 3 NPS and larger in service above 150 C (300 F) shall have flexible wedge.
(10.) Refer to API 941 ('Nelson' curves) for temperature limit for material at applicable H+ → P.P.
(11.) Use 6000# rating when branches are Sched.160 or heavier. See sub-section 3.15.3.
(12.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.
(13.) Heat treated means in normalized and tempered condition. Components shall be stamped to identify the heat-treated condition.
(14.) See sub-section 3.4.
(15.) See sub-section 3.5.
(16.) Valve stem packing shall be die formed flexible graphite rings with braided graphite filament end rings (bull rings) above 455 C (850 F).
(17.) PWHT is required. Valve bonnets, covers, gaskets, internals and trim shall be removed and tagged prior to stress relief. Reassemble with new gaskets.
(18.) An acceptable valve is HEX HG461. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.
(19.) Refer to Table 5.3 for the Tubing Class.
