## NOTES:

( 1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings.
( 2.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.
( 3.) Use flanges.
( 4.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched 80S Min. See sub-section 3.15.
( 5.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-175 microinch (3.2 to 4.5 micrometres) AARH.
( 6.) Use 3/4 NPS for vent and drain service.
( 7.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.
( 8.) The ratings of SW flanges shall be reduced by one-third of the values shown in ANSI B16.5.
( 9.) Heat treated means in the normalized and tempered condition. Components shall be stamped to identify heat-treated condition.
( 10.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.
( 11.) Valve stem packing shall be die formed flexible graphite rings with braided graphite filament end rings (bull rings).
( 12.) See sub-section 3.4.
( 13.) An acceptable valve is HEX HG461. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1-01, Plant Piping – Design.
( 14.) Refer to Table 5.7 for the Tubing Class.
( 15.) For pressure-temperature ratings not listed in ANSI B16.5 tables, establish these ratings in accordance with Annex D in B16.5 (1981).
