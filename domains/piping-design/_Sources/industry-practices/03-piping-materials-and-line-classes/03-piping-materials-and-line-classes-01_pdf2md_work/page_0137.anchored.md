## NOTES:

- ( 1.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched.160 Min. See sub-section 3.15.
- ( 2.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.
- ( 3.) Use flanges.
- ( 4.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-250 microinch (3.2 to 6.4 micrometres) AARH.
- ( 5.) Use 3/4 NPS and 2 NPS for vent and drain service. See sub-section 3.14.
- ( 6.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.
- ( 7.) Flexible wedge is acceptable. Gate valves 3 NPS and larger in service above 150 C (300 F) shall have flexible wedge.
- ( 8.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.
- ( 9.) See sub-section 3.4.
- (10.) 316 SS/HFS trim is acceptable.
- (11.) Where SW valve cannot be used, substitute SW/female thrd valve.
- (12.) Acceptable valves are: HEX HG461, Century CM9 or AGCO MSK. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping – Design.
- (13.) Refer to Table 5.2 for the Tubing Class.
