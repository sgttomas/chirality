**TABLE 3.1    MINIMUM ALLOWABLE WALL THICKNESS .FOR CARBON STEEL PIPING (SEE SUB-SECTION 3.10.3)**

| PIPE (NPS) | MIN. ALLOW. WALL THICKNESS |
| :---: | :---: |
| 3/4 to 3 | 2.54 mm (0.10 in.) |
| 4 to 6 | 3.05 mm (0.12 in.) |
| 8 to 24 | 3.30 mm (0.13 in.) |

**NOTE:** The above minimum allowable wall thickness limits shall not be used as arbitrary retirement limits. For inspection purposes, arbitrary retirement limits are indicated in Shell EG 118-52.01, General Plant Inspection Guidelines – Shutdown Planning and Inspection.
