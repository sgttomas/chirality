**NOTES:**

( 1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings.
( 2.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched.160 Min. See sub-section 3.15.
( 3.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.
( 4.) Swages made of barstock conforming to AISI C-1025 fully killed and stress relieved with plain ends are acceptable.
( 5.) Normally, use unions for 1-1/2 NPS and under.
( 6.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-250 microinch (3.2 to 6.4 micrometres) AARH.
( 7.) Use 3/4 NPS for vent and drain service.
( 8.) Combination Trim 13 CR with Stellite seats is acceptable.
( 9.) Bolted bonnet may be considered as an alternative for 2 NPS.
(10.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.
(11.) Flexible wedge is acceptable.
(12.) Heat treated means in normalized condition. Hardness shall not exceed 187 HB. Forgings shall be stamped (N) to identify heat-treated condition.
(13.) When PWHT is required use Class CAB(SR). For details refer to Metals Section - Corrosion Data Survey, NACE - Caustic Soda Chart.
(14.) Use 6000# rating when branches are Sched.160 or heavier. See sub-section 3.15.
(15.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.
(16.) Where API 606 valve cannot be used, substitute SW/female thr'd valve to API 602.
(17.) CI018 CS valve, normalized body is acceptable. The valve body shall be stamped (N) to identify normalized condition.
(18.) An acceptable valve is HEX HG461. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.
(19.) Refer to Table 5.6 for the Tubing Class.
