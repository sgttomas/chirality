# 1.0 INTRODUCTION

## 1.1 SCOPE

**1.1.1** This Standard covers Shell Canada Limited (Shell) requirements for piping, valves and fittings for use in refineries, chemical plants and related facility piping systems, whose design is in accordance with ANSI B31.3, Chemical Plant and Petroleum Refinery Piping.

**1.1.2** This Standard is intended to establish a standard of materials and to provide a basic guide for piping designers and engineers.

**1.1.3** The introductory Sections of this Standard (1.0 through 5.0 inclusive) must be attached to the selected Piping and Tubing Classes for the specific project.

**1.1.4** Any deviation from this Standard requires prior written approval by Shell Engineering Services Division.
