## 3.19 OPERATING BLINDS

Operating blanks (or blinds) shall conform with API Standard 590, Steel Line Blanks and the following:

- Blank materials shall be equivalent to those specified in the respective Piping Class.
- Gasket surfaces shall be in accordance with that specified for adjacent flanges in the respective Piping Class.
- The corrosion allowance shall be increased where deemed necessary by the designer.
- Handles and webs shall be integral with blanks or spacers.

Tables 4.1 to 4.84 in Appendix A contain the description of piping components for each Piping Class. The Piping Class Listing specifies the Piping Classes covered in this Standard.
