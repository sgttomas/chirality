## NOTES:

( 1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings.
( 2.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched.160 Min. See sub-section 3.15.
( 3.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.
( 4.) Use flanges.
( 5.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-250 microinch (3.2 to 6.4 micrometres) AARH.
( 6.) Use 3/4 NPS for vent and drain service.
( 7.) Combination trim 13 CR with Stellite seats is acceptable.
( 8.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.
( 9.) Flexible wedge is acceptable. Gate valves 3 NPS and larger in service above 150 C (300 F) shall have flexible wedge.
( 10.) Use 6000# rating when branches are Sched.160 or heavier. See sub-section 3.15.3.
( 11.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.
( 12.) Heat treated means in normalized and tempered condition. Components shall be stamped to identify heat-treated condition.
( 13.) Valve stem packing shall be die-formed flexible graphite rings with braided graphite filament end rings (bull rings) above 455 C (850F).
( 14.) See sub-section 3.4.
( 15.) See sub-section 3.5.
( 16.) PWHT is required. Valve bonnets, covers, gaskets, internals and trim shall be removed and tagged prior to stress relief. Reassemble with new gaskets.
( 17.) An acceptable valve is HEX HG461. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.
( 18.) Refer to Table 5.3 for the Tubing Class.
