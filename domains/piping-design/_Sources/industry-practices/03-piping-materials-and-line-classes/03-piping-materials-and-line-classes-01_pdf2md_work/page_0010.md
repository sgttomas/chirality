## 3.0 GENERAL NOTES

**3.1** PIPE

All pipe shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components - Manufacturing. All alloy pipe shall be subject to Shell STD. 18-2.01, Testing and NDE Methods and Procedures for Pressure Equipment and Piping, Appendix E.

**3.2** FITTINGS

**3.2.1** All fittings shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components - Manufacturing. All alloy fittings shall be subject to Shell STD. 18-2.01, Testing and NDE Methods and Procedures for Pressure Equipment and Piping, Appendix E.

**3.2.2** The descriptions of fittings such as thredolet, sockolet, weldolet, etc. are generic only.

**3.2.3** "Bull Plugs" shall be of the solid, round head, threaded type.

**3.2.4** For special fittings such as strainers, refer to Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components - Manufacturing. All special fittings of alloy material shall be subject to Shell STD. 18-2.01, Testing and NDE Methods and Procedures for Pressure Equipment and Piping, Appendix E.

**3.3** FLANGES

All flanges shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components - Manufacturing. All alloy flanges shall be subject to Shell STD. 18-2.01, Testing and NDE Methods and Procedures for Pressure Equipment and Piping, Appendix E.

**3.4** BOLTING

All bolting shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components - Manufacturing.

**important** NOTE that bolting in the Classes is specified by temperature. Many Classes show two bolting specifications for one Class. The user shall create a new Class with a suffix (H) to avoid the substitution of lower temperature limit materials [e.g. - PCE(H)], where (H) indicates change in the bolting specification for the higher temperature].
