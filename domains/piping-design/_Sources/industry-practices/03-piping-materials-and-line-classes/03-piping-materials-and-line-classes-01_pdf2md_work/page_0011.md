## 3.5 GASKETS

**3.5.1** All gaskets shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components - Manufacturing.

**important** **NOTE** that gaskets are specified in the Piping Classes by temperature or Construction Class. Some Piping Classes show two gasket types. The user shall create a new Piping Class with a suffix (H) to avoid the substitution of lower temperature limit materials or less severe Construction Class [e.g., PCEIH; where (H) indicates change in gasket specification for the higher temperature].

**3.5.2** Compressed asbestos composition gaskets are allowed for Construction Class 3, unless otherwise specified in Piping Classes.

**3.5.3** Spiral-wound gaskets with asbestos filler may be used where allowed by local regulations. They shall be limited to service temperatures up to 455 C (850 F). Flexible graphite filler may be used for service temperatures up to 705 C (1300 F).

**3.5.4** Piping Classes with ANSI Class 1500 flanges at service temperatures over 455 C (850 F) may utilize welded joint gasket design where justified by piping stress analysis, in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.

## 3.6 TUBING

**3.6.1** All tubing for steam tracing shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-3.31, Steam Tracing - Installation, Classes T1 and T2.

**3.6.2** Tubing for glycol/water tracing shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-3.32, Glycol Tracing - Installation, Class GAA.

**3.6.3** Tubing for instrumentation shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 16-1.12, Process Measurement Devices - Design.

**3.6.4** Tubing for piping installations shall be purchased in accordance with this Standard.

## 3.7 VALVES

**3.7.1** Gate, globe and check valves shall be purchased in accordance with Shell STD. 3-2.01, Piping Components - Manufacturing.
