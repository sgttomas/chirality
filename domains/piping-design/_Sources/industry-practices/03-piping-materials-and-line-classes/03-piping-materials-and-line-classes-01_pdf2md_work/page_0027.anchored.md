## NOTES:

( 1.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.

( 2.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 250-500 microinch (6.4 to 12.5 micrometres) AARH.

( 3.) Heat treated means in normalized condition. Hardness shall not exceed 187 HB. Components shall be stamped (N) to identify heat-treated condition.

( 4.) Connection between hydrant and main header to be a carbon steel fitting.

( 5.) The type and material of inlet will depend on location. Those locations that have slight frost penetration use a straight section (cast iron) adaptor between header and hydrant. Those locations that have heavy frost penetration use an elbow (carbon steel) between header and hydrant. Confirm design with location.

( 6.) Sizes and types of hose thread and cap nut to be confirmed by location.

( 7.) Hydrant to be either self draining or with drain depending on location.

( 8.) Where attached to buttweld fittings, use weld neck flanges.

( 9.) Use flat-face flanges against cast iron flanges.

(10.) All below-ground pipe shall be externally coated as per Shell STD. 19-2.11, External Shop Coating of Pipe for Underground Service - Manufacturing or Shell STD. 19-3.11, External Field Coating of Underground Pipe - Installation.

(11.) C1018 CS valve, normalized body socket/weld only, is acceptable. The valve body shall be stamped (N) to identify the normalized condition.

(12.) Acceptable valves are: HEX HG461, Century CM9 or AGCO MSK. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.

(13.) Refer to Table 5.1 for the Tubing Class.
