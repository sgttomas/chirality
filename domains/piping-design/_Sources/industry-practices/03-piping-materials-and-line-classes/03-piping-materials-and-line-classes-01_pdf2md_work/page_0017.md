**3.15 TERMINAL CONNECTIONS AND VALVED BRANCH CONNECTIONS**

**3.15.1** The term "Nipples" in this Standard means short pieces of pipe 1-1/2 NPS and smaller. Nipples shall be between 50 mm (2 in.) minimum and 150 mm (6 in.) maximum in length and have a wall thickness as indicated in the notes of the respective Piping Classes.

Nipples shall be used only between piping or equipment, and an adjacent block valve for terminal connections and valved branch connections.

Short pieces of pipe 1-1/2 NPS and smaller, used between various piping components are not considered nipples and shall have the same wall thickness as the schedule of a pipe run or arrangement.

**3.15.2** A terminal connection is defined as a minimum length of pipe 1-1/2 NPS and smaller, connected to other piping components or equipment that terminates at a plugged valve (e.g., vents, drains, etc.).

**3.15.3** A valved branch connection is defined as a minimum length of pipe 1-1/2 NPS and smaller, connected to other piping components or equipment, that includes but does not terminate at an adjacent block valve (e.g., sampling connections, drain lines, vent lines, etc.).

**3.16 DESIGN OF BRANCH CONNECTIONS**

For application of equal or reducing tees, weldolets, sockolets and stub-ins for branch connections, refer to Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping – Design.

**3.17 HYDROGEN SERVICE**

The actual upper temperature limit for the application of Piping Classes in hydrogen services listed in this Standard, must not exceed limitations of "Nelson" curves published in the latest edition of API Publication 941.

**3.18 HYDROGEN SULPHIDE SERVICE**

All materials in hydrogen sulphide service shall be in accordance with NACE MR0175.
