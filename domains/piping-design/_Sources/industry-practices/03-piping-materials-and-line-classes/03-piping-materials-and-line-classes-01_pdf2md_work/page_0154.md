## NOTES:

(1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings.

(2.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched 160 Min. See sub-section 3.15.

(3.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.

(4.) Normally, use unions for 1-1/2 NPS and under.

(5.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-250 microinch (3.2 to 6.4 micrometres) AARH.

(6.) Use 3/4 NPS for vent and drain service.

(7.) Combination Trim 13 CR with Stellite seats is acceptable.

(8.) Bolted bonnet may be considered as an alternative for 2 NPS.

(9.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.

(10.) Flexible wedge is acceptable.

(11.) Use 6000# rating when branches are Sched 160 or heavier. See sub-section 3.15.3.

(12.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.

(13.) Heat treated means in normalized or normalized and tempered condition. Components shall be stamped to identify the heat-treated condition.

(14.) Where API 606 valve cannot be used, substitute SW/female thd'd valve.

(15.) When PWHT is required, use valves with bolted bonnets and covers. Valve bonnets, covers, gaskets, internals and trim shall be removed and tagged prior to stress relief. Reassemble with new gaskets.

(16.) Cl018 CS valve, normalized body is acceptable. The valve body shall be stamped (N) to identify normalized condition.

(17.) Acceptable valves are: HEX HG461, Century CM9 or AGCO MSK. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.

(18.) Refer to Table 5.5 for the Tubing Class.
