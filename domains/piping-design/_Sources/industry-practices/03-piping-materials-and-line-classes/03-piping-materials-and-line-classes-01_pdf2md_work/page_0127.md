## NOTES:

( 1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings.
( 2.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched.80S Min. See sub-section 3.15.
( 3.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.
( 4.) For 1 NPS and under normally use unions.
( 5.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-250 microinch (3.2 to 6.4 micrometres) AARH.
( 6.) Use 3/4 NPS for vent and drain service.
( 7.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.
( 8.) Flexible wedge is acceptable. Gate valves 3 NPS and larger in service above 150 C (300 F) shall have flexible wedge.
( 9.) Where valves with non-metallic seats, seals, liners, etc. are used, the manufacturer's pressure and temperature ratings shall limit the service of this Class.
(10.) Unions and SO flanges are limited to 260 C (500 F) and in non-cyclic services.
(11.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.
(12.) See sub-section 3.4.
(13.) 316 SS/HFS trim is acceptable.
(14.) Where SW valve cannot be used, substitute SW/female thrd valve.
(15.) The pressure rating of Class 150 flange is less than 150 psi at less than 340 C (650 F).
(16.) Where attached to buttweld fittings, use weld neck flanges.
(17.) Acceptable valves are: HEX HG461, Century CM9 or AGCO MSK. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.
(18.) Refer to Table 5.3 for the Tubing Class.
