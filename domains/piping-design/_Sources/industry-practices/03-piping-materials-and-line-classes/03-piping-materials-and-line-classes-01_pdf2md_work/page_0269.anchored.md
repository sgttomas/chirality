**NOTES:**

( 1.) Use bolting insulation kits when connecting aluminum flanges to carbon steel or stainless steel flanges. Not required for aluminum-to-aluminum flanges.

( 2.) Pipe supports shall be clamp-on carbon steel with teflon insulation between pipe and clamp.

( 3.) Aluminum piping and flanges shall be fireproofed with insulation and 304 SS sheeting. Insulation shall be warranted by the manufacturer as suitable for direct application on aluminum and shall not contain corrosive or other harmful components.

( 4.) Flange gasket face shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125-250 microinch (3.2 to 6.4 micrometres) AARH.

( 5.) For socket weld connections, bore out thread. Valves shall be dismantled prior to welding of pipe to valve body.

( 6.) For vents and drains use 3/4 NPS valves with one end threaded for round head solid aluminum "bull plug".

( 7.) ANSI B16.5 Class 150 dimensions.
