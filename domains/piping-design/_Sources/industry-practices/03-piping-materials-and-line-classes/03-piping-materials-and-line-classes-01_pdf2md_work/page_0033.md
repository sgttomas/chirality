**NOTES:**

( 1.) Service conditions may require heavier wall pipe and fittings. Above 260 C (550 F), use A-106 GR.B SMLS.

( 2.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched.160 Min. See sub-section 3.15.

( 3.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.

( 4.) Swages made of barstock conforming to ANSI C-1025 fully killed and stress relieved with plain ends are acceptable.

( 5.) Normally, use unions for 1-1/2 NPS and under.

( 6.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 250-500 microinch (6.4 to 12.5 micrometres) AARH.

( 7.) Use 3/4 NPS for vent and drain service.

( 8.) Purchase Order shall identify that valves will be installed in steam service.

( 9.) Combination Trim 13 CR with Stellite seats is acceptable.

(10.) Bolted bonnet may be considered as an alternative for 2 NPS.

(11.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.

(12.) Flexible wedge is acceptable. Gate valves 3 NPS and larger in service above 150 C (300 F) shall have flexible wedge.

(13.) Heat treated means in normalized condition. Hardness shall not exceed 187 HB. Forgings shall be stamped (N) to identify heat-treated condition.

(14.) Use 6000# rating when branches are Sched.160 or heavier. See sub-section 3.15.3.

(15.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.

(16.) Where API 606 valve cannot be used, substitute SW/female thd'd valve to API 602.

(17.) C1018 CS valve, normalized body is acceptable. The valve body shall be stamped (N) to identify the normalized condition.

(18.) Acceptable valves are: HEX HG461, Century CM9 or AGCO MSK. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design.

(19.) Refer to Table 5.5 for the Tubing Class.
