## 3.11 IMPACT TESTING

The requirements for impact testing are to be based on the application of materials and in accordance with ANSI B31.3 Impact Testing Methods and Acceptance Criteria for Metals and as specified in Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping – Design.

## 3.12 HEAT TREATMENT

**3.12.1** Pipe, valves and fittings shall be heat treated as specified in the individual Piping Classes.

**3.12.2** When PWHT is required for Carbon Steel Piping Classes, the user shall create a new class with a suffix (SR) added to the line Class designation (e.g., SAGS8R).

## 3.13 MANUFACTURING

**3.13.1** Fabrication and quality control of shop fabricated piping, shall be in accordance with Shell STD. 3-2.02, Piping – Shop Fabrication.

**3.13.2** Fabrication and quality control of field fabricated piping, shall be in accordance with Shell STD. 3-3.01, Piping – Field Fabrication and Installation.

**3.13.3** Underground process and utility piping operating up to 60 C (140 F), shall be coated and wrapped in accordance with Shell STD. 19-2.11, External Shop Coating of Pipe for Underground Service – Manufacturing or Shell STD. 19-3.11, External Field Coating for Underground Pipe – Installation.

**3.13.4** Piping Classes which specify pipe and fittings in 2 NPS and under shall be socket-welded. When service considerations justify, buttwelded construction in 2 and 1-1/2 NPS is permissible and shall be in accordance with the requirements of Shell STD. 3-1.01, Plant Piping - Design, Shell STD. 3-2.02, Piping – Shop Fabrication and Shell STD. 3-3.01, Piping – Field Fabrication and Installation.

## 3.14 OPERATING AND INSTRUMENT CONNECTIONS

**3.14.1** Operating and instrument connection details shall be in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1.01, Piping – Design.

**3.14.2** Operating connections are vents, drains, hydrotest connections, etc. Instrument connections are thermowells, pressure instruments, instrument bridles, etc.
