## NOTES:

( 1.) Schedule of fittings and WN flanges to be the same as pipe schedule.

( 2.) Use flanges.

( 3.) All flange serrations shall be to ANSI B16.5, 125–175 microinch (3.2 to 4.5 micrometres) AARH.

( 4.) Use 3/4 NPS and 2 NPS for vent and drain service. See sub-section 3.14.

( 5.) Use flanged valves on nozzles and flanged equipment or where space is limited.

( 6.) Nipples and swages 1-1/2 NPS and under shall be Sched 80 Min. See sub-section 3.15.

( 7.) Use 2 NPS for flanged operating and instrument connections. See sub-section 3.14.

( 8.) Valve stem packing shall be die formed flexible graphite rings with braided graphite filament end rings (bull rings). See sub-section 3.4.

( 9.) See sub-section 3.4.

(10.) An acceptable valve is HEX HG461. A long body extension may be required where the line is insulated. A gauge valve may be used only for design pressures-temperatures within its rating and only for pressure instruments installations in accordance with Shell STD. 3-1-01, Plant Piping - Design.

(11.) Refer to Table 5.2 for the Tubing Class.

(12.) For pressure-temperature ratings not listed in ANSI B-16.5 tables, establish these ratings in accordance with Annex D in B16.5 (1981).
