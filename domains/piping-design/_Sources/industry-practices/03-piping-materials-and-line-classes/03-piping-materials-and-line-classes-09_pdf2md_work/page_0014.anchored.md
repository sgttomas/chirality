## 11   Preparation for Shipment

Fittings shall be carefully handled and protected during all phases of manufacture, shipping, and storage. All fittings shall be palletized or securely fastened to trailers for shipping purposes and shall not be permitted to come in contact with each other.

- All weld bevels shall be thoroughly cleaned and coated with a long-term rust inhibitor such as deoxaluminite.

- Threaded and socket welded connections shall be protected by tightly fitting plastic caps or plugs.

- Plastic end caps and plugs shall be suitable for a salt water tropical atmosphere and temperatures as low as -50 °F.

- All hooks and chains used for handling fittings must be soft-faced. Protected wire rope or chains, aluminum, Teflon, or rubber is suggested. Copper and copper alloys (bronze, brass, etc.) are specifically prohibited.

- The inner and outer surfaces shall be free of oil, grease, dirt, loose mill scale and other foreign matter.

## 12   Quality Documentation

The following certified reports shall be provided:

- Heat analysis reports on the chemical composition of each heat of steel used in the manufacture of the fittings.

- Report on results of all required physical tests, including impact and hardness tests.

- Description of heat treatments applied to the fitting material and test pieces.

- Certification stating that all requirements of this SPC have been met.

- For wet sour service, a written certification shall be included to certify the material meets the requirements of NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

- For materials to be used for low temperature services and for all cast steels, quality certificates from the primary raw materials manufacturers (steel mill, foundries, etc.) and ASTM standard designation shall be included.
