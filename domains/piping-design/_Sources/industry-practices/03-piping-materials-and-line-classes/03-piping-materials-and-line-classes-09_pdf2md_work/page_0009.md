## 5.2 Heat Treatment

- Post-forming heat treatment may be required to meet the test requirements. Fittings shall receive the heat treatment prescribed in the referenced standard, except as noted.
- Heat treatment shall be on the entire fitting by placing the fitting in a furnace of sufficient size to contain the piece. The temperature shall be as shown in the Manufacturer's approved procedure and shall be maintained within a maximum range of ±50 °F. Heat treating by passing the fitting through an induction coil is not allowed. Localized heat treatment on welds (of welded fittings) is not permitted. All split hot tap fittings requiring heat treatment shall have both halves heat treated in the same lot.

## 5.3 Manufacturing Restrictions

- All butt-welding fittings shall be sandblasted on the O.D. prior to bending to remove foreign particles. Under no circumstances shall the pipe come in contact with copper or zinc bearing materials after sandblasting.
- Fabricated branch fittings made by joining two pieces of pipe ("stub-in") are not permitted. Y-laterals may be submitted on an exception basis.
- Mitered type fittings are not permitted.
- Circumferential welds within the fittings are prohibited.
- The welding of a pup piece to correct fabrication defects or dimensional errors is not permitted.
- All fittings shall be made in such a manner as to preserve the cross-sectional shape of the pipe.
- For fittings made from pipe containing a longitudinal weld, the weld shall be positioned as close as possible to the least distortion axis of the fitting.
