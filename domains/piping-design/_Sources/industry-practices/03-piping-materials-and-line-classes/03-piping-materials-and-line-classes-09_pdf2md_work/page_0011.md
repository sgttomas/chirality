- For fitting sizes that accommodate full-size (0.394 in x 0.394 in) Charpy specimens and larger, the specimens shall be machined from the fitting without flattening. For sizes too small for full-size specimens, subsized specimens may be used.

- For NPS 6 and larger fittings, specimens shall be oriented transverse to the longitudinal axis with the notch oriented through the wall thickness so that the crack propagates in the longitudinal direction. Charpy specimens from the base metal of welded fittings shall be taken from a location 90 degrees from the weld. Charpy specimens from the weld shall be machined with the notch along the center line of the weld and in the same orientation described above.

- For fittings smaller than NPS 6, the Charpy specimens shall be oriented longitudinal to the fitting axis with the notch through the wall thickness.

- All Charpy tests shall be performed at -50 °F/-46 °C, unless otherwise specified.

- The minimum required Charpy V-notch absorbed energy in foot-pounds is based on the wall thickness (t) and grade of the fitting. In each case, the first number is the required average value for any set of three full-size (0.394 in x 0.394 in) specimens, the second is the lowest specimen tested.
