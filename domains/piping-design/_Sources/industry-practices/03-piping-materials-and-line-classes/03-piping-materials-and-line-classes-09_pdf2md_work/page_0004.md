# 1   General

## 1.1   Scope

This Specification (SPC) sets forth the minimum requirements for the manufacture, testing, and inspection of seamless and welded high-yield welding fittings, mandrel-formed fittings, factory hot-formed fittings, and extruded fittings of Manufacturers Standardization Society (MSS) SP-75, modified grades WPHY-52 through WPHY-70. High-yield Carbon Steel Flanges and Forged Fittings, such as integrally reinforced branch connections (o-lets), socket weld and threaded unions, elbows and couplings, and flanged pipettes shall conform to High-yield Carbon Steel Flanges and Forged Fittings (OGE-PISPE-000007).

## 1.2   Referenced Standards and Codes

This SPC states requirements that are in addition to the referenced codes and standards. Wherever conflicts exist between the requirements of the referenced codes and standards and this SPC, the requirements of the more stringent shall govern. Items not addressed in this SPC shall conform to the requirements of the referenced standards and codes.

## 1.3   Material Source

For all materials, a statement of country of origin shall be made at the time of quotation. If the item is formed in one location (e.g., forged or cast) and processed in another (e.g., machined or heat-treated), each shall be noted.

## 1.4   Terms and Definitions

[TABLE: Terms and Definitions]

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| 3R | A fitting with a centerline radius of three times the nominal pipe diameter. |
| Company | Oil Search Alaska, LLC or its designated representative |
| Factory Hot-formed | A pipe section formed through heating and controlled working without a mandrel |
| Fitting | Any factory-made, seamless, and welded butt-welding fittings such as elbows, segments of elbows, return bends, caps, tees, crosses, y laterals, reducers, sweepolets single or multiple-outlet extruded headers, reducers, extensions and transition sections. |
| Forged Fitting | A billet section formed through heating and controlled compression between a set of forging dies. |
