# 4   Design, Construction, and Dimensions

## 4.1   Design Requirements

- All butt-welding fitting designs shall be qualified by proof testing per MSS SP-75.

- High-yield carbon steel fittings specified are intended to be welded to high-yield carbon steel pipe of the same metallurgy, yield strength, and similar wall thickness. If lower yield fittings are offered as alternatives, they must be made thicker to compensate. The increase in wall thickness shall be on the outside diameter (O.D.) rather than on the inside diameter (I.D.) to facilitate "bridge welding" during field fabrication (refer to ASME B31.4, Figure 434.8.6(a) -2, sketch (f), for details). The quotation shall indicate the grade of low-yield fittings being offered, including wall thickness. In addition, the quotation shall include a detailed dimensional drawing of the butt-weld ends. All such substitutions must receive prior approval from the Company.

- All butt-weld fittings shall be made in such a manner that the mechanical properties of the pipe are not reduced below the minimum values allowed for the original pipe grade. Additionally, all butt-weld fittings shall meet the requirements of the appropriate code and standard specifications listed in Section 2.0.

- All butt-weld fittings shall be made in such a manner as to preserve the cross-sectional shape of the pipe, and shall be free from wrinkling, buckling, cracks, or other evidence of mechanical damage.

## 4.2   Dimensions

Fittings shall comply with the dimensional requirements of the referenced design standard in the purchase order description.

- High-yield fittings, 16 inch O.D. and larger, shall have dimensions and tolerances per MSS SP-75. All High-yield butt-welding fittings 14 inches and less shall have dimensions and tolerances per ASME B16.9.

- Unless otherwise noted, transition pieces shall have lengths equal to ASME B16.9 reducer lengths. When specified, some transition pieces will be required to have drawn outlets (e.g., for use with bypass valves).
