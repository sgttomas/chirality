### 7.3 Stepwise Cracking Resistance

For fittings formed from plate or welded pipe, one fitting from each heat shall be subject to stepwise cracking resistance testing. Tests performed on the parent plate or pipe at the mill are acceptable. One test shall be performed for each heat in accordance with NACE Standard TM0284. For acceptance, the crack length ratio (CLR) shall not exceed 15%, the crack sensitivity ratio (CSR) shall not exceed 2%, and the crack thickness ratio (CTR) shall not exceed 5% for the average of all planes on all specimens from a single lot of steel. The test specimen evaluation shall be by metallographic examination.

Stepwise cracking resistance testing is not required for cast fittings or for fittings formed from seamless pipe.

## 8 Nondestructive Examination

### 8.1 Visual Inspection

Each fitting shall be inspected to ensure that tolerances listed in this SPC are not exceeded. Every fitting shall be visually examined externally for surface faults. The full inside surface shall be inspected, if accessible.

All fittings shall be free from wrinkling, buckling, cracks, or other evidence of mechanical damage. Waves with a width to depth ratio of 12 to 1 or greater are acceptable. Smooth dents, less than 1/4 inch deep and no greater than one-half the pipe diameter in width, are acceptable.

### 8.2 Ultrasonic Testing

The wall thickness of all fittings shall be checked by ultrasonic testing in accordance with ASTM E114 or E 213 and shall not be less than the specified minimum wall thickness.

Each extruded or weld-fabricated fitting shall be inspected for wall thickness and defects in the entire area of cold or hot working or welding per MSS SP-75.

At five locations along the fitting, a series of three readings shall be taken. These locations shall include all the areas where the material was bent, stretched, compressed, or welded.

### 8.3 Hardness Testing

Hardness tests shall be made on at least one fitting from every post-fabrication heat treatment lot and on at least one fitting from each lot with no post fabrication heat treatment. No hardness shall exceed Rockwell C Hardness (HRC) 22 (Vickers 248, Brinnel 235).
