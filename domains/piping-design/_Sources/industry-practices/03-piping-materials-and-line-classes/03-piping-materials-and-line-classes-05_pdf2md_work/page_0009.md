#### Increased Thickness

Where materials are supplied in a greater thickness than required, the end should be tapered on the inside and /or the outside at an angle between 14 and 30 degrees.

### 3.8.2 Branch Fitting Preferences

The appropriate fitting to use for each branch connection will usually be determined by the type of fabrication (i.e., new construction, online service, or in-service) and the respective process and service conditions (i.e., corrosive/noncorrosive, instrumentation fitting, vent or drain connection, process throughput branch, etc.).
