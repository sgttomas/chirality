NOTES:

1. See Sour Service Definition and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification.
2. Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr 70N. For low temperature service, material shall be Charpy V-notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum design temperature.
3. Buried pipe to be externally coated with materials suitable for the application.
6. Full port valves for size ranges subject to piping operations.
7. Threaded connections allowed subject to compliance with Owner's Specification Piping Design.
8. Lines subject to piping operations to be 3 NPS or larger.
9. Design Factors for CSA Z662 equation 4.3.5.1 are F=0.8, L=0.625, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate. Re-calculate wall thickness, if required.
11. With approval of weld procedures by Owner PWHT exemption per Owner's Specification Welding Appendix D may be applied.
22. Dual certified materials may be used A333 Gr 6 / Z245.1 Gr. 241 Cat I SS; A420 WPL 6 / Z245.11 Gr. 241 Cat I SS; A350 LF2 / Z245.12 Cat I SS. Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 materials not available.
24. If using SW flanges, bore to match pipe.
32. Material conformance to CSA Z245.15 SS required.
