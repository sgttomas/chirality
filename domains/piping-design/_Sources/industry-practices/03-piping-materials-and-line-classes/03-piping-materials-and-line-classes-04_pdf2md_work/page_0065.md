**NOTES:**

1. See Sour Service Definition and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification GS 1.0.
2. Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr 70N. For low temperature service, material shall be Charpy V-notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.
4. Buried pipe to be externally coated with materials suitable for the application.
6. Full port valves for use on lines subject to pigging operations.
7. Threaded connections allowed subject to compliance with Owner's Specification 2.1 Piping Design.
9. Design Factors for CSA Z662 equation 4.3.5.1 are F=0.8, L=0.625, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate. Re-evaluate wall thickness, if required.
10. Flanged valves 1/2 – 1 1/2 NPS for use on Post Weld Heat Treated lines.
12. PWHT required when service conditions of ISO 15156 are met except as exempted in Owner's Specification GS 2.0 Welding Appendix D. When sour service piping is exempted from PWHT, it shall be indicated on drawings provided to the piping fabricator.
14. Dual certified materials may be used A234 WPB/Z245.11 SS, A106 Gr B/Z245.11 Cat I SS, A105/Z245.12 SS. Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 materials not available.
15. No threaded connections allowed as primary process connections.
24. If using SW flanges, bore to match pipe.
32. Material conformance to CSA Z245.15 SS required.
