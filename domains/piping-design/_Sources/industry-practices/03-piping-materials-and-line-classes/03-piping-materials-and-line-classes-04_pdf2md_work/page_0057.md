NOTES:

1. See Sour Service Definition and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification GS 1.0.
2. Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr. 70. For low temperature service material shall be Charpy V-notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.
3. Buried pipe to be externally coated with materials suitable for the application.
4. Full port valves for use on lines subject to pigging operations.
5. Lines subject to pigging operations to be 3 NPS and larger.
6. Design Factors for CSA Z662 equation 4.3.5.1 are F=0.8, L=0.65, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate. Re-evaluate thickness accordingly.
10. Flanged valves 1/2 - 1 1/2 NPS for use on Post Weld Heat Treated lines.
12. PWHT requirement: when service conditions of ISO 15156 are met except as exempted in Owner's Specification GS 2.9 Welding Appendix D. When sour service piping is exempted from PWHT, it shall be indicated on drawings provided to the piping fabricator.
13. No threaded connections allowed as primary process connections.
15. If using SW flanges, bore to match pipe.
33. Dual certified materials may be used A333 Gr. 6 / Z245.1 Gr. 241 Cat II (M45C) SS, A420 WPL 6 / Z245.11 Gr. 241 Cat II (M45C) SS, A350 LF2 / Z245.12 Cat II (M45C) SS. Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 materials not available.
