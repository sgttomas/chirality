NOTES:

1. See Sour Service Definition and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification GS 1.0.
2. Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A Blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr. 70. For low temperature service, material shall be Charpy V-notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.
3. Lines subject to piping operations shall be covered with materials suitable for the application.
4. Full port valves for use on lines subject to pigging operations.
5. Threaded connections allowed subject to compliance with Owner's Specification GS 2.1 Piping Design.
6. Lines subject to pigging operations to be 3 NPS or larger.
7. Design Factors for CSA Z662 equation 4.3.5.1 are F=0.8, L=0.625, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate.
8. Re: evaluate wall thickness, if required.
9. With approval of weld procedures by Owner PWHT exemption per Owner's Specification GS 2.8 Welding Appendix D may be applied.
10. Dual certified materials may be used A333 Gr. 6 / Z245.1 Gr. 241 Cal I SS, A420 WPL 6 / Z245.11 Gr. 241 Cal I SS, A350 LF2 / Z245.12 Cat I SS. Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 materials not available.
24. If using SW flanges, bore to match pipe.
32. Material conformance to CSA Z245.15 SS required.
