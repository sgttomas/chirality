NOTES:

1. See Sour Service Definition and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification GS 1.0.
2. Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr 70N. For low temperature service, material shall be Charpy V-notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.
3. Buried pipe to be externally coated with materials suitable for the application.
4. Maximum allowable temperature 100°C.
5. Full port valves for use on lines subject to pigging operations.
6. Threaded connections allowed subject to compliance with Owner's Specification GS 2.1 Piping Design.
7. Lines subject to pigging operations to be 3 NPS or larger.
8. Design Factors for CSA Z662 equation 4.3.5.1 are F=0.8, L=0.625, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate. Re-evaluation of thickness, St required.
9. Flanged valves 1/2 - 1 1/2 NPS for use on Post Weld Heat Treated lines.
10. With approval of weld procedure by Owner PWHT exemption per Owner's Specification GS 2.9 Welding Appendix D may be applied.
11. Dual certified materials may be used A234 WPB/Z245.11.SS, A106 Gr.B/Z245.1 Cat I SS, A109N/Z245.12 SS. Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 materials not available.
12. If using SW flanges, bore to match pipe.
13. Material conformance to CSA Z245.15 SS required.
