**NOTES:**

1. See Sour Service Definition and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification GS 1.0
2. Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr 70N. For low temperature service, material shall be Charpy V-notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.
6. Full port valves for use on lines subject to pigging operations.
7. Threaded connections allowed subject to compliance with Owner's Specifications GS 2.1 Piping Design.
8. Lines subject to pigging operations to be 3 NPS or larger.
9. Design Factors for CSA Z662 Section 4.3.5.1 are F=0.8, L=0.625, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate. Re-evaluate wall thickness, if required.
10. PWHT required when service conditions of ISO 15156 are met except as exempted in Owner's Specification GS 2.0 Welding
15. Appendix D. When sour service piping is exempted from PWHT, it shall be indicated on drawings provided to the piping fabricator.
16. No insulated connections allowed as primary process connections.
22. Dual certified materials may be used A333 Gr. 6 / Z245.1 Gr. 241 Cal I SS, A420 WPL 6 / Z245.11 Gr. 241 Cal I SS, A350 LF2 / Z245.12 Cal I SS. Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 materials not available.
32. Material conformance to CSA Z245.15 SS required.
