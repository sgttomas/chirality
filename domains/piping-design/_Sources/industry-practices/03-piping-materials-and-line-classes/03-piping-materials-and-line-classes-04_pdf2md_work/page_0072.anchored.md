## NOTES:

1. See Sour Service Definition and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification GS 1.0.
2. Buried pipe to be externally coated with materials suitable for the application.
3. Full port valves for use on lines subject to pigging operations.
4. Threaded connections selected subject to compliance with Owner's Specifications GS 2.1 Piping Design.
8. Lines subject to pigging operations to be 3 NPS or larger.
9. Design Factors for CSA Z662 (clause 4.3.5.1 are: F=0.8, L=0.625, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate. Re-evaluate wall thickness, if required.
10. PWHT required when service conditions of ISO 15156 are met except as exempted in Owner's Specification GS 2.9 Welding Appendix D. When sour service piping is exempted from PWHT, it shall be indicated on drawings provided to the piping fabricator.
15. No threaded connections allowed as primary process connections.
32. Material conformance to CSA Z245.15 SS required.
33. Dual certified materials may be used A333 Gr. 6 / Z245.1 Gr. 241 Cat II (M45C) SS, A420 WPL-6 / Z245.11 Gr. 241 Cat II (M45C) SS, A350 LF2 / Z245.12 Cat II (M45C) SS - Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 material not available.
