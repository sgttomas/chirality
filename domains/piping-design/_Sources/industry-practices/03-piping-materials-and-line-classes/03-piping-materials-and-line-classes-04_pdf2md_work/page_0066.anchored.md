NOTES:

1. See Sour Service Addendum and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification GS 1.0.
2. Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used on larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr 70N. For low temperature service, material shall be Charpy V-notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.
3. Full port valves for use on lines subject to pigging operations.
4. Design Factors per CSA Z662 equation 4.3.5.1 are F=0.8, L=0.625, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate.
Re: ovaluate wall thickness, if required.
10. Flanged valves 1/2 - 1 1/2 NPS for use on Post Weld Heat Treated lines.
12. PWHT required when service conditions of ISO 15156 are met except as exempted in Owner's Specification GS 2.9 Welding Appendix D. When sour service piping is exempted from PWHT, it shall be indicated on drawings to the piping fabricator.
15. No threaded connections allowed as primary process connections.
22. Dual certified materials may be used A333 Gr. 6 / Z245.1 Gr. 241 Cat I SS, A420 WPL 6 / Z245.11 Gr. 241 Cat I SS, A350 LF2 / Z245.12 Cat I SS. Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 materials not available.
24. If using SW flanges, bore to match pipe.
32. Material conformance to CSA Z245.15 SS required.
