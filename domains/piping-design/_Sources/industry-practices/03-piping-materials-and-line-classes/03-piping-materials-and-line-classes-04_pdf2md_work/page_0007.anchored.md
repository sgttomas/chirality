## NOTES:

1. See Sour Service Definition and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification.
2. Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr.70N. For low temperature service, material shall be Charpy V-notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.
3. See Owner's Specification for Nondestructive Examination requirements. When the amount of radiographic examination is less than 100%, the progressive examination requirements of B31.3 Clause 341.3.4 or CSA Z662 Clause 7.10.3.1 shall apply.
5. Maximum allowable temperature 100°C.
6. Threaded connections allowed subject to compliance with Owner's Specification Piping Design.
12. PWHT required when service conditions of ISO 15156 are met except as exempted in Specification Appendix D. When sour service piping is exempted from PWHT, it shall be indicated on drawings provided to the piping specification.
18. If using SW flanges, bore to match pipe.
24. As per A516 Gr. B pipe may be substituted with approval. (ERW for NPS 12-18; DSAW for NPS 20-24). Check wall thickness.
