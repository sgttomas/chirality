6. Threaded connections allowed subject to compliance with Owner's Specification GS 2.1 Piping Design.
7. PWHT required when service conditions of ISO 15156 are met except as exempted in Owner's Specification GS 2.9 Welding Appendix D. When sour service piping is exempted from PWHT, it shall be indicated on drawings provided to the piping fabricator.
