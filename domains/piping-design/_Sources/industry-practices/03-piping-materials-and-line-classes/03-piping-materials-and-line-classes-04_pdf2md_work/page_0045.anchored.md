**NOTES:**

1. Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr.70N. For low temperature service, material shall be Charpy V notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.
2. Buried pipe to be externally coated with materials suitable for the application.
3. See Owner's Specification GS 2.10 for Nondestructive Examination requirements. When the amount of radiographic examination is less than 100%, the progressive examination requirements of B31.3 Clause 341.3.4 or CSA Z662 Clause 7.10.3.1 shall apply.
4. All port valves on use on lines subject to pigging operations.
5. Lines subject to pigging operations to be 3 NPS or larger.
6. Design Factors for CSA Z662 equation 4.3.5.1 are F=0.8, L=0.625, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate. Re-evaluate wall thickness, if required.
7. If using SW flanges, bore to match pipe.
