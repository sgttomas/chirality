$$t_m = \frac{PD}{2(SE + PY)} + c$$

(Eq. 200-1)

**Pipeline Formulae.** Equations 200-2 and 200-3 (from ASME/ANSI B31.4 and B31.8) are for pipeline design only and are shown here only for comparison.

The ASME/ANSI B31.4 design formula is:

$$t = \frac{PD}{2S}$$

(Eq. 200-2)

The ASME/ANSI B31.8 design formula is:

$$P = \left(\frac{2ST}{D}\right) F E T$$

(Eq. 200-3)

These simpler formulae should not be used for the design of piping systems. **The allowable stresses and various design factors for the three formulae are defined differently, depending on the type of construction, area classification and pipe used. Consult the *Pipeline Manual* for guidance.**

Because the design formulae for piping and pipelines are different, be careful to select the correct code and apply the correct design factors.
