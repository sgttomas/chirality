## Bolt Size

Selection of proper stud bolt and machine bolt sizes, including length, is given in ASME/ANSI B16.5, Pipe Flanges and Flanged Fittings.

The calculation of proper bolt length always assumes the use of heavy pattern hex nuts per ASME/ANSI B18.2, Square and Hex Nuts. Heavy pattern nuts are desirable because of additional strength and the larger area on the flats that helps avoid rounding corners.

Certain pipe fittings and valves, such as the wafer valves discussed in Section 270, have extra long stud bolts. Such installations are a concern in fire hazard areas. Long bolts are more susceptible to expansion when subjected to flame impingement and are a potential leak source. If long bolts are used in such areas, they should have flame impingement shields as shown on Standard Drawing GB-L1110.

**Threading.** All machine bolts, stud bolts, and nuts should be specified with threads as described in ASTM A193 Paragraph 15.
