**TRIPLE AXIS DESIGN**

Single axis offset moves the shaft off the sealing edge allowing complete, uninterrupted seating contact around the entire rim.

Double axis offset moves the disc pivot-point off the valve centerline, producing cam-closure for positive torque sealing.

Triple axis offset moves the seat cone angle off-axis, to achieve compressive sealing.

**FRICTION FREE SEALING**

Disc and seat surfaces meet at a chosen contact angle for frictionless compression. All points of the seal ring contact the seat at the same instant and rotation stops.

The seat contour ensures compressive sealing around the entire seal ring.

Further torque applied to the valve shaft loads the seal ring equally at all points, with radial force, to assure zero leakage.
