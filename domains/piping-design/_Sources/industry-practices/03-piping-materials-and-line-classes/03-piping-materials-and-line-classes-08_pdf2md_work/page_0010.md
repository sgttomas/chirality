## 6.1 Tensile Properties

All high-yield flanges or connectors shall satisfy the mechanical properties shown in MSS SP-44 or API 6A as appropriate. All high-yield blinds and spacers shall satisfy the mechanical properties of the base material.

## 6.2 Hardness

Random hardness tests shall be made on at least one finished item from each heat. Hardness values shall not exceed RC22 (Vickers 248 or Brinnel 237). Testing shall be done in accordance with ASTM A370.

## 6.3 Fracture Toughness Test

- One set of Charpy V-Notch impact tests, in accordance with ASTM A370 (a set consists of three specimens), shall be performed on the base material heat for each lot of items or from one complete item from each lot. The sample may be removed from a prolongation to prevent destruction of a usable flange or connector. Flattening of specimens is permitted.

- Specimens shall be machined from samples oriented as required in the applicable MSS or ASTM specification. For slip blinds and spacers made from plate, specimens shall be machined from the plate perpendicular to the plate-rolling direction.

- All specimens shall be the thickest standard size that can be prepared.

- All Charpy tests shall be performed at -50 °F unless otherwise specified. Full-size (0.394-inch x 0.394-inch) specimens shall meet the following requirements:

[TABLE: Table 3 Full Size Toughness Test Specimen]

| SMYS (ksi) | Absorbed Energy (Ft-lbs.) |
| :--- | :--- |
| less than 60 | 40 avg / 32 min |
| 60 or greater | 50 avg / 40 min |

For subsize specimens, the absorbed energy shall be reduced in linear proportion to the actual specimen size (e.g., for a 0.934-inch x 0.295-inch specimen, the values shown are multiplied by 0.75).

- For all grades and sizes, the minimum percent shear area shall be 30%.

- A retest is allowed if there is reason to believe that the failure of a specimen to show the minimum impact value required is due to some fault in its preparation or testing, and that it has not provided a true indication of the notch ductility of the material.

- If any specimen fails to pass the test, the remainder of the items in the heat may be submitted individually for tests and acceptance.
