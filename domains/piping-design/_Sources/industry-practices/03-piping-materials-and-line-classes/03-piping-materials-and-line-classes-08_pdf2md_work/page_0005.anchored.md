### 1.4 Material Source

A statement of country of origin shall be provided at the time of quotation. For all materials, the country of origin shall be stated if other than the point of shipment. If the item is formed in one country (e.g., forged or cast) and processed in another (e.g., machined or heat-treated), each shall be noted.
