- All flange and connector design stresses shall be in accordance with ASME B16.5, Annex B or MSS-SP 44, Annex A, or ASME B16.47, Annex A.

- Purchase order or bill of material shall state the specified minimum yield strength in terms of the pipe to be matched (e.g., X65) or the material class (e.g., F65). Lower yield material shall not be substituted, unless specifically allowed in this specification or the purchase order.

  - Hub design and hub end bevels shall be in accordance with ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47, MSS-SP-44, or API 6A, Design Type 6B, as applicable.

  - The flange or connector bore shall match the inside diameter of the pipe.

  - The finish for all raised-face gasket contact surfaces shall be 125–500 microinches (μin) per MSS SP-6 and ASME B16.5, except flanges specified as "vacuum service," which shall have a maximum roughness average (Ra) of 125 μin.

  - All flanges with ring-type joints shall have a flat bottom groove per ASME B16.20, or API 6A, Design Type 6B, as applicable. Groove surface finish shall be 63 μin per MSS SP-6 and ASME B16.5.

- A detailed dimensional drawing of all connector assemblies shall be furnished with the quotation.

- All special flange and connector studs shall be American Society for Testing and Materials (ASTM) A320, Gr. L7.

- All special flange and connector nuts shall be ASTM A194, Grade 7.

- Slip blinds and spacers shall be in accordance with ASME B16.48. For sizes not shown in ASME B16.48, face dimension shall match flanges in 3:2 and "t" dimensions shall be per ASME B31.3 paragraph 304.5.3.

- All screwed and socket-welded fittings of nominal pipe size ½-inch to 1½-inches shall be made from billets by the hot forging process and shall meet the design requirements of ASME B16.11.

- Forged-steel 3,000-pound screwed and socket-welding unions shall be per MSS SP-83.

- Nonstandard and proprietary fittings that are not manufactured to an industry standard such as weldolets, laterals, etc., shall be furnished with the dimensions of the manufacturer referenced in the purchase description. All other manufacturing considerations shall be per the design standard referenced in the purchase description.
