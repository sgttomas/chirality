## 7    Inspection and Rejection

All sharp notches, imperfections, scratches, etc., shall be removed by grinding if they fall within the non-injurious defect tolerances listed in paragraph UF-37 of ASME, Section VIII, "Unfired Pressure Vessels." Correction by welding shall not be acceptable. The repair of dents is not to be permitted. All weld bevels are to be checked for lamination by ultrasonic, dye-penetrant, or magnetic particle processes. No lamination is acceptable.

## 8    Marking

All items shall be marked in accordance with MSS SP-25. The Impact Test Temperature shall be added if nonstandard for the material class. Flanges that meet more than one standard, (e.g. ASME 1500# and API 5000#) may be marked accordingly by the manufacturer only.

Cold dye stamping shall not be used except on the outside surface of the flange rim, connector clamp assembly, or handles of slip blinds and spacers. Low-stress round-nose or interrupted dot stamp shall be used for marking on any other part of the item.

- Preparation for Shipment
  - Sealing faces and weld bevels shall be thoroughly cleaned and coated with rust preventative.
  - Flanges or connectors shall be shipped without other surfaces coated. The inner and outer surfaces shall be free of oil, grease, dirt, loose mill scale, and other foreign matter.
  - All item sealing faces and weld bevels shall be protected by heavy wooden or plastic covers securely fastened in place. A minimum of 4 bolts or poly plugs shall be required for securing all flange face covers.

## 9    Documentation

Certified mill test reports shall be submitted for all items. The report shall contain the analysis and testing information related to chemical, physical, hardness, and notch toughness (if required) properties of the materials. The report shall also indicate the carbon equivalent. The reports shall be traceable to the flanges by heat number. Each document shall bear the signature of the authorized representative of the agency that performed the test or analysis.
