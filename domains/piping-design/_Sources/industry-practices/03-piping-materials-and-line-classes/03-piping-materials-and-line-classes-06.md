# 1 General

## 1.1 Scope

This Specification (SPC) sets forth the minimum requirements for the manufacture, testing, and inspection of pipe, fittings, and flanges constructed of low-yield strength carbon steel materials (less than 52,000 psi specified minimum yield strength [SMYS]). This includes seamless and welded low- yield carbon steel pipe, seamless and welded butt-weld, socket-weld, and threaded pipe fittings, and welding flanges (including designated "specialty" flanges and "Grayloc" or other proprietary type mechanical connectors) for low temperature and high temperature services in carbon steel materials.

## 1.2 Alternative Proposal

The Vendor shall quote as specified. The Vendor may propose alternatives to this specification. Alternative quotes shall indicate where the proposal deviates from this specification, the reason for the alternative and the effect on cost, delivery, and performance.

## 3 Design, Dimensions, and Tolerances

### 3.1 Design Requirements

#### 3.1.1 Fittings

All screwed and socket welding fittings of nominal pipe size ½-inch to 1½-inch shall be made from billets by the hot forging process and shall meet the design requirements of ASME B16.11. Where permitted by ASTM, fittings up to 4-inch NPS may be produced from forged or hot-rolled bar.

### 3.1.2 Flanges, Connectors, and Proprietary Fittings

All flange, connector, and proprietary fitting design stresses shall be in accordance with the applicable allowable stresses specified in ASME B31.3. Except as noted otherwise in this Specification or in the purchase description, all low-yield flanges shall be made in accordance with ASME B16.5.

All special flanges, connectors, and proprietary fittings shall be designed to extrapolations of the referenced flange standards. Since these special designs shall be of a "custom" or "special" nature, The Supplier shall submit all design calculations and detailed dimensional drawings for Company review before commencing fabrication or with the quotation.

When the specified minimum yield strength of the flange or connector hub material equals that of the adjoining pipe, the hub design shall be in accordance with ASME B16.5 or B16.47, or MSS SP-44 or API 6A, Design Type 6B, as applicable.

All special flanges and connectors shall be supplied complete with all required gaskets, studs, and nuts. All studs shall be of a quality and strength not less than ASTM A320, Grade L7. All nuts shall be of a quality and strength not less than ASTM A194, Grade 7.

## 3.2 Dimensions

### 3.2.1 Fittings

When 3-R bends are specified, the "A" dimension of Table.2 in ASME B16.9 shall be two times the dimension shown. Straight tangents 2 feet long shall be incorporated at both ends of 3-R induction bends unless otherwise specified.

Non-standard and proprietary fittings that are not manufactured to an industry standard, such as "weldolets," laterals, etc., shall be furnished with the dimensions of the manufacturer referenced in the purchase description. All other manufacturing considerations shall be per the design standard referenced in the purchase description, as far as it is applicable.

### 3.2.2 Flanges and Connectors

#### *3.2.2.1 General*

The Supplier shall bore the flange or connector to match the inside diameter of the pipe to which they shall be welded. The Supplier shall bevel the hub ends as per ASME B16.5 or B16.47 or MSS SP-44 or API 6A, Design Type 6B, as applicable.

#### *3.2.2.2 Flanges*

All flanges shall meet the requirements on the purchase order. If not specified on the Purchase Order, the applicable code, face type, and end type shall be as follows:

Face type shall be Raised Face for ASME Class 150# – 600# and Type RTJ for all others. All API flanges shall be RTJ type.

End connections shall be socket welded for 1½-inch and smaller and butt-welded for all others.

The finish, unless otherwise specified, for all raised-face gasket contact surfaces shall be per MSS SP-6 and ASME B16.5 or B16.47. For services specifying Raised Face Smooth Finish, a finish between 63 to 125 AARH is acceptable.

All flanges with ring-type joints shall have a flat bottom groove per ASME B16.20, or API 6A, Design Type 6B, as applicable. Groove surface finish shall be as per MSS SP-6 and ASME B16.5 or B16.47.

Where flanges are specified as "jackbolt flanges" they shall be modified with two drilled and tapped jackbolt holes 180 degree apart on the flange bolt circle and centered between adjacent bolt holes. Jackbolts shall be ASTM A320 Grade L7 hex head bolts. Jackbolt diameter shall be a minimum of one-half of the standard stud diameter or 3/4-inch, whichever is greater. Jackbolt length shall be a minimum of two times the flange thickness. Jackbolt tips shall be ground smooth with a rounded end and a radius equal to the bolt diameter. The jackbolt holes shall be tapped to the lesser of the full flange thickness or twice the jackbolt diameter.

#### 3.2.2.3 Slip Blinds and Spacers

All slip blind and spacer "T" dimensions shall be in accordance with ASME B16.48.

### 3.2.3 Pipe

All pipes end 1½-inch nominal O.D. and smaller shall be plain cut. All pipe ends 2-inch nominal O.D. and larger shall be beveled for welding per API 5L 7.9.3. Alternate end bevels per ASTM A53 are acceptable.

Pipe jointers shall not be allowed.

## 3.3 Tolerances

### 3.3.1 General

For items larger than 48-inch nominal O.D., the tolerances shall be the same as for 48-inch nominal O.D. Tolerances for weld bevels and root face shall be per ASME B16.25.

### 3.3.2 Fittings

The maximum wall thickness of the completed fittings shall not exceed the specified wall thickness by more than 25% at the tangent pipe (i.e. fitting ends) without prior agreement by the Company. If the minimum inside diameter is more than 3/16-inch smaller than the nominal I.D. of the adjoining pipe, the I.D. of the fitting shall be taper bored in accordance with the applicable code.

All fittings specified as having controlled roundness throughout shall have a maximum allowable outside diameter out-of-roundness at any point of +1%.

### 3.3.3 Pipe

For pipe larger than 20-inch outside diameter (O.D.), measurements shall be on the inside rather than outside, for a distance of 4 inches from the pipe end. The inside diameter based on circumferential measurement shall not differ from the nominal inside diameter (nominal outside diameter minus twice the nominal wall thickness) by more than +3/32 inch, -1/32 inch.

## 4 Process of Manufacture

### 4.1 General

The Company may witness the production, heat treatment and testing of all items. When specified in the purchase description, The Supplier shall provide a 10 working-day notice prior to any of the above events.

The Supplier shall fully describe the manufacturing process in his quotation.

### 4.2 Fittings

#### 4.2.1 General

All fittings shall be made in such a manner as to preserve the cross sectional shape of the pipe, and shall be free from wrinkling, buckling, cracks, or other evidence of mechanical damage. Waves with a width to depth ratio of 12 to 1 or greater are acceptable.

For fittings made from pipe containing a longitudinal weld, it shall be positioned as near as practicable to the axis of least distortion.

#### 4.2.2 Restrictions

Fittings manufactured by the Induction Bending Process shall not be furnished unless approved in writing by the Company. Suppliers proposing to furnish fittings made by this process shall submit a detailed manufacturing process description and quality control procedure with their quotation for review and approval.

Fabricated branch fittings made by joining two pieces of pipe (stub-in) are not permitted. Y-laterals may be submitted for approval by the Company on an exception basis.

Mitered type fittings are not permitted.

Circumferential welds within the fittings are prohibited.

The welding of a pup piece to correct fabrication defects or dimensional errors is not permitted on fittings.

Split hot tap fittings shall have no weld seams in the body of the fitting before use.

#### 4.2.3 Welding

Welds shall be made with the addition of filler metal; by welders, welding operators, and welding procedures qualified under provisions of ASME, Section IX. Welds shall be radiographically examined throughout the entire length of each fabricated weld in accordance with Paragraph UW-51 of ASME Section VIII, Div. 1, or ultrasonically examined per Appendix 12 of ASME VIII, Div. 1.

## 4.3 Pipe

All carbon steel pipe shall be seamless or double-submerged arc welded (DSAW) with single longitudinal weld seam.

## 4.4 Galvanizing

When specified, galvanizing shall be hot dip in accordance with ASTM A153. Electro- galvanizing in accordance with ASTM B633 is permitted for unions and small threaded and socket weld fittings only.

## 4.5 Heat Treatment

### 4.5.1 General

All items shall receive the heat treatment prescribed in the referenced standard, except as noted herein. All A-516 material that is cold formed shall be "Normalized" after forming.

### 4.5.2 Fittings

Heat treatment shall be on the entire fitting. Localized heat treatment on welds (of welded fittings) is not permitted. All split hot tap fittings requiring heat treatment shall have both halves heat treated in the same lot. The Supplier shall specify the proposed heat treatment on the quotation.

### 4.5.3 Pipe

Full body normalizing, or quenching and tempering, is acceptable for all pipe.

# 5 Chemical Properties and Test

## 5.1 Composition

The chemical composition of the steel used in the manufacture of pipe, fittings, flanges, and connectors shall fall within the limits of composition of the referenced standard in the purchase description. For A350-LF2, vanadium may not be increased above 0.05%, notwithstanding A350 Table 1, Note D.

## 5.2 Nickel Content

Carbon and low-alloy steel, including weld metal on welded items, shall have nickel content less than 1% per NACE MR-0175.

## 5.3 Acceptance

The analysis report shall include all elements listed in the applicable ASTM standard. If any value falls outside the specified limits, all the items from that heat shall be rejected, or at The Supplier's option, each item may be submitted for testing individually.

The Company reserves the right to make as many tests of the items as he considers necessary to ensure that the final product meets the specification.

# 6 Physical Properties and Test

## 6.1 Tensile Testing

- Low-yield carbon steel pipe and butt-weld fittings, excluding flanges and branch connections such as "weldolets", laterals, etc. shall have actual yield strength no greater than 25,000 psi above the ASTM specified minimum in the finished condition..
  - The ratio of actual yield strength to the ultimate tensile strength shall not exceed 0.85 for low-yield carbon steel in the finished condition.
  - The tensile tests shall be performed following final heat treatment.
- Tensile requirements of low-yield carbon steel items shall be verified by tension tests. All tensile tests shall be made by The Supplier per ASTM Standards as applicable, and the results reported to the Company.

## 6.2 Tensile Test Specimen Locations

### 6.2.1 Fittings

Tensile test specimens may be either transverse or longitudinal. Tensile test specimens shall be taken at the following regions:

- Tangent length (if heat-treated; base and weld).
- Transition zone (base and weld).
- Full formed and or extruded region(s): Locations shall be at both the most compressed and tensioned sections of the fitting base metal and at any welds.

Where fittings are too small to obtain test specimens from the fitting or a prolongation, the tests may be performed on specimens from a test bar that has been subjected to approximately the same working as the actual fittings, and is of the same heat as the actual fittings.

### 6.2.2 Flanges, Connectors, Blinds, and Spacers

The tensile test specimens shall be taken from a rough forged or finished flange or connector by cutting the test specimens from the hub parallel to the axis of flow through the hub. The samples may be taken from a prolongation of the hub to avoid destroying a usable flange or connector.

The tensile tests for blinds and spacers may be taken from the parent plate.

## 6.3 Fracture Toughness Test

### 6.3.1 General

When required by this or the applicable ASTM specification, or when specified on the Purchase Order, the items shall be impact tested in accordance with Appendix A. The toughness values shall not be less than the values listed in Table A-1 of this specification.

### 6.3.2 Materials Requiring Impact Testing

The following low-yield materials require impact testing per Appendix A, whether or not the requirement is listed on the Purchase Order:

- Low-yield, low-temperature materials, all thicknesses, including all blind flanges, slip blinds (blanks), and spacers.
- Low-yield, non-low temperature pipe, fittings, and flanges as specified in ASME B31.3, Section 323.2.2 for a minimum design metal temperature of –20 °F. See Appendix A for more specific details.

### 6.3.3 Materials Not Requiring Impact Testing

The following materials do not require impact testing, unless otherwise specified in the applicable ASTM specification or on the Purchase Order:

- Low-yield, non-low temperature pipe, fittings, and flanges with nominal wall thickness less than those requiring impact testing per ASME B31.3, Section 323.2.2 for a Minimum Design Metal Temperature of −20 °F.
- Items for which the ASTM specification waives testing because they are too small to obtain a one-quarter size specimen, such as A333 pipe smaller than ⅛ XXH, ¼ Schedule 160 or 1-inch Schedule 80.

# 8 Non-destructive Examination

## 8.1 General

All items, including pipe, shall meet the nondestructive examination (NDE) requirements of the applicable industry specifications, and the further requirements of this specification.

## 8.2 Fittings and Flanges

### 8.2.1 Tests

Each fitting and flange shall be inspected to ensure that tolerances listed in Paragraph 5.3 of this specification are not exceeded. Thickness measurements may be taken by ultrasonic inspection or by micrometer.

For small size fittings and flanges where ultrasonic testing is required but not feasible due to fitting configuration, magnetic particle examination per article 7, Section V of the ASME code may be used, except the yoke method of magnetization may not be employed. Acceptance requirements shall be per Appendix VI, Section VIII, Division I of ASME Code.

### 8.2.2 Rejection

If any fitting or flange fails to meet the test result requirements, two additional fittings or flanges from the same lot shall be tested. If either fitting or flange fails to meet the specified requirements, the entire lot of fittings or flanges shall be rejected unless subsequent heat treatment and 100 percent retesting shows acceptable results.

## 8.3 Hardness Testing

Testing shall be done in accordance with ASTM A370. Brinnell or Rockwell methods may be used.

Hardness tests shall be done on at least one piece from each heat treatment lot.

Hardness tests shall be performed after all forming and heat treatment. For heat-treated items, the test location may be anywhere on the outside surface. For non-heat treated items, the test location shall be in the most highly worked area accessible. Welded items shall also be tested on the weld seam and the heat affected zone.

Hardness testing shall consist of at least three impressions at each location.

No hardness shall exceed HB 237/HC 22. If any item does not meet the maximum hardness requirement, the entire heat lot shall be hardness tested.

In lieu of providing individual hardness test results, the Vendor may provide written certification that the finished material has been tested per the above, and meets the requirements.

# 9 Workmanship, Visual Inspection, and Repair of Defects

All sharp notches, imperfections, or gouges, shall be removed by grinding. A smooth curved surface shall be maintained and the final thickness after grinding shall not be less than the required minimum thickness. The remaining wall thickness shall be measured by ultrasonic methods.

If the Company Inspector concludes that the defect is excessive in accordance with the above, the item shall be rejected. Defects in pipe encroaching upon the specified minimum thickness shall be cut out.

The repair of injurious defects by welding is permitted only with the prior approval of Company.

Hard spots are not acceptable and shall be removed from the pipe as a cylinder.

All markings shall be low stress die stamped (round-nose or interrupted dot stamp), or electrochemically etched, or paint stenciled, or marked with permanent ink.

The following information shall be marked on each item:

- Manufacturer's name or trademark.
- Material Designation (ASTM specification number and grade)
- Rating Designation (Pressure Class) as applicable
- Temperature rating (if pertinent and with corresponding pressure limit)

Items may be marked per minimum ASTM requirements if space limitation is a factor.

For split hot-tap fittings mark the top half on one end and the bottom half on the opposite end.

Cold die stamping shall not be used except on the outside surface of the flange rim or connector clamp assembly or handles of slip blinds and spacers.

If marking is to be done by stamp, low-pressure (blunt-nosed-continuous or blunt-nosed-interrupted- dot die) steel stamps in accordance with MSS SP-25 shall be used. Manufacturer shall insure the depth of impression will not infringe upon the required design minimum wall thickness. Steel stamping on welded construction fittings shall not be on any weld or heat affected zones.

All stenciled, painted, and inked markings shall be protected with clear weather-protective coating.

## A.1.4: Number and Location of Specimens

### A-1.4.1 General

The samples may be removed from a prolongation of an original flange, connector, or fitting, where feasible, to prevent destruction of a usable item. All Charpy V-notch specimens shall be the thickest standard size that can be prepared from the item.

### A-1.4.2 Pipe and Fittings

- For pipe which requires impact testing (see Paragraph 8.3), samples consisting of three Charpy specimens each shall be taken from one length of pipe from each heat supplied on the order, but from not less than one length per each 50 lengths.

- For fittings that require impact testing (see Paragraph 8.3), one set of three specimens shall be taken from each heat lot.

- For fittings fabricated from plate, Charpy specimens shall be oriented so that the longitudinal axis of the specimen is transverse to the maximum rolling direction or working direction.

- For full formed and or extruded regions of fittings, specimen locations shall be at both the most compressed and tensioned sections of the fitting base metal and at any welds.

- For welded pipe and fittings, one Charpy specimen set shall be taken from the base metal, one set shall be taken at the centerline of the longitudinal weld, and one set shall be taken with as much of the notch as possible along the weld line of fusion. The base metal specimens shall be taken from a location 90 degrees from the longitudinal weld. The notch axis for all specimens shall be oriented through the pipe wall thickness.

- Charpy impact tests shall be conducted on the deposited weld metal and heat affected zone for each weld procedure applicable to welded fittings under this specification.

### A-1.4.3 Flanges, Special Flanges, and Connectors

- For 6-inch nominal O.D. and smaller flanges or connectors, three Charpy V-Notch specimens shall be machined from the samples in the longitudinal direction to the flow through the hub, and notched through the thickness of the specimen perpendicular to the surface of the hub neck.

- For 8-inch nominal O.D. and larger flanges or connectors, three Charpy V-Notch specimens shall be machined from the samples in the transverse direction to the flow through the hub, and notched through the thickness of the specimen perpendicular to the surface of the hub neck.

- For slip blinds and spacers made from plate, two sets of three Charpy V-Notch specimens shall be machined from the plate; one set parallel, and one set perpendicular to the plate rolling direction.
