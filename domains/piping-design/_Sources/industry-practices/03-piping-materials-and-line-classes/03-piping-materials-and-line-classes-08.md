# 1  General

## 1.1  Scope

This Specification (SPC) sets forth minimum requirements for the manufacture, testing, and inspection of high-yield strength carbon steel flanges for connection to pipes with yield strengths of 52 kilopounds per square inch (ksi) or greater. Designed "specialty" flanges and "Grayloc" type mechanical connectors; and fittings forged directly from billets such as integrally reinforced branch connections (o-lets, flanged pipets, vesselets), socket weld and threaded elbows, tees, and unions; are also included.

For low-yield strength flanges and flanges for low or high-temperature service, see OGE-PISPE-000002, "Low-yield Pipe, Fitting, and Flange Procurement Specification."

# 3   Design, Construction, and Dimensions

Except as noted otherwise in this Specification (SPC), all high-yield flanges shall be made in accordance with Manufacturers Standardization Society (MSS) SP-44. Flanges produced to American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) B16.47 shall use "Series A" dimensions, unless otherwise specified on the purchase order.

All special flanges or connectors shall be designed to extrapolations of the referenced flange standards. Since these special designs shall be of a "custom" or "special" nature, all design calculations and drawings shall be submitted before commencing fabrication. All special flanges and connectors shall be supplied complete with all required gaskets, studs, and nuts.

- All flange and connector design stresses shall be in accordance with ASME B16.5, Annex B or MSS-SP 44, Annex A, or ASME B16.47, Annex A.

- Purchase order or bill of material shall state the specified minimum yield strength in terms of the pipe to be matched (e.g., X65) or the material class (e.g., F65). Lower yield material shall not be substituted, unless specifically allowed in this specification or the purchase order.

  - Hub design and hub end bevels shall be in accordance with ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47, MSS-SP-44, or API 6A, Design Type 6B, as applicable.

  - The flange or connector bore shall match the inside diameter of the pipe.

  - The finish for all raised-face gasket contact surfaces shall be 125–500 microinches (μin) per MSS SP-6 and ASME B16.5, except flanges specified as "vacuum service," which shall have a maximum roughness average (Ra) of 125 μin.

  - All flanges with ring-type joints shall have a flat bottom groove per ASME B16.20, or API 6A, Design Type 6B, as applicable. Groove surface finish shall be 63 μin per MSS SP-6 and ASME B16.5.

- A detailed dimensional drawing of all connector assemblies shall be furnished with the quotation.

- All special flange and connector studs shall be American Society for Testing and Materials (ASTM) A320, Gr. L7.

- All special flange and connector nuts shall be ASTM A194, Grade 7.

- Slip blinds and spacers shall be in accordance with ASME B16.48. For sizes not shown in ASME B16.48, face dimension shall match flanges in 3:2 and "t" dimensions shall be per ASME B31.3 paragraph 304.5.3.

- All screwed and socket-welded fittings of nominal pipe size ½-inch to 1½-inches shall be made from billets by the hot forging process and shall meet the design requirements of ASME B16.11.

- Forged-steel 3,000-pound screwed and socket-welding unions shall be per MSS SP-83.

- Nonstandard and proprietary fittings that are not manufactured to an industry standard such as weldolets, laterals, etc., shall be furnished with the dimensions of the manufacturer referenced in the purchase description. All other manufacturing considerations shall be per the design standard referenced in the purchase description.

- Where flanges are specified as "jackbolt flanges," they shall be modified with two drilled and tapped jackbolt holes 180 degree apart on the flange bolt circle and centered between adjacent bolt holes. Jackbolts shall be ASTM A320 Grade L7 hex head bolts. Jackbolt diameter shall be a minimum of one-half of the standard stud diameter or ¾-inch, whichever is greater. Jackbolt length shall be a

minimum of two times the flange thickness. Jackbolt tips shall be ground smooth with a rounded end and a radius equal to the bolt diameter. The jackbolt holes shall be tapped to the lesser of the full flange thickness or twice the jackbolt diameter.

## 4 Process of Manufacturer and Material

ASTM A707 Grade L2 or L3, Class 3, may be used interchangeably as an alternative high-yield material. When specified as F65 or higher, Class 3 material shall be modified accordingly to meet the higher yield strength.

All flanges, blinds, spacers, connectors, and forged fittings shall be forged in one piece from fully killed, fine grain steel. The ferritic grain size, as defined in ASTM E112, shall be 8 or finer. Heat treatment shall conform to the requirements of the design specification (MSS SP-44, API 6A, paragraph 1.4.1, or other) and the ASTM material specification. Minimum heat treatment for all items shall be normalized, normalized and tempered, or quenched and tempered.

Flange faces, bores, and bevels shall be fully machined so that no "as-forged" surfaces remain.

Blinds and spacers only may be made from low-temperature plate of A516 Grade 70, tested to -50 °F. Lower yield materials, such as ASTM A350 Grade LF2 or LF6, may be substituted, providing full pressure rating and specified dimensions are maintained.

## 5 Chemical Properties and Test

The chemical composition of the steel used in the manufacture of the flanges or connectors shall fall within the limits of composition of the referenced standard in the purchase description. The carbon equivalent (C.E.) for high-yield flanges shall be calculated using the following formula and shall not exceed 0.43:

$$C.E. = C + \frac{mN}{6} + \frac{Cr + Mo + V}{5} + \frac{Ni + Cu}{15}$$

The content of all elements listed in the referenced standard, as well as any intentionally added elements, shall be reported. If any value falls outside the limits of the referenced standard, all items from that heat shall be rejected, or each item may be submitted for testing individually.

## 6 Physical Property Test

Tests shall be performed following final heat treatment from a rough forged, finished flange, or connector by cutting the test specimens from the hub parallel to the axis of flow through the hub. Samples may be taken from a prolongation of the hub to avoid destroying a usable flange or connector. Tensile tests for blinds and spacers may be taken from the parent plate.

## 6.1 Tensile Properties

All high-yield flanges or connectors shall satisfy the mechanical properties shown in MSS SP-44 or API 6A as appropriate. All high-yield blinds and spacers shall satisfy the mechanical properties of the base material.

## 6.2 Hardness

Random hardness tests shall be made on at least one finished item from each heat. Hardness values shall not exceed RC22 (Vickers 248 or Brinnel 237). Testing shall be done in accordance with ASTM A370.

## 6.3 Fracture Toughness Test

- One set of Charpy V-Notch impact tests, in accordance with ASTM A370 (a set consists of three specimens), shall be performed on the base material heat for each lot of items or from one complete item from each lot. The sample may be removed from a prolongation to prevent destruction of a usable flange or connector. Flattening of specimens is permitted.

- Specimens shall be machined from samples oriented as required in the applicable MSS or ASTM specification. For slip blinds and spacers made from plate, specimens shall be machined from the plate perpendicular to the plate-rolling direction.

- All specimens shall be the thickest standard size that can be prepared.

## 7    Inspection and Rejection

All sharp notches, imperfections, scratches, etc., shall be removed by grinding if they fall within the non-injurious defect tolerances listed in paragraph UF-37 of ASME, Section VIII, "Unfired Pressure Vessels." Correction by welding shall not be acceptable. The repair of dents is not to be permitted. All weld bevels are to be checked for lamination by ultrasonic, dye-penetrant, or magnetic particle processes. No lamination is acceptable.

## 8    Marking

All items shall be marked in accordance with MSS SP-25. The Impact Test Temperature shall be added if nonstandard for the material class. Flanges that meet more than one standard, (e.g. ASME 1500# and API 5000#) may be marked accordingly by the manufacturer only.

Cold dye stamping shall not be used except on the outside surface of the flange rim, connector clamp assembly, or handles of slip blinds and spacers. Low-stress round-nose or interrupted dot stamp shall be used for marking on any other part of the item.

- Preparation for Shipment
  - Sealing faces and weld bevels shall be thoroughly cleaned and coated with rust preventative.
  - Flanges or connectors shall be shipped without other surfaces coated. The inner and outer surfaces shall be free of oil, grease, dirt, loose mill scale, and other foreign matter.
  - All item sealing faces and weld bevels shall be protected by heavy wooden or plastic covers securely fastened in place. A minimum of 4 bolts or poly plugs shall be required for securing all flange face covers.

## 9    Documentation

Certified mill test reports shall be submitted for all items. The report shall contain the analysis and testing information related to chemical, physical, hardness, and notch toughness (if required) properties of the materials. The report shall also indicate the carbon equivalent. The reports shall be traceable to the flanges by heat number. Each document shall bear the signature of the authorized representative of the agency that performed the test or analysis.
