1.0 See Sour Service Definition and Material Requirements in Owner's Specification.

2.0 Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A516 Gr 70N. For low temperature service, material shall be Charpy V-notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.

3.0 Buried pipe to be externally coated with materials suitable for the application.

4.0 See Owner's Specification for Nondestructive Examination requirements. When the amount of radiographic examination is less than 100%, the progressive examination requirements of B31.3 Clause 341.3.4 or CSA Z662 Clause 7.10.3.1 shall apply.

5.0 Maximum allowable temperature 100° C.

6.0 Full port valves for use on lines subject to pigging operations.

7.0 Threaded connections allowed subject to compliance with Owner's Specification GS 2.1 Piping Design.

8.0 Lines subject to pigging operations to be 3 NPS or larger.

9.0 Design Factors for CSA Z662 equation 4.3.5.1 are F=0.8, L=0.625, J=1, T=1. Designer shall verify design factors are appropriate. Re-evaluate wall thickness, if required.

10.0 Flanged valves 1/2 - 1 1/2 NPS for use on Post Weld Heat Treated lines.

11.0 With approval of weld procedures by Owner PWHT exemption per Owner's Specification Welding Appendix D may be applied.

12.0 PWHT required when service conditions of ISO 15156 are met except as exempted in Owner's Specification Welding Appendix D. When sour service piping is exempted from PWHT, it shall be indicated on drawings provided to the piping fabricator.

13.0 Mill certificate with data supporting compliance with applicable sour service requirements of relevant CSA Z245 series of standards required.

14.0 Dual certified materials may be used A234 WPB/Z245.11 SS, A106 Gr.B/Z245.1 Cat I SS, A105N/Z245.12 SS. Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 materials not available.

15.0 No threaded connections allowed as primary process connections.

16.0 For glycol and hot oil service, use SW fittings, except valves which may be threaded.

17.0 Materials are appropriate for category 1 fluid service only.

18.0 A53 Gr. B Pipe may be substituted with approval. (ERW for NPS 12-18, DSAW for NPS 20-24). Check wall thickness.

19.0 Deleted.

20.0 Rich Sulfinol from high pressure contactor level control valve manifold, use 304L stainless steel with 316 fittings.

21.0 Rich Sulfinol from lean/rich heat exchanger level control valve to regenerator, use 304L Stainless Steel with 316 Stainless Steel valves and Spectacle blinds material A167 or A240 Grades 304 or 304L.

22.0 Dual certified materials may be used A333 Gr. 6 / Z245.1 Gr. 241 Cat I SS, A420 WPL 6 / Z245.11 Gr. 241 Cat I SS, A350 LF2 / Z245.12 Cat I SS. Fittings may have only ASTM specification only when suitable CSA Z245 materials not available.

23.0 Consider maximum pressure of 1035 kPag to be consistent with category "D" fluid service per B31.3 and use reduced testing requirements.

24.0 If using SW flanges, bore to match pipe.

25.0 Maximum Spectacle Blind size shall be 12NPS. A blind and spacer shall be used for larger pipe sizes. Spectacle Blind material shall be A240 Gr 304L. Material shall be Charpy V notch tested at temperatures no warmer than the minimum metal design temperature.

26.0 For FF Flanges use Class 150 - 3 mm THK Full Face Gaskets, "Klingernite 1000", "Durlon 8600" or "Garlock 3100."
