# 1   General

## 1.1   Scope

This Specification (SPC) sets forth the minimum requirements for the manufacture, testing, and inspection of seamless and welded high-yield welding fittings, mandrel-formed fittings, factory hot-formed fittings, and extruded fittings of Manufacturers Standardization Society (MSS) SP-75, modified grades WPHY-52 through WPHY-70. High-yield Carbon Steel Flanges and Forged Fittings, such as integrally reinforced branch connections (o-lets), socket weld and threaded unions, elbows and couplings, and flanged pipettes shall conform to High-yield Carbon Steel Flanges and Forged Fittings (OGE-PISPE-000007).

## 1.2   Referenced Standards and Codes

This SPC states requirements that are in addition to the referenced codes and standards. Wherever conflicts exist between the requirements of the referenced codes and standards and this SPC, the requirements of the more stringent shall govern. Items not addressed in this SPC shall conform to the requirements of the referenced standards and codes.

## 1.3   Material Source

For all materials, a statement of country of origin shall be made at the time of quotation. If the item is formed in one location (e.g., forged or cast) and processed in another (e.g., machined or heat-treated), each shall be noted.

# 4   Design, Construction, and Dimensions

## 4.1   Design Requirements

- All butt-welding fitting designs shall be qualified by proof testing per MSS SP-75.

- High-yield carbon steel fittings specified are intended to be welded to high-yield carbon steel pipe of the same metallurgy, yield strength, and similar wall thickness. If lower yield fittings are offered as alternatives, they must be made thicker to compensate. The increase in wall thickness shall be on the outside diameter (O.D.) rather than on the inside diameter (I.D.) to facilitate "bridge welding" during field fabrication (refer to ASME B31.4, Figure 434.8.6(a) -2, sketch (f), for details). The quotation shall indicate the grade of low-yield fittings being offered, including wall thickness. In addition, the quotation shall include a detailed dimensional drawing of the butt-weld ends. All such substitutions must receive prior approval from the Company.

- All butt-weld fittings shall be made in such a manner that the mechanical properties of the pipe are not reduced below the minimum values allowed for the original pipe grade. Additionally, all butt-weld fittings shall meet the requirements of the appropriate code and standard specifications listed in Section 2.0.

- All butt-weld fittings shall be made in such a manner as to preserve the cross-sectional shape of the pipe, and shall be free from wrinkling, buckling, cracks, or other evidence of mechanical damage.

## 4.2   Dimensions

Fittings shall comply with the dimensional requirements of the referenced design standard in the purchase order description.

- High-yield fittings, 16 inch O.D. and larger, shall have dimensions and tolerances per MSS SP-75. All High-yield butt-welding fittings 14 inches and less shall have dimensions and tolerances per ASME B16.9.

- Unless otherwise noted, transition pieces shall have lengths equal to ASME B16.9 reducer lengths. When specified, some transition pieces will be required to have drawn outlets (e.g., for use with bypass valves).

[Oil Search logo]

- Nonstandard and proprietary fittings that are not manufactured to an industry standard, such as weldolets, laterals, etc., shall be furnished with the dimensions of the manufacturer referenced in the purchase description. All other manufacturing considerations shall be per the design standard referenced in the purchase description.

## 4.3 Tolerances

- All tolerances shall be as per MSS SP-75 or ASME B16.9 as applicable and as further specified herein. For fittings larger than 48 inches nominal O.D., the tolerances shall be the same as for 48 inches nominal O.D.
- The maximum wall thickness at the fitting ends shall not exceed the specified wall thickness by more than 15 percent.
- All fitting ends shall be beveled for welding, per MSS SP-75.
- If the minimum I.D. is more than 3/16 inch smaller than the nominal I.D., the fitting shall be taper-bored at an angle of not less than 14 degrees nor more than 30 degrees from the axis of the pipe, so that the land on the bevel matches the nominal I.D.
- All fittings specified as having "controlled roundness" shall have a maximum allowable O.D. out-of-roundness at any point of +1 percent

# 5 Process of Manufacture

## 5.1 Construction

- The fitting manufacturing process shall be fully described in the quotation. Free bending shall not be permitted.
- When welded construction is specified, the fittings shall comply with MSS SP-75 Section 14.4 and the following:
  - The weld shall be made with the addition of filler metal by welders, welding operators, and welding procedures qualified under provisions of ASME, BPVC Section IX.
  - Filler material shall deposit a composition that corresponds to the chemical composition and mechanical properties of the base material.
  - The fitting shall be radiographically or ultrasonically examined throughout the entire length of each fabricated weld as per MSS SP-75, after final forming and heat treatment.
- Split hot tap fittings shall have no weld seams in the body of the fitting before use.

## 5.2 Heat Treatment

- Post-forming heat treatment may be required to meet the test requirements. Fittings shall receive the heat treatment prescribed in the referenced standard, except as noted.
- Heat treatment shall be on the entire fitting by placing the fitting in a furnace of sufficient size to contain the piece. The temperature shall be as shown in the Manufacturer's approved procedure and shall be maintained within a maximum range of ±50 °F. Heat treating by passing the fitting through an induction coil is not allowed. Localized heat treatment on welds (of welded fittings) is not permitted. All split hot tap fittings requiring heat treatment shall have both halves heat treated in the same lot.

## 5.3 Manufacturing Restrictions

- All butt-welding fittings shall be sandblasted on the O.D. prior to bending to remove foreign particles. Under no circumstances shall the pipe come in contact with copper or zinc bearing materials after sandblasting.
- Fabricated branch fittings made by joining two pieces of pipe ("stub-in") are not permitted. Y-laterals may be submitted on an exception basis.
- Mitered type fittings are not permitted.
- Circumferential welds within the fittings are prohibited.
- The welding of a pup piece to correct fabrication defects or dimensional errors is not permitted.
- All fittings shall be made in such a manner as to preserve the cross-sectional shape of the pipe.
- For fittings made from pipe containing a longitudinal weld, the weld shall be positioned as close as possible to the least distortion axis of the fitting.

# 7 Physical Properties and Test

## 7.1 Tensile Properties

Per MSS SP-75, one fitting from each lot shall be subjected to tensile testing. Test specimens may be any size conforming to ASTM A370, Figures 3 and 4, with full thickness specimens preferred. Specimens shall be oriented as specified in MSS SP-75, except that transverse weld tests are required on all sizes of welded pipes. Reduced size transverse weld test specimens are permitted for sizes smaller than NPS 8. Tensile tests specimens shall be taken at the following regions.

- Fully formed and/or extruded region(s): Locations shall be at both the most compressed and tensioned sections of the fitting base metal and at any welds.
- Transition zone (base and weld).
- Tangent length (if heat treated; base and weld).

Matched heats may be grouped into a single lot. All of the above specimens should be tested from one heat. The additional heats may then be qualified by tensile tests of the most worked areas of the base metal and of the weld.

The tensile properties shall be in accordance with MSS SP-75 for the grade of fittings specified, except that the ratio of yield strength to tensile strength of each test specimen shall not exceed

0.90. Per MSS SP-75 SR-6, the actual yield strength shall not exceed the specified minimum

yield strength by more than 20,000 pounds per square inch (psi).

## 7.2 Charpy V-Notch Test

- One set of Charpy V-Notch impact tests, in accordance with ASTM A370 (a set consists of three specimens), shall be performed on the base metal, one set on the weld, and one set on the heat-affected zone (HAZ) from one fitting from each lot. Matched heats may be grouped into a single lot.

- For fitting sizes that accommodate full-size (0.394 in x 0.394 in) Charpy specimens and larger, the specimens shall be machined from the fitting without flattening. For sizes too small for full-size specimens, subsized specimens may be used.

- For NPS 6 and larger fittings, specimens shall be oriented transverse to the longitudinal axis with the notch oriented through the wall thickness so that the crack propagates in the longitudinal direction. Charpy specimens from the base metal of welded fittings shall be taken from a location 90 degrees from the weld. Charpy specimens from the weld shall be machined with the notch along the center line of the weld and in the same orientation described above.

- For fittings smaller than NPS 6, the Charpy specimens shall be oriented longitudinal to the fitting axis with the notch through the wall thickness.

- All Charpy tests shall be performed at -50 °F/-46 °C, unless otherwise specified.

- The minimum required Charpy V-notch absorbed energy in foot-pounds is based on the wall thickness (t) and grade of the fitting. In each case, the first number is the required average value for any set of three full-size (0.394 in x 0.394 in) specimens, the second is the lowest specimen tested.

### 7.3 Stepwise Cracking Resistance

For fittings formed from plate or welded pipe, one fitting from each heat shall be subject to stepwise cracking resistance testing. Tests performed on the parent plate or pipe at the mill are acceptable. One test shall be performed for each heat in accordance with NACE Standard TM0284. For acceptance, the crack length ratio (CLR) shall not exceed 15%, the crack sensitivity ratio (CSR) shall not exceed 2%, and the crack thickness ratio (CTR) shall not exceed 5% for the average of all planes on all specimens from a single lot of steel. The test specimen evaluation shall be by metallographic examination.

Stepwise cracking resistance testing is not required for cast fittings or for fittings formed from seamless pipe.

## 8 Nondestructive Examination

### 8.1 Visual Inspection

Each fitting shall be inspected to ensure that tolerances listed in this SPC are not exceeded. Every fitting shall be visually examined externally for surface faults. The full inside surface shall be inspected, if accessible.

All fittings shall be free from wrinkling, buckling, cracks, or other evidence of mechanical damage. Waves with a width to depth ratio of 12 to 1 or greater are acceptable. Smooth dents, less than 1/4 inch deep and no greater than one-half the pipe diameter in width, are acceptable.

### 8.2 Ultrasonic Testing

The wall thickness of all fittings shall be checked by ultrasonic testing in accordance with ASTM E114 or E 213 and shall not be less than the specified minimum wall thickness.

Each extruded or weld-fabricated fitting shall be inspected for wall thickness and defects in the entire area of cold or hot working or welding per MSS SP-75.

At five locations along the fitting, a series of three readings shall be taken. These locations shall include all the areas where the material was bent, stretched, compressed, or welded.

### 8.3 Hardness Testing

Hardness tests shall be made on at least one fitting from every post-fabrication heat treatment lot and on at least one fitting from each lot with no post fabrication heat treatment. No hardness shall exceed Rockwell C Hardness (HRC) 22 (Vickers 248, Brinnel 235).

[Oil Search logo]

## 8.4 Radiographic Testing

All butt welds shall be radiographically or ultrasonically inspected per MSS SP-75, after forming and heat treatment.

## 8.5 Weld Bevels

All weld bevels are to be checked for lamination by ultrasonic, dye-penetrant, or magnetic particle processes. No lamination is acceptable.

# 9 Rejection and Repairs

## 9.1 Rejection

If any fitting fails to meet the test result requirements, the reason shall be determined, if possible. Retesting may be permitted as follows:

Two additional fittings from the same lot shall be tested. If either fitting fails to meet the specified requirements, the entire lot of fittings shall be rejected unless subsequent heat treatment and 100 percent retesting shows acceptable results.

## 9.2 Repairs

No welding repairs to the body or seam of any fitting shall be permitted. The repair of dents shall not be permitted. Defects in the fitting not encroaching upon the required minimum thickness may be removed by grinding, provided a smooth, curved surface is maintained and the final thickness after grinding is not less than the required minimum thickness. The remaining wall thickness shall be measured by ultrasonic methods. Defects in the fitting encroaching upon the specified minimum thickness shall be cause for rejection

# 10 Marking

All fittings furnished under this SPC shall be marked per MSS SP-25 and the referenced ASME or ASTM specification. For split hot tap fittings, mark the top half on one end and the bottom half on the opposite end. Serial numbers shall be traceable to the original mill heat number.

If marking is to be done by stamp, low-pressure (blunt-nosed-continuous or blunt-nosed-interrupted- dot die) steel stamps, in accordance with MSS SP-25, shall be used. The depth of impression shall not infringe upon the required design minimum wall thickness. Steel-stamping on welded construction fittings shall not be on any weld or HAZs.

## 11   Preparation for Shipment

Fittings shall be carefully handled and protected during all phases of manufacture, shipping, and storage. All fittings shall be palletized or securely fastened to trailers for shipping purposes and shall not be permitted to come in contact with each other.

- All weld bevels shall be thoroughly cleaned and coated with a long-term rust inhibitor such as deoxaluminite.

- Threaded and socket welded connections shall be protected by tightly fitting plastic caps or plugs.

- Plastic end caps and plugs shall be suitable for a salt water tropical atmosphere and temperatures as low as -50 °F.

- All hooks and chains used for handling fittings must be soft-faced. Protected wire rope or chains, aluminum, Teflon, or rubber is suggested. Copper and copper alloys (bronze, brass, etc.) are specifically prohibited.

- The inner and outer surfaces shall be free of oil, grease, dirt, loose mill scale and other foreign matter.

## 12   Quality Documentation

The following certified reports shall be provided:

- Heat analysis reports on the chemical composition of each heat of steel used in the manufacture of the fittings.

- Report on results of all required physical tests, including impact and hardness tests.

- Description of heat treatments applied to the fitting material and test pieces.

- Certification stating that all requirements of this SPC have been met.

- For wet sour service, a written certification shall be included to certify the material meets the requirements of NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

- For materials to be used for low temperature services and for all cast steels, quality certificates from the primary raw materials manufacturers (steel mill, foundries, etc.) and ASTM standard designation shall be included.
