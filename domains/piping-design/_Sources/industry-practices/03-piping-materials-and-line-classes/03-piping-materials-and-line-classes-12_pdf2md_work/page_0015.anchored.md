**11.1.7** Pipe nipples shall be of the same material as the piping to which they are attached.

## 11.2 Branch Connections

The selection and fabrication requirements for piping branch connections shall be per the requirements of REP S-5-1.

## 11.3 Bends and Mitered Bends

**11.3.1** Butt welding elbows shall be long radius, unless short-radius elbows are required and specified on drawings.

**11.3.2** To avoid excessive stiffness for piping flexibility, a maximum thickness limit of 30% above nominal shall be specified for elbows in sizes greater than NPS 12.

**11.3.3** (\*) Pipe bends shall be limited to use in lines handling solids, certain pigged lines, and other applications with special requirements. They shall not be used for general piping where weld fittings can be used unless approved by Owner's Engineer. When bends are used, an increased wall thickness may be required to satisfy the intended service. When used, pipe bends shall comply with the following:

1. Either seamless or welded pipe may be formed into bends, except the furnace buttwelded type.
2. Centerline radius of pipe bends shall be equal to at least 5 times the nominal pipe diameter.

**11.3.4** Miter bends may be used when more economical than welding elbows for the following services:

1. Water piping.
2. Drain lines.
3. (\*) Internal piping in pressure vessels when approved by Owner's Engineer.
4. (\*) Other services subject to the approval of Owner's Engineer.

**11.3.5** Fabrication requirements for pipe bends and miter bends shall be as specified in REP S-5-1.

## 11.4 Reducers, Pipe Caps, and Plugs

**11.4.1** Buttwelding and socket-welding reducers and swaged nipples shall be preferred to flanged reducers for reductions in run. Swaged nipples shall be used when a reduction in size is made at threaded equipment.

**11.4.2** Long-radius buttwelding reducing elbows made in accordance with ASME B16.9 shall be considered for line reductions at end suction pumps.

**11.4.3** In threaded pipe, reducing nipples shall be used; the use of bushings shall be prohibited. Socketwelding reducer inserts shall be used in socket-welded piping (NPS 2 and smaller) for reductions of two or more pipe sizes.

**11.4.4** Welding caps shall be preferred for closures in welded piping, except that bolted flanged covers shall be used where access is needed (for example, for cleaning, inspection, or a future tie-in). In sizes for which welding caps are not commercially available, closure shall be made by means of ellipsoidal heads.

**11.4.5** Threaded plugs shall be fabricated from solid barstock and match the material of the line class.
