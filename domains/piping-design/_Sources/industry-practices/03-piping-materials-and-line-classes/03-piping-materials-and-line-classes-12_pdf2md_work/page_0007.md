## 5.0 MATERIALS

### 5.1 General

**5.1.1** All materials used for piping components shall be per the requirement of this Practice and ASME B31.3 or ASME B31.8, as applicable.

**5.1.2** Reclaimed metallic and non-metallic materials shall only be allowed with inspection for service and approval from Owner's Engineer.

**5.1.3** (*) The use of materials at temperatures above or below those for which allowable stress values are shown in ASME B31.3 shall require prior approval from Owner's Engineer.

### 5.2 Metallic Materials

**5.2.1** Acceptable materials for all metallic pipe, fittings, forgings, and valves shall be as provided in Table 1. The selection of materials not listed in Table 1 shall require approval of Owner's Engineer before use.

**5.2.2** Piping materials and PWHT requirements for Aggressive Environmental Service shall be in accordance with the requirements of REP 10-2-2.

**5.2.3** Supplemental material requirements for carbon steel, low alloy steel, stainless steel and high alloys shall be as stipulated in REP 10-2-2. These requirements include items such as carbon equivalent, chemistry control, heat treatment, and Charpy impact requirements.

**5.2.4** Acceptable materials for tubing shall be as provided in Table 11. The selection of materials not listed in Table 11 shall require approval of Owner's Engineer before use.

**5.2.5** (*) The use of materials with greater than 70,000 psi minimum tensile strength for pressure containment shall require written approval from Owner's Engineer.

**5.2.6** Limitations on the use of copper, and copper bearing alloys (UNS Cxxxxx), shall be as given below for the following services:

1. Ammonia: Alloys with a copper content greater than 50% shall not be used in ammonia service or in aqueous solutions containing ammonia where the pH exceeds 8.5.
2. Caustic and Alkaline: Alloys with a copper content less than 85% shall not be used.
3. Acetylene: Alloys with a copper content greater than 50% shall not be used in services where acetylene in a wet environment is present.

**5.2.7** Copper, aluminum, and their alloys shall not be used for piping components in flammable service, except that aluminum may be used in ASME B16.5, Classes 150, 300, and 600 for temperatures below -50°F.

**5.2.8** If aluminum castings (for example, valve bodies) have been subjected to welding or thermal cutting, their ratings and/or allowable stresses shall require approval from Owner's Engineer. Use of aluminum castings other than ASTM B2.6 Grade 356 T6 shall require prior approval from Owner's Engineer.

**5.2.9** Lead, tin, and their alloys shall not be used in flammable fluid services. They shall not be used in other pressure piping without prior approval from Owner's Engineer.

**5.2.10** (*) Lead is not to be used in fire water or underground cooling water services. Ductile iron pipe shall be centrifugally cast and suitable for a minimum working pressure of 125 psig and a design pressure of 150 psig.
