**SELECTION OF PIPING COMPONENTS AND MATERIALS**

**5.2.11** Low melting point alloys, such as brass or bronze, shall not be used for piping or piping components in flammable material or combustible liquid services. Aluminum alloys shall be permitted in cryogenic service.

**5.2.12** Supplemental material requirements for piping in Hydrofluoric Acid (HF) service shall be as covered in REP 5–19–1.

**5.2.13** Austenitic stainless steel used for corrosive service shall be the extra low carbon or stabilized grades AISI Types 304L, 316L, 317L, 321 or 347. The use of Grades 304L, 316L and 317L shall be limited to a maximum design temperature of 800°F and a maximum operating temperature of 750°F without specific approval of the plant and an experienced Phillips 66 Materials/Corrosion Engineer.

**5.3 Non-Metallic Materials**

**5.3.1** (*) Unreinforced PTFE (Teflon™) and glass-reinforced PTFE (Teflon™) shall not be used where they would be exposed to temperatures above 350°F and 450°F respectively. However, when used as the filler for spiral wound gaskets, unreinforced PTFE (Teflon™) may be used to 450°F. Use of PTFE (Teflon™) filled with or reinforced by other materials shall require prior approval from Owner's Engineer.

**5.3.2** (*) Non-metallic materials shall not be used for pressure-containing piping components (other than gaskets, packing, and valve seats) in flammable services. They shall not be used in toxic service without prior approval from Owner's Engineer.

**5.3.3** Requirements for plastic piping shall be as covered in REP 5–9–1.

**5.3.4** Requirements for plastic-lined pipe shall be as covered in REP 5–9–2.

**5.3.5** Requirements for cement-lined pipe shall be as covered in REP 5–9–3.

**5.4 Identification**

**5.4.1** (*) Contractor or Fabricator shall submit their material control procedures to Owner's Engineer for approval. Special emphasis for color coding shall be placed on low temperature carbon steel material, low residual carbon steel material intended for HF alkylation service, and H Grade 300 Series stainless steel.

**5.4.2** Valves shall be marked in accordance with REP 5–3–21.

**6.0 PIPING**

**6.1 General Requirements**

**6.1.1** Selection of piping materials shall be per Table 1.

**6.1.2** Pipe dimensions shall be in accordance with the following:
1. ASME B36.10 for welded and seamless wrought steel pipe.
2. (*) ASME B36.19 for stainless steel pipe; stainless steel pipe per ASME B36.10 shall be acceptable if approved by Owner's Engineer.

**6.1.3** All pipe threads shall conform to ASME B1.20.1 taper pipe threads. Butt weld preparation shall conform to ASME B16.25.

**6.1.4** Pipe in sizes NPS 3 and greater shall be furnished with both ends prepared for buttwelding in accordance with ASME B16.25, Figure 2A or Figure 3A as applicable. Flame cut weld bevels shall not be permitted.
