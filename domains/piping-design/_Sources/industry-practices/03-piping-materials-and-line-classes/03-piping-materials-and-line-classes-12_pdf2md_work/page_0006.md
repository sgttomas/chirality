## 3.0 DEFINITIONS

**3.1** Aggressive Environmental Service (AES) – A fluid/material service, as defined in REP 10–2–1 and REP 10–2–2.

**3.2** Contractor – Company or business that agrees to furnish materials or perform specified services at a specified price and/or rate to the Owner.

**3.3** Fluid Service – Fluid service as defined in REP 5–1–1.

**3.4** Fluid Service and Service Classification – Fluid and service classification as defined in REP 5–1–1.

**3.5** Inspector – A Phillips 66 appointed engineer or inspector.

**3.6** Manufacturer – The recipient of a direct or indirect purchase order for materials and/or equipment. In this context, a direct order is one issued to a manufacturer by a contractor or the Owner. An indirect order is one issued to a manufacturer by a vendor (recipient of a direct order) for materials, fabricated components, or subassemblies.

**3.7** Owner – Phillips 66.

**3.8** Owner's Engineer – A Phillips 66 appointed engineer.

**3.9** Pipe Nipple – A short piece of pipe with or without threaded ends.

**3.10** Piping Service Class – Piping system classification as defined in REP 5–1–1.

**3.11** Piping System – Piping circuit as defined in REP 5–1–1.

**3.12** Purchaser – The party placing a direct purchase order. The Purchaser is the Owner's designated representative.

**3.13** Service Cyclic Conditions – Thermal Load conditions producing significant fatigue effects as defined in ASME B31.3.

**3.14** Seal Welding – A weld use to seal threaded joints in small bore piping; for purpose of this document it shall cover all the threads creating a smooth transition between the tap component and the nipple or valve body.

**3.15** Small-Bore Piping (SBP) – Piping with size as defined in REP 5–1–1.

## 4.0 GENERAL

**4.1** (\*) All piping components shall conform to the requirements of this Practice, ASME B31.3 or ASME B31.1, as applicable, and local jurisdictional regulations. In case of conflict among these requirements, the most stringent requirements, as determined by Owner's Engineer, shall govern. Also, refer to REP 3–1–1 for environmental requirements for piping.

**4.2** (\*) The use of piping components made to dimensional standards not listed in ASME B31.3 or ASME B31.1 shall require approval of Owner's Engineer unless specifically approved herein. Any valves, fittings, flanges, or unions made to such standards shall be marked in accordance with MSS SP-25.

**4.3** (\*) The use of components and material standards with a date other than the one referenced in the latest edition or addendum of ASME B31.3 or ASME B31.1 shall require approval of Owner's Engineer.
