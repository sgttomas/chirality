## Selection of Piping Components and Materials

*Refining Engineering Practices*

[TABLE: Table 12: Minimum Corrosion and Thread Depth Allowances (6) (7)]

| Type of Pipe | Pipe Material | NPS | Minimum Corrosion Allowance (inches) | Thread Depth Allowance (inches) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Cast | All metals | All | 0.0625 | — |
| Welded or Seamless | Carbon and Low Alloy Steels (1) | All | 0.0625 | — |
| Welded or Seamless | Intermediate Alloy Steels (2) | All | 0.030 | — |
| Welded or Seamless | High Alloy Steels (3) | All | 0.030 | — |
| Welded or Seamless | Nickel Copper Alloys (Alloy 400), Nickel, and Nickel Alloys (4) | All | 0.020 | — |
| Threaded end | All Metallic Materials | 1/2 and 3/4 | — | 0.0571 (6) |
| Threaded end | All Metallic Materials | 1 thru 2 | — | 0.0696 (6) |

NOTES:

(1) Includes all steels with an alloy content not exceeding 2-1/4 Cr-1 Mo.
(2) Includes all steels with an alloy content of 2-1/2 to 9 Cr, and 3-1/2 to 9 Ni steels.
(3) Includes all steels with an alloy content of 12Cr and greater.
(4) Includes materials such as Alloy 600, Alloy 625, Alloy 800, Alloy 800H, Alloy C-276, and Alloy C-22.
(5) When a corrosion allowance of 1/16 inch is specified, a minimum value of 0.050 is acceptable.
(6) Equals a maximum depth of ASME B1.20.1 threads.
(7) (*) The above corrosion allowances are minimum only; corrosion allowances for each line must be obtained from Owner's Engineer.
