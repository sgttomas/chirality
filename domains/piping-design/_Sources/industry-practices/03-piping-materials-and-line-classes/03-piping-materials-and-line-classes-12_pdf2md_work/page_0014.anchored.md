## 9.0 TUBING JOINTS

### 9.1 General Requirements

All tubing joints shall be designed, inspected, tested, fabricated, and assembled in accordance with REP 5–5–1, REP 5–5–3, and ASME B31.3 or ASME B31.1, as applicable.

### 9.2 Proprietary Joints

(\*) All proprietary and special tube joints shall be approved by Owner's Engineer prior to use.

### 9.3 Bite-Type Compression Fittings

**9.3.1** (\*) Joints with flared, flareless, or compression bite-type tubing fittings shall not be used in severe cyclic conditions without prior approval from Owner's Engineer.

**9.3.2** (\*) Bite-type compression fittings shall be used with tubing. Compression fittings shall be per Table 16 for refinery sites listed; otherwise, compression fittings shall be per individual refinery practice to ensure compatibility of connections throughout the refinery. Component parts of these fittings shall not be interchanged between manufacturers' models. These joints are limited to 375°F and 150 psig if made of brass, to 375°F and 1000 psig if made of carbon steel, alloy 400 (UNS: N04400) or alloy 625 (UNS: N06626), and to 1200°F and 3100 psig if made of AISI Type 304, 316 stainless steel or Alloy 20 (UNS: N08020). Derating factors per the manufacturer shall be used based on design temperature. Higher pressures and temperatures shall be permitted with submittal of ASME B31.3 Code calculations and approval of Owner's Engineer.

## 11.0 FITTINGS

### 11.1 General Requirements

**11.1.1** Fittings, including bends and branch connections, shall be in accordance with REP 5–1–1, ASME B16.3 or ASME B31.1, as applicable, and the additional requirements of this Practice.

**11.1.2** Materials for all fittings shall be per Table 1. All fitting materials shall be of the same nominal composition as the pipe to which they are attached.

**11.1.3** Fittings shall be per Table 17 and the additional requirements of this Practice. Unless otherwise specified by the Purchaser, fittings shall be furnished to match the pressure rating, size and wall thickness of the attached pipe.

**11.1.4** The use of cast steel buttwelding fittings conforming to ASME B16.9 shall be prohibited.

**11.1.5** The use of cast steel fittings conforming to ASME B16.5 shall be prohibited.
