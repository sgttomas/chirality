## 12.0 SPECIAL COMPONENTS

**12.1** (*) The use of piping components designed in accordance with 304.7.2, A304.7.2, or A304.7.3 of ASME B31.3, rather than listed Component Standards, shall require prior approval from Owner's Engineer. Exceptions to this requirement shall include any of the commonly used steam traps (such as Armstrong or Sarco-Spirax) and strainers (such as Mueller).

**12.2** The materials used in special components, such as steam traps, strainers, separators, and filters, shall conform to the manufacturer's standards and the requirements of Section 5.0.

**12.3** Metal and fabric expansion joints shall be designed to comply with the requirements of REP 5–4–1 and REP 5–4–2, respectively.
