**7.3.1.3** Threaded joints may be used in flammable/combustible fluid service (including light ends) and non-flammable hazardous fluid service providing that:

1. The joint is seal welded per REP 5–5–2.
2. Base pipe is a minimum of Schedule 80.
3. The nipple is a minimum of Schedule 160.
4. Valves used are Class 800 or Class 1500 extended body (EB) neck valves.

**7.3.1.4** Threaded joints may be used in alloy piping circuits in flammable/combustible and non-flammable – hazardous fluid services providing that:

1. Seal welding per REP 5–5–2 is used.
2. (\*) Any threaded joints in alloy materials requiring PWHT is approved by an experienced Phillips 66 Corrosion/Materials Engineer.
3. PMI requirements of REP 15–1–4 are followed.
4. Requirements for PWHT in REP 5–5–3 are followed.

**7.3.1.5** Threaded joints may be used in Hydrofluoric Acid (HF) alkylation service in Phillips 66 licensed HF units and UOP licensed HF units, with the provisions of REP 5–10–1.

**7.3.1.6** Threaded joints may be used in the following fluid services or applications with noted provisions:

1. In all category D fluid service.
2. In the following special application without seal welding:
   a) For galvanized piping, NPS 4 and smaller, in non-process service.
   b) Where access or openings need to be created downstream of the first normally close valve, such as plugs in vents, drains, and cleanouts.
   c) Where equipment and instrumentations require frequent removal for maintenance and has appropriate design components for access/removal, such as instruments, thermal relief valves, instrument taps, with approval of Owner's Engineer.
   d) In oil mist lubrication systems, per REP 6–9–3.
   e) (\*) Threads where one or both components are made from cast iron, ductile iron, or other materials on which welding is prohibited by Owner's Engineer.
   f) Union bonnets of valves.

**7.3.2** Prior to seal, full engagement shall be accomplished as required in paragraph 7.3.3.

**7.3.3** Threaded joints shall be tightened to ensure full thread engagement per the following:

1. For NPS 1 and less: Five engaged threads.
2. For NPS 1–1/2 (NPS 2 for heritage users): Six engaged threads.

**7.3.4** Threaded joints that are not to be seal welded shall be made up with a lubricant suited for the specific service. The lubricants shown in Table 1 of REP 5–5–3, or their equivalents, shall be recommended as indicated.

## 7.4 Flanged Joints

**7.4.1** Requirements for flanges, bolting and gaskets shall be as stipulated in REP 5–2–2.

**7.4.2** Materials for flanges shall be per Table 1 in this Practice. The flange material shall be the same as the piping material to which it is attached. Materials for bolting and gaskets shall be in accordance with REP 5–2–5.
