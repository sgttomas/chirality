## Selection of Piping Components and Materials

*Refining Engineering Practices*

[TABLE: Table 3: Acceptable Piping Materials for Low Alloy Steel]

| Service | Pipe | Flanges & Fittings <sup>(1)(2)</sup> | Valves |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| All Fluid Services | **All Sizes** | **Flanges & SW/THRD** | **Forged Valves** |
|  | A335/A335M Grades: P11 (1-1/4 Cr-1/2 Mo), P22 (2-1/4 Cr-1 Mo), P9 (9 Cr-1 Mo) | **Fittings (B16.11)** A182 Grades: F11, F11b, F22a, F22b, F9 | A182 Grades: F11, F11b, F22a, F22b, F9 |
|  | **> NPS 16** | **BW Fittings (B16.9)** | **Cast Valves** |
|  | A691 Grades: 1-1/4 Cr, 9 Cr, Classes: 22, 32, 42 | A234 Grades: WP9, WP11, WP11b, WP22, Class 2 | A217 Grades: WC6 (1-1/4 Cr-1/2 Mo), WC9 (2-1/4 Cr-1 Mo), C12 (9 Cr-1 Mo) |

NOTES:

(1) All welds shall meet the hardness requirements of REP 10-2-3.

(2) Fittings containing butt welds shall be 100% radiographed per REP 5-5-1.

(3) To comply with REP 15-9-1 that does not allow usage of 5 Cr materials, all listed 5 Cr materials were removed from Table 3.
