**7.4.3** Steel line blanks shall be per ASME B16.48 except that the blank thickness shall be based on ASME B31.3. The excess line blank thickness above the ASME B31.3 calculated thickness required for pressure shall be listed as available corrosion allowance for the line blank on the piping isometric.

**7.4.4** Above-ground cast iron pipe shall have flanged joints, either cast integral with the pipe or threaded carbon steel flanges.

**7.4.5** Underground ductile iron pipe with bell and spigot joints may be made up with rubber rings. Rings shall be per ASTM D1869, synthetic rubber, oil-resistant type.

**7.4.6** Requirements for proprietary flange connectors shall be as stipulated in paragraph 7.6.

## 7.5 Unions

**7.5.1** The use of pipe unions shall be governed by the following:

1. Unions shall not be used above ASME/ANSI Class 150.
2. Unions shall not be used in severe cyclic or Category M service.
3. The maximum permissible size for unions is NPS 1–1/2 for non-Category D service.
4. The maximum permissible size for unions is NPS 3 for Category D service.
5. The use of pipe unions between process vessels, lines, or other equipment and the first block valve shall be prohibited.
6. Unions shall be Class 3000 per MSS SP-83.

**7.5.2** Gasketed unions may be used in NPS 3 and smaller sizes, subject to the requirements of the previous paragraph.

## 7.6 Proprietary Joints

**7.6.1** (*) All proprietary and special pipe joints shall be approved by Owner's Engineer prior to use.

**7.6.2** Proprietary joints such as Grayloc connectors of REFLANGE E-CON or G-CON connectors may be considered as alternatives to ASME B16.5 flanges for Classes 600 and higher.

## 8.0 TUBING

## 8.1 General Requirements

**8.1.1** Selection of tubing materials shall be per Table 11. All tubing shall be seamless.

**8.1.2** (*) Tube shall be furnished with square cut ends unless otherwise specified by Purchaser.

**8.1.3** (*) Tube shall be furnished to the specific or random lengths as specified by Purchaser.

## 8.2 Size Limitations

**8.2.1** Tubing shall not be used in sizes larger than 2 inch outside diameter.

*Note: For tubing sizes larger than 1 inch, the ferrules must be pre-swaged through the use of a hydraulic swaging unit.*

## 8.3 Corrosion Allowance and Wall Thickness

**8.3.1** The wall thickness of tubing shall not be less than that required by ASME B31.3 or ASME B31.1, as applicable. The design conditions used to compute the wall thickness shall be established using REP 5-1–1.

**8.3.2** No corrosion allowance shall be required for tubing. Materials shall be selected such that anticipated corrosion during the tubing's service life does not exceed 10 mils.
