## 7.0 PIPING JOINTS

### 7.1 General Requirements

All piping joints shall be designed, inspected, tested, and fabricated in accordance with REP 5–1, REP 5–5–3, and ASME B31.3 or ASME B31.1, as applicable.

### 7.2 Welded Joints

**7.2.1** All welding of piping components shall be per REP 5–5–1 and REP 5–5–2.

**7.2.2** For piping larger than NPS 2, butt welded construction shall be used.

**7.2.3** For NPS 2 pipe, buttwelding shall be preferred.

**7.2.3.1** (\*) Threaded joints subject to the provisions of Section 7.3 may be used with site approval.

**7.2.4** For NPS 1-1/2 and smaller, socket weld design shall be preferred. Socket welding shall not be used where:

1. Crevice corrosion or severe erosion is expected.
2. Fatigue/cyclic service as defined in REP 5–1–1 will be experienced.
3. Required to be butt welded per site requirements.

**7.2.5** Where the use of butt weld or socket weld joints NPS 1-1/2 and smaller is not an option, threaded piping may be used per Section 7.3. Seal-welding requirements for threaded joints shall be per REP 5–5–2 and REP 5–5–3.

### 7.3 Threaded Joints

**7.3.1** Threaded piping joints shall only be allowed in piping NPS 2 and smaller (see paragraph 7.2.3.1), with exceptions provided in paragraph 7.3.1.6. Fluid service and/or service classification per REP 5–1–1 limit(s) the use of threaded joints, as follows:

**7.3.1.1** Threaded joints shall not be used:

1. In high pressure fluid service as defined in REP 5–1–1.
2. (\*) In any AES (with the exception of HF/HF Alkylation Service) unless approved by an experienced Phillips 66 Corrosion/Materials Engineer.
3. (\*) Where crevice corrosion or severe erosion is expected unless approved by an experienced Phillips 66 Corrosion/Materials Engineer.

**7.3.1.2** Threaded joints may be used in fatigue/cyclic service providing that:

1. The joint is seal welded per REP 5–5–2.
2. Base pipe is a minimum of schedule 80.
3. The nipple is a minimum of schedule 160.
4. Valves used are Class 800 or Class 1500 Extended Body (EB) neck valves.
5. Appropriate bracing is applied per REP 5–1–1.
