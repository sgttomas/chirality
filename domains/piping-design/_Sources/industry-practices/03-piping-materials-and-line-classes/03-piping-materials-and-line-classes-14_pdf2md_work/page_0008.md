## Supplemental Requirements for Pipe and Piping Components

*Refining Engineering Practices*

**7.2** Completed fittings containing defects originating with the Manufacturer's design, materials, fabrication or workmanship; or not in complete compliance with the requirements of the Purchase Order will be rejected.

**7.3** Discovery of Manufacturer defects, after inspection and acceptance of the fittings by the Purchaser, does not relieve the Manufacturer of his responsibility to comply with the requirements of this Practice and the Purchase Order.

**7.4** Positive Material Identification (PMI) shall be carried out in accordance with REP 15–1–4.

**7.5** The amount of permitted surface rust shall be determined in accordance with the grades specified in ASTM D2200. The acceptance or rejection of pipe or piping components based on these grades, at the destination specified in the Order, shall be as follows:

1. Grade A & B - acceptable
2. Grade C - acceptable (if not covering more than 25% of surface area)
3. Grade D - not acceptable
