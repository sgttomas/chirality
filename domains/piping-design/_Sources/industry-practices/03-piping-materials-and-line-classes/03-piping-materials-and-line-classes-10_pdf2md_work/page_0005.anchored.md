| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| Connector | The term "connector" shall mean dimensionally interchangeable mechanical connectors (e.g., Grayloc or Techlok). |
| Fitting | The term "fitting" both buttweld and socket weld fittings. |
| Flange | The term "flanges" designed to ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47, MSS SP-44, or API Spec 6A Type 6B, or special requirements (for flanges beyond the referenced ratings). |
| Forged Fitting | The term "forge fitting" billet section forged through heating and controlled compression between a set of forging dies, and includes socket welded fittings as well as branch connections. |
| Item | The term "item" shall refer to fittings, flanges, slip blinds, spacers, and connectors, collectively. |
| Lot | A lot is defined as all items made from the same heat of steel and heat treatment batch. |
| Slip Blind or Spacer | Blinds or spacers intended to be installed between flanges and rest entirely within the flange bolt circle. |

## 2 Symbols and Abbreviations

For the purpose of this specification, the following symbols and abbreviations apply:

API — American Petroleum Institute

ASME — American Society of Mechanical Engineers

ASNT — American Society for Nondestructive Testing

ASTM — American Society for Testing and Materials (now ASTM International)

BOM — bill of materials

CS — carbon steel

CVN — Charpy V-notch

HAZ — heat affected zone

ID — inside dimension, inner diameter

lb — pound

mil — thousandth of an inch

mm — millimeter

MSS — Manufacturers Standardization Society

NDT — Nondestructive Testing
