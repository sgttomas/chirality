# 1 General

## 1.1 Scope

This Specification covers the manufacturing, testing, and inspection of duplex stainless steel fittings made from plate or pipe, as well as line blinds and spacers made from forgings or plate, and flanges, forged connectors, and forged fittings for use in gas, oil, and water systems.

## 1.2 Applicable Codes and Standards

The following codes, standards, and specifications shall be considered as a part of this specification. All documents shall be the latest editions in force.

**American Petroleum Institute (API)**

API Spec 6A    Wellhead Equipment

**American Society for Non-destructive Testing (ASNT)**

ASNT-TC-1A    Personnel Qualification and Certification in Nondestructive Testing

**American Society for Testing and Materials (ASTM)**

ASTM A182    Forged or Rolled Alloy and Stainless Steel Pipe Flanges, Forged Fittings, and Valves and Parts for High-Temperature Service

ASTM A194    Carbon and Alloy Steel Nuts for Bolts for High Pressure and High Temperature Service, or Both

ASTM A320    Alloy-Steel and Stainless Steel Bolting for Low-Temperature Service

ASTM A370    Standard Test Methods and Definitions for Mechanical Testing of Steel Products

ASTM A815    Wrought Ferritic, Ferritic/Austenitic, and Martensitic Stainless Steel Pipe Fittings

ASTM A960    Common Requirements for Wrought Steel Pipe Fittings

ASTM A961    Common Requirements for Steel Flanges, Forged Fittings, Valves, and Parts for Piping Applications
