- The Supplier shall bevel the hub ends in accordance with ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47, MSS SP-44, or API Spec 6A, Design Type 6B, Material Type 4, as applicable.

- The Supplier shall bore the flange or connector to match the ID of the pipe to which it shall be welded.

- The finish for all RF gasket contact surfaces shall comply with MSS SP-6.

- All flanges with RTJs shall have a flat bottom groove in accordance with ASME B16.20 or API Spec 6A, Design Type 6B, Material Type 4, as applicable.

- All special flange and connector studs shall be of a quality and strength not less than ASTM A320 grade L7 with ASTM A194 grade 7 nuts.

- Where flanges are specified as "jackbolt flanges" they shall be modified with two drilled and tapped jackbolt holes 180 degrees apart on the flange bolt circle and centered between adjacent bolt holes. Jackbolts shall be ASTM A193 grade BBM hex head bolts. Jackbolt diameter shall be a minimum of one-half of the standard stud diameter or ⅝-inch, whichever is greater. Jackbolt length shall be a minimum of two times the flange thickness. Jackbolt tips shall be ground smooth with a rounded end and a radius equal to the bolt diameter. The jackbolt holes shall be tapped to the lesser of the full flange thickness or twice the jackbolt diameter.

- All slip blind and spacer "T" dimensions shall meet the requirements of ASME B16.48.

## 8 Nondestructive Testing

NDT shall be based upon ASME Section V. Each item, NPS 4 and larger, shall be full body, 100% UT inspected for longitudinal and transverse defects after forming and heat treating. Welded items shall have all welds 100% radiographically inspected after heat treating according to ASME Section VIII, UW-51 (e.g., ASTM A815 Class WP-WX).

All weld bevels are to be checked for lamination by UT or PT processes. No lamination is acceptable.

All personnel performing NDT shall be certified in accordance with ASNT SNT-TC-1A. Personnel interpreting or evaluating results of NDT for acceptance or rejection shall be certified to ASNT SNT-TC-1A Level II or Level III.

- Thoroughly investigate all indications of imperfections using UT and visual methods.

- Any imperfection equal to or greater than 10% of the nominal wall thickness is defined as a defect.

- Remove all detected imperfections and defects, provided 90% of the nominal wall remains after removal.
