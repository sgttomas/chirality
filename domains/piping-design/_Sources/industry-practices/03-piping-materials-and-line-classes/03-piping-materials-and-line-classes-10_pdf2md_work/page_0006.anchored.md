# 3 Manufacturing

## 3.1 General

All fittings shall be made in such a manner as to preserve the cross-sectional shape of the fitting, and shall be free from wrinkling, buckling, cracks, or other evidence of mechanical damage. Waves with a width-to-depth ratio of 12 to 1 or greater are acceptable.

Fittings made from pipe containing a longitudinal weld, it shall be positioned as near as practicable to the least distortion axis of the fitting.

Blind flanges, blind spectacles, blind paddles, and spacer paddles may be offered constructed of low temperature CS (-50 °F), with a weld overlay of Incoloy 825 (UNS N08825) or Alloy 20 (UNS N08020), instead of solid duplex. The Manufacturer shall submit welding and fabrication procedures for approval.

For connectors, only the hub pieces and seals must be duplex. The clamps may be -50 °F rated CS, unless otherwise noted.

All items must be solution annealed and water quenched within the temperature range requirements of ASTM A815 or A182.

After final heat treating, all items shall receive a chemical descaling and neutralizing treatment before the final repassivation of the product. Air repassivation is acceptable unless otherwise noted.

## 3.2 Longitudinal Seam Welds

All seam welds shall be made using a filler metal.
