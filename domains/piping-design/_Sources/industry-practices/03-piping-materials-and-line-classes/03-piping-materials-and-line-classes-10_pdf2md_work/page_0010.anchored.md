## 7.2 Flanges and Connector

The diameter, wall thickness, and grade of the flanges and connectors shall be as shown on the PO. Dimensions shall comply with the following:

- Except as noted otherwise in this specification, all flanges shall be made in accordance with ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47 or MSS SP-44.

- All connector assemblies shall have three components: a metal seal ring, hubs, and a clamp assembly. The connector hubs shall be made of the same generics material as the piping system they connect. All connectors shall be dimensionally interchangeable with mechanical connectors. The Supplier shall furnish with the quotation a detailed dimensional drawing of all connector assemblies for review.

- All special flanges or connectors in sizes and ratings not covered by ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47, or MSS SP-44 shall be designed to extrapolations of the referenced flange standards. Since these special designs shall be of a custom or special nature, the Vendor shall submit all design calculations and drawings for Company review before commencing fabrication. All special flanges and connectors shall be supplied complete with all required gaskets, studs, and nuts.

- All flanges of ½ - 1½ inches nominal pipe size shall be socket welded type. All flanges of 2-inch nominal pipe size and larger shall be butt welded type.

- All flanges of ASME ratings 150#, 300#, or 600# shall be RF. All flanges of ASME ratings 900#, 1500#, or 2500# shall be RTJ. All flanges of API ratings 2000#, 3000#, 5000#, 10000#, or 20000# shall be RTJ.

- All flanges of 2 - 14 inches nominal pipe size or ASME 1500# and 2500# flange rating shall meet the dimensional requirements of ASME B16.5. All flanges of larger nominal pipe size or ASME 150# through 900# shall meet the dimensional requirements of MSS SP-44, or ASME B16.47 Series A, unless otherwise specified.

- For materials not shown in ASME B16.5 Table 2 or meeting the requirements of MSS SP-44 Section 3, allowable pressure-temperature ratings shall be provided, calculated in accordance with ASME B16.5 Annex D.

- The PO or BOM shall state the wall thickness and SMYS of the pipe to which each flange or connector shall be welded.

- When the SMYS of the flange or connector hub material equals that of the adjoining pipe, the hub design shall be in accordance with ASME B16.5, ASME B16.47, MSS SP-44, or API Spec 6A, Design Type 6B, Material Type 4, as applicable.
