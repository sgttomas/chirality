Welding shall be performed by only qualified welders using Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) qualified in accordance with ASME Section IX. The qualification tests shall be conducted with base plate of the same nominal thickness as will be used for production. The test plate shall meet the requirements of this specification.

The brand name and designation of the weld filler metal and flux shall be included in the WPS and PQR (Procedure Qualification Record). The same filler metal or wire/flux combination used for qualification shall be used for production welding.

Welding repairs on the long seam welds may not be performed after the final heat treatment.

## 3.3 Cold Working

Cold working after final heat treatment is not allowed except for sizing and straightening of fittings. Cold sizing shall not result in more than 1.5% reduction in cross-sectional area. Cold bending for straightening shall not exceed 2 degrees. Cold work is not permitted for the purpose of enhancing mechanical properties.

# 4 Chemical Composition

## 4.1 Composition

For 22% Cr items, the composition of the steel to be used shall be as per UNS S31803 (F51) or as per UNS S32205 (F60), with an additional requirement that the nitrogen content shall be 0.14 to 0.20 weight percent. PRE (PRE = %Cr + 3.3x%Mo + 16x%N) shall be a minimum of 33.

For 25% Cr items, the composition of the steel to be used shall be as per S32750 (F53), or S32760 (F55), or S39274 (F54), or S39277 (F57). PRE shall be a minimum of 40.

# 5 Microstructure

The ferrite content shall be determined for the base, weld metal and HAZ. The average ferrite content of each region tested shall be between 30% to 65%.

# 6 Mechanical Properties

All mechanical property tests, including CVN impact tests, shall be performed after final solution annealed, sizing and straightening. All test procedures shall be in accordance with the relevant ASTM standard.

Unless otherwise noted, the physical properties for fittings, blinds, and spacers made from pipe or plate shall comply with ASTM A815, while the properties of flanges, connectors, forged fittings and blinds and spacers made from forgings shall comply with ASTM A182.
