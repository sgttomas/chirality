# 8 Coatings and Bevel Protectors

## 8.1 Coatings and Linings (Clause 12.1)

- Pipes shall be supplied free from coatings, except that the area on which the paint stencil marking is to be applied shall first be cleaned, then stencil marked, and, finally, have a light coat of a protective varnish applied without runs or puddling.
- Other forms of coating, if required, will be specified separately by Company.

## 8.2 Bevel Protectors

Plastic end caps shall be installed on all pipes prior to shipment. End caps shall be hookable bevel protectors with vent holes, supplied by others at Company approval. Duct tape shall not be used for holding Bevel Protectors in place.
