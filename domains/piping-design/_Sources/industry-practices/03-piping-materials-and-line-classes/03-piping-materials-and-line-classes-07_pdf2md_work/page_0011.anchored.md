## 2    Purchase Order and Delivery

### 2.1    Pipe Grade, Steel Grade, and Delivery Condition (Clause 6)

- All pipes shall be supplied as Product Specification Level 2G (PSL2G).
- Jointers shall not be used.
- All pipes shall be seamless in accordance with one of the following grades as specified in the Purchase Order:
  - X52NS
  - X52QS
  - X56QS
  - X60QS
  - X65QS
  - X70QS

### 2.2    Supplied Purchase Order Information (Clauses 7, G.2, H.2)

The information provided in the Purchase Order shall be as defined in American Petroleum Institute (API) 5L, except as modified by the requirements of this Specification (SPC).
