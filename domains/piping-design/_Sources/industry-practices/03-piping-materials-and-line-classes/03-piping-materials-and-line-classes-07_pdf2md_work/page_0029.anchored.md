# APPENDIX A: Manufacturing Procedure Qualification for PSL 2 Pipe

## A.1: Manufacturing Procedure Qualification Testing

- If required by the Purchase Order, the steel maker and pipe mill shall perform a Manufacturing Procedure Specification (MPS) qualification in accordance with this annex, which is a supplement to API 5L Annex B.

- The qualification will be witnessed by an authorized representative of Company. No change shall be made to a successfully qualified MPS without the approval of the Company Responsible Engineer. After such approval, the Company Responsible Engineer may require appropriate procedure qualification to be carried out on the new MPS.

- Qualification tests may be taken from a preproduction run or the first day production, as agreed with the Company Responsible Engineer. Pipe made from a successful preproduction run may be supplied as part of the order.

- Bead-on-plate hardenability testing shall not be required, unless specifically defined in the Purchase Order.

## A.2: Characteristics of the Manufacturing Procedure Specification (MPS)

- Before production commences, the manufacturer shall submit a detailed MPS that shall be subject to approval by the Company Responsible Engineer.

- The MPS shall include all factors that influence the quality and reliability of production. In particular, the MPS shall include:

  - Steel maker and plant at which the steel is produced.

  - Steelmaking process and nominal weight of each heat.

  - Proposed chemical target composition and range, including detail of any deliberately added elements and maximum and typical levels for residual elements.

  - Any techniques proposed for desulfurizing or degassing.

  - Any techniques proposed for sulfide shape control and methods used to minimize segregation and limits on the inclusion control parameter.

  - Casting technique and size and weight of ingots, slabs, or skelps.

  - Pipe manufacturer and location.
