[TABLE: Definitions table — Term and Definition]

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| Company | Oil Search Alaska, LLC or its designated representative |
| Company Representative | An inspector, Supplier, manufacturer, or inspection agency contracted by the Company to represent the Company. |
| Approve | As applied to Company, approve is used if the Company requires notification and a written agreement before an action is implemented by the Supplier. This does not imply that all details of a document have been considered by Company and does not mitigate the responsibilities of the Supplier, which are covered separately by Company, as appropriate, in the conditions of the contract. |
| Calibration | A term that is interchangeable with instrument standardization as defined in API 5L 4.23. |
| Company Inspector | The Company representative, based at the manufacturing facility during plate and pipe production, with responsibility for ensuring that the Supplier is working in accordance with the approved production and quality management procedures. |
| Company Responsible Engineer | A Company specialist engineer appointed to produce the line pipe specification appropriate for the pipeline design and application and to resolve queries concerning application and any issues that arise during pipe manufacture. |
| Heat | All steel from the same pouring and having the same chemical composition. |
| Lot | All pipes produced from the same heat and receiving the same heat treatment, but not to exceed the greater of 100 pieces or 50 tons. |
| Manufacturer | A pipe mill accepting a Purchase Order or contract from Company to supply line pipe (in which case, manufacturer and Supplier are the same entity) or from a trading house or steel merchant to which Company has awarded a contract. The duties of a manufacturer may include responsibility for technical and quality matters relating to steelmaking and supply of plate from which the pipe is manufactured. |
| Manufacturing Procedure Specification (MPS) | A document prepared by the manufacturer defining each stage of the manufacturing, inspection, and testing processes and the control parameters or acceptance criteria that apply to each activity. |
