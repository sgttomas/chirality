## 5 Hydrostatic Test (Clause 10.2.6)

- Test pressure for all pipe diameters shall be maintained for at least 10 seconds.
- Hydrostatic testing shall be carried out on the pipe in its final condition and before final inspection.
- The test pressure for all sizes and grades shall be such that the hoop stress is at least 90 percent of the specified minimum yield strength based on the nominal wall thickness. If mill equipment considerations limit pressures, pipes shall be tested to the greater of API 5L or the equipment capacity.
- If compressive end loadings are applied during hydrotest, end load compensation shall be calculated in accordance with API 5L Clause 10.2.6.6.
