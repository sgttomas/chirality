[TABLE: Definitions — Term and Definition (continued)]

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| Pipe Mill | The Supplier's manufacturing, testing, finishing, and supplying line pipe to Company and the facilities at which these activities are conducted. |
| Quality Assurance (QA) | The planned and systematic actions that is necessary to ensure quality and provide confidence that a product or service is fit for its intended purpose. |
| Quality Management System (QMS) | The organizations structure, responsibilities, activities, resources, and events that together provide organized procedures and methods of implementation to ensure the capability of an organization to comply with quality requirements. |
| Quality Manual | A document set out general quality policies, procedures, and practices of an organization. |
| Quality Plan | A document prepared by the manufacturer or Supplier that sets out the specific quality practices, resources, and activities relevant to a particular contract or order. |
| Seamless Pipe | Pipes without a welded seam that was produced by a hot forming process without the application of any cold finishing or cold sizing unless approved otherwise by the Company. |
| Steel Maker | The Vendor manufacturing, refining and casting steel to slab for subsequent plate manufacture and the premises where these activities are carried out. |
| Supplier | A trading house or steel merchant acting on behalf of the manufacturer or pipe mill, to which an enquiry has been issued or to which a Purchase Order or contract to supply line pipe has been awarded. |
