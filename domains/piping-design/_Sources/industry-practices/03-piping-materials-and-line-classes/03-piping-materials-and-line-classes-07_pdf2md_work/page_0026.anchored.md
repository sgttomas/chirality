# 7 Marking (Clause 11)

## 7.1 General (Clause 11.1.4)

- Units used for pipe markings shall be United States customary (USC).
- The following pipe markings shall be applied in addition to the mandatory marking requirements of API 5L:
  - Identification number (e.g. heat number) in accordance with API 5L Clause 11.2.1.i)
  - Hydrotest pressure in accordance with API 5L Clause 11.2.1.j)
  - Client name
  - Purchase order number
  - Company unique pipe number
  - Length
  - Weight

## 7.2 Pipe Markings (Clause 11.2)

### 7.2.1 Company Unique Pipe Number (Clause 11.2.1)

- Unique pipe numbers allocated by Company, traceable to the internal mill control pipe numbers, and defined in the MPS, shall be stenciled internally at both ends of each pipe.
- If the pipe is ordered with internal coating, stencil marking may be applied after coating.

### 7.2.2 Stenciling (Clause 11.2.2)

- Seamless pipe stenciling shall be at least 1 inch high and shall be applied to:
  - The outside surface for pipes with nominal diameter less than 8.625 inches.
  - The inside surface for pipes with nominal diameter of 8.625 inches or greater.

### 7.2.3 Paint Marking (Clause 11.2.7)

Company will advise any color code requirements at the enquiry stage or before submittal of the MPS.
