- At least one pipe of each size shall be manufactured in accordance with the approved MPS and subjected to inspection and testing required by the base specification for a pipe representing one heat of steel, plus additional testing, as follows:

  - Sets of Charpy test pieces and macro-sections for hardness tests shall be taken at approximately 6 feet from each end of the pipe. Test results shall comply with section 3.4.4 of this specification.

  - Additional pipe body Charpy tests shall be carried out at -50 °F (Shear area and lateral expansion shall be recorded in all cases. This data shall be presented in the form of transition curves for both energy and shear area.
