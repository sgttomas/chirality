## 4 Acceptance Criteria

### 4.1 Chemical Properties and Composition (Clause H.4.1)

a) The chemical composition in Table 1 shall apply for thicknesses up to 1.6 inches.

b) The steel maker shall nominate a preferred steel composition (heat and product analyses) within the Table 1 limits, together with the tolerances applied to each element, subject to approval by the Company Responsible Engineer.

c) No other element shall be added to the steel without the approval of the Company Responsible Engineer.

d) If the MPS defines the steelmaking process as electric arc furnace or if large volumes of non-line pipe scrap are used, additional analyses for arsenic (As), antimony (Sb), tin (Sn), lead (Pb), and bismuth (Bi) shall be carried out. The maximum content for these residual elements are 0.03% As, 0.01% Sb, 0.02% Sn, 0.01% Pb, and 0.01% Bi.

e) For thicknesses in excess of those specified in Section 4.1a, revisions to the limits defined in Table 1 shall be subject to agreement with the Company Responsible Engineer and defined in the approved MPS.
