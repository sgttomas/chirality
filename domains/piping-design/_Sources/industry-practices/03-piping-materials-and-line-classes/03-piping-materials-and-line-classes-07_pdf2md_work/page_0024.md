## 6    Documentation (Clause 10.1)

- The product analysis report shall list all pipes made from each heat of steel.
- Specific documentation requirements as listed in Table 4 shall be included.
- The pipe tally sheet shall be in Excel format, contain the following information, and arranged as follows:
  - PDS Item Number
  - Company Pipe Number
  - Mill Unique Pipe Number
  - Mill Work & Certification Number
  - Mill
  - Pipe O.D. (inches)
  - Wall Thickness (inch)
  - Heat Number
  - Plate Number
  - Weight (lbs)
  - Length
  - Shipment Number
