#### 4.5.3.3 Hard Spots (Clause 9.10.6)

Inspectors shall examine all pipe surfaces visually to detect irregularities in the curvature of the pipe that may be evidence of hard spots. Any suspect area shall be examined further with portable hardness testing equipment. Any spot of any size with hardness that exceeds the limit in Section 4.4.3 of this SPC shall be removed from the pipe as a cylinder.

#### 4.5.3.4 Other Surface Imperfections (Clause 9.10.7)

- The external surface of the full length of each pipe shall be inspected by electromagnetic inspection (EMI) automatic equipment in accordance with API 5L Annex K.3.4.3. The EMI equipment shall scan the pipe to ensure detection of longitudinal and transverse defects with 100 percent of the pipe surface being inspected.

- The extent of other surface imperfections identified by ultrasonic or visual inspections shall be determined using wet magnetic particle inspection.

- The acceptance criteria for other surface imperfections shall be in accordance with API 5L Annex H.5 as modified by this specification.

- Sharp-edged imperfections, regardless of depth, shall be considered to be defects.

- The pipe external surface shall be free from loose scale, scabs, scores, and laps as well as tears, raised slivers, sharp notches, scratches, or mechanical damage.

- Defects in the body of the pipe, such as laps, scabs, and slivers, shall be removed by grinding in accordance with API 5L Annex C. Wet magnetic particle inspection shall be used to confirm the removal of these defects.

- The wall thickness of ground areas shall be individually measured using an ultrasonic thickness gauge.

- If more than 10 percent of any pipe outside or inside surface has been ground, the pipe shall be subjected to a 100-percent wall thickness check using the approved automatic ultrasonic equipment.

- The end bevel shall be smooth and free from tool marks.
