# 9 Pipe Loading and Transportation

- Pipe handling, storage, and loading procedures shall be prepared by the appropriate parties and shall be subject to review and approval by the Company Inspector and Company Responsible Engineer.

- These procedures shall reference all equipment and define the methods to be adopted for handling pipes within the manufacturing facility, associated storage facilities, and transportation by truck, rail, barge, and vessel as appropriate. The procedures shall define the measures being taken to ensure safety of personnel and avoidance of pipe damage during pipe handling.

- Hooks used for handling pipe shall be soft faced, with rubber or plastic. Metal-to-metal contact shall be avoided.

- Pipes shall be lifted with protected wire rope, nonmetallic straps or, if practical, vacuum lifting methods. Unprotected chains shall not be used. Slings shall not be left in place during transportation.

- Railroad transportation of pipe shall comply with API RP 5L1.

- Marine transportation of pipe shall comply with API RP 5LW, unless directed otherwise by the Company Responsible Engineer.

- At the option of Company, loading of rail cars, trucks, ships, and/or barges may be witnessed by the Company-nominated inspectors.

- All pipes with closed plastic end protectors shall be loaded with dunnage between rows or bundles to permit unloading with slings. All dunnage shall be secured to prevent contact between the pipe and cargo lashing. For truck and rail transport, protected chains or nylon straps are allowed.
