# 1 General

## 1.1 Scope

This Specification covers the manufacturing, testing, and inspection of seamless and welded 22Cr and 25Cr duplex stainless steel line pipe. This specification supplements the following:

- ASTM A999 – "General Requirements for Alloy and Stainless Steel Pipe"
- ASTM A928 – "Ferritic/Austenitic (Duplex) Stainless Steel Pipe Electric Fusion Welded Addition of Filler Material"
- ASTM A790 – "Seamless and Welded Ferritic/Austenitic Stainless Steel Pipe

The guidance for 22Cr and 25Cr duplex stainless steel line seamless line pipe is based on API Spec 5LC – "Specification for Corrosion-Resistant Alloy (CRA) Line Pipe" and to standards as indicated in Section 2.0 Regulations, Codes and Standards. In the event of an inconsistency, conflict or discrepancy between any of the standards, specification or regulations, the contractor shall follow the most stringent and safest requirement applicable to the project. Any inconsistency critical to the design shall be brought to Company attention for approval.

## 2 Manufacturing

Pipe shall be either seamless or welded. All seamless pipes shall be in accordance with ASTM A790. All welded pipe shall require the addition of a filler metal, and shall be in accordance with ASTM A928, Class 1 or Class 3. Pipe shall be supplied in the solution annealed and water quenched, descaled and pickled condition.

The acceptable pipe manufacturing process for the production of 22% and 25% chromium (Cr) duplex stainless steel seamless line pipe shall be either:

- Blast furnace refining followed by de-carbonization using both the Argon Oxygen (AOD) and Vacuum Oxygen (VOD) processes. If necessary, final adjustment or molten metal analyses and condition shall be carried out using the Ladle Treatment (LT) process. or
- Electric furnace melting and refining followed by AOD (if considered necessary), VOD, and LT.

### 2.1 Longitudinal Seam Welds

All seam welds shall be made using a filler metal.

Welding shall be performed by only qualified welders using Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) qualified in accordance with ASME Section IX. The qualification tests shall be conducted with base plate of the same nominal thickness as will be used for production. The test plate shall meet the requirements of this specification.

The brand name and designation of the weld filler metal and flux shall be included in the WPS and PQR (Procedure Qualification Record). The same filler metal or wire/flux combination used for qualification shall be used for production welding.

### 2.2 Circumferential Welds

Circumferential welding is permitted to provide the lengths requested in the purchase order. PWHT (Post Weld Heat Treatment) of circumferential welds can only be done with prior approval of the Company representative.

### 2.3 Heat Treatment

All pipes must be solution annealed and water quenched within the temperature range requirements of ASTM A790 or A928.

Lubricants shall be removed from the surfaces prior to heat treatment. The Supplier shall submit heat treatment procedures to the Company for approval.

All pipes shall be descaled and pickled after long seam welding and final heat treatment. Welding repairs on the long seam welds cannot be performed after the final heat treatment.

## 2.4 Cold Working

Cold working after final heat treatment is not allowed except for sizing and straightening of pipe. Cold sizing shall not result in more than 1½% reduction in cross-sectional area. Cold bending for straightening shall not exceed 2 degrees. Cold work is not permitted for the purpose of enhancing mechanical properties.

For 25% Cr pipe, the composition of the steel to be used in the manufacture of the pipe shall be as per UNS S32750, or S32760, or S39274, or S39277. PRE~W~ shall be a minimum of 40.

A preferred steel composition (heat and product analyses) for the specific material nominated on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) shall be nominated for approval by the Company Representative. The proposed analysis and the tolerances applied to each element shall also be subject to approval by the Company Representative. Alternative grades may be proposed, subject to approval by the Company.

## 3.1 Acceptance

One product analysis shall be performed in accordance with Section 9 of ASTM A790 or A928, and the results reported to the Company.

If the results of either of the product analyses fail, every pipe from the lot shall be tested.

## 3.2 Heat Analysis

Each required analysis of steel shall be made by the steel manufacturer to determine the percentages of the specified elements.

The chemical composition thus determined, or that determined from a product analysis made by the tubular product manufacturer shall conform to the requirement specified. For this requirement, a grade is defined as an alloy describe individually and identified by its own unified numbering system (UNS) designation in a table of chemical requirements within any specification listed within the scope.

## 3.3 Product Analysis

Product analysis requirements and options, if any, shall be contained in the applicable product specification.

Product analysis carried out on sample representative of the full material thickness, shall be performed at a frequency of two pipes per heat. One of these analyses shall be performed on the first pipe rolled from each individual heat of steel.

Product analysis variation shall be as agreed upon in the manufacturing procedure specification.

# 4 Microstructure

## 4.1 Sampling Location and Frequency

One specimen representing each lot of pipe shall be removed from a length of pipe after the final solution annealed heat treatment for microstructural examination. For welded pipe, the specimen shall include the weld metal and Heat Affected Zone (HAZ).

## 4.2 Examination and Assessment of Ferrite Content

The metallographic specimen shall be polished and etched and examined at a minimum magnification of 400 times (x400), shall be carried out on both longitudinal and transverse specimens taken from a minimum of one pipe per heat after final heat treatment. Photomicrographs of all metallography shall be part of the permanent mill report to the Company. The material shall be essentially free from grain boundary carbides, nitrides and intermetallic phases such as sigma.

The ferrite content shall be determined by the manual point counting method in accordance with ASTM E562 or by a computerized quantitative image analysis technique. Use of any other technique requires prior approval of the Company.

The ferrite content shall be measured in the base material for all pipe and in the HAZ and weld metal of welded pipe. The average ferrite content of each region tested shall be 35% to 55%.

## 4.3 Sulfide Stress Cracking Test

Sulfide stress corrosion (SSC) cracking test shall be in accordance with Appendix A. This should be carried out on at least one of the pipes selected from each mechanical testing in accordance with Section 5. The test solutions and other test variables shall be defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless Steel Seamless).

## 4.4 Stress Corrosion Cracking and Pitting Corrosion Test

High-temperature Stress Corrosion Cracking (SCC) and pitting corrosion tests are required to "pre-qualify" the material for the intended application and shall be carried out in accordance with Appendices B and C, respectively, on one pipe selected from each of the first two heats as part of the manufacturing procedure qualification test. The test solution to be used shall be as defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless).

## 5.6 Hydrostatic Test

Each pipe length shall be hydrostatically tested in accordance with ASTM A999 Section 22.2.

The minimum hydrostatic test pressure shall be as stated on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) (it is the intention to test at a pressure corresponding to a fiber stress of 95% of the minimum specified yield strength based on the minimum allowable wall thickness).

Hydrostatic testing shall be carried out on the pipe in its final condition and before final inspection.

# 6 Dimensions, Weights and Lengths

## 6.1 Diameter

All outside diameters, wall thicknesses, weights, and tolerances shall conform to ASTM A999. Pipe ends shall be prepared with a single bevel, 30 degrees (+5, -0 degrees), unless otherwise stated.

Pipe diameter shall be measured using circumferential (pi) tape, and out-of-roundness shall be determined by direct measurement. The maximum tolerance for diameter shall be 0.012 inch for all pipes. In Addition, along the whole length of the pipe, the maximum outside diameter (OD) measured at any point shall not exceed the minimum OD, measured by the same technique at the same point, by more than 1% or 0.16 inch whichever is lesser.

The controlling dimensions shall be revised to inside diameter (ID) and tolerance on the ID within 4 inches of the pipe ends shall be ±0.08 inch. The internal out-of-roundness within 4 inches of any pipe end shall not exceed 0.12 inch or 0.75% of the ID whichever is lesser.

The finished pipe shall be reasonably straight. The deviation from straight line shall not exceed 0.10%.

## 6.2 Wall Thickness

The wall thickness tolerance shall be:

- ±12.5% for nominal wall thickness less than 0.4 inch.
- ±10% from nominal wall thickness greater than or equal to 0.4 inch.

Note:

For specific applications, enhanced tolerances on pipe I.D. may require the pipe to be bore machined. Any such requirements will be defined on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless).

## 6.3 Length

Pipe lengths shall lie within the range stated in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless). Normally, pipe lengths from 39.4 to 41 feet are acceptable. Unless

otherwise defined in the purchase order, the minimum average length in the entire shipment supplied shall not be less than 40 feet.

# 7 Nondestructive Testing

## 7.1 General

The full length of each pipe shall be inspected for longitudinal and transverse defects by multi-probe automatic ultrasonic examination equipment.

Nondestructive inspection for acceptance shall be carried out after completion of any cold expansion, heat treatment, and hydrostatic test using equipment, techniques, and procedures approved by the Company Representative.

If flaws are indicated by any of the inspection techniques, Company shall require additional testing to demonstrate the acceptability of the pipes.

Manual ultrasonic inspection shall be used to inspect any portion of the pipe length not covered by all automatic probes.

After end preparation, ultrasonic inspection using compression wave techniques shall be carried out on both ends of each pipe in a full circumferential band at least 2 inches wide to ensure freedom from laminations.

Dye penetrant inspection (DPI) shall be carried out on the finished pipe ends.

## 7.2 Personnel Certification

Personnel carrying out nondestructive inspection shall be experienced in using the equipment specified and shall be qualified to a minimum of American Society for Nondestructive Testing (ASNT) TC-1A Level 2.

The person responsible for the preparation of inspection procedures and supervision of inspection activities shall be qualified to ASNT-TC-1A Level 3.

## 7.3 Ultrasonic and Electromagnetic Inspection of Seamless Pipe

The automatic ultrasonic probe array shall scan the pipe along a helical path with a separation between adjacent probes, which ensures the:

- Detection of longitudinal and transverse defects with 100% of the pipe surface being inspected
- Measurement of wall thickness and detection of pipe body laminations over 100% of the pipe volume.

[Oil Search logo]

If ultrasonic indications above the alarm level are detected, an audible alarm shall be initiated and the suspect area shall be paint sprayed. The equipment shall also be fitted with an audible alarm to indicate loss of couplant between the probes and the pipe.

The results of each automatic ultrasonic examination shall be permanently recorded using a method that identifies the position of indications at or above the alarm level and with each record traceable to individual pipe numbers.

If any pipe end region is not scanned by the automatic technique, the appropriate area shall be inspected by compression and shear wave manual ultrasonic for longitudinal and/or transverse defects.

The reference pipe shall be of sufficient length to enable the standard reflectors to be detected during dynamic calibration of the automatic inspection equipment at its maximum inspection speed.

The ultrasonic reference standard for calibration of the automatic shear wave technique shall contain four N5 notches of the nominal wall thickness. One longitudinal and one transverse notch shall be machined on both the internal and external surfaces. Notches shall have a maximum length of 2 inches and have a maximum depth of 0.06 inch regardless of pipe thickness.

The reference standard shall also include suitably positioned reflectors that confirm the effective scan length (dead zone) of the automatic equipment and define any additional length of the pipe that is to be examined by manual ultrasonic as required by 11.2.

For pipe wall thicknesses in excess of 0.80 inch, an additional reflector located at mid-thickness may be required to assist in the detection of buried longitudinal imperfections. The type and position of the additional reflector shall be agreed upon with the Company.

In order to minimize inaccuracies in automatic ultrasonic inspection during production, the calibration pipe shall be passed through the system a minimum of ten times to demonstrate the repeatability of reflector response signals before any production run.

During pipe production, the reference pipe shall be run at the start of each shift and again at approximately mid-shift. No adjustments shall be made before any proving run.

If, during dynamic calibration, the standard defects do not produce signals of the required magnitude, the equipment shall be recalibrated and pipes accepted, since the previous successful calibration shall be reexamined.

The alarm level for automatic ultrasonic examination shall be set at a level below the lowest amplitude signal produced by any of the standard reflectors during the repeatability check procedure detailed in b. above. The alarm set point shall be agreed upon with the Company Representative.

Any imperfection causing the automatic inspection equipment to alarm shall be investigated by manual ultrasonic and/or DPI as appropriate to the defect type indicated.

Manual ultrasonic reassessment of imperfections shall be carried out with a reference level determined by the signal amplitude obtained from an external N5 notch, without added gain.

Acceptance limits for laminations and inclusion clusters shall be as given in ISO 10124; the pipe body shall comply with Acceptance Level B2, and the pipe ends shall comply with acceptance Level B1.

## 7.4 Dye Particle Inspection of Seamless Pipe

Any appropriate indications from ultrasonic examination, together with the end bevel and the pipe surfaces within 1 inch of the end of each pipe end, shall be inspected using DPI.

- Equipment: The equipment shall be capable of detecting the presence of any defects, such as laminations, in the end bevel.
- Reference Standard: Pipe containing appropriate defects shall be made available, or a suitable field strength indicator shall be proposed so that checks on the efficiency of the DPI procedure and consumables may be made at any time during processing of the order.
- Acceptance Limits: Any inclusion or inclusion cluster greater than 0.12 inch in diameter, or any lamination detected on the end bevel, shall be cause for re-beveling.

Imperfections shall not be repaired by welding.

# 8 Workmanship, Visual Inspection and Repair of Defects

## 8.1 Workmanship

No weld repairs to the body of the pipe shall be permitted after solution heat treatment. Pipe shall NOT contain any dents.

After blasting and pickling, if grinding is necessary to remove surface blemishes or remaining scale, the surface roughness of any ground areas shall be equal to or less than the adjacent pipe metal base.

Cracks, sweats, and leaks shall be clearly marked and the pipe length shall be set aside for inspection by the Company representative. Pipe bevels containing laminations or other discontinuities shall be cut back until imperfections detectable by dye penetrant or ultrasonic inspection have been removed. Sharp-edged imperfections, regardless of depth, shall be considered as defect.

All weld seam repairs shall conform to the requirements of ASTM A928, paragraph 6.2.4. A weld seam repair procedure shall be approved by the Company prior to repair of weld seam defects. The following shall include:

- Type of defect to be repaired and location drawing.
- Defect elimination procedure.

- Welding procedure specification and welder qualification

- Inspection test procedure

All repairs shall be completed prior to solution heat treatment.

## 8.2 Visual Inspection

Every pipe shall be visually examined externally and internally when diameter permits, for surface flaws in the body of the pipe. Defect acceptance levels for visual inspection shall be in accordance with this specification.

Illumination levels at all areas where visual inspection is to be carried out shall be a minimum of 33-foot candle (350 lx).

In addition, all mill run pipe shall be subject to inspection by the Company at all stages of the manufacturing and delivery process.

## 8.3 Repair of Defects

Minor cosmetic areas and flaws in the pipe not encroaching upon the required minimum thickness may be removed by grinding, provided a smooth curved surface is maintained and the final thickness after grinding is not less than the required minimum thickness. The remaining wall thickness shall be measured by UT methods. Defects in the pipe encroaching upon the specified minimum thickness shall be cut out.

Dye penetrant inspection (DPI) shall be used to confirm the removal of defects such as laps, scabs and slivers.

The repair of dents shall not be permitted after solution annealing.

The supplier shall maintain continuous supervision of inspection procedures by qualified supervisors directly responsible to management.

In addition to the above, all mill run pipe shall be subject to inspection by the Company at all stages of the manufacturing and delivery process.

All required test inspection shall be made at the place of manufacturer prior to shipment, unless otherwise specified, and shall be conducted so as not to interfere unnecessarily with the manufacturer's operation.

# 9 Traceability

Throughout the manufacturing process, a means of identification shall be used to maintain a strict accountability of all pipes.

Should heat, heat treat lot, or part number traceability be lost during processing, the affected pieces shall be rejected.

Disposition of rejected pipe shall be a matter of agreement between manufacturer and the purchaser.

Pipe that fails in any of the forming operations or in the process of installation and is found to be defective shall be set aside and the manufacturer shall be notified for mutual evaluation of the suitability of the pipe. Disposition of such pipe shall be matter for agreement.

# APPENDIX A: Sulfide Stress Cracking Test Procedure

## A.1: Introduction

For 22% and 25% Cr super-duplex stainless-steel materials in which the service is defined as "sour," four-point bend (4PB) SSC tests shall be carried out as part of the production control tests.

Precise details of testing required shall be included in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless). The information to be supplied shall consist of:

- Test solution(s) composition (fully formulated produced water and/or, when the service on the Data Sheet for Line Pipe [22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless] is defined as gas, simulated condensing water).
- Test solution(s) pH (for information purposes only).
- Partial pressure of hydrogen sulfide and carbon dioxide at the test temperature.

The proposed, detailed test procedures shall be supplied to Company for review/approval before the commencement of testing.

## A.2: A.2 Sulfide Stress Cracking Test Method

Four-point bend testing shall be carried out in accordance with the procedures specified in EFC 17 and ASTM G39, together with the additional requirements:

- 4PB tests shall be undertaken such that the inner pipe surface is in tension.
- The inner pipe surface finish shall be "as manufactured," with the exception that the test specimens shall be thoroughly cleaned/degreased before testing.

## A.3: Reagents

The reagents shall be in accordance with those specified in NACE TM0177.

## A.4: Gases

High-purity (99.99%) H₂S/CO₂ gas mixtures shall be used. If possible (this is a requirement when not using a "shut-in" autoclave/vessel), the test solution shall be purged with the gas mixture throughout the test period.

## A.5: Test Solution Preparation

A test solution corresponding to the produced water composition defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) shall be prepared in accordance with a procedure agreed upon with the Company.

If the line pipe is required for gas service, a test solution corresponding to the condensing water composition defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) (typically 1.60 grams per liter (g/l) NaCl, i.e., 1,000 parts per million (ppm) by weight of chloride) shall be prepared in accordance with a procedure agreed upon with the Company.

The procedure for solution preparation/conditioning shall be as follows:

- Prepare a base solution of the required levels of salts in distilled or deionized water.

- Add the organic acid salts and bicarbonate. (Note: in some instances, saturation with CO₂ is required before adding the bicarbonate to prevent precipitation. If this is the case, add AFTER the following step).

- Introduce the solution into a vessel, fully deaerate using "oxygen free" nitrogen, and saturate with a mixture of CO₂/H₂S (adjust composition of mixture to achieve the required partial pressure of H₂S and CO₂ at the test conditions of pressure and temperature).

- Transfer the preconditioned test solution to the vessel containing the 4PB test-piece(s). (Note: ensure the test vessel and associated pipework is totally oxygen free by flushing with a suitable gas for a suitable period before transfer of the test solution. Also, ensure that there is no contamination with oxygen during solution transfer).

- Continue to condition the test solution with the test gas after transfer.

- Shut the autoclave in and allow the autoclave to come to the necessary pressure to achieve the required partial pressures of H₂S and CO₂ at the test temperature. Heat test solution to the test temperature and check that the necessary test pressure has been achieved on reaching the test temperature.

During the test, the pH may alter, but shall not exceed 0.2 pH units above the required pH. This is achieved through complete exclusion of oxygen and the maintenance of a sufficient solution volume to test the piece surface area ratio. If the pH cannot be measured under the test conditions, measure the pH at the start and end of the test under ambient pressure/temperature conditions to demonstrate that the pH shift during the test has been less than 0.2 pH units.

During the immersion, specimens shall be kept away from direct contact with the container wall and the other specimens.

## A.6: Test Vessels and Solution Volume

This shall be a minimum of 30 milliliters per square centimeter (ml/cm²) of specimen.

## A.7: Test Temperature

The temperature shall be maintained at 194 °F or the maximum design temperature defined in the Data Sheet for Line Pipe (22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless) if this is less than 194 °F. The acceptable tolerance shall be ±9 °F.

## A.8: Test Pieces

Four-point bend test pieces shall be machined from the pipe wall in the circumferential direction. If circumferential specimens cannot be obtained, longitudinal specimens may be used. Test-piece dimensions shall be agreed upon with the Company before commencing testing.

## A.9: Number of Tests

One set of SSC tests per condition (i.e., produced water and/or, if required by the Data Sheet for Line Pipe [22% and 25% Chromium Duplex Stainless-steel Seamless], condensing water) per heat; a "set" shall comprise duplicate tests.

## A.10: Applied Stress

The applied stress shall be 100% of the measured (actual) yield strength at the test temperature. The yield strength shall be determined on a round bar test piece (Figure 4 of ASTM A370-94) using the 0.2 percent offset method in ASTM A370. The required displacement shall be verified using suitably strain-gauged samples, in accordance with EFC 17. The use of published formulae (e.g., in ASTM G39) is not acceptable.

As an alternative, the use of a methodology based upon an applied strain may be offered for approval by the Company. Again, the displacement required shall be verified.

## A.11: Test Duration

The test duration shall be a minimum of 720 hours.

## A.12: Failure Appraisal

Total fracture of a test piece during the 720-hour test period shall be considered a failure. To be considered a pass, the test piece shall be free of any signs of fissures and/or cracks after the exposure period.

This shall be assessed through:

- Visual examination of the full length of the outside surfaces of the specimen (at a magnification of x 10).

- Confirmation of any visual indications using DPI.

- Metallographic examination of any indications confirmed by DPI (at a minimum magnification of x 100).

- If there are no indications, metallographic examination (at a minimum magnification of x 100) of suitably polished longitudinal sections at 1/3 and 2/3 through the width of the four-point bend sample.

## A.13: Data Reporting

Data reporting shall include test piece geometry, test solution and conditions (temperature, gas mixture), pH (initial and final), test duration, test result (failure/pass), and test piece surface appearance after test termination, with photographs where appropriate.
