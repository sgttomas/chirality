## 2 Applicable Codes and Standards

The applicable codes, standards, specifications and regulatory requirements for the development of facility layouts are listed below. Wherever conflict exists between the requirements of the referenced standards and codes and regulation, the contractor shall follow the most stringent and safest requirement applicable to this project.

Any inconsistency critical to the design shall be brought to Company attention for approval. Wherever applicable, the Contractor shall be responsible for getting the design certification and approvals from recognized certifying bodies. The Contractor shall comply with the latest version of all relevant regulatory requirements.
