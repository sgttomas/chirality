# 1 Introduction

## 1.1 Objectives

The purpose of this philosophy is to define the minimum technical requirements for the piping design and layout of process and utility piping systems including associated equipment and structures within modular installation. It also includes making provision for areas such as construction laydown, temporary construction man camps, and access roads. In this context equipment includes power generation, switchgear and fire suppression equipment. It also covers overall arrangement of supporting structure and consideration of construction/installation methods plus hook-up and pre-start-up of the facility.

Safety in design is a key factor associated with the layout of the facility. The principal design criterion is to minimize or eliminate hazards in an environment where risk mitigation is critical to overall safety of the operating facilities, site personnel and the environment. The plant layout is to establish acceptable methods for conducting quantitative risk assessment associated with explosion, fire, or toxic releases that may affect personnel within occupied structures, due to their proximity to process facility. These criteria enable development of an initial design that will meet facility siting risk assessments to the requirements of API - RP 752/753 that is recognized as a valid approach for controlling the risk to building occupants. The piping general layout philosophy is to provide a framework and basis for the design of the facility.

This guideline covers the general plant layout principles that contribute towards a safe and operable plant. This document represents the starting point on the plant layout development. Project specific amendments will be issued to supplement this document where necessary.

## 1.2 Design Criteria

The facilities shall be designed to standards as indicated in Section 2.0, Regulations, Codes and Standards. Piping engineering and design shall be in accordance with In-Plant Piping Design Criteria (OGE-PICRT-000001). Deviations from these criteria shall be identified and communicated to the Company. Where the requirement is not specified in these criteria, the Contractor shall consult the Company.

Users of this document are strongly advised to review and investigate the possibility of applying the latest edition of all relevant regulatory requirements, and the supplied Company Specification and Standards. When there is a conflict between design documents, the Company shall be notified at the earliest opportunity for resolution.
