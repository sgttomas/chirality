## 4.16 Provision for Future Expansion

Some consideration is often given to the possibility of future expansion of the plant within the site. While these provisions can add costs, some minor, inexpensive adjustments can often be made to aid later expansion.

Space for probable future equipment shall be designated and located adjacent to the related equipment. The allocation of such space shall be addressed during the initial project risk assessment in order to identify potential hazards associated with probable future equipment.

## 4.17 Pipelines

The interconnection pipelines shall comply with the requirements of codes and standards as well as the required regulatory compliance. Pipelines shall be designed and routed and isolated at the battery limit or any location driven by safety for containment of isolation scenarios.

## 4.18 Battery Limit Isolation

Battery Limit stations are often provided at process unit boundaries to facilitate the quick isolation of interconnecting pipeline in an emergency. If all boundary isolations can be initiated from the one location, this limits the escalation risk of an event.

Battery limits are also utilized to designate separation of ownership.
