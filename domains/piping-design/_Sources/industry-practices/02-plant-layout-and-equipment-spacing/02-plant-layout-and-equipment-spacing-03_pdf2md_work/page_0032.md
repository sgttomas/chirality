### 9.2.6 Security

Sites shall be within a restricted access perimeter. Site access will be monitored with CCTV. Access will be granted via the control room.

Location of units on a site relative to buildings, the boundary, and surrounding areas may be governed by local regulations and require agreement with the local authorities. Buildings used by site personnel during normal daytime working hours should normally be located within the pad entrance to minimize the number of people leaving and entering the site.

## 9.3 Buildings

All occupied buildings need to be located away from process areas, upwind of flare, vent and fired equipment, and outside of blast overpressure contours to protect personnel in the event of vapor release, fire or explosion.

Since ventilation in a building is generally less than that outdoors, a leak of flammable or toxic material tends to disperse more slowly and a hazardous concentration is more likely to build up. Also, if an explosion occurs, the blast pressure generated tends to be much higher and result in more damage. Ventilation and/or dust collection systems are required in enclosed storage buildings as well to assist with removal of mobile equipment exhaust and any off-gassing from the product and minimize dust.

Building design and orientation should also address environmental considerations such as prevailing weather direction (wind, dust, rain, ice and snow) and provide adequate protection from falling ice at building entrances.

### 9.3.1 Location

- A central control building (with or without satellite control buildings) for process and safety control may be used for all units in a process complex. Alternatively, dedicated control buildings may be required for individual process units or for parts of the site remote from the main processing area, e.g. a main tank farm or product loading facilities.
- Occupied buildings shall be provided with access ways and ideally should be located in an area classified as electrically safe.
  a) Occupied buildings shall not be located in any area classified as Class I Division 1.
