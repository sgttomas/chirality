#### Offsite

The units and equipment that are not specifically part of the processing units, but which provide a service to the processing units. Offsite includes utilities (except within a process unit) and tankage.

#### Pipe Track (Sleeper)

A grouped of unburied piping at or slightly below ground level.

#### Plant

A general term to describe any one or all the units on a given site.

#### Plot

An area of the site where a unit e.g., Nanushuk Processing Facility (NPF), drill site unit, or storage terminal is located.

#### Section

Part of a unit, (e.g., the inlet separator part of the separation unit).

#### Service Areas

Workshops, stores, fire station, laboratory, or similar buildings.

#### Site

The land owned or leased by the Company or for which the Company is responsible (i.e., the land within the perimeter fence).

#### Storage Terminal

A storage area with no processing facilities, but with import and export facilities. If the storage capacity is less than 5000 tonnes (5512 tons), this is classified as a depot.

#### Tank Compound (Farm)

An area containing one or more tanks.

#### Unit

A main production component or plant in a central processing facility (e.g., process unit or compressor unit).

#### Utilities

Steam and power generation and transmission; condensate, process water, and cooling water treatment and supply; instrument and service air supply; effluent treatment and similar services.
