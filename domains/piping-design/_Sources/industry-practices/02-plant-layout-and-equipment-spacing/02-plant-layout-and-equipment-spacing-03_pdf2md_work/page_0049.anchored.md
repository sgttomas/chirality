- IP 15 classifies liquids as follows based on the closed-cup flash point:

a) Class I – Liquids that have flash points below 70°F.

b) Class II (1) – Liquids that have flash points from 70°F up to and including 131°F, handled below flash point.

c) Class II (2) – Liquids that have flash points from 70°F up to and including 131°F, handled at or above flash point.

d) Class III (1) – Liquids that have flash points above 131°F up to and including 212°F, handled below flash point.

e) Class III (2) – Liquids that have flash points above 131°F up to and including 212°F, handled at or above flash point.

f) Unclassified – Liquids that have flash points above 212°F.

Class 0 includes other similar liquefied flammable gases. Class II and III liquids are subdivided in accordance with the conditions under which they are handled. Class II (1) or III (1) refers to flammable liquids in Class II or III handled or stored at a temperature below the flash point. This is based on the maximum operating temperature of storage.
