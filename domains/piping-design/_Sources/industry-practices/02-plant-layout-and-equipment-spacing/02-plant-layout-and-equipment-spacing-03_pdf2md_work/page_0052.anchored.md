- For liquids which can cause damage to the environment or personal injury including IP Class III (1) (NFPA Class IIIA liquids stored/handled below their flash point), unclassified liquids (NFPA Class IIIB) and toxic liquids, a bund (dike) compound shall be provided as defined in clause 11.2 for IP Class I, II, and III (2) or other means of secondary containment to prevent threat to public health, personnel, water supplies or other environmental damage. This includes but is not limited to storage of acid and caustics.
