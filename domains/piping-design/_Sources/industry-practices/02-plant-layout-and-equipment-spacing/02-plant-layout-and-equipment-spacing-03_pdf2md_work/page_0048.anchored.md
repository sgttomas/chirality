b) Additional safety systems, e.g. equipment trips on detection of flammable material in the air intakes.

c) Reduced pressure on personnel working in the area.

Some air intakes can be tolerant of contaminants in the air. In these cases, it may be acceptable to locate the intake closer to the boundaries specified above.

However, there are cases when even a low level of contamination is unacceptable. In particular, the air intake for breathing air compressors must be sited where contamination is unlikely. The siting of breathing and instrument air compressors should consider possible expansion of the site, i.e. they should not be located where future processing plant may be constructed.

- Intakes and discharges shall be separated to prevent cross contamination by recirculation, taking into account natural wind effects. The distance between intakes and discharges shall be not less than 19.7 feet unless design provisions can be demonstrated to prevent recirculation.
- Air discharges from heating and ventilating systems from enclosed hazardous areas shall be considered as a source of hazardous release and shall be sited accordingly.
- Air discharges from heating and ventilating systems, and blow-off facilities associated with air compressors and fans, shall be located so that they do not cause a hazard or inconvenience to personnel nor affect the access to, and operation of, adjacent equipment.
- Gas turbine intakes must be located at an elevation at least 30 feet above grade to minimize snow and icing of filters.
- Gas turbine air intakes must be located in consideration of exhaust stack elevation and prevailing winds regarding possibility of exhaust entry into the intake.

## 13.9 Pressure Relief Devices

If atmospheric discharge from relief devices has been permitted and approved by the Company (see Sec. 7.4 – 4th bullet), flammable and toxic atmospheric discharges from the pressure relief devices shall comply with the listed guidelines below.

- Vapor from other low boiling point material storage vessels remote from process areas shall discharge to atmosphere not less than 6.6 feet above the pressure relief devices, and not less than 6.6 feet above any platform within a 9.7 feet radius.
