## 1.3 Exclusions

The focus of this guideline is on the consideration and development of layouts for plant facilities. The following exclusions apply:

- Specifications and allied design of process systems.
- Detailed pipe-work design and ergonomic/human factors aspects of pipe and valve positioning.
- Detailed engineering design of platforms, gratings, stairs and walkways.
- Design of support facilities, equipment, packages and modules.
- Overall safety systems design; other than the layout design for locations of safety equipment and allied facilities.

# 2 General

## 2.1 Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this document is to help maintain best current practices and requirements for plant layout and equipment spacing.

This guideline covers the general layout principles regarding OSA North Slope Facilities that will contribute towards a safe and operable plant. Aspects of determining the site location, such as environmental, regulatory and infrastructure are excluded.

Where there is Federal, Provincial or Local government legislation, regulation or practice applying to the plant layout and spacing, such requirements shall be complied.

Ideas to improve working methods are encouraged and anticipation that this document will be updated from time to time. Conflicts between this guideline and statutory requirements or referenced Codes and Standards shall be brought to the attention of the document custodian at the earliest opportunity.

This document represents the starting point for all projects. Project specific amendments will be issued to supplement this document where necessary.

## 2.2 Methodology

All recommendations made in this guideline are based on the following criteria:

- Safety & Fire Protection requirements.
- Human Factors, Engineering and Health requirements.
- Operating, Maintenance, and Construction requirements.
- Legal requirements.
