- Injury or death of personnel.

## 8.2 Hazardous and Safe Areas

Plants implicitly involve identifiable hazards in respect to the environment, location and presence of potentially explosive materials and ignition sources that have an impact on the safety of personnel and the facility / plant. Areas within such facilities require designation in relation to the level of effective safety or absence of particular hazards, and subsequently spaces are allocated for design and operational purposes as being a "Safe Area" or "Hazardous / Explosive Area". The determination and classification of the latter can be formalized through the systematic application of hazardous area classification which is prescribed by a wide range of methods; many of which fall under the category of State of Alaska specific standards or regulations.

### 8.2.1 Hazardous Areas

A hazardous area is defined in the applicable standards as an area in which an explosive atmosphere is present, or may be expected to be present, in quantities such as to require special precautions for the construction, installation and use of potential ignition sources. The explosive atmosphere is caused by the presence of a flammable liquid, gas or vapor or by the presence of combustible dust in suspension or in layers or a combination of gas and dust atmospheres.
