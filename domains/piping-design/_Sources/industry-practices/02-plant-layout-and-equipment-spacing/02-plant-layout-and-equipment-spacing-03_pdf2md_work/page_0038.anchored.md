a) Such pipes shall include flare lines and pipes connected to 353 ft³ or more of flammable liquid which cannot be remotely isolated.

b) If this is not practicable, the location of such pipes in the special fire risk area shall have external passive fire protection.

- Flammable or toxic liquid/gas piping that remains in service during a process unit outage shall not be routed through that process unit.

- Access and space accommodations shall be provided for:

  a) Designated removable pipe sections (e.g. for cleaning).

  b) Inspection needs.

  c) Complying with environmental requirements (e.g., for obtaining Volatile Organic Compound measurements).

## 12.3 Minimum Clearances

- Piping systems shall be designed, and equipment shall be laid out, to provide access by mobile equipment.

- Access ways shall be planned accordingly, and minimum clearances for this purpose shall be as described in Appendix A.

## 12.4 Piping Supports and Pipe Tracks

- Piping supports shall not block access ways nor interfere with access.

- Piping support layout should allow for removal of pipe spool if expected to be necessary for maintenance of equipment.

- Outside the plot and in tankage areas, pipes should normally be in pipe tracks.

- The minimum distance between the nearest edge of a pipe track and the toe of a bund (dike) shall be provided.

## 12.5 Pipe Racks

The following types of pipe racks may be required:

- Interconnecting pipe rack (elevated).

- Unit pipe rack (elevated).

- Pipe rack or sleeper way (at grade).
