### 8.2.5 Location of Safe Areas

All equipment that is critical to the plant safety support should always be located in a Safe Area (e.g. fire pumps, instrument/electrical equipment and an Emergency Assembly Area).

Safe Areas can also provide a controlled environment where welding can be carried out.

On large plants or complexes it may be necessary to provide more than one safe area. An escape and evacuation plan review will determine if this is required.

Muster Area /Temporary Safe Refuges (TSR) can be an allocated space within an already functional room or could be a separate designated area solely for the purposes of accommodating personnel during an emergency. The size of the main Muster Area/TSR should be 5 ft² per person.
