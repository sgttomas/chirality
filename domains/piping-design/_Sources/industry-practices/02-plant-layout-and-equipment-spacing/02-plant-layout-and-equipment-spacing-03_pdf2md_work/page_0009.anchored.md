**Flammable Vapor**

A general term to describe a flammable vapor or gas.

**Hazard Classification Analysis**

Quantitative and/or qualitative risk analysis that determined the hazard classification based on potential risk of fire or explosion and the consequences.

**Hazardous Area**

Class/Division hazardous location, around equipment items that define the likelihood of a flammable atmosphere being presents.

**Ignition/Open Flame Area**

An area containing equipment with open flame, and as practicable surfaces hot enough to ignite flammable material in the process area.

**In Plant Roads**

Roads serving one or more units or sections (of units) within a plot.

**Major Site Road**

The main network of thorough fare serving all plots within a site.

**Minor Site Road**

Others site roads serving one or more plots within the site.

**Module**

Any assembly of equipment items and their associated piping, instrumentation, electrical equipment, structure, combined into a transportable subsection of a process unit or offsite facility. The definition includes all sizes and sources of assembly, including contractor or vendor pre-assembled units, preassembled or dressed complex equipment items, e.g. towers or fired heaters and pipe rack units.

**Occupied Building**

A building is considering to be occupied if any of the following criteria are met:

- A person(s) is (are) assigned to a location as a workstation or command and control center in case of an emergency.
- A person(s) is (are) assigned to a location designated as safe haven in case of an emergency.
- A building is staffed 8 work-hours in a 24 hours period as part of an individual's primary job function.
- Cumulative occupancy is equal to or greater than 200 hours per week.
