a) A PSR should be considered for expansions, revamps, and temporary facilities.

b) This review ensures that security risks such as terrorist activity and vandalism are reviewed and mitigated by competent personnel.

- Projects shall undergo a Concept Safety Evaluation (CSE) for inherently safer design guidance.
- Environmental considerations shall be included in the plant layout design to ensure that projects and developments strive to achieve the corporate goal of "no damage" to the environment. Projects shall be in compliance with Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) in design and loss prevention.

  Damage can be caused by:

  a) Emissions to the air, water, and ground.

  b) Physical interactions (nuisance, e.g. visual impact, noise, footprint, odor, dust, etc.).

  c) Energy inefficiency.

  d) Material use and waste generation.

  e) Interference with other users of the local environment.

## 7.5 Design Philosophy

Plant layout is particularly important at the design stage because the potential for subsequent modification to the layout is very low. Plant layout is a major driver of the risk that must be managed throughout the lifetime of the facility.

Plant layout shall identify and adopt the safest and most cost-effective layout that meets the requirements of the process design, safe operation/maintenance, and emergency action.

- Minimum spacing within plant shall be in accordance with:

  a) Section 14 for aboveground storage tanks.

  b) API RP 752 Management of Hazards Associated with Location of Process Plant Buildings.

  c) International Building Code / Alaska Administrative Code

- Separation distances shall satisfy the following:

  a) Provide minimum access clearance for emergency response actions.

  b) Allow safe access for operation and emergency evacuation.

  c) Provide minimum access clearance for maintenance.

  d) Give an economic design.

  e) Fire ratings are based on building separation
