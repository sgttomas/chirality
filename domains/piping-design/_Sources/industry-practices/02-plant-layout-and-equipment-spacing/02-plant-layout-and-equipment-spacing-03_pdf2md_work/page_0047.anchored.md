Blowdown and water disengaging drums should be located in a separate unit area to avoid involvement in process unit fires.

Vessels containing catalyst, resins, filters, cartridges or packing require space for bins or pallets and lifting facilities for filling / emptying of materials. Where a series of beds are involved, access to each bed will be required. Reactors have a substantial top platform with lifting facilities for removal of reactor head and catalyst loading. Reactors can have platforms attached or have an independent access structure.

Towers and columns shall be located in a way that all working platforms and manholes face the access road. Manways shall be on or about a common center line.

Permanent access should be provided to manholes and instrumentation on all vessels. Access to platforms from ladders should be by side stepping wherever possible.

Vertical vessels shall be placed on a common outside diameter parallel to the main pipe way, unless design conditions or accessibility dictate otherwise.

## 13.8 Air Intakes and Discharges

- Air intakes, including intakes to heating and ventilating systems, air compressors for process, instrument, plant and breathing air, and to gas turbines, shall be located as far as is practicable away from areas where air contamination by dust, or by flammable or toxic material can occur.
- Air intakes shall not be located in any area classified nor located above or below an area classified as Class I Division 1.
- Air intakes other than for breathing air may be located:
  - a) Not less than 9.8 feet above a Class I Division 2 area if the flammable vapor release is heavier than air.
  - b) Not less than 9.8 feet below a Class I Division 2 area if the flammable release is lighter than air.
  - c) In a Class I Division 2 area where a dedicated flammable gas detection and protection system is provided.

    The density of the flammable vapor shall be assumed to be at the expected operating temperature.

    Special process conditions may mean lighter than air materials behave heavier than air and heavier than air materials behave lighter than air.

- The location of air intakes shall consider the effects of:
  - a) Possible air contamination with dust or with flammable or toxic material.
