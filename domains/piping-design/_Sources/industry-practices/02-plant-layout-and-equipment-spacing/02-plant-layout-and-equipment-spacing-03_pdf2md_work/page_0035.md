## 10.5 Plant Scale Model

Layout design may be aided using 3-dimensional drafting software models. The cost benefit of using these tools should be assessed.

## 10.6 Construction Consideration

- If a plot is being developed on (or major modification work is being carried out on an existing unit), or adjacent to, existing units, any interaction of the construction and commissioning of the unit(s) of the new plot and interconnections between the existing units shall be considered. Measures shall be taken to achieve safe construction and commissioning of new plant, and to minimize disruption to, or stoppage of, existing units if this is necessary. Risks from construction activity should be considered and included in the formal risk assessment to compare the relative risk levels of location options.

- During the construction activities, consideration needs to be given to temporary facilities, these may include the following:

  a) Vehicle parking and bull rail access.

  b) Temporary construction offices and temporary construction facilities.

  c) Construction laydown areas.

  d) Drilling activities.

  e) Warehousing and storage areas.

  f) A suitable access route to the construction site should be provided for the workforce and construction equipment (e.g., cranes). To avoid possible damage by bulky equipment, the route should avoid roads with overhead pipe crossings or obstructions. Because of the number of vehicles likely to be involved, the route should be away from areas with appreciable pedestrian traffic.

  g) The layout and plant location should allow adequate access for operating, maintenance and fire-fighting to the units, and provide for escape routes for emergency evacuation.

# 11 Access Ways and In-Plant Roads

## 11.1 Philosophy

- Access ways within each unit shall be provided for maintenance and for fire-fighting from the roads around the plot. The design of the road layout should consider the plant complexity and the type(s) of fire-fighting appliances (i.e. fire-fighting trucks) likely to be utilized during an emergency.
