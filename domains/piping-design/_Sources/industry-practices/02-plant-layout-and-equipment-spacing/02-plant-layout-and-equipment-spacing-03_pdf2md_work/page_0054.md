- Overhead clearance from top of rail for railroads to be confirmed by national (i.e. federal) and local railway clearances requirements as these vary from site to site.

## A.3: Horizontal

- Main access way horizontal clearance for mobile equipment shall not be less than:

  a) 20 feet.

  b) 10 feet if mobile equipment is to service a specific piece of process equipment and the mobile equipment can operate "straight-in" (no turns).

- Minimum horizontal clearance to piping, equipment, and instrumentation shall be as follows unless otherwise approved by the Company:

  a) 5 feet for aisles at equipment and machinery.

  b) 4 feet for operating and maintenance areas at grade.

  c) 4 feet for workspace on platforms servicing the heads of shell/tube exchangers mounted on a structure. Also refer to clause 13.5.

  d) 3 feet for workspace on platforms servicing manways, in addition, the center line of the manway should be at an elevation of 5 feet from the platform.

  e) 7 feet for workspace around compressors, turbines, and large motors shall be provided for operating and maintenance clearances to handle casings, rotors, crankshafts, flywheels, etc. This minimum spacing shall be validated by the equipment supplier and approved by the Company.

  Note: Clearances shall be measured from the furthest projection on equipment, including associated pipe work with its flanges and insulation boxes, filters, valves in their open position, drains, cabling, instruments, etc., between floor level and 6.6 feet above floor level.
