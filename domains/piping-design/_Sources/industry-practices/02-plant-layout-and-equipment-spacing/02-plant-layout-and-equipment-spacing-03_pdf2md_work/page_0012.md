## 5 Site Selection

Safety is a key consideration when deciding where to locate a plant. Other important factors include:

- Proximity of public and populated areas (particularly downwind).
- Access to raw materials and product markets.
- Availability of fuel supply.
- Availability of land, labor and cooling or process water.
- Means of effluent disposal.
- Interconnections with other plants and power grids.
- Risk to/from adjacent plants.
- Government policies (planning permission and investment incentives).
