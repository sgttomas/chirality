## 3   Applicable Codes and Standards

The design shall comply with the latest version of all relevant regulatory requirements. The latest version of the codes and standards listed below are considered a part of this guideline. Wherever conflict exists between the requirements of the referenced standards and codes and regulation, the design shall follow the most stringent and safest requirement applicable to this project.

Any updates to the codes and standards during the project execution (especially related to regulatory permits and safety) shall be brought to Company attention.
