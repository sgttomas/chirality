## 13.2 Detailed Equipment Layout Considerations

This section outlines the key aspects to be considered when developing detailed equipment layouts during the define phase. As well as reviewing all the preliminary layout considerations, equipment-specific spacing and arrangements are considered.

Equipment spacing guidelines are provided in the Appendix A. These should be used in conjunction with any project applicable regulatory codes and Company requirements.

In general, equipment shall be arranged with a view to providing:

- Separation of ignition sources from hazardous areas.
- Minimal explosion overpressure in case of ignited flammable release.
- All special process requirements (gravity flow, self-draining, critical NPSH, etc.).
- Access for fire-fighting and emergency response.
- Prevention of serious consequences from dropped objects.
- Facilitation of plant operations and control in normal and emergency situations.
- Avoidance of confined spaces wherever possible.
- Minimal possibility for escalation of fires and other failures or accidents.
- Safe containment of accidental release of hazardous liquids.
- Planned simultaneous operations (e.g. drilling and production, construction and production).

Spacing for equipment handling non-flammable materials is only that required for operations and maintenance. Special consideration should be given to equipment handling toxic or corrosive materials.

Similar equipment should be grouped together where practical (except firewater pumps). Parallel equipment should be located symmetrically, wherever possible to aid flow distribution.

Equipment containing large inventories of combustibles (e.g. oil/gas separators) should be separated to reduce the risk of escalating events.

Equipment should occupy the minimum of space; consistent with its function, maintenance, operational inspection and access requirements.

Equipment stacking is often necessary to achieve a reasonable utilization of plot space, however, to keep the fire involvement as low as possible, the following guidelines should be observed:

- Avoid stacking more than three levels of equipment.
- Do not locate equipment over any of the following:
  a) Pumps or compressors handling flammable materials.
