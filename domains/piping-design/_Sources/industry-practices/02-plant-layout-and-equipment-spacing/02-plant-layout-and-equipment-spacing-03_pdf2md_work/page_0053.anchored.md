# APPENDIX A:    Guidance on Clearances

## A.1:    General

- Items such as the following shall not block operating aisles or maintenance access.

  a) Projecting piping.

  b) Valve spindles (valve wheels).

  c) Instrumentation.

  d) Instrument air manifolds.

  e) Local panels.

  f) Transducer stations.

  g) Pneumatic and electrical junction boxes.

  h) Grouped steam tracing supply and condensate manifolds.

- A minimum of 3.3 feet clear working spaces shall be maintained around equipment that requires frequent servicing such as pumps, exchangers, control valves, instruments, tower manways, etc. Also refer to A.3. (3rd bullet) for more stringent minimum horizontal clearances for specific applications.

- Horizontal and vertical clearances shall meet or exceed those recommended by equipment suppliers for maintenance and operations.

## A.2:    Vertical

- Overhead clearances shall not be less than:

  a) 12 feet if carry deck crane access is required.

  b) 15 feet if cherry picker (13.6 to 16.3 t [15 to 18 ton] crane) access is required.

  c) 10 feet if 1.8 to 2.7 t (2 to 3 ton) motorized forklift access is required.

  d) 12 feet for main pipe racks.

  e) 7 feet from grade or elevated platform for personnel passageway.

  f) 10 feet from pavement for secondary roads (cars, pickups, no mobile equipment).

  g) 16 feet from pavement for main roadways without crane access.

  h) 18 feet from pavement for main roadways and, if necessary, over access ways for heavy equipment.
