## 6.1 Plant Layout Considerations

This section outlines the key aspects to be considered when preparing preliminary layouts during pre-FEED phase. Preliminary layouts are influenced by the following factors:

- Type of plant, process units
- Design specifications
- Overall site size and shape, natural slope
- Topography
- Permitting constraints
- Site soil and local environment conditions
- The selected construction and module movement method

These should be used in conjunction with the project applicable codes and standards, regulatory consideration and Company requirements.

The following points must be considered for optimum plant layout:

- Accessibility for normal operation and maintenance.
- Accessibility for fire-fighting.
- Accessibility for emergency shutdown.
- Accessibility for evacuation of personnel.
- Prevailing wind direction and effect of vapor clouds from or to the adjacent operating units.
- Individual fire risk areas, with their hazard classification and required fire protection measures.
- Segregation of high-risk facilities and sources of ignition.
- Protection of critical emergency facilities.
- Segregation and protection against blast and overpressure scenarios.
- Protection of adjacent facilities in the event of a fire.
- Security of installations from outside hazards.
- Minimizing danger and inconvenience to personnel and property beyond the boundary fence.
- Ergonomic Factors.
- Escape routes should be as straight and obstruction free as possible.
- Hazardous area classification.
