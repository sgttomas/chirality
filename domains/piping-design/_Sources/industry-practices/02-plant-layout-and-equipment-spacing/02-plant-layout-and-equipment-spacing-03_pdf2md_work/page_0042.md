b) Air coolers (fin-fans).

c) Heat exchangers or drums containing flammable materials above 600 °F or above its auto-ignition temperature (whichever is lower).

In order to minimize the length of piping / conveyor runs; process equipment should be arranged and oriented in a manner that is consistent with the main process flow and meet with piping / conveyor flexibility requirements.

To avoid unexpected interferences, clashes and congestion; the requirements for ancillary equipment should be established before finalizing the location of major equipment. These include:

- Access platforms to remote valves, instruments, Safety Relief Valves.
- Protective frames around equipment.
- Safety systems (e.g. eyewash stations, safety showers adjacent to any dangerous chemical handling units).
- Operator control stations and local instrument panels.
- Monorails required to assists dismantling work.
- Enclosures around engines.
- Drains and vents.
- Equipment auxiliaries such as pump seal systems.

## 13.3 Rotating Equipment

The layout of all rotating equipment should provide space for maintenance, removal and replacement of rotors and other major components. Rotating equipment should not be located below pipe racks.

Pumps should be grouped together with good maintenance access to the motor, and good personnel access to seals and suction / discharge valves. Adequate space for lifting and handling facilities for maintenance shall be provided. Maintenance access should allow for removal of the motor or any components over 50 lbs which include seals, coupling, rotors and bundles, either by 'A' frame, motorize forklift or monorail.

Spacing separates pumps with higher fire potential and provides access for fire-fighting. When a pump and its spare are exposed to a common fire hazard, additional separation between them may be justified to minimize the risk of losing both of them during a fire.
