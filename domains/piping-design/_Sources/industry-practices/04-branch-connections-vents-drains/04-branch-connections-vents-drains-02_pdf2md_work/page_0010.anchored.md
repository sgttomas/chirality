## 4.2 Piping Material Take-Off

4.2.1 Table 1 and the connection details can be used for an early piping material take-off.

*Comment:* Each combination of *PIP Piping Material Specification* and piping header size defines a single material take-off for each connection detail and ensures that materials purchased during an early material take-off are the same materials selected during detailed design.

4.2.2 Each connection detail provides only one choice of components for each header size.

*Comment:* This helps to provide consistency on large projects with multiple design areas. It also ensures that early material purchases will match those used during detailed design.

## 4.3 Insulation and Pipe Nipple Length

Pipe nipple length on insulated piping shall be a minimum of 75 mm (3 inches) or 25 mm (1 inch) plus the thickness of insulation, whichever is greater.
