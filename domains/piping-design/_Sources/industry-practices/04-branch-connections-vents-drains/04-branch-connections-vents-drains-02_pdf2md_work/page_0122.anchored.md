NOTES:

1. RATINGS AND TYPE OF MATERIAL SHALL BE IN ACCORDANCE WITH LINE CLASS.

2. TAPS, NIPPLES, ELLS, AND VALVES SHALL BE: NPS 1/2 FOR CL300 AND CL600 ORIFICE FLANGES.
   NPS 3/4 FOR CL900, CL1500, AND CL2500 ORIFICE FLANGES.

3. FOR TAP ORIENTATION SEE APPROPRIATE DETAILS IN PIP PCIF0000.

4. IF TWO VALVES ARE USED AT THE END OF AN ELL OR NIPPLE, SUPPORT SHALL BE ADDED FOR MOMENT ARM.

5. IF ROD-OUT IS REQUIRED, THE 45° ELLS SHALL BE ELIMINATED BY ROTATING ORIFICE FLANGE ONE BOLT HOLE. TWO 100(4) LONG NIPPLES SHALL BE USED.

DIMENSIONS ARE GIVEN IN MILLIMETERS. DIMENSIONS IN PARENTHESES ARE IN INCHES.
