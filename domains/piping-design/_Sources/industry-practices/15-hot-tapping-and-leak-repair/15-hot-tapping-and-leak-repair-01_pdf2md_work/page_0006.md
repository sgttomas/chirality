i) The **Inspector** directs the Inspection contractors to perform any inspections required to determine the suitability of the various repair options and forwards the results to the Fixed Equipment Engineer.

ii) The **Fixed Equipment Engineer** reviews and approves any drawings and calculations produced by the Leak Repair Contractor.

iii) The **Fixed Equipment Engineer** approves the rework method, and ensures that all supporting documents are filed in the Leak Repair folder described in Section 3.3.h. Supporting documents could include marked P&IDs, marked isometric drawings, clamp drawings and calculations, mill certifications, composite wrap calculations, etc.

v) If a clamp (i.e. Temporary Repair Enclosure) is to be reworked (e.g. repumped or retonqued), the **Fixed Equipment Engineer** ensures that the requirements of Section 5 of this STP are met and then completes Appendix B and Appendix A (if required) of this STP and routes it to the Unit Process Superintendent for review and approval. The **Process Superintendent** then reviews and approves Appendix A.

vi) The **Reliability Coordinator** creates Work Orders in the Maximo system as needed for the Leak Repair Contractor or BP Maintenance Shop.

d) The **Unit Control of Work Area Authority** arranges for a risk assessment appropriate for the rework method to be used and the system to be repaired.

i) The **Area Authority** ensures that the Control of Work risk assessment includes a discussion of emergency response plans in the event that a leak occurs or worsens during the repair.

e) The **Reliability Coordinator** and **Area Authority** authorize the Leak Repair Contractor or BP Maintenance Shop to begin the work using the Control of Work process.

f) The **Leak Repair Contractor** or **BP Maintenance Shop** complete the leak repair.

g) The **Reliability Coordinator** updates the Details of Leakage/Repair field on the Leak Repair Form with the rework information.

h) If the rework changes the scope needed for the permanent repair, the **Reliability Coordinator** updates the work order for the permanent repair with the pertinent details.
