### 1.2.3 Inspector

**1.2.3.1** Shall be responsible for completing the initial inspection of proposed hot tap location, checking and reviewing to make judgement whether a proposed location is acceptable. Necessary inspection data shall be recorded on Appendix-I.

**1.2.3.2** During preparation and fabrication work, the inspector or designate shall monitor the installation and welding. Prior to welding of the nozzle or fitting, the inspector shall verify the nozzle fit-up location against the location of UT measurements taken.

### 1.2.4 Design Engineer

**1.2.4.1** Shall initiate the Appendix-I by completing the required information and shall forward to Construction Coordinator for hot tap location approval.

**1.2.4.2** Shall ensure that the design is in accordance with applicable design codes and Suncor engineering standards.

**1.2.4.3** Shall complete the design checklist (Appendix-II) and prepare necessary drawings showing the fit-up / welding details, hydrostatic test pressures, NDE requirements, MAWP calculations, nozzle flange-pipe tolerances, stress analysis and Finite Element Analysis (if required).

### 1.2.5 Welding Contractor / Welding Operator

**1.2.5.1** Shall submit qualified on-line weld procedure specification and Procedure Qualification Record (PQR) for each specific on-line welding operation to Construction Coordinator and Design Engineer.

**1.2.5.2** Shall submit welding personnel qualification to the Suncor Inspector for approval.
