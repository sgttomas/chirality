1.2.2.3 Shall coordinate with Suncor field engineering or QA/QC to review and approve the online welding procedures (WPS/PQR).

1.2.2.4 Shall coordinate with all the concerned parties, mentioned herein, to execute the hot tap. Prepare an execution work plan including hot tap drawings (execution plan may be prepared by contractor for installation of hot tap), contingency plans, inspection and check lists and the operating condition at the time of hot tap. The execution plan shall also consist of standby equipment for safety and fire fighting, and any other requirement for such execution on a case-basis. Shall also obtain the hot tap authorization from Operations on Appendix-III.

1.2.2.5 Ensure that 'Safety Plan' is in place for each hot tapping operation and shall be agreed beforehand with relevant departments. The suggested topics to be included are:

- Site accessibility for personnel and equipment
- Roles and responsibilities of the personnel at site
- Monitoring of operating conditions (flow rate, pressure & temperature) and gas levels
- Communications on site
- Warning system and emergency shutdown
- Foreseeable hazards and contingency actions
- Fire fighting equipment and personnel
- Safety equipment and services
- First-aid facilities
- Means of escape

1.2.2.6 After final approval but before any field work (except scaffolding & site preparation), Construction Coordinator shall hold a "Kick-off" meeting with welders & pipe fitters, hot machine operator, permit issuer and other members as deemed necessary for each stage of the job. Shall also review the Appendix-IV with the concerned persons and obtain the necessary signatures.
