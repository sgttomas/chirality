## 4.12 Electrical Isolation

Flange electrical isolation kits shall be used whenever a galvanic corrosion cell would be created by connecting dissimilar metals, joining flanges like SS to a CS flanges. This mainly used to avoid electrochemical reaction to prevent from galvanic corrosion.

Isolation kits are typically not required in non-electrolyte environment.

## 4.13 Thermowells

Thermowells process connection shall be heavy wall, and typically will be threaded, or flanged type per the applicable piping class specification. The immersion length ("U") shall be defined by instrument engineer for the required minimum insertion depth.

Thermowells shall be constructed from drilled barstock, tapered and the material shall be 316 Stainless Steel (SS) minimum. Refer to Instrumentation Temperature Measurement Specification (OGE-ICSPE-000008) for thermowells specification and recommendation.

## 4.14 Insulation

Insulation shall be provided for personnel protection on hot surfaces (with a normal operating temperature greater than 140°F) within 3 feet of an established work space of a fixed access area. Protection should extend to a height of 7 feet above platforms and not less than 3 feet beyond handrails.

Outdoors piping other than stainless steel, with continuous operating temperature <300°F, shall be externally coated beneath the insulation to protect from corrosion under insulation. Coating shall be completely extended through the module wall penetration.

When any insulation other than foamed-in-place polyurethane is used on exterior piping, care shall be taken to ensure that the insulation segments and the jacketing are oriented to allow free draining.

# 5 General Design Consideration

## 5.1 Plot Layout

Plot layout shall be an economical design to meet the process and licensors objective reflecting the need for safety to life and property, and allowing adequate access for maintenance, operation and emergency action. To adopt the safest and most effective layout that meets the requirement of process design, the following will be considered:
