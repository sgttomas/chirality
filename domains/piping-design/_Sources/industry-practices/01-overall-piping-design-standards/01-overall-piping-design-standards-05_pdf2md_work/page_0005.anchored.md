## 3   Applicable Codes and Standards

This criteria state the Industry Source 03 projects on the Industry Source 03 Facility of Alaska requirements (if available) are to be used in addition to the referenced standards and codes. In the event of an inconsistency, conflict or discrepancy between any of the standards, specifications and regulations the contractor shall follow the most stringent and safest requirement applicable to the project. Any inconsistency critical to the design shall be brought to Company's attention for approval. Items not addressed in these criteria shall comply with the latest issue of all relevant regulatory requirements.
