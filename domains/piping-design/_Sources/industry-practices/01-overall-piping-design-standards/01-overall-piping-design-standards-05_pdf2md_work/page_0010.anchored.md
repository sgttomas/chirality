- Plot drainage – The location of spillage collection areas depends on the likely spillage, volatility of liquid and position of permanent ignition sources and the effect of pool fire on the equipment.

- Special fire risk area – The need for special fire risk areas shall be reviewed to decide if improved equipment design can justify their reduction or elimination.

- Construction consideration – Risk from construction activity should be considered and included in the final risk assessment. Movement of construction equipment and especially operation of cranes and lifting gear near operating units can be a hazard.

- Blast Effect – Consideration should be given to effects of blast where there is a significant risk of unconfined gas/vapor explosion.

## 5.2 Access Ways and In-Plant Roads

Access ways within each unit shall be provided for maintenance and for firefighting from the roads around the plot. The design of road layout shall consider the plant complexity and the type of fire-fighting equipment likely to be utilized during an emergency. Minimum widths for personnel access ways, stairways, elevated walkways, platforms and vehicular access ways shall be considered.

- Routing – Access ways may be run longitudinally under pipe racks, provided that all relevant clearance factors have been considered.

- Access to Platforms – Access to platform shall meet minimum clearance requirement. Platform escape routes shall not be adjacent to each other. Platform escape routes shall not be adjacent to the source of hazard.

## 5.3 Piping

Requirements for process piping system are covered in Plant Piping Design Specification (Industry Source 03 Doc PISPE-000005) that includes information on layout, access, pipe clearances, pipe support, piperacks, valve operation, spades, sample points and battery limit isolation.
