## 1.2 Scope

Plant layout shall identify and adopt a layout that is safe and cost effective and which meets the requirements of the process design, safe operation/maintenance, emergency response action and evacuation. The following items shall be used to guide piping engineering design:

- Piping and Instrument Diagram (P&ID's) shall be the basis for piping design and shall show piping schematics, line numbers, process equipment, valve, piping specialties, controls, and instruments. Changes to these diagrams after they are issued for design shall have prior approval from the Company project representative.

- Piping and fittings supplied integrally with vessels, compressors, pumps, and other equipment shall be in accordance with requirement of these criteria concerning limitations on type of piping connection.

- Separation distance for common items of equipment shall be regarded as a maximum for economic layout purposes. Any proposal to increase these separation distances shall be subject to cost benefit analysis.

- Space for probable future equipment shall be designated and located adjacent to related equipment. The allocation of such space should be addressed during the initial project risk assessment in order to identify potential hazards associated with probable future equipment.

- Piping layout shall allow for protection of piping systems, where necessary, to prevent freezing or to prevent difficulties resulting from high viscosities or wax formation.
