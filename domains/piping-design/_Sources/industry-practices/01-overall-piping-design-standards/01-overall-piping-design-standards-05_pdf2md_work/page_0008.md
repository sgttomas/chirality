Consult Process Design Criteria – NPF-PRCRT-000001 for line sizing and fluid velocity recommendation.

## 4.8 Wall Thickness

The Line Classes list specific wall thickness for each pipe diameter. It is the responsibility of the Piping Material Engineer to verify these dimensions for compliance with the Specification and Code of Construction.

For ASME B31.3, the nominal wall thickness (t) is calculated by factoring the mill tolerance into minimum wall thickness (t_m) including corrosion allowance (i.e., for ASTM A106: t_1 = t_m/0.875, where t_m is the minimum calculated wall plus any corrosion allowance). For ASME B31.4 and B31.8 the mill tolerance is accounted for in the respective design factors used in wall thickness calculations.

## 4.9 Branch Connections

Due to the complexities of design and installation, stub–on pipe branches may be used as branch connections only where the design and fabrication have the specific approval of the Oil Search project representative. Stub-on and stub-in branch connections with full encirclement reinforcement saddles are not allowed without the approval of the Company project representative.

Miter bends shall not be used in hydrocarbon, chemical, erosive or corrosive services.

## 4.10 Supports

Flanged connections at equipment should be capable of accepting maintenance slip blinds.

Dead legs for supporting pipe shall be avoided on all piping. Dummy legs may be provided when pipe runs fall short of supports.

Spring-loaded pipe support elements shall be designed to prevent complete release of piping load in the event of spring failure or misalignment. Hydrostatic load shall be included in design loads. Lines subject to blowdown and impulse loadings shall be identified by the process engineer and the layout and its support shall be designed to avoid high loading and vibration.

## 4.11 Piping Stress Analysis

The In-Plant Piping stress analysis shall be carried out by contractor in compliance with the Oil Search Alaska specification. Specific engineering codes, standards and practices shall be applied in the area where the project is developed. This will apply to module piping, in-plant piping and pipelines.
