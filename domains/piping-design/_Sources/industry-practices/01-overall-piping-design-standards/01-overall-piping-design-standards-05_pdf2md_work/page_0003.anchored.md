# 1 Introduction

This Piping Design Criteria is intended to be site specific for the Industry Source 03 projects on the Industry Source 03 Facility of Alaska. Listed are the minimum requirements for the design, materials, fabrication and testing of In-Plant Piping materials and furnished with packaged equipment. These design guidelines ensure uniformity and consistency of the design by describing the following:

- Piping materials specification
- Piping design and layout requirements
- Piping stress analysis and pipe support requirement
- Definition of design deliverables

## 1.1 Objectives

The purpose of this criteria define the minimum technical requirements for the piping design and layout of process and utility piping system for module piping installation, including the following:

- Plot Areas
- Equipment
- Piping
- Piperacks
- Platforms
- Roads
- Other miscellaneous items

These design criteria does not include the topics of spacing and layout consideration for minimizing losses due to fire.

The plant layout is concerned with the spatial arrangement of the infrastructure and process equipment interconnections within the plant boundaries. The requirement of battery limit beyond the plant boundary is outside the scope of these criteria. If there are local legislative regulations on plant layout in which the site is located, these must be complied with (such as statutory requirements based on local environment and security restrictions).
