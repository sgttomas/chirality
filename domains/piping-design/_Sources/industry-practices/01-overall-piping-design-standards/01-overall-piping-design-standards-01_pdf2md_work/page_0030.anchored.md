4.4.9 Bolt tensioning is not a normal practice at Industry Source 01 CHP and shall not be specified unless approved by the Industry Source 01 CHP Reliability Engineer. If bolt tensioning is approved by Industry Source 01 CHP, longer studs shall be specified as required for the specific bolt tensioning tools to be used, along with hardened washers with a minimum 35 Rockwell C hardness.

**4.5 Orifice Flanges**

4.5.1 Orifice flanges shall be specified and installed without jacking bolts/screws to prevent interference during installation which may inadvertently hinder proper bolt-up and gasket seating. Orifice flanges shall be specified per the applicable piping class in Industry Source 01 CHP STP 205. Separation of flanges for installation of orifices can be performed with separate wedging tools.

## SECTION 5: FLANGE FACINGS AND GASKETS

**5.1 Flange Facing**

5.1.1 Flange facings shall be in accordance with dimensions established in ASME B16.5 or ASME B16.47 as applicable. Raised face flanges are preferred for new installations. Do not use ring joint flanges for new work unless approved by Industry Source 01 CHP Reliability Engineer, except when needed for mating to existing flanges.

5.1.2 The raised portion of raised face steel or alloy flanges shall be removed if the flanges are to mate with flat faced flanges of cast iron, FRP, or other low strength materials susceptible to break under bending stress.

5.1.3 Flange facings and gaskets shall be in accordance with the appropriate service class in Industry Source 01 CHP STP 205.

**5.2 Sheet Gaskets**

Sheet gaskets shall be full faced when fitted to flat faced flanged valves and equipment. It is common for cast iron, fiberglass, and plastic flanges to be flat faced.

## SECTION 6: BENDS

**6.1 Bends**

Bends may be used when economical; however, minimum bend radius shall be six pipe diameters. Fittings manufactured by the Induction Bending Process are not allowed without specific approval from Industry Source 01 CHP Reliability Engineering.

## SECTION 7: VALVES – GENERAL

**7.1 Valve Standards and Service Definition**

7.1.1 Where no specific applicable design Standard exists, the design of all pressure containing components shall conform to the requirements of ASME B31.3 and ASME B16.34.

7.1.2 Valves shall be supplied per the applicable line class in Industry Source 01 CHP STP 205. When a line class provides the option of flanged or buttweld valves, flanged valves shall be supplied unless otherwise approved by Industry Source 01 CHP.

> **Note:** Flanged valves are advantageous for the purposes of blinding or maintenance/replacement. Buttweld valves provide less risk of leakage, but in most cases the benefit will be considered less than the inherent difficulties with maintenance/replacement.

7.1.3 When valves other than those specified in the individual service classes are required, they shall be indicated on the P&ID as specialty valves, with Specialty Item Numbers (SP#) assigned by the Document Control Group.
