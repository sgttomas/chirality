2.13.2 Unless specifically required for operation, block valves will not be provided at vessel nozzles when the line to or from the vessel is self-draining.

2.13.3 Normally a block valve will not be provided at the vessel nozzle for the following line services:

a) Overhead vapor to condensers

b) Thermosiphon reboilers; liquid and vapor

c) Overhead condensers to accumulator

d) Inter-connecting exchanger units in series

2.13.4 All valves shall be shown on P&I Diagrams.

## 2.14 Vents, Drains and Bleeds

2.14.1 Complete venting and draining of all piping, either thru equipment or separate connections, is required. Minimum size for vents and drains in piping shall be 3/4". Design and installation of vents and drains shall be in accordance with drawing LA-000-0100-0200-034.

2.14.2 All drains periodically blown down, including instrument, piping drains, and drains from major equipment, shall be independently routed to the sewer with discharge visible from the drain valve location. Hazardous material shall be released at a safe distance from the drain valve and operator.

2.14.3 Funnels with a splash rim should be used at drain point on elevated platforms with the funnel drains routed to a sewer at grade.

2.14.4 For vents and drains intended for use only for hydrotesting, it is preferred to have permanent valves installed per the pipe line class. If temporary threaded valves are used, they shall be removed and the threaded openings shall be plugged and seal welded per BP CHP STP 211 paragraph 14.4.4. Threaded pipe caps shall not be used in place of round solid plugs at vents and drains. Seal welding of the plugs is not required for pipe line classes which specify threaded fittings in sizes below 2".

2.14.5 All vent and drain locations shall be shown on the isometrics.

2.14.6 Vessels, exchangers, centrifugal pump casings, and other equipment shall be provided with a drain line not smaller in size than the drain connection on the equipment.

2.14.7 A closed pump-out system shall be provided for units which cannot be adequately cleared using system pressure. Pump-out lines shall be 2" minimum in size with a blind and double block and bleed arrangement as shown on drawing LA-000-0100-0200-003.

2.14.8 A positive means of isolation shall be provided for all vents and drains in VOC services and hazardous services. In these services, vents and drains that are **expected to be routinely opened** while the unit is operating shall be provided with double block valves. Vents and drains subject to this requirement include installations in process piping, utility piping, instrument piping and tubing, sight glasses, vessel trim, pump trim, and compressor trim. For all other vents and drains in VOC and hazardous services, where the valves are not expected to be routinely opened, other means of positive isolation besides double block valves are acceptable as noted below.

Note: Other acceptable means of positive isolation are: (1) a single valve with a threaded round head solid plug; or (2) a single valve with a blind flange.

Services considered to be hazardous include all process hydrocarbons, 600# steam and condensate, boiler feed water, caustic, acid, nitrogen, oxygen, natural gas, fuel gas, ammonia, sour gas, sour water, and other services containing toxic chemicals. Services considered to be non-hazardous include low pressure steam and condensate, lube oil.
