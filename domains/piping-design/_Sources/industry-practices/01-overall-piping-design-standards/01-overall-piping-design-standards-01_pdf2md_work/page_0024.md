### 2.28.4 Potable Water

Potable Water piping shall conform to applicable Washington State Regulations. Special attention shall be paid to the project reports mandated in WAC 246-290-110, with exclusions allowed by WAC 246-290-125. ## 2.29 Emergency Isolation Valves (EIVs)

EIVs (some referred to as Emergency Block Valves) are defined as a means to isolate flammable or toxic substances, in the event of a leak or a fire.

EIVs shall be considered where required by API 553 (Section 7). A risk review shall be conducted with the appropriate site personnel to determine if EIVs are required and to specify details of installation.

Consideration shall be given to the installation requirements of API 553.

### 2.29.1 Service Applicability for Pumps (also see BP ETP GP 42-10)

Service Requirements are as follows:

- for flammable liquid service; and
- on piping systems located downstream of vessels (such as tanks; towers; reactors) and upstream of pumps; and
- when vessel volume is greater than 100 bbl; or
- when vessel volume greater than 50 bbl if temperature greater than 410 F; or
- when vessel volume greater than 50 bbl if temperature greater than auto ignition temperature; or
- when vessel volume greater than 50 bbl if the contents are light ends.

*Note: Excluded from requirements that require EIVs are installations with glandless or sealless pumps.*

Definition of Dact is as follows:

- in the case of a single pump, the actual horizontal distance between the pump and the upstream EIV; or
- in the case of a double pump/single EIV arrangement, the actual horizontal distance between a pump and the single EIV located upstream of the branch to the second pump; or
- in the case of a double pump/double EIV arrangement, the actual horizontal distance between each pump and the EIV located on each branch line.

*Note: The preference is to use a single EIV for double pump arrangements.*

Dmin is 10 feet, and is the minimum allowable horizontal distance between the pump and the EIV for the above three cases.

Remote actuation is required for Dact less than 50 feet. The actuating device (such as local push button) must be more than 50 feet away from the pump.

Fireproofing of the EIV is required for Dact less than 25 feet.
