## 2.8 Support and Anchorage

**2.8.1** Pipe supports, guides, anchors and insulation supports shall be shown on the piping drawings.

**2.8.2** All piping shall be adequately supported, guided, or anchored so as to prevent excessive vibration, deflection, or stress on equipment. Loads imposed on equipment by the piping shall not exceed that allowed by the equipment manufacturer and the applicable Industry Source 01 CHP STPs 10, 12, 14, 20, 21, 70, 71, and CHP-GIS-34-201.

**2.8.3** A listing showing predicted thermal movement and proposed anchor locations for all lines crossing the process unit battery limits shall be submitted for approval by Industry Source 01 CHP and included in the Mechanical Equipment Data Books. The intent is to handle on-site piping expansion within the battery limits. When significant movement due to thermal changes is anticipated on an overhead line entering or leaving a process unit, the line shall be anchored at the process unit battery limit.

**2.8.4** See standard pipe support, shoe, and anchor drawings LA-000-0100-0200-040, LA-000-0100-0200-041, LA-0100-80, LA-0100-81, LA-0100-84, LA-0100-85, LA-0100-86, LA-0100-87, LA-0100-88, LA-0100-89, LA-0100-90, LA-0100-91, LA-0100-93, LA-0100-94, LA-0100-95, LA-0100-97, LA-0100-98, LA-0100-223, LA-0100-224, LA-0100-257, and LD-0100-20. These drawings shall be used where applicable unless Contractor's or supplier's alternate detail drawings have been approved for use by Industry Source 01 CHP. Note that clamp on type shoes are not permitted without prior Industry Source 01 CHP approval.

**2.8.5** Pipe shall not be welded to its support (other than a shoe) for either anchorage or vibration damping.

**2.8.6** Piping shall be arranged and supported to permit removal of equipment such as pumps and exchanger bundles for routine maintenance without having to install temporary supports. Equipment shall not be used as a pipe support.

**2.8.7** Above grade piping shall be supported by structural members. Use of dummy pipe supports shall be minimized per section 12.3, but dummy supports are preferred over use of rod hangers. Rod hangers may be used only where no other means of support is practicable. Rod hangers shall be assigned an RH number by submitting a request form to Document Control, and a detail drawing shall be created for each hanger using the Industry Source 01 CHP rod hanger drawing template.

**2.8.8** All piping shall be supported in a manner suitable for hydrostatic test loadings unless otherwise approved by Industry Source 01 CHP. In larger diameter piping handling gas during normal operation, it may be economically advantageous to design the permanent structural support for gas loading and install temporary support for hydrotest loads. The Contractor shall bring this to Industry Source 01 CHP's attention for consideration. If approved by Industry Source 01 CHP, the temporary supports shall be prominently noted on the engineering drawings.

**2.8.9** Spring hangers and spring supports shall be provided with permanent scales marked with hot and cold positions, position indicators, and limit stops. Spring hangers/supports shall be assigned an SH number by submitting a request form to Document Control, and a detail drawing shall be created for each spring hanger or support using the Industry Source 01 CHP spring hanger/support drawing template.
