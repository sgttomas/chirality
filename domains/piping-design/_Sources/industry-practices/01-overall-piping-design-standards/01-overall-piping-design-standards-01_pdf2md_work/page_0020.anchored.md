- 2.20.4 Piping shall be designed to allow for removal of compressor parts and for adequate operating and maintenance clearances. Valves, compressor controls, and accessories requiring operating checks and maintenance shall be accessible from grade or platforms. Piping leads on compressor nozzles shall be held to within the allowable established by Industry Source 01 CHP's compressor standard.

- 2.20.5 Cooling water shall be supplied as required from parallel manifolds and furnished with block valves on both inlet and outlet. The manifolds shall dead end with a plug or blind for cleanout purposes. Cylinder jacket water shall be provided with steam-water tempering spargers designed to operate silently while discharging between 90°F to 140°F unless separate dedicated cooling systems are supplied.

- 2.20.6 Distance pieces on reciprocating machines in hydrocarbon service shall be vented with pipe runs terminating at least 10' above grade or operating platforms.

- 2.20.7 Connections for pressure gauges and thermowells shall be installed in the nozzles (on the compressor side) of all pulsation bottles. The connections shall conform to the materials specification of the attached piping. They shall not interfere with maintenance of the machine. Pressure gauges shall be installed in piping downstream of discharge pulsation bottles.

- 2.20.8 Reciprocating compressor pipe supports and braces recommended by the equipment manufacturer shall be installed. These supports and braces will be identified by an Analog Study performed by the equipment manufacturer. Every deletion is to be justified by the engineering contractor and approved by Industry Source 01 CHP. Equipment reactions/loadings shall encompass the first vessel or exchanger located upstream of the suction pulsation damper and downstream of the discharge pulsation damper.

- 2.20.9 Recycle lines for centrifugal compressors shall be full discharge line size to the control valve and full suction line size downstream of the control valve.

- 2.20.10 Unless otherwise specified, all suction lines from the knockout pot to the cylinders of reciprocating compressors, including the suction pulsation dampers, shall be heat traced and insulated.

- 2.20.11 Check valves in centrifugal compressors which are installed in piping systems with ASME flange class 600 and lower shall be special non-slam, slow-closing check valves. Mokveld TKZ, Cameron Entech Non-Slam Nozzle Check Valve, Curtis-Wright Enertech NozzleCheckValve, or approved alternate. Check valves at centrifugal compressors which are installed in piping systems with ASME flange class 900 and higher, and check valves at all reciprocating compressors, shall be approved by Industry Source 01 CHP Reliability Engineering. Review and selection of check valves at compressors shall include both the Fixed Equipment and Rotating Equipment Engineers.

- 2.20.12 For additional piping requirements related to reciprocating compressors see Sections 2.1.10 and 2.5.6 in this standard, and applicable sections of Industry Source 01 CHP STP 10.

## 2.21 Product Sample Draw-Off Piping

Product sample piping shall be as short and direct to the sample cooler or sample outlet as much as possible to minimize product loss. Product sample systems shall be closed loop type. Consult with Industry Source 01 CHP Engineer for specific sample station requirements.

## 2.22 Vessel and Exchanger Piping

- 2.22.1 Coolers and condensers shall have inlet and outlet valves on the water sides with drains, vents, pressure gauges, and a thermal relief valve in between the block valves.

- 2.22.2 Piping shall be designed for the removal of bundles without additional temporary supports. Good access to exchanger flange bolting and pulling lugs on the sides of exchangers for rigging gear shall be provided. Adequate bundle removal space should be provided.
