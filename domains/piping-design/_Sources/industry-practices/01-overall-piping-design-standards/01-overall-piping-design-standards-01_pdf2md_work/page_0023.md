## 2.28 Water Piping

### 2.28.1 Utility Water

**2.28.1.1** Utility Water is general purpose water used throughout the refinery with a nominal pressure of 120 psig. The connection of utility water to process vessels or piping shall be made in accordance with drawing LA-000-0100-0200-003.

**2.28.1.2** The source of water to be used in water wash systems for fractionating towers or other process equipment as required by the plant design shall be confirmed with BP CHP Process Engineers.

### 2.28.2 Fire Water (Nominally 125 psig)

**2.28.2.1** Generally fire water shall be routed underground. Fire water underground headers shall be 8" minimum.

**2.28.2.2** Fire hydrants, hose reels, fire monitors, and hose racks shall be installed as directed by BP CHP. In general, fire hydrants shall be spaced 125' to 150' apart around the periphery of process units and 250' to 300' apart in tank farms and other open areas. Fire monitors will also be installed around the process units, readily accessible and strategically placed, to cover the critical areas of high fire potential.

**2.28.2.3** Fire water spray systems shall be provided for pumps and compressors in accordance with recommendations in API RP 2030 *Application of Fixed Water Spray Systems for Fire Protection in the Petroleum and Petrochemical Industries*. The spray systems shall be designed in accordance with drawings LA-000-0100-0200-015 and LA-000-0100-0200-016, and shall consist of spray nozzles located over the equipment to provide full spray coverage. Water flow shall be regulated by a clearly identified hand valve at a centralized water spray manifold located a safe distance from the equipment. The minimum line size shall be 1" (1-1/2" in the pipeways) but hose reels shall govern minimum size. Spray nozzles shall be minimum 1/2" nominal size, 316 stainless steel, Spraying Systems Co. type HHSJX SpiralJet with full cone spray pattern and extra-large free passage. Water piping supplying multiple spray nozzles shall be symmetrical in design to provide balanced flow to each nozzle. Consider the need for clean-out connections in the piping system and provide where prudent.

**2.28.2.4** Design of water deluge protection should generally provide a water rate of 0.5 GPM/sq.ft. over pumps and 0.3 GPM/sq.ft. over compressors as well as areas 2' around so as to also cool connecting piping, vessels, fin fan bays, and similar items. Where motors or drivers are expensive and/or difficult to replace, they should also be protected along with the pump or compressor.

### 2.28.3 Cooling Water

**2.28.3.1** Cooling water piping within the process unit areas shall be routed overhead. Connections 2" and smaller from the main header shall be valved at the branch take-off. The minimum size connection to the main header shall be 1". Connections shall take-off from the top of the main header.

**2.28.3.2** The supply and return headers for a process unit area shall be provided with both temperature and pressure indicators on the unit side of the battery limit valves.
