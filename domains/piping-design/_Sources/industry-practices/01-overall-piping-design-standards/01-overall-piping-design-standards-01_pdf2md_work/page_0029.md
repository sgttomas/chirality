4.4.1.3 The unthreaded points on the ends of stud bolts shall be either flat and chamfered, or rounded.

4.4.1.4 Bolting exposed to the process fluid shall be of a material at least as corrosion resistant as the flange material.

4.4.2 Unless otherwise approved by BP CHP, the design temperature for the bolts shall be at least the maximum allowable working temperature of the piping with no credit taken for cooling.

4.4.4 Flanged joint assembly, stud bolt lengths, and bolt tensioning shall be in accordance with BP CHP STP 50. The types of flanged joints and gaskets which require bolt torquing are also specified in STP 50, along with bolting procedures and torque values.

4.4.5 For flanged joints where the bolts are specified to be tightened with a torque tool, install sets of two lubricated hardened washers under each nut at only one end of the studs, on the side of the joint where the torque tool will be applied. The hardened washers shall be as specified in STP 50. Stud bolt lengths shall be increased to accommodate the thickness of the two washers. Washer thicknesses will vary based on stud diameter.

4.4.6 Bolting shall be assembled with a thread lubricant as specified in STP 50. The lubricant shall be applied to the threads, washers, and metal-contacting faces of the nuts.

4.4.7 For flanged joint situations where the stud bolt tables in STP 50 are not applicable, the length of stud bolts shall be designed for two threads to protrude through the full face nuts at each end. In accordance with ASTM A193, stud lengths are measured from first thread to first thread, and do not include the unthreaded points on the ends. The specified stud length may be rounded to the next higher commercial length, as long as the maximum thread protrusion criteria is not exceeded.

4.4.8 The acceptance criteria for installed stud bolts is a minimum protrusion of one full thread through the nuts, and a maximum protrusion of four threads. Stud bolts shall be installed centered in the flange joint with nearly equal lengths protruding at both ends. Longer studs with additional thread protrusion is allowed where bolt tensioning is specified or approved by the BP CHP Reliability Engineer.
