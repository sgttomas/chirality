## 2.7 Clearance and Accessibility

### 2.7.1 Overhead Clearances

The following minimum vertical clearance under overhead piping, pipe supports, instrument tubing, and instrument/electrical trays shall be maintained:

- a) Over railroads, top of rail to bottom of any obstruction – 22.5'
- b) Over plant roads – 17.5' minimum
- c) At pump row access way (under pipeway) to nearest obstruction - 12'
- d) Over pumps and turbines from high point of grade - 8'
- e) Overhead walkways, passageways, and platforms to nearest obstruction (except at dead ends) – 7.5'

### 2.7.2 Horizontal Clearance Guidelines

Equipment, structures, platforms, piping, and their supports shall be arranged to provide the following minimum horizontal clearances:

- a) Fired heaters to pump and other flammable-containing equipment (exclusive of compressors) - 50'
- b) Fired heaters to equipment handling nonflammable liquids - 25'
- c) Fired heaters to compressors handling flammable vapors - 100'
- d) Minimum aisle spacing between large compressors - 8' or rod pulling length
- e) Compressors handling flammable vapors to non-associated process pumps, vessels, or similar equipment - 25'
- f) Width of aisle way under the main pipe rack of process units – 12.5' (If pumps are mounted on the pipe rack perimeter, see k below.)
- g) Back of control panels - 4'
- h) Operating aisles - 4'
- i) At driver end of pumps where truck access is required - 10'
- j) At driver end of pump where truck access is not required - 5'
- k) Under pipeways, between driver end of pumps, where access is required - 18'
- l) At shell cover end of exchangers at grade, for access way - 4' (Exclusive of bundle pulling requirements)
