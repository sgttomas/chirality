## APPENDIX 3    FRICTION FACTORS AND ROUGHNESS FACTORS FOR FLOW IN PIPES

![Figure 3.1 Friction factors for Reynolds number up to 2x10⁵](figures/01-overall-piping-design-standards-08_p0098_fig01_friction-factors-reynolds-number.png)

EXAMPLE:

Determine the friction factor f for 300 carbon steel pipe with Re= 70,000.

1. Start "S" for 200 diameter and project vertically to intersect the "carbon steel/plus" line at "B"
2. From "B" project horizontally and follow the curve for ε/D = 0.0003 to point "C" to intersect the Re = 7 . 10⁴ line at "C"
3. From "C" project horizontally to intersect the "friction factor" line at "D"
4. Read the value of the friction factor = 0.0225
