## 10. INSULATION

### 10.1 THERMAL INSULATION

For thermal insulation of piping (including that for personnel protection), see Industry Source 08 Doc 30.46.00.31-Gen.

On top of insulated columns and tanks, and over piping, where applicable, grating should be provided to avoid damage to insulation.

In the following situations flanges shall not be insulated.

- flanges in hydrogen service;
- flanges in systems containing hydrocarbons above their auto-ignition temperature;
- if the 80% rule for bolting has been applied in accordance with ASME B31.3.

#### 10.1.2 **Insulated flanges**

If flanges with a design temperature above 400 °C are insulated, the bolt stress (after relaxation) shall remain within the required seating stress of the applied gasket type.

### 10.2 ACOUSTIC INSULATION

Industry Source 08 Doc 31.46.00.31-Gen. shall apply for acoustic insulation.
