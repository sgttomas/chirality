NOTES

1. Potential mechanisms are not exclusive, i.e. if there is more than one mechanism active, the materials and PWHT requirements may have to be combined to address the severity of more than one environment. For example, the fractionator overheads in a Fluidized Catalytic Cracking Unit (FCCU) may have a high severity for hydrogen blistering, HIC, SOHIC and carbonate cracking and in such a case the requirements for materials and PWHT shall be combined.

2. For "Moderate Sour" service, the piping classes shall satisfy the following:

   - seamless carbon steel pipe (ASTM A 106 grade B or ASTM A 333 grade 6);
   - seamless carbon steel fittings (ASTM A 234-WPB or ASTM A 420 WPL6);
   - if welded carbon steel pipe and fittings are used they shall be made from HIC-tested plate;
   - valves complying with NACE MR0103;
   - valves with AISI 316 + stellite trim;
   - normal bolting (ASTM A 193 B7 and ASTM A 194-2H); flanges shall not be insulated.

3. For "High Sour" service, the piping classes are identified by the word "Sour" in the title box. These piping classes shall satisfy the following:

   - seamless carbon steel pipe and fittings with low sulphur content (0.010% maximum);
   - if welded carbon steel pipe and fittings are used they shall be made from HIC-tested plate;
   - valves complying with NACE MR0103;
   - valves with AISI 316 + stellite trim;
   - sour bolting (ASTM A 193-B7M and ASTM A 194-2HM).

   Additional mitigation measures, such as injection of polysulphide or inhibitors, may be required; the materials engineer of the Principal shall be consulted.

4. PWHT indicates a requirement for postweld heat treatment of all shop and field piping welds. All welded piping products require PWHT or normalisation of the weld seam during manufacturing.
