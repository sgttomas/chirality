## 3.8 DISTANCE BETWEEN PIPES

The minimum distance between pipes or the insulation of pipes in pipe tracks and trenches and on pipe racks shall be 75 mm.

The minimum distance between a flange and a pipe or the insulation of a pipe in pipe tracks and trenches and on pipe racks shall be 30 mm.

Where required, the distance between pipes shall be increased to allow for movements caused by thermal expansion.

The distance between the insulation of a low-temperature pipe and any other object shall be at least 100 mm.

The distance between pipes shall allow for the turning of a spectacle blind, if present.
