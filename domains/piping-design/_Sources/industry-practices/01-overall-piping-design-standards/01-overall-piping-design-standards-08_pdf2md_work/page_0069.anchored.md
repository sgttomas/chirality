6. Repeat steps 2, 3 and 4 to 100 % of the required bolt stress.

The threads shall be clean and in good condition. The application of lubricants will have a substantial impact on the torque applied. The lubrication properties vary from one lubricant to the other and shall be taken into account when torque is applied. Hydraulic bolt tensioning provides more uniform bolt stress and eliminates variations due to different lubricants.

Gaskets used for the installation of orifice assemblies and between flanges located within the required straight length of pipe upstream and downstream of these assemblies shall not protrude into the free area of the pipe.

Gaskets shall never be re-used, since not enough resilience is left in the gasket material to give a leak-proof joint when compressed a second time.

### 8.3.1.5 Threaded joints

Threaded joints shall not be used in process piping.

Threaded steam traps may be used if downstream of a piping valve.

Threaded joints may be used only in galvanised piping (e.g. for water systems) DN 50 and smaller. The minimum wall thickness for these shall be Schedule 80.
