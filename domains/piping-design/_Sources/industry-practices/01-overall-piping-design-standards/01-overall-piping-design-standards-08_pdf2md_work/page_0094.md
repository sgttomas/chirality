## APPENDIX 1 PRELIMINARY SIZING OF PIPES CONTAINING LIQUID (continued)

[FIGURE: Figure 1.2 Flow rate 10 m³/h to 1 000 m³/h]

Select the pipe size for suction and discharge lines within the areas indicated above. If more than one selection is possible, use smaller size for lines up to 100 m length, use larger size for lines over 100 m in length.

The lines for suction and discharge lines have been based on the pressure drop values per 100 m pipe length for water at 20°C as indicated in Appendix 1. The pressure drop in the suction line area is min: 0.026 bar — max: 0.085 bar and in the discharge line area min: 0.189 bar — max: 0.625 bar.
