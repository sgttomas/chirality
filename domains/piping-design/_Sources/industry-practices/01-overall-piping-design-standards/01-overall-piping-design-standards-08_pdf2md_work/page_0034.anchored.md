## 3.17 ORIFICE FLANGES AND ORIFICE METER RUNS

Orifice flanges, raised face, with flange tappings shall be in accordance with S 38.130.

Orifice flanges, raised face, with corner tappings shall be in accordance with S 38.131.

Orifice meter run sizes DN 50 up to DN 600 shall be in accordance with Industry Source 08 Doc 61.38.10.10-Gen.

Orifice meter run sizes DN 15, DN 20, DN 25 and DN 40 shall be in accordance with S 38.134.

Typical bracings for small bore branches of piping (e.g. orifice instrument connection) shall be in accordance with S 44.031.

Material and components for instrument connections are given in the relevant piping classes. See Industry Source 08 Doc 61.38.10.10-Gen. for site fabrication of orifice meter runs.
