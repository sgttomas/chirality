### 2.3.5 Two Phase Flow

Extra precautions shall be taken for two phase flow. Particularly if the flow pattern is intermittent (slug or plug flow), the piping system will be subjected to high excitation forces and these flow regimes shall be avoided whenever possible. These flow regimes can be prevented or reduced by proper location of pressure control valves. The graphs in DEP 31.22.05.11-Gen. should be used to determine what type of flow pattern will occur, but they are only a rough indication. Furthermore the actual conditions in the plant could largely deviate from the design conditions. This shall be taken into account and in case of doubt the worst case flow pattern shall be taken (3.5).

For two-phase flow in flare pipes see DEP 80.45.10.10-Gen.

### 2.3.6 Straight length requirements for flow meters

Straight length requirements for flow meters shall be in accordance with DEP 32.31.00.32-Gen.
