## APPENDIX 14    MINIMUM REQUIRED WALL THICKNESS

This Appendix specifies the minimum required wall thickness for API 5L grade B and ASTM A 106 grade B pipe in the following pipe sizes:

DN 25, DN 40, DN 50, DN 80, DN 100, DN 150, DN 200, DN 250, DN 300, DN 350, DN 400, DN 450, DN 500 and DN 600.

**CAUTION:** *THIS APPENDIX SHALL NOT BE USED IF STRESS CORROSION CRACKING IS POSSIBLE DUE TO THE FLUID SERVICE OR THE ENVIRONMENT. THE INCREASED STRESSES DUE TO WALL THINNING COULD EXCEED THE THRESHOLD VALUE AND INITIATE STRESS CORROSION CRACKING.*

Thicknesses below those specified in this Appendix shall not be permitted without further analysis (which may permit localised thinner areas).

The thicknesses specified in this Appendix are not suitable in situations where additional stresses may be expected on the piping system, e.g. stresses caused by thermal expansion, settling or wind loads. In situations where the pipe is directly resting on the supporting structure, unacceptably high stresses and/or deformation may occur in the pipe at the support.

Future corrosion allowance shall be taken into account when deciding to reject or accept pipes using this Appendix.

This minimum required wall thickness is given for different pressure/temperature ratings and are based on the following:

- Material strength and internal pressure calculations in accordance with ASME B31.3. In these calculations a quality factor E = 0.85 has been applied. The wall thicknesses shown in *normal type* font are based on *mechanical strength*. The wall thicknesses shown in ***bold italics*** are based on *internal pressure*.

- Mechanical strength calculations are based on the maximum permitted span as defined in Appendix 11. In order to simulate external forces, an additional load in the middle of the free span has been taken into account. The free span and load used in the calculations is shown below each table.
