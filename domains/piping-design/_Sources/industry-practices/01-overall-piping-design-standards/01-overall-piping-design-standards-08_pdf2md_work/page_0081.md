#### 8.8.4.8 Solids sluicing valves

Special through-conduit gate valves may be used for solids sluicing duties (gravity flow).

#### 8.8.4.9 Isolators and dampers

Various valve types (e.g. flap, guillotine, louvre) are available for flue ducts and gas turbine exhausts. These valves are for low pressures and their tightness ranges from 95% to 100%. They are suitable for both shut-off service and regulating service.

### 8.8.5 Special properties of valves

#### 8.8.5.1 Tight Shut-Off (TSO) requirements

Tight shut-off valves shall be marked "TSO" on the PEFS.

Valves are considered TSO if they comply with seat leakage rates A or B according to ISO 5208 and BS 6755-1, or to class V or VI in accordance with IEC 60534-4.

TSO valves are more expensive than valves satisfying standard leakage requirements and shall only be used if standard seat leakage cannot be tolerated.

Control valves shall not be considered TSO because:
- Certain types of control valves (e.g. low noise valves) cannot, as standard, be manufactured to meet TSO requirements.
- Control valves will lose their TSO characteristics quickly after being used in operation.

A practical solution in such cases is to install a normal control valve in combination with a TSO valve.

#### 8.8.5.2 Valves with fire safe design or fire tested design requirements

##### 8.8.5.2.1 General

Fire safe properties of valves are determined by the materials and construction of the valves.

Valves with a fire tested design are those which have successfully passed prototype fire testing.

Fire safe valves are those which are individually capable of passing fire tests.

Valves shall be fire tested and certified in accordance with ISO 10497, BS 6755 part 2 API 607 or API 6FA and are subject to approval by the Principal.

Valves are considered fire safe if, after being engulfed in flames of a certain intensity for a certain period of time:
- the seat leakage of the valve does not exceed a set requirement;
- the external leak rate (e.g. via the valve spindle or valve body joints) remains within specified limits.

##### 8.8.5.2.2 Valves with fire safe design

Valves with a fire safe design shall be certified to API 607, ISO 10497, BS 6755-2 and API 6FA and API 6FB. For requirements for fire safe valves see DEP 80.47.10.30-Gen.

NOTE: Standards API 607, API 6FA, BS 6755-2 and ISO 10497 do not address the capabilities of the valve and connections. End connections shall be fire tested in accordance with API 6FB.

If a fire safe metal seated butterfly valve or dual plate check valve is required, valves with (dual) flanged ends or, for the larger sizes, butt welded ends shall be used. Alternatively, wafer lug type or single flanged valves may be considered since their long bolts are not exposed.

##### 8.8.5.2.3 Valves with a fire tested design

Soft seated quarter turn valves shall have a secondary or primary metal sealing arrangement.
