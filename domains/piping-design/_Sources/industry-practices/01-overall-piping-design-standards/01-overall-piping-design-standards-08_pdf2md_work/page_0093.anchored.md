## APPENDIX 1 PRELIMINARY SIZING OF PIPES CONTAINING LIQUID

![Figure 1.1 Flow Rate 0.1 m³/h to 10 m³/h](figures/01-overall-piping-design-standards-08_p0093_fig01_figure-1-1-flow-rate-0-1-to-10-m3-h.png)

Select the pipe size for suction and discharge lines within the zones indicated above. If more than any selection is possible, use smaller size for lines up to 100 m length; use larger size for lines over 100 m in length.

The areas for suction and discharge lines have been based on the pressure drop values per 100 m pipe length for water at 20°C as indicated in Appendix 1. The pressure drop in the suction line area is min. 0.034 bar – max. 0.065 bar and in the discharge line area min. 0.144 bar – max. 0.927 bar.
