## 8.6 FLANGES

Flanges shall be raised-face in accordance with ASME B16.5 or calculated in accordance with ASME VIII. Flanges over DN 600 shall be in accordance with ASME B16.47 series A or B. The use of other types of flanges is subject to the approval of the Principal.

The flange facing finish shall be in accordance with ASME B16.5 or ASME B16.47, as applicable.

Flange bolt holes shall straddle the centre lines.

If a flat face flange is required (e.g. GRE piping in ASME rating class 150) the counterflange shall also have a flat face in accordance with ASME B16.5. Flat face flanges shall be provided with full-face gaskets.

For bolt material and temperature limitations reference is made to (8.3.1.4) and DEP 30.10.02.11-Gen.

For bolt tensioning, see (3.11). and for bolt tensioning equipment, see DEP 70.08.10.11-Gen.

Bolted joints not classified as flanges, e.g. V-clamps and groove joints, may be used only with the approval of the Principal.
