Notes:

1. Spans are based on straight pipe, other configurations shall be multiplied by a shape factor (see sketch below).
2. Free draining pipes with a slope less than 1.5 mm/m require an additional check of the span.
3. Spans are limited by longitudinal bending stress.
4. The weight of insulation and sheeting is based on insulation thickness varying from 70 mm for DN 25 to 200 mm for DN 600 and a density of 190 kg/m³.
