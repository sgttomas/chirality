- the use of tapered thermowells (standard design);

- placing the thermowell at a point with lower fluid velocity;

- not installing thermowells downstream of flashing or cavitating valves or other restrictions where the flow is turbulent.

Flanged thermowells should normally be selected. Welding thermowells according to S 38.112 shall only be installed if, due to high velocity and density of the fluid, the bending loads are too high for flanged thermowells or if they may be subject to vortex induced vibration (see above).
