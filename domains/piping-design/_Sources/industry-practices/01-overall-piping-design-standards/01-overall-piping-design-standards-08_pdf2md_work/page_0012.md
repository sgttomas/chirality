## 3. PIPING BASICS

3.1 GENERAL

Piping shall be routed so that the optimum piping layout is achieved in terms of process requirements, ergonomics, operation, inspection and maintenance. Having considered these factors, the number of flanges, fittings, valves and welds shall be minimised.

The selection of piping construction materials is covered in the piping classes, which refer to the MESC system to specify the piping components. The following DEPs and MESCs shall apply:

DEP 31.38.01.10-Gen., DEP 31.38.01.12-Gen., and DEP 31.38.01.15-Gen.

MESC Group 74 (line pipe); MESC Group 76 (fittings and flanges); MESC Group 77 (valves); MESC Group 81 (bolting) and MESC Group 85 (gaskets and packing).

In order to have fixed working documents during a project and reference documents during the lifetime of an installation, a specification for piping systems shall be compiled for each project (see DEP 31.38.01.21-Gen.). Project modifications to piping classes shall be kept to a minimum.

For the minimum required wall thickness of carbon steel pipes see Appendix 14.

Protective steam heating of piping systems shall comply with DEP 31.38.30.11-Gen.

Pipe supports shall be in accordance with DEP 31.38.01.29-Gen.

Spans of straight pipe shall be in accordance with Appendix 11.
