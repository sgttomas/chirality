## 3.13 DRAIN AND VENT CONNECTIONS

Test drain and vent connections shall be installed, without valves, at all low and high points in piping. These connections shall not be shown on the PEFS.

If required for operation/maintenance, valved drain connections shall be installed at low points, and valved vent connections at high points in piping systems. These connections shall be shown on the PEFS. For drain and vent provisions on equipment, see (4). Valved vents and drains to atmosphere shall satisfy the double barrier concept. In this context, a blank or cap on an outlet to atmosphere counts as one barrier. This means that a blank or cap on a single valve should only be removed after depressurising. Where process requirements demand a quick outlet to atmosphere, the double barrier shall be obtained by installing two valves in series. If the effluent will flash and cause sub-zero temperatures, the distance between the two valves shall be at least 600 mm and the last valve shall be of the spring loaded, self-closing type. The number of vent and drain connections with valves shall be minimised.

Drain and vent arrangements are specified in the piping classes and are shown in Industry Source 08 Doc 31.38.01.10-Gen.

Vents and drains shall be as short as possible. If long connections are required (e.g. due to thick insulation on the main pipe), supports/bracings and/or a larger branch size shall be applied. See Standard Drawing S 44.030.

All drain and vent points shall be closed with a blind flange or a cap.

Drains shall be located so that there is sufficient free space underneath to install temporary facilities to discharge the drained liquid.
