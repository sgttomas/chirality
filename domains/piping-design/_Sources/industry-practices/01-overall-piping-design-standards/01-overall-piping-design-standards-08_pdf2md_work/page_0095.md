Select the pipe size for suction and discharge lines within the ranges indicated above. If more than one selection is possible, use smaller size for lines up to 100 m length; use larger size for lines over 100 m length.

The ranges for suction and discharge lines have been based on the pressure drop values per 100 m pipe length for water of 20°C as indicated in Appendix 1. The pressure drop in the suction line area min. 0.034 bar — max. 0.069 bar and in the discharge line area min. 0.144 bar — max. 0.627 bar.
