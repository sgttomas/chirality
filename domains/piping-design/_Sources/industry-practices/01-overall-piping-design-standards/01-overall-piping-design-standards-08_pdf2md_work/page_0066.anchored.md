#### 8.2.3.3 Rubber-lined piping

Rubber-lined piping shall be in accordance with Industry Source 08 Doc 30.48.60.10-Gen.

#### 8.2.3.4 Glass-lined piping

Glass-lined piping shall be in accordance with Industry Source 08 Doc 30.48.70.30-Gen.

#### 8.2.3.5 Cement-lined piping

Cement-lined piping shall be in accordance with Industry Source 08 Doc 30.48.30.31-Gen.
