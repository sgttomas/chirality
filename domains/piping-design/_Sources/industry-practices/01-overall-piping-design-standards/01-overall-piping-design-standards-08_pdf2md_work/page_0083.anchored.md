## 9. INSPECTION AND TESTING

### 9.1 SHOP-FABRICATED OR MANUFACTURER-SUPPLIED PIPING

#### 9.1.1 Metallic piping

Shop-fabricated metallic piping shall be inspected and tested in accordance with ASME B31.3 and Industry Source 08 Doc 31.38.01.31-Gen. Metallic piping materials not covered by Industry Source 08 Doc 31.38.01.31-Gen. shall be inspected and tested as specified by the Principal.

If expansion joints are installed in the horizontal position in pipes operating below 0 °C, and if the expansion joints cannot be removed for the hydrostatic test, the pipes may have to be tested Industry Source 07 Doc umatically instead of hydrostatically in order to avoid ice forming in the convolutions of the bellows. See Industry Source 08 Doc 61.10.08.11-Gen. For testing of expansion joints, see also ASME B31.3.

#### 9.1.2 Lined piping

Lined piping shall be inspected and tested in accordance with the Manufacturer's test and inspection procedures, which shall be subject to the approval of the Principal. Industry Source 08 Doc 30.48.00.31-Gen., Industry Source 08 Doc 30.48.30.31-Gen. and Industry Source 08 Doc 30.48.60.10-Gen. shall also apply.

#### 9.1.3 Non-metallic piping

Polyethylene (PE), polypropylene (PP), polyvinylchloride (PVC), glass-fibre reinforced epoxy (GRE) and glass-fibre reinforced polyester (GRP) piping shall be inspected and tested as specified in the relevant DEP, see (8.2.2). Other non-metallic piping shall be inspected and tested in accordance with the Manufacturer's test and inspection procedures, which shall be subject to the approval of the Principal.

### 9.2 FIELD-FABRICATED PIPING

#### 9.2.1 Metallic piping

Field-fabricated metallic piping shall be inspected and tested in accordance with ASME B31.3 and Industry Source 08 Doc 31.38.01.31-Gen. For field inspection prior to commissioning, see Industry Source 08 Doc 61.10.08.11-Gen.

### 9.3 VALVE INSPECTION

Valves shall be inspected when they arrive on site to verify that the following is in accordance with the purchase order:

- number, type, size, bore, ASME rating class and material;
- face-to-face dimension;
- flange-face finish for flanged valves;
- weld preparation for butt welded valves;
- MESC number and die stamp on the flange rim;
- heat number on the body/bonnet;
- nameplate and label with purchase order/indent number, MESC number, item, etc.;
- stem packing material;
- bonnet gasket material;
- arrow for flow direction or marking for cavity relief side, if applicable;
- general condition of the valve (e.g. no parts stained or corroded).

Review of documents should include:

- inspection certificates (e.g. materials of pressure-containing parts, non-destructive examination of body and welds, hydrostatic tests);
- operating/maintenance instructions, if required.
