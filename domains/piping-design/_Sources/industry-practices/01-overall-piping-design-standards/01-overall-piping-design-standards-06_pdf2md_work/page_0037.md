# 3 Valves

The factor involved in valve selection and provides the means of determining the most suitable type (or types) of block and check valves for a particular application given basic information about the service condition. Valve manufacturers should always be asked to confirm suitability of a particular type for the required service and specialist advice should be sought in the more complicated cases.

Factors Affecting Valve Selection

- Required function
- Service conditions
- Fluid type and condition
- fluid characteristic
- Frequency of operation
- Isolation requirements
- Maintenance requirements
- Environmental considerations
- Experience in comparable conditions
- Weight and size
- Cost

This section provides guidance and recommendations for the application of block valves, check valves, pressure relief, and throttling valves. Although factors such as elastomers, materials, maintenance, and availability must be considered when choosing any valve, those areas will be covered in other sections. Ball and butterfly valves application shall be limited to the pressure-temperature ratings given by the valve manufacturer. Exceeding these values will damage the valve soft seats.

## 3.1 Block Valves

Abrasive service is defined as production crude and produced water containing sand and sludge. Valves in these services shall include valves in the crude oil pipeline.
