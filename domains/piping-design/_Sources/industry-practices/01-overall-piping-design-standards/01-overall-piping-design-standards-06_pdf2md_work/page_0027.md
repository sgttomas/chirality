- Eliminate small diameter branch connections and overhang mass on all compressors, pumps suction and discharge lines in close proximity to source of vibration.

Where such conditions could exist, consideration should be given to the use of pulsation dampening designs and devices.

## 2.11 Branch Connections

### 2.11.1 Selection Guidelines

All branch connections should be performed using materials of compatible Yield Strengths.

Whenever any branch connection is being installed in previously commissioned services, it is imperative that the structural integrity of the piping be maintained. Depending on the type and size and weight of the branch connection, this will usually entail an analysis of the piping by a method such as "Auto-Pipe" or "Caesar II" to determine the need for additional structural reinforcement (supports, hangers, increased run pipe wall thickness, etc.).

The appropriate fitting to use per branch connection will usually be determined by the type of fabrication (new construction, online, or in-service) and the respective process and service conditions (i.e., corrosive/noncorrosive, instrumentation fitting, vent or drain connection, process thru-put branch, etc.).
