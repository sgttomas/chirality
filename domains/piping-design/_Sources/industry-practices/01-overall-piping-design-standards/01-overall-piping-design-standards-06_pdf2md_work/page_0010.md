## 2 Piping Design Criteria

### 2.1 General Design Considerations

It is the responsibility of the Contractor or other 3rd parties to perform all work in a manner consistent with all applicable codes on health and safety regulations.

P&ID's should be the basis for piping design, and should show piping schematics, line numbers, process requirements, equipment, valves, piping specialties, controls, and instruments. Changes to these diagrams after they are issued for design should have prior approval of the Company.

#### 2.1.1 Severe Cyclic Service

The piping analysis shall consider the effects of pressure cycling, thermal cycling, and other cyclic loading (e.g., displacement, wind sway, vibration etc.). The stress analyst shall determine the number of cycles a piping system shall be designed for.

Severe cyclic conditions occur when the Displacement Stress (or Operating Stress under B31.3 Appendix P) exceeds 80% of the Allowable Stress and the number of cycles (N) exceeds 7000 (equivalent number of full displacement cycles during the expected service life of the piping system). It shall be noted by the stress analyst that per B31.3, if the piping is under severe cyclic conditions, then there are additional Code requirements concerning the piping materials, types of components allowed, construction, examination, and safeguarding.
