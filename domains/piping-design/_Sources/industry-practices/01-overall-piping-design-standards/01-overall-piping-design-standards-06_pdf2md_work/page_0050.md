## 3.4 Valve Interchangeability

### 3.4.1 Valve Face to Face Dimension

Valve manufacturer's figure numbers may have to be specified in the piping line classes to denote specific types of valves for the required services. The valve code numbers are to be shown on the P&IDs. The piping designers will use the face to face dimension of the valve specified in the piping line class for dimensional purposes.

When a Manufacturer's figure number is specified in a line class, it does not mean that the particular make of valve has to be purchased. A valve of the same type and quality with the same face to face dimension can be purchased.

Manufacturing standards have been issued for gate, globe, and check valves with standardized face-to-face dimensions. The same is true with trunnion mounted ball valves. Lately, manufacturing standards covering face-to-face dimensions have been issued for floating ball type valves and high performance butterfly valves. However, in many cases the face to face dimensions vary from one manufacturer to another on these types of valves.

### 3.4.2 Specifying Type of Valve

At the time the valves are ready for purchase, the piping design drawings will have been issued for design and/or fabrication. To change valve face-to-face dimensions at this point will be costly, time-consuming, and will cause delay of the project. It is therefore necessary to specify valves which are the most economical, perform well in service, are most available, and have common face-to-face dimensions with other valve manufacturers to provide maximum interchangeability. Equivalent valves should be substituted only if the face-to-face dimensions are the same.

### 3.4.3 Valves for Non-Abrasive Services

#### *3.4.3.1    150-lbs and 300-lbs Valves*

- Specify floating ball type valves for sizes up to 6 inches
- Specify trunnion mounted ball type valves for sizes 8 inches and larger.

#### *3.4.3.2    600-lbs and Higher Reduced and Full Port Valves*

- Specify trunnion mounted ball type valves in all sizes.

Note: In nonabrasive services, ball valves, pressure balanced plug valves, and Orbit® valves may be substituted for each other.
