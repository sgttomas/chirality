Flush bushings shall not be used in any service condition (flowing or non-flowing).

Hex head bushings of one-size reduction shall NOT be used in any service condition. According to ANSI/ASME B16.11, hex head bushings of one-size reduction should NOT be used in services wherein they might be subject to harmful loads and forces other than internal pressure.

In the smaller bushing sizes, the hex head area may be too thin to fit a full size wrench.

During assembly, ensure that only one joint is tightened at a time and that each are correctly fitted with a least one full thread showing.

### 4.3.3 Reducers

Eccentric reducers, arranged flat-on-bottom, should be used for size changes in horizontal lines, except at pump suction connections, where they should be arranged flat-on-top.

## 4.4 Flanges

Large diameter flanges (26-inch and larger) shall be according to ASME B16.47. Series A flanges are preferred where space permits.

Use steel flat face flanges and full-face gaskets against cast iron flanged equipment.

Slip-on reducing flanges may be used only where shown in the line classes, for lines with flow into equipment, when pressure drop or turbulence is not a factor.

Expander flanges may be used in lieu of a weld reducer and flange if space requirements dictate, but should be specifically noted as such on the drawings.
