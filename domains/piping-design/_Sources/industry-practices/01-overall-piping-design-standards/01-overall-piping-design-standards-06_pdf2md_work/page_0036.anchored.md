## 2.17 Thermowells

Thermowells process connection shall be heavy wall, and typically will be threaded, or flanged type per the applicable piping class specification. The immersion length ("U") shall be defined by instrument engineer for the required minimum insertion depth.

Thermowells shall be constructed from drilled barstock, tapered and the material shall be 316 SS minimum. Refer to Instrumentation Temperature Measurement Specification (Industry Source 03 Doc ICSPE-000008) for thermowells specification and recommendation.

## 2.18 Tie-In Welding

For ASME B31.3, B31.4, and B31.8 tie-in and closure welds, the following requirements apply:

Unless otherwise modified, in this section "test" shall mean a hydrostatic pressure test in accordance with the applicable code.

Welds may be made on a piping system where all piping components have been previously tested. Such welds are considered tie-in or closure welds, for which pressure testing may be waived, providing:

- Up to two welds shall be considered tie-in welds, when making modifications to previously tested spools, where no piping components, except those which have been previously tested, are added.
- Up to two field fit-up welds on a previously tested flanged spool shall be considered tie-in welds, or
- More than two non-tested welds among separate spools in a previously tested system must have approval by the Company or their design representative to be defined as tie-in welds.

The use of O-let to run pipe welds as tie-in welds must be approved by the Company or design representative. These welds shall be subject to the tie-in weld inspection stipulations described below.

All piping components shall be tested; however, when the tie-in weld is a weld to a flange or an O-let, NDT by means of RT, UT, PT, or MT of the flange or O-let may be performed in lieu of testing with the approval of the Company or design representative. The weld shall be subject to the tie-in weld inspection stipulations described below.

All tie-in welds shall receive the following:

- For ASME B31.3 piping, in-process weld examination as defined in ASME B31.3 Section 344.7. For ASME B31.4 and B31.8 piping, use of in-process inspection shall be at the discretion of the Inspector or the Engineer.
