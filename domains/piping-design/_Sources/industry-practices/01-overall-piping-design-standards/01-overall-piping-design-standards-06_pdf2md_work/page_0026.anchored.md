- Main structural supports should not be welded to the pipe. This does not include pipe base supports as allowed by codes.

- Use angle or channel to clamp to the pipe. Wide flange may be used for noncorrosive onshore locations.

- Keep unused thread protrusion to a minimum.

- Pipe support design should be simple to facilitate painting and should not have pockets that may collect water.

- Pipe supports should be designed to allow removal of pipe.

## 2.7 Pipe Structural Supports – Package Equipment

Piping supports should be furnished on packaged equipment skids so that all pipes can be run parallel or perpendicular to the skid. Piping supports and bracing should be of sufficient strength to support stresses developed when the skid is lifted and transported. Maximum support spacing on skids should be 8 feet.

Consideration should be given to piping flexibility on packaged equipment due to weight and stiffness caused by short runs and close connections of flanges, valves, and other piping components.

Pipe support structures on a skid may be combined with support structures for other services such as instrumentation lines and electrical conduit to promote an orderly layout on the skid.

## 2.8 Pipe Support – Corrosive Environments

Pipe supports to be erected in highly corrosive environments should be designed to maintain the integrity of structural coatings and minimize drilling of structural members. Consideration should be given to locating secondary supports which provide adjustable locations for pipe hangers over the area where field erected supports will be needed.

Engineering of the metallurgy and methodology of welded supports to alloy piping shall have Company approval.

All engineered pipe support components should conform to MSS SP-58.

## 2.9 Vibration

Precaution should be observed in the design of piping connected to equipment producing harmonic effects or pulsations, excessive vibrations, improper operation, or damage to equipment and instruments.

- Evaluate system for flow-induced vibration at branches.

- Evaluate dynamic excitation from low frequency rotating equipment.

- Eliminate coincidences of piping system acoustical and mechanical resonances.
