## 2.13 Isolation Gaskets

### 2.13.1 Insulating Kits

Flange isolation kits are used to electrically isolate flanged connections and still maintain sealing integrity. They are used for various reasons:

- To prevent galvanic corrosion when two different materials are connected by a flanged connection.
- To isolate wells from induced currents in piping or from other wells, causing corrosion of the casing.

### 2.13.2 Dissimilar Metal Connection in a Flanged Connection

When a flange connection is made with two dissimilar materials and the solution being contained is an electrolyte, an isolation kit should be installed. Isolation kits are not required in non-electrolyte environments such as dry crude, dry gas, lean TEG, methanol, and most hydrocarbon based chemicals.

### 2.13.3 Wellhead Isolation

Isolation kits may be installed between the piping and the wellhead if specifically required by the Corrosion Engineer. The electrical connection between the pipeline and the tree should not be shorted by a direct connection between the lines and the well house or via the hydraulic safety system connections. When there is electrical power in a well house, care should be taken to keep the heater and electrical ground connections isolated from the tree and process lines.

### 2.13.4 For Services That Do Not Contain CO₂

The kit consists of a NEMA LI 1 G10 epoxy glass laminated 316 SS core ring type gasket with a Viton® GLT seal ring (300 °F maximum design temperature), a NEMA G10 washer, and a zinc-plated, steel back-up washer for each nut, plus a Mylar sleeve for each bolt, Pikotek VCS, or approved equivalent.

### 2.13.5 For All Services Containing CO₂

The kit consists of a NEMA LI 1 G10 epoxy glass laminated 316 SS core ring type gasket with a spring-energized Teflon® seal ring (300 °F maximum design temperature), a NEMA G10 washer, and a zinc-plated, steel back-up washer for each nut, plus a Mylar sleeve for each bolt, Pikotek VCS, or approved equivalent.
