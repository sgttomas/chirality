## 2.11.2 Minimum Wall Thickness for Hot Topping

As a general rule of thumb and for structural purposes, (i.e., regardless of hot tap welding requirements for wall thickness minimums), the run (header) piping should be a minimum of ¼-inch wall thickness for at least three (3) run pipe diameters upstream and downstream of branch connections. This does not apply to inline butt-weld branches which match existing walls. Any structural requirements in addition to or instead of this should be identified by pipe stress analysis.

## 2.11.3 Group 1: Integrally Reinforced Branch Connections of O-lets

### 2.11.3.1 Conventional O-lets

Conventional o-lets are groove and fillet weld-on type of fittings as opposed to the insert or butt-weld type (e.g., Vesselolet®, Sweepolet®, insert Weld-o-let®). These types of fittings are used but limited for instrument, vent, and drain connections and only on approved exception basis are used for larger branch sizes required for process flow rate through-put.
