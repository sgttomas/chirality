Operational vents, sample points, and drains which may discharge flammable fluids shall be minimized and shall not be less than 50 feet minimum from possible source of ignition (e.g., hot lines, furnace, and rotating machinery).

Drainage line for hazardous or valuable process chemical shall be accessible for inspection and maintenance. Unless otherwise specified by Company, drainage from such equipment shall be permanently piped to a sump tank provided with means of emptying. Where hard pipe cannot be provided, consider block and throttling valve for high pressure drains.

Where a significant release of H₂S, other toxic gas, or large volumes of flammable vapor could occur, vents and drains shall be piped to closed disposal system.

## 2.14.2 Instrument Connections

Instrument connection on piping should be as specified in the individual line classes.

Where flanged temperature connections are specified in Line Classes, a one-piece Thermalets® should be used for connection sizes below 2-inch nominal diameter.

When flanged Vesselets® or flanged pipets (flangets) are used as Thermalets® alternatives, detailed dimensional checking should be performed to confirm that there will be no fit-up interference of the flanged thermowells to companion flange at the flange faces to hubs radii. These fittings should normally be 2-inch nominal diameter, with 1½-inch nominal diameter allowable with proper fit-up verification.
