### 3.2.3 Swing Check Valves

- Recommended for general service and most often used in sizes over 2 inches.
- Works best where flow seldom changes direction; chattering caused by frequent flow reversals can be a problem. Also, rapid closing can cause severe hydraulic hammer.
- In high velocity applications, can create severe turbulence that may cause erosion of downstream piping.
- External lever arms are not recommended because of potential for seal leakage problems.

### 3.2.4 Lift Check Valves

- Recommended for steam, gas, or vapor service.
- Preferable for services having irregular or frequent flow reversals because they have no tendency to slam shut. In fact, spring assist is recommended where rapid closing is a must.

### 3.2.5 Piston Check Valves

- This is another version of the lift check, but is available with floating piston.
- The floating piston valve is specifically designed for pulsating flow and is widely applied in difficult services such as pump and compressor discharge.

### 3.2.6 Ball Check Valves

Recommended for high viscosity fluids and where scale formation and sediment buildup is possible.
