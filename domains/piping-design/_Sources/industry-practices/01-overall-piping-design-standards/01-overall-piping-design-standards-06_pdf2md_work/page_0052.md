All sample connections should have a primary block valve at the line or equipment. The sample valve should be a needle valve per the applicable secondary piping line class. All installations should be made as compact as possible to minimize overhung mass.

All pressure relief valves except for thermal relief, should have full-port block valves CSO, upstream and downstream with a port area at least as great as the inlet or outlet bore of the PSV. Block valves or rupture disks should be full bore.
