Vessel or tanks at which piping terminates should be considered as rigid for initial piping analysis. If vessel or pipe stresses thus obtained are unacceptably high, pipe rerouting or reconfiguring and reanalysis may consider restraint flexibility. The increase in allowable design stress permitted for occasional vibration above design condition shall not be used for flexibility analysis.

Expansion of piping or associated equipment should be accommodated wherever possible by the inherent flexibility of the piping. If necessary, the route of the piping should be modified or expansion loops should be incorporated to obtain sufficient flexibility. Only where it is impractical to increase flexibility sufficiently to reduce the stress range or anchor loads to acceptable levels bellows expansion joints may be considered. If bellows expansion joints are proposed, the approval of the Company shall be obtained before proceeding.

Piping should be adequately flexible for the installation and removal of spectacle or slip blinds where required. Permanently installed blinds should be as indicated on the P&IDs. Flanged connections at equipment should be capable of accepting maintenance slip blinds.
