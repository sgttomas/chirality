### 3.4.4 Valves for Abrasive Service

Pressure balanced plug valves should be specified for all sizes. Occasionally, specialty valves, such as choke valves or abrasive service ball valves, are suitable selections for this service, depending upon service conditions.

## 3.5 Valve Procurement

Valve Procurement Specification will be developed during FEED. All valves should be as specified on the applicable valve data sheet, except as noted in the line classes.

# 4 Specific Items Notes

The following notes apply to all material items listed in the individual line classes.

## 4.1 Valves

Check valves at compressors and large pumps discharge should be investigated for the need of special non-slam, slow-closing check valves. Types of check valve shall be as indicated in the P&ID.

Valves 1½ inches and smaller, when used against flanged nozzles on equipment, should have flanged ends and a rating equal to or greater than that of the nozzle connection.

Butterfly valves should not be bolted directly against water check valves due to clearance problems.

Valves should have gear operators provided according to Manufacturer's standard or starting with the nominal sizes noted on Table 7. Gear operators on rising-stem type valves should be of the enclosed bevel gear type. Unless otherwise indicated, pinion shafts should be perpendicular to the valve stem.
