### 5.12.9. Gusseting of Small Bore Connections

- **5.12.9.1.** Where branch connections details are represented on drawing GD00-L-1003-1, the branch connection shall be gusseted. Small bore connections on uninsulated headers shall be per GD00-L-1005-1, which do not require gussets.

- **5.12.9.2.** Drawing No GD00-L-1003-1 shows gussets for mechanical strength and for vibrating service. Mechanical strength gussets may be required for unusual loads such as falling ice or maintenance activities and these shall be identified by Owner's Engineer. Typical requirements for gusseting in vibrating service are shown in Table 2 below. Vibrating lines shall be indicated on LDTs by stating Slug Flow, Two-Phase Flow, or any other unusual process condition. Isometric drawings will indicate where, and which gusset detail is to be used.

- **5.12.9.3.** Gusseting is not required in Category D services for mechanical strength

- **5.12.9.4.** Process and stress engineering shall identify systems in vibration service.

- **5.12.9.5.** Integrally reinforced fittings such as nipolets shall be used on small bore connections in lieu of standard nipples and gussets where justified by cost savings.

- **5.12.9.6.** For instrument connections refer to Standard Drawing DD00-L-1-1

- **5.12.9.7.** For vessel bridle connections and assembly see Standard Drawings DD00-L-1062-1 and DD00-L-1062-2.
