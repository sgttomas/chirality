5.11.6.2. Service water branch lines shall be taken from the top of the header. Block valves are required for every branch and shall be located both at the header and at the equipment or utility station.

5.11.6.3. Service water shall normally be piped to utility stations at grade only.

5.11.7. Plant Air Piping

5.11.7.1. The plant air supply header shall normally enter the process unit from an outside source and shall have a block valve at the process unit limit. Plant air shall be used as follows:

a) As an auxiliary source of instrument control air.

b) For process purposes.

c) For utility stations.

5.11.7.2. Plant air branch lines shall be taken from the top of the header. Block valves are normally required for every branch and shall be located at both the header and at the equipment or utility station.

5.11.7.3. Plant air supply lines shall be provided with drip legs and blowdown connections, unless the plant air has been dried to a dew point acceptable for the site.

5.11.7.4. In high humidity areas or coastal areas that are also subject to freezing, the plant air stream shall be dried downstream of the compressor and ahead of the accumulator.

5.11.8. Instrument Air Piping

5.11.8.1. The instrument air supply header shall normally enter the process unit from an outside source and shall have a block valve at the process unit limit.

5.11.8.2. Instrument air shall be dried to the required dew point upstream of the supply header.

5.11.8.3. Branch connections from a horizontal instrument air header shall be from the top of the header, with a block valve provided at each branch connection. A ½″ NPS drain valve shall be installed at all low points of the header. The sizing of the main and tributary headers shall be NPS 1 for a maximum of 50 instrument air users, and NPS 2 for a maximum of 150 instrument air users.

5.11.8.4. Spare instrument air header branch connections NPS 1 shall be provided on the basis of 20% spare, or at least two spares per area on all instrument air supply systems.

5.11.8.5. The instrument air supply to an individual user shall be NPS ½ minimum, but not more than four users shall be connected to one NPS ½ branch.

5.11.8.6. See paragraph 5.4.12.2 for thread lubricants in instrument air pipe joints.
