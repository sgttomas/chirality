5.12.5. Piping In or About Structures

5.12.5.1. Horizontal pipe runs are supported from beams with trapeze hangers, single hangers or brackets. See Industry Source 10 Standard 0210 for details.

5.12.5.2. Vertical runs outside the structure shall be supported and guided from support beams. See Industry Source 10 Standard 0210.

5.12.6. Relief Valve Stacks

5.12.6.1. Relief valve stacks venting to atmosphere can be categorized into short [under 6 ft (1830 mm)] and long [up to 20 ft (6.1 m)]. Short stacks do not need special supporting unless relieving high pressure or fabricated with cantilevered piping.

5.12.6.2. Long stacks require supports to prevent pipe load and relief forces from overstressing the nozzle. Guides must allow vertical thermal movement but prevent rotation due to vertical loads and forces and thus take the moment off the nozzle. These are classed as special supports.

5.12.7. Pipe Rack Piping

5.12.7.1. All long pipe runs shall be anchored to prevent creeping. See anchor details Types DA1 thru DA8, DA10 and DA11. Hot lines expand away from their anchors and cold lines contract towards them. Refer to Industry Source 10 Standard 0210 and paragraph 5.12.4.6 of this standard.

5.12.7.2. The locations of pipe anchors, critical guides and expansion loops shall be designated by the stress analysis group.

5.12.7.3. Minimum pipe size for headers in pipe racks shall be NPS 2.

5.12.7.4. Butt weld joints shall be used at pipe rack module interconnects for NPS 2 pipe in lieu of couplings.

5.12.8. Pipe Support Shoes

5.12.8.1. Insulated lines shall be provided with shoes to allow movement over the supports.

5.12.8.2. The shoes must be sized and located properly to permit movement without running off the supports.

5.12.8.3. All hot or cold insulated lines, irrespective of size, shall be provided with shoes at all supports.

5.12.8.4. Shoe lengths are 12", 18", 24", 30", 36" and 40", as per Industry Source 10 Business Unit Standard, or 300mm, 450mm, 600mm, 750mm, 900mm, and 1000mm.
