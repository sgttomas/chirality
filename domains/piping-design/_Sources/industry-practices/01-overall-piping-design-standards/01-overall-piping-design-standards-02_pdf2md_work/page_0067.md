5.11.9.1. Hose stations for cleanup and air tool operation shall be located to permit areas at grade, on elevated structures, and in buildings to be served by a 50 foot (15 m) length of hose.

5.11.9.2. The utility outlet connections shall be grouped together as much as possible in the same sequence, namely: water, steam, air, and inert gas (optional).

5.11.9.3. Inert gas shall be provided only as required by special circumstances.

5.11.9.4. Service water shall normally be provided at grade stations only.

5.11.9.5. The following locations shall normally have utility stations or standpipes for steam and air only provided:

  a) At alternate platform levels required for servicing of manways in towers or vessels.

  b) At alternate levels of elevated structures and so that all parts of the structure can be served with 50 foot (15 m) lengths of hose.

5.11.9.6. Steam service at hose stations shall be limited to less than 200 psig (1378 kPag) and 400°F (204°C).

5.11.9.7. Install traps on steam and air lines where required. For trap details see Suncor Standard Drawing DD00-L-1064-1.

5.11.9.8. Insulate and/or trace utility steam lines up to the first block valve at the utility station and where necessary for personnel protection.

5.11.9.9. Each utility service shall be clearly identified (at the utility station) for personnel safety.

5.11.9.10. Utility stations shall be installed such that they shall remain in operation during a unit shutdown.

5.11.9.11. For utility hose reels and hose connectors, plant operations shall be consulted to determine plant preferences.

5.11.9.12. A bleed valve shall be installed immediately upstream of the hose connector for air, steam, or inert gas. The exhaust of the bleed valve(s) shall be directed to preclude injury to personnel by direct impingement and/or by debris blown into the air by the exhaust of water, steam, air, or inert gas.

5.11.10. Process Sample Stations

5.11.10.1. Sample connections shall be provided where shown on the P&I diagrams.

5.11.10.2. Sample coolers shall be installed to keep the material being sampled below its flash and/or vaporization point and also for personnel protection.

5.11.10.3. All connections for sampling shall normally be NPS ¾ except:

  a) Piping handling materials of high viscosity shall be not less than NPS 1.

  b) Valve sizes, types and metallurgy to be determined for each specific application.
