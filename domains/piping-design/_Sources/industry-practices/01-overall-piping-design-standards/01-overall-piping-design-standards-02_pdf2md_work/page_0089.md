5.28.3.3. All connections shall be welded or flanged; however flanged connections shall be reduced to the least number possible to avoid leakages.

5.28.3.4. All pipe flanges valve flanges and valve bonnet flanges shall be painted with one coat of HF-detecting paint and shall not be insulated.

5.28.3.5. All valves and instruments shall be located at an elevation of maximum 3 feet 3 inches (1 m) above the working floor for safe easy handling during operation and maintenance.

5.28.3.6. All HF piping shall be installed above grade and shall be self draining (minimum slope of 1:100) to equipment or low point drains.

5.28.3.7. Process piping shall not be smaller than NPS 1. Vents and drains shall not be smaller than NPS ¾. Pipe and fittings shall be threaded in sizes NPS 1 and smaller.

5.28.3.8. All threaded connections shall be made up with PTFE tape only. Threaded connections shall not be seal-welded.

5.28.3.9. Socket welded fittings are not permitted.

5.28.3.10. Drains and vents shall be connected to a closed system.

5.28.3.11. All control valves in HF service shall be installed with block valves and a by-pass globe valve and shall have flush connection on both sides of the control valve.

5.28.3.12. Level indicators shall be of the magnetic type. Gauge glasses are not permitted.

5.28.3.13. For material classes, see relevant business unit Piping material Specifications.

5.28.4. Sodium Hydroxide (Caustic Soda) Service

5.28.4.1. Steam connections shall be provided on caustic piping for line cleaning and blowdown purposes.

5.28.4.2. All attachment welds to piping shall be done prior to PWHT. PWHT requirements shall be determined in accordance with NACE SP0403.

5.28.4.3. Hot spots due to direct wall to wall contact with steam or electric tracing shall be avoided by fitting spacers (ceramic, glass fibre, or filled phenolic resin).

5.28.4.4. All drawings for the fabrication of carbon steel piping for this service shall be clearly marked "SODIUM HYDROXIDE SERVICE".

5.28.4.5. Where heat loss through pipe supports could result in process solution freezing, clamp type supports shall be used with thermally insulating strips (e.g., glass-fibre material) shall be fitted between the pipes and supports.

5.28.4.6. For material classes, see relevant business unit Piping Material Specifications.
