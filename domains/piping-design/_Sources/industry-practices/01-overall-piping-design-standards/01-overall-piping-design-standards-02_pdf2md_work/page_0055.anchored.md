5.9.6.1. Industry Source 08 and Tube Exchangers

a) Piping; except removable local interconnections, shall not be run over channel or Industry Source 08 cover areas, or in the way of built-in or mobile handling facilities.

b) Wrench clearance shall be provided at the exchanger flanges.

c) Spool pieces shall be provided beyond the channel nozzles to permit the removal of the exchanger channel head and tube bundle.

d) Cooling water supply and return lines shall be routed to minimize bypass lengths.

5.9.6.2. Spiral Heat Exchangers

a) Break out spool pieces shall be provided at spiral heat exchangers to allow for cover plate swing area.

5.9.6.3. Plate Heat Exchangers

a) Piping shall not be run in the area required for plate removal from plate heat exchangers.

5.9.7. Rotating Machinery

5.9.7.1. The use of cold spring for piping systems which connect to rotating equipment is prohibited.

5.9.7.2. The use of couplings or expansion joints in the suction or discharge lines of pumps in cooling water service is acceptable. They shall be located on the pump side of the pump suction or discharge block valve, as appropriate.

5.9.7.3. All pressure thrust loads shall be absorbed by anchors or tie rods, and not by the coupling or expansion joint. The type of coupling and piping systems design shall be reviewed with the Owner's Engineer.

5.9.8. Piping at Pumps

5.9.8.1. Supports for pump piping shall be designed to take the valve and pipe loads when pumps are removed for repairs and to prevent bending moments being transmitted to the pump nozzles due to eccentric loadings.

5.9.8.2. Where frost heave or grade settlement may be encountered, supports from grade must be mounted on the pump foundation.

5.9.8.3. Check valves shall be installed between the discharge nozzles of pumps and the first primary block valve where applicable.

5.9.8.4. For front or top suction pumps, the pump suction line shall have a straight run equal in length to a minimum of 5 times pipe diameter, based on pump suction nozzle size. (Ref. API 686).

5.9.8.5. Pump suction and discharge piping shall be reviewed with respect to field welds to facilitate alignment of piping with pump nozzles.
