5.7.4.3. When no outriggers are used in an overhead pipe rack, the pump discharge nozzle shall be located 5'-0" (1525 mm) minimum from the centre line of the pipe rack column.

5.7.4.4. Pump nozzles shall be a maximum reduction of 2 nominal pipe sizes from line size.

5.7.4.5. Pumps and connecting piping shall be arranged so the pumps and their drivers can be removed with mechanical equipment.

5.7.4.6. Pumps shall preferably be located at the opposite side of fractionating equipment from furnaces.

5.7.4.7. Pumps shall be located outside of fire or spill walls which surround tanks or vessels containing NFPA Class I, II, or IIA flammable and combustible liquids. It is preferable that pumps not be located directly adjacent to these tanks in any case.

5.7.4.8. Space shall be left in pump rows for future expansion, if practicable.

5.7.4.9. Access to pump auxiliaries such as seal pots, starter button stations, motor terminal boxes, etc., shall be provided.

5.7.4.10. Clearances for removal of pump, drivers, pump rods, impellors and other parts shall be provided.

5.7.4.11. Access around pumps and between pumps shall be provided. This shall include access to strainers, valves, flushing systems and instruments.

5.7.4.12. Vertical pumps shall have appropriate overhead clearance for removal of drivers, shafts, impellers and other parts.

5.7.4.13. Motor cable termination boxes can be large for voltages higher than 480V and need to be considered when determining pump spacing.

## 5.7.5. Compressors

5.7.5.1. Large compressors shall be elevated sufficiently to permit piping and auxiliaries to be located below operating platform. Small compressors shall be grade mounted with piping and auxiliaries arranged for convenient access and maintenance.

5.7.5.2. Provide either a bridge or gantry crane for compressor components and unobstructed access for a mobile crane to permit easy removal of compressor.

5.7.5.3. Shelters shall be provided for protection of equipment and personnel. If a mobile crane is to be used for removal of compressor components, shelter roof and/or wall sheeting shall be designed in removable sections. The design of the removable sections shall minimize the need for scaffolding for section installation and removal.

5.7.5.4. Compressor lube and seal oil units, if located below the compressor, shall be offset from the compressor.

5.7.5.5. If practicable, compressors shall be orientated as follows:

a) Longitudinal axis of reciprocating compressor cylinders shall not be pointed toward control houses, or other continuously occupied buildings.
