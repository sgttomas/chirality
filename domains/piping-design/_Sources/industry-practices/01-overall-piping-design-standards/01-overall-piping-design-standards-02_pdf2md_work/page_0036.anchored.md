5.7.11.1. New pipe racks shall be sized for present needs plus 25%. This practice assumes a contingency of 10% at the outset of the job, but anticipates this will have been used upon completion of engineering, leaving 15% for future needs.

5.7.11.2. The arrangement for process unit pipe racks is to have process lines on the lower level(s) and utility lines on the upper level(s). See paragraph 5.8.1.10 for lines that require special consideration.

5.7.11.3. Cable trays are to be placed on the upper utility level when space is available, or they shall be placed on a separate level above all pipe levels.

5.7.11.4. Group hot lines together towards the outside of the pipe rack to maximize length of expansion loops and provide for maximizing loop "nesting" across rack width.

5.7.11.5. Large bore; heavy wall and large liquid lines shall be positioned towards the outside of the pipe rack, to minimize loads on the structure and foundations.

5.7.11.6. Lines in acid, caustic or other corrosive services shall be located on the lower pipe rack level. Flanged joints shall not be located above access ways or platforms. Overhead flanged joints in these services shall be fitted with spray shields.

5.7.11.7. Lines outside process units shall be run at grade on sleeper ways.

5.7.12. Clearances

5.7.12.1. Overhead Clearances

Equipment, structures, platforms, piping and its supports shall be arranged to provide the following minimum, overhead clearances:

a) over railroads, top of rail to bottom of any obstruction 22'-0" (8.71 m)

b) over plant roads, for major mobile equipment [as a minimum, one access road shall have an overhead clearance of 25'-0" (7.62 m). Special overhead clearances more than 25'-0" (7.62 m) shall be established in the beginning of the project between construction, operations and maintenance personnel.] See Industry Source 10 Standard 0300. 20'-0" (6.1 m)

c) at pump row access way, (under pipe rack) nearest obstruction 12'-0" (3.66 m)

d) over pumps and turbines, from high point of grade 8'-0" (2.44 m)

e) overhead walkways, passageways, and platforms, to nearest obstruction (except at dead ends) 7'-6" (2.28 m)

5.7.12.2. Horizontal Clearances

Structures, platforms, piping and its supports shall be arranged to provide the following minimum horizontal clearances:
