a) Oxygen leakages, especially in the form of liquid oxygen can easily create Oxygen concentrations well above the auto ignition levels for combustible materials like hydrocarbons and clothing.

b) Access to the ASU shall only be when necessary, unnecessary walk through the unit as a short cut shall be prevented.

c) Risk of contamination with hydrocarbon from neighbouring units shall be minimized.

d) Buildings near the ASU, which are continuously occupied, shall be designed using the blast resistance parameters.

e) The ASU air intake shall be located with an orientation and elevation that takes into account potential ingress of all possible emissions from within the plant or external to the plant.

f) Aggregate risk to employees and public shall be considered in siting ASU's.

5.7.28. Filters.

5.7.28.1. Basket filters and strainers

a) Access shall be provided for removal and cleaning of elements. Lift points, monorails or crane access shall be provided to handle weight of basket and contents.

b) For special filters, the manufacturer's recommendations shall be carefully followed concerning removal and operating clearances.

5.7.28.2. Process filters.

a) Special precautions (ventilation, vapor hoods, spray nozzles, extractor fans etc) shall be needed if the liquids are toxic or inflammable.

b) The floor shall be designed so that it can be washed and drained.

c) For larger filters having internals such as plates, lifting facilities shall be needed. An area shall be provided if required for storing cloths or filter media and for cutting filter cloths.

5.7.29. Spiral Heat Exchangers

5.7.29.1. Sufficient space shall be allowed on either side of spiral heat exchangers to allow for a clear cover plate swing area.

5.7.30. Plate Heat Exchangers

5.7.30.1. Sufficient space shall be allowed for plate removal from plate heat exchangers.

5.7.31. Mixers

5.7.31.1. Access to manways and the drive mechanism shall be provided, as well as space to remove the whole or parts of mixer for maintenance purposes.
