5.15.1.7. Lines with operating temperatures 160°F (71°C) and above shall not be buried without written permission of the Owner's representative.

5.15.1.8. Underground lines beyond the unit area limits shall be NPS 3 minimum pipe size except for sanitary water and chemical lines.

5.15.1.9. Elevation of underground lines shall be given to the outside bottom (OB) of pipe or to the invert or inside bottom (IB) of sewer lines.

5.15.1.10. Non-insulated pipes going through casings must be installed with commercially available casing insulators which will support the pipe and isolate it electrically from the casing.

5.15.1.11. Refer to Standard Drawing GD00-L-1050-1 for requirements for lines running through dykes.

5.15.1.12. There shall be no direct burial of flanges, non-seal welded threaded connections, or other mechanical connections which have the possibility of leakage.

5.15.1.13. Where underground metallic piping is cathodically protected, insulation kits shall be provided at all flanged joints where the underground piping is connected to above ground piping.

5.15.1.14. The need for cathodic protection shall be assessed as part of the design and prior to construction of underground piping systems.

5.15.1.15. Minimum clearance for sewer lines shall be 2'-0" (610 mm) between outside diameter of the pipe. Clearance between underground lines shall be 12" (305 mm) minimum except cooling water lines shall have 18" (460 mm) clearance where heat transfer is probable. Local building codes must be followed for clearance.

5.15.2. Road Crossings

5.15.2.1. Piping below city, county, provincial or refinery roads shall run in sleeves, tunnels or culverts and shall conform to local, provincial or state regulations. The design of such crossings shall be submitted to the appropriate regulatory authority for approval before installation is started. For typical configuration of insulated and uninsulated lines see Standard Drawing GD00-L-1050-1.

5.15.2.2. Road crossings shall normally be at 90° to the road, never less than 45°.

## 5.16. Tank Field Piping

5.16.1. Tank field piping requires special restraints and allowances for large non-thermal movements. The special restraints are due to dike penetrations and the non-thermal movements due to possible tank settlement.

5.16.2. Dike penetrations may be in open sleeves, sleeves sealed with the lines welded to one end with steel collars and with the lines buried.
