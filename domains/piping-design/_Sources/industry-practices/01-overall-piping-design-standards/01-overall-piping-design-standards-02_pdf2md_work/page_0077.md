5.13.2.12. NPS ¾ (minimum) gate valves, for draining and/or release of pressure, shall be provided at the low points in the piping on both sides of the control valve and the adjacent block valves. These drain/depressurising valves shall be installed on the underside of the line and shall point downward. Round head pipe plugs shall be inserted in the open ends of the valves, or where the drain valves are flanged due to line class requirements, the blind flange shall be equipped with a 4" long nipple and cap.

5.13.2.13. By-pass valves in steam control sets shall be located at the top horizontal portion of the by-pass line in order to avoid a build up of condensate.

5.13.3. Level Instruments

5.13.3.1. For Level Instrument installation details, refer to Suncor Standard 0401-P1.

5.13.4. Orifice Runs

5.13.4.1. Horizontal meter-runs are preferred. Determine the length of each meter run in accordance with Suncor OSG Standard 0401-P1. Sufficient clearance shall be provided at orifice flanges for installation of instrument piping and seal pots where they are required. Refer to Standard Drawing GD00-L-1049-1 and GD00-L-1049-2.

5.13.4.2. Orifice flanges shall be equipped with jack screws.

5.13.5. Thermowell Connections

5.13.5.1. Threaded thermowells are permitted only in services where threaded fittings are acceptable in the Pipe Class Table.

5.13.5.2. Except where listed in the Pipe Class Table as NPS 1½ for Energy Services, flanged thermowell nozzles shall be pipets, NPS 2 x 6" long with wall thickness per Pipe Class and Class 300 flange minimum. If butt welds are used, the root pass must be ground smooth.

5.13.5.3. On NPS 4 lines, thermowells shall be installed on elbows per Standard Drawing GD00-L-1035-1.

5.13.5.4. Lines NPS 3 and smaller shall be increased to NPS 4 at the thermowell elbow. Straight line increases require prior approval from Instrument and Process Engineering.

5.13.5.5. Thermowell nozzles on clad or weld overlayed pipe shall be sized to account for the reduction of internal diameter due to the cladding or weld overlay.

## 5.14. Pressure Relief Systems

5.14.1. Safety and relief valves shall be provided, sized and installed in compliance with the applicable boiler, pressure vessel and piping codes.

5.14.2. Piping

5.14.2.1. The size of the inlet line shall not be less than the size of the relief valve inlet.
