5.7.8.6. Air cooler housings shall be equipped with two ingress/egress doors.

5.7.8.7. Air coolers shall be located in the equipment areas or over pipe racks.

5.7.8.8. Sufficient access for mobile equipment shall be provided to permit removal or replacement of tube bundles, fans and drivers.

5.7.8.9. Air coolers shall not be located over a potential source of fire such as a light ends pump or hot oil pump with product at or over its auto-ignition point.

5.7.8.10. Care shall be taken to keep bare hot lines or other hot equipment out from under the air in-take to coolers and also to prevent recirculation of heated air.

5.7.9. Double Pipe and Multitube Hairpin Heat Exchangers

5.7.9.1. Double Pipe and Multitube Hairpin Heat Exchangers shall be placed at grade level to ensure maintainability.

5.7.10. Fired Heaters

5.7.10.1. Furnaces and boilers shall be located near plot boundary for easy access. Tube pulling shall generally be by mobile crane. Adequate dropout and swinging space shall be provided. External piping shall be minimized at the tube pulling end of equipment. Road access shall also be provided at tube pulling end for mobile equipment.

5.7.10.2. Where practical and economical, fired equipment shall be grouped together. Common stacks are permitted when they are more economical; however, dampers or suitable barriers shall be provided in individual ducting to the stack.

5.7.10.3. Prevailing winds shall be considered, and vessels, pumps, compressors and other equipment handling light hydrocarbons shall be located downwind of fired heaters to minimize the possibility of vapors being carried toward open fires. Heater orientation and use of wind boxes shall be considered if operation would be affected by the maximum winds anticipated in the area.

5.7.10.4. Fired heater stacks discharging vapours to atmosphere shall have a stack height ending at least 10'-0" (3 m) above any operating platform within a 70 ft (21 m) radius.

5.7.10.5. Grading under furnaces shall be sloped on one side to direct spills to a suitable drain point.

5.7.10.6. For fired heaters in liquid hydrocarbon service, a suitably design system to collect firewater run-off, storm water run-off and equipment drips/leaks shall be included. For example, this may include a catch basin and oily water sewer system.

5.7.10.7. Space and provision for pigging of furnace tubes shall be allowed for in furnace layouts.

5.7.10.8. Sufficient access and clearance shall be provided for removal of soot-blowers, air pre-heaters, burners, fans, etc., and for operation of pressure relief doors.
