## 5.28. Special Services

5.28.1. Sulphuric Acid Service

- 5.28.1.1. Threaded or socket welding connections are prohibited for carbon steel piping in sulphuric acid service.
- 5.28.1.2. Butt-weld connections shall be used for all pipe sizes.
- 5.28.1.3. All flanged connections shall have polyethylene safety covers with snap type fasteners.
- 5.28.1.4. Copper or copper-bearing alloys are not be permitted in this service.
- 5.28.1.5. For material classes, see relevant business unit Piping Material Specifications.

5.28.2. Chlorine Service

- 5.28.2.1. Chlorine shall be treated as a very toxic substance.
- 5.28.2.2. A minimum of schedule 80 seamless pipe of minimum size NPS ½ shall be used to ensure rigidity and for protection against mechanical damage resulting in possible leaks.
- 5.28.2.3. Piping arrangements shall be as simple as possible, with a minimum of welded or flanged connections. For pipe of NPS 4 and smaller, pipe bending shall be applied rather than using elbows. Cold bending is not permitted unless the finished bend is stress relieved.
- 5.28.2.4. Horizontal pipes shall be self draining with a slope of at least 1:100.
- 5.28.2.5. The number of field welds shall be minimized.
- 5.28.2.6. Socket welding is not permitted. All piping shall have butt-welded joints, except for NPS ½ vents, drains, and instrument connections which are to be threaded and seal welded.
- 5.28.2.7. For material classes, see relevant business unit Piping material Specifications.

5.28.3. Hydrogen Fluoride (HF) Service

- 5.28.3.1. HF shall be treated as a very toxic substance.
- 5.28.3.2. Expansion bellows shall not be used.
