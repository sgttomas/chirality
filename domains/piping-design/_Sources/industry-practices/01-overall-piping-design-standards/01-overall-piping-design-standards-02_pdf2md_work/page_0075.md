5.13.1.2. Maintenance access is required to all instruments but can be by portable ladder from grade or by platform.

5.13.1.3. All level instruments shall be accessible from grade, platform or ladder. Their location shall be consistent with function and with convenience of plant operation.

5.13.1.4. Where strong backs are provided for external level instruments, their minimum pipe size will be NPS 3, STD. WT for carbon and low alloy steels and NPS 3, SCH 80S for S.S. Block valves shall be provided at the vessel connections.
