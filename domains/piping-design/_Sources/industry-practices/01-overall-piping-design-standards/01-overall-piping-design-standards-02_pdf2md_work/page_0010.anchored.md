## 4. DEFINITIONS AND ACRONYMS

4.1. The "**Owner**" - means Industry Source 10

4.2. The "**Buyer**" - means Industry Source 10 or its agent designated for that project

4.3. "**Owner's Engineer**" - means a registered, professional engineer employed directly by Industry Source 10 responsible for the technical integrity of the project.

4.4. "**Owner's Inspector**" - means the person authorized by Industry Source 10 for inspection.

4.5. The "**Contractor**" – is the party which carries out all or part of the design, engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning or management of a project, or operation or maintenance of a facility. The Owner may undertake all or part of the duties of the Contractor.

4.6. The "**Supplier**" - means the entity, manufacturer, fabricator, vendor, or contractor that supplies the material or services.

4.7. "**Local Authorities**" - means the governmental regulatory authority controlling laws, codes, rules, or regulations for the design, fabrication and testing of industrial piping in the Province of Alberta.

4.8. "**P&ID**" - means Process and Instrumentation Diagram.

4.9. "**ISO**" - means Piping Isometric Drawing.

4.10. "**NPS**" - means Nominal Pipe Size in inches.

4.11. **Small Bore Piping** - Piping in sizes NPS 2 and smaller.

4.12. **Constructability** – Optimum use of construction knowledge and experience in planning, design/engineering, procurement, and field operations to achieve overall project objectives.

4.13. **Offsites** – Equipment grouped outside a process unit battery limits (e.g., tanks, loading/unloading facilities, cooling towers, flares etc.).

4.14. **Upstream Facilities** – Upstream facilities for oil and gas wellsites and gathering systems.

4.15. The word **shall** indicates a requirement.

4.16. "**Process Unit**" is defined as an assembly of sequence related equipment and process control valves functioning as, or within, an identifiable system which performs a specific function of a process within a process plant, and bounded by battery limits. Battery limits are as shown on the plot plan.

4.17. "**Main Operating Areas**" are defined as areas at grade or levels above or below grade, where plant personnel are present continuously or are scheduled for presence at least once every shift while the plant is operating.

4.18. "**Auxiliary Service Areas**" are defined as all other plant areas and levels above or below grade.

4.19. "**Equipment**" is defined in the equipment list and includes such items as vessels, heat exchangers, furnaces and pumps.
