5.3.3.4. Design pressure for relieved piping systems shall be the safety valve set pressure plus the static head below the relief valve adjusted for allowances per design criteria of ASME B31.3.

5.3.3.5. Design pressure for piping systems operating under vacuum shall be full vacuum, and shall be stated in the Line Designation Table.

5.3.3.6. For piping systems protected by relief valves, the relief valve set pressure shall be equal to the design pressure minus the static head below the relief valve.

5.3.3.7. Design pressures shall be as stated in the Line Designation Table.

## 5.4. Materials

### 5.4.1. Piping Material Specifications

#### 5.4.1.1.

Std. 0202, Piping Materials Specification, or the Piping materials Specification of the particular business unit, includes individual Pipe Class Tables. The specification shows in detail pipe, valve, flange, fitting, bolting, gasket, branch connection and other specific material requirements for various classes of fluid, temperature and pressure services.

#### 5.4.1.2. Material Specifications within Skid or Vendor Packages

a) Although every attempt shall be made to utilize Industry Source 10 Materials Specifications, the practice of vendors designing skid packages based on their own sound engineering practices utilizing materials, end preps, fabrication, installation, testing, etc does occur.

b) Where piping used in Skid or Vendor Packages is designed to standards other than Industry Source 10 standards, the design shall be subject to review and approval by Owner's Engineer prior to fabrication or supply as part of the design evaluation process. It shall be understood that in these cases, the equipment/skid manufacturers assume all responsibility for design, material selection, procurement, fabrication, inspection, quality, registration, and performance of their finished product.

c) Industry Source 10 Piping Material Specifications have been developed and shall be used for the safe design of interconnecting piping between equipment, skid packages and other components within Industry Source 10 Operations' units.

### 5.4.2. Corrosion Allowances

#### 5.4.2.1.

Corrosion allowances shown in the Piping Material Specifications are minimum for the respective services.

### 5.4.3. Wall Thickness and Requirement

#### 5.4.3.1.

Unless specified otherwise in the Industry Source 10 Piping Material Specification, pipe wall design thickness shall be calculated in accordance with the applicable codes. Refer to section 5.2.
