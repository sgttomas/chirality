5.8.1.21. All valves which must be frequently operated (accessed and operated > one time per week), are critical to plant operations, or need to be accessed and operated quickly, such as during emergencies, shall be accessible from grade, platforms, or stairways. This includes main header block valves for process piping conveying flammable/toxic materials. Such valves shall be located such that the centreline of the valve stem is located between 37 inches (940mm) and 68 inches (1725mm) above grade or platform; however, when access is via stairway, centreline of the valve shall be located 6 inches (150mm) to 26 inches (660mm) above the handrail. When access is via stairway, valve handle shall be a maximum of 20 inches (500mm) from the handrail when the valve stem is parallel to the handrail. When valve stem is perpendicular to the stairway, the valve handle shall be a minimum distance from the handrail to prevent stem from protruding into the stairway.

5.8.1.22. Infrequently operated valves, NPS 4 and smaller, which are to be located more than 7 feet (2.1m) above grade, shall be arranged for access from a 14 foot (4.3m) maximum length portable ladder; for example, branch block valves at utility headers, valves at orifice flange taps, etc.

5.8.1.23. Frequently operated valves located in trenches, shall be provided with extension stems extending to within 4 inches (100mm) below the cover plate if the handwheels are more than 12 inches (300mm) below the cover plate.

5.8.1.24. Manually operated valves, which are used in conjunction with locally mounted flow indicators, shall be arranged such that the flow indicators are readable from the valve location.

5.8.1.25. Control valve by-pass piping shall be positioned a sufficient distance from the control valve to permit a full range of maintenance activities. The following clearances shall be provided between the top of the control valve actuator and the bottom of insulation on the by-pass:

| VALVE | ACTUATOR | CLEARANCE |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| < NPS 8 | < 40 inches | 1 foot |
| < NPS 8 | > 40 inches | 3 feet |
| > NPS 8 | -- | 3 feet (915 mm) w/offset bypass. offset shall be 6 inches (150 mm) > than diameter of actuator. |

5.8.1.26. Operating drains subject to plugging, shall terminate at a minimum distance of 10 inches (250 mm) above grade to facilitate access for draining, unplugging, and removal/installation of threaded caps/plugs.

5.8.1.27. Future pipe rack space shall be provided as specified in Paragraph 5.7.11.

5.8.1.28. Avoid compound angles in piping configuration, unless absolutely necessary.

5.8.1.29. Personnel protection insulation shall be applied to pipe or equipment up to 7′–6″ (2.28 m) above a walkway or platform and within a 2′–6″ (762 mm) lateral direction from a walkway or platform.

5.8.1.30. In dirty cooling water systems, all branches from headers shall be taken off from the top of the header.
