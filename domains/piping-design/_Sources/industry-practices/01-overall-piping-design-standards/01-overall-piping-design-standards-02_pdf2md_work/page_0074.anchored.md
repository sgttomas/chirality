5.12.10. Branch Reinforcement

5.12.10.1. For branches NPS 3 and larger:

a) Reinforcement pads shall be designed per ASME B31.3 Section 304.3.3.

b) When the branch is at least two pipe sizes smaller than the header, and the design temperature less than 800°F (427°C), the following design criteria shall be used:

   i. The re-pad width shall be half the nominal diameter of the branch pipe and pad thickness shall be the same as the header pipe. On the outside diameter of the pad, the pad lines shall have a throat of not less than 0.7 times the pad thickness. The header to nozzle and re-pad to nozzle weld shall be full penetration.

c) Reinforcing pads shall be drilled and tapped for ¼" NPT telltale hole. If the pad is in two sections, a hole is required in each section. The hole shall be sealed with a water resistant grease.

d) When piping is to be insulated, a nipple (minimum schedule 40) shall be installed in the telltale hole and shall extend beyond the insulation a minimum of ½".

e) Reinforcement pad shall be subjected to an air or nitrogen pressure test at minimum 15 psig (104 kPag).

5.12.11. Branch Connections

5.12.11.1. Branch connections shall normally be at 90° to the run pipe. 45° laterals for uses other than blowdown, relief and drain lines shall normally be avoided. In no case shall a welded branch connection be designed with an intersecting angle of less than 45°.

5.12.11.2. Branch connections shall be in accordance with Tables in the relevant business unit Piping Specification.
