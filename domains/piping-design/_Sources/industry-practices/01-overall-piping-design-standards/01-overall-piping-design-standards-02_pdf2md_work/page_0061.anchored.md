5.9.17.3. Pipe loops between furnace outlet nozzles and the transfer line shall all have the same configuration and length.

5.9.17.4. Spacing (S) of pipe loop entry points into the transfer line shall be S = d + D/5, where d = pipe loop diameter at point of entry and D = transfer line diameter.

5.9.17.5. Support and guiding of the transfer line shall be carefully considered for loadings such as:

a) Possible liquid loading of system during start-up as well as during hydro-testing.

b) Wind Loading

c) Slug flow conditions

On large diameter transfer lines, thermal bowing during start-up shall be considered.

5.9.18. Coker Furnace Transfer Lines

5.9.18.1. Coker furnace transfer lines to coke drums shall be laid out with no pockets and shall contain break flanges and removable elbows and/or cleaning ports to facilitate hydro-cleaning of the system.

5.9.18.2. Crane access or provision for lifting removable elbows shall be provided. Removable elbows shall have lifting lugs.

5.9.19. Liquid Sulphur Piping

5.9.19.1. Liquid Sulphur Piping shall be sloped a minimum of 1 inch per 20 feet.

5.9.19.2. Steam/heat Traced Piping

a) External steam/heat tracing, such as CSI "Controtrace" is to be used whenever cost effective for diameters NPS 8 and larger. Refer to product installation details and practices to assist in design details. Design shall comply with the applicable Industry Source 10 line classes as listed in the Piping Material Specification class of the relevant business unit.

5.9.19.3. FSE Heated Transfer Lines

a) Ferromagnetic Skin Effect heating is applicable for transfer lines having a minimum of valves and flanges. Conductor and tubes shall comply with the requirements of the applicable Industry Source 10 line classes as listed in the Piping Material Specification of the relevant business unit.

5.9.19.4. Steam Jacketed Piping.

a) For steam jacketed pipe standard see Industry Source 10 Std. drawings DD00-L-1084-1, DD00-L-1084-2 and DD00-L-1084-3. These drawings may also be used to assist in design of hot oil or glycol jacketed systems.

b) The sulphur lines are in the centre of the larger-diameter steam lines. Steam pressures and temperatures shall comply with the applicable Industry Source 10 line classes as listed in the Piping Material Specification of the relevant business unit. The wall thickness of the inner pipe shall be checked for internal and external pressure.
