5.24.3.7. Branch headers will connect to the top of the header from which it originates. Wherever possible, branch headers will be sloped toward the main oil mist header so that oil drains back to the oil mist console.

5.24.4. Equipment drop piping installation shall comply with the following:

5.24.4.1. Equipment drop piping will be connected to the top of the main oil mist header.

5.24.4.2. Equipment drop piping will be sloped toward the oil mist header from which it originates up to the first section of pipe that extends vertically down. From thereafter, all drop piping will sloped toward the equipment being serviced. Drop piping will have the same slope as the oil mist header.

5.24.4.3. Equipment drop piping shall not exceed 35 feet (10.6 m) in length.

5.24.4.4. Drop piping shall be located so that it:

- a) Terminates no less than 2 feet (600 mm) nor more than 6 feet (1.8 m) above the equipment to be serviced.
- b) Is located off centre so that equipment can be serviced without the need to remove the oil mist drop piping.
- c) Does not impeded access to the equipment.
- d) Does not interfere with installation or removal of the equipment.

5.24.5. Drop piping shall be anchored to permanent structures or piping not normally removed for maintenance.

5.24.5.1. Drop piping shall not be branched.

5.24.5.2. Drop piping shall terminate with a manifold block. Mist manifolds shall include:

- a) A self closing valve for draining oil that collects at the end of the drop piping. Valve will be such that it cannot vibrate open.
- b) Tapped connections for installation of reclassifiers.
- c) A site viewing glass to reveal oil that collects in the bottom of the drop, and to allow viewing of mist flow when the drain valve is opened.
- d) If specified, a drain line shall be installed from the mist manifold drain outlet connection to the oil collection container. Drain tubing shall be 3/8 inch OD.

5.24.6. Oil mist distribution header, branch header and equipment drop piping shall be supported to limit sagging to a maximum deflection of 1/8 of the pipe ID.
