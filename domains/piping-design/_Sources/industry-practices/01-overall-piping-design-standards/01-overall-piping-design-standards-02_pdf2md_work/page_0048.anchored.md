5.7.32. Fans

5.7.32.1. Adequate space, access and headroom must be allowed for removal of impellors, shafts, motors, etc., particularly for dirty or corrosive duties.

5.7.33. Mechanical Conveyors

5.7.33.1. A combination of horizontal belt conveyors and vertical bucket elevators gives maximum flexibility with regard to plant spacing.

5.7.33.2. Belt conveyors can be used to a certain angle with the horizontal (generally inclined up to 20 degrees and declined up to 15 degrees). Usually fine granular materials can be conveyed at steeper angles than course or lumpy material. Conveyors of some manufacturers can be inclined more steeply depending on belt type. Manufacturers shall be consulted before layout proceeds.

5.7.33.3. A maintenance walkway, 30 inches (760mm) wide shall be provided alongside belt conveyors, located approximately 30 inches (760mm) below the belt line.

5.7.33.4. It is advisable to consult manufacturer on layout and operation/maintenance aspects of both belt and bucket conveyors.

5.7.34. Industry Source 07 Doc umatic Conveying

5.7.34.1. Industry Source 07 Doc umatic conveying systems require selection of a number of specialized components and materials dependant on product to be transported. Manufacturers of these systems shall be consulted to achieve the best design and layout. Equipment layout is generally influenced by long sweep elbows in the piping system.

5.7.35. Specialized Equipment.

5.7.35.1. Specialized equipment shall be laid out in consultation with the manufacturer.

## 5.8. Piping Layout

5.8.1. Pipe Routing

5.8.1.1. All process and utility piping shall be located above grade where possible. Installation of piping in trenches shall require approval of Owner's Engineer.

5.8.1.2. Piping shall be arranged in an orderly manner and routed as directly as practical preferably in established banks or pipe racks, so as to offer minimum obstruction to maintenance operations and fire fighting.

5.8.1.3. Piping shall run at different elevations designated for north-south and east-west banks, and shall change elevation when changing direction.

5.8.1.4. Lateral pipe racks interconnecting two parallel pipe racks shall be at least 80 feet (24m) apart if the laterals cross major accessways. This provides space for raising and lowering crane booms.

5.8.1.5. Pipeways shall be installed outside of areas enclosed by fire walls or spill walls.
