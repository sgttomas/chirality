5.8.3.4. Other severe services include erosive, corrosive, high or low temperature or pressure conditions or any fluids containing solids. Many such services require alloy or special materials, or the use of long radius bends.

5.8.4. Severe Cyclic Service

5.8.4.1. Piping shall be designed per ASME B31.3, Section 302.3.5 and ASME Section VIII Div. 2, Appendix 5. Inspection for piping in cyclic loading shall be;

a) Visual examination shall be carried out on 100% of the welded joints and components.

b) 100% radiography is required on all girth, longitudinal and miter groove welds including "sweep-o-let" branch welds.

c) Magnetic particle or liquid penetrant examination shall be carried out on all welds not radiographed.

d) Where Ultrasonic (UT) Examination is to be used in lieu of radiography, the requirements of Suncor Standard PQA-GS-0017 shall be complied with.

5.8.4.2. Where appropriate, Process Engineers shall put notes on P&IDs instructing the detailed disciplines to design for cyclic flow. LDTs and appropriate data sheets will also have these notes.

5.8.4.3. Mechanical Engineers must pay particular attention to equipment in cyclical flow, and ensure that vendors are informed.

5.8.4.4. Supports on lines in cyclic flow shall be attached using continuous fillet welds where appropriate. No stitch welding designs are permitted. Weld contours shall have no sharp transitions and no radii less than 3/8" (9mm).

5.8.4.5. Where fittings are manufactured with one or more seams, all accessible weld roots and caps shall be removed by grinding. Grinding shall be longitudinal to the pipe axis (or transverse to the welding direction); any grind marks shall be buffed out smooth.

5.8.4.6. Supplementary S7 testing is required on all welds in A672 pipe. One weld per batch shall be hardness tested to Supplementary S3. All hardness readings shall be less than 200 BHN.

5.8.4.7. All valves shall be bolted bonnet or full penetration weld. No socket welds or threaded connections of any kind are permitted.

5.8.4.8. Slip-on flanges are not permitted.

5.8.4.9. Refer to paragraph 5.17 for pipe stress analysis requirements.

5.8.5. Seismic Loads

5.8.5.1. If the geographic region is susceptible to earthquakes, the anticipated earthquake loads shall be established. For Canadian installations, refer to the National building Code (NBC) for earthquake zone ratings. For U.S. installations refer to applicable earthquake zone loadings, for example UBC or ASCE.
