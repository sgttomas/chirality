5.4.16.5. Systems in which expansion joints are installed shall be controlled by suitable anchors, guides, supports, or stops, to avoid deflecting the joints beyond their designed limits (see Standards of the Expansion Joints Manufacturers Association).

5.4.16.6. Unless otherwise specified by supplementary design data, the material selection for the flexible elements and all external attachments, such as lugs, rods, etc., shall be as recommended by the expansion joint manufacturer. The material for the other components shall be in accordance with the piping material specification of the line in which it is to be installed.

5.4.16.7. To avoid chloride stress corrosion cracking of expansion joints subject to metal temperatures in excess of 175°F (79°C), and to increase low cycle fatigue life, Alloy 625 shall normally be used in lieu of 300 series stainless steels. The suitability of Alloy 625 for the intended service shall be reviewed and approved by the Owner's Engineer.

5.4.17. Bolts

5.4.17.1. All bolting materials shall be as listed the Piping Material Specification (of the relevant business unit) line classes.

5.4.18. Gaskets

5.4.18.1. All gaskets shall be in accordance with the applicable line class in the Piping Material Specification of the relevant business unit.

5.4.19. Bolt and Stud Tensioning

5.4.19.1. Bolt and stud tensioning shall be executed in all new and retro-fit piping installations and shall comply with applicable business unit or project specific procedures such as Industry Source 10 Procedure No. 03-00004-SE-2A-00-2.4.4-12 Industry Source 11 Project bolt and stud tensioning.

5.4.19.2. Where the use of hydraulic bolt tensioning equipment is required, designers shall ensure there is sufficient space around flanges for access.

5.4.20. Compression Type Couplings

5.4.20.1. Compression type couplings that utilize elastomeric sealing elements (such as Dresser™ brand) may be installed in tank farm piping to compensate for differential settlement subject to Owner's Engineer approval.

5.4.21. Tubing

5.4.21.1. Tubing used for sample lines shall be cold drawn and solution annealed 316L seamless stainless steel to ASTM A269, connected with 316 SS compression fittings.

5.4.21.2. For tubing requirements associated with instrumentation see Standard 401-P1.

5.4.21.3. For tubing requirements associated with steam tracing see Standard 209.

5.4.21.4. For tubing requirements associated with jumpovers on jacketed piping systems see Standard 209.
