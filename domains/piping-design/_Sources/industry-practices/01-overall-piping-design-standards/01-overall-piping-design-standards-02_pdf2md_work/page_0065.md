5.11.2.14. Bellows thermostatic air vents shall be used for venting air from steam in coils, jackets, heat exchangers, or other equipment where air can accumulate in steam spaces and reduce heat transfer efficiency.

5.11.2.15. Recommended traps for specific services are shown in the relevant Piping Specifications.

5.11.2.16. Valves shall be provided on each side of trap, to facilitate removal of trap in closed systems. Double Block and Bleed valves are required on traps in HP steam service. See Standard Drawing DD00-L-1064-1.

5.11.3. Steam Out Connections

5.11.3.1. Steam out connections are required for purging vessels and pipelines containing hydrocarbon and hazardous fluid.

5.11.3.2. Steam out connections shall not be less than NPS 1.

5.11.3.3. On horizontal vessels, steam out connections shall be located at the opposite end of the vessel from the vent and manway.

5.11.4. Steam Tracing and Winterizing

5.11.4.1. See Suncor Energy STD 0209, Protective Steam Warming System.

5.11.5. Cooling Water Piping

5.11.5.1. Cooling water distribution headers shall normally be located on overhead pipe racks.

5.11.5.2. Cooling water supply and return headers shall have block valves at the process unit limits. A by-pass loop with block valves shall be installed joining the ends of the supply and return headers to maintain flow.

5.11.5.3. Cooling water branch lines shall be taken from the top of the headers.

5.11.5.4. When the manufacturer's service rating of cooling water jackets on equipment is less than the water supply pressure, provisions for a pressure reducing valve and relief valve shall be made in the line upstream of the water jacket connections. The relief valve shall not be isolated by valves from the water jacket.

5.11.5.5. The installation of cooling water lines shall ensure that the equipment being cooled will remain full of water in the event of a shutdown of the cooling water pumps.

5.11.6. Service Water Piping

5.11.6.1. The service water supply header shall normally originate at the cooling water supply main outside of the process unit limit to ensure that a water supply will be available at all times. When salt water is used as a cooling water system, a separate freshwater supply line shall be installed for service water.
