**6.24 Valve Reconditioning – FCCU Slide Valves**

## 7.0 EXPANSION JOINTS

### 7.1 Metal Expansion Joints

Requirements governing the design, fabrication, inspection, and testing of circular and rectangular metal bellows expansion joints are covered in REP 5–4–1.

### 7.2 Fabric Expansion Joints

Requirements governing the design, fabrication, inspection, and testing of circular and rectangular fabric expansion joints are covered in REP 5–4–2.

### 7.3 Supplemental Requirements for Expansion Joints

Supplemental requirements including data to be furnished by the Purchaser, manufacturer's responsibility, documentation, marking, packaging, and shipping for metal and fabric expansion joints are covered in REP 5–4–3.

## 8.0 PIPING FABRICATION, WELDING, ERECTION AND TESTING

### 8.1 Piping Fabrication

**8.1.1** Requirements for shop and field fabricated piping and inspection of piping assemblies, including expansion joints, furnace headers, and furnace tube assemblies are covered in REP 5–5–1.

**8.1.2** Selection of piping components and materials, other than furnace tube materials, are covered in REP 5–2–1. Selection of furnace tube materials is covered in REP 8–2–1.

### 8.2 Welding Requirements for Piping

Requirements for welding of piping assemblies are covered in REP 5–5–2. Additional requirements for seal welding and welding of pipe supports are also covered by this Practice.

### 8.3 Piping Erection and Testing

Requirements for field erection, testing, inspection, and cleaning of fabricated metallic piping are covered in REP 5–5–3.

### 8.4 Supplemental Requirements for ASME Code Section I Piping

Supplementary requirements for ASME BPVC, Section I piping associated with power boilers are covered in REP 5–5–8.

### 8.5 Supplemental Requirements for Jacketed Piping

Supplemental fabrication requirements for jacketed piping systems are covered in REP 5–5–5.
