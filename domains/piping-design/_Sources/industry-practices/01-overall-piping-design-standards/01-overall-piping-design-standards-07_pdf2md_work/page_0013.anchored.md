**4.3.4** For two-phase flow, the parameter:

$$\rho_M V_M^2 \leq 10{,}000$$

*With:*

$$\rho_M = \frac{M_T}{\dfrac{M_L}{\rho_L} + \dfrac{M_G}{\rho_G}}$$

$$V_M = \frac{M_T}{3600\,\rho_M A}$$

A = cross sectional area of pipe, ft²

$\rho_M$ = mixture density, lbs/ft³

$\rho_L$ = liquid density, lbs/ft³

$\rho_G$ = vapor density, lbs/ft³

$M_T$ = total mixture flowrate, lbs/hr

$M_L$ = liquid flowrate, lbs/hr

$M_G$ = vapor flowrate, lbs/hr

$V_M$ = mixture velocity, ft/sec

(Note: This erosion velocity equation is taken from API RP 14E, Paragraph 2.5).

**4.3.5** Piping in intermittent service (for example, start-up, pumping-out, bypasses) shall be sized on the basis of available pressure differentials and subject to the line sizing criteria of this Practice.

**4.3.6** Pipe size restrictions on bore and outside diameter shall be as specified in REP 5–2–1.

**4.3.7** Pump suction line sizes shall be determined as follows:

**4.3.7.1** (*) Pump suction lines shall be sized to provide a Net Pump Suction Head Available (NPSHA) not less than the Net Pump Suction Head Required (NPSHR) by the pump selected. NPSHA shall be at the rated pump capacity. The economics of increasing line size versus adjusting vessel elevation shall be considered in providing sufficient Net Positive Suction Head (NPSH). The line shall not be smaller than the pump nozzle and velocity shall not exceed 10 ft/sec. Line velocities higher than 6 ft/sec shall require approval of Owner's Engineer.

**4.3.7.2** The static head used in calculating the available NPSH shall be measured from the bottom tangent line for vertical vessels, or the bottom of horizontal vessels to one of the following:

1. The centerline of a horizontal centrifugal pump or rotary pump.
2. The suction nozzle on a vertical centrifugal pump or reciprocating pump.
