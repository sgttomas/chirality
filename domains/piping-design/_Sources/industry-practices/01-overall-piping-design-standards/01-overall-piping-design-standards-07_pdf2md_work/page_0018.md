## 6.13 FCCU Slide Valves

Requirements governing the design, inspection, and testing of FCCU slide valves are covered in REP 5–3–19.

## 6.14 Pressure Relief Valves

**6.14.1** Requirements governing the design, inspection, and testing of pressure relief valves (PRVs) are covered in REP 5–3–14.

**6.14.2** Sizing requirements and use of PRVs are stipulated in REP 3–7–1.

## 6.15 Control Valves

Requirements governing the design, inspection, and testing of control valves are covered in REP 6–3–17.

## 6.16 Supplemental Requirements for Valves

Supplemental requirements including data to be furnished by the Purchaser, manufacturer's responsibility, documentation, marking, packaging, and shipping for valves are covered in REP 5–3–21. Specific requirements for each valve type are provided in a separate Practice.

## 6.17 Three-Way Valves

Requirements for three-way valves are covered in REP 5–3–16. Tandem PRVs, which are designed to ensure that one PRV in the tandem set is open while the other is isolated for maintenance and/or testing, are covered in this Practice.

## 6.18 Valve Packing

Requirements covering stem packing for valves are covered in REP 5–3–23.

## 6.19 Valve Inspection and Testing

Requirements for inspection and testing of valves are covered in REP 5–3–24. These requirements include procedures for pressure testing, material hardness limitations for valves in aggressive environmental services, and casting quality and inspection guidelines for valves.

## 6.20 Flue Gas Block and Bypass Valves

Requirements governing the design, inspection, and testing of flue gas block and bypass valves are covered in REP 5–3–18.

## 6.21 Double Flanged High-Performance Rotary Valves for Hydrocarbon Service

Mandatory requirements governing the design, inspection, and testing of double flanged rotary valves (butterfly) for hydrocarbon service are covered in REP 5–3–20.

## 6.22 Valve Reconditioning – Gate, Globe, Check, Ball and Plug Valves

Requirements covering valve reconditioning of manual valves are covered in REP 5–11–5.

## 6.23 Valve Reconditioning – Pressure Relief Valves

Requirements covering valve reconditioning of PRVs are covered in REP 5–11–6.
