**3.4.7** High Pressure Fluid Service – A fluid/material service, as defined in accordance with ASME B31.3, in which the potential for personnel exposure is judged to be significant and in which Owner specifies the use of Chapter IX of ASME B31.3 for piping design and construction (pressure in excess of that allowed by the ASME B16.5 Class 2500 rating); (see also paragraph K300 of ASME B31.3).

**3.4.8** Normal Fluid Service – A fluid/material service, as defined in accordance to ASME B31.3.

**3.4.9** Fatigue/Cyclic Service – A service in which specific piping components or joints operate under conditions that will produce fatigue damage due to cycling condition. Sources of cycling loads on conditions include but are not limited to the following:

1. Pressure cycling.
2. Thermal cycling.
3. Mechanical load cycling not induced by pressure.
4. Loads associated with vibration that may arise from such sources as impact, pressure pulsation, turbulent flow vortices, resonance in compressors, and wind.
5. Any combination of loads described above.

Specific fatigue service design considerations are provided in ASME B31.3.

Examples of piping components operating in fatigue service include the following:

- Reciprocating compressor piping.
- Centrifugal compressor piping.
- High pressure to low pressure let-down valves.
- Relief valve piping operating below 100% of Relief Valve (RV) setting (relief valve chatter).
- Steam traps.
- Know piping circuits of water hammering.
- Drains with components without proper gussets.
- Piping containing two phase flow which induces cyclic stresses.

Other classifications of service are used in other associated piping REPs to classify or define a specific service condition that might affect design, selection, construction, and erection of piping systems.

**3.5** Inspector – A Industry Source 05 appointed engineer or Inspector.

**3.6** Manufacturer – The recipient of a direct or indirect purchase order for materials and/or equipment. In this context, a direct order is one issued to a manufacturer by a contractor or Owner. An indirect order is one issued to a manufacturer by a vendor (recipient of a direct order) for materials, fabricated components, or sub-assemblies.

**3.7** Owner – Industry Source 05.

**3.8** Owner's Engineer – A Industry Source 05 appointed engineer.

**3.9** Piping Service Class – Simplified classification of piping system as established in API 570, based on the risk classification and that is used to establish frequency and extent of inspection. This classification includes four piping service classes, 1 through 4.
