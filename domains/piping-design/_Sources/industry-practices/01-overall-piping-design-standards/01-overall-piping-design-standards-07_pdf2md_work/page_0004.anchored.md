## 1.0 SCOPE

**1.1** This Practice covers the design, selection, fabrication, erection, inspection, and testing of all piping systems within refinery limits. This Practice stipulates overall requirements for these items and is arranged as an index to all of the piping Practices to facilitate information retrieval by users.

**1.2** Any deviation from this Practice must be approved by the procedure described in REP 1–1–3.

**1.3** An asterisk (*) indicates that a decision by Owner's Engineer or Owner is required, or that additional information is furnished by the Purchaser.

**1.4** A revision bar indicates all changes made to this Revision.
