**4.3.7.3** The design of storage tank suction lines shall be based on an NPSH taken from the lowest specified liquid level in the tank at which rated pump capacity is required. If vortex-breaking equipment is required, the pressure drop through the equipment shall be considered.

**4.3.7.4** In sizing suction lines for reciprocating pumps, acceleration head shall be considered.

**4.3.8** The pressure drop available for control valves shall be as specified in REP 5-3-17.

**4.3.9** In areas where the level of noise is a concern, the requirements of the *Industry Source 05 Engineering Services Recommended Environmental Guidelines* shall be followed.

## 4.4 Piping Line Lists

**4.4.1** (*) Unless otherwise specified by Owner's Engineer, the Piping Line List (PLL) shall contain the following information for each line:

**4.4.1.1** Line Description:

1. Line number per this Practice, including: the nominal pipe size, unit identifier, line service symbol, line sequence number, Industry Source 05 standard piping class per REP 5-2-3, and insulation code.
2. Pipe thickness (inches) schedule identification and specified corrosion allowance (inches).
3. Origin and termination points.
4. Piping & Instrumentation Drawing (P&ID) drawing number range with contains the referenced line.
5. Service description.

**4.4.1.2** Design Conditions:

1. Design pressure (psi) and temperature (°F).
2. Normal operating pressure (psi) and temperature (°F).
3. Upset pressure (psi) and temperature (°F) per this Practice.
4. Test pressure (psi) (indicate hydrostatic or Industry Source 07 Doc umatic).
5. MDT (°F) and coincident pressure (psi).

**4.4.1.3** Flow Conditions:

1. Fluid phase description: liquid, vapor, mixed or two-phase, or slurry.
2. Velocity (ft/sec) and flow rate (lbs/hr).
3. Density (lbs/ft³).
4. Computed head loss (lbs/in²/100ft).

**4.4.1.4** Additional Notes:

1. Identifier to indicate Steam Traced (ST) or Electrical Traced (ET) lines.
2. Identifier to indicate Hydrostatic (H) or Industry Source 07 Doc umatic (P) pressure test.
3. Identifier to indicate a Stress Relieved (SR) line.
4. Identifier to indicate underground Coated and Wrapped (CW) pipe.
