### 11.3 Strainers for Rotating and Other Equipment

Requirements governing the design and application of temporary and permanent strainers for the protection of equipment are covered in REP 5–6–5. This basic Practice does not cover metal screen or porous media filters with openings finer than 150 mm.

## 12.0 PIPING FOR FIRED HEATERS

Requirements governing the design, layout, and arrangement of piping systems connected to fired heaters, including fuel gas systems, are covered in REP 5–6–3.

## 13.0 REQUIREMENTS FOR IN-SERVICE PIPING SYSTEMS

### 13.1 Inspection, Repair, Alteration, and Rerating of In-Service Piping Systems

Requirements for inspection, repair, alteration, and rerating of in-service piping systems are covered in REP 5–11–2. This Practice is a supplement to API 570.

### 13.2 Requirements for On-line Leak Repairs

Requirements for performing on-stream repairs leaks in flanges and valve packing are covered in REP 5–11–3. In addition, the on-stream repairs for pipe flanges covered in REP 5–11–3 may be used for pressure vessel nozzle and girth flanges.

### 13.3 Installation of Repair Patches and Sleeves

Requirements governing the design and installation of surface repair patches and sleeves that are fillet welded directly to the pipe, flush insert patches that are butt welded directly to the pipe, and flush insert patches that are butt welded to the pipe are covered in REP 5–11–4. The requirements of REP 5–11–4 apply to damaged areas that are either in-service or out-of-service and are not leaking.

### 13.4 Evaluation and Reuse of In-Service Piping Systems

Requirement covering the evaluation and reuse of in-service piping systems constructed to ASME B31.3 are covered in REP 5–11–2.

## 14.0 PIPING FOR STEAM SYSTEMS

### 14.1 Steam Piping

Requirements governing the design, layout, and arrangement of steam piping systems are covered in REP 5–6–8.

### 14.2 Steam Traps

Requirements for the selection and use of steam traps are covered in REP 5–6–7.

### 14.3 Steam Tracing

Requirements governing the design, layout, and arrangement of steam tracing systems are covered in REP 5–6–8.
