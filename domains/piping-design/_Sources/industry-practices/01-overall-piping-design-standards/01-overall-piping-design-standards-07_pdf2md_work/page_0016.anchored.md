## 5.0 PIPING SELECTION AND PURCHASE GUIDELINES

### 5.1 Selection of Piping Components and Materials

**5.1.1** Basic requirements for the selection and application of piping components and materials for refineries, petrochemical plants, and similar industrial installations are covered in REP 5–2–1.

**5.1.2** The requirements in REP 5–2–1 establish the basis for the Industry Source 05 Piping Standards and shall be followed when additions, changes, or new specification standards are necessary. The Industry Source 05 piping standards are listed in REP 5–2–3.

### 5.2 Flanges, Gaskets, and Bolting

**5.2.1** Requirements governing the selection of flanges, flange facings, gaskets, bolting, and the design of flanged joints for piping are covered in REP 5–2–2.

**5.2.2** The requirements of REP 5–2–2 establish the general basis for the Industry Source 05 Piping Standards and shall be followed when additions or changes are necessary.

**5.2.3** Orifice flanges are not within the scope of this Practice.

### 5.3 Industry Source 05 Piping Standards

The selection of pipe and piping components for general service categories are covered in REP 5–2–3.

### 5.4 Supplemental Requirements for Pipe and Piping Components

Supplemental requirements including data to be furnished by the Purchaser, manufacturer's responsibility, documentation, marking, packaging, and shipping for pipe and piping components are covered in REP 5–2–4. This Practice shall be used in conjunction with REP 5–2–3 to purchase these components.

### 5.5 Valve Data Sheets

Valve data sheets for all valves contained in Industry Source 05 Piping Standards (see REP 5–2–3) are provided in REP 5–3–2. The data sheets included in this Practice are suitable for procurement and should be included in all purchase orders for valves. In addition, criteria for completing valve data sheets for specialty valves (safety valves, reformer Motor-Operated Valves [MOVs] and Fluid Catalytic Cracking Units [FCCU] slide valves) are given in the respective valve Practices. Requirements for HF service are covered in REP 5–10–1.

### 5.6 Metal Flexible Hose

Mandatory requirements governing the design, fabrication, inspection, testing, and installation of metal flexible hoses are covered in REP 5–4–4.

## 6.0 VALVES

### 6.1 Valve Design and Selection Criteria

**6.1.1** Requirements governing the design and selection of valves, including the use of single and double block valves are covered in REP 5–3–1.

**6.1.2** Specific valve design requirements and general purchase requirements are covered in supplemental practices.
