000-PES-5000
Piping Design

4.1.1   Piping shall be sized for normal operating conditions. Provision for future increased capacity shall be made only when specifically required.

4.1.2   Each line shall be sized individually in accordance with good engineering practice and economical service. The following velocity and pressure values shall be used as a guide for line sizing. Consideration shall also be given to length of line, working pressure, availability of pressure drop, vapour pressure of fluid, required pump NPSH and any other considerations of significance. In instances where normal operations requires a large pressure drop between the terminals of line, the line shall be sized to take an appreciable amount of the drop rather than through a valve.

**4.2 Velocities**

Velocities in lines shall not exceed the following:

4.2.1

Light hydrocarbons and water:

Pump discharge    1.5 - 2.5 m/s    (5-8 fps)
Pump suction      0.9 m/s          (3 fps)
Gravity flow      0.76 m/s         (2.5 fps)

4.2.2

Gases:

100 psig              18-24 m/s    (60-80 fps)
200 psig              15-18 m/s    (50-60 fps)
300 psig              12-15 m/s    (40-50 fps)
500 psig              9-12 m/s     (30-40 fps)
1000 psig             3-6 m/s      (10-20 fps)
Compressor suction    15 m/s       (50 fps)
Compressor discharge  18 m/s       (60 fps)

4.2.3

Steam:

0 - 30 psig (saturated)            30 m/s    (100 fps)
30 - 150 psig (saturated or superheated)  50 m/s    (167 fps)
150 psig and up (superheated)       75 m/s    (250 fps)

In corrosive service line velocities shall be reduced to 80% or less of the maximum.
