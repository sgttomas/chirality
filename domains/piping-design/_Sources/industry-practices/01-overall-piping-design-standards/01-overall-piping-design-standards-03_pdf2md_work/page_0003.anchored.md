## 3.0 DESIGN CONDITIONS

### 3.1 Operating Conditions

3.1.1 Normal design conditions of pressure and temperature shall be the most severe conditions expected to co-exist under long-time operating conditions. These usual operations include all manipulation and control functions such as throttling, blocking and bypassing likely to be used for operation and control.

3.1.2 Usual operating conditions do not include more severe temporary conditions, such as those incidental to start-up, shutdown, steam-out or abnormal operations. Temporary conditions govern as design conditions only when there is clear evidence they definitely exceed time and severity limits in paragraph 302.2.4 of Refinery Piping Code, ANSI B31.3.

### 3.2 Design Temperature

3.2.1 Design temperature is the most severe sustained fluid temperature, subject to conditions of Section 3.1 above. Design temperature of internally insulated or non-insulated components may be the metal temperature rather than the fluid temperature.

3.2.2 Design temperature for steam traced piping shall be the fluid temperature or 10° C below saturation temperature of tracing steam, whichever is greater.

3.2.3 Design temperature for piping with a fluid operating temperature below -29° C, shall be the minimum fluid operating temperature. In addition, design shall be suitable for design pressure at 38° C.

### 3.3 Design Pressure

3.3.1 Design pressure shall be the most severe condition of internal or external fluid pressure, subject to conditions of Section 3.1 above.

3.3.2 Minimum positive design pressure shall normally be 175 kPA.

3.3.3 Design pressure for unrelieved piping systems subject to pump discharge pressure, shall be pump differential pressure at shutoff of stalling conditions, plus normal pump suction pressure and hydrostatic head.

3.3.4 Design pressure for piping systems operating under vacuum shall be full vacuum. Exception may be taken where suitable protection against vacuum failure is provided.

3.3.5 For piping systems protected by relief valves, the relief valve set pressure shall be equal to the design pressure minus the hydrostatic head below the relief valve.

### 3.4 Other Conditions

3.4.1 Allowable stress values apply to total loads imposed on piping materials. Design shall provide for all loading significantly affecting pipe material stresses. See sections 301.4 through 301.7 of Refinery Piping Code for loadings in addition to fluid pressure, which may affect piping stresses.
