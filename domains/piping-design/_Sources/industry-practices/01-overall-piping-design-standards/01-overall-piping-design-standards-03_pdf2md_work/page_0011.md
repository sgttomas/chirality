## 11.0 SAMPLE CONNECTIONS

### 11.1 Requirements

**11.1.1** When specified, double valved sample connections (using a NPS 3/4 main block valve and a NPS 1/2 bar stock needle valve for regulation) shall be provided as required to permit full evaluation without disconnecting piping or instruments during plant performance tests. Every line containing a process flow meter shall be provided with a sample connection. Liquid sample connections shall be taken off the side of the pipe.

## 12.0 SOUR SERVICE REQUIREMENTS

Piping in sour service shall be designed per Owner's General Specification Sour Service, 000-PES-5001 Piping Material Service Index and 000-PES-5002 Piping Material Line Class Detail. Design for critical sour service is outside the scope of this specification.

### 12.1 Threaded Piping and Instrument Connections

**12.2.1** Threaded connections shall be classified according to the following definitions, and use of threaded connections shall comply with Table 12-2:

- (a) Primary threaded connections are connections made directly to a process stream, without capability for isolation from the process by means of upstream flanged, socket welded, or socket welded by screwed block valves connected directly to the process by welds, flanges, or welded fittings.

- (b) Secondary threaded connections are connections with capability for isolation from the process by means of upstream flanged, socket welded, or socket welded by screwed block valves connected directly to the process by welds, flanges, or welded fittings.

**12.2.2** When threaded connections are used for sour service, the following conditions apply:

- (a) Pipe diameter shall be less than NPS 2.

- (b) Pipe thickness shall be Schedule 80 minimum.

- (c) Threading shall be only be accomplished by a cutting operation. Rolled threads shall not be used without the written concurrence of the Owner engineer. If rolled threads are considered, a suitable heat treatment shall be applied after thread rolling, so that the microhardness at all thread roots is below 248 HV₁₀₀. Microhardness test data shall be supplied to support any request for use of rolled threads.

- (d) Connections shall be located away from areas where liquid water may accumulate.

- (e) Requirements of applicable codes, standards and specifications shall be satisfied.
