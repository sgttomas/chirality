### 4.4 Other Limitations

4.4.1 NPS 3/8, 1-1/4, 2-1/2, 3-1/2, 5, and 7 pipe sizes shall not be used, except where required to connect to equipment. Conversion to standard piping shall be as near the equipment as possible.

4.4.2 Minimum pipe size run shall be NPS 3/4, except for connections to equipment or instrument services.

4.4.3 In pipeway runs the minimum size for process and utility piping shall be NPS 2 or as otherwise governed by length of span.

4.4.4 All piping size NPS 2 and larger shall be flanged/butt-welded unless otherwise specified.

4.4.5 In threaded piping, swage nipples shall be used for change in pipe size. Bushings are not permitted.

## 5.0 EQUIPMENT PIPING

### 5.1 Compressor Piping

5.1.1 Large centrifugal compressors may be elevated sufficiently for piping and auxiliaries to be located below main operating platforms. Smaller compressors shall be grade mounted with piping and auxiliaries arranged for convenient access and maintenance.

5.1.2 Particular consideration shall be given to design of piping subject to vibration from dynamic loading associated with reciprocating compressors. Volume bottles shall be provided as required at the suctions and discharges. Suction and discharge lines shall be securely clamped. Small piping around compressors and on the same supports as suction and discharge lines shall be well braced to reduce vibration. Reciprocating compressor suction and discharge piping shall be run on substantial supports near grade if at all possible.

5.1.3 The piping drawings shall show the limits of compressor suction piping where special internal cleaning is required. Temporary screens or strainers shall be provided.

5.1.4 Removable spool pieces shall be provided at compressors where needed to permit maintenance without major piping disassembly.

## 6.0 PRESSURE RELIEF SYSTEMS

### 6.1 General

6.1.1 A schedule showing the location, type, marking size, flange rating, orifice identification, area and setting pressure of all pressure relief devices on each unit shall be supplied to Owner. This shall also include the type of fluid, gravity or molecular weight, maximum operating inlet pressure and temperature and relieving capacity as well as the presence of any corrosive agent.

6.1.2 Consideration shall be given to providing pressure relief devices between block valves where thermal expansion due to ambient temperature changes or steam tracing may cause damage.
