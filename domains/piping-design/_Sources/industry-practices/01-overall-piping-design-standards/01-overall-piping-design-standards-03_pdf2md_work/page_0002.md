## 1.0 GENERAL

### 1.1 Scope

This specification describes the basis for the overall design of process and utility piping and shall apply to all piping systems and parts of such systems.

### 1.2 Intent

The intent of this specification is to provide for a complete package in accordance with standard practice. This is a general specification, and therefore subject to variations in materials, design and construction, provided that the requirements of standard practice are met. Prior approval shall be obtained from the Owner before making any change to these specifications.

### 1.3 Conflicting Requirements

Should a conflict arise between this specification and other specifications or government regulations, the Vendor shall contact the Owner and a decision reached in writing before any work is done.

### 1.4 Vendor Responsibility

The Vendor shall be responsible for conformance to governing codes, Owner's specifications, drawings, legal and contractual requirements. In the event of on compliance with the foregoing, the Owner may correct any deficiencies, and the cost of such repairs shall be charged to the Vendor.

## 2.0 CODE REQUIREMENTS

### 2.1 Codes and Standards

Minimum piping requirements are to be equal to or shall exceed requirements of latest editions of the following applicable codes. Any mandatory federal, provincial or local codes in effect at the project site are to be complied with:

- Local pressure piping regulating authority
- ABSA, Alberta Boilers Safety Association
- API 520, Design and Installation of Pressure-Relieving Systems
- API 521, Guide for Pressure-Relieving and Depressurizing Systems
- ASME, B31.3, Chemical Plant & Petroleum Refinery Piping
- ASME, Section IX, Welding & Brazing Requirements
- CSA Z662, Oil & Gas Pipeline Systems
- Department of Labour, Boilers Branch for the applicable province
- OHSA, Occupational Health and Safety Act
- Regulations of the applicable province and Canada
