## 4.3 Pressure Drop Requirements

4.3.1 Pressure drop shall not exceed the following for Pump Suction and Discharge Lines:

[TABLE: Pump Suction and Discharge pressure drop limits]

| | **Pump Suction** | **Pump Discharge** |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Water, amine, caustic and hydrocarbon liquids more than 28°C below boiling point. | 11.3 kPA/100m (0.5 psi/100') | 22.6 kPA/100m (1 psi/100') |
| Water, amine, caustic and hydrocarbon liquids less than 28°C below boiling point. | 2.26 kPA/100m (0.1 psi/100') | 22.6 kPA/100m (1 psi/100') |

NPSH considerations shall govern selection of pipe size on pump suctions; however, pressure drop shall not exceed maximum set out above. On plunger pumps size shall also account for acceleration head loss.

4.3.2 For reboiler vapour and liquid gas individual attention shall be given to each case considering the hydraulics for the system and operating levels resulting from the design pressure drops.

4.3.3 Size of compressor suction and discharge piping shall be determined in accordance with compressor manufacturer's recommendation. Pressure drops shall not exceed the following:

[TABLE: Suction and Discharge Piping and Headers pressure drop limits]

**Suction and Discharge Piping**

| | **kPA/100m** | **(PSI/100')** |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| 10 psig and under | 2.8 | 0.125 |
| 11 to 50 psig | 5.7 | 0.25 |
| over 50 psig | 22.6 | 1.0 |

**Headers**

| | **kPA/100m** | **(PSI/100')** |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| 75 psig and under | 11.3 | 0.5 |
| over 75 psig | 22.6 | 1.0 |

4.3.4 Piping in flare systems shall be adequately sized to prevent over pressuring of equipment in case of an operational failure. Flare header shall be designed for largest vapour load caused by a single failure or full ESD blowdown capacity - greater shall apply.

4.3.5 For other lines, pressure drops shall not exceed the following:

| | **kPA/100m** | **(PSI/100')** |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Steam - above 50 psig | 22.6 kPA/100m | (1.0 psi/100') |
| - below 50 psig | 11.3 kPA/100m | (0.5 psi/100') |
| Flashing steam condensate return | 11.3 kPA/100m | (0.5 psi/100') |
| Cooling water | 22.6 kPA/100m | (1.0 psi/100') |
| Hydrocarbon vapours | 11.3 kPA/100m | (0.5 psi/100') |
| Hydrocarbon liquids | 22.6 kPA/100m | (1.0 psi/100') |
| Hot oil and condenser effluents | 45.2 kPA/100m | (2.0 psi/100') |
