9.3 Control valve by-pass piping shall provide sufficient clearance for the removal of the control valve from the loop.

9.4 All control valves shall be provided with block, and by-pass valves rated for the same design pressure as the upstream piping.

9.5 By-pass valves shall be designed for a capacity at least equal to the capacity of the control valve.

9.6 Flanged end reducers or spools shall be used between flanged control valves and block valves. The length of the reducers or spools shall be sufficient to permit removal of bolts, and the installation of drain or vent connections.

9.7 Valved NPS 1 drains shall be provided upstream of control valves.

9.8 All control valves in socket weld piping shall be flanged.

9.9 The only devices which may be mounted on control valves shall be valve positioners, volume boosters, lock-up relays and pressure controllers. These devices shall be installed so as to be easily accessible for servicing. The devices shall not be so mounted when vibration may cause malfunction.

9.10 Orifice flanges shall be located adjacent to walkways, platforms, or structures and shall be installed in horizontal pipe runs, wherever possible. Orifice flanges shall have socket weld taps except where screwed connections are specifically permitted in Owner's Specification GS 2.3 Line Class Detail.

9.11 Instrument air distribution headers shall be NPS 2 minimum. A block valve shall be provided for each take-off connection from these headers.

9.12 All gauge glasses and instruments in process services shall be provided with NPS 1/2 minimum drain connections, piped to their respective closed drain systems. They shall be supplied complete with isolation valves in addition to the gauge cocks.

## 10.0 THERMAL TRACING

10.1 All equipment, piping and instrumentation which may be subject to freezing shall be heat traced per Owner's General Specification Industry Source 06 Doc 5005 Winterization.
