## 6.2 Piping

6.2.1  The size of the inlet line shall not be less than the size of the relief valve inlet. The size of the outlet line shall not be less than the size of the relief valve outlet.

6.2.2  The length of the inlet piping shall be kept to a minimum.

6.2.3  At maximum flow, the pressure drop due to entrance losses and pipe friction shall not exceed three percent of the set pressure of any valve.

## 6.3 Discharge to Atmosphere

6.3.1  A pressure relief valve discharging vapours to atmosphere shall be provided with outlet piping as follows:

6.3.1.1  For hydrocarbons, shall be vented a minimum of two metres above building peak. Regardless of height or conditions the riser shall be at least 15 m horizontally from any furnace.

6.3.1.2  For steam, to atmosphere at a safe location.

6.3.1.3  A pressure relief valve in liquid service and discharging to atmosphere shall do so at a visible safe place.

## 6.4 Piping to Closed System

6.4.1  Any pressure relief valve discharging to a closed system shall be installed so that the discharge piping slopes into the header.

6.4.2  Blowdown headers shall be installed so that the piping slopes into the blowdown drum. Where this is not practical, the low points in the header shall be traced and insulated, and a NPS 3/4 valved drain tie shall be provided.

6.4.3  The bonnet vent of a relief valve with bellows shall be piped to the discharge line.

## 6.5 Block Valves

6.5.1  Where a block valve is installed to permit removal of a relief valve during unit operation it shall be located immediately below the relief valve, and be car-sealed open.

6.5.2  Where a pressure relief valve discharges into a header and an outlet block valve is installed, this valve shall be car-sealed open.

6.5.3  Car-sealed open valves shall be numbered on the engineering flow plans and a list of numbers and locations shall be supplied to Owner.

6.5.4  Block valves at relief valves and in blowdown lines shall be positioned so that the block valve remains open if any component of the valve accidentally fails.
