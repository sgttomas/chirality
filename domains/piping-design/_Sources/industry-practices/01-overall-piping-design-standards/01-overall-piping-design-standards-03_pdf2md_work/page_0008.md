6.5.6 An NPS 3/4 bleeder valve shall be provided between any block and pressure relief valve.

6.5.7 The size of the inlet valve shall not be less than the size of the relief valve inlet. The size of the outlet valve shall not be less than the size of the relief valve outlet.

**6.6 Location**

6.6.1 Relief valves may be located on the piping instead of on the equipment and shall be located at the lowest possible elevation that may be economically justified.

## 7.0 FLEXIBILITY

### 7.1 General

Piping shall be designed with sufficient flexibility to absorb stress due to the following:

7.1.1 Line expansion or contraction as a result of operating or ambient conditions.

7.1.2 Expansion or contraction of connected equipment.

7.1.3 Settlement of equipment or tankage.

### 7.2 Design

7.2.1 Stresses due to thermal expansion or contraction shall be analyzed by the methods in ANSI B31.3 or ANSI B31.1 as applicable.

7.2.2 Piping shall normally be laid out to provide sufficient flexibility without the use of expansion loops or joints.

7.2.3 Anchors and guides shall be provided where necessary to minimize the movement of lines across supports.

### 7.3 Provision for Expansion

Where the layout does not provide sufficient flexibility the following may be used:

7.3.1 Expansion loops with guides and anchors.

7.3.2 Cold springing.

7.3.3 Expansion joints with guides and anchors, when approved by Owner.

7.3.4 Spring loaded supports or hangers where rigid supports restrict line movement or cause over stressing of the line.
