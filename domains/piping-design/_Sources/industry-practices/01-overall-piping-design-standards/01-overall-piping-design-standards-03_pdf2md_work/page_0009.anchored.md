8.1.1 Supports shall normally be designed to transmit all piping loads directly to foundations.

8.1.2 Loadings to be considered in design calculations include:

- Weight of pipe and insulation
- Weight of the line contents based on water
- Wind loading
- Lateral loading due to movement of lines on supports, guides and anchors
- Vibrations due to pulsations from reciprocating pumps, compressors and possible line surges.

8.1.3 The material for pipe supports shall be structural steel or reinforced concrete, unless otherwise approved in writing by Owner.

## 8.2 Requirements

8.2.1 Supports shall be located so that the force transmitted to connections on machinery such as pumps, compressors, turbines, etc. does not exceed that allowed by the machinery manufacturer. The design of such supports shall be detailed and not left for field improvisation.

8.2.2 Supports shall be located to minimize the load transmitted to connections of equipment such as vessels, exchangers, etc.

8.2.3 Piping to equipment requiring removal for maintenance, including control valve assemblies shall be supported so that temporary supports are not required during removal.

## 8.3 Shoes and Guides

8.3.1 Shoes shall be installed on insulated pipe at supports.

8.3.2 Non-insulated lines shall rest directly on the supports but shoes shall be provided on lines subject to excessive or continuous movement.

8.3.3 Guides shall be installed on supports to restrict lateral movement.

## 8.4 Spring Supports and Hangers

8.4.1 Spring supports may be either variable or constant load type provided that overstressing of line or nozzles is not produced.

8.4.2 Variable sprint hangers and supports shall be provided with a scale showing the range of working load and a means of adjustment.

8.4.3 Constant support spring hangers shall be provided with the calibrated load clearly indicated, a scale showing the range of movement and an accurate means of adjustment.

8.4.4 All spring supports and support shall be adjusted in the field outer unit start-up and in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions.
