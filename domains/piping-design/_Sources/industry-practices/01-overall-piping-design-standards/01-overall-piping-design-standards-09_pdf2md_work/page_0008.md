The ordering description for each piping component includes material, process of manufacture, pressure rating, physical attributes, manufacturer, special trim or construction, etc. The full ordering description (long) is used to place an order.

The Chevron L-numbers are generated from a comprehensive classification index that segregates piping components in a systematic order. The item coding requires 5 to 9 alphanumeric characters, depending on the type of component. Each code character describes a particular feature of the component and the complete item code chronicles the significant identifying features. The heart of the coding system is the classification index found in the first section of the *Piping Item Description Database* under the tab "Index." Refer to the "Foreword" for an example of how the description is categorized.

Piping components cannot be created simply by the construction of an L-number. The items must already exist as a manufactured item conforming to one of the national or industrial standards (ASME, ANSI, API, MSS) that will satisfy the requirements of the ASME/ANSI B31 section used as the design basis. The manufacturer should be technically and commercially acceptable.

The *Piping Item Description Database* valve descriptions contain figure and/or model numbers obtained from published information provided by the manufacturers. Most valve manufacturers print a disclaimer indicating that they reserve the right to change their catalog without notice. Because of this, the full text of the item description on your purchase order is especially important.

Minimum inspection requirements are normally inherent in the purchase order if one of the national standards (e.g., ASME, ANSI, API, MSS) has been included in the ordering description. Additional inspection and nondestructive examination may be required because of process conditions. These requirements can normally be handled by one of the notes located in the "Material Ordering Description Notes" pages of the Database.

Item selections should be based on three considerations relating to process conditions (1) type of material, (2) design pressure, and (3) design temperature. Technical consulting services dealing with material selection, piping components, ASME/ANSI piping codes, acceptable manufacturers, and quality assurance are available through CRTC's Materials and Equipment Engineering group. Related commercial information is available through Chevron U.S.A. Purchasing Department.

During the course of a project, distributors or alternate manufacturers will offer "equal to" items. Prudent bid evaluation is highly recommended with the burden of proof for documentation placed on the bidder. A good way to limit the number of "equal to" evaluations is to request quotations from only those who represent the manufacturers already reviewed by CRTC's Materials and Equipment Engineering - Quality Assurance.
