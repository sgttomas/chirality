## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This Practice provides requirements for designing metallic piping systems to *ASME B31.3, Process Piping* (hereinafter referred to as the *Code*).

### 1.2 Scope

This Practice provides requirements for piping systems arrangement, design conditions, components, and joints. This Practice shall be used in conjunction with the *Code*.

The requirements of this Practice are more stringent than the *Code* for the purpose of standardization and not because the *Code* is considered unsafe.

Piping systems designed in accordance with other piping codes are not included in this Practice.
