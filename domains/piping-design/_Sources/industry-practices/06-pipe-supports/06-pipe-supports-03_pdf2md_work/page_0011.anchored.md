## 5. MARKING AND PAINTING

Painting shall be in accordance with DEP 30.48.00.31-Gen.

Except for small items which have restricted space for die-stamping (e.g. hanger rods, eye nuts, etc.) each standard support component shall, in addition to paint marking, be die-stamped with its size and identification mark.

The items which are not die-stamped shall have their size and identification legibly marked on a securely attached durable tag.

Hot-dip galvanised supports shall be die-stamped with their identification mark and serial number before galvanising. After hot dip galvanising, these marks and numbers shall be painted on.
