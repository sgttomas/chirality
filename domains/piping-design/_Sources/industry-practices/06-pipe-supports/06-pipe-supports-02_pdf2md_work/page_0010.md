## 17.0 Relief Valve Stacks

17.1 Relief valve stacks venting to atmosphere may be categorized into short (under 6' - 0") and long (up to 20' - 0"). Short stacks do not need special supporting consideration unless relieving high pressures

17.2 Long stacks require supports to prevent pipe load and relief forces for overstressing the nozzle. Guides must allow vertical thermal movement but prevent rotation due to vertical loads and forces and thus take the moment off the nozzle. These are classed as special supports.

## 18.0 Pipeway Piping

18.1 Pipeway piping may be divided into short and long pipe runs. The short runs are generally process lines between pieces of equipment. Long pipe runs, while not limited to any one area, are characteristic of off plot piping.

18.2 All long pipe runs should be anchored to prevent creeping. See anchor details Types DA1 thru DA11. Hot lines expand away from their anchors and cold lines contract towards them.

18.3 The locations of pipe anchors, critical guides and expansion loops are designated by the stress analysis group.

## 19.0 Pipe Support Shoes

19.1 Insulated lines are provided with shoes to allow movement over the supports.

19.2 The shoes must be sized and located properly to permit movement without running off the supports. Should the shoes run off the supports, followed by a line temperature reversal, the supports could be broken or the line ruptured.

19.3 All hot or cold insulated lines, irrespective of size, shall be provided with shoes at all supports.

19.4 Shoe lengths are 12", 18", 24", 30", 36" and 40", as per Standard Detail Drawings.
