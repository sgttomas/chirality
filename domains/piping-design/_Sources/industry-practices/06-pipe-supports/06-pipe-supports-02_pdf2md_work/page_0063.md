**NOTES:**

1. ALL STRUCTURAL MEMBERS ARE ASTM A36/A36M AND ARE WELDED, AS REQUIRED, TO PIPES AND EACH OTHER. ALL WELDS SHALL BE PROPER FIT AND OF THE PROPER QUALITY FOR THE WORK BEING PERFORMED.
2. PIPE SUPPORTS SHALL NOT BE ATTACHED TO INSTRUMENT PIPING. TRUNNIONS NEED NOT BE WELDED TO PIPE.
3. TRUNNIONS SHALL BE MINIMUM SCHEDULE TO MATCH PIPE. PROPER FITTING QUALITY IS REQUIRED.
4. PIPE WELD DONE USING D1.1 OR EQUIVALENT WELDING PROCEDURE QUALIFIED TO APPLICABLE DESIGN CODE. WELDER TO BE QUALIFIED TO D1.1 TO PERFORM THE WELD.
5. THE LOAD LOAD IS THE FULL LOAD INCLUDING SEISMIC LOADING USING THE STATIC LOAD APPROACH.
6. THE SUPPORT LOAD IS THE FULL LOAD INCLUDING SEISMIC LOAD WHICH THE SUPPORT IS DESIGNED.
