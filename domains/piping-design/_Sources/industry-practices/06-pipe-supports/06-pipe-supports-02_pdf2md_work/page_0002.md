## 1.0 Preamble

1.1 This engineering standard is established to update without losing sight of the philosophy of the original plant design established by the Bechtel Corporation (1965) "The Basic Pipe Support Design Manual".

1.2 This standard references the existing identification system and pre-engineered components manufactured by the Grinnell Corporation. Alternate manfacturers' components may be submitted for approval by the Owner's Engineer.

## 2.0 Scope

This manual outlines the general methods used for supporting, guiding and anchoring piping systems.

## 3.0 Codes and Regulations

### 3.1 Codes

All pipe support components shall comply with the latest edition of the following codes and standards. These documents shall be deemed to form part of this specification.

  Alberta Building Code

  ASME B31.1, Power Piping Code, latest edition.

  ASTM A325, High Strength Bolts for Structural Joints
- 3.1.4 Canadian Standards Association (CSA)
