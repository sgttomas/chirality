## 4.0 Definitions

### 4.1 Abnormal Conditions

Abnormal conditions, as related to requirements for piping anchor design, refer to conditions encountered in pipe lines during:

(a) A loss of cooling medium flow.
(b) Cessation of process flow while line heating facilities remain operative.
(c) Anticipatable startup and shutdown requirements, including steam out and process upsets causing temperature fluctuations.

### 4.2 Anchor Bays

Anchor bays are elevated pipe supports acting as anchors, which use cross braces to transmit thrust to the anchor bay foundations by tensile and compressive member forces.

## 5.0 Materials

### 5.1 Structural Steel

Unless otherwise specified, all standard and special pipe support brackets and their structural members shall be manufactured using the materials specified below. Alternative materials shall only be used with prior approval of the Owner's Engineer.

(a) Structural Steel CSA - G40.21
(b) Bolting ASTM A325 High Strength Bolts for Structural Joints

### 5.2 Fabrication

5.2.1 All welds to pressure containing parts shall meet the requirements of Suncor Energy Inc. STD-0903.

5.2.2 Welded pipe support attachments to coated, lined or stress relieved piping shall be affixed to the piping prior to coating, lining or stress relieving.

5.2.3 Pipe supports when specified, shall be painted in accordance with Suncor Energy Inc. STD-0214, "Painting". Shoes to be same color as pipe. Steel brackets to be same color as steelwork.

## 6.0 General Design Criteria

6.1 Pipe support systems and detail design shall comply with the appropriate piping codes.

6.2 Loading criteria for environmentally generated forces shall comply with the Alberta Building Code.

6.3 The structural design of support brackets shall comply with CSA S16.1.

6.4 Where pre-engineered components are specified, e.g., support clamps, spring hangers, etc., they shall comply with MSS SP-58 and SP-69.

6.5 Snow loads on piping shall be neglected. Thrust due to changes in line temperature shall be considered in the design of anchor bays.

6.6 Elevated pipe supports, other than anchor bays, shall not normally be designed to resist thrust due to changes in line temperature.
