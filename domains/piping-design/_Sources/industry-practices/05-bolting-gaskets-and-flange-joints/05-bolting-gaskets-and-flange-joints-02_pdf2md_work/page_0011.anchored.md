| Service | Gasket Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| All services with temperature limitations of −100°F to 900°F (not including chemical oxidizers) | Flexible graphite filled 0.175 inch thick spiral wound gasket per ASME B16.20 with a steel outer compression centering ring ^(1) and inner ring ^(2), ^(3), ^(4). The flexible graphite shall be asbestos free and meet the following specifications: - Density (nominal): ± 70 lbs/ft³ - Carbon content: 95 wt% min. - Leachable chlorides: 100 ppm max. - Total sulfur: 100 ppm max. - Fillers or binders: None The winding and inner ring materials shall be as follows: - Reformer MOV valves: AISI Type 316 - Hydrofluoric acid: Monel 400 ^(6) - Hydrochloric acid: Monel 400 or Hastelloy C ^(5) - All other services: AISI Type 316 ^(7) |
| All services with temperature limitations up to 1500°F in oxidizing environments (not including chemical oxidizers) | High temperature rated vermiculite or mica filled 0.175 inch thick spiral wound gasket per ASME B16.20 with a steel outer compression centering ring ^(1), and inner ring ^(2), ^(3), ^(4). The winding and inner ring materials shall be as follows: All other services: AISI Type 316 ^(7) |
| Chemical oxidizers (e.g., sulfuric acid, and phosphoric acids −100°F to 400°F) | PTFE (Teflon®) filled 0.175 inch thick spiral wound gaskets per ASME B16.20 with a steel outer compression centering ring ^(1) and inner ring ^(2), ^(3), ^(4). The winding and inner ring material shall be as follows: - Sulfuric acid: Alloy 20 - All other services: AISI Type 316 |
| Oxygen | For carbon steel piping systems the filler material shall be PTFE with Monel (UNS N04400) windings, and Monel (UNS N04400) inner and outer rings. For all other oxygen piping system materials of construction, see Table 6. |
| All other services | (*) Manufacturer's recommendation requires approval of the Owner's Engineer. |

NOTES:
1) The outer compression centering ring material shall be carbon steel, except for operating temperatures below −50°F and above 1000°F, where the material of this ring shall be an austenitic stainless steel.
2) (*) Inner rings are specified per ASME B16.20 as the default, supplied option, and per that document the inner ring metallurgy should match the winding material. Owner's Engineer approval is required to order spiral wound gaskets without inner rings, or to order inner ring metallurgy different than the winding material of construction. For pipe thicknesses less than Schedule 40 and 40S engineer shall verify inner ring does not protrude into inside diameter of pipe or flange bore. Inner ring may be omitted if it does protrude into inside diameter of pipe or flange bore.
3) Inner ring materials shall be as follows for the given services: a. In Hydrofluoric Acid (HF) Service: a PTFE inner ring shall be supplied except when otherwise specified by the Owner's Engineer. b. For sizes NPS 10 and smaller in chemical oxidizer service, a PTFE inner ring shall be used.
4) Outer and inner rings are not required for flanges with a hub groove to confine the gasket.
5) Material selection depends on concentration of hydrochloric acid.
6) Supplemental information can be included in REP 5-10-1 for Phillips 66 Licensed HF Units (Alliance and Ferndale refineries) and REP 5-10-2 for UOP Licensed HF Units (Number and Trainer refineries).
7) (*) For high pressure processes using non-ferrous materials, an experienced Phillips 66 corrosion/metallurgist should be consulted for winding material selection.
