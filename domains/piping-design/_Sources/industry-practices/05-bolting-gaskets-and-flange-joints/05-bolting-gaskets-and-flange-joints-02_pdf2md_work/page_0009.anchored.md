# Flanges, Gaskets, and Bolting

**Refining Engineering Practices**
Page: 9 of 18

NOTES:
1) Steel flange rating class is in accordance with ASME B16.5, cast iron flange rating in accordance with ASME B16.1, and ductile iron flange ratings are in accordance with ASME B 16.42.
2) Gasket design temperature shall be same as flange design temperature, see paragraph 4.4 for the flange design temperature.
3) Liquid, vapor, or gas, except water or refrigerants.
4) Metallic gasket components subject to hydrogen service will be specified by the Owner's Engineer.
5) Includes natural gas service.
6) Includes fluids such as chlorine or hydrogen sulfide, contained in solutions or mixtures.
7) Includes propane, ethylene, ammonia, Freon, nitrogen, LNG.
8) Spiral wound gaskets to be in accordance with Paragraph 5.3 and Table 4.
9) Ring joints gaskets shall be in accordance with paragraph 5.4 and Table 5.
10) Sheet gaskets to be in accordance with paragraph 5.5 and Table 6.
11) Sheet gaskets compatible with the fluid service shall be used on API 650 tank manway flanges.
12) Used for construction prior to May 2004 or to match existing flanges in any rating. RTJ may be required per unit technology specification for particular units (i.e., some coker units mandate RTJ flanges).
13) Flat face flanges and full face gaskets shall be used when mating against gray cast iron flanges.
14) Supplemental requirements are included in REP 5-10-1 for Phillips 66 Licensed HF Units (Alliance and Ferndale refineries) and REP 5-10-2 for UOP Licensed HF Units (Number and Trainer refineries).
15) With Owner's Engineer Approval, spiral wound conversion gaskets with inner and outer rings (Lamons Spiraset Style WRI-RTJ or equivalent) can be used in place of ring joints on RTJ flanges. The face of the RTJ flange where the conversion spiral wound gasket will seat shall have a 125-250 µ inch arithmetic average (Ra) spiral or phonographic finish.
