## Table 6: Sheet Gasket Requirements

> **Table - Table 6 — Sheet Gasket Requirements** - see [source crop](tables/05-bolting-gaskets-and-flange-joints-02_p0013_tbl01_table-6-sheet-gasket-requirements.png)

NOTES:

1) (*) Sheet gaskets shall not be used in Hydrocarbon or steam service unless otherwise specified by the Owner's Engineer; see Table 3 for recommended gaskets.
2) Steel flange rating class in accordance with ASME B16.5, cast iron flange rating in accordance with ASME B16.1, and ductile iron flange ratings are in accordance with ASME B16.42.
3) Gasket design temperature shall be same as flange design temperature, see paragraph 4.4 for the flange design temperature, but not exceeding the temperatures listed in this table. Gasket design temperature and pressure shall not exceed the manufacturer's pressure-temperature rating.
4) All services, excluding hydrogen service, with temperature limitation of -100°F to 850°F in oxidizing environments (not including chemical oxidizers) and -100°F to 1500°F in non-oxidizing environments.
5) Flexible graphite sheet gaskets shall be UCAR Grafoil GH-E or equivalent per the following specifications. All sheet gaskets shall be branded with the manufacturer's identification.
   Nominal Density: 70 lbs/ft³ &emsp; Asbestos Content: None
   Carbon Content: 95 wt. %, min. &emsp; Leachable Chlorides: 100 ppm max.
   Total Chlorides: 200 ppm max. &emsp; Total Fluorides: 200 ppm max.
   Total Sulfur: 1000 ppm max. &emsp; Reinforcing Insert: AISI Type 316, 0.024 inch thick corrugated steel ring
6) (*) If approved by the Owner's Engineer, UCAR Grafoil GH-E or equivalent sheet gaskets per the following specification can be used. All sheet gaskets shall be branded with the Manufacturer's identification.
   Flexible Graphite: see Note (5) above &emsp; Reinforcing Insert: AISI Type 316, 0.002 inch thick sheet
   The flexible graphite sheet shall be chemically bonded to the top and bottom of the metal insert by an adhesive layer not more than 0.0015 inch thick that will carbonize when subjected to heat.
7) Flexible graphite corrugated metal sheet gasket shall be M&P Graphonic or equivalent per the following specification. All sheet gaskets shall be branded with the Manufacturer's identification.
   Flexible Graphite: see Note (5) above &emsp; Reinforcing Insert: AISI Type 316, 0.024 inch thick corrugated steel ring
   The flexible graphite sheet shall be chemically bonded to the top and bottom of the metal insert by an adhesive layer not more than 0.0015 inch thick that will carbonize when subjected to heat.
8) Phenolic gasket or equivalent electrical insulating gasket is to be used only at the flanged connection between the below ground to above ground instrument airline.
9) When approved by the Owner's Engineer, 0.0625 inch thick. Sheet gasket may be used with new or like new flanges where design conditions approach the maximum pressure-temperature gasket rating.
