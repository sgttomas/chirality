**3.6** Manufacturer – The recipient of a direct or indirect purchase order for materials and/or equipment. In this context, a direct order is one issued to a manufacturer by a contractor or the Owner. An indirect order is one issued to a manufacturer by a vendor (recipient of a direct order) for materials, fabricated components, or subassemblies.

**3.7** Owner – Phillips 66.

**3.8** Owner's Engineer – A Phillips 66 appointed engineer.

**3.9** Purchaser – The party placing a direct purchase order. The purchaser is the Owner's designated representative.

**3.10** Severe Cyclic Conditions – Thermal Load conditions producing significant fatigue effects as defined in ASME B31.3.

**4.1** (*) All flanges shall be in accordance with the applicable industry standards shown in Table 1. Permissible flange materials shall be in accordance with REP 5-2-1 and REP 7-1-1D, as applicable. The design of flanges not shown in Table 1 shall be subject to the approval of the Owner's Engineer.

**4.2** Design limits, facing and gasket requirements for piping, nozzle and manways flanges shall be in accordance with Table 2. Design limits, facing and gasket requirements for services not covered in Table 1shall be subject to the approval of the Owner's Engineer.

**4.3** Pressure-temperature ratings for flanged joints shall be per the applicable flange standard; see Table 1.

**4.4** (*) The temperature reduction permitted by ASME B31.3 and REP 5-1-1 for uninsulated flanges shall not be used unless approved by the Owner's Engineer.

**4.5** ASME B16.5 class 400 flanges shall not be used.

**4.6** (*) Flange facing finish shall be per the applicable flange standard; see Table 1. Unless otherwise specified by the Owner's Engineer, the gasket contact surface for flanges to be used with spiral wound gaskets shall have a finish within the range of 125 μ inch to 250 μ inch arithmetic average (Ra).

**4.7** (*) Proprietary piping components such as PLIDCO® flanges, Weld-Ends®, and Garlock® or Reftane® connections shall not be used without approval of Owner's Engineer. When the use of such devices is approved, they shall be designed in accordance with the ASME Code, Section VIII, ASME B31.1, or ASME B31.3 and shall be applied in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations and additional instructions (if any) from the Owner's Engineer.

**4.8** (*) The use of slip-on flanges shall be subject to the following limitations:

1. Subject to approval by the Owner's Engineer, they shall be used only as piping flanges; weld neck flanges or long weld necks shall be used for all pressure vessels and heat exchangers per REP 7-1-1D.
2. They shall not be used above 500°F or for ASME B16.5 Class 600 and higher pressure ratings.
3. They shall not be used in erosive service or corrosive service where the specified corrosion allowance exceeds 1/8-inch.
4. They shall not be used in cyclic service.
5. They shall not be used in lethal service as defined by the ASME Code.
