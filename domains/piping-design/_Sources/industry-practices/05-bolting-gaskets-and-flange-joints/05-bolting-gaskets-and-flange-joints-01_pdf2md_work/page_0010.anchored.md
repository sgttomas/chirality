e) 5th/Final Pass: 100% of final torque value applied in a circular pattern

f) Relax torque: See Sections 2.2.9 and 2.2.10 below

2.2.9 Equipment (e.g., vessels, exchangers, etc.) flanges torqued per *Section 1.1: Joints* (in this document) shall be relax torqued after the torqued joint has rested for at least four hours. The relax torque shall be completed using the final torque value and performing one circular pass around the joint. Any exceptions should be discussed with the Reliability Engineer.

2.2.10 Flanged piping joints shall be relax torqued when specified by Reliability Engineering, and in the special case of hot hydrogen service (> 500°F) when double gaskets are used on a single flange pair (e.g., spectacle blinds, orifice flanges, drip rings, spacers, wafer check and butterfly valves).

2.2.11 BP Cherry Point exchanger torque report tags shall be used on all heat exchanger torqued joints (per *Section 1.1.2 a* of this document). The tags shall be turned into the BP Turnaround (TA) Exchanger Planning group (or the BP Reliability Coordinator if exchanger work is non-TA) after flange is hammer tested by BP Operations. Exchanger torque report tags are available in the Storehouse.
