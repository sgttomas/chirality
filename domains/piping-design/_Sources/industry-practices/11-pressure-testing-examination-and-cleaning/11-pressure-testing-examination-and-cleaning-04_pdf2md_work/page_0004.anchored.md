# 1 Definitions and Abbreviations

## 1.1 Definitions

> **Table - Table of Definitions** - see [XLSX](tables/11-pressure-testing-examination-and-cleaning-04_p0004_tbl01_table-of-definitions.xlsx) and [source crop](tables/11-pressure-testing-examination-and-cleaning-04_p0004_tbl01_table-of-definitions.png)

| Term | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Construction Contractor/ Installation Contractor | Any individual, business, or organization employed by the Company through direct contract, purchase order, or agreement that performs the work or services for construction, fabrication and installation of the equipment and materials at the site or other designated location, and as described in this specification |
| Engineering Contractor | Any individual, business, or organization employed by the Company through direct contract, purchase order, or agreement that performs engineering services through its professional engineers, or other such engineers |
| Manufacturer | The organization or business responsible for design and manufacturing or fabrication of parts, equipment, materials, various system components and related accessories for the work described in this specification |
| Company | Oil Search, including Company's Engineer or designated representative |
| Vendor/Supplier | Any individual, business, or organization that supplies parts, equipment, materials, various system components and related accessories for the work described in this specification |
