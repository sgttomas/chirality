4. All restrictions that would interfere with filling, venting, draining, or flushing shall not be installed until after completion of the pressure test and line flushing operations. This includes orifice plates, flow nozzles, venturi tubes, variable area meters, positive displacement (PD) meters, turbine magnetic meters, strainers, and any other similar in-line equipment.

5. Ensure valves subject to hydrotest are in the open position and the piping is filled with water in the direction of flow if a check valve is installed.

6. Engineered supports (Spring cans / hangers) shall have there shipping stops left in during testing.

7. Instrument takeoff piping and sampling system piping, up to the first block valve (i.e., "root valve"), shall be leak tested with the piping or equipment to which it is connected.

8. Instrument lead lines, between the first block valve and the instruments to which they are connected, shall be leak tested to the test pressure of the associated piping or equipment. Testing may be performed separately or at the same time as the piping or equipment is tested, but the instruments shall be disconnected.

9. Joints, including all welds and bonds, shall normally be left unprimed and unpainted for examination during leak testing. This requirement can be waived upon OSA request and documented in the test plan. This requirement applies to the following:

   a. Joints located in environmentally sensitive areas, when specified by the Company's Engineer.

   b. Joints located in buried piping systems.

   c. Joints shall be left uninsulated during leak testing.

## 2.8 Hydrostatic Testing

1. A strainer shall be installed in the fill line to minimize the possibility of foreign matter being introduced into the system during the hydrostatic testing and line flushing operations.

2. The following shall not be tested with water:

   a. Instrument air supply lines

   b. Air lines to air motor operated valves

   c. Hydraulic power piping and tubing

   d. Lube, seal, and control oil piping

   e. Liquid natural gas (LNG) piping

   f. Nitrogen systems.
