## B–4. Acidizing

1. Establish circulation and hydrotest the system per steps 4 and 5 in Section C-1 of this Appendix.
2. Add acid corrosion inhibitor at a rate of 4 lbs. inhibitor per 100 lbs. citric acid or per vendor recommendation.
3. Add 3.0 percent by weight citric acid to circulating water.
4. Add aqueous ammonia to circulating solution until a pH of 3.0 to 4.0 is reached.
5. Elevate temperature of solution to 160°F to 170°F.
6. Circulate at minimum (2 ft/sec) at the elevated temperature for 6 to 8 hours or until titrations and/or a representative sample (see step 5 in Section C-1 of this Appendix) indicates complete removal of iron oxide, mill scale and weld slag.

## B–5. Passivation

1. Lower temperature of acid solution to 130°F to 140°F. Add 0.5 percent excess (by weight) citric acid to circulating solution.
2. Add aqueous ammonia to circulating citric acid solution until a pH of 9.5 is reached.
3. Add 20 lbs. of soda ash per 1000 gallons circulating volume to ensure that elevated pH remains high.
4. Add 1.0 percent sodium nitrate to solution and aerate solution with compressed air. Circulate for one hour at 130°F to 140°F.
5. Drain and/or purge solution from system per steps 4 and 5 in Section C-2 of this Appendix.

## B–6. Draining and Drying System

1. Following passivation, the piping shall be dried by purging it with nitrogen gas or dry filtered air at a temperature maintained between 150°F and 170°F.
2. This shall be followed by a visual inspection of internal piping surfaces to ensure that they are free from traces of contamination.
