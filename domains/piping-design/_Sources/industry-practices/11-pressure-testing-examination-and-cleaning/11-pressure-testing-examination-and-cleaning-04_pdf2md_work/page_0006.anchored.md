a. When the inside surface of branch weld reinforcing pads cannot be visually inspected, pressure tightness (as evidenced by no loss of pressure) shall be determined by a pressure gauge.

## 2.3 Pressure Test Procedures

1. Piping shall not be pressure tested at metal temperatures below 35° F or below the piping design minimum temperature (i.e., minimum design metal temperature [MDMT]).

2. Fabricator may submit request to use test mediums other than water, and testing plan for Owner's approval in reasonable time before testing is scheduled.

3. Test/wash fluid quality shall be as follows:

    a. For carbon steel pipe systems, potable water with a maximum chloride content of 250 parts per million (ppm) is preferred.

4. When austenitic stainless steel piping/equipment designed to operate above 1500 psi (105 bar) will be exposed to hydrostatic test water, the water shall have less than 50 ppm (50 mg/kg) chloride ions and a pH between 6 and 9.

5. When stainless steel not covered by Item (1), above, or when aluminum materials will be exposed to hydrostatic test or wash water, only water having less than 250 ppm (250 mg/kg) chloride ions and a pH between 6 and 9 shall be used. Potable water will meet this requirement.

6. All equipment and piping fabricated with stainless steel exposed to hydrostatic test or wash water shall be drained immediately after hydrostatic testing or water washing and dried by blowing with air [less than 140°F (60°C)]. When air-drying stainless steel process stream analyzers, the air used shall be clean and filtered.

7. The flange gaskets used for hydro or pneumatic testing shall be of the same type and grade as that to be used in the final piping installation.

8. Test procedures and test schedules of lines or systems shall be reviewed by the Company's Engineer prior to the start of the test. Procedures shall include the following:

    a. Source and disposition of test fluid.

    b. Provisions to prevent the creation of significant negative pressure (vacuum) during draining of the test fluid, etc.

    c. Specific lines to be included within a system test. A spreadsheet should be presented that includes line #'s, isometric sheet #'s, P&ID's, test medium, duration, test hold time, pressure and temperature. Most of this information, including test pressures are indicated on Engineers line summary which can be adapted for purpose.
