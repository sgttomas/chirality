## 4.2 Restrictions and Precautions during Cleaning

1. Equipment containing austenitic materials shall not be contacted with:

   a. Acid solutions containing halides and chemicals such as hydrochloric acid. Alternative solvents are acceptable provided the chloride content does not exceed 50 ppm in acidic solvents and 250 ppm in neutral and alkaline solvents.

   b. Caustic soda (NaOH) solutions unless:

      i. Chloride level is less than 250 ppm to avoid chloride stress corrosion cracking.

      ii. Caustic solutions are diluted and flushed prior to startup to avoid caustic stress corrosion cracking.

   c. If degreasing is required, sodium carbonate and trisodium phosphate solutions may be used provided the chloride content does not exceed 250 ppm.

   d. Rinse water that exceeds 250 ppm chlorides.

   e. Hot air used to dry equipment such that metal temperatures will exceed 125°F (50°C).
