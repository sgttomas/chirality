3. Lines in hydrofluoric (HF) acid service shall not be tested with water, unless approved by the Company's Engineer. If HF acid piping is tested with water, the piping system shall be flushed and dried to ensure all water is removed prior to pipe commissioning.

## 2.9 Pneumatic Testing

1. Pneumatic proof testing of instrument air piping and tubing shall be performed with dry instrument air following test pressure indicated on the line summary.

2. Pneumatic proof testing of other piping is acceptable only if the Company's Engineer has approved the test procedure and requires an approved technical MOC. If deemed acceptable, a detailed pneumatic leak testing procedure shall be assembled and approved by the Company's Engineer. The procedure shall include, at a minimum, the following: stored energy calculations, brittle fracture info of equipment to be tested, assumptions made, radius of exposure (i.e., personnel exclusion zones), test duration, and leak identification methods. The procedure must also include an initial "low" test pressure hold and inspect step as well as a "high" pressure hold and inspect step along with ramp up rate information.

# 3 Leak Testing and Purging

This section defines the method in which hydrocarbon service systems are to be leak tested (Service, Hydraulic, Bubble, and nitrogen [N2]/helium [He]) and purged after mechanical completion but prior to the introduction of hydrocarbons to prove the integrity of the system and provide safe startup conditions. The joint reliability (i.e., leak free state) of a pipe system is proven by a combination of 1) holding at a leak test pressure for a specified period of time, and 2) visual inspection of all joints not previously leak or proof tested or that were disassembled and then reassembled after proof testing.

## 3.1 General

1. Procedures used for leak testing and inert gas purging of hydrocarbon service systems shall be in accordance with the following requirements, unless superseded by more stringent local regulations.

2. The installation contractor shall be responsible for performing the following:

   a. Leak testing to prove the integrity of the mechanically completed system at the original construction site as a commissioning activity, and

   b. Leak testing at the systems' final destination following installation as a commissioning activity to reconfirm system integrity which may have been compromised during transit and installation due to equipment and piping movements.

3. Systems shall not be accepted until the required leak testing documentation has been completed and verified by the Company.
