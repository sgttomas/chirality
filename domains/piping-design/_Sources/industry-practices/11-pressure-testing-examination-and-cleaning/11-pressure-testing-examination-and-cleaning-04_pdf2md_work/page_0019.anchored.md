2. The compressor piping and equipment to be cleaned includes:

   a. The first-stage suction line between the knockout drum or filter and the compressor

   b. Intercoolers

   c. Interstage suction lines

   d. Bypass lines

   e. Suction and interstage pulsation dampers.

3. Orifice plates and other potential obstacles to effective cleaning shall be removed from the piping system during the cleaning operation.
