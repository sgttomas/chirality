# 2 Pressure Testing

## 2.1 General

1. This section specifies multiple types of testing for pipe systems. When reference is made to a "proof" test it is referring to that required by the applicable code (i.e., hydro or pneumatic test) to demonstrate that the pipe system can withstand the intended design pressure times some factor (1.1 thru 1.5 times design pressure). When the reference is made to a "leak" test, the intent of that requirement is merely to prove that the assembly does not leak at a pressure up to its rated maximums. In all cases involving fabricated piping, a proof test must be conducted. The leak test may only be used to verify the integrity of a flanged or threaded joint which may not have been included in the proof test.

2. For all pressurized proof testing (pneumatic and hydrostatic), the test pressure shall be recorded on a two-pen pressure/temperature chart recorder or Company-approved equivalent. The pressure recorder shall be calibrated with a dead weight tester at least six months prior to the test and calibration certification information shall be retained as part of the project files. The recorder range shall be such that the test pressure is above 75 percent of the chart's range for accuracy and readability. The use of alternative types of test pressure recorders (i.e., digital) is permitted only with Company's Engineer approval.

3. The hydrotest pressure shall also be monitored with a bourdon tube pressure gauge that has been calibrated with a dead weight tester within the last 30 days.

4. Calibration records for gauges and chart recording equipment (or equivalent) used for testing shall be provided to the Company and shall become part of the permanent project documentation.

## 2.2 Reinforcing Pads

1. Each reinforcing pad (or segment) for branch connections shall be provided with a 1/4 in. diameter NPT threaded hole for testing and venting.

   a. Test and vent holes shall not be plugged, except each opening shall be filled with heavy grease or sealant after testing for corrosion protection.

   b. Each reinforcing pad (or segment) for structural attachment shall be provided with one untapped 1/4 in. diameter hole for venting,

   c. The weld at the perimeter of branch reinforcing pads and structural attachment pads, such as wear pads, shall be a continuous full fillet weld. Each branch reinforcing pad (or segment thereof) shall be tested with air at a pressure of not less than at gauge 15 psi for a minimum of 10 minutes.

2. A bubble test solution (such as Snoop® or Company-approved equivalent) shall be applied to inspect all welds.
