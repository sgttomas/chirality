## Appendix B: Ammoniated Citric Acid Chemical Cleaning

This procedure may be used to degrease, pickle, passivate and dry various carbon steel piping systems. The set-up, quantities or agents, volumes of water, etc., are system dependent.

### B-1. Precleaning

1. Obtain all safe work permits and conduct safety meetings.
2. Check personal protection, such as eye washes, showers, and other equipment.
3. Mechanically prepare system for flow, ensuring that system has low-point drains, high-point vents and circulation connections.
4. Establish circulation of potable water from pumping equipment via chemical circulating hoses and/or temporary piping:
   a. Circulate water through the piping and exit piping system via chemical hoses and/or temporary piping back to the circulation tank.
   b. Vent the system at high points to eliminate air pockets.
   c. Test hoses and connections for leaks.
   d. Make repairs as needed.
5. Hydrostatic test system with potable water at 100 psi prior to injecting chemicals. Place a representative sample (a piece of pipe or any small spool pieces with the same type of fouling system) in the circulation tank.

### B-2. Degreasing

1. Add sodium hydroxide to the circulating water until a 0.5 to 1.0 percent (by weight) solution is reached; add 0.4 to 1.0 percent (by volume) surfactant.
2. Heat solution to 160°F to 180°F while circulating through system.
3. Circulate chemical solution for two hours once the solution has reached temperature.
4. Stop circulation, close inlet and outlet valves on system.
5. Place chemical return hose (from system) into vacuum truck, reopen valves and drain or pressure solution from system.

### B-3. Flushing

Fill chemical circulator with potable water and flush system (minimum [2 ft./sec]) into vacuum truck until the pH of the effluent flush water equals the pH of the influent flush water.
