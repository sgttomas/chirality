## 4 Piping System Cleanliness

1. During field storage, all covers shall remain on major pipe components (valves, pipe joints, large fittings, etc.). During field fabrication, all covers shall remain on pipe components as long as possible so foreign matter, rodents, etc., will not enter the piping system. Prior to flange joint assembly on suction lines to rotating equipment, the Owner's Engineer shall witness with recorded sign off of the removal of temporary covers and pipe internal cleanliness, which shall apply each and every disassembly and reassembly of a flange joint in piping to rotary equipment.

2. After completion of piping system pressure tests, all piping shall be cleaned by "flushing," "blowing" or "chemical cleaning." (High point vents and valved low point drains shall be accessible for these activities). An exception is large piping that can be cleaned internally by manual methods.

3. In general, hydrocarbon lines shall be flushed with clean potable water, unless water is not permitted in the piping system or is not available in sufficient quantity or at high enough pressure. Blowing with dry air is an acceptable alternative if air is available in sufficient pressure and volume.
