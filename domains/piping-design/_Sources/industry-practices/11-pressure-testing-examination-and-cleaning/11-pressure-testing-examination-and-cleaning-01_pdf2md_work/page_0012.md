The soap suds shall be a solution such as Sherlock, Snoop or equal. Throughout the test period, the bubble formation properties of the soap suds shall be checked.

4.8.3 All defects revealed by the test shall be repaired and retested.

4.8.4 Welds in joints in shop fabricated spools that have been successfully pressure tested elsewhere and for, which documentation acceptable to the Owner's Inspector is included in the test package, need not be retested in a system test.

4.8.5 A system under test pressure shall never be left unattended. If a test cannot be completed before the end of a work period, the system shall be depressurized.

## 4.9 ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS FOR BOILER EXTERNAL PIPING

4.9.1 Pressure testing procedures for piping designed according to ASME Section 1 and B31.1 External Boiler Piping, shall conform to the requirements of ASME B31.1, PARA. 137

4.9.2 The test medium temperature must be 70° F min and a maximum metal temperature of 120° F.

4.9.3 All pressure tests of boiler external piping shall include the involvement of the authorized inspector.

## 4.10 POST TEST PROCEDURE

4.10.1 Following the acceptance of a successful test, the system shall be immediately depressurized, drained and vented in a gradual and safe manner and thoroughly drained allowing no pockets of fluid to remain. The system shall then be blown out with dry filtered air until dry to the satisfaction of the Owner's Inspector.

4.10.2 All items removed for the test shall be replaced and all test blinds, temporary supports, etc. shall be removed. The replacement of piping component internals shall be carefully controlled to ensure that they are reinstalled in the correct place. Flange joints opened for these purposes shall be remade with new gaskets.

4.10.3 The system shall be re-inspected for mechanical completeness by the Supplier's and the Owner's Inspectors and signed off as complete. The signed test package shall be retained as part of the contract documentation.
