**Fiberglass Piping.** Fiberglass piping is fabricated per the manufacturer's instructions. These systems use bell-and-spigot joints and epoxy adhesive.

After the adhesive joints are cured, the fiberglass piping section is given three tests for quality of workmanship.

- A 10-psig air and soap test for initial tightness.

- A shock test for joint strength. The pipe section is air-pressurized to 100 psi for one minute, then depressurized. The cycle is repeated 5 times. After completion of all cycles and final depressurizing, all joints are inspected for evidence of joint separation.

- After making the final connection to the tank and backfilling the trench, a volume-loss (Petro-tite) test is performed at 40 psig, with product as the test medium. The piping section must not lose more than 0.01 gal/hr to be acceptable.
