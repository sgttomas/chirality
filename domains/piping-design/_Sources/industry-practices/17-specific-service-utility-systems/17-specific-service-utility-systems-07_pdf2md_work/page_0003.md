## 910 General Considerations

Marketing facilities include: light products terminals (gasoline, diesel, etc.), lube blending and packaging plants, package warehouses, airport fueling facilities, asphalt terminals, asphalt emulsions plants, asphalt refineries, and service stations. For piping used in these facilities, excluding service stations, Marketing endorses the piping design practices found in this manual.

## 911 General Facilities

Piping installations for all Marketing facilities, except service stations, conform to the ANSI/ASME B31.3 Code. Company Specification PIM-EG-2505 should be used for all major piping installations. Marketing piping must comply with OSHA regulations and no cast iron piping elements or equipment shall be used in any hydrocarbon service. Underground hydrocarbon piping is not recommended.

## 912 Service Stations

Service station piping must comply with the Uniform Fire Code and any applicable local codes. Fiberglass is the predominant material for underground piping, including hydrocarbon product piping, because it will not corrode or contaminate the product. Service stations are the only locations where underground hydrocarbon piping is permitted.

Secondary containment of underground piping is required in California and is expected to be required in all states within the next few years. Methods of secondary containment are being explored at this time. Figure 900-1 shows one method using fiberglass troughs around the underground piping.

The steel and stainless steel components of an underground system must be cathodically protected or be externally coated. The choice between cathodic protection and external coating will be determined by the method of secondary containment, because the containment structure can block the flow of cathodic current.

No metal components of an underground piping system are allowed to be in contact with the soil. Therefore, when a flexible joint must be used in a fiberglass piping system, the metal parts of the flexible connector (plastic lined braided metal hose) must be enclosed in a sealed high density polyethylene vent jacket.

Galvanized screwed fittings are only allowed on the top swing joint section of the tank vent riser piping. The rest of the underground vent piping is fiberglass.

## 913 Light Products Terminals

In light products terminals, the piping is standardized on ASTM A106 Schedule 40 carbon steel seamless pipe with ANSI Class 150 carbon steel flanges (Corporation Piping Class E), because it is not cost effective to design, inspect, and administer programs for thin wall pipe or pipe made by other than standard manufacturing processes.
