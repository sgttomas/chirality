### 933 Other Plants

Lube plants and asphalt refineries have no set hydraulic standards. The piping systems are designed to meet the desired "nameplate" process capacity of the plant.

## 940 Layout

### 941 General Facilities

Lube plants, emulsion plants, and asphalt refineries use the same piping layout, clearance rules, and flexibility rules that are used in major refineries.

### 942 Light Products Terminals

All piping systems for light products terminals are designed to the same rules as refinery tank farms. All require good fire protection practices. See Section 200 of the *Tank Manual* for guidance. In the loading rack areas, special attention is paid to providing loading arm cross-over space and good, clear escape lanes for truck drivers in case of a flash fire. Load rack piping is placed overhead to control spills and groundwater pollution. See Figures 900-2 and 900-3

## 950 Installation

### 951 Piping Specifications

Marketing endorses the Corporation Piping Specification for all of its nonretail facilities. Terminals and lube plants use pipe classes A, C, and E. Emulsion plants use classes A, C, E and R. Asphalt refineries use classes A, C, E, H, L, N and R. See the Corporation Piping Specification in Volume 2 of this manual.

### 952 Construction Specifications

**Carbon Steel Piping.** Marketing uses Company Specification PIM-EG-2505 for construction of carbon steel piping systems and has no other standard piping specification.
