## 1080 Upgrading Existing Facilities

### 1081 Application of This Guideline

Most existing Company facilities meet the standards presented in this guideline. Where they do not, they should be evaluated and a priority system developed to upgrade the facility on the basis of relative importance and potential danger. Where massive replacement or renovation of a piping system is judged necessary, and no immediate personnel risk is involved, the changes can be achieved during routine maintenance, scheduled shutdowns or when planned modifications are made. Where a detected personnel hazard exists, the need to eliminate that specific hazard should provide justification for a timely expenditure.

The standards presented here are for new construction and should be used any time extensive modifications or additions are made to existing facilities.

In upgrading existing facilities, the following items are considered of greatest importance and should be checked for deficiencies. They are listed in a possible order of priority. Their correction should receive early attention:

1. Install appropriate warning signs and color coding.
2. Install breathing air systems or self-contained breathing apparatus.
3. Install atmospheric H₂S monitoring equipment.
4. Seal-weld screwed piping.
5. Replace any unconfined gaskets.
6. Eliminate piping smaller than NPS ¾. (Use ½ inch stainless steel tubing at instruments as specified in Section 1055 under Instrument Tubing.)
