**Polythionic Acid Stress Corrosion Cracking**

This may cause failure of austenitic stainless steels that have been sensitized by welding, heat treatment, or long-time service at temperatures over about 750°F. Damage usually occurs during shutdowns when iron sulfide scale is exposed to moisture and oxygen; a soda ash wash prior to opening the equipment to air will prevent polythionic cracking.

**Hydrogen Blistering**

In H₂S-containing services, atomic hydrogen formed in the corrosion reaction may diffuse into the steel, causing blisters to form at internal voids or discontinuities.

# 1050 Design and Construction Requirements

Piping and equipment should be designed and constructed in accordance with the following requirements (also summarized in Figure 1000-4). Equipment handling sour LPG should conform to the recommendations in Section 1100 as well as those given here.

## 1051 Columns, Vessels, and Exchangers

#### Mechanical Design and Construction

Mechanical design and construction shall conform to Section VIII of the ASME Code, and to the *Pressure Vessel Manual*.

**Columns and Vessels.** Columns and vessels should also conform to Company Specification PVM-MS-4750 or be of equivalent quality, except in those instances where other more rigorous specifications apply because of heavy wall thickness or use of special alloys.

**Heat Exchangers.** Heat exchangers should conform to Company Specification EXH-MS-2583 or be of equivalent quality.

#### Radiography

Random radiography is required as a minimum. Depending on service conditions, more extensive radiography will often be justified.

#### Postweld Heat Treatment

Postweld heat treatment of carbon steel is required only when specified by ASME Code, or where special service conditions demand it (amine service, for example).

#### Hydrotest

Hydrostatic testing is required.
