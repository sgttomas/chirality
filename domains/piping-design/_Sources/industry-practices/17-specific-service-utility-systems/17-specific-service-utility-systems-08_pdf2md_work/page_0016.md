Note that the connection in Figure 1000-7 provides good protection for the utility system, but in case of pump failure, can expose operating personnel in the vicinity of the break tank to H₂S hazard if the check valve doesn't function properly. This is especially true when used in conjunction with a centrifugal pump. To minimize the hazard, a closed atmospheric tank with an elevated vent and low pressure alarm on each pump discharge are required. It is recommended that only a low-hardness water be used, i.e., demineralized water or steam condensate. Low-hardness water will minimize calcium precipitation in piping that can cause scale buildup and plugging.
