## APPENDIX 1   TYPICAL AIR SUPPLY PLANT WITH DOUBLE HEADER SYSTEM

![APPENDIX 1 Typical Air Supply Plant with Double Header System](figures/17-specific-service-utility-systems-04_p0022_fig01_appendix-1-typical-air-supply-plant-doub.png)

The schematic shows a P&ID-style flow diagram of a typical air supply plant with double header system. Key elements visible in the diagram include:

- **Air compressors** (left section, within dashed boundary): multiple compressor units including a turbine-driven compressor and a diesel/emergency compressor, each with associated coolers, aftercoolers (if required), and water separators.
- **Air drier and after filter (Appendix 3-6)**: two parallel drier/filter skids on the upper right of the compressor section.
- **Instrument and control signals**: dashed lines indicating capacity control signals, start stand-by compressor signal, and start emergency compressor signal.
- **Instrumentation**: PZA (pressure switch high/low), LL (level switch low-low), PRC (pressure recording controller), QI (quality indicator), QA (quality analyser), Humidity (H), PG (pressure gauge), FE (flow element).
- **Main instrument airheaders (Appendix 8)**: double headers at the bottom of the diagram.
- **Buffer vessel**: vertical pressure vessel on the far right.
- **Legend**:
  - →○→ Open
  - →●→ Closed
