**FIGURE 7   FUEL SYSTEM FOR SUPPLYING SYNGAS BLENDED WITH LPG TO A DUAL GAS FIRED GAS TURBINE**

**NOTES:**

1. Syngas supply variable gas turbine fuel control package regulators for maintaining steady load.
2. LPG Blended under ratio control to compensate for variations in firing rate during start-up, run-up and at steady load.
3. Two controllers configured with a dpd of 0.1 to 0.2 bar to minimise flaring and improve tuning.
