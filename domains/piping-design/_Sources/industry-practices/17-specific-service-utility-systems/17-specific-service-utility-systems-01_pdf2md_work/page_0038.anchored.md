**FIGURE 3    NATURAL GAS BACK-UP (ALTERNATIVE TO LPG VAPORISER)**

The schematic shows a natural gas back-up system. Natural gas from grid enters a Heater. From the heater, gas flows through a TC (temperature controller) loop and into the Collection header, where plant gas producers also feed in. The collection header connects to a Buffer/mixing vessel. Two PC (pressure controller) instruments labelled H and L (Note 1) control flow from the vessel to the Distribution header. A relief/flare line runs from the top of the buffer vessel to flare, and another flare connection exits the distribution header area.

Note 1: Two controllers configured with a 'gap' of 0.1 to 0.2 bar to minimise flaring and improve tuning.
