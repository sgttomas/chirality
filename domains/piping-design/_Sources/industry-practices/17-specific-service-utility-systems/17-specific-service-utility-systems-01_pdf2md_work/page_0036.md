**FIGURE 1    REFINERY FUEL GAS SYSTEM**

The schematic shows the interconnection of the following major components:

- **HP Gas producer** (dashed boundary): includes a Heater (if required) with TC control, plant control valve, and connection to the collection header.
- **LP Gas producers**: three inlet lines with isolation valves connecting to the Collection header.
- **Collection header**: receives LP gas producer streams and HP gas producer stream; feeds the buffer/mixing vessel via pressure controllers (PC, with Note 1).
- **Buffer/mixing vessel**: horizontal vessel with pressure relief to flare ("To flare"), and outlet to Distribution header and to flare.
- **Distribution header**: supplies fuel gas to consumers (arrow up).
- **LPG Vaporiser**: fed from LPG Supply via LC (level controller); heated by LP steam; pressure controlled by PC; discharge joins the collection header.
- **Note 1**: Two controllers configured with 'gap' of 0.1 to 0.2 bar to minimise flaring and improve tuning.
