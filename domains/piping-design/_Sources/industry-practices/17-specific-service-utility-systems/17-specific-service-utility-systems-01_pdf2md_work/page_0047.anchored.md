**FIGURE 12    ALTERNATIVE ASPHALT**

Piping and instrumentation schematic showing an alternative asphalt circulation system. The diagram includes the following major components and flow paths:

- **Furnace (or Heat Exchanger with HTF)** — left side; fuel gas and fuel supply connections; vent gas line.
- **Surge vessel** — central vessel with level controller (LC); safe connection with level alarm (LA).
- **Circulation pumps** — two pumps downstream of surge vessel.
- **Hot filters** — two parallel filter units on the supply side of the main asphalt loop.
- **Return filters** — two parallel filter units on the return side of the main asphalt loop.
- **Main asphalt loop** — right-hand vertical header connecting hot filters, return filters, and consumer sub-loops.
- **Consumer sub-loops** (flushing connections not shown) — three groups of consumers each with flow controllers (FC or LC) and block/control valves.
- **Optional filters** — two parallel filter units at the bottom, fed by a temperature controller (TC) from the furnace circuit.
- **Fuel gas or N2 inlet** — connects to the surge vessel area via control valve.
- **Product outlets at bottom right:**
  - Asphalt
  - Short residue
  - Long residue
