## 2.5 FUEL SYSTEM GENERAL DESIGN

The fuel system piping shall be designed according to DEP 31.38.01.10-Gen., DEP 31.38.01.11-Gen. and DEP 31.38.01.12-Gen.

Thermal insulation shall comply with DEP 30.46.00.31-Gen.

Electrical heat tracing shall comply with DEP 33.68.30.32-Gen.

For pressure reliefs and connections to the flare system refer to DEP 80.45.10.10-Gen.

Shell and tube heat exchangers shall be in accordance with DEP 31.21.01.30-Gen. Heat transfer fluid systems shall be in accordance with DEP 20.05.50.10-Gen. Electric heaters may be used for fuel gas heating on gas plants, particularly where other heating mediums are not available (e.g. plant start-up). These heaters shall be approved by the Principal.

Fired heaters shall be in accordance with DEP 31.24.00.30-Gen.
