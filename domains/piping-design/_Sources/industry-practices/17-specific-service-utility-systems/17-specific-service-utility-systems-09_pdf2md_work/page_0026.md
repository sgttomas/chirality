**6.2.2.5.4** *(Addition)* **Utility Connections**

Minimize connections to utility systems. Utility connections should include provisions for disconnection when they are not in use, such as a turn spool or spectacle blind, which will positively prevent entry of LPG into the utility lines. Ensure that a check valve is installed in each utility connection to a process system.

Figures 1100-1 and 1100-2, Utility Connections, present suggested ways to do this. The connection in Figure 1100-1 is preferred. This type of connection uses a turnout or removable spool to isolate the process system from the utility system. The connection in Figure 1100-2 is permitted where the utility is frequently used, for example, a water flush connection to a process instrument. This type of connection uses a spectacle blind for isolating the process system from the utility system.
