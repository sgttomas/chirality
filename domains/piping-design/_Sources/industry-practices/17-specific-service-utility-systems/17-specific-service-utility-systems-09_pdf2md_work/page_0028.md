## 6.2.4 Pressure Tubing

### 6.2.4.1 *(Addition)* Tubing Specifications

Type 304 SS and/or 316 SS, ½-inch minimum diameter tubing with 0.065-inch (16 gage) minimum wall is recommended. Some facilities allow ⅜-inch, 0.049-inch wall and 3/8-inch, 0.035-inch wall tubing. No copper fittings or tubing are permitted. Do not weld the tubing or the tubing connectors.

### 6.2.4.2 *(Addition)* Tubing Fittings

Tubing fittings may be from any recognized national manufacturer, but should match fittings currently used in the facility. Ensure that all fitting components in a single joint are from the same manufacturer, that the components are installed in the correct sequence and orientation, and that they are tightened in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions.

### 6.2.4.3 *(Addition)* Tubing Runs

Minimize the length of small instrument tubing and piping that contains LPG. Mount transmitters as close as possible to root connections and brace securely against damage. Minimize the length of vent and bleed connections from instruments to the first valve (positive shutoff) location.

## 6.2.5 *(Addition)* Vibrating Services

For vibrating services, 100% radiography and heavily reinforced branch connections are recommended in accordance with Table E of the Corporation Piping Specification. Small connections (1½ inches and under) shall be bridge welded per Standard Drawing GD-L1057. This would apply to rotating and reciprocating machinery and associated piping. Refer to Appendix C of this manual for more information.

Avoid mounting instruments on vibrating equipment or piping. Where this is unavoidable, mounting and support shall be arranged to allow longer sections of bent or coiled tubing so that no significant cyclic bending stresses can be induced (see Appendix C). Tubing is recommended because it can be bent without the use of fittings. In both pipe and tubing, vibration (bending) stresses are greatly intensified at fittings. Threaded not covered by seal welding and overtightened tubing compression fittings worsen the situation.
