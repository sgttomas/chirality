#### 3.2.3.3 (Remote Impoundment Location)

*(Exception)* Locate remote impoundment areas downwind of LPG equipment to minimize the chance of flame contact with adjacent storage areas. A 100-foot separation is preferred.

Impoundment should be at least 200 feet from and preferably downwind from any fixed ignition or static discharge source (furnace, loading dock, etc.).

#### 3.2.3.4 (Impoundment Design Criteria)

*(Addition)* Design impoundment to minimize fire potential:

1. Minimize the liquid surface area:
   - Slope the bottom and make judicious arrangement of drainage paths.
   - Make provisions to drain rain and fire water. Use valves and piping suitable for the autorefrigeration temperatures. Locate drain valves so they may be operated manually or remotely during a spill or fire.
2. Minimize the wetted surface area:
   - Avoid using loose material such as gravel or rock on the surface of the drainage path or impounding area. (These materials will increase turbulence and vaporization of LPG.)
3. Minimize heat input:
   - Use special substrate such as insulating concrete or compacted clay where warranted. The latter, when slightly damp, can freeze, further isolating the pool from subsurface heat.
   - Avoid very wet soil or standing water, which serve to increase heat transfer before the surface freezes.
4. Minimize external fire hazards:
   - Keep all combustible materials (including vegetation) well away from LPG facilities, LPG drainage paths, and impoundment areas so that they will not become involved in any fire.
