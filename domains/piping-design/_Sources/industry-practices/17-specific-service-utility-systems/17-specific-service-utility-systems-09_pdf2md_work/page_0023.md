#### 5.1.8 Temperature Indicator

##### 5.1.8.1 *(Addition)* Location of Temperature Point

Liquid temperature indication shall be at (or visible from) grade.

##### 5.1.8.2 *(Addition)* Thermowell Construction

Thermowells may be flanged or threaded. Threaded thermowells are safer in a fire because they have no bolts that may overheat, but care should be exercised to ensure that threaded thermowells are not unscrewed in error while a vessel is in service (instead of unscrewing their dial thermometer or temperature element).

## 5.2 Optional Equipment

### 5.2.2 Sampling Connections

#### 5.2.2.1 *(Addition)* Mounting

Mount analyzer sample pressure regulators near the process root or source valve. Run the sample system at the lowest practical pressure. (This minimizes the volume of gas required to sweep the loop, minimizing the potential release of gas should the line rupture.)

#### 5.2.2.2 *(Addition)* Self-Closing Valves

All liquid sample systems must be designed with self-closing "deadman" valves. Where the operation requires that the self-closing valve be held in the open position for prolonged periods, a fusible link remotely operated valve shall be used. A fusible link remotely operated valve, however, should never be assumed to replace having an operator present during LPG sampling.

#### 5.2.2.3 *(Addition)* Vessel Vapor Space Sampling

Provisions for drawing gas samples of the vapor space should be provided.
