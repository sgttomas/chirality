# 1130 Section 3—Siting Requirements and Spill Containment

### 3.1 Siting

*(Addition)* When choosing a site and laying out new LPG facilities, consider the probability and effect of a major release incident and what additional precautions may be taken. Consider whether nearby facilities are frequented by personnel and whether the new facility is critical to operations. Refer to AIChE's *Guidelines for Hazard Evaluation Procedures* and API Publication 2510A, *Fire Protection Considerations for the Design and Operation of LPG Storage Facilities*. See also the *Fire Protection Manual*.

#### 3.1.2 Minimum Distance Requirements For Aboveground LPG Tanks

**Spacing** *(Exception)*

Storage vessels, except spheres, should be arranged in groups of not more than 6 with a minimum of 10 feet between vessels and 50 feet between groups. Install spheres at least one diameter apart. CRTC's Health, Environment and Safety Group staff is available to offer guidance on spacing and layout.

### 3.2 Spill Containment

**Objectives** *(Addition)*

- Prevent the accumulation of liquid under LPG storage vessels and piping.
- Minimize the chance of flame impingement from a burning spill.
- Provide an impoundment location that minimizes the risk to critical facilities, piping and equipment if the pool ignites.
- Minimize the surface area of a spill to reduce the rate of vaporization of the material that collects and limit the size of the resultant vapor cloud.

#### 3.2.1 (Surface Drainage)

*(Addition)* General recommendations for surface drainage are covered in the *Civil and Structural Manual*, Section 500, Drainage.

##### 3.2.1.1 (Grading)

*(Addition)* The drainage system shall slope away from vessels, tanks, manifolds, and piping to a safe containment location.

#### 3.2.3 (Remote Impoundment)

*(Addition)* Remote impoundment is strongly recommended.

#### 3.2.3.2 Containment Dikes and Swales

*(Addition)* Containment dikes and swales are acceptable means of directing spills and are less costly than walls. These, as well as all drainage areas, should be designed to prevent spilled LPG from approaching closer than 5 feet to other piping, vessels or equipment.
