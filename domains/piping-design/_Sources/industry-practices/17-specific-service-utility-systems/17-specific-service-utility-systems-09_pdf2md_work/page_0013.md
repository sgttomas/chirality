## 4.2 Special Requirements

### 4.2.2 Materials

*(Exception)* LPG vessels shall **not** be supported using unreinforced concrete or masonry.

*(Exception)* Concrete used for support of an LPG vessel shall be reinforced in all seismic zones (1, 2, 3, and 4) as defined by the Uniform Building Code, latest edition.

### 4.2.4 Settlement of Foundation

*(Addition)* Combine or tie together adjacent equipment, structures, and/or support foundations to avoid overstressing piping and connections from differential settlement. Where this is not practical, design the piping to accommodate 1.5 times the predicted differential settlement and install bench marks so settlement can be monitored. Watch specifically for differential settlement between vessel and pipe supports.

### 4.2.6 Floating Foundation or Piling

*(Exception)* The use of floating foundations is not recommended. Use spread or pile-supported foundations.

### 4.2.7 Loads on Supporting Structure

*(Addition)* In addition to the loads required by API Standard 2510, the loads and load combinations specified in the Uniform Building Code shall be incorporated in the design.

### 4.2.8 Support Design

*(Addition)* Support structures or piers shall be designed to withstand friction-generated forces from vessel thermal expansion or contraction.
