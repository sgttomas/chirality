#### 2.1.1 (Vessels)

*(Exception)* Vessels (and columns if any) shall conform to Specification PVM-MS-4750, or be of equivalent quality, except in those instances where other, more rigorous specifications apply because of heavy wall thicknesses or use of special alloys.

## 2.3 Design Vacuum

*(Addition)* API Standard 2510 requires protection against vacuum collapse. It recommends full vacuum design for LPG vessels as the first choice followed by a vacuum breaker admitting (in order of preference) inert gas, hydrocarbon gas, or air.

The Company requires that all LPG vessels be designed for an external pressure of at least 10 psi at 450°F. Full vacuum design is recommended where the added cost would be less than vacuum breaking using inert gas, fuel gas, natural gas, or air.

In determining whether a vacuum breaking system is warranted, factors to be considered include extreme low winter temperatures, rundown temperatures, rapidity of storage turnover rates, and stored product mix.

## 2.4 Materials of Construction

### 2.4.3 *(Addition)* **Brittle Fracture Design**

All vessels should be designed to resist brittle fracture at the temperature that would result from the autorefrigeration resulting from the vessel's pressure suddenly being reduced from 100% to 40% of its maximum allowable working pressure. **Take precautions to ensure that the equipment does not autorefrigerate below the minimum pressurizing temperature (MPT)** when being filled or removed from service.

Low temperature fracture-resistant materials such as ASTM A516 are now readily available at costs only somewhat higher than for A285C. These materials are increasingly used in all types of pressure vessels, as well as for increased protection from brittle fracture at the wall thicknesses and autorefrigeration temperatures commonly found in LPG storage vessels.

For more information on fracture-safe design, see the *Pressure Vessel Manual*, Section 500.

## 2.5 Vessel Connections

### 2.5.2 (Flange Connections)

**Gaskets** *(Exception)*

Use confined gaskets for all manways and closures. Raised-face flanges with spiral-wound gaskets (with a centering ring) or ring joints are acceptable.
