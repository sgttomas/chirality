## 5.1.7 Shutoff Valves

### 5.1.7.1 (Shutoff Valves)

*(Exception)* Shutoff or "root" valves permit isolation of instruments from the process at its source, or "root." A shutoff valve shall be installed immediately at any pipe or tubing connection to a process vessel or line on all instrument installations. Minimum root valve size is NPS ¾, except for orifice flange tap valves, which, up through ANSI Class 600 flange rating, require NPS ½ root valves.

### 5.1.7.3 (Safety)

#### 5.1.7.3.1 *(Addition)* **Quick-Closing Valves**

Quick-closing valves shall be installed immediately downstream of the block valves on all LPG storage vessel fill and pump suction lines, and on all other lines below normal liquid level (except instrument connections) which communicate with piping and equipment external to the specific vessel. Swing-check valves are required on fill lines, and air-operated quick-closing valves are required for suction lines.

#### 5.1.7.3.2 *(Addition)* **Remote Vessel and Pump Shut-In**

Shut-in valves shall be capable of being manually actuated from remote locations, as well as being automatically actuated. Automatic shut-in shall be from fire detection system(s) at all associated LPG pumps and loading stations within 200 feet. Alarm actuation of these systems shall be installed in attended control centers. (See also Section 7.3.1, Pumps, covering simultaneous shutdown of associated pumps.) Valves shall close upon power failure.

#### 5.1.7.3.3 *(Addition)* **Fire Detection Systems**

LPG services; fire detection systems Acceptable fire detection systems are:

1. Pneumatic systems using plastic tubing (or other types of sensors) that is run close to any potential location for a fire
2. Flame detectors
3. Heat detectors, when they can be located close to any potential location for a fire. Alarm actuation of these systems should be installed in attended control centers. (Flame and heat detectors should be relied upon only in facilities with a strong maintenance and testing program for them.)
