### 2.5.9 *(Addition)* Manways

Provide two manways on spheres, one top and one bottom. Horizontal vessels should have two manways for ventilation and access if handling sour LPG; otherwise, only one manway is required on horizontal vessels. The manway should preferably be located on the head opposite the piping end.

## 2.6 Previously Constructed Vessels

#### 2.6.1 *(Addition)* Moving Existing Vessels

Existing vessels that are moved to a new location (or facility) for use in the same (or a new) service and newly purchased used vessels shall:

1. Meet the criteria of API 2510, Section 2.6, and
2. Be hydrotested to 1.5 times the maximum design pressure (or maximum allowable pressure if downgraded).

#### 2.6.2 *(Addition)* **Full Penetration Butt Welds**

Prior to 1950 the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII did not require full penetration butt welds. Vessels constructed to this older code **must have all butt welds replaced with full penetration butt welds** if they are moved to a new location (or facility) for use in **identical or similar design conditions.**

Vessels which are moved to a new location (or facility) for use at **less severe conditions must be downgraded to the appropriate joint efficiencies prescribed by the current ASME Code** to arrive at the new or revised design conditions.

Contact the Pressure Vessel Consultant at CRTC Materials and Equipment Engineering for any necessary guidance in this area.

## 2.7 Insulation

*(Addition)* External shell surface corrosion has been experienced in insulated LPG storage vessels. Contributing factors are: 1) lack of an effective vapor barrier, 2) humid climate, and 3) rundown or storage temperatures below the dew point.

To determine the need for insulation, consider all of the factors discussed in API 2510, Section 2.2, Design Pressure and Temperature, so that insulation will not have to be added later to prevent overpressure conditions during the summer months.

The requirements for use of thermal insulation as fireproofing are discussed in API 2510, Section 8.10, Fireproofing of LPG Vessels. If insulation is required for fire protection, control of external shell corrosion should be considered in the selection and installation of insulating materials. Consult Materials and Equipment Engineering at CTRC for specific recommendations.
