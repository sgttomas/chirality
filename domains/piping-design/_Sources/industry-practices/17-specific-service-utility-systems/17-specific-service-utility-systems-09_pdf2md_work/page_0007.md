**AICHE**

Guidelines for Hazard Evaluation Procedures, 1985

**Local regulations.** In some geographic locations, special standards and/or regulations may exist that influence the design and construction of equipment or facilities handling LPG. Where they are legally required, these standards and regulations must be followed in addition to the requirements covered here.

#### 1.4 Definitions

This guideline incorporates all of the definitions listed in API 2510, Section 1.4.

#### 1.5 Characteristics of LPG

See API Publication 2510A, Section 1.6, for a listing of important LPG properties.

#### 1.6 Safety

*(Addition)* A hazard analysis should be performed for every LPG facility when new, then repeated periodically, to uncover possible but realistic accident scenarios. Such factors as site location, equipment spacing, fire protection systems, operational and maintenance procedures, and proximity to public and industrial areas should be reviewed.

# 1120 Section 2—Design of LPG Tanks

## 2.1 Applicable Design Construction Codes

**Spheres** *(Exception)*

Field constructed tanks shall conform to Specification PVM-MS-997 or be of equivalent quality.

**Heat Exchangers (if any)** *(Exception)*

Heat exchangers shall conform to Specification EXH-MS-2583, or equivalent quality.

**Repairs and Alterations** *(Addition)*

Repairs and alterations shall be performed in compliance with API Standard 510, *Pressure Vessel Inspection Code*, or the code under which the vessel was initially constructed; they should also comply with any state or local jurisdictional requirements.

**Burying and Mounding** *(Exception)*

Direct burial or mounding of LPG vessels is not recommended. See API Standard 2510, Section 8.12.
