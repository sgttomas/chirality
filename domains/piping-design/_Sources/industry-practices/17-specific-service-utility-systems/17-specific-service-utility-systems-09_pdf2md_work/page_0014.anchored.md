#### 4.2.10 Diagonal Members

*(Exception)* Diagonal members shall be attached to the structural support system (i.e., ring girder or top of structural column). This point of attachment should be designed and reinforced to resist the loads of the brace.

#### 4.2.11 Design of Supports

*(Addition)* In addition to the references suggested in API Standard 2510 and the vessel design programs listed in Section 4.2.9 above, Company Standard Drawing GA-C99694 is recommended for use in the design of vessel supports.

#### 4.2.12 Saddles

*(Addition)* Use support saddles for all horizontal LPG vessels to:

1. Provide a means to anchor the vessel to the foundation.
2. Distribute vertical support reactions around 120 degrees or 150 degrees of the vessel circumference.

Computer program ENGR 17 may be used to design saddles for horizontal vessels. It calculates vessel bending moments and stresses based on two supports per vessel at user selected locations. This data is then used for a trial and error optimization of support points. The program will select the appropriate support saddle from Standard Drawing GA-C99694 for the vessel geometry and calculate support reactions for two loading conditions: operating weight and temperature, and hydrostatic test loads.
