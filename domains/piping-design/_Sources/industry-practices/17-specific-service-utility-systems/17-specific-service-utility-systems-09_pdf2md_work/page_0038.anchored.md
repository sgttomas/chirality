#### 7.10.4 Protection Against Vehicular Damage

Protect loading racks against damage from vehicles:

1. All loading/unloading points shall be arranged so that trucks may enter and park head-in to be loaded/unloaded. Provide a combination of roads or, where space permits, a generously sized area for turn-around of the largest anticipated truck so that drivers may avoid backing. Provide ample space so trucks arriving may wait at a safe distance (50 feet) until it is permitted to enter the loading area.
2. Provide curbing, posts, and/or other barriers to guard against a truck damaging pressure-retaining equipment and piping.

#### 7.10.5 Drainage

The loading area should be graded and paved to direct spills away to a safe area. Locate gas-sealed drain catch basins (Standard Drawing GD/S99992) away from LPG loading positions. Loading spots shall be sufficiently level to ensure that liquid will not wet or cover the relief valve opening on any vehicle as it is parked during LPG transfer.

# 1180 Section 8—Fire Protection

Refer to API Publication 2510A, *Fire Protection Considerations for the Design and Operation of Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) Storage Facilities*, which covers this subject in considerable depth.

## 8.1 General

**Ignition Source Control** *(Addition)*

Risk is best controlled by good design, although proper maintenance and operating procedures play a major role in reducing potential ignition sources to a minimum.

- **Site selection:** See Section 1130, Siting Requirements and Spill Containment.
- **Site layout:** Route roads well away from LPG facilities. Lay out facilities to eliminate unnecessary personnel and vehicular access and to allow close control of necessary maintenance and operations activities. Plan for (and install as needed) segregated areas for equipment and materials assembly, smoking, parking areas, and smoking areas for employees, visitors, tradesmen, maintenance contractors, etc.
- **Electrical design:** Provide electrical grounding and bonding as recommended by API 2003, *Prevention of Ignitions Arising Out of Static, Lightning and Stray Currents*. Prepare and follow area classification drawings with particular attention to conduit seal fittings. Refer to the *Fire Protection Manual* for electrical area classification.
