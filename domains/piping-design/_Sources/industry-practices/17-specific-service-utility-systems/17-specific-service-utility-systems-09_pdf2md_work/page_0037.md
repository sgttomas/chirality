## 7.6 Blowdown or Venting of Loading and Unloading Lines

### 7.6.1 *(Addition)* Block Valves

LPG loading hoses or swivel joint loading arms may be equipped with a block valve located at the truck/car terminus of the loading hose to reduce escape of vapor or liquid when disconnecting the hose after product transfer. This valve should be compatible with the truck/car terminus, and may be brass, bronze or aluminum. It should be rated for the maximum operating pressure. Quick shutoff lever-operated ball (or other) valves may be used. This block valve may be lightweight and need not be fire-resistant. Refer to API 2510A, Section 3, for recommendations on proper operating procedures for product transfer.

### 7.6.2 *(Addition)* Pressure Relief Valves

Install pressure relief valves to protect the loading hoses/arms from thermal expansion pressure buildup.

### 7.6.3 *(Addition)* Return Vent Line

A return vent line (usually running back to the supply or suction vessel) shall be provided to prevent truck and car loading over-pressures. This line shall be equipped with a steel check valve to prevent backflow.

## 7.10 *(Addition)* Loading Area

### 7.10.1 Control of Loading Operations

A supervisory center to control traffic and limit access to the LPG loading/unloading facilities may be justified where more than two trucks might be waiting and where drivers and vehicles should be monitored.

The supervisory center shall be located outside the fire zone and overlooking the loading area, if possible. The **Emergency Response Plan** and the **Loading Operating Procedures** shall be available in the supervisory center. The loading operating procedures shall also be posted at the loading facility.

### 7.10.2 Fire & Shutdown Trouble Alarms

Install trouble alarms and annunciators in the nearest attended supervisory center that has responsibility for the area. See Section 5.1.7.3.

### 7.10.3 Communications

If radio communication equipment is not always carried, provide alternate means of communicating with appropriate control center(s) near the loading racks, pumps, and with all remote manual shutdown points.
