- Use water to knock down and direct vapor. Do not spray water directly onto the leak or onto a liquid chlorine pool.

#### 1314 Key Emergency Actions—Fire

- Alert downwind personnel and facilities.
- Keep containers cool. Fusible plugs melt at 158°F.
- Remove containers from fire zone.
