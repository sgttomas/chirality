Each module is designed with a sealed floor and a 4" tall rim around the module floor perimeter. This generates a containment volume to hold drips or spills which may occur at any location within the module. To avoid having spills running across the module floor, module sumps will be located throughout the module to allow easy collection of liquids to a common point for disposal. The location of the sump(s) would be as close as possible to high potential spill equipment. In addition to the 4" tall rim the module floor dams at interface with utilidor or equipment corridor for ease of moving equipment may be also considered or provide a ramp to facilitate equipment move from one module to another, especially from non-classified to classified module.

The response to a spill within the module will be handled differently depending on the size of the release:

- Small drips and spills will be cleaned using absorbent materials.
- Liquid on the module floor due to a larger leak will be displaced using squeegees toward a module drain sump where it will be vacuumed out. Each module sump will have a dedicated air pump that can be used to drain each sump within the module.
- Large liquid release will be contained within the 4" lip sealed floor. A hard piped open HC drain sump will allow pumping out the bulk of the liquid prior to the Operator entering the module to finalize the cleaning effort.

## 3.3.1 Small Drips and Spills

Small spills will be collected with absorbent materials. A small containment will be immediately placed under the leak until it can be attended to.

For Rotating Equipment, all skids extend beyond all equipment and have a drip rim or a drip containment lip to trap any liquid drips or small spills within the skid. This includes both process liquids and lubricating oil. The skid is sloped to a low point drain which has a valve in it. For small spills, manual cleanup of the skid can be done with towels and mops. Larger spills can be drained to small pans and transferred to a bucket for disposal.

## 3.3.2 Significant Leaks

Until it is discovered, significant leaks may run on the module floor to the nearest sump. Again, containment will be immediately placed under the leak until it can be attended to. Liquids on the floor will be directed using squeegee to the nearest module sump where it will be disposed of using a vacuum truck.
