### 3.3.3 Catastrophic Liquid Release

Catastrophic liquid release due to failure of equipment or fine water discharge will be accumulated within the 4" tall rim of the sealed module floor. The open HC drain sump will be equipped with a permanent drain pipe so the large liquid release could be pumped out to the open pit without having an operator entering the module – detail for firewater release discussed in Fire Water System Sizing Criteria. The hard piped sump (or trench) will be located adjacent to the external wall and run the full width of the bay (run perpendicular to the wall). The intent is to provide an overflow edge as long as possible so, upon completion of the bulk liquid removal, the remaining liquid can overflow in the sump as easily as possible.

Once the bulk of liquid is removed and the Operator is authorized to enter the module, remaining puddles will be drained using squeegee to the hard pipe sump or vacuum directly with a spark free squeegee head (like a wet shop vacuum). Remaining small sumps will be drained using the module air pumps as described above. Each module will have its own dedicated air pump that can be used to drain each sump within the module.

## 3.4 Spills Outside of Modules

### 3.4.1 Low Pressure Equipment

Equipment with design pressure less than 15 psig, such as tanks, will be provided with spill containment of 110% of their inventory. If several tanks are located in a common area, single spill containment can be provided and will be sized to 110% of the largest volume within the containment. Liquids collected within the containment will be hauled away by a tank truck. A level transmitter at the edge of the spill containment will be provided to alert operations that the containment has taken on some liquid.

### 3.4.2 Higher Design Pressure Equipment

Equipment can be located outside the module enclosure without full spill containment provided that:

- All equipment has a minimum design pressure of 15 psig.
- Temporary spill containment is provided during maintenance of equipment that is suitable to contain a reasonable amount of liquid after it has gone through the draining process.
- All valves and instrument are located inside the module enclosure where possible.
- Flange connections on equipment in all services are minimized.
