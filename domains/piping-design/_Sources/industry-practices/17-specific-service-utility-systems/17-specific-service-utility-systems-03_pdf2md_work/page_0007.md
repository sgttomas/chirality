Low boiling degradation products shall be vented on pressure control and routed to the flare. If regular venting of vapours is unavoidable and/or the collection of HTF in the flare knockout drum undesirable e.g. for operational or HSE reasons, the vent line should be routed via a condenser to a collecting drum prior to venting the non-condensables to flare. The liquid HTF is collected for safe disposal off-site. Collected liquids can also be routed via the on-line purification unit for recovering part of the HTF (2.9.5).

The expansion vessel shall be equipped with a safety relief valve arrangement capable of protecting the system against over-pressure caused by events such as fluid degradation, contamination, malperation, overheating or tube failure in equipment. Other relief cases are to be considered during HAZOP or safety review.

The safety relief outlet line shall be routed to flare.

If the ambient temperature can fall below the freezing point of the fluid or its degradation products, or if accumulated high boiling point components can plug the lines, nitrogen lines and valves, vent-back-pressure lines and valves, and safety relief lines and valves shall be heat traced in order to prevent plugging.

If the ambient temperature can fall below the HTF's pumpability limit, heating the expansion vessel should be considered. In order to eliminate the risk of HTF contamination in the event of coil rupture, either electrical heating or external heat tracing should be applied.

The expansion vessel shall be insulated and shall be a vertical vessel.

## 2.3.3 Sizing

The size of the expansion vessel depends upon the expansion coefficient, maximum temperature and quantity of the fluid in the circuit. The preliminary sizing shall be based upon the estimated quantity of fluid and shall be adjusted when the final design stage is completed. It is the responsibility of the Contractor to review and, if necessary, revise the vessel dimensions during the detailed design phase, when the relevant equipment sizes, line sizes, plot plans and piping layouts are known.

The expansion vessel has the combined function of an expansion vessel and a knock-out vessel. It shall have sufficient capacity to cater for various operating upsets in the system. The vessel dimensions shall be calculated and the maximum level set according to DEP 31.22.05.11-Gen.

The expansion vessel shall allow for degassing of the HTF and shall as a minimum be fitted with a half open pipe inlet device.

The vessel can be divided into three sections, as follows:

1) Low-Low up to Low level

   The expansion vessel shall be located high enough to ensure adequate NPSH for the HTF circulation pump at Low-Low level. It shall be checked whether the NPSH requirements are met at all relevant working temperatures. The inventory between Low-Low and Low level should be 25 % of the vessel volume.

2) Low up to High level

   The volume between High and Low level shall be equal to the expansion volume of the total fluid inventory from minimum possible operating temperature to maximum possible operating temperature in the system. An additional 20 % shall be added to cater for various operating upsets in the systems such as vaporisation of residual water in the system and a tube burst.

   Although the design pressure on the HTF side may be higher than that on the process side, the working pressure may be the reverse, which means that process fluid could enter the HTF in the event of a tube rupture.
