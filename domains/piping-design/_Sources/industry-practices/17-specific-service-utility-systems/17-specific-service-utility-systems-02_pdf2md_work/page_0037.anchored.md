## APPENDIX 2    EFFECTS OF OILY CONTAMINATION

Regarding the concentration of oily contamination of metal surfaces, the following information may contribute to a better understanding of the problem.

- At a concentration of 1 g/m² oil is not visible and smears cannot be detected when the surface is rubbed with a finger and the area inspected under a bright white light.
- At 2.7 g/m² the surface has a slightly oily appearance but no smearing is discernible after testing as above.
- At 5.4 g/m² the oil will flow and a slight smear can be detected.
- At 10.8 g/m² smearing is considerable.
- At 27 g/m² the oil can be pushed with a finger.

NOTE:    The most rigorous cleaning can reduce oily contamination to about 0.05 g/m².
