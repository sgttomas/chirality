**velocity, m/s**

The chart plots gas velocity (m/s) on the vertical axis against pressure in bar (ga) on the horizontal axis. A curve labelled **SONIC VELOCITY** marks the upper boundary, starting at approximately 350 m/s at ~10 bar (ga) and declining steeply before flattening toward ~8 m/s at 100 bar (ga). Four hatched regions below the curve indicate material selection zones:

- Carbon steel, up to 100 °C
- Stainless steel, up to 150 °C
- Nickel-based alloy if in turbulent or impingement areas, otherwise carbon steel (up to 100 °C and 60 m/s) or stainless steel (up to 150 °C)
- Nickel-base alloy
