## 5. ORDERING, MARKING, PACKAGING

Orders for materials for oxygen service shall not be combined with those for other materials. The materials shall be delivered free from rust, scale, oil and grease, see (6). Small items shall be packed in plastic bags so that foreign material cannot enter during transport. Orifices should be sealed by metal, plastic or rubber plugs. Sealing with adhesive tape shall be avoided.

The openings of prefabricated pipework and components shall be sealed by means of blind flanges, metal or plastic covers.

The Manufacturer shall clearly label all materials and equipment in bold lettering:

***"SUITABLE FOR OXYGEN SERVICE"***
