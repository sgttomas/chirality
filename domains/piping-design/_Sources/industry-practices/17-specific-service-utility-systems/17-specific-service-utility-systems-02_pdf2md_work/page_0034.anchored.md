By-pass piping (shaded in sketch) shall be made from Monel at design pressures above 2000 kPa (ga).

By-pass to be made above or in some horizontal plane as main pipe but never below.

By-pass on the vavle body, as supplied by certain valve manufacturers, is acceptable.
