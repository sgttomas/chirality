### 1.3.2 Specific definitions

**dry gaseous oxygen** &emsp; gaseous oxygen whose dew point at the maximum operating pressure is below the lowest ambient temperature and at least 10 °C below the minimum operating temperature

**fire safe valve** &emsp; valve constructed according to the fire safe requirements of BS 6755-2, API 6FA or API 607

**flammable fluid** &emsp; fluid having a flash point below 37.8 °C, except any mixture having components with flash points equal or higher than 37.8 °C, the total of which make up 99 percent or more of the total volume of the mixture

**oxygen** &emsp; any gas mixture with an oxygen volume fraction of 90 % or greater
