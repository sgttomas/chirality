#### 4.4.5 Check valves

Check valves should be of the plate-disc type (compressor valve type) without helical springs. Valve plates and spring plates shall be of stainless steel, or of Inconel X-750 for pressures above 70 bar (ga).

#### 4.4.6 Instruments

All instrument parts in contact with oxygen shall be stainless steel or nickel-based alloy, and have smooth surfaces. Filling fluids for capsules and diaphragm seals shall be such that they do not present a hazard if the diaphragm should fail. The liquid shall be a fluorocarbon and the selected type shall be subject to the approval by the Principal. The special liquid shall be indicated, e.g., by etching, on the outside of the item.

#### 4.4.7 Identification

All valves and instruments shall bear the warning:

***'OXYGEN! KEEP FREE FROM OIL AND GREASE!'***
