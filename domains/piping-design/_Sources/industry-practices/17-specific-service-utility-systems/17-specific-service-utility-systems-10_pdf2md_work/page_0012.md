When operating conditions make it advisable, a remotely operated shutoff valve should be installed on connections located below the maximum liquid level. See under Valve Selection in Section 1244.

## Repairs/Alterations

Tanks constructed per API Standard 620 shall be repaired or altered in accordance with API Standard 620 and any state or local requirements. See Section 1242 for ASME Code requirements.

# 1244 Piping

## Piping Code

As a minimum all piping should conform to the requirements of the latest edition of ANSI/ASME B31.3, Chemical Plant and Petroleum Refinery Piping, or to the design and construction project piping specifications where stricter standards were used in the design. Piping should be ANSI Class 300.

## Piping Manual

General piping design should follow the guidelines set out in the *Piping Manual*, with emphasis on flexibility to resolve thermally induced stresses.

## Fabrication Specification

All piping fabrication should conform to the general requirements of Company Specification PIM-EG-2505, Carbon Steel Piping Fabrication.

## Flanges and Valves

Minimize the number of flanges and flanged valves. Where flange leaks could cause direct ammonia impingement on columns or vessels, consider providing metal shields. In cold service piping (below minus 20°F), a low-temperature grade of carbon steel must be used. Refer to ANSI/ASME B31.3 Section 323.2.2 to select materials for cold service piping.

## Piping NPS 2 and Larger

Piping NPS 2 and larger shall be butt-welded.

## Piping NPS 1½ and Smaller

Minimum NPS is ¾-inch and minimum wall thickness Schedule 80. Small piping, NPS ¾ to 1½, should be socket-welded, or completely seal-welded if screwed piping is used. Socket-welded is preferred; seal-welded is acceptable but care is required to ensure that all exposed pipe threads are covered. Use small (NPS ¾ to 1½) piping only for auxiliary lines that can be separated from the main operating lines by a valve located in the root position.

Root valves shall be bridge-welded per Company Standard Drawing GD-L1057. Examples are drains, sample connections, and instrument root valves. Unions are not allowed.
