Notes.
1. Differential pressure device (ΔP)—such as a control valve, heat exchanger, pump or restriction orifice.
2. Fresh air inlet—locate a minimum of 15 feet above adjacent work areas.
3. Vent locate a minimum of 15 feet above adjacent work areas.
4. 3-way valve—Conbrametrics #C-111-BC-TWO-Sp or equal. Include mounting plate and nameplates for safe operating instructions. Connect 3-way valve ports as shown.
5. Close couple to minimize stale sample.
6. Sample valve—self closing, AGCO 600 series or equal.
7. Sample dip tube, detail "A"—provide grommet to seal tube entry into sample box. Minimize tube length to reduce sample loss to vent.
8. Hazardous sample box—with sealed door, integral funnel and screen to support sample container.
9. 3 × 4-inch viewing window.
10. Hinged door—sized for sample container such as 1 quart bottle. Orient door away from walkway.
11. Eductor—Penberthy #GH N or equal.
12. Eductor air valve—Smith 125R ball valve or equal. Brace extension handle to valve to prevent opening sample box door without first opening air valve to start eductor.
13. Connect to a closed system or route to a safe drain away from work areas.
