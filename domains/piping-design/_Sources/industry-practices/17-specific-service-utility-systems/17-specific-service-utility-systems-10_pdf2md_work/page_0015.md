## Blinds

Spectacle blinds are not acceptable for ammonia service. The convenience of this device does not justify the increased hazards of lighter flanges, fewer bolts, the gasket materials used, and personnel exposure.

## Flexibility

Design liquid ammonia piping with adequate flexibility for autorefrigeration, fire conditions, or differential settlement of foundations. Small branch connections from larger lines are particularly vulnerable to overstressing. While achieving flexibility be sure to provide adequate support.

## Vents and Drains

Discharge all drains to a closed system such as a vapor recovery system. If this is impractical and if acceptable under governing regulations, discharge the drains to a safe place where the discharge point is visible from the valve location.
