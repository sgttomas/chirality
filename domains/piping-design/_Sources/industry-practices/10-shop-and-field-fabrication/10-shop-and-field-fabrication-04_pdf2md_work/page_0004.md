## 2. GENERAL

MESC numbers for orifice meter runs in sizes from DN 15 to DN 600 are contained in the piping classes. These MESC numbers lead to the required buying descriptions and specifications.

Orifice meter runs in sizes from DN 15 to DN 40 shall be ordered and supplied as complete items for site installation, via the MESC number. These are detailed in Standard Drawing S 38.134.

Orifice meter runs in sizes from DN 50 to DN 600 shall either be ordered and supplied as complete items, via the MESC number, or shall be shop/field fabricated in accordance with this DEP.

Shop and field fabricated orifice meter runs shall be made as a pipe spool (shown on an isometric drawing). The total straight upstream and downstream lengths shall be in accordance with ISO 5167-1. The length of meter runs according to Figure 2.1 will usually satisfy the minimum straight length requirements. Where this is not the case, the straight length requirements should be achieved by the adjacent piping being installed with the same internal diameter as the meter run. For allowable tolerances, see (3).

The application of orifice meter runs is explained in DEP 32.31.00.32-Gen.
