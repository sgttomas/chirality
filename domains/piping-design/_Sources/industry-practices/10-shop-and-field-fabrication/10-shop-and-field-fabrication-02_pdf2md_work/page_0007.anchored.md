## 4.7 Final Installation

4.7.1 Piping spools shall be dry, thoroughly cleaned and free from any foreign material before installation.

4.7.2 Any obstructions or foreign matter remaining in the pipe after installation thereof shall be removed at the expense of the Vendor.

4.7.3 Before shipping the inside of all compressor suction piping shall be pickled to SSPC-SP-8 to remove rust and scale and a suction screen installed.

4.7.4 Lube-oil piping shall be pickled, neutralized and given a protective coating.

4.7.5 Cleaning, purging, etc. shall be under supervision of OWNER. Vendor shall provide men as required to disconnect, dismantle, and clean all equipment, pipe, valves, fittings, etc. On gas or airlines where it is impossible to ensure complete elimination of moisture, methanol is to be fed into pipe to absorb the moisture and then re-purged with air or nitrogen.

## 5.0 INSPECTION

### 5.1 General

Vendor's facilities, work and records shall be accessible at all reasonable times to OWNER's authorized inspectors. Such inspectors may reject any materials, procedures or work which, in his opinion alone, fail to meet the requirements of applicable codes, specifications, procedures, drawings or purchase orders.

### 5.2 Specific Requirements

5.2.1 Alloy and non-ferrous piping, carbon steel piping requiring heat treatment or mill certification, and carbon steel piping so designated on piping drawings are subject to full inspection. Other carbon steel piping is subject to spot inspection.

5.2.2 Vendor's quality control shall include 10 percent radiographic examination of the welds by each welder. OWNER may require examination of a greater proportion of welding if, in his opinion, this is justified to insure satisfactory workmanship. The cost of radiographic examination for quality control shall be borne by the Vendor.

5.2.3 Procedures for radiographing girth welds shall be such that conclusive images of the entire welds are available. Welds of a size or type preventing conclusive radiographic images such as branch connections, shall have magnetic particle or dye penetrant examination.

5.2.4 Where examination of any type shows a weld fails to meet code requirements, examination shall be made of two more welds made by the same welder. If either of the additional welds fails to satisfy the coe, OWNER may reject work by the Welder. Repairs to defective or rejected work and costs of above additional examinations shall be at Vendor's expense.
