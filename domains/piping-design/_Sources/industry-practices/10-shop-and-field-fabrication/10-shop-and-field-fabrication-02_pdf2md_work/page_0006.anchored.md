## 4.2 Installation Details

4.2.1 No threaded connections shall be seal welded unless required by Piping Material Specifications. Seal welds shall cover exposed pipe threads completely.

4.2.2 Where seal welding is required, no pipe thread lubricant or sealant shall be used.

4.2.3 For stainless steel pipe threads thin Teflon tape shall be used on male threads as a lubricant. For other pipe and flanged-bolt threads a lubricant equal to Crane Anti-Seize thread compound shall be used on male threads. Threaded piping in corrosive or sour service is permitted as per OWNER's Specification GS 2.1 Piping Design shall not be back welded, socket welded joints are required.

## 4.3 Bending

4.3.1 Cold bends to a radius of five times the nominal pipe diameter or greater may be made without subsequent stress relieving.

4.3.2 Cold bending to a radius of less than five times the nominal pipe diameter shall not be permitted in the field.

4.3.3 Hot bending shall not be permitted in the field.

## 4.4 Underground Piping

Underground piping shall be carefully laid to grade and depth as specified on the drawings. Where possible underground piping is to be laid so that it will drain to one end of the pipe with traps.

## 4.5 Supports

4.5.1 Piping and equipment shall be adequately supported, rigid enough to prevent excessive vibration, and anchored sufficiently to prevent undue strains on the equipment served to the complete satisfaction of the OWNER.

4.5.2 Hangers and supports shall be made of non-combustible material and capable of sustaining the load to which subjected. Wood or wire shall not be used for permanent installations.

4.5.3 Where piping crosses supports and is tied down, rubbing pads fabricated as shown on the Drawings, or as approved by OWNER, shall be placed on the support to provide a rubbing surface for the pipe.

## 4.6 Coated Pipe

Coated pipe shall be handled with wide rubber or canvas belts without forward or backward scuffing motion. Rope of any kind, hooks, chains or cables shall not be used.
