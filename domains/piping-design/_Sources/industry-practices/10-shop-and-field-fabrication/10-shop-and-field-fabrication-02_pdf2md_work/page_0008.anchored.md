## 6.0 TESTING

### 6.1 General

**6.1.1** Vendor shall test all piping to the complete satisfaction of OWNER before placing in service. A testing procedure and method of draining all lines shall be submitted in writing to the OWNER for approval. Each system shall be tested to the pressure and for the time period specified by OWNER and in strict accordance with the following procedures. The piping shall be carefully examined for leaks and/or distortion. Vendor shall be responsible for rectifying all defects in workmanship and materials disclosed by the test. OWNER shall compensate Vendor for all extra labour in rectifying defects due to faulty material supplied by OWNER.

**6.1.2** Vendor shall supply all materials, equipment and labour required to successfully test all piping systems.

**6.1.3** Under no circumstances shall any test be carried out unless approved and witnessed by the OWNER.

**6.1.4** If testing is conducted with water as the test medium, when the temperature may fall below the freezing point, some form of antifreeze must be used to prevent ice forming during the testing.

**6.1.5** In no case will natural gas be permitted as a test medium, unless approved by OWNER.

**6.1.6** All liquid used for hydrostatic testing shall be removed from piping and lines dried out in accordance with service requirements and to the satisfaction of OWNER.

**6.1.7** Testing records shall be kept by Vendor and turned over to the OWNER upon completion.

### 6.2 Code Requirements

Process and utility pressure piping shall be tested as required by the applicable code and described herein.

### 6.3 Pressure Piping Defined

Pressure piping includes all piping designed to convey or contain process or utility fluids, at either a positive or a negative internal pressure. Open-ended vent, drain and like piping operating at essentially atmospheric pressure is not included.

### 6.4 Boiler Code Piping

Piping governed by Section I, Power Boilers, of the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code shall be tested hydrostatically as required by Paragraphs P-329 and P-300 of that code, at 1 1/2 times maximum allowable boiler working pressure.

### 6.5 Process Piping

Pressure piping governed by the Process Piping Code, ANSI B31.3, shall be tested as required by Section 337 of that code.
