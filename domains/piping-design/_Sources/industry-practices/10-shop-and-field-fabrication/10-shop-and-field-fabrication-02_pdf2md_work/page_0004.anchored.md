3.2.3 The spool sheets must show the spool number, materials, heat (MTR) numbers, dimensions, fabrication details, applicable codes and procedures.

3.2.4 A number consisting of the line number, assembly letter and piece number shall identify each pipe spool. Assembly letters and piece numbers shall be assigned alphabetically and numerically in direction of flow.

3.2.5 Spool sheets must identify all equipment and lines connecting to that spool.

3.2.6 The isometric drawing must be shown as reference.

3.2.7 Vendor shall provide reproducible copies of spool sheets.

3.2.8 Spool sheets shall show the limits of field run piping and locations of field welds required between spools.

3.2.9 Shop fabricated piping shall include all couplings, fittings, etc. required for connection of field fabricated branch connections.

3.2.10 Other size stubs or branches shall extend to the nearest flange or will be extended in the required direction as far as possible (limited only to shipping requirements).

## 3.3 Fabrication Details

3.3.1 All welding must be carried out in accordance with a suitable registered welding procedure by a competent certified welder who has demonstrated ability to weld to the procedure. This ability may be demonstrated from past performance using the procedure or one fully radiographed test weld.

3.3.2 Where not otherwise noted, flange boltholes shall straddle principal centrelines of equipment and piping.

3.3.3 Pipe and fittings shall be accurately aligned at weld joints. Concentricity of adjacent parts and alignment of internal surfaces shall be within 1.6 mm. Where parts with internal diameters differing by more than 1.6 mm are joined concentrically, the inside of the smaller bore shall be tapered to match the larger bore within 1.6 mm.

3.3.4 All threaded connections shall be gauge-checked or chased after welding or heat treating. Openings for thermowells and other inserts shall be drilled through the connection and be free from obstruction.

3.3.5 Templates shall be used in laying out headers, laterals, and other irregular details to insure accurate cutting and proper fit-up.

3.3.6 Machine cut bevels to form the welding groove are preferred, but smooth, clean, slag-free flame cut bevels are acceptable.

3.3.7 In fitting up details preparatory to final welding, spacers shall be used while tack welding the pipe and connections in position so that a proper gap is made for full penetration of welds.

3.3.8 Flange faces shall be in a plane perpendicular to the axis of the pipe to which they are welded.
