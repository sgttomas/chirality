2.2 **Conflicting Requirements**

2.2.1 In the event of a conflict between this specification and other specifications or government regulations, the Vendor shall consult the OWNER and a ruling in writing shall be obtained before any work is done.

**3.0 FABRICATION**

3.1 **General**

3.1.1 The following piping shall be shop fabricated unless otherwise approved by OWNER.

- Welded alloy piping
- Welded non-ferrous piping
- Welded carbon steel piping requiring heat treatment
- Above ground piping NPS 6 and larger

3.1.2 All other piping including closure welds of shop welded piping may be field fabricated unless otherwise directed by OWNER.

3.1.3 Vendor is responsible to insure that dimensions agree with the piping drawings.

3.1.4 Vendor must submit to OWNER the mill test reports of certifications required by applicable codes or other requirements.

3.2 **Shop Fabrication for Spools**

3.2.1 Vendor shall keep a running record of the status of his work. At the end of each workweek fabricator shall report all changes to status record since the previous report. The record shall show:

- Plant, area, drawing and spool number
- Date drawings received, their revision numbers, and their priority, if assigned
- Dates shop details started and released
- Dates material committed and available
- List of material committed
- Dates fabrication started and finished
- Dates spools released and shipped
- Dates last spool for each area shipped

3.2.2 Vendor shall make spool sheets, if required, for all piping.
