**PIPING FABRICATION, INSTALLATION AND TESTING**
**PIPING SPECIFICATION**
**000-PES-5007**
**Rev. 1.0** | **Rev Date: April 16, 2010**

## 1.0 GENERAL

### 1.1 Scope

This specification is to establish the quality of work procedures to be followed in the fabrication, installation and testing of all process and utility piping systems. Requirements herein shall govern field and shop fabrication.

### 1.2 Intent

The intent of this specification is to provide for a complete package in accordance with standard practice. This is a general specification, and therefore subject to variations in materials, design and construction, provided that the requirements of standard practice are met. Prior approval shall be obtained from the OWNER before making any change to these specifications.

### 1.3 Conflicting Requirements

Should a conflict arise between this specification and other specifications or government regulations, the Vendor shall contact the OWNER and a decision reached in writing before any work is done.

### 1.4 Vendor Responsibility

The Vendor shall be responsible for conformance to governing codes, OWNER's specifications, drawings, legal and contractual requirements. In the event of non-compliance with the foregoing, the OWNER may correct any deficiencies, and the cost of such repairs shall be charged to the Vendor.

## 2.0 CODE REQUIREMENTS

### 2.1 Codes and Standards

The following codes and standards shall apply:

- Provincial regulations governing the design, fabrication and testing of boilers, pressure vessels, and related facilities. In cases of conflict, the most rigid shall apply.
- American Standard Code for Pressure Piping, ANSI B31.1 or B31.3
- American standard Code for Steel Pipe Flanges and Flanged Fittings, ANSI B16.5
- ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code Section VIII and IX
- CSA Standard for Oil and Gas Pipeline Systems, Z-662.
- All completed piping, both bent and welded, shall conform to the fabrication and dimensional tolerances specified in the Standard of Pipe Fabrication Institute and the American Standards Association.
