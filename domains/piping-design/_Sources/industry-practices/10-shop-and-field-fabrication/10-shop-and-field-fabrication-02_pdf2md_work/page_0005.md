3.3.9 Tack welding should be performed to the same procedure as the final weld. Only small tack welds which penetrate to the bottom of the welding groove may be used and may become a part of the finished weld. Tack welds lacking penetration are not acceptable and must be removed. Each weld shall be cleaned of all scale, slag, flux and other foreign matter before additional welding beads are applied.

3.3.10 In general, permanently welded-in back-up rings shall not be used. If back-up rings should be necessary or desirable, they may be used only upon specific approval by OWNER.

3.3.11 Galvanized piping shall not be bent or welded. Galvanized piping required to be bent or welded shall be galvanized after fabrication.

3.3.12 Completed bends shall have smooth surfaces and shall be free of flat spots and corrugations.

3.3.13 Spools not too small for painted numbers shall also be identified by numbers painted or stenciled in characters at least 2 inches high. Painted numbers shall be located on spools, and repeated as necessary, to permit easy identification of any spool with turning or lifting.

3.3.14 Openings in pipe spools shall be securely closed and flange faces and threaded connections protected with suitable plugs, flange covers and thread protectors. Small diameter branches shall be adequately braced or otherwise protected to prevent damage in handling, shipping and storage.

3.3.15 Teflon tape shall be used as thread sealant on all tapered threads unless operating temperatures prohibit use. OWNER's approval is required for thread sealant for such conditions. Joints to be seal welded shall not be coated with thread sealant.

4.0 FIELD INSTALLATION

4.1 General

4.1.1 All pipe trenches or ditches shall be excavated and backfilled in accordance with OWNER's Specification GS 4.1 Earth Work and OHSA Regulations.

4.1.2 The piping is to be run true to vertical, horizontal and the axis of the building in such a way that it is pleasant to the eye unless the type of installation demands a slope on the pipe in which case the slope is to remain even along the length of the pipe.

4.1.3 The assembly of piping shall be performed so as to impose the least possible mechanical stress.

4.1.4 All piping shall be installed in a neat workmanlike manner, properly insulated, guided and supported.

4.1.5 Suitable points for venting and draining of liner shall be provided.
