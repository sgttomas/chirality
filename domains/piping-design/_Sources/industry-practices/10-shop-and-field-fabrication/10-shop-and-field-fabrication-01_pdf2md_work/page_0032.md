## A4 POSTWELD HEAT TREATMENT

A4.1 Postweld heat treatment shall be achieved by locally heating both welds simultaneously, using the electrical resistance method, or Halogen Quartz lamps.

A4.2 Postweld heat treating, including heating rate, and cooling rate, shall be in accordance with ASME B31.3 and STP 42. Holding time shall be 1/2 hour at temperature.

A4.3 Valve bodies and welds shall be insulated as shown in Figure 211-2, with at least 1" (24.4 mm) thick "Kaowool" or other insulation material approved by BP CHP. Insulation should not only be as thick as is practicable to reduce heat loss, but should also extend along the pipe, on both sides of the valve, a minimum of 6" to provide a gradual temperature gradient in both directions when electric heating coils are used. Special applications will be used when Halogen Quartz lamps are the heat source.

A4.4 Do not insulate upper (bonnet) portion of the valve.

A4.5 Valves shall be closed completely, then backed-off two full handwheel revolutions during the postweld heat treatment cycle. If short, open-ended piping is involved, the pipe ends shall be sealed to prevent heat loss by convection.
