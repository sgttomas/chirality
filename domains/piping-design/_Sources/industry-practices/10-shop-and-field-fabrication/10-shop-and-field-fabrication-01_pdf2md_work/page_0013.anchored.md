16.3.5 Connections made to in-service piping (i.e. hot taps) shall be performed as outlined in this standard and Cherry Point Safety & Health Policy 520.

## 16.4 Circumferential and Longitudinal Welds

### 16.4.1

Adjacent sections of welded pipe that are joined by buttwelding shall have the longitudinal weld seams positioned so that they are at least 30° apart. The longitudinal weld joints are not to contact or be positioned directly on the pipe supports.

### 16.4.2

The fabricator shall position the longitudinal weld seam away from the top and bottom of pipe, and away from any branch connection fitting locations such as Pipets and Olets so they will not be positioned on a weld seam.

### 16.4.3

Circumferential buttweld joints shall not be positioned directly on pipe supports unless the crown of the weld has been ground flush with the pipe O.D.

## 16.5 Slip-on Flanges

### 16.5.1

For all services, slip-on flanges shall be double-welded (front and back) as shown in ASME B31.3, Figure 328.5.2B - Typical Detail (1). Flanges shall be positioned so that the distance from the flange face to the pipe end provides 1/8 inch clearance from the front fillet weld to the flange face. The weld shall be applied in such a manner that refacing of the flanges is not necessary.

**Note:** Double-welding for all services exceeds the requirements of B31.3, but is required by BP CHP to reduce corrosion potential and provide a stronger flange-to-pipe joint.

## 16.6 Socket Weld Joints

In accordance with ASME B31.3 or B31.1 as applicable, socket weld joints shall incorporate a gap between the bottom of the socket and the end of the pipe to accommodate thermal expansion. Use a GAP-A-LET (or approved equivalent) socket weld contraction ring to ensure proper gap. The pipe end shall be inserted into the socket a minimum of 1/4". A minimum of two weld passes are required, and the minimum fillet weld size shall conform to the applicable ASME piping code. See standard drawing LA-000-0100-0200-018.

## 16.7 Meter Runs and Orifice Flanges

### 16.7.1

Pipe ends and bevels at the flanges shall be machine cut, and be perpendicular to the axis of the pipe. Welds at the flange shall be ground smooth and flush inside.
