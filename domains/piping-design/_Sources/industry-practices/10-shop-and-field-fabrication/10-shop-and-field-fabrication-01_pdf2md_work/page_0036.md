## C4 CHECK VALVES

**C4.1** Install the valve with the flow arrow on the valve body pointed in the direction of the fluid flow indicated on the drawing. Check valves installed in reverse will not open.

**C4.2** Swing check valves may have been shipped with the disc blocked with a wood or other material in the closed position to prevent damage during shipment. Inspect the inside of the valve and remove the block before installation.

## C5 EXPANSION JOINTS

**C5.1** Check the expansion joint drawing for installation and special instructions. Make a final check to see that the shipping ties have been removed after the line pressure test and that any preset that may be specified has been accounted for.

## C6 SPRING/ROD HANGERS

**C6.1** Install all spring hangers and rod hangers per the hanger detail drawings and the manufacturer's instructions. The variable and constant support type spring hangers are preset at the factory for the cold condition. The gags and shipping spacers shall not be removed until the piping system pressure test has been completed and/or the line has been filled with the product at a temperature less than 100°F. Any dimensional adjustment to be made to the load flange on compression-type springs, or to the rod assembly on suspension-type springs, should be made before the gags are removed. Once the spring is unlocked, it is very difficult to make any adjustment.

## C7 UNDERGROUND PIPE

**C7.1** The minimum pipe cover for underground pipe shall be 30 inches unless approved otherwise by the BP CHP Reliability Engineer. See standard drawing LB-000-0100-0801-008.
