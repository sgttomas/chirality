## SECTION 26: PAINTING AND SPECIAL COATING

26.1 Fabricated pipe spools shall receive surface preparation and painting in accordance with STP 500 and, if specified underground coating per STP 503.

26.2 Prior to system hydrotest, shop and field welds may only have one coat of inorganic zinc primer, properly applied in accordance with STP 500, System #4. For fabricated piping which will be field pressure tested, shop finish coat painting may be applied if welds are masked off.

## SECTION 27: MARKING AND IDENTIFICATION

27.1 Piece Marking

27.1.1 Piece marking and identification shall be used to identify the construction area for each fabricated spool.

27.1.2 Each shop fabricated piping spool piece shall be identified as shown below. The identification shall be painted in the shop fabricator's facility before shipment.

[FIGURE: 10-shop-and-field-fabrication-01_p0026_fig001]

27.2 Surface Preparation

27.2.1 Surfaces to be marked shall be free of oil, grease, dirt and other surface contaminants which would be detrimental to the adhesion of the paint used for marking.

27.3 Marking Materials

27.3.1 Piece marking shall be done using silicone acrylic finish paint or a Nissen ball paint marker.

27.3.2 For austenitic stainless steel and nickel alloy piping, all marking materials including labels, marking pens, tapes, and similar items that will come in direct contact with the piping shall be low chloride type with maximum 50 ppm leachable chloride content when tested per ASTM C871. These marking materials also shall not contain any metal or metal salts such as zinc, lead, or copper, which could cause corrosive attack either upon cold application or with process heating.
