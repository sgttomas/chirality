21.3 All examinations shall be performed after completion of PWHT. Radiographic examination and ultrasonic examination shall cover the entire circumference or full length of the weld.

21.4 The welds to be randomly radiographed or ultrasonic examined shall represent each welder's work and welding procedure used. Weld selection shall be spread throughout the fabrication, from start-of-job to completion and shall include the heaviest wall pipe and largest pipe size fabricated.

21.5 Where threaded joints are required to be seal welded, five (5) percent of these joints shall be randomly selected and radiographed for defects and also to determine that there is proper thread engagement.

21.6 All butt welds joining new pipe to existing pipe shall be 100% RT examined.

21.7 For ASME B31.3 piping, acceptance criteria shall be in accordance with "Normal Fluid Service" in Table 341.3.2 of the code, unless **a more stringent requirement** is specified on the isometric drawings or in the contract documents, in which case the more severe acceptance criteria shall govern.

21.8 Progressive examination, as required by Paragraph 341.3.4 of ASME B31.3, shall apply to defective welds.

21.9 Cost of examination and repair of additional welds brought about by "progressive examination" shall be to the fabricator's account.

## SECTION 22: REPAIR OF WELDS

22.1 Any unacceptable defect disclosed by any of the required examination methods shall be repaired by the fabricator.

22.2 Required welds shall be re-examined using the required NDE processes for new welds as stated in Paragraph 21.2 of this standard.

22.3 Cost of repairs and re-examination of defective welds shall be to the fabricator's account.

22.4 Only two repair attempts are allowed on any one defective area. No further attempt to repair shall be initiated without prior BP CHP Reliability Engineering approval.

## SECTION 23: POSITIVE MATERIAL IDENTIFICATION (PMI)

23.1 The fabricator shall perform PMI analysis on alloy (all materials other than carbon steel) piping components including but not limited to pipe, flanges, fittings, and valves, to ensure that the proper amount of alloying elements are present in the material.

23.2 PMI analysis of welds on alloy piping including repair welds shall be in accordance with STP 42.
