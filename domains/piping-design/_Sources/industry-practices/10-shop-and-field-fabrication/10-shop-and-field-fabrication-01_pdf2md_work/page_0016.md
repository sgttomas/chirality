16.13 Welded Austenitic Stainless Steel

16.13.1 Austenitic (300 series) stainless steel materials which will be welded during fabrication shall be dual grade (such as 316/316L) or low carbon grade (such as 316L), unless otherwise approved by the BP CHP Reliability Engineer. Dual grade material should normally be specified since it fully meets the requirements of both grades and provides the combined benefits of both grades. Low carbon grade provides better resistance to carbide precipitation and intergranular corrosion of welds and heat affected zones. The dual grade then combines the better corrosion resistance with the higher mechanical properties of the standard grade material.

## SECTION 17: PIPE BENDING

17.1 Pipe bending shall not be used unless specifically shown on piping drawings, and it shall indicate the required radius.

17.2 Pipe bending shall be in strict accordance with ASME B31.3, Paragraph 332, and the following:

17.2.1 Unless noted otherwise, the minimum radius for pipe bends shall be six times the nominal pipe diameter.

17.2.2 Wall thickness of completed bends shall not be less than that required for pressure-temperature and corrosion allowance. The drawings shall show the final wall thickness as fabricated.

17.2.3 Tensile and impact properties of the base materials shall not be impaired during bending operation.

17.2.4 Austenitic stainless steel pipe shall not be bent unless it is subsequently solution heat treated per the ASTM material standard specified.

17.2.5 Seam welds shall not be located in the inside and outside radius of bent pipe.

17.2.6 Weld repairing of cracks in pipe bends is not be acceptable.

17.2.7 Induction pipe bending requires prior approval of BP CHP Reliability Engineering. Induction bending is restricted to low-yield, carbon-steel pipe and austenitic stainless steel pipe. The pipe shall be seamless. Process controls shall be built into the equipment console, which permit the operator to set the equipment in accordance with qualified pipe bending programs. A separate program is required for the carbon and stainless steel pipe materials for each pipe diameter, wall thickness, and change in specified mechanical properties.
