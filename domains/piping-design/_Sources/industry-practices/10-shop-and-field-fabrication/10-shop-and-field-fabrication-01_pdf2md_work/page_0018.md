## SECTION 20: HARDNESS REQUIREMENTS

Weld hardness testing requirements, procedures, and acceptance criteria shall be in accordance with STP 42.

## SECTION 21: EXAMINATION REQUIREMENTS

21.1 Where random examination or inspection is required in this standard, the welds or parts to be examined or inspected shall be randomly selected by BP CHP or their designated representative. If unacceptable defects are found, the additional parts or welds to be examined or inspected (progressive examination as required by paragraph 21.8) shall be selected by BP CHP or their designated representative. If there is reason to believe that the requirements of this standard are not being met, BP CHP or their designated representative may increase the percentage of welds or parts to be examined or inspected to ensure that the quality of the welds or parts meet the requirements of this standard.

21.2 As a minimum, pressure containing welds shall be examined as shown in the table below. The inspector or engineer may increase the examination requirements. Additional examination requirements are listed in the paragraphs below the table. The Radiographic (RT) or Ultrasonic (UT) examinations listed in the table apply to both buttwelds and socketwelds, and the minimum percentage applies separately to both types of weld. The required MT or PT shall be made on the final cap pass for socketwelds, buttwelds and branch connection welds (Pipets, WOL, TOL, SOL, etc.).

**Note 1:** Ultrasonic examination may be substituted for liquid penetrant examination, at the discretion of the fabricator, without additional cost to BP CHP.

**Note 2:** On jacketed piping, the fillet weld joining the inner pipe and the back of the slip-on flange shall be 100% magnetic particle examined before the jacket is placed.
