9.8 Reducers are concentric unless otherwise noted.

9.9 Dimensions locating lateral pipets are to the intersecting point on the outside of the pipe.

9.10 All vent and drain locations, including hydrotest vents and drains, shall be shown and identified on the isometric drawings.

## SECTION 10: FABRICATION DIMENSIONAL TOLERANCES

10.1 The tolerances for fabrication dimensions in this section apply to piping in-line items, branch connections and continuation of lines to other drawings.

10.2 These tolerances may be exceeded to a maximum of 1/2 inch for vent and drain connection locations, dummy support lengths, temperature and pressure tap locations, providing the deviation does not affect an in-line item.

10.3 Tolerances for general dimensions such as face-to-face, face-to-end, end-to-end, face- to-center, end-to-center, and center-to-center shall be as follows:

- 10.3.1 ±1/8 inch for NPS 10 and smaller;
- 10.3.2 ±3/16 inch for NPS 12 through 36;
- 10.3.3 ±3/16 inch for NPS 38 and larger with additional ±1/32 inch for each 12 inch size increment over 36 inches;
- 10.3.4 When closer tolerances are required, they shall be noted on the piping isometrics. Closer and more stringent tolerances will be required for all rotating equipment.

10.4 The tolerance for inclination of flange face from true in any direction is 3/64 inch per foot.

10.5 Rotation of flange bolt holes shall not exceed 1/16 inch from two-hole.

10.6 Fabrication tolerances not specified here shall comply with PFI Standard ES3.
