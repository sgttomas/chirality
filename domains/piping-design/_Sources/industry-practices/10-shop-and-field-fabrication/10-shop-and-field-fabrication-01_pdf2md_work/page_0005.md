7.2. The procedure shall include a plan on segregation and tracking of OFM to ensure correct metallurgy and traceability of the completed spools.

## SECTION 8: DEFINITIONS

8.1 The terms "shop fabricated" and "field fabricated" piping shall be defined as follows:

   a) Shop Fabricated - fabrication performed at a "vendor" shop remote from the job site.

   b) Field Fabricated - fabrication performed at the job site either in the field shop or at the installation site area. Where necessary, piping to be fabricated in the field shop may be identified in the drawings.

## SECTION 9: DRAWINGS

9.1 The pipe fabricator shall be responsible for the accuracy of their pipe spool drawings, and shall maintain them current. Each spool shall be detailed on a separate sheet.

9.2 The field fabricator shall maintain drawings current and updated as to any field changes that may be required and shall provide BP CHP with as-built drawings upon completion of the job. Field changes shall be authorized by BP CHP in writing before proceeding.

9.3 Piping isometrics shall show all components for welding and assembly.

9.4 Requirements for field fabricated portions of shop fabricated lines should be indicated on the drawings. The shop fabricator shall not supply or fabricate piping that is so noted.

9.5 The fabricator shall indicate on the shop drawings the flange orientation as defined in section 16.2.

9.6 Drawing Dimensional Tolerances

   9.6.1 Dimensions on drawings are in feet and inches. Only inches are shown when dimensions are less than 1'-0". Feet and inches are shown when dimensions are 1'-0" and larger.

   9.6.2 All dimensions shall be field verified for accuracy prior to fabrication. When field verification is not possible a "field fit up joint" should be used.

9.7 Where cold spring (CS) or pre-spring (PS) is required, the dimension shown on the piping isometric includes the required amount subtracted or added. The piping shall be fabricated exactly to the dimension shown. Use of cold spring or pre-spring in the design requires approval by the BP CHP Reliability Engineer.
