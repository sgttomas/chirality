24.6 Items Excluded From Field Piping Pressure Test

The following items shall not be subjected to the pressure tests or test fluids during field piping tests:

a) Lines normally open to atmosphere, such as open drains beyond the final valve, relief valve discharge stacks, etc.

b) Control valves and all instruments, without BP CHP approval.

c) Pumps, turbines, and compressors.

d) Rupture discs, relief valves, and flame arresters.

e) Package filters and expansion joints that have been shop tested, unless approved by BP CHP Reliability Engineer.

f) Heater coils and manifolds which are a part of the heater, such as crossovers, etc., which are included in a separate heater test, unless approved by BP CHP Reliability Engineer.

g) Any equipment or device which cannot withstand the test pressure or test fluid.

h) Any other equipment which may be designated by BP CHP.

24.7 Test Procedures and Restrictions

24.7.1 Joints in underground piping shall not be backfilled until testing is complete. Joint should be accessible 360° around the circumference of the pipe.

24.7.2 Spring supports shall be protected with adequate travel stops to prevent overloading.

24.7.3 The duration of holding piping at the test pressure shall be as determined by BP CHP's Inspector but in any case shall not be less than 10 minutes.

24.7.4 Test blinds or operational blinds that are used in testing must be capable of withstanding the test pressure without distortion.
