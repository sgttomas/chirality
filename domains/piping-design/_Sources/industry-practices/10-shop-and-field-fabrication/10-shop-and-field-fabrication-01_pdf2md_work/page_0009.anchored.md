### 13.2.2 Metals Prone To Galling & Potable Water (-65°F to 400°F)

Metal pipe thread sealant for metals prone to galling shall be PTFE based paste, Loctite 567 (Storehouse #3370005670) or an approved alternate. For potable water applications use Loctite 567 in 250 ml or 1 liter containers (not stocked – order as needed), which are NSF-61 certified. Use with Loctite 7649 activator (highly flammable, Storehouse #3363076490) for cold applications below 40°F or for inactive metals (galvanized steel, plated fittings, stainless steel, titanium, Inconel, aluminum). DO NOT USE Loctite 567 for temperatures above 400°F or for pure oxygen, oxygen-rich services, chlorine, or other strong oxidizing services.

### 13.2.3 High Temperature Applications (401°F to 1175°F)

Metal pipe thread sealant for high temperature applications up to 1175°F for 2" & smaller pipe shall be graphite based paste, Grafoil GTS (Storehouse #28510-46049) or an approved alternate. DO NOT USE Grafoil GTS for temperatures above 1175°F, for pipe larger than 2", or for pure oxygen, oxygen-rich services, chlorine, or other strong oxidizing services.

### 13.2.4 Extreme Temperature Applications (1176°F to 1800°F)

Metal pipe thread sealant for extreme temperature applications up to 1800°F shall be copper based paste, Jomar Hi-Temp Anti-Seize Lube/Thread Seal (Storehouse #28450260000) or an approved alternate. DO NOT USE Jomar Hi-Temp Anti-Seize Lube/Thread Seal for temperatures above 1800°F, or for pure oxygen, oxygen-rich services, chlorine, or other strong oxidizing services.

### 13.2.5 Oxygen & Aggressive Chemicals

Metal pipe thread sealant for pure oxygen, oxygen-rich services, chlorine, or other strong oxidizing services, shall be PTFE tape or Hernon Dripstop 950 paste, if service temperature allows. See the following paragraphs for the sealant material specifications. For service temperatures above 500°F contact Reliability Engineering.

a) PTFE tape (-212°F to 500°F) shall be industrial grade, ½" width, 3.5 mil thickness, Dixon TT-150 (Storehouse free bin) or an approved alternate. Wrap the external threads with 2 or 3 layers of tape. DO NOT USE PTFE tape for general applications, unless approved by Reliability Engineering.

b) Hernon Dripstop 950 (-65°F to 450°F, Storehouse #28450950500) is a non-hardening PCTFE/PTFE based paste sealant for harsh environments. DO NOT USE Dripstop 950 for general applications since it is very expensive.
