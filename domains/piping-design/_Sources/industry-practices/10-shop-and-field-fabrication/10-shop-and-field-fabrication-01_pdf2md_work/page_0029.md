29.3    Austenitic stainless steel and nickel alloy piping shall be stored under cover and out of contact with the ground.

## SECTION 30: FIELD INSPECTION

The "Field Inspection Procedures and Records" attached hereto as Appendix B shall be followed.

## SECTION 31: CHEMICAL AND MECHANICAL CLEANING

31.1    Any chemical or mechanical cleaning as specified in the contract documents or on the isometric drawings shall be performed after fabrication and field installation is complete.

31.2    All compressor suction piping shall be mechanically cleaned or pickled after installation.

31.3    All oxygen piping shall be mechanically and chemically cleaned as specified in the applicable pipe classes in STP 205.

31.4    General cleaning requirements shall be in accordance with PFI Standard ES5.
