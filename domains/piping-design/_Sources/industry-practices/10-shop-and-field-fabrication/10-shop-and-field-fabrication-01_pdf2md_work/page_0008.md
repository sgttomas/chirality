11.4.2 Miscellaneous parts such as hose couplings, steam traps, tube fittings, strainer internals, and screens.

11.5 The fabricator shall submit to BP CHP the correct Manufacturer's Data Report forms P4A for all pipe manufactured to Section I of the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code. Pressure and Temperature entries to the P4A form shall indicate the maximum allowable operating conditions unless otherwise stated.

## SECTION 12: SOURCES OF MATERIALS

12.1 All piping components shall be furnished in accordance with the BP CHP approved manufacturers or suppliers lists. The country of origin shall be as stated in the lists.

12.2 All foreign material shall require Certified Material Test Reports in addition to ASTM or ASME markings for acceptance (see Paragraph 11.2.7).

## SECTION 13: THREADED JOINTS

13.1 General

13.1.1 Where threaded fittings or pipe nipples are welded to pipe spools, the threads shall be checked for fit and roundness following stress-relieving. If threads are not in acceptable condition, chase threads with a tap or die. Threads shall then be cleaned with cutting oil and a suitable solvent.

13.1.2 Threaded pipe joints shall be assembled with the use of thread sealant as specified below, unless otherwise approved by BP CHP Reliability Engineering. For applications that do not fit the categories below, consult with the BP CHP Reliability Engineer.

13.2 Metal Pipe Thread Sealant

13.2.1 General Applications & Potable Water (-65°F to 300°F)

Metal pipe thread sealant for general applications shall be PTFE based paste, Loctite 565 (Storehouse free bin) or an approved alternate. For potable water applications use Loctite 565 in 250 ml or 1 liter containers (not stocked – order as needed), which are NSF-61 certified. Use with Loctite 7649 activator (highly flammable, Storehouse #3363076490) for cold applications below 40°F or for inactive metals (galvanized steel, plated fittings, stainless steel, titanium, Inconel, aluminum). Use Loctite 567 for metals prone to galling and for temperatures from 301°F to 400°F. DO NOT USE Loctite 565 for temperatures above 300°F or for pure oxygen, oxygen-rich services, chlorine, or other strong oxidizing services.
