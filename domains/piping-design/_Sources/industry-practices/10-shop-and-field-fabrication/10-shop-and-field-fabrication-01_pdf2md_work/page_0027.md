When requested by BP CHP, test reports shall be provided to verify conformance of the chloride content of the material.

27.3.3 Piece marking color shall be white.

27.4 Application

27.4.1 Piece marking and identification paint shall be applied to a dry clean surface in accordance with Manufacturer's instructions.

27.4.2 Unless otherwise recommended by the paint manufacturer, marking shall **NOT** be done when the ambient temperature is less than 50°F, or the relative humidity is more than 90 percent.

27.4.3 Marking paint shall be applied in one coat.

27.4.4 When marking paint is to be applied to a primed surface, the primer shall be completely dry before the marking paint is applied.

## SECTION 28: PREPARATION FOR SHIPMENT

28.1 After required examination and inspection, all shop fabricated carbon, alloy and stainless steel pipe for domestic shipping shall be protected as follows:

28.1.1 Flange faces shall be cleaned, coated with rust preventative LPS No. 3 manufactured by Research Laboratories or BP CHP approved equal and protected with "K-FOAM" low density 1/4" thick, plastic flange covers and secured with plastic lock plugs as manufactured by Mark V Corporation, Houston, Texas or BP CHP approved equal. Wood or other material that can absorb and retain water are not acceptable as flange facing protectors unless 1/8 inch thick rubber or plastic is inserted between the flange facing protector. Other methods and/or products for flange protection are acceptable, subject to prior Buyer authorization.

28.1.2 Threaded or socket weld connections shall be cleaned and plugged or capped with metal or plastic protectors.

28.1.3 The open ends of the pipe shall be protected with metal caps secured with tape.

28.1.4 A metal cap secured by a steel strap or tape around the pipe shall be used to protect open end Pipets or Olets.

28.1.5 Austenitic stainless steel and nickel alloy pipe shall not be exposed to salt or salt spray. Protection shall also be provided when shipping by truck in areas where road salt is used.
