## SECTION 18: WELDING

18.1 Welding, including preheat, bake-out, and PWHT shall be in accordance with ASME B31.3 or B31.1 as applicable, and STP 42.

18.2 Metal that has been exposed to alkaline services, such as caustic, catacarb, or amine, shall be neutralized prior to welding per STP 42.

18.3 The root pass for the following piping shall be made by the Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW) process with adequate purging. This piping shall be identified on the pipe isometric drawings.

a) Suction piping between knockout drums and all compressors.

b) Turbine inlet piping.

c) All seal and lube oil piping.

d) Alloy and stainless steel piping. (GMAW process may be allowed in some cases in accordance with STP 42 with approval of BP CHP Inspector and Reliability Engineer.)

e) Acid piping.

18.4 The use of GMAW process in welding branch connection fittings such as weldolets and sockolets shall not be used unless approved in writing by BP CHP. If approved, the fabricator must demonstrate to BP CHP's satisfaction that the weld is sound and free of unacceptable defects. Additional non-destructive examinations on production welds may be required at BP CHP's option.

**Note:** Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW) is not permitted for field fabrication, unless approved by BP CHP.

18.5 Dissimilar metal welds are not allowed for pressure containing welds in wet H₂S or HF services, since hydrogen can be charged into the metal and result in hydrogen stress cracking. Exceptions apply for some clad pipe applications. See additional information in STP 42 section 13.

## SECTION 19: POSTWELD HEAT TREATMENT (PWHT)

19.1 In addition to any mandatory PWHT requirements of the applicable piping code, see STP 42 for typical BP CHP requirements for PWHT.

19.2 PWHT shall be performed after all welding and forming operations are completed, including non-pressure attachment welds.
