23.7.4 PMI sampling and analysis shall be done in such a way as not to damage the component or reduce the wall thickness below the minimum required.

23.8 Alloy Acceptance

23.8.1 All materials shall comply with the requirements of the specified ASTM or ASME standard. The PMI analysis shall measure the concentration of Cr, Ni and other essential alloy elements, such as Mo, Cb (or Nb), and Ti. The results shall be within the range specified by the applicable standard.

23.9 Rejection Criteria

23.9.1 If the measured concentration of any of the major alloying elements in Paragraph 23.8 are below the range specified by ASTM or ASME, all the components in the respective heat lot shall be rejected, or a wet chemistry check may be performed. If a wet chemistry check is performed, it will determine acceptance or rejection of the heat lot. For all rejected components which cannot be identified by heat lot, all similar piping components without heat lot identification shall be PMI analyzed.

23.9.2 If the measured concentration of any of the major alloying elements in Paragraph 23.8 are greater than the range specified by ASTM or ASME, the alloy content must be reported to BP CHP for acceptance or rejection.

23.9.3 The fabricator shall identify and remove all rejected materials and replace them with the correct materials. For fabricator supplied materials, the fabricator shall bear the cost of replacement of any rejected materials.

## SECTION 24: PRESSURE TESTING

24.1 General

24.1.1 Hydrostatic pressure testing to insure the integrity of all piping joints shall be required of all piping, except as modified herein. Pressure testing will be accomplished in the field, unless otherwise approved by BP CHP, and must be satisfactorily completed prior to insulating the piping joints, prior to any required chemical cleaning, and prior to commissioning and start-up. The witnessing of any or all testing is the prerogative of the BP CHP Inspector.
