13.3 Plastic Pipe Thread Sealant

13.3.1 Thread Sealing Requirements

Thread sealant shall be used for all threaded thermoplastic pipe joints and shall be chemically compatible with the fluid service and the pipe material. Acceptable sealants are non-hardening paste certified for thermoplastic piping, or PTFE tape. Sealant material specifications are in the following paragraphs. Paste sealant is preferred over PTFE tape, since overtightening is more likely to occur with use of tape. Non-lubricating or low-lubricity (non-PTFE) pastes should be used to prevent over tightening the threaded joints. Thermoplastic pipe threads need sealant but do not need lubrication. Tighten joints finger tight (not hand tight) plus 1½ turns to 2 turns maximum with strap wrench or other non-damaging tool. Note that the upper temperature limits for the sealants below may exceed the limits of the piping material. Typical maximum temperatures for plastic pipe material are 210°F for CPVC and 140°F for PVC.

13.3.2 Paste Sealant (-50°F to 400°F)

Paste sealant for plastic pipe threads shall be non-hardening non-PTFE paste. Spears Blue 75 (Storehouse #2870075020) or an approved alternate. The maximum useage temperature may be limited by gaseous service. For gaseous fluids verify the maximum allowable temperature with Spears.

13.3.3 Tape Sealant (-212°F to 500°F)

Tape sealant for plastic pipe threads shall be industrial grade PTFE tape, ½" width, 3.5 mil thickness, Dixon TTA50 (Storehouse free bin) or an approved alternate. Wrap the external threads with 2 or 3 layers of tape.

13.3.4 Paste Sealant For Harsh Environments (-65°F to 450°F)

Paste sealant for plastic pipe threads in harsh environments shall be non-hardening PCTFE/PTFE based paste, Hernon Dripstop 950 (Storehouse #2845095050) or an approved alternate. DO NOT USE Dripstop 950 for general applications since it is very expensive.

## SECTION 14: FIELD WELDS

14.1 Drawings shall indicate all field welds (FW) required for erection and shipping, and will also indicate shop fabrication and field fabrication requirements. See paragraph 16.4 for positioning of circumferential and longitudinal weld joints.

14.2 All field welds shall be located so as to minimize field welding.
