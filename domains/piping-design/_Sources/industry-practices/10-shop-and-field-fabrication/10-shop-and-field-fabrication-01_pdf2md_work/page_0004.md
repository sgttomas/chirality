## SECTION 4: PIPING COMPONENTS AND MATERIALS / METALLURGY

4.1 Piping components, materials and metallurgy shall be in accordance with the contract documents and the specified piping classes in STP 205. Any conflicts between the contract documents and STP 205 shall be reported to the BP CHP Reliability Engineer.

4.2 Materials which the fabricator is responsible to furnish will be defined in the contract documents. Material descriptions provided in the contract documents shall be used along with STP 208 to purchase any piping components specified for purchase by the fabricator.

## SECTION 5: CONFLICTS AND DEVIATIONS

5.1 Any conflicts between the requirements of this standard, related specifications, codes, standards, purchase orders, drawings, and data sheets shall be referred to the BP CHP Reliability Engineer for clarification before proceeding with the fabrication of the affected parts.

5.2 In the event that any of the designs, specifications, or other criteria furnished by BP CHP to the fabricator under this order shall prevent fabricator from conforming to his obligations to meet the codes and standards as defined above, fabricator is required to notify BP CHP prior to proceeding.

5.3 Any deviation from this standard shall be submitted in writing to BP CHP for authorization. Deviation is not permitted unless prior approval in writing from BP CHP Reliability Engineering is obtained.

## SECTION 6: SUBSTITUTIONS AND CHANGES

6.1 Piping components and materials, including thicker wall materials, shall not be substituted without prior written authorization. When "minimum wall" or "guaranteed minimum wall" piping is specified in the contract documents, the fabricator shall advise BP CHP Reliability Engineering of the nominal wall thickness prior to procurement and fabrication.

6.2 Alternative configurations to the piping arrangements on the drawings shall not be furnished without prior written authorization.

## SECTION 7: CONTROL OF OWNER-FURNISHED MATERIALS (OFM)

7.1 The fabricator shall submit his procedure for receipt, control, and issuance of OFM to BP CHP within two weeks after purchase order award for review and approval by the BP CHP Project Manager.
