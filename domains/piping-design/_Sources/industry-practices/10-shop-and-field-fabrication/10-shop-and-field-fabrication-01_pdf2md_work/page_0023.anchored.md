24.3.2 Hydrostatic test pressure for piping not covered by ASME Codes B31.1 and B31.3 will be established by the BP CHP Engineer.

24.3.3 Gravity lines shall be tested by filling with water. For underground gravity sewer lines, see test requirements as specified in STP 801.

24.3.4 Where practical, new piping to be connected to existing piping shall be hydrotested after the weld tie-in connection is completed. At a convenient point in the existing pipe, the system shall be blind and isolated. As a minimum requirement, the test pressure shall comply with ASME B31.3 or B31.1, as applicable. Alternative NDT and examinations for closure or tie-in welds, as allowed by the applicable piping code, may be acceptable upon approval by the BP CHP Reliability Engineer.

24.3.5 Steam traps will be tested per the manufacturer's recommendations and requirements.

The hydrotest pressure shall be introduced through the inlet side of the trap.

24.3.6 Bellows sealed valves shall be in the full open position during the test to prevent accumulation of debris in the bellows.

24.4 Pneumatic Testing

24.4.1 The use of pneumatic pressure testing shall be minimized. The hazards of pneumatic testing, especially of large volume systems, shall be recognized and provision must be made to minimize the exposure of personnel to these hazards. Testing procedures and requirements shall comply with requirements in the BP CHP Control of Work Policy.

24.4.2 Instrument air lines shall be tested to at least the operating pressure with dry instrument air or with nitrogen.

24.4.3 Reinforcing pads shall be pneumatically tested at a minimum pressure of 5 psig. Telltale holes shall be filled with heavy grease after pressure testing.
