## SECTION 1: PURPOSE

This standard establishes the minimum requirements for the fabrication, inspection and testing of piping systems for BP Cherry Point Refinery referred to as BP CHP in this standard.

## SECTION 2: SCOPE

2.1 This standard applies to shop and field fabrication of all process and utility piping including piping supplied with packaged equipment or modules, and shall be used along with the applicable job specific piping specifications and drawings.

2.2 When terms such as "authorized", "approved" or "accepted" are used in this standard, it means that the intended action will be by BP CHP Reliability Engineering, unless otherwise specified.

2.3 Where jurisdictional authorities have requirements exceeding or in conflict with this standard, such requirements shall supersede this standard and BP CHP Reliability Engineering should be notified of the conflict.
