16.1.1 Use spreaders or internal and/or external line-up clamps to correct moderate out of round condition. Backing rings or consumable inserts shall not be used.

16.1.3 Taper bore or grind the wall (in accordance with ASME B31.3) of the components extending internally (maximum mismatch per paragraph 16.1) after obtaining approval of BP CHP Reliability Engineering to ensure wall thickness is not reduced below the minimum required by the code, including corrosion allowance.

16.2 Flange Orientation

16.2.1 Flange bolt holes are to be oriented as follows, unless otherwise indicated on the piping isometrics.

a) For vertical flange faces, bolt holes shall straddle the vertical centerline.

b) For horizontal flange faces, bolt holes shall straddle the north-south centerline established by the piping drawings.

16.3 Branch Connections

16.3.1 Branch connections such as pipets, weldolets (WOL), threadolets (TOL), and sockolets (SOL) shall be joined to the header by full penetration welds, and shall be fully welded out per the manufacturer's instructions. See standard detail drawing LA-000-0100-0200-019. Acceptance shall be in accordance with ASME B31.3, Table 341.3.2, "Normal Fluid Service", unless a more stringent requirement is specified in the purchase order.

16.3.2 Start and stop welds on the root pass shall be feathered in before subsequent passes are welded.

16.3.3 Branch connections shall be prepared in accordance with Figures 328.4.4 and 328.5.4D of ASME B31.3. A minimum root gap (g) of 1/16" shall be established before welding. The root gap shall be stated in the weld procedure specification.

16.3.4 One hundred (100) percent visual inspection of the weld shall be performed on both OD and ID of the weld. To facilitate visual inspection of the inside weld, inspection shall be done before welding any piping component to the branch fitting. When direct visual inspection, as defined in ASME Section V, Article 9, Paragraph T-952 cannot be performed, a remote visual inspection in accordance with Paragraph T-953 of ASME Section V shall be performed.
