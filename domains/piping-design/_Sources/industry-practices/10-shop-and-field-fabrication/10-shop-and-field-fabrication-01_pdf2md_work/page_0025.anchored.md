24.7.5 Pressure gauges that are a permanent part of the plant design shall not be used for testing the piping. Test gauges shall have been properly calibrated with calibration date marked on gauge prior to use. A suspect gauge should be recalibrated per paragraph UG-102(c) of ASME, Section VIII, Div. 1.

24.7.6 After the pressure tests are complete, all lines and equipment shall be completely vented and drained. The piping system shall be restored with all components installed and connected. All flanged joints that were blinded shall be made up tight with the proper gaskets. All strainers shall be removed, cleaned, and reinstalled.

24.7.7 Austenitic stainless steel piping must be drained completely.

24.7.8 All unvalved hydrotest vents and drains shall be plugged or blinded and the plug seal welded to the branch fitting per paragraph 14.4.4 after the hydrotest. For low alloy and stainless steel piping, the plug shall be PMI'd (positive material identification) prior to seal welding the plug.

24.8 Test Records

The minimum requirements shall be to maintain records in accordance with the provisions of ASME B31.3, the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, or any other local governing agency having jurisdiction over the installation.

24.9 The inner pipe of jacketed piping is required to be pressure tested before the jacket is placed. Pressure testing of other fabricated spools is not required in the fabrication shop unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order.

24.10 Unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order, pressure testing of installed fabricated spools will be accomplished in the field after final assembly, but before insulating the joints and prior to any chemical cleaning that may be required (see paragraph 26.2 for painting of welded joint prior to hydrotest). Any weld that leaks during this pressure test will be repaired at fabricator's expense.

24.11 Fabricated pipe spools that have been pressure-tested in the fabrication shop and accepted by BP CHP need not be pressure-tested in the field.

## SECTION 25: DIMENSIONAL INSPECTION

25.1 The fabricator shall dimensionally inspect each spool piece before shipment.

25.2 The release of pipe spools by BP CHP's Inspector shall not relieve the fabricator of his responsibility to furnish spools to the required dimensions.

25.3 Pipe spools that are discovered to be out of tolerance in the shop or after arrival in the field, will be repaired at the fabricator's expense.
