23.3 BP CHP Inspection shall be notified at least 5 days prior to beginning PMI work, to allow the inspector to designate any PMI analyses that will be witnessed. All PMI analysis results will be verified by BP CHP or their alternate to confirm that all required PMI inspections have been performed.

23.4 100% of piping components in the following alloys shall be PMI analyzed:

1¼Cr-½Mo
2¼Cr-1Mo
5Cr-½Mo
9Cr-1Mo
400 Series Stainless Steel
Duplex Stainless Steel
Exotic Alloys (20, C, C276, 825, Monel, & similar)

23.5 The results for all PMI analyses shall be provided to BP CHP Inspection.

23.6 For piping components in 300 Series stainless steel, PMI analysis as specified below shall be performed on completed pipe spools prior to any required PWHT.

23.6.1 At least 10% of each type of fitting (elbows, tees, reducers, pipes, caps, couplings, crosses, plugs, etc.) per heat lot, and 10% of each type of flange (socketweld, buttweld, slip-on, blind flange, etc.) per heat lot shall be PMI analyzed. 100% of fittings and flanges which cannot be identified by heat lot shall be PMI analyzed.

23.6.2 At least 10% of the completed pipe spools for each pipe diameter size shall be PMI analyzed on a straight section of pipe.

23.6.3 Selection of piping components to be PMI analyzed shall be made by BP CHP or their designated representative. The selection shall be evenly distributed among the completed spools.

23.7 PMI Analysis Procedures

23.7.1 The instruments or methods used for PMI analysis shall have the capability of positively identifying and providing a quantitative measurement of the alloy elements specified in section 23.8.

23.7.2 For non-destructive verification, use a portable x-ray emission analyzer or other equipment approved by BP CHP Inspection.

23.7.3 When the portable analyzer or other approved equipment is unable to identify the material of a piping component, that component shall be analyzed by a laboratory method approved by BP CHP.
