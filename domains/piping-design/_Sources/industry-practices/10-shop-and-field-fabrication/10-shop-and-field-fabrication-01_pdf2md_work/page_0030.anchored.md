# APPENDIX A: FIELD WELDING & PWHT OF SOCKETWELD CARBON STEEL VALVES

## A1 SCOPE

This Appendix defines the welding, postweld heat treatment, examination, and testing requirements of socketwelds and seal welds made on NPS 1.5 and smaller carbon steel (P-1) valves.

## A2 WELDING

A2.1 All welding shall conform to the requirements of STP 42.

A2.2 Proper fit-up of the socket joint is mandatory. A 1/16" minimum gap shall be maintained during tack welding.

A2.3 Shielded Metal Arc Welding (stick) process shall be used.

A2.4 All socketwelds and seal welds shall be made using a filler material of the same analysis as the base material being welded.

A2.5 A low hydrogen type electrode with a maximum carbon content of 0.05 percent is preferred, e.g., E7018, etc.

## A3 PREHEAT AND INTERPASS TEMPERATURE

A3.1 Preheat pipe and valve body to the requirements of ASME B31.3 and STP 42.

A3.2 Do not apply heat directly to upper portion (bonnet) of valve so as not to damage gasket and packing.

A3.3 Preheat shall be applied prior to tack welding.

A3.4 Interpass temperature shall be maintained within the range required by ASME B31.3 and STP 42. Temperature indicating crayons, or other suitable means, shall be used to assure that the preheat and interpass temperatures are maintained
