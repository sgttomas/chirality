28.1.6 Pipe nipples and other slender branches not permanently braced, shall be temporarily braced and blocked to prevent damage during shipment and handling.

## 28.2 MATERIAL SAFETY DATA SHEET (MSDS)

28.2.1 A Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) is required for each chemical or other OSHA HCR regulated material received at BP CHP facilities.

28.2.2 Equipment or materials that contain or are coated with any regulated substances, such as the following, shall be prominently tagged at openings to indicate nature of contents and precautions for shipping, storage, and handling:

a) Insulating oils.

b) Corrosion inhibitors.

c) Antifreeze solutions.

d) Desiccants.

e) Chemical substances.

f) Hydrocarbon substances.

28.2.3 Federal OSHA HCR (Occupational Safety and Health Administration's Hazards Communication Rule) regulated substances shall have a current Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS).

28.2.4 MSDSs shall fully comply with Federal/State OSHA HCR regulations for MSDS preparation and shall include a statement that the substance is considered hazardous by regulation.

28.2.5 If any substance is exempt from regulation, a statement to that effect shall be included.

28.2.6 Before shipment, MSDSs shall be forwarded to BP CHP.

28.2.7 At shipment, MSDSs in protective envelopes shall be affixed to the outside of the shipment.

## SECTION 29: FIELD HANDLING AND STORAGE

29.1 Piping materials shall be stored at the job site in a manner to avoid contact and exposure to dirt, debris, etc.

29.2 Before installing prefabricated piping, it shall be examined to ensure that all foreign matter and debris have been removed.
