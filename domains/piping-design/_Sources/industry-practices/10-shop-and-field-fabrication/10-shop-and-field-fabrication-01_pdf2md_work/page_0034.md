## APPENDIX B: FIELD INSPECTION PROCEDURES AND RECORDS

### B1 FIELD INSPECTION PROCEDURES

**B1.1** The field erection Contractor shall provide an inspection report form on which the lines are listed, identified, and signed off as complete.

**B1.2** Each line shall have a number of check-off items to serve as a check list toward a full and complete inspection. This guide covers the minimum requirements for pipe completion. The inspection shall not necessarily conclude with the check-off list. Any other discrepancy, apparent or otherwise, shall be so noted and brought to the attention of the BP CHP Project Engineer.

**B1.3** At least one signed copy of the test sheet shall be retained by the BP CHP Inspector, and at least one signed copy shall be delivered to BP CHP's Project Engineer. Both the Contractor's and BP CHP's Inspector shall check off each item and sign off each line.

**B1.4** When the Contractor is satisfied that a line is ready for pressure testing, he shall notify BP CHP's Inspector. They shall then inspect the line together. The subject lines shall actually be checked out against the drawings as inspection progresses. All of the necessary data shall be checked on the report form. It shall be the Contractor's responsibility to obtain the signatures from BP CHP's Inspector for all check-off items and for his final signature subsequent to testing and reassembly.

### B2 REPORT FORMS

Report forms shall use the format as outlined in the Major Projects QA/QC Manual, specific EWR, or other format. The type of reporting form required should be discussed and developed with the BP Project QA/QC representative or the BP Inspector.
