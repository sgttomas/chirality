14.3 Unless otherwise shown or approved, field welds shall be buttweld or socketweld type. Stub-in or miter weld joints are not allowed.

14.4 Unless otherwise specified, end connections of shop fabricated spools shall be made as follows.

- 14.4.1 Where field welding is required to join the ends of a fabricated pipe spool to pipe or piping components, the pipe spool shall be furnished with the ends beveled for field welding and fabricated to the drawing dimensions.

- 14.4.2 Where field fit-up is required, the end of one of the spool pieces at the fit-up field weld shall be furnished with a plain end 4 inches longer than the dimension indicated on the piping isometric drawings. The adjacent spool piece shall have a beveled end, and shall be fabricated to the dimension indicated.

- 14.4.3 Austenitic stainless steel pipe shall be beveled using mechanical devices unless otherwise approved by the BP CHP Reliability Engineer. Flame cutting may be acceptable, subject to prior approval specified above.

- 14.4.4 Seal welding of threaded fittings shall be minimized and generally limited to pipe plugs in vents and bleeds used for initial hydrostatic testing. Threaded joints which are specified to be seal welded shall be assembled without teflon tape, liquid teflon, or other thread compounds. No threads shall be left exposed but shall be covered with weld metal. See standard drawing LA-000-0100-0200-017.

14.5 For tie-in welds to existing piping in alkaline services, such as caustic, catacarb, and amine, the existing piping to be welded shall be neutralized prior to welding per STP 42.

## SECTION 15: SHIPPING LENGTHS

Pipe spool dimensions shall not exceed 40 feet by 8 feet by 8 feet to facilitate shipping.
