## APPENDIX C: FIELD INSTALLATION OF VALVES AND OTHER PIPING COMPONENTS

### C1 GENERAL VALVE INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS

C1.1 Visually inspect the valve for damage such as bent shafts, broken handle or handwheel, and damaged flange faces. Inspect the inside of the valve and remove any foreign material.

C1.2 Inspect the valve stems for any stem protection during painting at the factory or for shipping and remove it. If the stem was inadvertently painted, it shall be cleaned before installing the valve.

C1.3 Some valves such as globe valves, check valves, butterfly valves, Orbit valves, and metal seated ball valves, have directional flow arrows. These valves shall be installed accordingly. Disregarding this will result in valve failure and leakage.

### C2 SOFT SEATED VALVES

C2.1 Where soft seated valves will be installed, piping shall be thoroughly cleaned, flushed, and drained before the valves are installed. Debris in the line (i.e., metal chips, weld rod, weld slug, grit, sand) can damage the soft seats. Soft seated valves that had been welded in the line (vents, drains, in-line) shall be in the wide open position during the flushing operation. The hydrotest for these lines may be conducted at this time, and the flanged soft seated valve installed after the hydrotest.

### C3 BUTTERFLY VALVES

C3.1 Ensure that the interior of mating pipe and flanges are free of any obstructions such as weld rods and weld slugs before installing the valve. When the valve flange facing is lined with a resilient material, the mating pipe flange shall be separated sufficiently so as not to damage the resilient facing. Do not force or wedge the valve into position.

C3.2 Rubber lined butterfly valves shall be installed with the disc in the open position prior to tightening the bolts. After the bolts are tight, check the operation of the valve through a complete open and close cycle to ensure that the disc has not been jammed during the installation.
