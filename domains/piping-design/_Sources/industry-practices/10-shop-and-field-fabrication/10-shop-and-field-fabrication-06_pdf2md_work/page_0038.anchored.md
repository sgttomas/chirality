- **Gases:** Gaseous products are effective heat removers only if under relatively high pressures (1000 psi) and if they are flowing at significant velocities (10 fps)

*Note   Recent research has shown that the arbitrary minimum and maximum flow rates previously specified by API 2201 (and included in some OPCO hot tapping instructions) are not necessary.*

#### Preheating

Preheating of all hot tap and repair welds is desirable but typically cannot be done. Unfortunately, a line with flowing product represents a very large heat sink. Preheat will be most effectively maintained by electrical resistance heating, but the required heat input may be very large. Experience has shown it difficult, if not impossible, to maintain preheat on liquid or high velocity gas lines.

When possible, carbon steels (P1) should be preheated to a minimum of 200°F. Preheat is best measured using a temperature indicating crayon.

## 674 Safety Considerations

### General Safety Considerations

The general provisions of the Corporation *Fire Protection Manual* should be followed. Specifically, standby fire extinguishers and/or water should be provided in the work area. All personnel concerned with use or operation of the lines or equipment being hot tapped must be advised of the proposed job plan. Use of a hot work permit and an accident prevention tagging system is recommended. In this procedure, the supervisor in charge of the work will tag all valves or blinds controlling flow through lines in the work area, and will remove his tags only after completion of the job.

### Special Precautions for Liquids Above Flash Point

The following special precautions should be followed to avoid a fire hazard when draining the tapping machine after a tap into a line containing a liquid at a temperature above its flash point.

- If the hot tap is on the bottom of a line or horizontal, install stainless steel tubing to the tapping machine drain connection. The tubing will be used to drain and cool the hot stock from the tapping machine. Route the tubing through a container of water or provide sufficient length to cool the liquid below its flash point
- If the hot tap is made into the top of a line, the hot liquid held in the space above the gate valve wedge and below the bleeder of the tapping machine
