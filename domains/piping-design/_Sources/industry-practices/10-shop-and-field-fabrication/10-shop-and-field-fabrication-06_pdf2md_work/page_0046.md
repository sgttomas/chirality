**Verify the following before cutting:**

\_\_\_\_\_ 1. Welds have been nondestructively examined.

\_\_\_\_\_ 2. Hot tap valve, packing, gasket, and bolts have been checked for possible leakage.

\_\_\_\_\_ 3. If the operating code requires that the hot tap connection be hydrotested, the system has been checked for the proposed hydrotest pressure. The hydrotest pressure should not exceed the collapse pressure of the main run, the flange rating, or the rupture pressure of the reinforcement sleeve.

\_\_\_\_\_ 4. The packing or seals on the hot tap machine have been checked and are suitable for the temperature and gas or liquid in the line.

\_\_\_\_\_ 5. The bleed-off valve has been checked to be sure it has the correct pressure rating, is operable, and is not obstructed.

\_\_\_\_\_ 6. All bolts on the cutter and pilot bit are tight.

\_\_\_\_\_ 7. The coupon catcher is on the pilot bit.

\_\_\_\_\_ 8. The valve is centered on the flange.

\_\_\_\_\_ 9. The cutting depth has been calculated to avoid cutting the opposite side of the pipe.

\_\_\_\_\_ 10. The boring bar has been run through the valve to ensure free passage.

\_\_\_\_\_ 11. The cutter can be fully retracted so that the hot tap valve can be closed.

**Verify the following before removing the hot tap machine:**

\_\_\_\_\_ 1. The manufacturer's instructions have been followed to be sure the boring bar is fully retracted before the hot tap valve is closed.

\_\_\_\_\_ 2. The hot tap valve has been closed.

\_\_\_\_\_ 3. The stock is below its flash point.

\_\_\_\_\_ 4. The bleeder valve has been opened.

\_\_\_\_\_ 5. All pressure has been bled off the hot tap machine before removal of bolts from the flange.

**Verify the following before returning the line to service:**

\_\_\_\_\_ 1. The line stock has been verified.

\_\_\_\_\_ 2. The flow and pressure have been returned to normal.

\_\_\_\_\_ 3. All valves that were closed, tagged, etc., have been returned to service and tags removed.
