1. Install an additional ⅝-inch minimum bleeder valve on the tapping machine adapting stub as near to the gate valve end of the machine as is practical.

2. Install a valve on the top vent connection at the head crank end of the tapping machine. Install stainless steel tubing at the vent connection. Route the tubing through a container of water, or provide sufficient length to cool the liquid below its flash point.

3. Install the hot tapping machine on the gate valve, pressure test, and tap.

4. Retract boring bar, close gate valve, open top vent, and sample stock from the tubing.

5. Pump glycerin into the bottom bleeder. Glycerin has a higher specific gravity than most petroleum fractions and will displace the hot stock above the valve wedge upward and out the top vent connection. Pump at approximately 1 gpm for long enough to ensure that the hot stock has been pumped out.

6. Drain the glycerin through the bottom bleeder and remove the machine.

## 675 Procedure and Equipment

### Selecting the Hot Tap Location

The line location for the hot tap should not be near a longitudinal or circumferential weld seam. Several pipe diameters' spacing should be maintained between the hot tap and existing welds. A hot tap should also not be made in the area of a bend. The proposed hot tap location should be thoroughly inspected for actual wall thickness and out-of-roundness before proceeding. Avoid taps on the bottoms of lines; cuttings will fall toward the packing gland and drive shaft of the tapping machine, creating the possibility of damage or jamming.

### Welded Attachments

Hot tap connections should be designed for internal pressure and external loads (e.g., the weight of the valve and the hot tap machine) using the area replacement method in accordance with the appropriate specification (e.g., ASME, ASME/ANSI B31.3, B31.4, B31.8, etc.).

For plant piping, the branch reinforcement may be selected from the piping class. Note that the branch reinforcement tables in the Corporation Piping Specification only consider the internal pressure; additional reinforcement may be required for the external loads.

Figure 600-9 specifies the recommended welding details for hot tap connections.
