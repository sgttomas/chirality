## Inspection

Prior to hot tapping, the line thickness must be checked using an ultrasonic thickness gauge. Inspection of the completed hot tap connection welds is recommended. Visual inspection should be the minimum requirement. For ambient temperature lines, the welds may be examined by either the wet magnetic particle or dye penetrant methods. For higher temperature hot taps, dry magnetic particle inspection can be done. For critical hot taps, ultrasonic examination can be done on the full penetration welds. The standard hot tapping details typically cannot be inspected by radiography.

## Mechanical Attachments

Special saddle attachments are available (Plidco or equal) that permit a flange or coupling to be fastened to the line without welding. The connection is made by bolting and gasketing. These fittings are limited in size and pressure capability, and the chemical resistance of the packing and seals must be suitable for the proposed service. The manufacturer's product literature or representative should be consulted to determine suitability for a specific application.
