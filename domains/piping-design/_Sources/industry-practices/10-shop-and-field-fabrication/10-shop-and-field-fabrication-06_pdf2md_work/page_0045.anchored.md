## Verify the following before starting the hot tap:

____ 1. If required, the properly approved work permit has been obtained.

____ 2. If required, the hot work permit has been obtained.

____ 3. If required, the properly approved fire permit has been obtained.

____ 4. Combustible and toxic gas tests have been conducted in the area of hot tapping.

____ 5. The exact location of the hot tap on the line or vessel has been identified and marked, and the locations of the nearest block valves determined.

____ 6. For tanks and vessels, the hot tap location is located below the liquid level.

____ 7. The area to be welded has been inspected for thickness and freedom from laminations, hydrogen attack, and other metallurgical imperfections.

____ 8. The branch connection and welding electrode have been selected to match the metallurgy of the line or vessel.

____ 9. The flanges, bolts, gaskets, pipe, and valves to be installed meet the pipeline, piping, tank or pressure vessel code for the line or vessel to be hot tapped.

____ 10. If the contents are flammable, fire-fighting equipment has been provided.

____ 11. If required, suitable protective equipment has been provided to all exposed personnel in the hot tapping area.

____ 12. Sufficient external clearance exists to accommodate the operation of the proposed hot tap machine.

____ 13. The hot tap fitting is of the proper length to accommodate the operation of the hot tap machine.

____  a. The tapping machine has adequate cutter travel for this job.

____  b. Sufficient internal clearance exists to retract the cutter through the valve.

____ 14. The hot tap valve is repacked and the valve guides are ground off to permit free passage of the cutter.

## Verify the following before welding:

____ 1. Weld area has been preheated, if possible.

____ 2. An approved welding procedure exists, qualified in accordance with the appropriate code, and the procedure uses low hydrogen electrodes.
