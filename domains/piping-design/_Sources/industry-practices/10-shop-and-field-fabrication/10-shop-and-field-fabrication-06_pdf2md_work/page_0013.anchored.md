Stress relief is also needed for all caustic services above 140°F, and for caustic services above 110°F where caustic concentration exceeds 30 wt%.

**External Stress Corrosion Cracking**

Even when internal stress corrosion cracking is not a concern, PWHT may be required for 300-series stainless steels due to the potential for external cracking. If service temperatures are above 200°F, PWHT should be done to eliminate external cracking when:

- saltwater is used for firewater or plant washdown water, **or**
- the piping is in a coastal location and the consequences of a leak threaten personnel safety or plant reliability, **or**
- the piping is in a costal location and long lead times for delivery and high costs are likely (e.g., large diameter, thick-wall, "special order" material, etc.).

# 630 Piping Fabrication

## 631 Fabrication of Piping Spools

Information required for fabrication of piping spools includes:

- Piping design and materials, as defined by isometric drawings (often CAD-produced) and Piping Class Sheets (see the Corporation Piping Specification in Volume 2 of this manual). The fabricator or contractor may choose to make individual spool drawings in his format and system
- Fabrication specifications appropriate for the material category:
    - PIM-MS-2505 for carbon steel piping
    - PIM-MS-4770 for stainless steel piping
    - PIM-MS-4772 for alloy steel piping

    See Volume II of this manual for guidelines on the use of these specifications.

    The purchase order list sheets or supplemental specifications, or the plant construction specifications may give particular requirements for a project that modify or supplement the standard specifications. Also, when appropriate, include specifications for radiographic inspection and painting.
