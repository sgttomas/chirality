Colors and methods of identifying the service of particular systems should conform to standards of the operating organization; see Section 270 of the *Coatings Manual*, and the *Safety in Designs Manual*.

After insulating and painting, lines should be clearly and permanently identified and marked with flow direction arrows. Identification of auxiliary piping at machinery should not be overlooked. The plan and method of identifying should comply with the operating organization's practice and preference; details may vary from location to location. In some cases, plant operators will apply or install the identification markings in conjunction with their line checkout and plant familiarization program.

## 660 Descaling, Cleaning, and Pickling

### 661 What These Terms Mean

The terms descaling, cleaning, and pickling refer to procedures for removing oil, rust and mill scale from the interior of piping.

### 662 When Cleaning Is Necessary

Most piping contains internal oil, rust, weld spatter, and mill scale when it is first placed in service. Cleaning prior to startup generally involves nothing more than liquid circulation and flushing to remove loose debris. However, there are services in which this degree of cleanliness is inadequate. Lube and seal oil systems for machinery, and compressor suction lines, for example, must be scrupulously clean to protect downstream equipment from damage by solid particles. There are also occasional fluid services where product purity requirements demand more than the usual cleanliness.

Older lines may also require periodic cleaning to reduce excessive pressure drop resulting from scale accumulation during service. Examples are closed cooling systems with untreated makeup water, and boiler feedwater piping. This work is often deferred until the entire system needs cleaning.

### 663 How Cleaning Is Accomplished

By far the most common technique for internally cleaning piping is chemical cleaning. Abrasive blasting is also used occasionally. Another method is called vapor phase descaling, a technique in which the Company has little experience.

Chemical cleaning dissolves the rust via either an acid solution or a chelating agent. In either case, a corrosion inhibitor must be added to the solution to minimize corrosion of the metal being cleaned.
