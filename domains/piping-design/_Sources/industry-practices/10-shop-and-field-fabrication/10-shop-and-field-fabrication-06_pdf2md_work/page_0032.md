## 670 Hot Tapping

A hot tap involves welding a branch line or fitting onto a pressurized main line (piping, pipeline, tank, vessel, etc.) and then using specialized equipment to cut into the main line (see Figure 600-7). This section discusses the considerations, precautions, procedures and equipment necessary for making a hot tap safely.

**Fig. 600-7** Hot Tap Machine Arrangement

[FIGURE: Fig. 600-7 Hot Tap Machine Arrangement]

Most hot taps are made on carbon steel equipment, and this section emphasizes carbon steel hot taps. Other materials are discussed in Section 677.

Welding the branch connection on the line is typically a Company activity. Making the actual hot tap is often done by a specialist contractor, although Company personnel also do this work in some operating locations.
