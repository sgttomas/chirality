**Fig. 600-4** Decision Tree for Stress Relief of 300 Series Stainless Steel Piping

The decision tree flows from **Start** through the following sequence of decision nodes, with **Concerns** listed to the right of each branch:

1. **Is intergranular corrosion of greater concern than SCC?**
   - Yes → Stress Relief Not Recommended — *Sensitization*
   - No ↓

2. **Type 304, 316, or 317? (Unstabilized or non-L-grade)**
   - Yes → Stress Relief Not Recommended — *Sensitization*
   - No ↓

3. **Operating Temp below 140°F?**
   - Yes → **> 30% Caustic and T > 110°F?**
     - No → Stress Relief Not Recommended — *Internal Caustic SCC*
     - Yes ↓
   - No ↓

4. **Known Stress Corrosion Cracking Service?**
   - Yes → Stress Relieve — *Internal SCC*
   - No ↓

5. **High Pressure Hydrogen Service? (H2 pp Above 100 psi)**
   - Yes → Stress Relieve — *Leak Consequences*
   - No ↓

6. **Operating Temperature Below 200°F?**
   - Yes → Stress Relief Not Recommended — *External SCC*
   - No ↓

7. **Coastal Location?**
   - Yes → Stress Relieve if: 1. Leak threatens personnel safety or plant reliability, or 2. Long lead times for delivery and cost of piping — *External SCC*
   - No ↓

8. **Stress Relief Not Recommended**
