# 610 General Considerations

## 611 Piping Systems

Sections 610 through 660 discuss welding, fabrication, testing, cleaning and special features of the assembly of piping systems. Certain elements of these systems are covered in other manuals:

- For welding information, see the *Welding Manual*
- For pipe supports, see the *Civil and Structural Manual*, Section 300
- For steam traps and heat tracing, see the *Utilities Manual*
- For insulation of hot and cold lines, see the *Insulation and Refractory Manual*, Sections 100 and 200
- For painting, see the *Coatings Manual*, Section 200, Section 300, and Specification COM-MS-4743
- For installation and flushing of piping at compressors, see the *Compressor Manual*, Section 700
- For auxiliary piping for mechanical seals on centrifugal pumps, see the *Pump Manual*, Section 700
- For instrumentation piping, see the *Instrumentation and Control Manual*
- For cathodic protection of buried piping, see the *Corrosion Prevention Manual*

Construction engineers and inspectors should refer to Section 200 of this manual for piping components. See also the tabbed section, Safety Instruction Sheets, in Volume 2 of the manual.

Chapter V of the Chemical Plant and Petroleum Refinery Piping Code, ASME/ANSI B31.3, covers fabrication, assembly and erection of piping. Code B31.3 should be available as a reference to construction engineers and inspectors.

## 612 Shop Fabrication

Economy and consistent quality are achieved by shop fabrication of piping into subassembly units called spools which are then transported to the jobsite, erected, and joined. The fabrication shop may be a permanent facility operated by a piping fabrication company and equipped for fabrication, quality control inspection and examination, and testing of pipe spools. Such shops usually have a wide range of qualified welding procedures and a core of regularly employed production and quality control personnel.

Shop fabrication allows better control of quality and project schedules by minimizing problems owing to bad weather, labor unavailability, and shipping delays.
