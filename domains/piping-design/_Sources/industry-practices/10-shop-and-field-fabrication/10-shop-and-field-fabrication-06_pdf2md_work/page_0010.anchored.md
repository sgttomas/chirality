(1) Postweld heat treat on-plot vessels. On tanks, postweld heat treat the coils only.
(2) "Large piping" is butt-welded 2" & larger. "Small piping" is socket or seal welded 1½" & smaller.
(3) "Hot caustic" is over 140°F for concentrations below 30% (wt.), and over 120°F for concentrations above 30%.
(4) PWHT all main fractionator overhead piping at 1150°F – 1250°F. Furnace PWHT is preferred. Consult M&E Engineering if local PWHT is being considered.
(5) For other services, such as HP or nitrate, see the *Corrosion Prevention Manual* for specific recommendations regarding PWHT.
(6) Carbon steel pipe in wall thicknesses over ¾ inch shall be postweld heat treated.
(7) The Company's EG specifications are more restrictive than codes.
