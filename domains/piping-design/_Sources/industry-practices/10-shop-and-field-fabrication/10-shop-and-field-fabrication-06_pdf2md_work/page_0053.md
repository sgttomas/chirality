## Emergency Pipe Repair Clamps

Small leaks, especially in less hazardous services such as steam, can sometimes be temporarily controlled with a pipe clamp. Figure 600-15 shows a simple clamp.

Clamps are installed with A307 machine bolts or A193 stub bolts. Clamps up to and including 4-inch NPS require two bolts; large clamps require four bolts. A gasket must be used to contain the fluid. Use a composition sheet gasket, neoprene rubber or other material compatible with the fluid.

These clamps have no maximum pressure rating. They can, however, exert enough force on a section of corroded pipe to cause a general failure. Be certain the pipe wall is strong enough to withstand the clamping forces.
