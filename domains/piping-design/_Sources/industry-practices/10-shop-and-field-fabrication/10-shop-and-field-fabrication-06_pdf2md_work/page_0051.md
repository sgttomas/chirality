Extended storage can also affect the bending properties of fusion bonded epoxy. Chalking of the fusion bond epoxy is not detrimental.

# 690 Safety

## 691 Construction, Commissioning and Repair Procedures

To ensure the safety of personnel and operating facilities, installation, repair, testing, flushing and commissioning of piping require strict conformance to safe practices and procedures. The following activities and others may be hazardous:

- Erection and rigging. Properly select and control erection equipment, and properly use scaffolds. See the *Safety in Designs Manual*

- Torch cutting, welding, and postweld heating (stress relieving). Prevent ignition and combustion of flammables

- Radiographic inspection. Protect personnel in the area from exposure to radiation

- Hydrostatic and pneumatic testing. Protect personnel and equipment against failure of piping and equipment under test and unforeseen high-energy release of line contents. See Section 640 and Model Specification PIM-MS-3541

- Flushing, pre-commissioning and commissioning. Protect personnel and facilities from exposure to hot, corrosive, toxic, or high-pressure fluids

- Tie-ins and repairs on existing piping and equipment. Prevent ignition of flammables, exposure to toxic or corrosive fluids, loss of product, etc., and protect personnel and facilities from these hazards. Before cutting into an existing line or unbolting flanged joints or valve bonnets, ensure that the line is depressured at that point

## 692 Safety Checklists (Work Permits)

Company operating organizations normally have an established and systematic procedure for ensuring safety while working in or on existing operating facilities. The procedure typically includes checklists covering site conditions, work methods, potential hazards, and notifications to other plant organizations that may be affected by the work.

If the work involves risk of ignition and fire, the checklist is called a hot work permit. It is appropriately initialled and posted at the work site. Valves, blinds, machinery motors, etc., are tagged in "safe" positions until work is completed and the tags have been removed by the responsible personnel involved. As a minimum, Operations, Maintenance, and Fire and Safety must sign for hot work and tie-ins. (See Figures 600-13 and 600-14.)
