## 694 Emergency Pipe Repairs

Leaks that occur in lines critical to the operation of a plant sometimes must be controlled without shutting down the plant. Several methods are available for controlling leaks. All are temporary measures at best, and permanent repairs must be made as soon as possible.

### Pipe Repair Fittings

Several suppliers, such as the Pipeline Development Company (Plidco), can provide specialized pipe repair fittings. These fittings are either off-the-shelf or specially engineered. In either case they have a specific pressure rating and blowout-proof seal designs, and they can be made into permanent repairs by external welding. Plidco-type sleeves are recommended over pipe repair clamps (discussed below).

The most commonly used Plidco fittings are the "Weld + Ends" and the "Split + Sleeve". The former requires a depressured and cut pipe. The latter can be installed on an operating pipe as a seal welded after flow is resumed. For more information on these and similar fittings, see Section 300 of the *Pipeline Manual*.
