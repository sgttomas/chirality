# 680 Storage and Handling

## 681 Identification and Segregation

### Marking and Identification

Typically the pipe manufacturer stencils the grade, diameter, wall thickness, heat number, pipe number, manufacturer, etc., on the pipe. For 10-inch and larger pipes the marks are usually painted on the inside of the pipe. For 8-inch and smaller they are on the outside.

The pipe can be color-coded to distinguish the grade and/or wall thickness.

Valves and fittings should be tagged with the purchase order number, size, ANSI class, item number, and manufacturer.

### Segregation

Pipe of different lengths, grades, outside diameters or wall thicknesses should be racked separately. Adequate access to individual racks should be provided.

## 682 Storage of Valves and Fittings During Construction

Valves and fittings should be segregated by size, class, etc. They should be stored on pallets or a slab to keep them out of mud and water. In wet or cold climates, a storage tent may be appropriate.
