#### 3.4.9 Bolting

Each stud bolt shall be dapped with a spot of paint on each end.

#### 3.4.10 Special notes

Paint shall not be applied, in any circumstances, to the following:

- inside surfaces of pipe, flanges or fittings;
- threads;
- weld bevels;
- symbols or markings which would obliterate identification;
- gasket seating surface.

### 3.5 MATERIAL TRACEABILITY

Material certification, colour coding, intake inspection, storage and material handout shall be strictly controlled to exclude rogue materials from equipment and plant.

The Vendor shall organise its manufacturing process so that material traceability is ensured, in which case positive alloy material identification after material assembly is not required.

### 3.6 POSITIVE MATERIAL IDENTIFICATION (PMI)
