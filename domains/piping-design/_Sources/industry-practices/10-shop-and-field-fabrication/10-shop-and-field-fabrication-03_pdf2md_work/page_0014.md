An approved third party contracted by the Vendor shall carry out PMI. An action plan for PMI testing shall be submitted by the Vendor for the approval of the Principal.

Any portable X-ray emission analyser, such as Texas Nuclear analyser Model 9266 or equivalent with computerised read-out, is acceptable.

The Contractor shall ensure that auditable measures of quality assurance are in place to verify that welds are made with the consumables specified on the WPS, in particular for the root pass of austenitic stainless steel welds. Also, these measures shall ensure that the degree of oxidation which occurs at the weldments in stainless steel, whether applied as solid material or as cladding, is acceptable in accordance with the criteria given in of DEP 30.10.60.31-Gen., Appendix 1.

## 3.7 PROCEDURE FOR CONCESSION REQUEST / MATERIAL SUBSTITUTION

If the Vendor intends to deviate from the originally specified requirements, it shall provide in its written proposal to the Contractor a detailed list of the items so proposed with a copy of a full description, along with all the particulars of intended deviations and the underlying reasons. All deviations shall be subject to the approval of the Principal.

DEP 30.10.02.11-Gen. may be used in considering material substitutions.
