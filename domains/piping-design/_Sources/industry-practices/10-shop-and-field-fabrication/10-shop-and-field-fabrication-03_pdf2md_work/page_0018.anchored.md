Hardness measurements shall be carried out after PWHT (if any).

## 5. PRESERVATION

Painting shall comply with DEP 30.48.00.31-Gen. Painting requirements should be shown on the line list; see DEP 31.38.01.92-Gen.

Pre-fabricated pipe spools shall be properly protected against corrosion and damage during storage and transport to site. Flanges shall be fitted with flange protectors, including a proven protection against corrosion for the flange face. Plain and bevelled pipe ends shall be provided with plastic covers.

All stainless steel weldments required by the Principal (or the Principal's inspectors) to be pickled and passivated shall be carried out in accordance with DEP 30.10.60.31-Gen.
