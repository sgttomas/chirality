1.6 SUMMARY OF CHANGES SINCE PREVIOUS EDITION

The previous edition of this DEP was dated March 1994. Other than editorial changes, the following are the main changes since that edition.

The prescriptive requirements for high integrity protective systems have been replaced by cross-references to newly available DEPs that specify requirements for IPFs. DEP 32.80.10.10-Gen. provides the requirements for the classification and implementation of IPFs, including risk assessment and determination of the criticality and the required level of reliability and redundancy. DEP 32.80.10.13-Gen. provides requirements and recommendations for the management of IPFs including optimisation of maintenance and inspection requirements to ensure long-term integrity.

Some definitions have been revised to align with those in ISO 13623.

In (2.4), limitations to the MAIP required for certain pipelines designed in accordance with ISO 13623 or ASME B 31.8 have been added.
