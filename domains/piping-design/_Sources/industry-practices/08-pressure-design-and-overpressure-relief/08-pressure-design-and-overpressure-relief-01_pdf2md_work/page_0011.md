5.3.7 Piping

Piping with exception to piping protected by connected protected vessels shall be protected by either mechanical relief or by mechanical design. Maximum Upset Operating Pressure as determined above shall be utilized for piping design.

## 5.4 Selection of Piping Line Class and LDT Completion

Piping components shall be selected from one Suncor Standard line class between line breaks as designated on the P&IDs. The line class selected shall have design pressure and design temperature exceeding the Maximum Upset Operating Pressure for the piping under consideration. Design pressure on the LDT may be designated as any value equal or exceeding the Maximum Upset Operating. The LDT design pressure shall be equal or less than the line class MDWP. Line class components are designed to meet or exceed the pressure rating as defined in the line class. Wall thicknesses for piping and fittings designated as "Calc" shall be determined by the engineer to satisfy the appropriate piping design code (e.g. B31.1) utilizing the design pressure as designated on the LDT and the line class corrosion allowance. Design temperature shall be the coincident temperature utilized to derive the design pressure.

Testing pressure shall be established according to Standard 0205. In general, hydrotest pressure to be defined on the LTD shall be defined as the maximum pressure that the limiting mechanical component can withstand. The "Code Design test pressure" as designated on the LDT shall be defined as the Design Pressure times the applicable code multiplier. For elevated temperature conditions, Code Design test pressure shall increased to account for differences between allowable stresses at testing temperature and Maximum Upset Operating Temperature.
