### 3.11.5 Leak detection

The requirements for and type of leak detection systems should be derived from an evaluation of the criticality of the pipeline with regards to the consequence of a leak, depending on the transported product, the potential amount released, the sensitivity of the environment and, for onshore pipelines, the location class.

### 3.11.6 Blowdown

Facilities for operational and emergency pipeline depressurisation shall be available at one end of the pipeline and, for onshore pipelines transporting category C and D fluids, at each sectionalising valve location. The capacity of the blowdown system should be such that the pressure in the pipeline can be reduced as rapidly as practicable. For category C and D fluids, the material specified for the blowdown system should be compatible with the low temperatures encountered during blowdown.
