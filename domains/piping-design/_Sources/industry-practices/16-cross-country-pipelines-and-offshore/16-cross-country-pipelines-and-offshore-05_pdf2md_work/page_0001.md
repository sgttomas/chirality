# 700   Onshore Facilities

**Abstract**

This section presents guidelines, tables, drawings, references, and piping practices applicable to onshore producing facilities such as wells, gaging stations, and field compressors.

A brief discussion of internal pressure design is presented for piping used upstream and in operations, such as field process plants, gathering systems, and steam plants. See Section 200 for a more thorough treatment of pressure design.

This section will assist you in:

- Selecting pipe, valves, and fittings for safe onshore producing facilities
- Standardizing on commonly used piping components
- Complying with national codes and standards
