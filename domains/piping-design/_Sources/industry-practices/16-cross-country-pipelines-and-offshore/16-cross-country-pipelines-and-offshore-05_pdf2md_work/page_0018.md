- High pressure piping and components are much heavier. Special care is required to provide adequate support, particularly for valves.

## 737 Compressor and Pump Piping

Piping for rotating machinery must resist vibration and convey products in a streamlined manner.

### Vibration

Failures from vibration occur most often at points of stress concentration such as pipe threads, poor weld penetrations, supports welded to pipe, and rapid changes in wall thickness such as reinforcing saddles and pipe of different wall thickness welded without beveling.

A large unsupported mass on a relatively small pipe stub, such as a relief valve, bleeder valve, high pressure blind flange, or pressure gage, could also cause vibrational failure.

Fatigue failures can be reduced by the following measures:

- Use rotating rather than reciprocating machines.
- Install pulsation dampeners. See Appendix C and the *Compressor Manual*.
- Change the natural frequency of piping with bracing, by changing lengths and wall thicknesses, etc., or change the machinery speed so that the exciting frequency does not match the natural frequency of the piping.
- Hire a consultant to make an analog study of the piping layout and locate possible problem areas. Contact Materials and Equipment Engineering at CRTC for guidance. See also the discussion on vibration studies in the *Compressor Manual*.
- Remove stress concentrations by seal or bridge welding threaded pipe; use smooth transitions (Sweepolets, tees, etc.) instead of reinforcing saddles; and use full penetration welds.
- Reduce the pendulum effect with small, lightweight, close-coupled fittings; use hub-and-clamp connections and stainless steel tubing instead of rigid screwed pipe; use reduced port valves for vents, drains, and pressure taps; and locate branch line valves and gages as close as possible to the header. Note that reduced port valves are more prone to plugging problems than full port valves.
- Use NPS ¾ Schedule 80 pipe as the minimum pipe size.
- Minimize the number of connections.
- Use pipe clamps instead of U-bolts.
