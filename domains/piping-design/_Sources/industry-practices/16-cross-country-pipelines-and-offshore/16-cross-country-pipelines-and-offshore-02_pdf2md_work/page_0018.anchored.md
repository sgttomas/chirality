## 2.7 SPECTACLE BLINDS

Spectacle blinds are not normally required in pig trap systems. However, if required for isolation of the trap from the pipeline the spectacle blinds shall be located on the pig trap side of the pig trap valve, kicker valve, isolation valve on the pressurising line (if fitted) and any vent and drain lines which are connected to other facilities. See EP-95000 for isolation requirements.
