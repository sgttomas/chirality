**Figure 3    Schematic of minimum required pig trap facilities**

**Horizontal launcher**

*(Schematic showing a horizontal pig launcher with: Vent and PI (pressure indicator) connections at the barrel closure end; two globe/isolation valves on the vent line; a kicker line with XI (pig indicator) and additional Vent and globe valves; a main line isolation valve (check valve type); an XI indicator on the main line; a bypass with PI; a drain connection; and a trap barrel of minimum length L from the main line isolation valve to the closure.)*

**Vertical launcher**

*(Schematic showing a vertical pig launcher with: a Vent valve at the top of the barrel; PI connection; the trap barrel oriented vertically with a conical reducer at the bottom; isolation globe valves on the barrel and on the mainline; XI (pig indicator) symbols on the mainline; PI connections; a Drain connection with isolation valve; and dimension L indicating the minimum straight length downstream of the launcher on the main pipeline.)*

**Receiver**

*(Schematic showing a pig receiver with: XI indicator on the main line entering from the left; main line isolation valve; PI connection; Vent connections with globe valves at two locations; XI indicator at the trap inlet; a horizontal trap barrel with minimum length L; an end closure; PI connection at the barrel end; Vent with globe valves; a Drain connection; and bypass/drain isolation valves.)*

L = Minimum length equal to maximum length of pig, usually intelligent pig - see Table 3.
