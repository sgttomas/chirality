## 2.2 BARREL

The barrel is the section of the pig trap, from the pig trap valve up to and including the end closure, which is required to launch and receive pigs.

It shall consist of four parts, as follows:

**End closure** &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; A quick opening closure welded to the major barrel allowing the insertion and removal of pigs.

**Major barrel** &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; An enlarged section of the barrel used for loading or retrieving pigs

**Reducer** &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; A reducer between major and minor barrel

**Minor barrel** &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; A section of the barrel between the pig trap valve and the reducer.

For pipelines smaller than 20 inch diameter the diameter of the major barrel should be 2 inches more than the pipeline diameter. For pipelines with a diameter of 20 inch and larger the oversize should be 4 inches.
