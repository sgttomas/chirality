**Figure 2    Schematic of typical vertical (offshore) pig trap system**

The schematic shows a vertical (offshore) pig trap system including the following labeled components and connections:

- **ISOLATION VALVE** — at the top inlet
- **ADJOINING FACILITY PIG TRAP SYSTEM** — connection at the top right (dashed boundary line)
- **VENT** — connection at the upper launcher area
- **KICKER VALVE** — on the left side branch
- **BALANCE LINE** — vertical line on the left connecting upper and lower sections
- **BYPASS LINE** — outermost left vertical line
- **LAUNCHER** — main vertical vessel body
- **MAJOR BARREL** — upper section of the launcher (annotated with dimension bracket on the right)
- **MINOR BARREL** — lower section of the launcher (annotated with dimension bracket on the right)
- **PI** (Pressure Indicator) — shown at upper and lower positions on the launcher
- **XI** (Pig Signaller) — shown at two positions
- **RELIEF VALVE** — on the right side of the upper barrel
- **TO FLARE/VENT SYSTEM** — outlet connection on the right
- **PURGE VALVE** — in the minor barrel section
- **TO CLOSED DRAIN SYSTEM** — outlet connection on the right of the minor barrel
- **PIG TRAP VALVE** — below the minor barrel
- **MAIN TEE** — connection point at the bottom of the launcher
- **BYPASS VALVE** — on the left bypass line at the main tee level
- **PIG TRAP SYSTEM BOUNDARY LIMIT** — dashed line indicating system boundary
- **PIG TRAP SYSTEM MAIN SECTION OF PIPELINE** — label at the bottom boundary
- **RISER ESD VALVE** — below the boundary limit at the bottom
