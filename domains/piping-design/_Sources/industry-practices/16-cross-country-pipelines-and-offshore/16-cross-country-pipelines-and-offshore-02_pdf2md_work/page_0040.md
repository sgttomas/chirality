**Figure 6    Recommended pig trap dimensions**

1. Launcher

The launcher diagram shows a pig trap assembly with the following labeled dimensions:
- **B_L** — overall launcher barrel length
- **A_L** — distance from kicker line to the barrel end flange
- **X** — working area length (dashed box, from barrel end flange to end wall)
- **Y** — working area height (indicated by arrows on the right side of the dashed box)
- **Kicker line** — indicated with a label at the transition point
- **¢** — centreline symbol shown at mid-height of the working area

2. Receiver

The receiver diagram shows a pig trap assembly with the following labeled dimensions:
- **B_R** — overall receiver barrel length
- **A_R** — distance from kicker line to the barrel end flange
- **X** — working area length (dashed box, from barrel end flange to end wall)
- **Y** — working area height (indicated by arrows on the right side of the dashed box)
- **Kicker line** — indicated with a label at the transition point
- **¢** — centreline symbol shown at mid-height of the working area

**Recommended Values**

- A  =  see values in table 3
- B  =  see values in table 3
- X  =  maximum length of pig plus approx 1 m
