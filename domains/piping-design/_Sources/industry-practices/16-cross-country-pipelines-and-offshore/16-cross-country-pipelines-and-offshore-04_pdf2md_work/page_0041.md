**FIGURE 3    TYPICAL ANCHOR CLAMP**

The figure shows two views of a typical anchor clamp assembly used to connect a riser to a jacket leg.

**Elevation view (top):** Side-by-side depiction of the Jacket leg and the Riser. The riser connection features a circumferential fillet weld top and bottom, and a doubler plate on the outer surface. Dashed centrelines indicate the pipe axes both vertically and horizontally.

- **Jacket leg** — left pipe member shown in elevation
- **Riser** — right pipe member shown in elevation, with callouts:
  - Circumferential fillet weld top and bottom
  - Doubler plate

**Plan view (bottom):** Top-down cross-section showing the jacket sleeve encircling the jacket leg (left circle with internal S-curve indicating the sleeve grip profile) and the riser (right concentric circles). Dashed centrelines intersect at both pipe centres. A callout arrow identifies the **Jacket sleeve**.
