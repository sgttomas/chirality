**FIGURE 6   J-TUBE SEAL PRESSURE**

PRESSURE OF SEAL

$$P_s = (P_j + \rho_j g H_j) - (\rho_s g H_s)$$

WHERE:

- $P_S$ = Differential pressure at seal
- $P_J$ = Gauge pressure at top of J-tube fluid
- $\rho_J$ = Density of fluid in J-tube
- $\rho_S$ = Density of sea water
- $H_J$ = Height of top of J-tube fluid above seal
- $H_S$ = Water depth of seal below mean sea level
