### 1.3.2 Specific definitions

**J-Tube**    J-shaped tube installed on a platform, through which a pipeline can be pulled to form a riser.

**Piping components**    items integrated in the pipeline/riser such as flanges, tees, bends, reducers and valves.

**Riser support**    structure intended for fixing the riser to the platform or for local or continuous guidance of the riser assembly.

**Riser system**    riser pipe, supports, integrated piping components and corrosion prevention system.
