**FIGURE 1    TYPICAL GUIDE CLAMP**

The figure shows two views of a typical guide clamp assembly:

**Top view (elevation/isometric):** Shows the jacket leg and riser in a side-by-side arrangement. The riser clamp is attached to the riser, with the clamp mechanism engaging the jacket leg. Labels indicate:
- Riser clamp
- Jacket leg
- Riser

**Bottom view (plan/cross-section):** Shows the top-down cross-section of the guide clamp, with the jacket sleeve on the left (circular section) and the riser clamp on the right (with hinge pin and bolts). Labels indicate:
- Jacket sleeve
- Hinge pin
- Bolts
