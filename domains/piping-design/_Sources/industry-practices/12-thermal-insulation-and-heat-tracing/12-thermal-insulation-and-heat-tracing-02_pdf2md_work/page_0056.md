## ELEVATION

Elevation view showing vertical piping rising 20° or more with the following details:

- Support to be 3" min. above weld line
- Insulation heavier than 1 1/2" thick
- Metal cover

## COLLAR DETAILS

1/4" Carbon Steel Plate

Plan view cross-sections showing:

- 1/4" clearance on each side
- Size plate 1/2" or 3/4" smaller than insulation thickness
- Tack weld to pipe

**NOTES:**

A. THE INSULATION DETAILS ARE APPLICABLE FOR INSULATION THICKNESS 1 1/2" AND LARGER, AND LINES LONGER THAN 20' VERTICAL LENGTH.

B. PIPE INSULATION SUPPORTS MUST BE COMPATIBLE WITH THE METALLURGY OF THE ALLOY LINES AND MUST BE ADDED TO ALLOY LINES.
