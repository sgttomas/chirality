## 8.3. Hot Vessels and Equipment 601° F Through 1200° F

**8.3.1.** Insulation shall be applied in double layer construction within the limits of commercially available thickness.

**8.3.2.** Insulation for tanks, vessels and equipment over 30" diameter shall be block form. For items 30" diameter and smaller, the insulation shall be blocks, or sectional or segmented pipe insulation. For items with irregular surfaces, the insulation shall be blocks or insulating cement. Insulating and finishing cement shall be composed of mineral fiber pellets and insulating fillers, blended with hydraulic setting binders and corrosion inhibitors. Recommended products: Thermal-1-Kote 1-FW by Pro-Tec-T-Kote or Suncor approved equal.

**8.3.3.** Insulation shall be applied by the broken joint method. For multiple layer construction, the second layer shall be applied so that both circumferential and longitudinal joints are staggered.

**8.3.4.** All joints shall be tightly butted together. Voids shall be filled with broken block insulation or with insulating cement.

**8.3.5.** On multiple layer construction, each layer shall be banded separately.
