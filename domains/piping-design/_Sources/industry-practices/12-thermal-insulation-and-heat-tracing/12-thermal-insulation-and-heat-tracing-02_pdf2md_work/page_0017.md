7.4.9. Insulation securement shall be applied as follows:

7.4.9.1. On the inner layer the tape shall be placed 3" back from all end joints on piping where insulated outside diameter is under 12" and 4 ½" from all end joints on pipe where insulated outside diameter is 12" and over. Tape shall be installed with 100% overlap.

7.4.9.2. On single and outer layer the band spacing shall be 9".

7.4.9.3. Steel bands shall be tightened with a mechanical tightening tool.

7.4.10. Care shall be taken that the single or outer layer of Foamglas is not cracked when secured with stainless steel bands. Cracked or broken Foamglas shall be replaced.

7.4.11. Insulation at fittings shall not be of less thickness than the insulation on adjacent piping. Fitting insulation (covers) shall be held together and applied with sealer (Pittseal 444N or Chil-Pruf CP-22/24). All fitting covers shall be made in sections so as to be readily applied and shall fit with a minimum of void spaces. All insulation on welded fittings shall fit snugly to the contour of the fitting and shall be applied in the same manner and with the same materials as specified for pipe.

7.4.12. Fitting insulation shall be applied with sealer on all joints and each segment banded in place.

7.4.13. Insulation for piping, fittings, valves and flanges shall be finished with Pittsburgh Corning Pittcote 300 or Chil-Pruf CP-22/24 vapour barrier coating. Apply a wet coat of 4-5 gal/100 ft2. Total wet thickness should be 3/32" - 1/8" thick, giving a dry film thickness of 1/16". No fabric membrane is required.

N = Non-Flammable

## 7.5. *Weatherproofing Application*

7.5.1. For general process and offplot areas, the jacketing material shall be aluminum type 3003 or 5005, 0.016" thick, with stucco embossed finish, and with polykraft or poly-surlyn moisture barrier.

For high fire risk process areas or acid or caustic handling areas, the jacketing material shall be 304 stainless steel 0.010" thick, stucco embossed with polykraft or poly-surlyn moisture barrier and will be identified on piping isometric or module drawings.

All jacketing material shall be supplied with factory applied integral flame resistant moisture barrier such as Childers "Polykraft" or Suncor approved equal.

7.5.2. Straight portions of insulated piping shall have the basic insulation covered with metal jacketing, with all joints lapped 2" and arranged on horizontal center line with the lap turned down in order to shed water. All seams shall have a 2" hem on the overlapping edge. Elbows, transitions, etc., shall be installed using slip joint, lockformed or similar method. On horizontal lines, the pipe jacketing shall overlap the fitting covers.

The jacketing shall be secured with stainless steel bands on maximum centres of 12" using a mechanical tightening tool and secured with stainless steel wing seals.
