**Acceptable Insulation Material/Manufacturers:**

For Piping and Vessels:

- Foamglas by Pittsburgh Corning
- Suncor approved equal

The thicknesses in the above table will limit condensation from forming on the outer surface of aluminum jacketing at an ambient temperature of 80°F with a relative humidity of 80% and a wind velocity of 5 MPH with an emittance of 0.3.

Based on the above design parameters the minimum heat gain will be limited to 6.9 btu/hr sq.ft. and the maximum heat gain will be limited to 10.1 btu/hr sq.ft.

All piping and equipment with operating temperatures less than -60°F shall be insulated using double layer insulation configuration with the thicker layer being the inner layer.
