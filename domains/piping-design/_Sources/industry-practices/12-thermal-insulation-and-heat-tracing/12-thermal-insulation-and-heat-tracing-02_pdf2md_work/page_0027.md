## Material Specifications

The pipe lagging materials must meet the following requirements:

*Mineral wool (mineral fiber) insulation:*

- thickness as called for by system type
- for Systems A, B1, B2 and C: 8 lbs/ft³ nominal density; actual material delivered density per pipe size is to be verified on-site to be strictly no less than 7 lbs/ft³ and no more than 9 lbs/ft³
- fibers should be oriented in a longitudinal or circumferential direction, not in a radial direction
- shot content should be minimized but must be less than 30% by weight, as caught on a 100 mesh screen or larger
- binder content should be minimized but sufficient for proper binding
- Precision cut V-grooved, beveled lags from board stock, preformed or pre-molded per exact pipe size for round pipes, as per ASTM C-547 and ASTM C-585.

  **Note**: Caution is issued to the contractor especially when using pre-molded products, as densities can vary; the contractor is advised to confirm density requirements when ordering

*E-glass (high temperature fiberglass) insulation:*

- 1" thickness
- 11 lbs/ft³ nominal density; actual material delivered density is to be verified on-site to be strictly no less than 10½ lbs/ft³ and no more than 12 lbs/ft³
- alumina-silica products are not acceptable
- fibers should be oriented parallel to the surface being treated (i.e. in a longitudinal or circumferential direction, not in a radial direction); needled products are acceptable
- product must be free of shot
- wrap-around type, sized per pipe size for round pipes

*Fiberglass insulation (for System C only):*

- between 3 to 5 lbs/ft³ nominal density
- pre-molded, pre-formed, or wrap-around type, sized per pipe size for round pipes
- board stock for other flat surfaces
- impregnated vinyl septum;
  - ¾ lb/ft² or 1 lb/ft² reinforced mass loaded vinyl fabric
  - mass loading material shall be free of any lead or lead composites
