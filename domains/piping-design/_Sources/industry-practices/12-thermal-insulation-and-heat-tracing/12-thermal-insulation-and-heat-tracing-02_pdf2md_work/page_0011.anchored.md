## 5. COLD INSULATION, TYPE CC (BELOW 70° F THROUGH -265° F)

### 5.1. *Cold Insulation Classes*

5.1.1. On P&IDs, Mechanical and Utility System Flow Diagrams, Pipe Line Designation Tables and Piping Isometric Drawings, these areas shall be coded as CC.

### 5.2. *Insulating Materials for Piping, Vessels, and Equipment*

5.2.1. Insulation material shall be cellular glass or combination rigid polyisocyanurate and cellular glass.

- DOW - Trymer 2000 polyisocyanurate
- Pittsburgh Corning - "Foamglas"
- Suncor approved equal

5.2.2. Thickness of insulation shall be in accordance with Item 5.3, Chart C, or as noted otherwise in the LDT's.

5.2.3. Where combination layers of Polyisocyanurate and Foamglas are specified they are indicated as follows:

*Example:* 2" + 3" = 2" Polyisocyanurate + 2 layers of Foamglas 1 ½" + 1 ½"

5.2.4. Where Foamglas Insulation is specified, the thickness shall be achieved using the layer configurations shown in Item 5.3, Chart "C".
