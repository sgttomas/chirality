## 4. PERSONNEL PROTECTION INSULATION, TYPE PP (140° F THROUGH 1200° F)

### 4.1. Personnel Protection Classes

On P & ID's, Mechanical and Utility Flow Diagrams, Line Designation Tables, and Piping Isometric Drawings, Personal Protection classes shall be coded as PP.

### 4.2. Pipe, Vessels, and Equipment Insulating Materials

4.2.1. Pipe insulation material for operating temperatures 350° F and below on vertical lines and on horizontal lines which are outside of high traffic areas shall be mineral Fiber type II as per ASTM C547.

Acceptable manufacturers and materials are:

- IIG – MinWool – 1200 Pipe Insulation
- Fibrex Inc. – Coreplus 1200 Pipe Insulation
- Roxul Inc. – Techton 1200 Pipe Insulation
- Suncor Approved Equal

4.2.2. Insulation materials for operating temperatures 350° F and below on vertical and horizontal outside of high traffic areas shall be mineral Fiber type IVA as per ASTM C612.
