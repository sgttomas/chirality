## 1. SCOPE

1.1. This specification covers the general requirements for materials and application for external thermal insulation to piping, vessels and equipment operating at temperatures from -265° F through 1200° F.

1.2. Insulation shall be required where necessary to:

- Conserve heat.
- Maintain stabilized process temperatures during atmospheric temperature changes.
- Prevent burning of personnel if surface temperature in normal operation is 140° F and over.
- Avoid condensation and sweating.
- Prevent freezing.
- Conserve refrigeration.
- Fireproofing.
- Noise control.

1.3. Definitions:

- The "**Owner**" means Suncor Energy Inc.
- The "**Supplier**" means the manufacturer, fabricator, vendor or contractor that supplies the materials or services.
- The "**Buyer**" means Suncor Energy Inc. or its designated agent.

## 2. GENERAL

2.1. Line Designation Tables shall indicate insulation type, operating temperature and insulation thickness for piping. Piping Isometric Drawings will repeat this information plus identify the insulation material. Equipment Drawings shall indicate insulation thickness and material for each equipment item requiring insulation. These thicknesses will reflect any special process control requirement and shall take precedence over thicknesses shown elsewhere. As traced piping will require oversizing of insulation, the O.D. of the material must be determined accordingly.

2.2. Operating temperatures of this specification refer to the internal fluid or material temperature of the process system.

2.3. Extent of insulation shall be as noted on P&ID's, Mechanical and Utility System Flow Diagrams, Line Designation Tables and/or equipment or module drawings. Thickness of insulation for piping, vessels and equipment shall be determined by reference to operating temperatures and to the applicable Insulation Thickness Charts. Insulation thickness shall not be changed when heat tracing is applied unless otherwise noted on drawings. The greater of insulation thickness on the line designation tables or the thickness charts in this specification shall be used.

2.4. Insulation shall not be applied to piping, vessels and equipment before completion of pressure testing.
