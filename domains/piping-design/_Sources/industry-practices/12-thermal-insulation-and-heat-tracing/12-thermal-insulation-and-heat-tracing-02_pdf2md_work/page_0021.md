8.3.10. The springs shall be Childers Products Company's 4" Chil-Springs Type 300 series stainless steel or Suncor approved equal.

8.3.11. Springs shall be stressed while being applied, and shall not be stretched more than ½" per spring.

8.3.12. The table is based on a maximum secondary stretch (after item is heated to operating temperature) of 2" per spring.

8.3.13. Where more than one spring is used per band, the springs shall be equally spaced around the insulated item.

8.3.14. A 3/8" thick layer of insulating cement shall be applied over the insulation on unexposed heads and irregularly shaped items which cannot be suitably weatherproofed with metal jacketing.

## **8.4. Cold Vessels and Equipment Below 70° F Through -265° F**

8.4.1. Insulation blocks of appropriate width shall be bevelled where necessary to obtain tight fitting joints.

8.4.2. All joints shall be staggered by starting the first course with alternating short and full length tags (blocks).

8.4.3. The insulation shall be applied with sealer (Pittseal 444N or Chil-Prof CP-22/24) on all joints of single and outer layers of double layers of Foamglas. Sealer shall be applied to both sides of adjoining joints. Cut off any squeezed-out sealant flush with surface.

8.4.4. To aid in the application of insulation on bottom heads of vessels, Pittsburgh Corning PC88 adhesive shall be used. (The use of this adhesive does not contribute to the efficiency of the insulating system.)

8.4.5. After final application of insulation, all unfilled joints and chipped edges shall be pointed up with sealer or filled with pieces of Foamglas cut to fit and cemented in place so as to leave a smooth and even surface.

8.4.6. Polyurethane shall be used for inner layer on combination type cold insulation and shall be applied in the same manner as Foamglas.
