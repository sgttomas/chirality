WHEN USING RIGID INSULATION SUCH AS CALCIUM SILICATE, AN AIR GAP WILL OCCUR, DUE TO THE OVERSIZING OF THE INSULATION TO ELIMINATE AIR FLOW IN THIS GAP, 1200°F E-GLASS INSULATION IS TO BE USED. THE E-GLASS INSULATION SHOULD BE INSERTED IN THE GAP WHERE BREAKS IN THE INSULATION ARE PRESENT. THESE BREAKS INCLUDE 90'S, TEES, FLANGES, SKID AND MODULE BOUNDARIES. ON STRAIGHT RUNS OF PIPE, E-GLASS SHOULD BE INSERTED EVERY 20 LINEAR FEET.

WHEN USING NON-RIGID INSULATION, COMPRESSION OF THE JACKETING CAN OCCUR WHEN EXTERNAL PRESSURE IS APPLIED. TO MINIMIZE THE COMPRESSION, AN EXTRA LAP OF JACKETING IS TO BE APPLIED BETWEEN THE 9 O'CLOCK AND 3 O'CLOCK POSITIONS.
