NOTES.

1. SPACE CIRCUMFERENTIAL SUPPORT, ANGLE B, AT APPROX. VERTICAL INTERVALS OF 12–1". LOCATION SHALL CLEAR PROJECTING NOZZLES, AND BE NO CLOSER THAN 6" TO A CIRCUMFERENTIAL WELD. ANGLE TO BE DIVIDED INTO SUITABLE SEGMENTS AND INSTALLED WITH THE LONGER LEG IN THE HORIZONTAL. SEE TABLE FOR SIZES.

   CLIP A, 3/8" × 3" WIDE SPACED AT ABOUT 24" INTERVALS WITH A MINIMUM OF FOUR (4) CLIPS PER CIRCUMFERENCE. SEE TABLE FOR LENGTHS.

2. TYPE B SUPPORT REQUIRED AT SELECTED ELEVATIONS WHERE VERTICAL SHELL EXPANSION APPROACHES 1 1/2" BETWEEN ANY 2 OR MORE SUPPORTS TO LIMIT THE VERTICAL MOVEMENT OF THE WEATHERPROOF METAL JACKET TO NOT MORE THAN 1 1/2". TYPE B DIFFERS FROM TYPE A SUPPORT BY THE ADDITION OF A 1 1/2" × 1/4" FLAT. LENGTH OF FLAT IS SUCH THAT IT FITS BETWEEN SPACING OF SUPPORT CLIPS A.

3. TYPE C SAME AS TYPE A SUPPORT EXCEPT FOR ADDITION OF 1" × 1" × 1/4" ANGLE 1" LONG SPACED AT APPROXIMATE INTERVALS OF 16" ON PERIPHERY OF ANGLE B AND ACTING AS SUPPORT FOR FIRST COURSE OF SHEET METAL WEATHERPROOF JACKET.

4. TYPE D ACTS AS FLASHING FOR THE INSULATION APPLIED AS FIREPROOFING AND WHERE THE INSULATION DOES NOT EXTEND TO THE FULL HEIGHT OF THE VESSEL. THIS TYPE SHALL NOT BE INSTALLED ON VESSELS SUBJECT TO THERMAL EXPANSION.

5. TYPE E SUPPORT REQUIRED WHERE INSULATION IS 1" THICK.

6. WELD 1/2" SIZE SQUARE NUTS OR T-SLOTTED WELDING PINS ON 16" SPACING TO INSIDE OF SKIRT FOR INSULATION WIRING SUPPORT. ONE ROW REQUIRED FOR EACH LAYER OF INSULATION.

7. METALLURGY OF SUPPORTS MUST BE COMPATIBLE WITH EQUIPMENT METALLURGY.

8. 1" WIDE × 1/4" DEEP SLOT ON 12" CENTRES AROUND CIRCUMFERENCE OF TOP INSULATION SUPPORT RING ONLY REQUIRED ON EQUIPMENT WITH OUTSIDE DIAMETER 48" AND LARGER FOR INSULATION BANDING SECUREMENT.
