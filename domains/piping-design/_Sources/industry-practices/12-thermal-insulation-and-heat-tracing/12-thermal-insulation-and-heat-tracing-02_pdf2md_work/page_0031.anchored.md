- longitudinal edges of adjacent jacketing panels at the same position (o'clock) should not line up exactly, they should miss-align by alternating (rotationally) 1" back and forth

- circumferential edges of the jacketing should overlap the previously installed jacketing panel by 2"

- on vertical outdoor piping, the circumferential edge of the higher jacketing panel shall overlap the circumferential edge of the lower jacketing panel by 2"

- all overlapped longitudinal and circumferential edges of the external jacketing should be continuously caulked

- all jacketing segments between beads ("lobster-back" crimped joints between segments) of mitered (gored) elbows for piping 16" or greater shall have at least two band straps applied, one at the center of each segment, and one right alongside the bead on the overlap side

- for Systems A and B1 only, all beads of mitered elbows shall be visually inspected after installation and banding of the jacketed elbows for any gaps whatsoever between adjacent segments, and any gaps found shall be caulked to seal

- all aluminum jacketing surfaces to be caulked shall first be wiped dry with an alcohol based solvent, to remove the commonly found oil film from the aluminum

- all caulking installed shall be a continuous bead, placed on the under layer of all hem or hug overlaps, so as not to expose any of the caulking

- all caulking shall consist of an aluminum colored or clear silicone elastomer sealant, such as Dow Corning Silastic 732 RTV or 999A Adhesive/Sealant, or approved equal

- for vertical piping exceeding 6m (20 ft.) in height, where the insulation was mechanically supported from the pipe wall, the contractor shall also prepare "S clips" from ¾" x 0.020" stainless steel banding with a functional depth of 3" and insert these into the top edge of each vertical section of jacketing in quadrant positions to receive and support each succeeding tier of jacketing

- all jacketing should be banded according to standard banding practices, using only ¾" x 0.020" Type 304 stainless steel banding along with stainless steel wing seals, on not more than 9" centres; sheet metal screws or pop-rivets should not be used, except by approval

- banding shall be installed in such a way to prevent "fish-mouthing" openings that separate the caulking.

## General:

- prefabricated valve covers, flange covers, etc. may be used, presuming that they meet all of the material and installation requirements mentioned herein
