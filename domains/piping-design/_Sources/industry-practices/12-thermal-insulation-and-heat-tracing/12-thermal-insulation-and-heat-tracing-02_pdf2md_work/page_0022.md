8.4.7.1. Inner Layer(s) - ½" wide x 0.020" stainless steel bands on 9" centres with stainless steel wing seals for vessels 6'0" and under in diameter and ¾" x 0.020" stainless steel bands on 9" centres with stainless steel wing seals for vessels larger than 6'-0" in diameter.

8.4.7.2. Single and Outer Layer - ¾" wide x 0.020" thick stainless steel bands shall be placed at each end of vessels where necessary to hold head bands. Head band connections to bridle bands where more than one bridle band is used shall be staggered.

8.4.8. Anchor or bridle bands shall be ¾" wide by 0.020" thick stainless steel bands and shall be placed at each end of vessels where necessary to hold head bands. Head band connections to bridle bands where more than one bridle band is used shall be staggered.

8.4.9. The insulation shall be finished with Pittsburgh Corning Pittcote 300 or Chil-Pruf CP-22/24 vapour barrier coating. Apply a wet coat of 4-5 Gal/100 ft2. Total thickness should be 3/32" - 1/8" thick, giving a dry film thickness of 1/16". No fabric membrane will be required.

8.4.10. A backing strip of 3/8" x 2" wide expanded neoprene shall be placed between the jacket and vapour barrier to prevent the screws from puncturing the vapour barrier.

## 8.5. Weatherproofing Application

8.5.1. Vessel and tubular equipment shells shall be finished with metal jacketing. The jacket shall not be installed in contact with bare carbon steel.

8.5.2. Metal jacketing material shall be aluminum type 3003 or 5005, 0.016" thick stucco embossed with factory applied integral flame resistant moisture barrier, I.T.W. "Polykraft" or Poly-surlyn or Suncor approved equal. For vertical equipment, the jacketing shall be 1 ¼" x ¼" corrugated. For top heads and for horizontal equipment, the aluminum jacketing shall be flat.

Where the insulation system is considered as fireproofing, or is insulating a caustic or sulphuric acid commodity, the use of stainless steel jacketing in place of aluminum is mandatory.

When insulating process towers, vessels and exchangers with liquid hydrocarbon inventory, stainless steel jacketing in the place of aluminum is mandatory up to 35 feet (10.5m) above grade.

Stainless steel jacketing shall be type 304, 0.010" thick stucco embossed with factory applied integral flame resistant moisture barrier, I.T.W. "Polykraft", Poly-surlyn or Suncor approved equal.

8.5.3. The jackets shall be secured in place with stainless steel self-tapping sheet metal screws or stainless steel pop rivets on maximum centers of 6" for vertical seams and 9" centers for horizontal seams. DO NOT use screws or rivets on vessels and equipment in COLD SERVICE using smooth or 3/16" corrugated jacket. When jacket is 1 ¼" deep corrugated sheets (hot or cold service) screws or rivets shall be installed on the high point of corrugation.

8.5.4. Horizontal joints shall be lapped a minimum of 2". Vertical joints shall be lapped a minimum of 3".
