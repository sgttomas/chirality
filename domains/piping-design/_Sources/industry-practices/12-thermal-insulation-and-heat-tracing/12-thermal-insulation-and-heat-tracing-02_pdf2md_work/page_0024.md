8.5.17. The moisture barrier shall isolate the metal jacket from the insulation. Any damaged moisture barrier on the jacket shall be patched before the jacket is applied, or the sheet shall be rejected.

8.5.18. All seams and joints shall be carefully sealed with a continuous bead of flashing compound Pittseal 444N or Chil-Pruf CP-22/24.

# 9. APPLICATION - INSTRUMENT INSULATION

## 9.1. General

9.1.1. Instruments, including components, valves, tubing and piping shall be insulated when indicated on the P&IDs, Mechanical and Utility System Flow Diagrams.

9.1.2. In cases where installed lengths of tubing or piping are field routed, actual field measurements will be required to determine dimensions.

9.1.3. Flanges and unions shall be insulated in instrument lines unless otherwise noted on the drawings.

9.1.4. Pressure tests shall, if possible, be completed before insulation is installed. If insulation is applied before testing, all connections, including welds shall be left uninsulated and exposed until completion of testing.

## 9.2. In-Line Application

9.2.1. Insulation materials and application shall be in accordance with this specification.

9.2.2. Special applications shall be in accordance with details and drawings prepared for specific instruments.

## 9.3. In-Line Instruments

9.3.1. In-line instruments are defined as those which are an integral part of a piping system, i.e. cannot be removed without affecting normal process flow.

9.3.2. In-line instruments shall be insulated with removable thermal insulation blankets with the same thickness as that specified for adjacent piping.

## 9.4. Remote Instruments

9.4.1. Remote instruments are defined as those which are not an integral part of a piping system, ie. can be removed without affecting normal process flow.

9.4.2. Remote instruments, except analyzers, generally are insulated only to conserve sufficient heat to maintain the fluidity of the line medium.

9.4.3. Normally, remote instruments that require protection are insulated with removable thermal insulation blankets with impulse line insulated from the root valve to the enclosure.

9.4.4. Insulation requirements for analyzer lead lines must be determined for each installation by Control Systems Engineering Department.

## 9.5. Level Instruments

9.5.1. Level Glasses and displacer type Level instruments shall be insulated as specified by the vessel trim drawings.
