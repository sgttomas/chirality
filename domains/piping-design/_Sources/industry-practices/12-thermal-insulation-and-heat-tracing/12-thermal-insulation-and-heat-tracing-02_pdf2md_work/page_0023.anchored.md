8.5.5. Joints on 1 ¼" deep corrugated jacket shall be lapped 2 full corrugations.

8.5.6. On horizontal joints the seam shall be placed below the horizontal center line with the lap turned down.

8.5.7. Except for 1 ¼" deep corrugated jacket, a 1" hem shall be turned on overlapping edge of vertical and horizontal seams.

8.5.8. On vertical vessels and equipment, the jacket shall be supported on "S" clips spaced on 24" centers. The jacket shall be secured with stainless steel bands spaced on 18" centers with one band at each circumferential lap. The bands shall be tightened with a mechanical tightening tool and secured in place with stainless steel wing type seals. "J-clips" shall be installed on each band to prevent vertical movement. "J-clips" shall be placed on maximum centers of 5'0".

8.5.9. When springs are required over insulation, the same number of springs shall also be used over the aluminum jacketing.

8.5.10. On horizontal vessels and equipment, the jacket shall be secured with stainless steel bands spaced on 18" centers. The bands shall be tightened with a mechanical tightening tool and secured in place with stainless steel wing type seals.

8.5.11. Top heads on vertical vessels and tubular equipment shall be finished with metal jacketing, with a minimum of 6" turned down on the straight side of the vessel as an overlap. The overlap shall be fastened to the jacketing on the shell with sheet metal screws on 6" centers. The metal head cover shall also be secured in place at the circumferential overlap with a band. The heads of all gore sections of heads shall be filled with caulking and then all gore sections shall be secured to one another with screws on 6" centres along the beads of each gore section.

8.5.12. Heads on horizontal vessels and tubular equipment and bottom exposed heads on vertical vessels shall be finished with metal jacketing with weatherproof seams, with a minimum of 6" turned under the shell metal jacketing. The overlap shall be fastened to the jacketing on the shell with sheet metal screws on 6" centers. The metal covers shall also be secured in place at the circumferential overlaps with a band.

8.5.13. Transitions and stiffener rings shall be finished with metal jacketing with weatherproof seams. The jacketing shall extend under the upper shell jacketing a minimum of 6" and down over the lower shell jacketing a minimum of 6". The overlap shall be fastened to the jacketing on the shell with sheet metal screws on 6" centers. The metal head covers shall also be secured in place at the circumferential overlaps with a band.

8.5.14. Unexposed heads and skirt interiors shall not be weatherproofed.

8.5.15. Heavy fillets of flashing compounds Pittseal 444N of Chil-Pruf CP-22/24 shall be applied as flashing at all possible sources of moisture penetration such as nozzles and other protrusions through the metal surface.

8.5.16. The moisture barrier shall be hole free and continuous before applications. Bare metal cladding shall not be installed in contact with calcium silicate or other alkaline insulations.
