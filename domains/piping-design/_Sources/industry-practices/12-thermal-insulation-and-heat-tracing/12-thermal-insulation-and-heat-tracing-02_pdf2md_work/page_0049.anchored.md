## 5. Design Criteria

5.1 Blankets on flanged valves and equipment shall be designed to cover adjacent mating flanges and overlap line insulation by a minimum of 50 mm.
5.2 Blankets on valves shall be designed to cover the valve body and the bonnet flange of the valve.
5.3 Blankets for pressure gauges include block and bleed valve.
5.4 Blankets for PSV's shall include the outlet flange. Blanket shall cover entire spring chamber.
5.5 Supplier/ Manufacturers will do all dimensional measurement of each equipment and assume responsibility for fit.
5.6 Manufacturer will maintain the documentation of items furnished, which includes dimensional tractability.

### 6.1 Blanket Jacket Material

#### 6.1.1 Outside Jacket Cover

- Weight = 16.5 OZ/ Sqyd ± 10%
