## 8.2 Hot Vessels and Equipment 70° F and 600° F

**8.2.1.** Block insulation on vessels and equipment shall be applied with staggered joint arrangement.

**Note:** In high fire risk areas where the insulation serves as fire proofing, both layers of the insulation shall be held in place with ½" wide bands on 9" centres. Wire is not acceptable.

**8.2.2.** Insulation for side walls of tanks, towers, vessels and equipment over 30" diameter shall be in board form. For items 30" O.D. and smaller, the insulation may be sectional or segmented pipe insulation.

**8.2.3.** The broken joint method shall be used to install the insulation and shall be banded in place.

**8.2.4.** All voids shall be filled with broken block insulation or with insulation cement. Insulating and finishing cement shall be composed of mineral fiber pellets and insulating fillers, blended with hydraulic setting binders and corrosion inhibitors. Recommended products: Thermal-1-Kote 1-FW by Pro-Tec-T-Kote or Suncor approved equal.

**8.2.5.** Bottom heads of towers and vessels that are concealed by skirting will not require jacketing but will require a layer of 1" galvanized hexagonal wire mesh for the purpose of insulation reinforcement.

**8.2.6.** On conical tank roofs the insulation shall be held in place by 10 gauge stainless steel welding studs or pins, and speed clips. Studs or pins shall be applied by Nelson, or Continental Stud Arc, or Stud Welding or Capacitor Discharge Stud Welding Procedure. Spacing of studs or pins to be 12" center to center. The procedure shall be submitted to Suncor for approval prior to commencement of stud welding. Other tank insulation systems for sidewall and roofs may be acceptable but shall be submitted to Suncor's Engineer for approval prior to application.

**8.2.8.** Anchor or bridle bands shall be ¾" wide by 0.020" thick and shall be placed at each end of vessels where necessary to hold head bands. Head connections to bridle bands where more than one bridle band is used shall be staggered.

**8.2.9.** The bands shall be tightened with a mechanical tightening tool and secured with stainless steel wing type seals.

**8.2.10.** A 3/8" thick layer of insulating cement shall be applied over the insulation on unexposed bottom heads and irregularly shaped items which cannot be suitably weatherproofed with metal jacketing.
