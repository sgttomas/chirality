## Corrosion Protection

Above ground piping exposed to the outdoor elements may already be painted, coated or otherwise treated with corrosion inhibiting protection. Piping inside compressor buildings, to a lesser extent, also requires consideration. With the addition of acoustical pipe lagging, two concerns are typically raised: 1) the piping will be more prone to corrosive buildup when contained within a porous insulation system; and 2) the piping cannot be easily inspected for corrosive buildup when covered. To mitigate the potentially adverse effects of corrosion, Bantrel / Suncor at their option, may wish to supplement the acoustical design with an implementation plan for additional corrosion inhibiting protection treatments. While not specifically preferred, recommended nor specified herein, such corrosion inhibiting protection treatments could include the following:

- priming and painting with a urethane paint system

- priming and application of a corrosion inhibiting tapewrap, such as first an elastomeric adhesive, a rubber and synthetic resin, or a bituminous rubber compound, and second a polyethylene film backing

- utilizing pre-coated pipe.
