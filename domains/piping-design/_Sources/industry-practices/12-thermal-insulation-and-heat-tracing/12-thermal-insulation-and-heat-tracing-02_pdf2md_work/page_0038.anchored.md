## Scrim Cloth

Acceptable products and/or manufacturers are:

- "Luben 58 or 59" by Alpha Associates, Inc.
- Other materials approved by Owner
- Purchaser's approved equal.

**Note:** The scrim cloth is only necessary when glue-bonding multiple layers of e-glass together, or when glue-bonding a layer of e-glass to another material layer. The purpose of the scrim cloth is to prevent glue saturation into and hardening of the e-glass insulation. The scrim cloth layer(s) can be deleted when the fabricator determines that layers should not be adhered together.

## E-glass Insulation (high temperature fiberglass)

- 1-inch (25 mm) or 2-inch (50 mm) thickness per layer
- 11 lbs/ft³ (176 kg/m³) nominal density; actual material delivered density is to be verified on-site to be strictly no less than 10½ lbs/ft³ (160 kg/m³) and no more than 12 lbs/ft³ (192 kg/m³)
- alumina-silica products are not acceptable
- fibers should be oriented parallel to the surface being treated (i.e. in a longitudinal or circumferential direction, not in a radial direction); needled products are acceptable
- product must be free of sand and shot
- Other materials approved by Owner
- Purchaser's approved equal.

Acceptable E-glass products and manufacturers are presented in Table B-1. Manufacturer's technical trade literature for materials mentioned in these Specifications are available on the internet at websites as indicated below the product's name in the table.

## Fiberglass Insulation

- 0.75 lbs/ft³ to 2.0 lbs/ft³ (12 kg/m³ to 32 kg/m³) density.

Acceptable products and/or manufacturers are:

- "Certified R Metal Building Insulation" (formerly AF-300) by Owens Corning Canada Inc.
- "RA Series Appliance Insulation" (RA-26), (formerly AF-180) by Owens Corning Canada Inc
- "AK Blanket" by Manson Insulation
- Other materials approved by Owner
- Purchaser's approved equal.

## Impregnated Vinyl Barrier

- ¾ lb/ft² or 1 lb/ft² reinforced mass loaded vinyl fabric
- mass loading material shall be free of any lead or lead composites
- vinyl barrier shall be manufactured utilizing a continuous cast-in-place process or utilizing a calendering process; material manufactured utilizing an extruded process will not be acceptable
- reinforcing membrane shall be embedded in the barrier in a two-pass process
