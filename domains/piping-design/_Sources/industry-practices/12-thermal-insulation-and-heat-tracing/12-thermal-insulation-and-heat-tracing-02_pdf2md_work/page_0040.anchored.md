To mitigate the potentially adverse effects of corrosion, Bantrel / Suncor at their option, may wish to supplement the acoustical design with an implementation plan for additional corrosion inhibiting protection treatments. While not specifically preferred, recommended nor specified herein, such corrosion inhibiting protection treatments could include the following:

- priming and painting with a urethane paint system, compatible with the operating temperature of the equipment
- priming and application of a corrosion inhibiting tapewrap, such as first an elastomeric adhesive, a rubber and synthetic resin, or a bituminous rubber compound, and second a polyethylene film backing, compatible with the operating temperature of the equipment
- utilizing pre-coated materials.

## HIGH-TEMPERTURE PROTECTION

When the Acoustical Blanket will be in contact with high-temperature surfaces, the Teflon coated fiberglass cloth may not be deemed an appropriate inner layer of the Acoustical Blanket. Common materials might then consist of stainless steel mesh and/or untreated woven fiberglass cloth (i.e. without silicon). These considerations are NOT covered within these Specifications, and must be evaluated carefully by Bantrel / Suncor.

## IDENTIFICATION

A. All custom-fit flexible reusable acoustical covers shall have permanently attached identification tags indicating the equipment tag number. The tag shall be constructed from 304 stainless steel, with either etched or raised lettering (ease of reading when painted). The tag shall include the manufacturer's fabrication drawing reference.
B. Off-the shelf flexible reusable acoustical covers shall identify the size, pressure rating, and intended use (e.g. Valve, Gate, NPS 2, 300#).

## DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS

A. No section of a flexible, reusable acoustical cover shall weigh in excess of 88 lb (40 kg) without prior approval of the Purchaser.
B. It is the responsibility of the Vendor to field measure equipment requiring custom made covers. The Vendor is responsible for accurate fit-ups.
C. Bolting accessibility and clearance for removal of bolting should be considered in cover design.
D. Welded support rings will be provided on vessels and exchangers in which insulation covers will hang in the vertical position.
E. Type 1 blankets shall have a 4-inch (100 mm) shiplap joint half way through the total blanket thickness at all joints to ensure a tight fit at the seams.
F. Where the blanket abuts a pipe jacketed sheet, the blanket should overlap the jacketing by a minimum of 4-inches (100 mm), this overlap should be banded but not caulked.
G. Any protrusions such as by shafts, small piping, support structure members, gauges, pressure/temperature transmitters, etc. do not require treatment by the blanket.
