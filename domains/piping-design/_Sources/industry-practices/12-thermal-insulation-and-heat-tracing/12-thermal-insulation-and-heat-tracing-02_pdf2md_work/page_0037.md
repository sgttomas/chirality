## TYPE II

### *First blanket:*

A. Teflon coated fiberglass cloth
B. Scrim cloth
C. 4-inch (100 mm) thick E-glass insulation, 11 lbs/ft³ (176 kg/m³) density, [four 1-inch (25 mm) layers or two 2-inch (50 mm) layers], utilizing scrim cloth bond between layers
D. Scrim cloth
E. Teflon coated fiberglass cloth

### *Second blanket (overlapping first blanket):*

F. Teflon coated fiberglass cloth
G. Scrim cloth
H. 1-inch (25 mm) thick E-glass insulation, 11 lbs/ft³ (175 kg/m³) density [one 1-inch (25 mm) layer]
I. Impregnated vinyl barrier sheet, 2 lb/ft² (9.8 kg/m²), having a sound transmission class (STC) rating of at least 25 as per ASTM E90 tests
J. Scrim cloth
K. 1-inch (25 mm) thick fiberglass insulation blanket, between 0.75 lbs/ft³ to 2.0 lbs/ft³ (12 kg/m³ to 32 kg/m³) density
L. Teflon coated fiberglass cloth.

## MATERIALS

All materials shall be non-combustible.

### Teflon Coated Fiberglass Cloth

Materials for the outer jacket liner and straps shall be woven fiberglass cloth impregnated with Teflon for water proofing. Acceptable products and/or manufacturers are:

Note: The material for the inner jacket shall be the same as for the outer jacket for surface temperatures less than 400° F (204° C). From 400° F (204° C) to 1000° F (540° C), materials shall be vermiculite treated woven fiberglass cloth (i.e. without silicon).

The gauge or weight of cloth shall be selected to suit the durability and flexibility required for the application, but shall not be less than 16.5 oz/yd² (550 gm/m²) with Teflon and 18 oz/yd² (600 gm/m²) on vermiculite treated.
