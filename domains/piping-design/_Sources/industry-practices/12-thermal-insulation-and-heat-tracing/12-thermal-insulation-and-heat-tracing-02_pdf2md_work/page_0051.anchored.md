- 8.6 Perimeter flaps shall be formed as an extension of the outer jacket, and shall not be attached to the blanket as separate pieces.
- 8.7 All drawcord and closure flaps, required inside the perimeter of the blanket shall be attached inside the seam, between the liner and jacket layers of the blanket.
- 8.8 No external fasteners shall be used to prevent shifting of the insulation. The insulation shall be mechanically fastened in place internally.

## 9. Accessories

Use standards accessories, fasteners, D-rings, Knitted mesh, Nomex/ Stainless Steel thread, Hook & loop for insulation blanket.

**Sewing Material**

- For temperature up to 550 °F, sewing material ALPHA MARITEX D-24 Teflon coated twisted fiberglass thread shall be used or equal
- For temperature range from 551 °F to 1200 °F, sewing material ST-304 (304 SS) thread shall be used.

## 10. Identification Tag

Stainless Steel 304 or aluminum tag shall be used. Stainless Steel tag is preferred in corrosive application. The tag dimensions shall be 2"x2.5". The corner of the tag should be chamfered by 1/8". Tag shall be riveted with a piece of same Teflon coated EJ 1050 fabric material, of 2.5"x3.5" size and sewed with the blanket cover. All blankets shall be fitted with an identification Tag clearly marked with the following information:

- Equipment #                        e.g. VGA1003#12
- Equipment size and type            e.g. 6" 300# Gate Valve
- Plant # / ISO #                    e.g. 52 / 52P3031-1510
- Co-ordinates and Elevation         e.g. E 200', N350' and EL 135'
- Max operating temperature          e.g. 300 °F
- Manufacturer's identification #    e.g. As per manufacturer standards
