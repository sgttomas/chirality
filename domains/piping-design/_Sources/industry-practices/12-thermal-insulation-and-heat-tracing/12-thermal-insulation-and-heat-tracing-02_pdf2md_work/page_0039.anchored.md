## Miscellaneous

*Stitched seams* shall be double folded and top stitched on the interior of the finished cover and strap.

The *flaps* shall be constructed with the vinyl barriers continuous through the inside of the flap. Flaps shall be 4" in length. Stainless steel lace anchors are preferred. Stainless steel quilting pins for fastening are preferred; no gluing is permitted for fastening.

*Straps* are the preferred method of attachment for frequent removal; they shall be provided in sufficient quantity and stitched to the outer covering with sufficient reinforcement to adequately secure the blanket in place and not degenerate with frequent handling. Strap material shall be the same as the outer blanket jacket although a heavier material weight may be required. Minimum strap width shall be 1-inch (25 mm).

*#304 stainless steel* dual D-rings may be used for securing of straps. They shall be made from 1/8-inch (3.2 mm) minimum diameter solid stainless steel rod, which is bent and welded closed to form 7/8-inch (22 mm) flat opening. These shall be attached to the blanket with a looped strap. Hog ring construction may be used where required; stainless steel shall be used. Alternatively, 14 ga. stainless steel quilting buttons may be used for securing of straps.
