6.6.4 For equipment larger than 16 feet diameter, the aluminum jacket shall be 0.032" thick with 5/8" x 2-1/2" corrugations, with factory attached moisture barrier. Jacket material for vessel heads shall be smooth or stucco embossed 0.032" thick aluminum.

6.6.5 For walls on hot storage tanks 25 feet in diameter and smaller, the jacket shall be 0.024" thick with 1/4" x 1-1/4" corrugations, with factory attached moisture barrier.

6.6.6 For walls on hot storage tanks larger than 25 feet diameter, the jacket shall be 0.040" thick with 5/8" x 2-1/2" corrugations, with factory attached moisture barrier.

6.6.7 For roofs on hot storage tanks, the jacket shall be smooth or stucco embossed 0.032" thick aluminum with a factory applied moisture barrier.

6.6.8 Thermal insulating cement shall comply with ASTM C449 or ASTM C195 and shall be RAMCO RAMCOTE 1200 rated for surface temperatures up through 1200°F, or RAMCO SUPERTEMP 1900 rated for surface temperatures up through 1900°F. RAMCOTE 1200 shall not be used in direct contact with unpainted aluminum, to prevent corrosion of the aluminum. RAMCOTE 1200 and SUPERTEMP 1900 insulating cement for use on stainless steel piping and equipment shall be special ordered to include certification and chemical analysis to confirm compliance with MIL-I-24244, as indicated on the product data sheets.

6.6.9 Breathing type mastic sealer for coating hardened insulating cement for hot thermal insulation applications shall be gray Childers VI-CRYL CP-10-1 (trowel) or CP-11-1 (spray/brush).

6.6.10 Nonbreathing type (vapor barrier) mastic sealer for coating hardened insulating cement for cold thermal insulation applications shall be gray Childers ENCACEL X CP-40-1 (trowel) or ENCACEL V CP-45-1 (spray/brush).

6.6.11 Breathing type asphalt emulsion coating for hardened insulating cement for hot thermal insulation applications shall be Childers CHL-BRATE CP-25 (trowel).

6.6.12 Nonbreathing type (vapor barrier) asphalt based fibrated coating for hardened insulating cement for cold thermal insulation applications shall be Childers CHIL-PRUF CP-22 (trowel) or CP-24 (spray).

6.6.13 Synthetic fiber reinforcing mesh for use with mastic sealer coatings or asphalt based weather barrier coatings shall be Foster MAST-A-FAB (9x8 mesh).

6.6.14 Caulking material for temperatures up through 500°F shall be aluminum gray Red Devil HVAC/R silicone sealant.
