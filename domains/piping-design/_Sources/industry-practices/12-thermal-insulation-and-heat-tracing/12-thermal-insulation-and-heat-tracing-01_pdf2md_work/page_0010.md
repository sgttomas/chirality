3.1.6 Pipe insulation shall be square cut, stopped short of studs and nuts on flanged joints to permit their removal from both sides without damage to the insulation. Clearance shall be equal to the stud length plus 1".

3.1.7 Insulation for 2" and larger valves and fittings shall be fabricated from segmental or preformed sectional pipe insulation, except as specified in paragraphs 3.1.4 and 3.1.5. Prefabricated insulation shall be constructed to conform to the body of the fittings to which they are applied, and shall be secured in place with 16 gauge galvanized soft annealed iron wire spaced not to exceed 3" on centers over the long radius of the fitting insulation. Valves and fittings 1-1/2" and smaller that require insulation shall be insulated with insulating cement, applied in not greater than 3/4" layers, to the same thickness as the adjacent piping insulation.

3.1.8 Insulation support rings shall be furnished by the piping contractor on 6" and larger pipe inclined at 45° or more where the pipe length is 12 feet or greater. The width of the support ring shall be 1/2" less than the total insulation thickness. The first support ring shall occur within 18" from the bottom of the rise or a flanged connection. One support ring shall be provided at least every 20 feet.

3.1.9 Piping up to but not including steam traps shall be insulated for heat conservation. Depending on the type of steam trap, traps shall be insulated with soft removable covers per section 3.5, or personnel protected with removable shields, as directed on drawings LA-000-0100-0200-006 & -007. Piping out of traps shall be insulated for heat conservation where applicable, or personnel protected per section 2.

3.1.10 Piping 1-1/2" and larger that requires insulation shall be provided with pipe shoes. Insulation for piping 1" and smaller shall be cut back from the bottom of the pipe a distance of 9" on each side of the center of the support, so that the bare pipe rests on the support.

3.1.11 Steam traced lines are to be insulated as an integral unit using molded pipe and fitting insulation wherever possible.

3.1.12 Where steam tracer tubing or electric heating cable jumps over valves, flanges, or similar items, the tracer shall be spirally double wrapped with 2" wide x 1/8" ceramic fiber or Nomex tape. Tape shall be secured by spirally wrapping with 16 gauge galvanized soft annealed iron wire using not more than 3/4" spacing for the spirals. Weatherproof by sealing with metallic gray breathing type mastic. Unions shall be left bare.

3.1.13 Mineral wool insulation shall be used for heat traced oil and was lines operating up through 350°F. The required thickness shall be as indicated in Table 2 series.

3.1.14 Underground lines shall be insulated with Gilsonite material, foamglass, or an approved equal. The insulation shall be installed per the manufacturer's instructions.

3.1.15 Insulation applied to type 18-8 stainless steel shall comply with section 6.8.
