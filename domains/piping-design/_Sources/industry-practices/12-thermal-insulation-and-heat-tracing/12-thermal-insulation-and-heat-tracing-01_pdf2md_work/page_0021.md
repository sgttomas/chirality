4.3.5 The base row of insulation for vertical equipment shall rest on a solid metal ring, which is seal welded to the shell and sloped down to the outside for water drainage. A solid metal ring seal welded to the shell and sloped down to the outside shall also be used as applicable at the top of the insulation, 2" below the head to shell seam weld and at any shell transition sections, to prevent water intrusion behind the insulation. All intermediate insulation support rings shall be bolted or welded to clips that are welded to the shell, and shall have a minimum clearance of 0.375" between the shell and the inside edge of the support rings to provide a clear drainage path for any water that gets behind the insulation. The outside radius of intermediate support rings shall be at least 1/2" less than the outside radius of the insulation. Vertical spacing of insulation support rings shall not exceed 5 feet. Welding of insulation supports on new equipment should be done by the equipment fabricator. No welding shall be done on installed equipment without approval of BP CHP Reliability Engineering.

4.3.6 Insulation bands and girdles shall be machine stretched and sealed.

4.3.7 Alternate methods for securing insulation to equipment, such as welded studs, will be given consideration, providing full details and justification of the proposed alternate methods are furnished to BP CHP Reliability Engineering for approval.

4.3.8 Skirts supporting insulated equipment shall be partially insulated inside and outside with the same insulation system and thickness as the equipment. The insulation shall extend downward from the junction of the skirt and the equipment a minimum distance of 18" or four times the thickness of the insulation, whichever is greater.

4.3.9 Equipment support legs, cradles, structural steel members, hangers and other connections attached directly to the equipment or machinery shall be insulated beyond the equipment or machinery insulation surface for a minimum distance of 18" or four times the thickness of the insulation, whichever is greater. The end of the insulation must be sealed to the attachment in such manner as to prevent the entrance of moisture or vapor.

4.3.10 Insulation shall be square cut, stopped short of studs and nuts on flanged connections to allow for bolt removal. The end of the insulation shall be covered with 1/16" coat (wet) of nonbreathing type asphalt based fibrated coating. Studs and nuts shall be capable of being removed without damage to the insulation. Space shall be filled with loose material and flanges shall be enclosed in box of same thickness and material as equipment.

4.3.11 Side-wall insulation on horizontal equipment and machinery shall extend past the head by an amount equal to the thickness of the insulation so that the head insulation will fit flush and inside the extended side-wall insulation.

4.3.12 Unless otherwise specified, access openings and flanged connections shall be fully insulated.
