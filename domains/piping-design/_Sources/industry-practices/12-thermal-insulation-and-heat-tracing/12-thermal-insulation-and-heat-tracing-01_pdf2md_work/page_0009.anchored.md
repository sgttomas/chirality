3.1.1.2 Piping vent branch connections less than 12" long are not required to be insulated except for personnel protection. All piping drain connections (low points) shall be insulated to prevent freezing.

3.1.2 Each layer of insulation shall be separately wired in place with 16 gauge galvanized soft annealed iron or stainless steel wire on 12" centers, unless the insulation is supplied with a factory applied metal covering.

3.1.3 Expansion joints for single layer application shall be provided on straight run piping and spaced in accordance with chart below. Expansion joints shall consist of a 1" gap between the sections of preformed insulation. This gap shall be filled with loose mineral wool compressed in place. The aluminum weatherproofing shall be lapped at this point and banded in such a manner to allow movement.

3.1.4 Flanges in insulated services operating up through 500°F shall be insulated along with the piping and equipment, except for VOC services. For insulated flanges in VOC services, a removable type insulation cover per section 3.5 shall be provided to accommodate the periodic removal for VOC monitoring of the flange. For services operating above 500°F, piping flanges and equipment flanges shall not be insulated, to keep the bolts exposed to atmospheric cooling and reduce the potential for flange leaks. Personnel protection in accordance with Section 2 shall be applied where needed for uninsulated hot flanges.

3.1.5 All field end valves and fittings shall be insulated to the thickness of the adjoining piping. All flanged valves in process lines up through 750°F shall be insulated with removable type covers per section 3.5, except that the flanges and bolts shall not be insulated above 500°F. The contractor shall install the cover on valves as specified for temperature control, personnel protection, steam and electric tracing, corrosion protection, appearance improvement, or heat loss reduction for energy conservation. When it is necessary to insulate valves, they shall only be insulated up to the packing gland. Insulation shall not interfere with adjustment or removal of packing gland.
