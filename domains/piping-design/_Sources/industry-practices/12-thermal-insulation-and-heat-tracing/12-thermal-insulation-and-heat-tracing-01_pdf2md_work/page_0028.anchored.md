## 6.5 LOW TEMPERATURE SERVICE MATERIAL (-320°F TO AMBIENT)

Insulation for low temperatures shall be the materials listed below for the temperature ranges indicated, unless otherwise approved by BP CHP Reliability Engineering. The preferred insulation materials for low temperature service are polyurethane foam or foam glass, which are suitable throughout the low temperature range. The polyurethane material shall have an average actual density of 2.0 lb/ft³, have a thermal conductivity of 0.15 Btu-in/hr-ft²-°F or better at -50°F, and shall be non-combustible.

## 6.6 WEATHERPROOFING MATERIALS

6.6.1 Insulation jacketing shall normally be aluminum and shall meet the requirements of ASTM C1729 except for jacket minimum thicknesses and corrugation types which shall be as specified in this standard. Stainless steel jacketing is allowed for special applications as specified in section 1.6. Aluminum jacketing shall be in accordance with the following ASTM C1729 classifications:

- Type I = bare outer surface.

- Grade 1 = ASTM B209 aluminum alloy 3105 or 3003, half hard temper H14.

- Class A (polysurlyn) or Class C (polykraft) moisture retarder/barrier. Class A is preferred.

6.6.2 For piping and equipment 5 feet in diameter and smaller, jacket shall be 0.016" thick 3/16" corrugated aluminum with factory attached moisture barrier. Jacket material for vessel heads shall be smooth or stucco embossed 0.016" thick aluminum.

6.6.3 For equipment larger than 5 feet in diameter and up through 16 feet diameter, the aluminum jacket shall be 0.024" thick with 1/4" x 1-1/4" corrugations, with factory attached moisture barrier. Jacket material for vessel heads shall be smooth or stucco embossed 0.024" thick aluminum.
