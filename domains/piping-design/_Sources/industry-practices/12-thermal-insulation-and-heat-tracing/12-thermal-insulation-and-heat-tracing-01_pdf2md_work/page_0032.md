7.1.2 Personnel Protection Insulation Thickness Table 1 Series is based on the following conditions:

- 7.1.2.1 Ambient conditions of 100°F still air.
- 7.1.2.2 Maximum 140°F surface temperature.
- 7.1.2.3 Calcium silicate or mineral wool insulation.

7.1.3 Economic Insulation Thickness Table 2 Series is based on the following conditions:

- 7.1.3.1 Table selection per the current value of fuel gas, available from the BP CHP computer network at R:\CHPcomm\CI - Energy Conservation Team\utility calculator.xls or the BP CHP Energy Engineer. If the fuel gas value falls between two tables, use the next higher fuel gas value table.
- 7.1.3.2 Calcium silicate, mineral wool, or fiberglass insulation.
- 7.1.3.3 New construction.
- 7.1.3.4 Ambient conditions of 50°F with 10 mph wind velocity.
- 7.1.3.5 15% rate of return on capital after taxes.
- 7.1.3.6 5% of capital per year for maintenance etc.
- 7.1.3.7 7% energy cost inflation rate per year.
- 7.1.3.8 Ten year project life.

7.1.4 Polyurethane Foam Insulation Thickness For Cold Temperatures Table 3 is based on the following conditions:

- 7.1.4.1 Polyurethane foam insulation.
- 7.1.4.2 Ambient conditions of 80°F still air at 80% relative humidity.
- 7.1.4.3 Prevention of condensation on the insulation surface.
