## 6.7 MATERIALS FOR REMOVABLE COVERS

6.7.1 Outer insulation jacket exposed to weather shall be silicone rubber coated heavy duty fiberglass fabric, 17 oz/sy, rated for temperatures up through 500°F, Integrated Marketing Group (IMG) Style 1750LSS.

6.7.2 Insulation shall be non-resinous needled fiberglass blanket in accordance with Military Specification MIL-I-16411, rated for temperatures up through 1200°F, GLT Tempmat; or needled ceramic fiber blanket, rated for temperatures up through 2300°F, INSWOOL-HP Blanket. For thick insulation blankets, ceramic fiber is preferred to reduce the weight for easier handling.

6.7.3 Heat treated fiberglass fabric, 18 oz/sy, rated for temperatures up through 1000°F, for use as an inner liner for surface temperatures from 401°F through 600°F, and for use as an intermediate liner for surface temperatures from 601°F through 750°F, shall be LEWCO 2025-9383 H-I.

6.7.4 Knitted stainless steel mesh for use as an inner liner for surface temperatures 601°F and higher, shall be 304 stainless steel, 0.008" wire diameter, 60 openings per square inch density, rated for temperatures up through 1200°F, Integrated Marketing Group (IMG) Style T304SS.

6.7.5 Quilting fasteners shall be Midwest Fasteners product code QP, 304 stainless steel, length specified for the insulation thickness, with 12 gauge nails and self-locking washers.
