3.4.5 The jacketing material shall be cut to provide for a single wrap around the circumference of the equipment shell where possible. When the shell diameter is too large to allow the use of a single wrap, the jacket may be applied in two or more sections with overlap and sealing of joints as specified in paragraph 3.4.4.

3.4.6 Jacketing shall be secured in place by 3/4" x 0.020" type 18-8 stainless steel bands on 12" centers and with a band at each circumferential joint. If necessary, rivets may be used between the bands in order to close gaps in the lap joint. Expansion springs shall be used the same as specified in paragraph 3.3.6.

3.4.7 Horizontal overlaps of jacketing material shall be provided with S-clips spaced 24" on centers. S-clips shall be 0.040" x 3/4" wide 304 stainless steel.

3.4.8 Vessel Heads

#### 3.4.8.1

Bottom heads on vertical vessels and other surfaces not covered with metal jacketing shall be weatherproofed with a metallic gray breathing type mastic coating with fiber reinforcing mesh, or breathing type asphalt emulsion coating with fiber reinforcing mesh. Weather barrier coatings shall be installed per the manufacturer's instructions. After the surface has been prepared as described in paragraph 3.3.11, the surface shall be coated with 1/8" thick metallic gray breathing type mastic and covered with one layer of fiber reinforcing mesh smoothly stretched and fastened to eliminate all bulges and loose spots. A final coat of metallic gray breathing type mastic shall be applied over the fiber reinforcing mesh to a minimum wet thickness of 1/4". The final coat shall be troweled and brushed to a smooth surface free of lumps, indentations, or excessive feathering cracks. The junction between the mastic on the head and the weatherproofing jacket shall be provided with a 0.016" soft aluminum metal shield to provide positive water shed.

#### 3.4.8.2

Top heads on vertical vessels and horizontal vessel heads shall be weatherproofed with pie shaped aluminum segments, fitting the contour of the insulation. Weatherproofing shall be 0.032" thick smooth aluminum jacketing with attached moisture barrier. The aluminum jacketing shall have a 3/4" minimum overlap with crimped edge. Crimped joints shall receive a bead of caulk. Rivets shall be used instead of metal screws to secure aluminum jacketing to weather shield angle. The head to shell lap on horizontal vessels shall be 5" minimum.

3.4.9 Caulking material shall be applied at all possible locations of moisture penetration, such as the intersections of insulation at piping; equipment manways, supports and nozzles; pipe supports and hangers; and other parts protruding through the surface of the finish. The caulking material shall be applied over all intersecting surfaces without leaving pockets or voids, to prevent water infiltration.
