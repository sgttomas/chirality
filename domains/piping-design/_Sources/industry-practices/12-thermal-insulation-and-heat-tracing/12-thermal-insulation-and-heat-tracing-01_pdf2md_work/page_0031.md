6.7.6 Lacing hooks shall be Midwest Fasteners product code LA, 304 stainless steel, length specified for the insulation thickness, with 12 gauge nails and self-locking washers. To improve the ergonomics of hand cutting the nails, lacing hooks with an aluminum nail and stainless steel head may be used for services with operating temperatures 400°F and lower which are in environments not corrosive to aluminum.

6.7.7 Thread shall be Teflon coated fiberglass suitable for machine sewing, 0.021" diameter, rated for temperatures up through 600°F, Alpha Maritex D-24. Use of other thread materials for service temperatures over 600°F requires prior approval by BP CHP Reliability Engineering.

6.7.8 Lacing wire shall be minimum 20 gauge Type 304 stainless steel, soft annealed.

6.7.9 Hog rings shall be King-Hughes Fasteners (KHF), 16 gauge, 304 stainless steel, sized for the application.

6.7.10 Stainless steel foil for use as an intermediate liner for surface temperatures 751°F and higher shall be 0.002" thick 304 stainless steel.

6.7.11 Rivets shall be the same as paragraph 6.6.15 and 6.6.16.

6.7.12 Staples shall be the same as paragraph 6.6.17.

## 6.8 INSULATION MATERIALS FOR STAINLESS STEEL PIPE & EQUIPMENT

6.8.1 All insulation and insulating cement for application on stainless steel piping, equipment, and tanks shall be certified to conform to ASTM C795 or MIL-I-24244, to assure that contaminants are not present in the insulation material which may cause stress corrosion cracking in the stainless steel. When requested by BP CHP, test reports shall be provided to verify conformance of the materials to these standards.

6.8.2 See paragraph 6.6.8 for special ordering requirements for insulating cement.

6.8.3 See painting requirements in paragraph 1.3.1 for insulated stainless steel surfaces.
