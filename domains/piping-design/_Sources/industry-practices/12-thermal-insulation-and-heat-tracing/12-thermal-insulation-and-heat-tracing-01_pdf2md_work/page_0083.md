## A4 STUD WELDING

**A4.1** Spot welding shall be done with a Nelson stud gun or equivalent. The capacitor discharge process is required as this process minimizes heat input and therefore depth of penetration. Any other process such as electric arc, stored arc or gas arc requires BP CHP Reliability Engineering approval.

**A4.2** Minimum heat input shall be used to attach the pins to pipe and equipment to minimize depth of penetration of the heat affected zone. The method to be used should be tested by striking the pin with a hammer and reducing heat input until the pin connection fails, then increasing heat input just enough to produce a sound weld.

**A4.3** When stud welding on in-service pipe and equipment, the provisions of STP 42B and all Control of Work procedures shall be followed.

**A4.4** When stud welding on code piping and equipment, including piping and equipment subject to PWHT, the welding procedure shall be the capacitor discharge process without PWHT and shall be qualified in accordance with QW-192 of ASME Section IX (Stud Weld Procedure Qualification).

**Note:** An EWR (work process described in STP 42B) is required for all in-service welding (see S&H manual 520 - Section II), and all new and retrofit installations.
