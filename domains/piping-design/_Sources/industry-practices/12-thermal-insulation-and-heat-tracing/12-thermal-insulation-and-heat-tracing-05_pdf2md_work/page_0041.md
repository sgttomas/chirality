### NPS 3 & SMALLER FLANGED VALVES

3/8" TUBING — MAY BE USED FOR WRAPPING NPS 3 VALVES

NOTE: PROVIDE THREE INSULATING SPACERS ON VALVES FOR ISOLATED TRACING ONLY.

TUBE UNION — LOCATE OUTSIDE INSULATION

---

### PLAN VIEW LAYOUT BEFORE WRAPPING AROUND A VALVE

USE MAXIMUM NUMBER OF HAIRPINS AS PERMITTED BY BEND RADIUS

VALVE TRACE

LINES NPS 8 & LARGER REQUIRE TWO TRACERS. 2ND TRACER SHALL BE WRAPPED AROUND END FLANGES AND ALSO WRAPPED ONCE AROUND BONNET ON BONNETED VALVES.

---

### NPS 4 & LARGER VALVES

ISOLATED TRACER: PROVIDE INSULATING SPACERS AT INTERVALS OF TWO FEET ALONG TUBING.

LOOP

TUBE UNIONS LOCATED OUTSIDE OF INSULATION.

LOCATE TUBE UNIONS AT HIGH POINT TO PREVENT CONDENSATE BUILD-UP OUTSIDE OF INSULATION.

---

NOTE: ALL TUBING TO BE 1/2" O.D.
