## TYPICAL DETAILS FOR JACKETED AND TRACED LEVEL GLASS

[PIP logo]

[FIGURE: JACKETED LEVEL GLASS]

**JACKETED LEVEL GLASS**

Steam supply enters via tubing from above, connecting through the jacketed level glass assembly mounted on the vessel. Steam condensate exits to steam trap at the bottom.

Components labeled:
- STEAM
- TUBING
- LEVEL GLASS

[FIGURE: TRACED LEVEL GLASS]

**TRACED LEVEL GLASS**

Steam supply enters via tubing from above, connecting through a steam tracer alongside the level glass assembly mounted on the vessel. Steam condensate exits to steam trap at the bottom via tubing union.

Components labeled:
- STEAM
- TUBING
- STEAM TRACER
- DO NOT INSULATE GLASS
- LEVEL GLASS
- TUBING UNION
