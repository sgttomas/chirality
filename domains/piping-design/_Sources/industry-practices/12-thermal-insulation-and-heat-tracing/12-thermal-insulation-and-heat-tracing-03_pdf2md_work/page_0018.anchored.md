6.5.5 Steam traced piping shall be covered with oversized insulation.

6.5.6 Heat traced piping placed in sleeves under road crossings, or similar installations, shall have the insulation extend 300 mm (12") into the sleeve.

6.5.7 Acoustic insulation where required, shall be applied to avoid noise leakage from the major noise source or the noise transmission path. All openings in the insulation covering shall be completely sealed with loose insulation and/or insulating cement. In addition, all metallic pipe supports, support brackets, support plates, gussets, etc., that are connected to the major noise source or transmission pack shall also be acoustically insulated for a distance up to 3 m (10 ft) as required.

## 6.6 Insulation of Instruments

6.6.1 Insulation shall be installed in accordance with the requirements of Specification J-16.

## 6.7 Expansion Joints

Expansion joints shall be provided taking into account the following:

a) The expansion of the piping or equipment, based on the design temperature of the item.

b) The expansion, contraction, or shrinkage of the insulating material, based on the design temperature of the item.

c) The resilience of the insulation.

d) The method of supporting the insulation.

Seller's design of insulation expansion joints, including their location, and type of insulating weatherproofing material used shall be submitted to purchaser for approval.

## 6.8 Electrically Traced Equipment and Piping

6.8.1 Prior to the application of thermal insulation, the insulation contractor shall verify that the type, inside diameter and thickness of the insulation agrees with that shown on the electrical heat tracing isometric.
