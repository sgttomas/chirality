6.3.10 Fittings larger than 2" NPS shall be insulated with segments of pipe insulation, and voids packed with ceramic fiber. Alternatively, segmented premolded fittings may be used on an economical basis.

6.3.11 Pipe fittings up to 12" NPS shall be weatherproofed with two piece stamped metal covers. Fittings larger than 12" NPS shall be protected with interlocking gore elbows.

6.3.12 Tie wires or banding used for piping insulation shall be located on 230 mm (9") centers. Wires shall be properly tightened, have loose ends cut off and twisted ends bent down flush with the insulation.

6.3.13 Jacketing shall be overlapped a minimum of 76 mm (3") and positioned to shed water. To allow for thermal expansion and movement of jacket sections, screws shall not be used in the end laps of jackets.

6.3.14 Where continuous insulation of piping at support points is specified, the insulation shall be of the load bearing type capable of carrying the piping load.

## 6.4 Insulation of Equipment

6.4.1 No permanent insulation shall be provided for the following:

- a) Vessel manhole nozzles and covers
- b) Vessel nozzles and flanges
- c) Exchanger girth and piping flanges

A customized insulation system, easily removed and replaced shall be provided -- where specified on the Equipment Insulation Schedule; it shall extend a minimum of 50 mm (2") over adjacent jacketing.

6.4.2 Vessel equipment shall be covered with a weatherproofing jacket as specified in Section 4.7 and as indicated in the Equipment Insulation Schedule. Jacketing shall be held in place by banding spaced on 460 mm (18") centers maximum, and on vertical vessels the bands shall be supported by 'u' clips every 1100 mm (4 ft) with a minimum of 4 per band.

6.4.3 Corrugated sheets shall overlap by 76 mm (3") or two corrugations. If flat sheets are used the sheets shall overlap a minimum of 76 mm (3") and an expansion joint shall be provided as specified in Standard Drawing N-514.
