- **6.4.4** For equipment less than 3 m (10 ft) diameter and operating at a temperature of 150°C (300°F) or less, and insulated with mineral wool, installation of breather springs is not required. All equipment greater than 3 m (10 ft) diameter or with an operating temperature in excess of 150°C (300°F) shall have breather springs installed. Quantity of breather springs to be used per band shall be such that no single spring is extended more than 50 mm (2") by the expansion of the equipment.

- **6.4.5** Insulation on vessel heads shall be installed as per details in Standard Drawings C-531, N-505, N-506, N-509, N-511, or N-515, as applicable.

- **6.4.6** Each layer of insulation on vessels shall be secured to the shell by stainless steel bands or studs and pins. The spacing of bands for single layer insulation shall be 300 mm (12"). The spacing for inner layers of multi-layer insulations shall be 460 mm (18") and 300 mm (12") for the outer layer. Where bands are impractical due to an irregular surface, 16 ga stainless steel wire shall be used at 230 mm (9") centers.

- **6.4.7** The Seller must advise the Purchaser as to what provisions have been made for both horizontal and vertical thermal expansion of tanks or vessels.

- **6.4.8** Insulation on tank roofs shall be a rigid type suitable for light foot traffic.

- **6.4.9** Edges of insulation openings such as around manholes, nozzles, cutouts for stud removal, pipe supports, and other attachments, shall be sealed to prevent water from entering. Metal covers shall be used if temperatures are beyond the usable ranges of weatherproofing coatings.

- **6.4.10** Equipment nameplates shall be insulated. Insulation surrounding these plates shall be sealed to prevent water from entering.

## 6.5 Insulation of Machinery and Other Irregular Surfaces

- **6.5.1** Irregularly shaped equipment to be insulated shall use either rigid type prefabricated sections or resilient type blanket insulation.

- **6.5.2** Insulation for pumps and turbines, where required, may be of the removable box type. The fabricated box shall have the interior surface lined with resilient type mineral fibre insulation.

- **6.5.3** The thickness of insulation on pumps, compressors and turbines including inlet and outlet flanges, shall be the same as that required for the inlet piping.

- **6.5.4** Steam ends of reciprocating pumps shall be insulated to permit the covers to be removed without damaging the insulation.
