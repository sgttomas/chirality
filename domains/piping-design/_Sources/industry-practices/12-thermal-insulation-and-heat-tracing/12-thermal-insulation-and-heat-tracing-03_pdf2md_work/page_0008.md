- 4.7.1 Aluminum sheet weather jacketing for piping and equipment shall be in compliance with ASTM B 209. Alloy and temper of aluminum sheet shall be 3003 - H14 with the exception of two piece, die-formed aluminum elbow covers which shall be 1100 - 0, dead soft.

- 4.7.2 Aluminum piping jackets shall have an exterior "stucco-embossed" finish and have a factory applied epoxy-polyester enamel coating. This coating shall have a minimum file thickness of .015 mm (0.6 mils). The enamel coating shall face the insulation. Sheet shall be 0.4 mm (0.016") thick.

- 4.7.3 Stainless steel weather jacketing for piping and equipment shall be in compliance with ASTM A 480, and shall be supplied in grade 304 (unless otherwise stated) tempered to soft anneal and a 2 "D" (dull) finish. The sheet thickness shall be 28 ga (0.0156"). Stainless steel jacketing requires no moisture barrier.

- 4.7.4 Metal jackets for vessels and column may be flat or corrugated. When the diameter of the vessel or column is up to 7.3 m (24 ft) the corrugations shall be on a 32 mm (1-1/4") pitch. When the diameter is larger than 7.3 m (24 ft), the corrugations shall be on 54 mm (2-1/2") pitch.

## 4.8 Bands, Seals and Breather Springs

- 4.8.1 Banding for piping and equipment shall be 0.5 mm (0.020") thick, 300 series stainless steel conforming with ASTM A 480. Banking shall have a smooth finish with edges free from burrs. Width of bands for piping shall be 12.7 mm (1/2") for lines 12" NPS to 24" NPS inclusive and 19 mm (3/4") for larger lines. Width of bands for equipment shall be 19 mm (3/4") or as indicated on the appropriate Standard Drawing.

- 4.8.2 Finger strap banding shall have fingers 50 mm (2") long by 19 mm (3/4") wide by 0.5 mm (0.020") thick and 230 mm (9") on center.

- 4.8.3 Seals shall be 300 series stainless steel, heavy duty, wing type conforming with ASTM A 167.

- 4.8.4 Breather Springs shall be 300 series stainless steel, with tensile strength of 1,900 MPa (275 psi), 100 mm (4") long, Techaloy, or equal approved by Purchaser.
