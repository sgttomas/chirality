#### 4.13.2 High Temperature Fabrication Adhesive

Fabrication adhesive shall be suitable for adhering dense high temperature insulation such as calcium silicate to itself and to nonporous surfaces. It shall be formulated for application with heating.

## 4.14 Flexible Removable Insulation Covers

- **4.14.1** Where specified, flexible removable insulation covers shall be used for valves, fittings, manways, exchangers, pumps, etc., and shall be O'Brien Flexpak design or equal, approved by the Purchaser.

- **4.14.2** Flexible removable insulation covers are defined as prefabricated, custom fit over designed and fabricated specifically for the items requiring insulation.

- **4.14.3** Jacket material shall be teflon coated fiberglass type 1 (Chemfab g - Guard 14D 14 ounces per square yard) or equal.

- **4.14.4** Liner materials shall be as follows:

  Up to 500°F - same as 4.14.3 above.

  501°F to 1000°F - knitted stainless steel wire mesh, 0.011 inches diameter, 60 density mesh size (shall not be used in hydrocarbon, acid or hazardous services).

- **4.14.5** Insulation material and thickness shall be as follows:

  Operating temperature 250°F to 400°F Manville Spingles MTB or equal with stainless steel quilt pins and insulation thickness 1".

  Operating temperature 401°F to 1000°F Pittsburgh Corning Temp-Mat, or equal with stainless steel quilt pins, insulation thickness 1" up to 500°F 2" - 501°F to 1000°F.
