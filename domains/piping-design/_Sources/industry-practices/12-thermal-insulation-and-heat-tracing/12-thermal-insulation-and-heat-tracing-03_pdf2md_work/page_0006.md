4.2.4 Acceptance of the insulating materials chemical composition and their effects on SCC of the stainless steel shall be per ASTM C 795.

## 4.3 Insulation Materials for Piping

4.3.1 Preformed rigid piping insulation shall be furnished in standard lengths with ends cut square. Dimensions shall conform to ASTM C 585. Rigid block insulation shall be furnished true and square in compliance with ASTM C 550.

4.3.2 Insulation for piping shall be sectional, segmental, beveled lag, or scored block.

4.3.3 Insulation for fittings, flanges, and valves may be field fabricated, shop fabricated, or molded. All insulation shall be in compliance with ASTM C 450. Insulation thickness shall match the adjacent insulation.

4.3.4 Materials of insulation for piping shall meet the requirements of the applicable ASTM specification.

- a) C 533 Calcium Silicate Block and Pipe Thermal Insulation

- b) C 547 Mineral Fiber Preformed Pipe Insulation
  Class 2: for Mineral Wool (8-10 lb/ft3)
  Class 2: for Fiberglass (6-8 lb/ft3)

- c) C 552 Cellular Glass Block and Pipe Thermal Insulation

4.3.5 The minimum compressive strength of calcium silicate insulation shall be 0.97 MPa (140 psi) to produce 5% compression as per ASTM C 165.

## 4.4 Insulation Materials for Equipment

4.4.1 Insulation for cylindrical shells of equipment shall be sectional or segmental pipe insulation, bevelled lags, scored block or rigid block.

4.4.2 Insulation for vessels, exchangers and tanks shall meet the requirements of the following ASTM Specifications:

- a) C 533 Calcium Silicate Block and Pipe Thermal Insulation

- b) C 612 Mineral Fiber Block and Board Thermal Insulation
  (Class 3 or Class 4)

- c) C 449 Mineral Fiber Hydraulic - Settling Thermal Insulating and Finishing Cement
