7.2.1 Compliance with governing specifications shall be checked by the Inspector during the course of installation of the insulation system.

7.2.2 Work shall not proceed with the next step in the insulation system sequence (i.e., insulation, weather barrier application, caulking, etc.) until the previous work has been inspected and approved.
