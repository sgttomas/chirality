## 5.2 Storage of Insulation Materials

Proper warehousing or other weather protected storage shall be provided for materials and accessory items. Cartons of insulation and accessories shall be stacked in such a manner that cartons in lower levels will not be compressed, damaging the material within those cartons. Containers shall be stacked in the manner recommended by the manufacturer.

## 5.3 Storage of Mastics

Plastic containers of mastic shall not be stacked over two high upon each other to prevent collapse and ultimate loss of material. Mastic shall be stored in protected areas so that temperature is never below 5°C (40°F).

## 5.4 Storage of Metallic Components

Aluminum sheets, or coils or other metals, shall be protected from rapid changes of temperature to prevent oxidation or other staining (discoloration) film forming on surface.

# 6.0 INSTALLATION OF INSULATION

## 6.1 General

6.1.1 All surfaces to be insulated shall be presented to the insulation installer free of all contaminants, including oil, grease, dirt, etc. Where surfaces require coating in accordance with painting Specifications X-1 they shall be applied by others and fully cured prior to application of insulation. All piping joints (including welded joints) and welded joints of field fabricated equipment shall be left unpainted and uninsulated until after completion of field pressure testing.

6.1.2 Austenitic stainless steel equipment surfaces that have been exposed to salt spray during the period of transportation, field storage, or construction shall be washed with water containing less than 500 ppm of chloride ions prior to application of the insulation. Syncrude shall determine the necessity for, and shall be, responsible for performing any required washing operations. Insulation contractor shall ensure that surfaces are completely dry before commencing installation of insulation.

6.1.3 Insulation and accessory materials, other than cements and mastics, shall be applied only if dry. Stored insulation that has become wet or damaged shall be replaced at the discretion of the Purchaser.
