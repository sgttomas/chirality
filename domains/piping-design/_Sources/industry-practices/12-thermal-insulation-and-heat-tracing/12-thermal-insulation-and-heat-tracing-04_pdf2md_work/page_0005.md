## 6.0 DESIGN

### 6.1 General

#### 6.1.1

The type, thickness, and extent of insulation shall be specified on Equipment Insulation Schedules for equipment and Line Designation Tables (LDT) and piping isometrics for piping where heat conservation, process stability, noise attenuation, winterization, fireproofing, heat tracing and protection of personnel from heat are required.

#### 6.1.2

Insulation shall be specified with the following insulation code for each line and piece of equipment, with a bracket after each line designation or under the equipment number. The insulation code is illustrated as follows:

**Insulation Code:**

```
(xx  x  X  x)
  |  |  |  +-- Jacketing
  |  |  +----- Material
  |  +-------- Thickness
  +----------- Function Code
```
