6.2.7 Insulation over valves and flanges (and manholes when specified to be insulated) shall be customized assembly for easy removal and replacement. It shall extend a minimum of 50mm (2 inches) over adjacent jacketing.

6.2.8 Seal pots, vent chambers and drip pots shall be insulated only if they are heat traced.

6.2.9 Instruments and associated piping shall be insulated according to Specification J-16.

6.2.10 Inverted bucket type steam traps shall be insulated if installed outside.

6.3 Items not Insulated

Insulation systems designed for heat conservation shall have the insulation omitted from the following surfaces:

6.3.1 Piping or equipment components designed on the basis of reduced metal temperature resulting from heat loss to atmosphere.

6.3.2 Piping or equipment which are insulated internally.

6.3.3 Water side surfaces of coolers and condensers.

6.3.4 Cooling water piping, except bypass which shall be traced and insulated.

6.3.5 Steam traps (except inverted bucket type), expansion joints, rotating joints.

6.3.6 Body flanges of heat exchangers.

6.3.7 Unless specified to be fireproofed, or insulated to afford process protection against the effects of ambient temperature (viscosity change, condensation, etc), for steam service or for noise control, insulation shall also be omitted from the following:

a) Pumps turbines and compressors at operating temperatures below 120°C (250°F).

b) Flanged fittings, unions.
