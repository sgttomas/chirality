6.2.2 Unless specified otherwise, internally insulated equipment shall not be externally insulated for personnel protection, even though their surface temperatures exceed 60°C (140°F). Where personnel protection is required, guards, shields, or railings shall be provided.

6.2.3 Acoustic service insulation systems shall be used on external surfaces of equipment and piping as required by the limits set in Specifications A-4 and/or A-9

6.2.4 Items requiring insulation for noise control shall be insulated with fibrous glass for temperatures up to 204°C (400°F) and Mineral Fiber with phenolic binder from 204°C (400°F) to 454°C (850°F).

6.2.5 Valves and flanges shall not be insulated in hydrogen service or in the following toxic services: caustic, chlorine, ammonium polysulphide, carbon disulphide, toluene, Diethanolamine, Benfield solution, sulphuric acid or hydrogen sulphide. The contractor shall review the toxicity of the material to be handled with the Syncrude Industrial Hygiene group if further clarification is required.
