## 6.4 Insulation and Weatherproofing for Electrical Heat Tracing Systems

This section covers the mandatory additional requirements for the design of insulation and weatherproofing for EHT systems and is intended to supplement the requirements of the previous paragraphs of this specification.

**6.4.1** No equipment or fitting whatever is permitted to be left bare, even though this may be inconvenient for maintenance or operations. The only exception shall be due to safety concerns.

**6.4.2** Unless prohibited by paragraph 6.2.5 all flanges shall be completely insulated.

**6.4.3** Insulation on valves shall cover the valve bonnet, unless in services listed in paragraph 6.2.5.

**6.4.4** Equipment that must have the insulation removed for maintenance and/or operations purposes shall have removable enclosures, sectionalized with latch type fasteners, or alternatively, blanket type custom enclosures with Velcro fasteners. Enclosures shall be designed with gaskets or other means to prevent the ingress of water and to facilitate the repeated removal and reinstallation without deterioration.

**6.4.5** Additional insulation thickness shall be provided for enclosures on valves/flanges/pumps etc. due to the increased heat loss over the larger surface area of the enclosure. On large enclosures (e.g. on pumps) with large air voids inside the enclosure, the additional insulation shall be applied directly on the traced equipment to concentrate the heat from the electric tracing cables. Foil back fiberglass insulation with tie wire is acceptable for this purpose.

**6.4.6** All pipes having skin effect or steam tracing shall have their insulation oversized by 25 mm (1 inch). In the event that dual tube skin effect tracing is selected, the insulation shall be oversized by 50 mm (2 inches).
