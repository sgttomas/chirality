6.4.7 Oversized insulation shall be specified for all heat traced piping. Where paragraph 6.4.6 does not apply, insulation over-sizing shall be as follows;

[TABLE: Insulation oversizing requirements by material and pipe size]

| Insulation Material | Pipe Sizes | Insulation Oversize (gap between OD of pipe and ID of Insulation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mineral Fiber | NPS 8 & Smaller | ½ inch |
| Mineral Fiber | NPS 10 & Larger | None |
| Calcium Silicate | All pipe sizes | ½ inch |
| Perlite | All pipe sizes | ½ inch |
