## 1.0 SCOPE

1.1 This specification covers the design criteria for the hot service thermal insulation, acoustic insulation on piping and equipment such as vessels, heat exchangers, drums, small diameter tanks, heaters, pumps, compressors, turbines and filters, etc.

1.2 The use of insulation for instrumentation, heating, ventilating and air conditioning systems (HVAC), and internally refractory lined piping and equipment is not within the scope of this specification.

1.3 Hot service insulation system shall be considered for:

- Conservation of heat.
- Protection of personnel from heat.
- Maintenance of temperature for process stability.
- Protection of process fluids from freezing (winterization)
- Conservation of heat for heat traced systems (Reference –Specs L-10 and P-24).
- Insulation systems used as fireproofing.
- Noise attenuation

## 2.0 ABBREVIATIONS AND DEFINITIONS

2.1 Abbreviations

ETI - Economic Thickness of Insulation

LDT - Line Designation Table

P&ID - Process and Instrument Diagram

2.2 Definitions

2.2.1 MINERAL FIBER – Mineral Wool

2.2.2 PIPE/PIPING – Cylindrical shapes with outside diameters of 24 inch NPS or smaller which can be insulated with preformed sectional pipe insulation materials. Subject to the size limitation of 24 inches outside diameter, this category includes piping with or without heat tracing, pipe fittings, valves, flanges, in-line pipe accessories, vessels, heat exchangers, piping, and process equipment includes attachments such as nozzles.

2.2.3 FLANGES – Flanges of PIPE or Equipment.
