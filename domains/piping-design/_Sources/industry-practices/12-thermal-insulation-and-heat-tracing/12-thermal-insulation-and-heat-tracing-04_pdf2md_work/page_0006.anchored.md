## 6.2 Insulation Requirements

**6.2.1** The basic requirements for insulating equipment and piping are defined as follows:

a) Heat Conservation – Economically optimum insulation based on insulation and energy costs.

b) Process Stability – Insulation required to control process temperature for stable process operation.

c) Winterization – The protection of piping and equipment against the effect of low ambient temperature including hydrate formation, freezing and pour point suppression.

d) Fireproofing for equipment when specified as defined in Specification UE-1 N-3.

e) Personnel Protection – Insulation shall generally be applied to hot surfaces operating above 60°C (140°F) which can be contacted by operating personnel in normal performances of their duties. Such hot surfaces shall be insulated to a height 2m (6 feet – 6 inches) above normal walking or standing surfaces and within 0.8m (2 feet), measured horizontally, from stairways, ramps, or ladders.
