6.6.2 For traced applications, insulation thickness will be based on the HOLD or personnel protection temperatures shown on the LDT's and NOT the normal operating temperature, unless the heat conservation insulation thickness required by the normal operating temperature is thicker.

6.6.3 ETI required for heat conservation shall be in accordance with Table 3 and 4 of this specification. Insulation thickness for winterization or heat traced piping shall be in accordance with Table 5 and 6. Where process stability requires a greater thickness of insulation than that listed in the tables then the required thickness shall be calculated by the Process Engineer.

6.6.4 When insulation function is used both for heat conservation and heat tracing or process stability and heat tracing, insulation thickness shall be the thicker of that required for heat conservation based on normal operating temperature or that required for heat tracing based on the hold temperature.

6.6.5 The type of insulation (calcium silicate or mineral fiber) shall be selected based on the normal operating temperature.

6.6.6 The thickness of insulation required for personnel protection shall be in accordance with Table 2. If the requirement of heat conservation is associated with personnel protection, the thickness of insulation will be determined by the more stringent requirement.

6.6.7 When a dual insulation system is used for combined thermal and acoustic control, the insulation for acoustic control shall be installed as the outer layer. No credit for noise reduction may be taken for any insulation material in the system other than the fibrous glass or mineral fiber unless approved by the Purchaser.
