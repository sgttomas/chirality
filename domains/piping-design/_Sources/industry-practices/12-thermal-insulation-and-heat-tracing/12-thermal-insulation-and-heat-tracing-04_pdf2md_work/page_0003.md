2.2.4 EQUIPMENT – Cylindrical shapes of more than 24 inches outside diameter for which preformed sectional pipe insulation materials are not suitable. This category includes items such as pressure vessels, heat exchangers, piping and process equipment including attachments such as nozzles.

2.2.5 OPERATING TEMPERATURE in this specification refers to the internal fluid or material temperature operating conditions as noted on the P&IDs, LDT and/or drawings. Where a range is noted for the operating temperature, the upper limit of the range shall be considered as the operating temperature for hot service. Temperatures reached during upset conditions and steam-out temperature shall not be considered as the operating temperature. Steam-out temperature and heat tracer temperature shall not be considered as a basis for the insulation thickness selection for traced pipe and equipment.
