- when indicated by specifications, line lists, or drawings; and
- for all P No. 5 materials regardless of thickness, except for socket welds in sizes NPS 2 and smaller, and non-pressure containing fillet welds with throats not exceeding 12.7 mm (1/2").

4.8 Maximum hardness shall be:

- 200 HB for all P No. 1 materials; and
- in accordance with applicable industry codes, standards, and specifications for all other materials. Also refer to PES-5008 Welding specification for maximum production hardness in P.6.3.

4.9 When PWHT is required for pressure vessels or heat transfer equipment, it shall be completed as a single unit in a furnace of adequate capacity, unless prior written approval has been obtained from the OWNER. When it is not possible to PWHT pressure vessels or heat transfer equipment as a single unit, the fabricator shall address the effect of aggregate times a temperature for areas subject to multiple PWHT cycles, to the satisfaction of the OWNER.

4.10 PWHT procedures for lined or clad piping or equipment shall be approved by the OWNER. Consideration shall be given to venting requirements for lined piping or equipment.

## 5.0 Production Welding

5.1 On pressure equipment having skirts, no tack welds are permitted on the inside of the skirt between the head and the skirt.

5.2 For double-welded joints, the backside of the joint shall be cleaned to sound metal before welding the back side. See Owner's Specification 000-PES-5009 NDE Examination for specific NDE requirements prior to welding the back side.

5.3 For heat transfer equipment, when full penetration groove welds are required and access or high preheat requirements do not permit double welded joints, the root pass of single welded joints shall be made with the GMAW or GTAW processes.

GMAW-sc. (short-circuiting mode of metal transfer) process can only be used in flat and rolled-on positions.

## 6.0 Workmanship, Quality Control, Nondestructive Examination, and Repairs

6.1 Additional NDE requirements are contained in Owner's Specification 000-PES-5009 NDE Examination.

6.2 Arc strikes and arc burns shall be:

- removed from pressure vessels and heat transfer equipment by grinding or filing;
- removed from the outside surface of process piping systems and process coils by grinding, filing, or cut out as a cylinder; and
- removed by cut out as a cylinder if present on the inside surface of sour process piping systems or process coils, except that subsequent heat treatment of the affected area may be applied as a remedy, subject to compliance with minimum wall thickness requirements and approval from the OWNER.

6.3 When arc strikes or arc burns are removed by grinding or filing, the OWNER may require verification of removal using a suitable etching technique, verification of minimum wall thickness by ultrasonic methods, and magnetic particle examination of the affected area for evidence of cracks. Cracks are unacceptable regardless of size.
