Welding of Process Piping and Pressure Equipment

## 1.0 Introduction

### 1.1 Scope

1.1.1 This specification supplements requirements contained in the main body of the PES 5008 specification, and covers welding and postweld heat treatment of process piping, pressure vessels, and heat transfer equipment. Nondestructive Examination requirements are contained in Owner's Specification 000-PES-5009 NDE Examination.

### 1.2 Reference Documents

1.2.1 All welding and postweld heat treatment shall satisfy the minimum requirements of the latest edition of governing codes, standards, and specifications including:

- ASME Section IX.
- ASME B31.3.
- ASME Section VIII, Division 1.
- Applicable API standards.

## 2.0 General and Administrative Requirements

2.1 ASME welding procedures shall be registered by appropriate regulatory authorities.

## 3.0 Welding Qualifications

3.1 Each welder and welding operator shall be qualified to ASME IX for each welding process to be used in production welding.

## 4.0 Technical Requirements

4.1 The finished edges of plates thicker than 12.5 mm (1/2") shall be examined for lamination using MT or UT methods. Indications shall not exceed the limits of ASME SA-20, Table A2.14.

4.2 Low-hydrogen welding consumables shall be used on all pressure retaining welds, with the exception of the root bead and hot pass of P No. 1 piping materials, which may be welded with cellulose coated electrodes.

4.3 Welds requiring notch toughness shall be welded using filler metals with notch toughness proven at the minimum design temperature (i.e., use of E7018-1 rather than E7018 when impact testing is required of P No. 1 steels).

4.4 Preheat for pressure piping shall satisfy the minimum requirements of ASME B31.3. Recommended preheat within ASME B31.3 shall be treated as a mandatory requirement.

4.5 Preheat for pressure vessels and heat transfer equipment shall satisfy the minimum requirements of ASME VIII, Division 1, Appendix R, or ASME VIII, Division 2, Appendix D, as applicable. Recommended preheat within the applicable divisions of ASME VIII shall be treated as a mandatory requirement.

4.6 For ASME welding procedures, unless otherwise specified on the WPS, the preheat temperature shall also be considered as the minimum interpass temperature. The minimum interpass temperature shall be maintained throughout the welding operation.
