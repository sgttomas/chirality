Welding of Process Piping and Pressure Equipment

6.4 The fabricator/contractor shall provide the OWNER with all information necessary to verify that the quality control, detailed examinations and tests required by governing industry codes, standards, and specifications have been performed at the stages of fabrication necessary to permit them to be meaningful.

6.5 Pressure vessel production impact test plates shall be made when impact testing is required. The production impact test plates shall be made for each qualified welding procedure as per ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII, Division 1, UG-84(i). Results of production impact tests shall be incorporated into the fabrication records and submitted to the OWNER.

## 7.0 Sour Service Requirements

7.1 All welding and materials for sour service shall meet the requirements of NACE MR0175.
7.2 Butt, tee and corner joints attaching non-pressure parts to pressure parts on the process side of any pressure vessels or heat transfer equipment shall be made using full penetration groove welds.
7.3 Butt, tee and corner joints attaching non-pressure parts to pressure parts on the outside surface of any pressure vessels or heat transfer equipment shall be made using full penetration groove welds. Lap type joints, such as those used for reinforcing pads, poison pads, and lifting lugs, shall be made using continuous fillet welds, and the interface between the parts shall be vented to prevent accumulation of hydrogen.
