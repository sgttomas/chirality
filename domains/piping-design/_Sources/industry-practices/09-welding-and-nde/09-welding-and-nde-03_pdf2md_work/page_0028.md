# Corporate Technical Standard

Department: **PROJECT SERVICES** | Number: **0903**
Subject: **SHOP AND FIELD WELDING REQUIREMENTS** | Revision: **7**

## C3.0 WELDING (including weld overlay, clad restoration and repairs)

C3.1 All production welds, including tack welds and tube-to-tubesheet welds, shall be made with addition of filler metal. Autogenous welding (that is, welding without the addition of filler metal) is not allowed.

C3.2 Plasma arc welding (PAW) and Flux cored arc welding (FCAW) shall not be used.

C3.3 The following consumables shall be used unless otherwise authorized by the Purchaser.

- SMAW: SFA 5.4 E2209.
- GTAW/GMAW: SFA 5.9 ER2209.
- SAW: SFA 5.9 ER2209. Welding shall be with a flux designed for Duplex stainless steel.

C3.4 Heat input shall be in the range of 12.5 to 62.5 kJ/in (0.5 to 2.5 KJ/mm).

C3.5 Maximum interpass temperature shall be 350°F (177°C).

C3.6 When back gouging and welding on the reverse side are not possible, the root pass shall be made with GTAW root pass and back purged until at least 1/4" (6 mm) of weld metal thickness has been deposited. Back purge gas shall be a welding grade of one of the following:

- 90% nitrogen/10%/hydrogen mixture,
- pure nitrogen, or
- argon/nitrogen mixture with not less than 5% nitrogen.

The oxygen content of the back-purged volume shall be less than 2500 ppm.

## C4.0 WELDING PROCEDURE SPECIFICATION AND QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

C4.1 Essential variables for procedure qualification shall be in accordance with ASME Section IX with the addition of the following supplementary essential variables:

a. notch toughness (ASTM A923 test method B)

b. heat input: any change greater than +/-10%

c. interpass temperature: any increase in the maximum interpass temperature

d. filler metal: any change in consumable type including manufacturer, trade name and flux. Welding procedures shall be qualified with the same consumables manufacturer and trade names to be used in production

e. back purge gas composition

f. Welding of components with wall thicknesses that deviate by more than 20% from that of the PQR specimen is not permitted.
