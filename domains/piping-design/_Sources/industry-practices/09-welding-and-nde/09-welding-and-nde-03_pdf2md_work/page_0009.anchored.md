## 5.3. Welding Performance Qualification

5.3.1. Welders and welding operators employed in shop fabrication, field erection, and alteration and repair shall be qualified to the requirements of ASME Section IX for each welding process they are to follow.

## 5.4. Welding Process and Filler Metals Limitations

5.4.1. The following processes and filler metals are allowed, subject to the listed limitations:

5.4.1.1. Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)

a. The roots of single-sided, full penetration butt welds shall be welded with Exx10 or Exx11 electrodes.

b. E7024 electrodes shall limited to welding tank roof and tank floor plates. Tank floors shall be welded with minimum two passes. Low hydrogen electrodes shall be used for annular rings, shell courses, and tank bottom to shell welds. For annular rings in tanks, single-sided full penetration welds shall be made with a permanent backing strip.

c. E6012, E6013, E7014, and E7020 electrodes shall not be used.

d. Electrode diameters 3/16" and over shall be restricted to the flat position only (1G).

5.4.1.2. Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)

a. Back purge gas:

i. Shall be applied for two layers or 3/8" (9.5 mm) minimum weld deposit on pipe and tube butt-welds for:

- 2¼ Cr and higher chrome-molys, stainless steels, Nickel based alloys,
- lube oil lines, hydraulic lines, instrument lines, steam inlet lines to steam turbines and fired heater tubes.

b. Autogenous welding (without filler metal) shall not be permitted.

c. Purge gases shall not be substituted with welding techniques or flux-coated wire.
