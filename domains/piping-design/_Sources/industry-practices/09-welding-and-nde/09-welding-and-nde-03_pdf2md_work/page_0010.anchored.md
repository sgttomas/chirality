# Corporate Technical Standard

Department: **PROJECT SERVICES** | Number: **0903**
Subject: **SHOP AND FIELD WELDING REQUIREMENTS** | Revision: **7**

5.4.1.3. General Requirements for All Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)

a. Applicable materials: carbon steel, low alloy, austenitic stainless steel (300 series).

b. GMAW-SC shall be limited to root pass welding only.

c. GMAW, using globular, spray or pulse modes of transfer, shall be limited to fill passes only.

d. GMAW shall be limited to shop use only.

e. Pipe less than NPS 4" shall be done in the 1G flat rolled position (plus/minus 15 degrees of top dead center). Branch connections shall be done in 2G horizontal position.

f. Back purge shall be applied for two layers or 3/8" (9.5 mm) minimum weld deposit on pipe and tube butt-welds for:

- 2¼ Cr and higher chrome-molys, stainless steels

- lube oil lines, hydraulic lines, instrument lines, steam inlet lines to steam turbines and fired heater tubes

5.4.1.4. Requirements for Gas Metal Arc Welding – Modified Wave Short Circuit (GMAW– MWSC) and Gas Metal Arc Welding – Pulsed (GMAW-p)

a. GMAW-MWSC shall be limited to root pass welding only.

b. Only a inverter power source shall be used with GMAW-MWSC and GMAW-p processes. Production welding power sources shall be of the same makes and models as those applied in PQR's.

c. Production welding shall utilize the same wires (consumable manufacturer and filler metal classification), shielding gases, shielding gas flow rate range, and gas cup size as was used in the PQR.

d. Welders shall pass a root bend test prior to production welding which shall be on material of the same P # and using the same WPS as to be used in production. The test weld shall be subject to a full volumetric inspection by UT (RT for austenitic material) prior to the bend test.

e. PMI shall be completed on all low alloy and stainless steel consumables per Suncor Standard 0213.

f. GMAW-MWSC may be used to weld low alloys and stainless steels without backing gas subject to the following requirements:

i. Tri-mix gases are required (eg. 85% - 92% Helium, 4.5% - 10% Argon, 2.5% - 3.5% CO2).

ii. High silicon wires (eg. ER-308L-Si; ER-347-Si) shall be used for austenitic materials.
