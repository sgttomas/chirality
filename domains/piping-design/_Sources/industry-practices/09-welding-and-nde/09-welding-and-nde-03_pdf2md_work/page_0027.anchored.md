# Corporate Technical Standard

Department: **PROJECT SERVICES** | Number: **0903**
Subject: **SHOP AND FIELD WELDING REQUIREMENTS** | Revision: **7**

## APPENDIX C – DUPLEX STAINLESS STEELS

The following requirements are supplementary to the requirements of Suncor Standard 0903:

These requirements are limited to the following grades of duplex stainless steel: S31803 and S32205 for wrought products and J93380 and J92205 for castings. Excluded is 2507 grade of duplex.

## C1.0 MATERIAL GRADE

Material shall be supplied in accordance with the following specifications:

- Plate: ASME SA-240, UNS S31803 or UNS S32205
- Forgings: ASME SA-182 grade F51 (UNS S31803) or grade F60 (UNS S32205).
- Seamless pipe: ASME SA-790, UNS S31803
- Tubing: ASME SA-789, UNS S31803
- Casting: ASME A351 J93380 (CD3MWCuN) or ASTM A890 grade 4A, J92205 (CD3MN)

## C2.0 MATERIALS REQUIREMENTS

Materials shall also satisfy the following requirements:

**C2.1** Certified Materials Test Report required.

**C2.2** Materials shall be supplied in the solution annealed and bright-annealed condition. If solution Annealing is not carried out in an argon atmosphere (inert), materials shall be white pickled. Materials shall be liquid quenched immediately after solution annealing

**C2.3** Nitrogen content shall be in the range of 0.14 to 0.20%

**C2.4** Ferrite content shall be 35 to 65 vol%

**C2.5** Each heat of material shall be tested per ASTM A923 Test method B (Charpy V-notch test) to ensure adequate corrosion resistance. Acceptance Criteria per table 2 of ASTM A923. For product forms that cannot be evaluated by the Charpy V-notch test, Test Method C (Ferric Chloride corrosion test) of ASTM A923 shall be used. The rapid screening tests included in ASTM A923 (test method A) shall not be used to accept material in lieu of test methods B or C.

**C2.6** Welded tubing shall receive both hydrostatic testing and nondestructive electric testing in accordance with ASME SA-789

**C2.7** No weld repairs to tubes are permitted.

**C2.8** Tube bends shall be solution annealed after bending at 1870°F (1021°C) to 2010°F (1099°C) (in an inert gas atmosphere) for sufficient time to obtain the required duplex structure and then water quenched or inert gas quenched. ID of tubes shall be purged with Argon during hot bending and solution annealing. OD of tubes shall be white pickled (chemical descaling) followed by a neutralization and rinsing treatment after solution annealing.
