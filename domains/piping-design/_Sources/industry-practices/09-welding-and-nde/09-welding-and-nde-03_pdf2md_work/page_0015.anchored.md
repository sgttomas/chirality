## 5.9. Inspection and Workmanship

5.9.1. When PWHT is required, all final NDE to prove the integrity of welds, except for ferrite testing, shall be performed after the final PWHT.

5.9.2. All NDE procedures shall be in full compliance with ASME Section V and the acceptance/rejection criteria of the applicable code(s).

5.9.3. When piping is fabricated with 5% or 10% radiography, such radiography shall be performed progressively throughout the course of fabrication and shall cover the specified percentage per welder's product per line class.

5.9.4. Arc strikes shall be removed by grinding, followed by Wet Fluorescent Magnetic Particle Examination for welds exposed to Stress Corrosion Cracking Services, and dry powder MT or PT for all other welds.
