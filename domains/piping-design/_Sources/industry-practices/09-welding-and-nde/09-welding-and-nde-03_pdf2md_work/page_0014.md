## 5.8. Heat Treatment

5.8.1. Written post weld heat treatment procedures including heating and cooling rates, thermocouple location and its method of attachment, type of heating equipment, etc. shall be developed in all cases, and shall be approved by the Owner or designate prior to proceeding.

5.8.2. A legible time/temperature chart, identifying the component or components heat-treated, shall be signed and dated upon completion of PWHT by the heat treatment operator. Certification shall be provided for the calibration of heat treatment equipment within the previous 6 months.

5.8.3. Post weld heat treatment shall be performed in a furnace whenever practical. Direct flame impingement on the work piece by furnace flames is not permitted. Alternately PWHT may be achieved by the use of gas rings, electrical induction or resistance methods.

5.8.4. For new construction, PWHT is required for welds in ASME Section IX Group P5B and P5C materials without exception. For field maintenance welding, PWHT for welds in ASME Section IX Group P5B and P5C materials can be waived as permitted by the applicable Code, provided the Owner's Engineer conducts a thorough risk assessment in which all of the risks identified in the applicable Code are considered.

5.8.5. Grades 321 and 347 stainless steel base materials welded with grade 347 stainless steel consumables (root and fill) shall receive a stabilizing heat treatment at 1650°F (899°C)for a minimum of 2 hours, if any one of the following conditions are encountered (also see Table 1):

5.8.5.1. Fired heater tubes with wall thicknesses below 1 inch (25 mm), in which the flame side tube metal temperature exceeds 800°F (427°C), or

5.8.5.2. Piping or equipment with wall thicknesses below 1 inch (25 mm), where the operating temperature that exceed 800°F (427°C).
