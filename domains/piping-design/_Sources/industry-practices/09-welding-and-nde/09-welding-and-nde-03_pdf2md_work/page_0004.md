# Corporate Technical Standard

Department: **PROJECT SERVICES** | Number: **0903**
Subject: **SHOP AND FIELD WELDING REQUIREMENTS** | Revision: **7**

- MT: magnetic particle testing
- PT: penetrant testing
- RT: radiographic testing
- UT: ultrasonic testing, specifically angle beam techniques
- PWHT: post weld heat treatment
- FCAW: flux core arc welding
- GMAW: gas metal arc welding
- GTAW: gas tungsten arc welding
- SMAW: shielded metal arc welding
- SAW: submerged arc welding
- RE: responsible engineer
- repair rate for piping: = (no. of joints failed / no. of joints inspected) X 100, reported as a percentage per inspection method applied
- repair rate for equipment: = (total length of weld metal failed / total length of weld metal inspected) X 100, reported as a percentage per inspection method applied
- batch: manufacturers' designation for a heat of wire and mix of flux
- MDMT: minimum design metal temperature
- Dissimilar metal weld: the weld joining two base metals of different alloy compositions by using a filler metal that is compatible with both. It also includes the joining of base metals of the same alloy composition with a compatible filler metal that has a different alloy composition.
- GMAW-MWSC: Gas Metal Arc Welding - Modified Wave Short Circuit; is not an ASME nor AWS welding process designation; is used in this standard to refer to a form of pulsed GMAW capable of transferring single drops of filler metal per pulse
- WPS: Welding Procedure Specification
- PQR: Procedure qualification Record
- NDE: Non-Destructive Examination
