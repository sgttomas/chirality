Department: **PROJECT SERVICES** | Number: **0903**
Subject: **SHOP AND FIELD WELDING REQUIREMENTS** | Revision: **7**

5.9.8. Ferrite Content and Testing for Austenitic Stainless Steels

5.9.8.1. Ferrite testing of 300 series stainless steel is required for welding procedure qualification and production welds.

5.9.8.2. Ferrite testing shall be carried out before any post weld heat treatment.

5.9.8.3. Testing shall be done with a properly calibrated Fischer Ferritescope.

5.9.8.4. Production ferrite testing shall be done on a minimum of 5 percent of the 300 series stainless steel piping welds and a minimum of 10 percent of the 300 series stainless steel fired heater tube welds. Each welding procedure shall be represented.

5.9.8.5. For 300 series stainless vessel or tank butt welds, a minimum of one location per seam shall be tested. One test shall be made for each 10 feet of weld seam. In addition, one test shall be made on each nozzle flange-to-neck and nozzle neck-to-shell/head weld. Each welding procedure shall be represented.

5.9.8.6. The weld metal ferrite number (FN) shall be as follows:

a. 300 series stainless steel weld metal (excluding grade 347 weld metal): 3 to 10

b. Grade 347 stainless steel weld metal exposed to operating temperatures less than 1000°F (538°C): 5 to 10

c. Grade 347 stainless steel weld metal exposed to operating temperatures greater than or equal to 1000°F (538°C): 5 to 8

## 5.10. Weld Repairs

5.10.1. Repairs to defects in welds shall be as follows:

5.10.1.1. All acceptance radiographs for weld repairs shall be made after any PWHT is conducted.

5.10.1.2. Only two repairs are permitted on a location in which a rejectable defect has been identified. If a third repair is required, a detailed repair procedure as well as an explanation of root cause is to be submitted to the Owners Engineer which must be approved in consultation with Suncor before the repair is performed a third time. If the repair procedure is rejected, any subsequent actions are to be approved by the Owners Engineer in consultation with Suncor, which may include weld removal / re-welding.

5.10.1.3. For all welding, should the average repair rate on a per Suncor job/ scope basis reach 4%, an evaluation of root cause is to be completed.

5.10.1.4. If the root cause is determined to be either due to the FCAW or MWSC processes, (i.e., all or the majority of rejectable indications are on welds completed by FCAW process), then the fabricator shall cease all their use and use an alternate welding process resulting in an acceptable repair rate.
