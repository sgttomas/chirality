5.9.6. Method, Location and Extent of Production Hardness Testing:

5.9.6.1. Comparison hardness testers shall be in accordance with ASTM A833.

5.9.6.2. Location and Extent:

a. Location: Weld cap only (readings not required on heat-affected zones

b. Piping: Welds shall include a minimum of 10% of butt welds and a minimum of 10% of fillet welds, per WPS per line class. When locally post weld heat treated, 100% of welds shall be tested. Each location shall be prepared by grinding the weld test area flush with the base metal. Test areas shall be smooth and relatively flat, to facilitate a successful reading.

c. Number of points per location to be:

   i. up to 6" nom. dia.: 2 testing locations (~ 180 degrees to each other)

   ii. 8" and above nom. dia.: 4 testing locations (~ 90 degrees to each other)

d. Equipment: Welds shall include 4 testing locations per longitudinal, circumferential and nozzle welds, per WPS where multiple WPS's applied. The location shall be prepared by grinding the weld test area flush with the base metal. Test areas shall be smooth and relatively flat, to facilitate a successful reading.

e. Attachment Welds: Process-side attachment welds, on equipment or piping requiring production hardness testing, shall also have production hardness testing (even if they are not pressure boundary welds) to the extent of testing as described above.

5.9.7. Results Exceeding Limits:

5.9.7.1. Weld deposits found to exceed the maximum hardness criteria given in Table 2 are unacceptable and shall be reported to the Owner or designate for review. Unless accepted as-is, the weld(s) in question shall be either removed and re-welded, or, heat treated to reduce the hardness to an acceptable value. The specific approach to be used to correct the high hardness condition shall be subject to approval before any corrective action is taken. Regardless of the method of corrective action taken, the weld deposits shall be retested to ensure that the corrective action has resulted in acceptable hardness values.
