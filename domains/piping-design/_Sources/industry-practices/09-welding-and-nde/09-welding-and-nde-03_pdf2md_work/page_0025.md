# Corporate Technical Standard

Department: **PROJECT SERVICES** Number: **0903**
Subject: **SHOP AND FIELD WELDING REQUIREMENTS** Revision: **7**

B8.0 The supplementary requirement for maximum carbon equivalence shall be specified for castings supplied to the following ASME specifications:

SA-216 (S11) and SA-352 (S23).

B9.0 All austenitic castings supplied in accordance with SA-351/SA-351M (except Grades HK, HT, and CT15C), which have been subjected to any weld repairs, shall be given a post weld solution heat treatment in accordance with supplementary requirement S11.

B10.0 The cavity prepared for welding of all major weld repairs shall be inspected by either magnetic particle inspection or liquid penetrant inspection to ensure the cavity is free from defects.

B11.0 As a minimum requirement, all major weld repairs shall be inspected by either magnetic particle inspection or liquid penetrant inspection but in no case shall the type and extent of inspection be less than that used to identify the original defect.

B12.0 Non-destructive examination of all weld repairs shall be performed after the final heat treatment
