## 5.11. Review and Approval Requirements for Contractors

5.11.1. In addition to specific contract specifications compliance, contractors shall submit all documentation to the Buyer who shall forward it to the appropriate Owner's personnel for review and approval. Approval documents shall be returned to the contractor via the Buyer prior to proceeding with fabrication.

5.11.2. For vessels, exchangers, tanks, and pumps, a drawing showing details of each joint or each class of joints with welding symbols, WPS # and NDE used shall be submitted.

5.11.3. For piping, a summary detailing welding and inspection per piping class (see Attachment A, Piping Fabrication, Welding Procedure Specifications and Inspection Summary), shall be completed. This form, or Buyer approved alternate, shall be submitted with the WPS/PQR.
