# Corporate Technical Standard

Department: **PROJECT SERVICES** | Number: **0903**
Subject: **SHOP AND FIELD WELDING REQUIREMENTS** | Revision: **7**

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1. This standard, and its appendices, establishes the welding, heat treatment and inspection requirements for shop and field fabrication, erection, alteration and repair of pressure vessels, piping systems, boilers, heaters, heat transfer equipment, storage tanks, and pumps addressed in the codes and standards listed in below section 3. This standard shall apply to all Suncor facilities. This standard excludes pipelines which are covered by CSA Z662, ASME B31.11, B31.4 and B31.8, and excludes structural steel welding which is covered in Canada by CSA W59 and W47.1 and in the United States by AWS D1.1.

## 2. RESPONSIBILITIES

### 2.1. Director Of Engineering, Project Services

2.1.1. Responsible for approving and implementing this standard.

### 2.2. Subject Matter Expert

2.2.1. Responsible to revise and update this Standard.

### 2.3. Project Engineering Manager

2.3.1. Responsible to ensure this standard is properly implemented when adopted for a project.

### 2.4. Discipline Engineer

2.4.1. Responsible to ensure the requirements of this standard are incorporated in the course of a project design or maintenance job.
