5.1.13. A welding datasheet shall be issued by the fabricator / contractor to each welder defining the process variables and constraints prior to commencement of welding.

5.1.14. Unless otherwise specified, all butts welds shall be full penetration welds.

5.1.15. For stainless steels and nickel alloys, flame cutting and air arc gouging/cutting are not allowed.

## 5.2. Welding Procedures

5.2.1. Applicable bidders' welding procedures and procedure qualifications records shall be submitted with each bid, for bid evaluation. After award, applicable vendor welding procedures and procedure qualifications records shall be submitted for review and approval by Owner's Engineer or designate. Welding shall not start until the welding procedures have been approved.

5.2.2. All welding shall be performed in accordance with a Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) that has been qualified in accordance with ASME Section IX and the requirements of the Code or Specification under which the weldment is to be fabricated. All Canadian welding procedure specifications shall have Canadian provincial regulatory authority approval prior to commencement of welding. An exception to this is the welding of rotating elements in pumps and rotating equipment where compliance with AWS D14.6 is required.

5.2.3. WPS's shall describe all of the essential, nonessential, and when required, supplementary essential variables for each welding process used. It shall also address additional requirements not covered under ASME Sec. IX, when specified.

5.2.4. All PQR's submitted for Stress Corrosion Cracking (SCC) Services shall have cross sectional hardness testing performed. Hardness limits shall be per NACE RP0472 for carbon steels and NACE MR0103 for other materials. Typical hardness test indentations shall be as per Figures 1 and 2. The maximum distance from the fusion line for microhardness indentations in the HAZ shall be 0.5 mm.
