# Corporate Technical Standard

Department: **PROJECT SERVICES** | Number: **0903** | Revision: **7**

Subject: **SHOP AND FIELD WELDING REQUIREMENTS**

C4.2 Welding procedure qualification (PQR's) shall be supplemented by ASTM A923 Test Method B or C as follows:

Test Method B: Charpy V-Notch at –40°F (–40°C)

- 2 specimens for weld centerline
- 2 specimens for fusion line
- 2 specimens for fusion line + 2.5 mm
- 2 specimens for fusion line + 4.75 mm

Acceptance Criteria per table 2 of ASTM A923. For product forms that cannot be evaluated by the Charpy V-notch test, Test Method C of ASTM A923 shall be used. The rapid screening tests included in ASTM A923 shall not be used to accept material.

Test Method C: Ferric Chloride Corrosion Test: 2 specimens

The rapid screening tests included in ASTM A923 shall not be used to accept material.

C4.3 Welding procedure qualification (PQR's) shall be supplemented by Ferrite Test per ASTM E562 and the following:

- PQR's shall have a ferrite testing of the weld deposit and HAZ. The measured ferrite content shall be 35 to 65 vol%.
- for HAZ, about X700 to X1000 is recommended.
- For weld overlays, only the weld deposit shall be tested.

C4.4 Dissimilar metal welds, i.e. carbon steel to duplex stainless steel or austenitic stainless steel to duplex shall require qualification and testing to the requirements of this specification except that the non-duplex material need not be subject to Charpy or corrosion testing.

C4.5 Welded Tube-To-Tubesheet Joints

Where the production test requirements (ferrite testing) cannot be practically obtained, a tube-to-tubesheet mock-up is required as a supplement to the welding procedure qualification record. The mock up shall be done in accordance with ASME IX, QW-193 and QW-288, and the following requirements:

- The test assembly shall simulate all steps of the production joint including both rolling and welding. In addition, if the production welding is to be performed through the plug sheet, this shall be simulated in the test assembly. (Access hole diameter and distance from hole to tubesheet shall be equal to production distances)
- Hardness (HV10) readings shall be taken in the heat-affected-zone (HAZ) of the tubesheet, HAZ of the tubes and weld metal. A minimum of 9 readings shall be taken on each quadrant. A minimum of one quadrant shall be tested for each welder. The average hardness shall not exceed 310 HV10, and no individual reading shall exceed 320 HV10.
