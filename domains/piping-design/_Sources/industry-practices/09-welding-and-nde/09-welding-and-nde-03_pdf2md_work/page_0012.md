b. Equipment (pressurized equipment, other than piping)

   i. For pressure retaining envelop welds in which FCAW is to be applied, the design of these joints shall be such that full 100% volumetric inspection can be completed. Applicable methods are RT or angle beam UT to detect inherent indication / defects anywhere in the examined volume.

   ii. FCAW is permitted on internals and attachments welds.

5.4.1.6. Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)

   a. Semi-automated welding is not allowed.

   b. Longitudinal welds shall be made using removable starting and stopping (run-on and run-off) tabs.

   c. Deliberate addition of alloying elements through the flux is prohibited. Flux cored wires are not permitted. Only neutral fluxes shall be used. Welding of alloys shall be done with an alloy filler wire and neutral flux. The manufacturer's wire and flux trade/brand names shall be considered as essential variables for the WPS and PQR and must be qualified whenever a change is made.

5.4.1.7. General Requirements for Welding Consumables

   a. Carbon steel welding consumables (A-No.1) intended for SCC service applications shall contain no more than 1.0 weight percent Nickel.

   b. When 1 Cr - ½ Mo, 1 ¼ Cr - ½ Mo, or 2 ¼ Cr - ½ Mo pipe is permitted by the pipe class or equipment specifications to be fabricated without the use of PWHT in 2 inch NPS and smaller, a low carbon filler metal (i.e. 0.05 wt% C maximum) shall be used. Preheat shall be 350°F (177°C) minimum.

   c. For new fabrication, dissimilar metal welds are not allowed for pressure retaining welds (for example, circumferential and longitudinal welds). For field maintenance welding, all welding procedures for dissimilar metal welds shall be identified prior to award of contract and shall be subject to prior approval from the Owner's Engineer or designate; a welding engineer shall be consulted as part of the approval process.

   d. The storage and handling of welding consumables used in production and qualification shall be carried out in accordance with the consumable manufacturer's recommendations and ASME Section II, Part C. Each fabricator shall have this requirement included in their quality assurance program. Quality assurance programs are subject to review and approval by the Owner or designate.

## 5.5. Fabrication and Fit-Up

5.5.1. When alignment tolerances have not been specified in the applicable fabrication code or standard, the alignment tolerances of Section VIII, Division 1 of the Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code shall govern.

5.5.2. Before fitting up the weld joint, the profile and dimensions of the weld end preparation shall be checked. If the specified tolerances are exceeded, the preparation shall be corrected by grinding, machining or any other methods subject to prior approval from Owner's Engineer.
