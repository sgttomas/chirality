# Project Document

**Department:** VOYAGEUR UPGRADER

**Subject:** CLOSURE WELD PROCEDURE

**Revision:** 10 Dec 2007 Rev 1

A weld repair is considered such after radiography or ultrasonic testing of the completed weld.

When weld repair of a closure weld is required, the Contractor shall notify Voyageur QA and log the welder as having a repair including the type and physical location in a closure weld joint log.

Constant monitoring of welder proficiency is the responsibility of the In Process Examiner and should the welder log more than three repairs in five subsequent welds; the IPE shall report the findings to the Construction Contractor and Voyageur QA. Voyageur QA has the right to remove any Welder that is producing questionable quality in closure welds.

When a weld repair is detected, the NDE Technician shall notify the IPE immediately and provide the report documenting the repair location. The IPE shall mark the repair area under the supervision of the NDE Technician.

a. Location of defects shall be confirmed by the Welding / Fabrication Supervisor prior to excavation.

b. The IPE shall confirm visually during the excavation to ensure removal of defects, application of pre-heat and repair as required by this procedure completing in-process examination during the repair phase and recording it on an additional Traveler.

c. MT of the excavation to ensure removal of defects can be utilized at the discretion of the IPE.

d. Upon completion of the repair and once the IPE confirms visual examination is acceptable, the same NDE, either radiography or ultrasonic examination shall be completed on the repair area to confirm acceptance. Repair of repairs will not be permitted. For example, if a repair is completed in a specific location and a second repair at that location is required, the entire weld will be subject to cut out at the discretion of Voyageur QA and the Responsible Engineer.

## 5.4 Record Keeping

For each closure weld (or group of closure welds in the same piping system), the In-Process Examiner shall legibly complete a Closure Weld Traveler (see Appendix A for example).

Upon satisfactory completion of the closure weld(s) the In-Process Examiner shall file the Closure Weld Traveler along with all supporting documentation, in an easily retrievable system, to be maintained by Contractor for turnover to the Owner upon completion of the work.
