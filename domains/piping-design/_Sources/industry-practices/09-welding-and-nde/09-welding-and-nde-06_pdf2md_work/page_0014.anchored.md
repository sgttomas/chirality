h. Filler metal size, classification, and condition complies with the Welding Procedure and other applicable standards referenced in the engineering design and where rod ovens are required, rod is properly stored.

i. Preheat prior to tacking is checked and acceptable at least one minute after removing applied heat and up to 2 inches away from both joint faces about to be welded.

j. Fit up after tacking is confirmed acceptable and preheat again confirmed as above prior to commencement of root pass installation.

k. Root pass quality is acceptable externally and internally, where accessible and found acceptable visually and by 100% dry Magnetic Particle Testing if deemed necessary by the IPE while pre-heat is maintained if deemed necessary by the IPE. Root passes should be partially completed to allow for visual access.

l. Interpass quality, profile and cleanliness are visually acceptable for deposition of subsequent passes.

m. Deposition rate, and heat input controls when applicable to the work, complies with requirements of the Welding Procedure and the engineering design (requires actual measurement of welding parameters randomly to confirm).

n. Visual inspection in between passes is completed randomly after cleaning to ensure adequate fusion and removal of stop start defects, slag and or inclusions.

o. Visual inspection of the completed weld is performed and any and all repairs are completed prior to applying final NDE.

p. Black on White Magnetic Particle examination is conducted on the entire completed weld and deemed acceptable. Verify by report.

q. Radiographic or Ultrasonic examination is conducted as required in this procedure on the entire completed weld and deemed acceptable. Verify by report. (NDE complies with Owner accepted NDE procedures, using suitable equipment in good working condition, with NDE results confirming compliance with the engineering design).

r. Supplementary tests such as hardness and ferrite tests, when required, comply with written testing procedures accepted by the Owner, and comply with requirements of the engineering design.

s. All documents are identified by the unique Closure Weld Number and filed in an easily retrievable filing system acceptable to the Owner.
