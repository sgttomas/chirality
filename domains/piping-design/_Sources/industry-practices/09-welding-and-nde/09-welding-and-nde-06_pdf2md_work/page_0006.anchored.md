Department: VOYAGEUR UPGRADER

Subject: **CLOSURE WELD PROCEDURE**

Revision:
**10 Dec 2007 Rev 1**

Construction Contractor shall provide all tools, equipment, codes, standards, skilled and qualified labour, and any other resources necessary for successful completion of closure welds in accordance with this procedure.

Construction Contractor shall provide adequate examination and test equipment that has been calibrated and documented per their Quality Manual, in good working condition that is acceptable to the Owner, and readily available at or near the location of closure welds. As a minimum, examination and test equipment shall include flashlights or other suitable lighting, inspection mirror, marking device that are non-damaging to the materials to be marked, tape measure, diameter (pi) tape, straight edge / ruler, hi-lo gauge or equivalent, feeler gauge or equivalent, surface thermometer or selection of temperature indicating crayons suitable for measurement of preheat and interpass temperature, amp meter, voltmeter, and timing device (stopwatch or wrist watch with second sweep) suitable for determining welding heat input and field portable hardness tester when hardness testing is required. Other suggested equipment such as: magnifying glass, borescope, protractor or bevel profile gauge, calliper, micrometer or digital thickness meter should be readily accessible.

Construction Contractor shall manage the In-Process Examination of closure welds using an In-Process Examiner (IPE) with qualifications described herein. The IPE shall be an employee (or equivalent) of the Construction Contractor where the contractor shall retain copies of all pertinent qualifications.

Voyageur Quality shall audit work associated with completion of closure welds, including in-process, non-destructive, and other examinations, sufficient to assure compliance with ASME B31.3 and this procedure. Voyageur inspector shall sign all Closure Weld Travelers. Copy of audits shall be made available to Voyageur QA Management and Suncor's Owner Representative QA Inspector upon request.

Construction Contractor shall not limit the flexibility of a piping system, or impart any unacceptable displacements, stresses or strains on a piping system, during alignment and welding of closure welds. The IPE shall verify this as a hold point on the Closure Weld Traveler.

In-Process Examiners shall be responsible for conducting and managing ASME B31.3 in-process examination requirements for closure welds in accordance with this procedure and Owner accepted supplementary procedures. In-Process Examiners shall:

a. Ensure that all closure weld work, examinations, and tests are completed in accordance with ASME B31.3, this procedure, and other requirements of the engineering design

b. Sign off at the bottom on each Closure Weld traveler when work is completed

c. Ensure that requirements are understood by all personnel

d. Request clarifications for any issue that is not clear
