It is the responsibility of all personnel performing closure welds to be aware of, and maintain all safety requirements of the Construction Contractor, Owner, and site.

## 2.0 REFERENCED CODES, STANDARDS AND DOCUMENTS

### 2.1 Reference documents

The latest edition of the following documents shall be used in conjunction with this procedure.

a. Pressure Equipment Safety Regulation (AR 49/2006)

b. Process Piping (ASME B31.3)

c. ASME Section V

d. ASME Section IX

e. Pressure Welders Regulation (AR 169/2002) Certification of Welding Examiners

f. Suncor Standard 0202 – Piping Material Specifications

g. Suncor Standard 0204 – Piping Fabrication and Installation

h. Suncor Standard 0205 – Piping Pressure Tests

i. Suncor Standard 0903 – Shop and Field Welding Requirements

In the event of conflict between this procedure and reference documents, the more stringent requirements shall apply unless otherwise indicated in writing from the Voyageur Project Engineering Director, or designate, and/or the Owner.

## 3.0 PREREQUISITES

### 3.1 Personnel Qualifications

All personnel working on closure welds shall satisfy the certification, qualification and experience requirements of this procedure, applicable legislation, codes, standards, and the engineering design.
