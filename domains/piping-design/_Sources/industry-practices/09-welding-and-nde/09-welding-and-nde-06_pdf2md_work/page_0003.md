# Project Document

Department: **VOYAGEUR UPGRADER**

Subject: **CLOSURE WELD PROCEDURE**

Revision: **10 Dec 2007 Rev 1**

Suncor Closure Weld – is defined as a field weld which has been approved through use of this procedure to receive in-process examination and NDE in lieu of hydrostatic testing of the entire system. This is intended for supporting modularization on Suncor Voyageur sites and does not imply the absence of hydrostatic testing on either side of the weld that will become the closure weld. Piping assemblies to be joined in the field shall, where possible, be hydrostatically tested prior to arriving at location and the subsequent interconnecting location will be the Suncor Closure Weld.

A Suncor Closure weld indicated as "SCW" within this procedure shall be restricted to:

a. Systems designed to ASME B31.3

b. P1 to P1/ S1 to S1 / P1- S1 (carbon steel) material

c. Piping systems that are not subject to Post Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT)

d. Systems that are in normal service or in Category 'D' as defined in ASME B31.3

**NOTE:** Category 'D' fluid service systems as defined in ASME B31.3 are considered for this procedure as they can be service tested per code. This shall be approved and documented by the Responsible Engineer. However, for quality and simplicity reasons, Category 'D' service lines are included in this procedure and an appropriate level of in-process examination will be elected.

e. The joining of piping systems installed onto module assemblies that have received hydrostatic testing prior to installation to meet the intent of the design and the code. This is defined as piping sections that exist as part of two modular assemblies that need to be connected, and the connection of expansion loop between piping modules.

**NOTE:** This procedure shall not be applicable to any of the following circumstances:

- Where branch piping from a modular assembly leaves the confines of the modular envelope to join with piping to or from another installation outside of the module envelope. In this case the design shall consider means to do localized or system hydro static tests by applying flanges or other means of isolation.

- Where flanges are installed, localized hydro static testing is required.

Module Envelope – The area considered as part of the module assembly, including piping that enters and leaves a module to join another module. See exclusion Note above.
