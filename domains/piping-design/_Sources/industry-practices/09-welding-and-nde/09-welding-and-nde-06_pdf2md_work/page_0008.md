All examiners and technicians shall be approved for work on the applicable site. In-Process Examiners will be required to be in-house certified by the contractor and meet the certification and qualification requirements of this procedure. They may be subject to a written examination at the discretion of Voyageur Construction Quality Manager or the EOR, and will be monitored by Suncor Voyageur Construction Quality Manager as described within this document.

In-Process Examiners shall have the following certification and or qualification through demonstrated experience in order to be eligible to acquire in-house certification as an IPE to function as described within this document:

The qualification requirement is defined as having the minimum number of years of qualifying experience of documented experience conducting quality control inspections on pressure piping construction to ASME B31.3, including weld evaluations, witnessing tests, and maintaining inspection records.

## 3.1.1 Certification and Qualification Years

- CSA W178.2 Level 1, 2 or 3 certified welding inspector with current ASME B31.3 or ASME VIII div.1 / IX endorsement with at least 1 year qualification
- ABSA Certified Boiler and/or Pressure Vessel Inspector current certification with at least 1 year qualification
- API 510 or API 570 current certification with at least 1 year qualification
- Welding Engineering or Materials Engineering Technology graduate with at least 2 years qualification
- Alberta B-Pressure Welders with a valid W# and at least 3 years qualification

## 3.1.2 Experience

The experience of each IPE shall be evaluated during the pre-screening process described below by the Construction Contractor. In that review, the Construction Contractor shall ensure each IPE applicant has adequate experience in working to established quality systems where the applicant has had experience in reading isometrics, understanding code and design requirements, weld symbols, filling out and following inspection and test plans, retaining and managing inspection job files, and understanding the right of the IPE to stop work.

'B' Pressure Welders that have been in-house certified as In Process Examiners are not permitted to weld at any time while functioning as an IPE.
