## 3.2 IPE In-House Certification

In-House certification is divided into two parts: pre-screening/referral by the Construction Contractor, and Voyageur QA administration of In-House Certification Test to this procedure which requires a demonstration of knowledge.

### 3.2.1 Pre-Screening and Referral process

The Construction Contractor shall review all IPE applicant certifications, qualification years and experience to ensure they meet or exceed the minimum requirements as indicated in this section. They shall also ensure evidence of visual acuity that support the requirements of CSA W178.2 and ASME Section V, Article 9. All records acquired shall be retained in confidence by the Construction Contractor to support the referral process.

Successful applicants that meet the certification and qualification years indicated in these sections may be referred to Voyageur QA for consideration. The referral will be provided in writing with attached supporting documents satisfying the requirements above.

### 3.2.2 Voyageur QA Certification Test

IPE applicants provided by the Construction Contractor shall be required to read this procedure and complete a proficiency test provided and supervised by Voyageur QA. The test shall contain questions with regard to the IPE's responsibilities as written in this procedure. The test shall be revised and maintained secure by the Voyageur Construction Quality Manager (CQM). In addition, an IPE written examination may be administered by Voyageur CQM to determine qualification of an IPE.

Successful completion of the test is at the discretion of Voyageur CQM. Upon acceptance, Voyageur CQM shall provide a letter stating the applicant's appointment as an IPE for the length of said project defined as follows: The appointment shall be non-transferable between projects, shall be valid only while employed with the Construction Contractor, shall be valid for a maximum of 2 years and shall be given a unique number traceable to the files kept by Voyageur QA. Test documentation and evidence of appointment shall be retained by Voyageur CQM.
