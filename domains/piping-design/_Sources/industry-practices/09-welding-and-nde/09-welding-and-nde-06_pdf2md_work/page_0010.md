## 3.3 NDE Technicians

NDE Technicians shall be CAN/CGSB-48.9712 Level 1, 2, or 3 certified for the required NDE method to be used. The certification level shall be in accordance with CAN/CGSB-48.9712, but all final interpretation of results shall be performed by a Level II or III certified for the NDE method used. SNT TC-1A adherence is a requirement.

## 3.4 Retention of Documentation

As mentioned throughout this section, the Construction Contractor and Voyageur CCM have specific responsibilities to retain documentation records. The records shall be made available for audit or review by Suncor Voyageur QA Management or Suncor's Owner Representative QA Manager upon request, and must be kept available on the job site during the project construction activity.

## 4.0 PRECAUTIONS

Where conventional UT has been requested as the final method of NDE, it is mandatory that the UT scan be performed and interpreted independently by two separate technicians. The subsequent reports shall be reviewed for acceptance by the Suncor Voyageur Central Construction Management Team (CCMT) (QA and/or Engineering representative) and/or the Owner's Inspector for the purpose of ensuring consistent technician interpretation.

## 5.0 IMPLEMENTATION

### 5.1 Closure Weld Technical Requirements

All welding and related issues shall comply with the requirements of Suncor Standard 0903 as applicable, including but not limited to: general welding requirements, welding procedures, welder performance qualification, welding process and filler metal limitations, fabrication & fit-up, welding, preheat and interpass, and inspection & workmanship

Pipe ends for closure welds shall be cut square, at 90 degrees to the pipe or component central axis with bevel angles as per the WPS and indicated on the Traveler. Maximum deviation from square at any location shall be 0.0591 in (1.5 mm), measured as the gap created when a carpenter's square or equivalent is placed across the face of the cut end.

End preparations for groove welds shall be prepared according to ASME B31.3, and suitable for full penetration. If alternative end preparations are required for welding processes or procedures proposed by the Construction Contractor, these ends shall be approved by the Owner and prepared by the Construction Contractor.
