# Project Document

**Department:** VOYAGEUR UPGRADER

**Subject:** CLOSURE WELD PROCEDURE

**Revision:** 10 Dec 2007 Rev 1

Hydrostatic end caps may be removed by cold cutting methods and ends prepared as depicted in the design drawings. Once are prepared they shall be examined visually for signs of damage and dimensional code requirements. Should the Inspector suspect any defects, magnetic particle testing shall be employed to determine if defects are present and report of any defects shall be brought to the attention of the Voyageur CCMT QA Representative.

All end preparations shall be clean, smooth, and uniform, and free from cracks, nicks, gouges, or other discontinuities that could negatively affect weld quality. Relevant NDE for the discontinuity shall be performed to verify detection and subsequent removal. Welding surfaces shall be free of paint, coating, grease, oil, or other surface contaminants that could negatively affect weld quality.

All closure weld ends left for field welding shall be securely capped with caps of a type and quality acceptable to the Owner, to prevent end area damage and entry of dirt or other debris into the piping system. Piping shall be visually examined for internal contaminants and deemed to be clean before capping.

Before fit-up of closure weld ends, the inside of the piping shall be checked for dirt, debris, or other unacceptable contaminants, and removed if found.

Closure weld joint types have not been restricted by this procedure, but they must be suitable for 100% volumetric examination by Radiography or automated advanced Ultrasonic Testing methods in accordance with ASME B31.3 and the engineering design, and acceptable to the Owner. The use of automated advanced Ultrasonic Testing shall be employed once the procedure supplied by the NDE Company is reviewed and accepted by ABSA and approved by Voyageur QA. Voyageur QA shall retain documented evidence of said acceptance and approval in Livelink and on hand at the project site.

In cases where the automated advanced ultrasonic technique is unavailable, the Project Manager may apply to the Suncor Owner Representative QA Manager with an appropriate Voyageur technical deviation. In this case conventional Ultrasonic testing may be considered, as described in section 4.0, providing separate examinations are performed by different NDE technicians and that the results are comparably acceptable.

Backing rings shall not be permitted for use with closure welds at any time.

Closure weld joints shall be adequately protected from wind, precipitation, or other adverse weather conditions during welding, to the satisfaction of the In-Process Examiner and the Owner's Inspector.

Closure weld joints shall be cleaned to bright metal inside and outside, for a distance extending at least 1/4 inch (6.4 mm) from the edges of the joint.

Low hydrogen electrodes shall be used and maintained in accordance with recommendations of the electrode manufacturer.
