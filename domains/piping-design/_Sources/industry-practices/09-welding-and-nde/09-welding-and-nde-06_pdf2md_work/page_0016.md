# Project Document

**Department:** VOYAGEUR UPGRADER

**Subject:** CLOSURE WELD PROCEDURE

**Revision:** 10 Dec 2007 Rev 1

Construction Contractors records for closure welds shall be completely legible. Records shall be systematically filed on a current basis, using a system that provides for easy retrieval, with all records fully traceable to closure welds represented.

The In-Process Examiner shall prepare a Closure Weld Log (paper or electronic) to track the progress of the work. The log shall be updated progressively and kept up to date daily. (See Appendix B for a sample log).

The Owner retains the right to request and obtain copies of document associated with any closure weld at any time.

Suncor Representative QA Manager reserves the right to audit any and all application of this procedure and shall be given full access at time of request to any welds or records concerned.
