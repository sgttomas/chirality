# Project Document

Department: **VOYAGEUR UPGRADER**

Subject: **CLOSURE WELD PROCEDURE**

Revision: **10 Dec 2007 Rev 1**

Construction Contractor – Organization with contractual responsibility for final erection, testing, and turnover of completed piping systems to the Owner, and with a current Alberta Quality Management System (QMS) certificate.

Engineering Contractor – Organization with contractual responsibility for piping system design. May employ / provide a responsible engineer as defined within. The Engineering Contractor also has the responsibility to include this procedure as part of the piping design registration submission.

Engineering Design – Requirements of all applicable legislation, codes, standards, specifications, drawings, as required by supplementary Engineering Contractor and Owner requirements.

Engineer of Record (EOR) – Suncor appointed and qualified as per the requirements defined by APPEGA. The EOR is ultimately accountable for all responsible engineers' use of this procedure, and shall approve individual use for all ASME B31.3 normal service piping designs. Use of this procedure for Category 'D' fluid service systems shall be approved by the Owner's Responsible Engineer.

Responsible Engineer (RE) – Suncor appointed engineer assigned to utilize this procedure in the design of piping systems to ASME B31.3 and Suncor standards.

In-process Examination – the monitoring of the fit up and welding process in its entirety including NDE and documentation.

In-process Examiners (IPE) – Persons responsible for In-Process Examinations as required by ASME B31.3, (344.7), with appropriate qualifications and experience as described by this document. In-Process Examiners shall not be pressure welders engaged in any welding activity as part of the project assigned.

Qualified NDE Contractor – Contracting Company or organization providing Non-destructive Testing. To be considered as qualified, the NDE contractor must adhere to the minimum requirements of ASME in compliance with the requirements of ASNT recommended practice #SNT-TC-1A. The NDE contractor must retain a technician with dual Level III current qualifications to ASNT and CGSB.

Certificated NDE Technicians – Persons responsible for non-destructive examination (NDE), with appropriate qualifications and experience in the NDE methods to be used, as described by this document.

Owner – Suncor Energy Inc. including affiliates and authorized designates.

Owner Inspector (Voyageur Construction Management Team QA Representative) – For the purpose of project construction, this is the person appointed by the Major Projects Group of Voyageur or their designate to fulfill the requirements of this procedure and ASME B31.3 (Sec-340, Inspection) during construction.
