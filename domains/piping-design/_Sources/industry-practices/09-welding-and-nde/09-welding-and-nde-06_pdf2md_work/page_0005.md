## 1.3 Responsibilities

This procedure assigns responsibilities to organizations and persons defined in Section 5.0, for work required during different phases of piping system design and construction.

The Engineering Contractor, in consultation with the Voyageur EOR, shall determine if closure welds are a permissible alternative to hydrostatic leak testing for piping systems under consideration, and shall provide the necessary safety and technical justifications for use of closure welds if requested.

When closure welds are approved by the Voyageur EOR, the Engineering Contractor shall indicate same on documents submitted to ABSA for design registration, and provide ABSA with a copy of this procedure. These actions are necessary because Alberta Regulation 49/2006 requires hydrostatic leak testing of piping systems, so alternatives to hydrostatic testing must be accepted by ABSA.

When it is agreed and/or specified to the use of a closure weld as an alternative to hydrostatic leak testing, the Construction Contractor shall be responsible for all work activities including completion of the welds, In-Process Examinations, Non-destructive Examinations, and shall provide a Procedure or Work Instruction that has been developed according to the requirements of this procedure.

Where the requirements of this procedure are considered insufficient for the work or conflicting with practices of the Construction Contractor, the Construction Contractor shall prepare supplementary forms or procedures and submit these to the Voyageur EOR for acceptance. All supplementary requirements shall satisfy ASME B31.3 and ABSA requirements.
