**Department:** VOYAGEUR UPGRADER

**Subject:** CLOSURE WELD PROCEDURE

**Revision:** 10 Dec 2007 Rev 1

When ambient temperature in the area of the joint is, or has the potential to be, less than 32°F (0°C), prior to the start of any cutting, grinding or welding operation, material shall be preheated to a minimum of 100°F (38°C), or to higher temperature as may be specified by the weld procedure or engineering design.

Minimum preheat temperature for completion of closure welds shall not be less than 50°F (10°C) or the highest of preheat temperatures specified on the welding procedure, required or recommended under ASME B31.3, Table 330.1.1, or specified by the engineering design.

Minimum preheat for weld repair of carbon steel closure welds shall be 250°F (120°C). Clarify to 13 & 14. 250 vs 100 vs 10.

Minimum preheat temperature shall be maintained until the operation requiring preheat is complete.

When preheat is required for welding, the required preheat shall be applied before tack welds are deposited.

Root tacks incorporated into the finished weld shall exhibit complete penetration and fusion.

Bridge tacks shall be completely removed during the welding of the root pass.

## 5.2 In-Process Examination Requirements

The Construction Contractor is responsible to ensure that an IPE is responsible for monitoring the welding process to meet the requirements of B31.3 and this procedure. Voyageur QA is required to monitor for compliance and issue non-conformance to the Construction Contractor and copied to the Project Director each time a violation is confirmed. As a guideline, the IPE should be limited to a maximum of five welds to be monitored at one time. Depending on diameter, thickness and location, this can be varied.

All examinations shall be carried out according to written procedures prepared in accordance with ASME Section V and ASME B31.3, and accepted by the Owner.

When the Engineering Contractor has permitted use of closure welds for a piping system, and the RE, Voyageur QA and Construction Contractor have accepted use of closure welds in lieu of hydrostatic testing, the In-Process Examiner shall initiate closure weld work with the following actions:

a. Refer to the Line Designation Table (LDT) and verify that closure welds are permitted² for the piping system in question.

---

2 If permission to use closure welds has not been provided on the LDT, an application to use a closure weld can be made to the Owner and subsequently to ABSA on a case-by-case or group basis.
