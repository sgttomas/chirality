10.4.2 PWHT of P-8 stainless steels shall only be performed when specified by BP CHP.

10.4.3 When specified, PWHT shall be performed at 1650°F ±25°F for one hour per inch of thickness, but not less than one hour.

## 10.5 INSPECTION

10.5.1 A minimum of 10% of completed welds in P-8 material for piping and pressure vessels shall be PT examined. Additional PT may be specified by the BP CHP Inspector or Engineer.

10.5.2 The ferrite content of completed welds that are to have PWHT or that are to be in service above 800°F shall be measured with a magnetic measuring instrument calibrated in accordance with ANSI/AWS-A4.2, Standard Practice for Calibrating Magnetic Instruments to Measure Delta Ferrite Content of Austenitic and Duplex Austenitic-Ferritic Stainless Steel Weldmetal. A minimum of six readings shall be taken on each weld. The individual test readings shall be averaged to obtain FN. FN shall be within the 3 to 10 range. The ferrite readings shall be taken prior to PWHT.

## SECTION 11: WELD OVERLAY AND CLAD RESTORATION

## 11.1 GENERAL

11.1.1 Weld overlay and clad restoration (alloy weld over base material seam welds in clad plate) shall be qualified in accordance with ASME Section IX, using the same type and brand of flux and welding wire to be used in production overlaying.

11.1.2 Austenitic stainless steel weld overlay and clad restoration may be made in either one or two layers. Unless specified otherwise, the first layer in a 2-pass overlay shall be type 309L. A single layer, or the second layer in a 2-pass overlay, will normally be type 347 or type 321. Use of other alloy materials requires BP CHP approval. The undiluted chemistry shall be at least 0.0625" deep as measured from the finished outermost weld surface. After the first or single layer of alloy weld is applied, the surface shall be tested with a copper sulfate solution to verify that there is no exposed carbon steel material.

11.1.3 The PQR shall demonstrate that the WPS can deposit the minimum specified depth of undiluted overlay or cladding.

11.1.4 Filler metal for austenitic stainless steel overlay and clad restoration shall be selected to achieve a Welding Research Council (WRC) ferrite number (FN) of 3 to 10 in the deposited weld metal. The PQR shall demonstrate that the WPS can achieve a FN of 3 to 10. Testing of the PQR sample shall include six ferrite readings taken on the overlay sample in accordance with Section 11.4.2.
