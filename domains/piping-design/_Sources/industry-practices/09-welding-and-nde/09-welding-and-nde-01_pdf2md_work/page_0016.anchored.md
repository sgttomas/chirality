## 7.6 REPAIR BY WELDING

7.6.1 Material defects beyond those allowed by the ASTM or ASME material specifications shall not be repaired without prior approval by BP CHP. Vendor shall notify BP CHP in writing of the nature and extent of the defects and the proposed method of repair, including non-destructive examination methods, WPSs, and PQRs.

7.6.2 Major weld defects shall not be repaired without prior approval by BP CHP. Major repairs are defined as defects having a depth exceeding 0.375" or 20% of the thickness, whichever is smaller. Vendor shall notify BP CHP in writing of the nature and extent of the defects and the proposed method of repair, including non-destructive examination methods, WPSs, and PQRs.

## SECTION 8: CARBON STEEL REQUIREMENTS

## 8.1 PREHEAT AND HYDROGEN BAKE-OUT

8.1.1 Preheat shall be as specified in the applicable WPS, except that the minimum preheat temperature for P-1 carbon steel material thicker than 1" shall be 175°F.
