2) Fluid with greater than 0.05 psia partial pressure of H2S in the gas phase of a water wet system.

3) Fluid with greater than or equal to 1 ppmw total sulfide content in the water phase, and with pH less than 4.

4) Fluid with greater than or equal to 1 ppmw total sulfide content and greater than or equal to 20 ppmw free cyanide in the water phase, and with pH greater than 7.6.

**Note:** Carbon steel material is commonly used for wet H2S services, so hydrogen bake-out is typical for the services above.

b) Concentrated (90 to 98 wt%) sulfuric acid service, such as acid storage tanks.

c) Hydrogen (gas or liquid) service operating above 400°F, and operating above the API 941 carbon steel Nelson Curve or down to 50°F below the Nelson Curve.

**Note:** Bake-out for this service should be uncommon, since carbon steel equipment and piping are normally designed for hydrogen service at temperatures no higher than 50°F below the Nelson Curve.
