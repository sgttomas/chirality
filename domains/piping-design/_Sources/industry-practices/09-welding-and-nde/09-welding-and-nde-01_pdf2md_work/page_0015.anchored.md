b) All hardness tests shall be performed at ambient temperature.

c) For welds that require PWHT, hardness tests shall be performed after completion of the PWHT.

d) Pressure containing welds on vessels and piping shall have a minimum of one hardness test performed for each welding process employed and for each welder or welding operator.

e) Pressure vessel hardness tests shall be performed on both the process and external sides of the weld where possible.

f) Pressure vessel hardness tests shall be performed on each 10 feet of weld in each seam (minimum of one test per seam), at all weld seam intersections, at each nozzle-to-shell weld, and at each nozzle-to-flange weld.

g) For each pressure vessel test location, one hardness determination shall be made at the center of the weld.

h) Piping hardness tests shall be performed on the outside of pressure-retaining welds.

i) For each piping spool piece, a minimum of one weld shall be hardness tested for each welder and welding procedure used.

j) For each piping test location, three hardness determinations shall be made; one at the center of the weld, and one on each side of the weld in the heat affected zones as close as practicable to the edge of the weld.

7.4.8 If any single weld hardness test result exceeds the specified maximum value, notify BP CHP immediately for consideration of retest.

7.4.9 Production welds that are rejected for hardness shall be removed and re-welded, or have PWHT per the Code, to achieve specified hardness test results. Prior approval of the PWHT procedure shall be obtained from BP CHP. The vendor shall assure that the welds, heat affected zones, and base metal meet the specified minimum tensile strength, low temperature toughness, and other design properties after PWHT. This will normally require requalification of the welding procedure per ASME Section IX.

7.4.10 All welds corrected for hardness by re-welding or PWHT shall be retested to establish that the hardness does not exceed the specified maximum value.
