9.1.4 If preheat maintenance fails during welding, the entire uncompleted weld seam shall be MT examined prior to resuming welding.

## 9.2 BAKE-OUT FOR HOT HYDROGEN OR WET H2S PRIOR SERVICE

9.2.1 For Cr-Mo steel equipment and piping that have previously been in service and exposed to wet H2S environments or high temperature hydrogen, prior to welding directly onto the Cr-Mo base metal, a bake-out is required to prevent hydrogen embrittlement cracking. For Cr-Mo equipment that has been operating in hydrogen service above the API 941 carbon steel Nelson Curve, follow the same bake-out temperatures and times specified for carbon steel in section 8.1.2. Cr-Mo steels are not normally used in wet H2S environments, but when they are used for that service, follow the carbon steel bake-out requirements in sections 8.1.2 and 8.1.3. Bake-out is not required for Cr-Mo equipment and piping (typically 5Cr and 9Cr) in high temperature H2S service operating above 500°F without hydrogen, because this service does not charge hydrogen into the steel.

9.2.2 For SS clad or overlaid Cr-Mo material, bake-out is not required before welding to the cladding or overlay for either of the following conditions:

a) The cladding or weld overlay is thick enough to prevent forming a heat affected zone (HAZ) in the base metal (TIG welding on minimum 0.125" thick cladding or overlay is one acceptable option); or,

b) The "wire slugging" welding technique or some other approved method is used to prevent forming a HAZ in the base metal.

9.2.3 When the options in 9.2.2 are not acceptable, the bake-out requirements of 9.2.1 shall apply for welding to SS clad or overlaid Cr-Mo material. Prior to bake-out, the cladding or overlay in the weld area shall be removed down to the base metal. The bake-out will not be effective without removing the SS layer.

9.2.4 See Appendix A for additional commentary on this issue related to 2.25Cr material.

## 9.3 PWHT

9.3.1 PWHT is required for all welds to P-3 materials used in hydrogen service, regardless of thickness, when after adding 100°F to the maximum expected operating temperature and 50 psia to the maximum expected hydrogen partial pressure the adjusted operating point is on or above the Nelson curve for the material. Use the Nelson curves from the latest edition of API Recommended Practice 941.

9.3.2 PWHT is normally required for welds to P-4, P-5A, & P-5B materials, regardless of thickness. Exceptions to the PWHT requirement may be allowed for small external non-pressure containing welds if the application complies with the applicable design code, such as ASME B31.3, and is approved by the BP CHP Reliability Engineer and Metallurgist.
