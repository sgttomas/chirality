Measure Delta Ferrite Content of Austenitic and Duplex Austenitic-Ferritic Stainless Steel Weldmetal. The ferrite readings shall be taken prior to postweld heat treatment. FN shall be within the 3 to 10 range. The test frequency shall be as follows:

a) For clad restoration, a minimum of six readings shall be taken on each weld. The reading locations shall be evenly spaced along the length of the seam. The individual test readings shall be averaged to obtain FN.

b) For weld overlay, a minimum of six readings shall be taken on each shell course, head, nozzle, tubesheet or other component. The readings shall be evenly spaced along the length and around the circumference of each component. The individual test readings shall be averaged to obtain FN.

11.4.3 100% of the surface of austenitic stainless steel weld overlays shall be PT examined. Clad restorations shall be 100% UT and PT examined including 3" on each side of each weld seam. Inspection shall be done after any required PWHT and hydrotest.

11.4.4 All weld overlays shall be spot checked for bond consistency by UT examination. When PWHT is required, this examination shall occur after PWHT. For vessel and pipe cylinders, the examination shall be performed on four 3" wide full length longitudinal strips, evenly spaced around the vessel shell or pipe. For vessel heads the examination shall be performed on one 3" wide strip across each head. Any lack of fusion causing a loss of back reflection accompanied by an echo indication from the plane of the interface of the overlay and base metal and having an area greater than that of a 1" diameter circle shall be repaired. This area may be of any shape. If any repair is needed, 100% of the surface shall be checked as above. Vendor shall prepare a procedure for performing this examination and submit it to BP CHP for approval.

## SECTION 12: AUSTENITIC TO FERRITIC WELDS

## 12.1 FILLER, PROCESS, AND INSPECTION

12.1.1 Welds joining austenitic stainless steels to ferritic steels shall be made with filler metal as follows:

a) For services not exceeding 650°F, use ASME SFA-5.4 classification E309L, SFA-5.9 classification ER309L, or the filler metals specified in b) below.

b) For services exceeding 650°F, use ASME SFA-5.4 classification ER NiCr-3 (comparable to International Nickel's INCO 82), or use ASME SFA-5.11 classification E NiCrFe-3 (comparable to International Nickel's INCO 182) or E NiCrFe-2 (comparable to International Nickel's INCO A) unless BP CHP specifies another filler metal. BP CHP approval is required for welding dissimilar materials in services exceeding 650°F.
