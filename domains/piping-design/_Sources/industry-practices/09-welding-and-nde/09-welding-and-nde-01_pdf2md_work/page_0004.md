b) When procedure qualification hardness tests are otherwise required by BP CHP engineering standards or job specifications.

2.2.4 When hardness testing for procedure qualification is required, testing shall be in accordance with NACE MR0103 Appendix C or similar industry standard, and shall include hardness testing of the weld metal, heat affected zone, and adjacent base metal. Hardness surveys shall be performed using the Vickers Hardness test method with a 10 kgf load per ASTM E384, or BP CHP approved alternative method. Hardness testing per ASTM E92, which has been superseded by ASTM E384, is acceptable for PQRs completed prior to the July 2010 withdrawal date of ASTM E92. Maximum hardness for PQR testing of Cr-Mo steels shall be 248 HV 10 or equivalent. Maximum hardness for PQR testing of other materials shall be as stated in the engineering standards or job specifications. Hardness test results shall be submitted with the PQRs.

2.2.5 Hardness testing for PQRs shall be done after PWHT when applicable. Specimens used for PQR hardness testing shall be given the same PWHT as the production welds will receive.

## 2.3 WELDER QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

Welder Performance Qualification (WPQ) records shall be made available upon request for review by the BP CHP assigned inspector or their designated alternate.

## 2.4 PIPING SOCKET WELDS

2.4.1 A minimum of two weld passes are required for socket welds in piping.

2.4.2 For restrictions on use of socket welds for small trim piping at reciprocating compressors, see BP CHP STP 200 section 4.1.5.

## 2.5 NEUTRALIZATION FOR AKALINE SERVICES

2.5.1 Metal that has been exposed to alkaline services, such as caustic, catacarb, or amine, shall be neutralized prior to welding. The metal shall be thoroughly rinsed with water and then neutralized with ample amounts of undiluted vinegar. Allow the solution to react for approximately 5 minutes and then rinse again with water. Verify neutralization using litmus paper and repeat if necessary. After the metal is neutralized, dry the area and proceed with welding.
