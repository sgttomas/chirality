## 11.2 PREPARATION FOR CLAD PLATE WELDING

11.2.1 The alloy cladding shall be stripped back a minimum of 0.25" from the edge of the clad plate joint in preparation for welding the base metal seam. Prior to welding, the stripped-back area shall be tested with a copper sulfate solution to verify that all of the alloy clad material has been removed.

11.2.2 Removal of the cladding shall not reduce the base material thickness below the minimum design thickness.

11.2.3 Base metal welds in clad plate shall be MT examined before the clad restoration weld is applied.

11.2.4 For carbon steel base material which has previously been in service and exposed to hydrogen or wet H₂S environments, before adding weld overlay follow the hydrogen bake-out requirements in 8.1.2.

## 11.3 REPAIRS TO OVERLAY, CLADDING, AND CLAD RESTORATION

If a defect in the overlay, cladding, or clad restoration penetrates into the base material more than 0.1875", restore the base material thickness by welding, prior to weld repairing the alloy material.

## 11.4 INSPECTION

11.4.1 Completed weld overlay and clad restorations shall be PMI analyzed with a portable x-ray emission analyzer or other equipment approved by BP CHP. The analysis shall measure the concentration of Cr, Ni and other essential alloy elements such as Mo and Cb (or Nb). The weld deposit shall have a Cr, Ni and other essential alloy content that meets the requirements of the ASME/AWS specification for the weld filler metal. The results shall be reported to BP CHP. The extent of the PMI analysis shall be as follows:

a) For clad restoration, a minimum of one PMI spot analysis shall be made for every 10 feet of weld, with a minimum of one PMI spot analysis for every weld and every welder.

b) For weld overlay, a minimum of one PMI spot analysis shall be made for every 4 foot by 4 foot area (16 square feet), with a minimum of one PMI spot analysis on each shell course, head, nozzle, tubesheet or other component.

c) A minimum of one PMI spot analysis shall be made for each welder doing overlay work for each different weld procedure.
