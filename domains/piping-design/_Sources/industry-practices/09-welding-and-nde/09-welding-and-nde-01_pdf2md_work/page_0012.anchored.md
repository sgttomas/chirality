5.1.4 When PWHT is required, the preheat temperature shall be maintained on the part being welded until intermediate heat treatment or final PWHT is completed, unless otherwise specified or approved by BP CHP. For P-5B group materials, do not go directly from preheat to PWHT, but follow the hydrogen bake-out requirements in Section 9. For other low alloy materials, see Section 9 for preferred alternatives.

## SECTION 6: GENERAL PWHT

### 6.1 PROCEDURAL GUIDELINES

6.1.1 The applicable BP CHP engineering standard or the job specification may require PWHT for equipment due to service even when not mandated by the applicable design and fabrication code. For such welds, a WPS qualified with PWHT is required.

6.1.2 Holding temperature range (maximum and minimum) and holding time at temperature shall be stated on the WPS.

6.1.3 PWHT procedures shall describe cleaning requirements, heating and cooling rates, type of heating, thermocouple locations and necessary equipment, and shall be submitted to BP CHP for approval.

6.1.4 For local PWHT of material thicker than 2", thermocouples shall be attached to both sides of the joint in three locations around the circumference, to verify that the minimum required temperature is attained.

6.1.5 PWHT shall be performed after all welding and forming operations are completed, including non-pressure attachment welds. Equipment and piping shall not be subjected to welding, hammering, pressing, or forming after PWHT.

### 6.2 METHODS

6.2.1 Acceptable methods for postweld heat treatment are:

- a) Furnace heating
- b) Resistance heating
- c) Induction heating
- d) Radiant heating
- e) Internal firing with thermocouples attached to the non-fired side of the affected walls.

6.2.2 Methods not acceptable for postweld heat treatment are:

- a) Direct impingement furnace burners or torch flames
