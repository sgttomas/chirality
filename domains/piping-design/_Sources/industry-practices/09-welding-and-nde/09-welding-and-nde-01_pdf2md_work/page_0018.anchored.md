8.2.2 PWHT is required for all welds to P-1 carbon steel materials used in caustic and amine services. Caustic service is defined as 5 wt% or greater concentration of NaOH.

8.2.3 PWHT is required for all welds to P-1 carbon steel materials used in anhydrous ammonia service to prevent stress corrosion cracking. Ammonia with less than 0.2 wt% water shall be considered anhydrous ammonia. The ammonia normally supplied to BP CHP is not anhydrous since it has 0.2 wt% or greater water.

8.2.4 For additional PWHT requirements, see the applicable design and fabrication codes, job specifications, and BP CHP standards including but not limited to STPs 22, 40, 205, and 211.

8.2.5 When PWHT is required, the PWHT temperature for carbon steel P-1 group materials shall be 1100 to 1200°F. Holding time shall be in accordance with the applicable design and fabrication code, but shall not be less than one hour.

## SECTION 9: LOW AND INTERMEDIATE ALLOY STEEL REQUIREMENTS

## 9.1 PREHEAT AND HYDROGEN BAKE-OUT

9.1.1 Minimum preheat temperature shall be as follows:

> **Table - Minimum preheat temperature for alloy steel groups** - see [XLSX](tables/09-welding-and-nde-01_p0018_tbl01_minimum-preheat-temperature-alloy-steel.xlsx) and [source crop](tables/09-welding-and-nde-01_p0018_tbl01_minimum-preheat-temperature-alloy-steel.png)

| Material | Minimum Preheat Temperature |
| :--- | :---: |
| P-3 steels | 175 °F (≥ ½ inch) |
| P-4 steels | 300 °F |
| P-5A steels (≤3%Cr) | 400 °F |
| P-5B steels (>3%Cr) | 450 °F |

9.1.2 For P-4, P-5A, and P-5B materials, including new or prior service piping, preheat is to be maintained after completion of welding through PWHT. However, for 9Cr-1Mo steel only (a P-5B material), welds shall first be temporarily cooled to 200°F to assure complete metallurgical transformation prior to PWHT.

9.1.3 If pre-heat cannot be maintained through PWHT such that welds in P-4, P-5A, or P-5B steels are to be intentionally cooled to ambient temperature for more than a few hours prior to PWHT, an additional hydrogen bake-out step is required. In this case, after completion of welding and before removing the preheat, the welds shall be heated to a minimum of 600°F and then wrapped with a 1" thick insulation blanket for a distance of the greater of one pipe diameter or 6" on both sides of the weld, and then allowed to slow cool to ambient. There is no specific hold time requirement at 600°F other than the time to wrap the pipe.
