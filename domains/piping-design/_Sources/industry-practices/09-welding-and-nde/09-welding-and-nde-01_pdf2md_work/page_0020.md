9.3.3 PWHT temperature for P-4 material shall be 1300°F ±25°F, as described in API 938.

9.3.4 PWHT temperatures for all other low and intermediate alloy steels shall be per the applicable design code.

9.3.5 Holding time shall be in accordance with the applicable design and fabrication code, but shall not be less than two hours.

## 9.4 INSPECTION

9.4.1 Thermally cut edges for joint preparation shall be MT or PT examined prior to welding.

9.4.2 Root passes in vessels and exchangers shall be MT examined. This includes root passes of butt welds welded from one side and back-gouged root welds to be welded from the second side.

9.4.3 Welds shall be MT examined after PWHT.

## SECTION 10: AUSTENITIC STAINLESS STEEL REQUIREMENTS

## 10.1 FILLER METAL

Filler metal shall be selected to achieve a Welding Research Council (WRC) ferrite number (FN) of 3 to 10 in the deposited weld metal.

## 10.2 GRINDING AND CLEANING TOOLS

Grinding and cleaning of stainless steels shall be done with tools that will not leave detrimental deposits. Examples of acceptable tools are aluminum oxide and silicon carbide grinding wheels and austenitic stainless steel wire brushes not previously used on other types of materials.

## 10.3 PREHEAT AND INTERPASS TEMPERATURE

The maximum preheat and interpass temperature for P-8 materials shall be 350°F.

## 10.4 PWHT

10.4.1 PWHT is required for all welds to 316L SS material used in caustic service.
