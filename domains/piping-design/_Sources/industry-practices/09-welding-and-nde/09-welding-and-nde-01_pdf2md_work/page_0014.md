7.4.3 Production hardness testing is required for carbon steel pressure vessels and piping which are welded using carbon steel filler metals or welding processes not listed in 7.4.2 above, such as FCAW or SAW processes.

7.4.4 Production hardness testing is required for all vessel and piping pressure boundary welds in P-3, P-4, P-5A, P-5B, P-6, and P-7 materials unless otherwise specified by BP CHP.

7.4.5 Production hardness testing is required for tanks in sour (H₂S) service for all field welding done using machine or automatic weld processes. Hardness testing shall be performed on the weld surfaces which will be exposed to the sour tank contents. A minimum of one test shall be performed for each vertical weld, and one test shall be performed for each 100 feet of circumferential weld. Weld hardness shall not exceed 200 HB.

7.4.6 When piping governed by ASME B31.3 is required to have PWHT, and a weld hardness limit is specified in B31.3 for the applicable piping material, the following production hardness test requirements shall apply:

a) Tests shall be performed on at least 10% of furnace heat treated welds and 100% of locally heat treated welds.

b) When dissimilar metals are joined by welding, the hardness limits specified for the base and welding materials in B31.3 shall be met for each material.

7.4.7 When production hardness testing is required by this document or any other applicable specification, code, or standard, the testing shall be performed as follows:

a) Hardness tests shall be performed with Brinell hardness equipment in accordance with ASTM E10, or comparison hardness testers in accordance with ASTM A833. (Hardness test methods for weld procedure qualification shall be per paragraph 2.2.4.)
