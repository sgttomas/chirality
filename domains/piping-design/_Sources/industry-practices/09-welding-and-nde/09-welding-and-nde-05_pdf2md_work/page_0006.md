B5.12 Work shall be organized so that: (Z662-94 Cl. 7.2.7.10, 7.2.7.11, and 7.2.7.12)

- the time interval between root bead finish and hot pass start does not exceed 5 minutes
- the completed root pass does not cool below the preheat or minimum interpass temperature specified on the welding procedure, before starting the hot pass;
- the time interval between hot pass finish and fill pass start does not exceed 30 minutes;
- fill and cap pass welders are not to be more than 600 meters behind the hot pass welders at any time during the workday;
- the maximum number of uncapped mainline welds at the end of the workday does not exceed the number of cap pass welders;
- all passes except the cap pass are completed by the end of each working day; and
- all welds left incomplete at the end of a work day are completed on the following work day.

When welding recommences on an incomplete weld, the weld must be preheated to the temperature specified by the welding procedure.

B5.13 Night caps of material and construction acceptable to the OWNER shall be used to cover:

- open ends of welded pipe sections at the end of each day's work;
- the free end of a pipe section being welded, to prevent movement of air through the pipe; and
- open ends of pipe sections to be tied-in later, as may occur at crossings.

Nightcaps shall not be tack welded to the pipe and shall not be removed until work is ready to resume.

B5.14 For tie-in welding:

- alignment shall be done with an OWNER representative present, to the OWNER's satisfaction; and
- after coated sections are lowered in, the pipe shall be cut in a manner which provides a proper bevel, correct alignment, uniform spacing, and the required amount of slack in the finished line.

External line-up clamps are permitted.

B5.15 The minimum length of pipe which shall be used in the mainline shall be 2 meters. Joints of 2 meters or longer shall be carried forward and welded into the line.

B5.16 Welded sections of pipe shall not be moved for any reason, until all welds have cooled to ambient temperature.

B5.17 Any fire damage to insulation or coating due to preheating, etc., shall be repaired.
