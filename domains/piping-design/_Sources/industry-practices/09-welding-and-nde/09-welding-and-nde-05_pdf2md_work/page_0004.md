B5.0 PRODUCTION WELDING

B5.1 Before alignment of each joint, pipe and components shall be visually inspected for dirt, debris, and damage. All dirt and debris shall be removed. Mainline pipe shall be cleaned with a leather or a canvas belt disc of the proper diameter, or by another method acceptable to the OWNER. No dirt or debris will be permitted in the pipeline system. Any damage observed during visual inspection shall be corrected to the satisfaction of the OWNER, before fitting and welding.

B5.2 If the pipe has a longitudinal or spiral seam, the seam of each successive mainline joint shall be offset from the previous joint by at least 20°, such that the seams remain on the top quadrant of the pipe after lowering into the ditch. Longitudinal seams in field bends shall be located on the neutral axis of the bend, so that the longitudinal seam is in the top quadrant after the bend is installed.

B5.3 The line must be adequately and securely supported by proper skids or cribbing to prevent movement or sagging during welding.

B5.4 The pipe shall be supported in a manner which provides a minimum clearance of 400mm (16") between the joint to be welded and the ground.

B5.5 Cutting or welding, other than that required for the making of normal circumferential joints, will not be allowed without prior consent of the OWNER.

B5.6 Electrode holders shall be insulated.

B5.7 All mainline and tie-in welds shall be aligned using internal or external line up clamps.

- When external line-up clamps are used for the mainline, the clamp shall not be removed until at least 50% of the root bead has been completed and evenly spaced about the circumference of the pipe.
- For pipe 219.1 mm (8.625") OD and larger, after the clamp has been removed, the side boom tractor shall not be released for the next pipe until the root bead has been completed.
- When line-up clamps are used for fabrication welding, they may be removed whenever the joint is securely tacked for welding.

B5.8 During mainline construction over rough terrain, the OWNER may require that application of a hot pass equal to 25% of the pipe circumference, on the bottom of the joint, before release of the line-up clamp, and before release of the side boom tractor for the next joint.
