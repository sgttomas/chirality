## B3.2 Welder and Welding Operator Performance Qualification

**B3.2.1** Each welder used by the Pipeline Contractor shall pass performance qualification tests according to CSA Z662. Qualification tests may be made on the job site or at a site suitable to both Pipeline Contractor and OWNER.

**B3.2.2** For performance qualification tests, the Pipeline Contractor shall:

- prepare coupons for welding from line pipe supplied by the OWNER
- furnish all necessary welding equipment and welding consumables
- allow test coupons to cool in still air to ambient temperature before testing
- satisfy the test requirements defined by CSA Z662 and any supplementary NDE requirements stated in Owner's Specification 000-PED-5009 NDE Examination
- record the results of all welder and welding operator performance qualification tests on forms similar to those presented in CSA Z662
- prepare and maintain a list of welders and their qualifications, which shall be updated if there are changes of welding personnel or at least once per week, and submitted to the OWNER.

**B3.2.3** If the OWNER and Pipeline Contractor agree that a test failure was caused by conditions outside the control of the welder, a retest may be permitted.

## B4.0 TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

**B4.1** All welding shall be completed using the manual SMAW process, unless otherwise approved by OWNER.

**B4.2** Mainline production welds shall be completed using a vertical down progression, cellulose-coated electrodes, and DC current with reverse polarity (electrode positive).

**B4.3** All welding consumables shall be supplied by the Pipeline Contractor, and subject to OWNER approval.

**B4.4** The following requirements govern welding electrode selection.

- Electrodes classified with a minimum tensile strength of 550 MPa (80 ksi) or higher shall not be used.
- E6010 electrodes shall be used for root beads.
- E6010 or E7010-G electrodes shall be used for mainline downhill welding of the hot pass, fill and cap. Selected classification shall be consistent with tensile strength requirements.
- Carbon steel low hydrogen electrodes (i.e., E7018-1) shall be used for fill and cap of above ground facility piping.
