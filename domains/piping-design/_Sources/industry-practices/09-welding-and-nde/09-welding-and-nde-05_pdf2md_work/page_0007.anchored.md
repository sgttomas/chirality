**B6.0    WORKMANSHIP, QUALITY CONTROL, NONDESTRUCTIVE EXAMINATION, AND REPAIRS**

B6.1    Requirements governing workmanship, quality control, and nondestructive examination are found in this specification, Owner's Specification 000-PES-5009 NDE Examination and CSA Z662.

B6.2    Arc strikes and arc burns shall be:

- cut out as a cylinder for mainline pipe;
- treated according to CSA Z662 for non-sour above ground pipe and piping components;
- treated according to CSA Z662 if present on the outside surface of sour above ground pipe and piping components; and
- cut out as a cylinder if present on the inside surface of sour pipe and piping components, except that heat treatment of the affected area may be considered as a remedy, at the discretion of the OWNER. (Z662-94 Cl. 7.2.8)

B6.3    When arc strikes or arc burns are removed by grinding or filing, verification of removal is required in accordance with CSA Z662, and the OWNER may also require verification of minimum wall thickness by ultrasonic methods, and magnetic particle examination of the affected area for evidence of cracks. Cracks are unacceptable regardless of size.

B6.4    Welders shall identify their work by marking their assigned symbol beside each weld on the top quarter of the pipe, with a waterproof crayon or paint stick. Punch marks or die stamps are not permitted. (Z662-94 Cl. 7.2.7.12)

B6.5    The Pipeline Contractor shall cooperate fully with personnel of the NDE Contractor. Welds, including repair and replacement welds, shall be left uncoated, uncovered and accessible for NDE until verbal confirmation of final acceptability has been received from the OWNER.

B6.6    The OWNER may require cutouts to be tested during the course of construction. If a weld fails the test it will not be considered one of the cutouts. The cost of cutting out and replacing up to the maximum number of cutouts specified in the table below shall be borne by the Pipeline Contractor. The Pipeline Contractor shall supply the testing equipment and prepare the coupons.
