000-PES-5008-A
Welding of Process Piping and Pressure Equipment

[... logo Plains Midstream]

## 1.0 Introduction

### 1.1 Scope

1.1.1 This specification supplements requirements contained in the main body of the PES 5008 specification, and covers welding and postweld heat treatment of process piping, pressure vessels, and heat transfer equipment. Nondestructive Examination requirements are contained in Owner's Specification 000-PES-5009 NDE Examination.

### 1.2 Reference Documents

1.2.1 All welding and postweld heat treatment shall satisfy the minimum requirements of the latest edition of governing codes, standards, and specifications including:

- ASME Section IX.
- ASME B31.3.
- ASME Section VIII, Division 1.
- Applicable API standards.

## 2.0 General and Administrative Requirements

2.1 ASME welding procedures shall be registered by appropriate regulatory authorities.

## 3.0 Welding Qualifications

3.1 Each welder and welding operator shall be qualified to ASME IX for each welding process to be used in production welding.

## 4.0 Technical Requirements

4.1 The finished edges of plates thicker than 12.5 mm (1/2") shall be examined for lamination using MT or UT methods. Indications shall not exceed the limits of ASME SA-20, Table A2.14.

4.2 Low-hydrogen welding consumables shall be used on all pressure retaining welds, with the exception of the root bead and hot pass of P No. 1 piping materials, which may be welded with cellulose coated electrodes.

4.3 Welds requiring notch toughness shall be welded using filler metals with notch toughness proven at the minimum design temperature (i.e., use of E7018-1 rather than E7018 when impact testing is required of P No. 1 steels).

4.4 Preheat for pressure piping shall satisfy the minimum requirements of ASME B31.3. Recommended preheat within ASME B31.3 shall be treated as a mandatory requirement.

4.5 Preheat for pressure vessels and heat transfer equipment shall satisfy the minimum requirements of ASME VIII, Division 1, Appendix R, or ASME VIII, Division 2, Appendix D, as applicable. Recommended preheat within the applicable divisions of ASME VIII shall be treated as a mandatory requirement.

4.6 For ASME welding procedures, unless otherwise specified on the WPS, the preheat temperature shall also be considered as the minimum interpass temperature. The minimum interpass temperature shall be maintained throughout the welding operation.

4.7 Process piping PWHT shall be performed:

- according to ASME B31.3 and this specification:

- for all P No. 5 materials regardless of thickness, except for socket welds in sizes NPS 2 and smaller, and non-pressure containing fillet welds with throats not exceeding 12.7 mm (1/2").

4.8 Maximum hardness shall be:

- 200 HB for all P No. 1 materials; and
- in accordance with applicable industry codes, standards, and specifications for all other materials. Also refer to PES-5008 Welding specification for maximum production hardness in P.6.3.

4.9 When PWHT is required for pressure vessels or heat transfer equipment, it shall be completed as a single unit in a furnace of adequate capacity, unless prior written approval has been obtained from the OWNER. When it is not possible to PWHT pressure vessels or heat transfer equipment as a single unit, the fabricator shall address the effect of aggregate times a temperature for areas subject to multiple PWHT cycles, to the satisfaction of the OWNER.

4.10 PWHT procedures for lined or clad piping or equipment shall be approved by the OWNER. Consideration shall be given to venting requirements for lined piping or equipment.

## 5.0 Production Welding

5.1 On pressure equipment having skirts, no tack welds are permitted on the inside of the skirt between the head and the skirt.

5.2 For double-welded joints, the backside of the joint shall be cleaned to sound metal before welding the back side. See Owner's Specification 000-PES-5009 NDE Examination for specific NDE requirements prior to welding the back side.

5.3 For heat transfer equipment, when full penetration groove welds are required and access or high preheat requirements do not permit double welded joints, the root pass of single welded joints shall be made with the GMAW or GTAW processes.

GMAW-sc. (short-circuiting mode of metal transfer) process can only be used in flat and rolled-on positions.

## 6.0 Workmanship, Quality Control, Nondestructive Examination, and Repairs

6.1 Additional NDE requirements are contained in Owner's Specification 000-PES-5009 NDE Examination.

6.2 Arc strikes and arc burns shall be:

- removed from pressure vessels and heat transfer equipment by grinding or filing;
- removed from the outside surface of process piping systems and process coils by grinding, filing, or cut out as a cylinder; and
- removed by cut out as a cylinder if present on the inside surface of sour process piping systems or process coils, except that subsequent heat treatment of the affected area may be applied as a remedy, subject to compliance with minimum wall thickness requirements and approval from the OWNER.

6.3 When arc strikes or arc burns are removed by grinding or filing, the OWNER may require verification of removal using a suitable etching technique, verification of minimum wall thickness by ultrasonic methods, and magnetic particle examination of the affected area for evidence of cracks. Cracks are unacceptable regardless of size.

6.4 The fabricator/contractor shall provide the OWNER with all information necessary to verify that the quality control, detailed examinations and tests required by governing industry codes, standards, and specifications have been performed at the stages of fabrication necessary to permit them to be meaningful.

6.5 Pressure vessel production impact test plates shall be made when impact testing is required. The production impact test plates shall be made for each qualified welding procedure as per ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII, Division 1, UG-84(i). Results of production impact tests shall be incorporated into the fabrication records and submitted to the OWNER.

## 7.0 Sour Service Requirements

7.1 All welding and materials for sour service shall meet the requirements of NACE MR0175.
7.2 Butt, tee and corner joints attaching non-pressure parts to pressure parts on the process side of any pressure vessels or heat transfer equipment shall be made using full penetration groove welds.
7.3 Butt, tee and corner joints attaching non-pressure parts to pressure parts on the outside surface of any pressure vessels or heat transfer equipment shall be made using full penetration groove welds. Lap type joints, such as those used for reinforcing pads, poison pads, and lifting lugs, shall be made using continuous fillet welds, and the interface between the parts shall be vented to prevent accumulation of hydrogen.
