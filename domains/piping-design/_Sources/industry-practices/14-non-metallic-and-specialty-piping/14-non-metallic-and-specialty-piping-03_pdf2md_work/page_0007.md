c. Necessary cure time shall be provided

d. Pot life of adhesive shall not be exceeded

e. Shelf life of adhesive shall not be exceeded

#### 4.3.4.2 Butt and Wrapped Joints

Based on extensive experience from failures, the following precautions shall be strictly observed:

a. Improper fit-up of joints shall not be permitted

b. Excessive mismatch in wall thicknesses shall not be permitted

c. Inadequate guiding or contamination of joining surface shall not be permitted

d. Pot life of resin mix shall not be exceeded

#### 4.3.4.3 Curing of Joints

1. Proper curing of joints shall be performed in accordance with manufacturer's recommendation.

2. Many manufacturers recommend heat application to cure the bonded joints. Heat curing shall be performed strictly in accordance with Manufacturer's recommended heat cure requirements.

3. Thermostatically controlled heat blankets shall be used to assure proper application of heat to cure the bonded joints. Hot air guns, infrared lamps, or alternate forms of heat application shall not be permitted.

### 4.3.5 Branch Connections

4.3.5.1 Branch connections to the main shall be made with reducing saddle fittings, tees or tees with reducing bushings.

4.3.5.2 Reducing saddle fittings shall be properly bonded to the main pipe.

## 4.4 Installation

### 4.4.1 General

4.4.1.1 Installation shall be in accordance with the pipe manufacturer's recommendations and this Practice.

4.4.1.2 All necessary precautions shall be taken to ensure a safe working environment in accordance with applicable codes and standards.

4.4.1.3 The support spacing for horizontal RTR piping shall not exceed the pipe manufacturer's recommendations for the type and size of pipe, wall thickness, operating temperature, and liquid specific gravity.
