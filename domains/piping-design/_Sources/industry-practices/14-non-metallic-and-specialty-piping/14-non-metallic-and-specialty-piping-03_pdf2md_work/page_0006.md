e. "Fiberglass" (Glass-Fiber-Reinforced Thermosetting-Resin) Pressure Pipe Fittings - ASTM D 5685

4.3.1.9 Fabricated parts requiring trial shop assembly shall be match-marked to facilitate erection in the field.

## 4.3.2 Application of Adhesive

4.3.2.1 Piping joints shall be made in accordance with the manufacturer's adhesive application procedures.

4.3.2.2 Records of trained personnel shall be maintained, and shall certify that training was received not more than 6 months before commencing work.

4.3.2.3 To prevent internal buildup of adhesive and restriction to the pipe flow, the use of excessive adhesive shall be avoided. However, if after joint assembly adhesive is not extruded from the free end of the socket, insufficient adhesive was applied prior to make up.

4.3.2.4 Manufacturer's recommended repair procedure shall be followed to correct problems with joint assembly.

4.3.2.5 Following assembly or reassembly, extruded adhesive shall be wiped away to form a smooth fillet which covers the free end of the socket.

4.3.2.6 After applying adhesive to the joints, the assembly shall only be moved after the adhesive has cured in accordance with the manufacturer's recommended application instructions.

## 4.3.3 Butt and Wrapped Joints

4.3.3.1 Butted joints shall be made by overwrapping the prepared butted ends of pipe or fitting with multiple layers of resin-saturated fiberglass mat and roving.

4.3.3.2 Joints for contact-molded pipe and fittings shall be in accordance with *ASTM D 6041*, Paragraph 6.2.

4.3.3.3 Custom-made supply kits with the necessary materials and detailed instructions shall be obtained from the manufacturer of filament-wound or centrifugally cast pipe and fittings.

## 4.3.4 Precautions with Joints

### 4.3.4.1 Adhesive Joints

Based on extensive experience from failures, the following precautions shall be strictly observed:

a. Contamination of joining surfaces shall not be permitted
b. Excessive use of adhesive shall not be permitted
