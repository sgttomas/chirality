## 3.0 Execution

### 3.1 *Field Fabrication*

#### 3.1.1 Piping

All butt fusion bonding shall be in strict accordance with the fusion bonding procedure approved for the project.

#### 3.1.2 Internal and Misalignment

a. Internal misalignment shall be less than 10% of the pipe wall thickness.

b. If the 10% misalignment is exceeded, use one of the following procedures listed in order of preference:

   1. First, rotate the pipe or fittings to reduce misalignment to the acceptable tolerances.
   2. Use the internal lineup clamps to correct moderate out-of-round condition
   3. Taper the wall of the component internally after obtaining approval from Suncor Engineering, to ensure wall thickness is not reduced below the minimum required.

#### 3.1.3 Bead (Flash) Removal

The bead or flash may interfere with slipping lap joint flanges against the stub end. Removal of part of the bead to accommodate easier removal of the flange is permitted as long as the removal does not leave any sharp notches. Use of a bead removal tool is preferred but use of a copping knife of file is also permitted. Proper safety precautions shall be used to assure method of removal does not cause injury.

#### 3.1.4 Pup-Pieces

Pup-pieces Installed to make up a length shall be a minimum of 2 pipe diameters long or 6" long, unless authorized otherwise by Suncor Engineering.

### 3.2 *Inspection and Test Prior to Installation*

#### 3.2.1 Inspection

Fabricator's Inspection shall ensure fusion bonds and completion of inspection records are in accordance with practices equal to those in Attachment E, "Fusion Bonding - Field Inspection Checklist" and Attachment F, "Fusion Bonding - Field Inspection Record".

#### 3.2.2 Records

Records, equal to those indicated in Attachment E and F, shall be maintained by the fabricator and submitted to Suncor QA no later than the next working day.

#### 3.2.3 Testing

Suncor QA may select bonds at random prior to installation, which will be removed for destructive testing to verify quality standards are being met. The type of destructive testing will be left to the discretion of Suncor. Normal testing will involve performing 180° bends in accordance with Attachment D, "Butt Fusion Bonder Qualification Testing".
