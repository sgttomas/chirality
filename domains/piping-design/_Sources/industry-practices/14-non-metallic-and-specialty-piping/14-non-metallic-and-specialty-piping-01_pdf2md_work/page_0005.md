## 4.0 Materials

### 4.1 Pipe

HDPE pipe - PE3408 DR11, in accordance with OSG Piping Specifications "CHY", "CHZ" or "APB".

### 4.2 Fittings

HDPE fittings shall be in accordance with OSB Piping Specifications "CHY", "CHZ" or "APB" and shall be factory manufactured. Stub end adaptors to be HDPE Type, ASTM F714-DR11. Flanges to be pipe manufacturer's approved lapped joint. Gaskets shall be neoprene as per manufacturer's recommendation. Stud Bolts shall be ASTM A307 Gr. B, including washer for both sides (ASTM F606). All fittings to Suncor Energy piping specifications "CHY", "CHZ" or "APB".

### 4.3 Valves

All valves shall be as per OSB Piping Specifications "CHY", "CHZ" or "APB".

### 4.4 Insulation

Insulation shall be styrofoam min. HI-40 to CAN22CGSB-51.20-M87 (Type 4) or CAN/ULC S701 (Type 4).

### 4.5 Thrust Block

Concrete for thrust blocking shall be sulphate resistant, Type 50 developing a compressive strength of not less than 20 MPa in 28 days.

### 4.6 Bedding
