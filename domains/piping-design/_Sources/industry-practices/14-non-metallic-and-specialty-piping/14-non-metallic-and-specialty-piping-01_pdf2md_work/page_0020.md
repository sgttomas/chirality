1.3  *Submittals*

Documents created and used by Fabricator to produce, control, and manage this Work are subject to review of approval by responsible representatives of Suncor as follows:

1.3.1  QA (Quality Assurance) Manual

QA Manual shall be submitted to Suncor for review, comment and approval. The manual shall include the procedures, instructions, and QA systems, including responsibilities, accountabilities and lines of communication to ensure that assigned field fabrication and workmanship, and other related services conform to the design engineering specifications and other contractual requirements.

1.3.2  Bonding Procedures Specification (BPS)

Bonding procedures shall be submitted to Suncor for comment and review. The procedures shall be qualified in accordance with this specification.

1.3.3  Bonder's Performance Qualifications

Bonder's performance qualification records shall be submitted to Suncor for review. The bonder shall be qualified in accordance with this specification.

1.3.4  Inspection and Test Plan

All inspection and test plans, as required by this specification, shall be submitted to Suncor for approval by QA and Engineering prior to commencement of fabrication.

1.4  *Quality Assurance*

Suncor QA shall audit the activities of the fabrication and installation to ensure that procedures are being followed to meet quality objectives.

1.4.1  The Fabricator shall be responsible for fusion bonded joint integrity. Each joint may be subject to check by Suncor.

1.4.2  The fabricator's inspection shall examine and inspect the final product to ensure that quality criteria have been met.

1.4.3  The Fabricator shall examine and inspect piping components for damage and improper manufacturing prior to beginning fabrication, in accordance with ASME 831.3, Paragraph A341. After fabrication has been completed, components shall be re-examined and re-inspected for possible damage. Repairs shall be made before installation.

1.4.4  Suncor QA may select bonds at random prior to installation, which will be removed for destructive testing to verify quality standards are being met.

## 2.0  Products

2.1  *Materials*

2.1.1  Piping materials shall be in accordance with ASTM D 2513, Class PE 3408 and ASTM D3350, Class PE3408 or PE45434. The pipe dimensions shall be in accordance with ASTM F 714. The SDR rating shall be specified on the drawings.

2.1.2  Substitutions, including thicker or thinner wall materials, are not permitted without written authorization from Suncor Engineering.
