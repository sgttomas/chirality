## 3.0 Design

### 3.1 General

3.1.1 HDPE does not react with most fluids being pumped. The Engineering Characteristics brochure should be reviewed to check HDPE pipe resistance to various chemicals, the operating temperature and the concentration of the chemical itself.

3.1.2 HDPE pipe should not be used in areas where significant concentrations of hydrocarbon are present in soils.

3.1.3 In considering the design Limits for a HDPE system, the interdependence of the operating pressure, temperature, safety factor and the expected life should be taken into account Special consideration should be given to surge pressures and vacuum in the system.

3.1.4 Pipe wall thickness (DR) selection should be based on earth loads, live traffic loads, soil compaction and trench design.

3.1.5 The piping system can be designed as conventional deep bury system or shallow bury insulated system.

### 3.2 *Deep Bury Installation*

3.2.1 Pipes carrying water and other fluid that have similar freezing temperature to water should be buried at a minimum depth of 10'-8" to the top of the pipe. See Drawing A1.0 in Appendix A for details.

### 3.3 *Shallow Bury Insulated Installation*

3.3.1 Where deep bury installation is not possible, practical or economical, the piping system can be installed in insulated trenches at shallow depths. The minimum depth to the top of the pipe should be 6'-0". A layer of 5" styrofoam insulation should be placed at a minimum depth of 5'-0". In special circumstances, pipe bury depth can be revised to less than 6'-0", however, the insulation thickness will have to be increased. Insulation manufacturer's instruction shall be followed to calculate the revised thickness of the insulation. See Drawing A1.1 in Appendix A for details.

3.3.2 The width of the insulation for the pipe buried at a minimum of 6'-0" shall be 12'-0". The insulation can be designed as horizontal layer or as "inverted U". The sum of the width and vertical legs of the "inverted U" insulation shall be minimum 12'-0". See Drawings A1.2 and A1.3 in Appendix A for details.

3.3.3 Styrofoam insulation is manufactured in varying compressive strengths; insulation strength should be designed for a particular application which depends on a total load calculation.
