- 2.1.3 All HDPE materials supplied shall include material test certificates which include the test results as required by ASTM D3350. The resin manufacturer and melt index per ASTM D1238 used shall be identified in the fusion bonding procedure.

## 2.2 Material Receiving and Storage

- 2.2.1 Within the storage area, segregate pipe and fittings by material resin type, size, and SDR rating.

- 2.2.2 Upon receipt, Fabricator shall visually inspect every pipe and components. Items which contain injurious defects shall be rejected. Injurious defects are considered as misalignment in access of 10% for fabricated fittings, Internal or external surface gases, scars, scratches, blisters, or discontinuities that produce a notch effect or reduce the specified wall thickness by 5 percent or more.

## 2.3 Bonding Procedure Specification

- 2.3.1 A qualified BPS in accordance with ASME B31.3 par. A328 shall be supplied for hot plate butt fusion bonding. The qualification shall be performed on the same resin which will be used for the work.

- 2.3.2 Each qualification coupon shall be tested in accordance with ASTM D2857, par. 9.3. The testing shall be performed in accordance with Attachment A, "Butt Fusion Bonding Procedure Qualification Testing".

- 2.3.3 The BPS shall identify pre-bonding equipment inspection requirements. Attachment B, "Fusion Bonding - Work Instructions - Equipment Pre-Inspection" may be used as an example.

- 2.3.4 The BPS shall identify each step in the fusion process. The heating temperature, pressure and time as well as the fusion pressure and time shall be clearly identified for each pipe size and SDR rating. The fusion machine's hydraulic pressure shall be calculated. The use of nomographs, like those supplied by McElroy Manufacturing Inc. are, discouraged. Attachment C, "Butt Fusion Bonding Procedure Specification" may be used as an example of an acceptable procedure.

## 2.4 Fusion Bonder Performance Qualifications

- 2.4.1 Fusion bonders must pass a project qualification test prior to performing any fusion bonding. The qualifications shall be performed in accordance with ASME B31.3, par. 3282.5 with the additional essential requirement of using the heaviest SDR pipe wall that will be used during production, for the qualification testing. In lieu of the hydro testing qualification, fusion bonders may be qualified by testing in accordance with Attachment D, "Fusion Bonder's Qualification Testing".

## 2.5 Bonding Procedure Specification for Fusing Dissimilar HDPE Pipes

- 2.5.1 Butt fusing different HDPE resins require a separate BPS and must be qualified as stated in paragraph 2.3 of this specification. The guidelines of PPI-TN-13, "General Guidelines for the Heat Fusion of Unlike Polyethylene Pipes and Fittings", shall be used. The difference in heating time for each pipe must be established and identified in the procedure. This should be based on the flow characteristics (melt index) of each pipe.
