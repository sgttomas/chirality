**CADWELD**

Components labeled on diagram:
- HANDLE
- STARTING POWDER
- WELD METAL (IGNITES AND BURNS THROUGH DISC)
- CAVITY IN WELDER
- COPPER SLEEVE OVER WIRE
- METAL DISC
- WIRE
- HYDRANT OR VALVE

**WELD METAL CAPSULE**

Components labeled on diagram:
- PLASTIC CAPSULE AND LID
- WELD METAL
- STARTING POWDER (SQUEEZE CAPSULE TO REMOVE)

**FINISHED WELD**

Components labeled on diagram:
- WIRE
- HANDY CAP
- PRIMER
- HYDRANT
- WIRE STRIPPED TO 1.5"
- THERMITE WELD

NOTE: REPAIR COATING AT THERMOWELD LOCATION USING HANDICAP & TRENTON TEC-TAPE OR EQUAL.
