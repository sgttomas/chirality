## 1.0 Objective

### 1.1 *Scope*

1.1.1 This specification outlines the basis for design, installation, testing and quality control procedures for the High Density Polyethylene (HDPE) pipes in underground applications.

1.1.2 Standard Detail Drawings, attached at the end of these Specifications, illustrate installation details.

### 1.2 *Terminology*

1.2.1 The '**Owner**' means Suncor Energy Inc.

1.2.2 The '**Buyer**' – means Suncor or its agent designated for that project.

1.2.3 '**Owner's Engineer**' - means the person responsible for the technical integrity of the project.

1.2.4 '**Owner's Inspector**' – means the person authorized for inspection.

1.2.5 The '**Supplier**' - means the entity, manufacturer, fabricator, vendor or contractor that supplies the material or services.

## 2.0 Referenced Codes and Regulations

The following codes and standards form an integral part of this specification. Any conflict between this specification, or referenced standard or code shall be brought to the attention of the Owner's Engineer for resolution prior to proceeding. All referenced codes and standards are to be of the latest edition or revision.
