*4.0 TORQUES*

4.6.1 Three torques shall be performed. This shall be done by placing the coupon In a vice with the bond bead located approximately 1" above the top of the vice jaws. A pipe wrench shall be used to make twist the coupon a minimum of 3600, with the edge of the pipe wrench being located 1" from the edge of the bead.

4.6.2 A failure which snaps in 1800 of the twist may indicate a poor bond. Failures which occur beyond 3600 could be considered acceptable.

*4.7 IMPACTS*

4.7.1 Three impacts shall be performed. This shall be done by placing the coupon in a vice with the bond bead located approximately 1" above the top of the vice jaws. A heavy hammer or pipe can be used to "impact" the specimen.

4.7.2 A failure which occurs quickly and in a brittle manner may indicate a poor bond area. When the coupon bends and shows elongation at the bond from the force of the impacts, this can be considered acceptable.

*4.8 RE-TESTING OF PROCEDURE QUALIFICATION COUPONS*

4.8.1 If a coupon failure is indicated, upon approval from Succor's QA, two re-tests of the same coupon may be performed. If one of these coupons were to fail, the qualification needs to be redone.

## 5.0 Fusion Bonding-Work Instructions-Fusion Bonding Equipment

5.1 Fusion bonding equipment shall be inspected prior to use on the project to verify ability to perform. Once the equipment leaves the Succor site this inspection must be redone upon return at any other time to the site. Re-inspection may need to be performed if problems with fusion bond quality are encountered. The results of the inspection shall be recorded on the attached form "Attachment E - Fusion Bonding Field' Inspection Checklist". The inspection shall include as a minimum:

5.1.1 Verify heating plate has a thermostat which can control the overall heater plate surface between the temperatures of 350 to 500°F.

5.1.2 Verify heating plate has a non-stick coated surface which is free from scratches, dirt, grease, etc.

5.1.3 Verify heating plate has a calibrated built in thermometer.

5.1.4 Verify a calibrated surface pyrometer is available to check the heating surface temperature.

5.1.5 Verify the pressure gauge range and increments are suitable for the fusion pressures to be used throughout the production fusion bonding (i.e. A gauge with a maximum pressure rating of 10,000 PSI with 500 PSI pressure indicator increments would not be suitable for production bonding pressures of 100 PSI).
