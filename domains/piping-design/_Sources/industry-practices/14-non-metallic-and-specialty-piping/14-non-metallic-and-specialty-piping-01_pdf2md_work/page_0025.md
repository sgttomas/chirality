5.1.6 Verify a calibrated hydraulic pressure gauge is installed. The calibration shall have been performed within the last three months. Liquid filled gauges are preferred. Gauges shall have increments which are easily readable during the fusion bonding process.

5.1.7 Verify the hydraulic cylinder sizes and areas are clearly indicated.

5.1.8 Verify the hydraulic oil level is adequate. Add oil if necessary.

5.1.9 Verify the facing cutters are in good operating condition. Machines using sealed bearings on the cutters rather than requiring manual lubrication maintenance are recommended. Blades should project between 0.010" and 0.015" and be parallel to the face. Replace any worn or chipped cutters.

5.1.10 Verify alignment clamps, jigs and pipe pullers are in good operating condition.
