# 1.0 General

## 1.1 *Summary*

### 1.1.1 Scope of Specification

a. This specification prescribes the criteria for fusion bonding high density polyethylene piping and fittings used for pressure applications. Fusion bonding using this specification will satisfy pressure piping used for ASME B31.3 pressure piping applications and firewater piping meeting National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) requirements.

b. No deviation shall be made from this specification without written authorization from Owner's Engineer.

c. This specification may be used for shop as well as field fabrication.

### 1.1.2 Terminology

a. Shop Fabricate: Fabrication performed at a Supplier's shop, normally remote from the jobsite.

b. Field Fabricate: Fabrication performed at the jobsite, either in a field shop or at the installation site area.

c. HDPE: High density polyethylene.

d. Butt-Fusion Bonding: The use of a hot plate to heat the ends of HDPE pipe to a temperature which is conducive to allowing the ends to be fused together upon the application of pressure.

e. Sidewall-Fusion Bonding: The use of a contoured heating plate to heat the ends of a saddle face simultaneously with the surface of the pipe to a temperature which is conducive to allowing the saddle to be fused to the pipe upon application of pressure.
