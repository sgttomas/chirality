5.2.5 Follow recommendations of the pipe manufacturer regarding HDPE flanged connections. The flanged connection must be tightened initially and then allowed to creep to relieve the cold working stresses for a minimum of 8 hours, and then re-tightened.

*5.3 VALVES*

5.3.1 Place valve level to ensure the vertical stem and above grade post indicator can be placed directly above the valve in a vertical orientation.

5.3.2 All buried components, including the valve body, stem, box and below grade post indicator assembly or valve box are to be wrapped in a continuous water barrier wrapping. See Drawings A1.4 and A1.5 in Appendix A for details.

5.3.3 An annular space around the stem box is to consist only of packed gravel. The packed gravel annulus must extend a minimum of 4" radially around the valve stem box. Place a Sono-Tube to contain the gravel during backfilling.

*5.4 THRUST BLOCKING*

5.4.1 Concrete reaction blocks and supports are to be in accordance with CAN/CSA A23.1. Reinforcement shall be placed in accordance with CAN/CSA-A23.1. Place thrust blocking under valves, hydrant bowls and monitors. Do not place load upon new concrete until it is set.

5.4.2 Concrete reaction blocks shall be placed between the undisturbed ground and the fitting or valve. A layer of 12 mil polyethylene sheet is to be placed between the valve body or hydrant bowl and the concrete to prevent surface corrosion.

5.4.3 The placement of the concrete block must allow access to all flange bolts on both ends of valves.

*5.5 INSULATION*

5.5.1 Protect insulation from exposure to ultraviolet light, physical damage and contact with incompatible chemicals.

5.5.2 Place a minimum of 2" thickness of granular sand material as bedding for the insulation.

5.5.3 Install insulation with staggered joints to maintain continuity of thermal protection. Cut and trim insulation neatly to fit spaces. Butt joints tightly, offset vertical joints. Use only insulation free from chipped or broken edges. Use largest possible dimensions to reduce the number of joints. Place insulation in staggered fashion with long axis parallel to the crown of the embankment.

5.5.4 Take precautions to prevent movement of the insulation during placement of the granular cover. Immediately following placement of insulation, carefully place 10" thickness of granular material over the insulation, taking special precautions not to damage the boards. This minimum coverage of the granular material is required before the surface can be subjected to any vehicular traffic.

5.5.5 Place insulation between pipes to be crossed for shallow-bury installation. See Drawing A1.6 in Appendix A for details.
