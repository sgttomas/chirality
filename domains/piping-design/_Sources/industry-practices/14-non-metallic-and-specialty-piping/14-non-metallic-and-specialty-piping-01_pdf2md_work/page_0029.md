7.1.3 Coupons shall be cut from each 1200 section of the bond. Four coupons shall be extracted from each 1200 section. The coupons shall be 12" thick through the thickness of the pipe wall perpendicular to the bond. Refer to Figure 1, Paragraph 4.3.

## 7.2 Visual Examination

7.2.1 The coupon shall be visually examined for soundness. The melted bead shall be uniform around the entire circumference and show complete roll over on both the internal and external side of the joint. There shall be no misalignment.

7.2.2 Melt bead width will depend on the pipe size and thickness and shall not be used as a rejection or acceptance method.

## 7.3 Destructive Tests

7.3.1 Six coupons shall be extracted from the test coupon as indicated in Figure 1. Three 1800 inside face bends and three 1800 outside face bends shall be performed. There shall be no indication of disbondment or separation during the bend test.

7.3.2 Where the thickness of the pipe prevents performing a complete 1800 bend without buckling, the coupon may be cut in half Care shall be exercised to prevent implanting notches into the coupons while performing the cutting operation (remove any suspicious notches).

7.3.3 If a failure is indicated, upon approval from Suncor, two additional coupons can be removed and tested next two the failure. Failure of any of these two coupons constitutes a failed qualification.

## 7.4 Hydro Testing

7.4.1 If requested by Suncor, the full size coupon shall be hydrotested at four times the design pressure of the pipe in accordance with ASME B31.3, par. A328.52.

## 7.5 Bonder's Re-Qualification Testing

7.5.1 Bonder's who have their fusion bonds fail the above testing, shall not be re-tested without Suncor's QA approval.

## 7.6 Records

7.6.1 The fabricator shall maintain records of all bonders who have passed the above qualification testing. The record shall include the BPS qualified, size of and SDR rating of coupon qualified and date.
