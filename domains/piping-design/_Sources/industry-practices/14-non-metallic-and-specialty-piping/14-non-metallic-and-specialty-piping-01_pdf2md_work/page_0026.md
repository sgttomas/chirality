6.2.6 Position the facing tool in between pipe ends. Cut the pipe until the stops on each side of the facer are in contact with the clamp bushings. Separate the pipes and check for squareness of the cuts. DO NOT TOUCH THE PIPE SURFACE AT ANY TIME AFTER THIS STEP. Oil from the hands will contaminate the pipe and cause a poor bond.

6.2.7 Bring the two pipes together and check for misalignment. If misalignment is greater than 10% of the wall thickness at any single location, it must be corrected. Minor misalignment may be corrected by tightening the inside clamps at the high side. If the inside clamps have been adjusted or 15 minutes has elapsed since the pipe has been trimmed to when the heater plate is inserted, it will be necessary to re-trim the pipe.

6.2.8 Verify that the heater plate is at the desired fusion temperature by examining the thermometer. It is necessary to check the heater plate temperature accuracy four times daily using a contact pyrometer. The temperature shall be checked at 450 intervals around both sides of the heater plate circumference. The allowable variance is ± 10°F.

6.2.9 Separate the pipe ends and insert the heater plate, making sure that the plate does not come into contact with the pipe. Bring the two ends together against the heater. Apply, by hand or automatic actuation, the calculated heating pressure plus the drag pressure to the pipe ends. This pressure shall be applied until the appearance of a bead starts to form around the entire circumference on the outside of the pipe. Once this bead has initially formed, the pressure shall be removed, and the heating time shall start. The heating time is identified on the Fusion Bonding Procedure Chart 1. The "melt" should form an even bead around the entire circumference. Uneven melt beads are an indication that one side of the pipe is hotter than the other. This may occur during a cold sunny day, when the sun heats up the top of the cold pipe. It may be necessary to insulate the top of the pipe, to prevent this form of uneven heating. Wind and rain may also cause uneven pipe temperatures. The fusion bonding area should be protected from weather.

6.2.10 Once the heating time has elapsed, the pipes shall be separated away from the heater plate. The heater plate shall be quickly removed and the pipes brought back together with the fusion pressure, plus drag applied. The pressure shall be applied for the time indicated on the Fusion Bonding Procedure Chart 1. Upon squeezing the pipe ends together, the force applied should cause the bead to roll back onto the pipe producing an even bead around the circumference.

Note: If the bead does not "roll over" back onto the pipe in an even manner, it is an indication of a faulty fusion, and the fusion bond shall be cut out and redone. When removing the heater plate, if it comes in contact with the pipe where the melt is disturbed, the fusion shall be stopped and redone. If it takes longer than 12 seconds to remove the plate and squeeze the pipes, the fusion shall be stopped and redone.
