## 4.0 Scope-But-Fusion Bonding Procedure Qualification Testing

4.1 This specification identifies the procedures used for destructive testing a thick walled Hi h Density Polyethylene pipe joint for the purpose of qualifying the fusion bonding procedure. The basis for type of tests is based on ASTM D2657, par. 9.3. The four types of tests identified are:

- 4.1.1 Tensiles
- 4.1.2 Bends
- 4.1.3 Torques
- 4.1.4 Impacts

4.2 These tests should be considered qualitative in determining the difference between a good bond and a poor bond. The quality depends on the type of failure which occurs. What may appear as a failure may not indicate a poor bond. It is important that for thick sections, all notches are removed from the coupons. This may be achieved by removing the flash from the fusion. For qualification interpretation, Suncor shall be notified if a coupon fails a test.

*4.3 TEST COUPON SIZE AND LOCATION*

4.3.1 Coupons shall be cut from each 1200 section of the bond. Four coupons shall be extracted from each 1200 section. The coupons shall be ½" thick through the thickness of the pipe wall perpendicular to the bond. Refer to Figure 1.

4.4 *TENSILES*

4.4.1 Three tensile tests shall be performed. The specimens shall be machined to a size similar to ASME Section IX weld coupon requirements.

4.4.2 The specimen shall fail in a ductile manner showing a distinct amount of elongation. A specimen which fails quickly and one which indicates a shear non bonded face indicates a poor bond.
