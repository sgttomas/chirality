or

$$P = (2S \times TF \times EF \times JF)/(SDR-1)$$

(Eq. 400-3)

where:

- P = Design pressure, psi
- S = Hydrostatic design stress (HDS)
  - = 2000 psi for PVC 1120, PVC 1220, PVC 2120 and CPVC 4120
  - = 1600 psi for PVC 2116
  - = 1200 psi for PVC 2112
  - = 1000 psi for PVC 2110

*Note   The last two digits in the material designation multiplied by 100 equal the HDS. The HDS equals the hydrostatic design basis (HDB) divided by two. ASTM D2837 is used to determine the HDB.*

- t = Minimum wall thickness, in.
- D_o = Outside diameter, in.
- SDR = Standard dimension ratio
  - = D_o/t
- TF = Temperature derating factor, as follows:
