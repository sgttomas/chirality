**LAP JOINT FLANGES (also called Van Stone flanges):** Although this is the most common method for joining HDPE pipe to dissimilar piping materials, other types of nonmetallic pipe can also be joined this way. However, ANSI B31.3 prefers flat face flanges and gaskets and does not allow lap joint flanges for severe cyclic service.

**ADHESIVE JOINTS:** Pipe ends are joined with adhesives which match the FRP material used (i.e., epoxy, polyester, vinyl ester, etc.). The adhesives are applied to male and female pipe ends with a brush prior to joint mating and typically cure in less than six hours at 70°F. This is a common, low cost method for joining FRP, but unfortunately these joints are very sensitive to installation practices and are the most likely joints to come apart due to installer error. Adhesive joints come in various mating end configurations and those which combine mechanical (threaded) with adhesive joining are more reliable. "Heat packs" decrease curing time and can increase joint reliability (less chance of damaging the joint before it sets).
