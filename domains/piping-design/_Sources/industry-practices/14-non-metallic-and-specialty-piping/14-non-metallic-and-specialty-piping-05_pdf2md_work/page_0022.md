Notes: 1. The higher the Hazen-Williams coefficient or lower the Manning N-value the less fluid flow is restricted. By using pipe materials with improved flow characteristics, energy costs for pumping can be reduced or smaller pipe sizes can be used.

The Hazen-Williams formula for pressurized water is

$$\Delta P_{100} = 4521^{1.85}/C^{1.85}D^{4.86}$$

The Manning formula for gravity flow is

$$q = 1.486 \, AR^{2/3} S^{1/2}/N$$

where:

$\Delta P_{100}$ = pressure loss per 100 ft. of pipe, psi
Q = flow rate, GPM
C = Hazen-Williams coefficient
D = pipe internal diameter, in.
q = flow rate, ft³/sec
N = Manning value
A = cross sectional area of flow, ft²
R = hydraulic radius (=I.D./4), ft
S = slope of hydraulic gradient

See also the *Fluid Flow Manual* for further guidance on performing flow calculations.
