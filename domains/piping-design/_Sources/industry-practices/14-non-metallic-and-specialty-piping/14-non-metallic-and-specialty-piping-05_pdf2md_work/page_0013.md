**O-RING JOINTS (Restrained):** Because of reliability problems with field-fabricated adhesive joints, many FRP pipe manufacturers have developed proprietary O-ring joints which are mechanically restrained by various methods including threads, clamps and keys. These joints add cost to the pipe but these added costs can usually be recovered during installation, which is far less labor intensive than adhesive joints. HDPE Spirolite pipe can be axially restrained by fusion welding. Concrete cylinder pipe can be axially restrained by welding the steel cylinder.
