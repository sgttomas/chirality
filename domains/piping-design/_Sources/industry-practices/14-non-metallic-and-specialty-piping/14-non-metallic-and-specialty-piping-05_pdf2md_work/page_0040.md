#### Flow Properties/Pressure Surge, PVC and CPVC

PVC and CPVC have very good flow properties that do not degrade with time because there is no corrosion roughening. An accepted Hazen-Williams flow coefficient is 150. Should pigging ever become necessary, use smooth poly pigs only. Never use scraper pigs.

Most manufacturers recommend that fluid flow not exceed 5 ft/sec to avoid excessive pressure surges from sudden changes in flow (such as from closing a valve or starting a pump).
