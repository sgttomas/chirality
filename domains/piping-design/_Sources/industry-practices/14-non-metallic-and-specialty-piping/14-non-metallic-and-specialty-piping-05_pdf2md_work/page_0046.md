## Design Pressure Calculation:

Design pressures for FRP pipe are calculated as follows (see ASTM D2992, Obtaining Hydrostatic or Pressure Design Basis for Fiberglass Pipe and Fittings, for determination of design basis S):

$$P = [2St/(D_o - t)] \times SF$$

(Eq. 400-4)

where:

P = Design pressure, psi
