**O-RING JOINTS (Nonrestrained):** This is the primary method for joining HDPE Spirolite pipe and concrete pipe. However, this type of joint is also available for other nonmetallic pipe, including FRP, PVC and CPVC. These joints are easy to install but provide no axial restraint. Axial restraint must be provided by burial, thrust blocks, etc.
