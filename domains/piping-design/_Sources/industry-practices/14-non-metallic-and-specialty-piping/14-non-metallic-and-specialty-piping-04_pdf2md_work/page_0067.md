NOTES:

1. MATERIALS OF CONSTRUCTION SHALL BE PER PIPING MATERIAL SPECIFICATION LINE CLASS.
2. FOR VALVE SIZES 1" AND SMALLER WITH 2" INSULATION, NIPPLE TO BE 1/2" x 4". FOR VALVE SIZES 1-1/2" AND LARGER WITH 2" INSULATION, NIPPLE TO BE 3/4" x 4". NIPPLE MATERIAL AND SCHEDULE TO BE PER THE LINE CLASS SPECIFICATION.
3. FOR JACKET SIZES SMALLER THAN 2" USE 1/2"-3000LB COUPLING, SIZES 2, 1/2" AND LARGER USE 3/4"-3000LB COUPLING. FOR JACKET SIZES 1" THRU 2" WITH 2" INSULATION, USE 1/2" x 5" NIPPLE. FOR JACKET SIZES 2-1/2" AND LARGER WITH 2" INSULATION, USE 3/4" x 5" NIPPLE.
4. IN PIPING SYSTEMS WITH 4" INSULATION, ADD 2" TO LENGTH OF NIPPLES.
5. THREADS HIDDEN UNDER INSULATION SHALL BE THOROUGHLY CLEANED BEFORE ASSEMBLY AND SHALL BE SEAL WELDED.
