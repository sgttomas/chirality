PIPING JUMPOVER

![JMP-5 Piping Jumpover Details — ¾" – 1½" Socket Weld and 2" – 4" Butt Weld](figures/14-non-metallic-and-specialty-piping-04_p0069_fig01_jmp-5-piping-jumpover-socket-weld-butt-w.png)

The drawing shows two piping jumpover configurations:

**¾" – 1½" SOCKET WELD**
- Field weld (tack) at the top of the jumpover loop
- Dimension: 1'-0 1/8" span
- Height dimension: T

**2" – 4" BUTT WELD**
- Field weld (tack) at the top of the jumpover loop
- Dimension: 1'-2 1/8" span
- Height dimension: F-F

NOTES:

1. MATERIALS OF CONSTRUCTION SHALL BE PER PIPING MATERIAL SPECIFICATION LINE CLASS.
