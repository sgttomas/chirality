## 2. CENTRAL CONTROL ROOM IN A GAS TERMINAL

### 2.1 Scope

The scope of the project was to build a new control room for a gas terminal. The control room, and all of the functions that are carried out in and for the purposes of the control room (e.g. permit-to-work system), were all examined. The project team brainstormed the list of work systems that will be affected by the project along with the functions that will be carried out in the new working environment.

### 2.2 Work Systems

- Control Room
- Shift Supervisor's office
- Cloak Room
- Meeting Room
- Storage Facility
- Laboratory Technician office
- Hydrocarbon Technician office
- Site Technician workstation
- Permit Control Facility
- Printer/Copier/Fax Room
- Workshop
- Scheduler's office
- Aux/Telecom Room
