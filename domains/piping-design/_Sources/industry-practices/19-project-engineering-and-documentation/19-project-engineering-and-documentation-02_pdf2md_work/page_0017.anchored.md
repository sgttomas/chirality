## 5. ORGANISATION

Many disciplines are involved in human factors engineering during the preparation and execution of projects. Co-operation between these parties is essential and is described in this procedure and outlined in Table 1.

The different parties are:

- PE
  Process Engineer (PE)
- O/M rep
  Operations/Maintenance representative (O/M rep)
- HF Engineer
  Human Factors Engineer
- PM
  Project Manager (i.e. delegate Project Engineer) (PM)
- PET
  Project Ergonomics Team (PET)
- CM
  Construction Manager (CM)
- IT rep.
  IT representatives if necessary (IT rep)
