## 2.5 Rationale and Scope

### HFE Activity Level Criteria

[TABLE: HFE Activity Level Criteria]
| Activity level | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| - (not applicable): | In an emergency situation, the meeting room will be evacuated and no maintenance tasks of any kind will be carried out. |
| Standards (1): | The team had adequate standards for furniture and counter height and local legislation required the application of these HFE tested standards with such a work system |
| Standards and Review (2): | Health and safety within the control room is very important to the design team. The design team needed assurance that health and safety hazards were properly managed in the early design stages. Therefore, they decided that a review was required. |
| HFE Analysis (3): | The Shift Supervisor plays an integral role in managing the control room operations in an emergency situation. It was identified that this working environment needed to be analysed to ensure that there is proper communication between the Shift Supervisor and the control room operators both verbally and visually. The design of this work system is of particular importance due to the fact that there are serious demands on the time of the Shift Supervisor throughout the day and he is required to be immediately available during an emergency situation. |
