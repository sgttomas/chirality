## 4.2 DESCRIPTION OF ACTIVITIES

### 4.2.1 General

Figures 2 and 3 below illustrate the flow of required HFE activities during the life cycle of new projects. In the left hand column of the flow scheme are the necessary inputs into the process. The middle column lists the activities that take place during the design stage. The right-hand column lists the required documents (in green) that must be produced in order to document HFE results and meet audit requirements.

The process shall be auditable according to the Auditing Guidelines in DEP 30.00.60.14-Gen. Sections (4.2.2) through (4.2.3) provide a description of the HFE activities and follow Figures 2 and 3.

Tools to help identify whether and where HFE is required are provided as good practice guides e.g. in Appendices 1 and 2 of this DEP and in DEP 30.00.60.12-Gen.

PTs having similar tools that allow the above goals to be achieved can use their existing good practices/tools to carry out the activities presented in this DEP. An audit trail is however required.

To assist Project Teams in completing the content of the Focusing and Work Systems Tools, an example of this work process is provided in Appendix 6.
