[TABLE: Bottleneck and explanation table for control room issues]

| Bottleneck | Explanation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Display Control<br>System (DCS) | Require any of the Display Screen Equipment (DSE) to be able to bring up any of the pictures as well as normal personal computing interfaces<br><br>Requires 2-man operation<br><br>Due to fact that DCS is not reliable, operators are forced to monitor and operate off back wall<br><br>Too many actions required to carry out simple operation<br><br>Inability to handle large amounts of information |
| Traffic within<br>control room | All visitors enter control room<br><br>Anyone seeking signature from Shift Supervisor also enters control room<br><br>- In some cases visitors 'hang around' when Shift Supervisor is not there and interact with on duty control room operators (CROs)<br><br>Integration of normal personal computer (PC) screen onto DCS<br><br>Information on control monitoring system (CMS) is fragmented and therefore difficult to interpret |
| Shift Supervisor | Needs visual and auditable contact with CROs<br><br>Requires to be adjacent to PCF to sign off permits to work<br><br>Because Shift Supervisor sits in same room as CROs, confidential talks are carried out in any office that can be found (not ideal for good people management); this includes discussions with visitors |
| Physical<br>Environment | Lighting:<br><br>- Direct lighting creates glare on DSE and cannot be controlled by operators<br><br>- Lighting at Scheduler's office is poor and makeshift glare barriers have been erected.<br><br>- During night shift, back wall hard-wired outputs difficult to read<br><br>Thermal Environment<br><br>- Variable air conditioning and poor ventilation causes CROs to constantly take off and put on jackets during the course of a shift<br><br>- Complaints that the CCR is too warm in the summer and too cold in the winter<br><br>Noise<br><br>- Non-enclosed dot-matrix printers that provide data every 15 minutes are a distraction and add to overall background noise of the CCR |
| Console Layout | Mixture of controls that have been added as new systems are included in the project<br><br>Safeguard that protects against inadvertent operation also covers the label for the control (therefore stickers are used to identify controls that cannot be seen)<br><br>Automatic Shutdown (ASD) required to be on same panel (needs to be able to incorporate expansion)<br><br>Requires that all controls are within view of operator |
| Scheduler | Does not need visual contact with Shift Supervisor |
