The overall process of HFE Focusing Tool and Work Systems Analysis Tool and further HFE activities is summarised in Figure 4.

The Project Team shall determine what level of HFE analysis is required for each system state in the matrix. The levels are presented in Table 6 below:

[TABLE: Table 6 HFE Activity Level Criteria]

**Table 6** HFE Activity Level Criteria

| Level | Application | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0 | Not applicable | - |
| 1 | Standards only required | The application of existing HFE standards/processes is considered sufficient.<br><br>These standards/processes explicitly cover the relevant human factors issues of concern, and once applied within the design will deliver the required outcome in terms of specification and human performance<br><br>Competent members of the PET can easily apply these standards/processes.<br><br>Audit trail: Provide details of the standards/processes being applied, for each work system/aspect considered |
| 2 | Standards plus a review required | In addition to existing standards/processes, a specific review is also required for additional assurance purposes. A review should be specifically tailored to the work system/aspect being considered, should explicitly cover human factors issues of concern, and should be applied at the correct point(s) (typically in conjunction with the normal design review points, e.g. 30%, 60% and 90%) in the design process to ensure specifications are met.<br><br>Audit trail: As above, plus details of the review scope and timing in the course of the project. |
| 3 | Human factors assessment required | In order to meet the desired system performance objectives:<br><br>The application of existing standards/processes will not suffice - or no appropriate standards/processes exist - and so additional assessment and analysis is required in order to guide the design. Professional Human Factors support is required for this part of the project, to execute further HFE review and/or a focused program of study. |

If the only HFE activity levels identified are 0 or 1, the relevant activities shall be recorded and no further HFE activities are required.

If Level 2 activities are identified, the relevant activities shall be recorded and the timing plus the scope of the HFE review shall be set by the PM; see Activity 13, (4.2.3.2).

If Level 3 activities are identified, the relevant activities shall be recorded and the PET membership shall be adjusted so that those activities can be accomplished as outlined in this procedure.
