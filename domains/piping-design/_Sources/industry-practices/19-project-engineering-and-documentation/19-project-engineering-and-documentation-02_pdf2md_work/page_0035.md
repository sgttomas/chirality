# 7. Example of Index of HFE Report

## 0 Summary

## 1 Introduction

- Short description of the project; relevant work system(s).
- General areas of attention, HSE or economic risks.

## 2 HFE design analysis

- Goal and execution; documents consulted, scope.

- Valve Analysis.

- Other human factors engineering analysis (if applicable).

- Members of Project Ergonomic Team.

## 3 Human Machine Interface design requirements/considerations

### 3.1 Design requirements to be specified during BDEP/PS, D&E phase

- Plot plan layout.

- Design requirements (operational, maintenance, clean out/up, hoisting/transport, inspection task) per equipment.

- Results of Valve Analysis.

- Operational/maintenance codes of practice; e.g. characteristics of operator's route, hoisting/transport philosophy, how to deal with spills, plant lighting requirements in relation to operation/maintenance tasks, etc.

## 4 Organisation/responsibilities during implementation

- Who, when, what.

## 5 HFE activities during detailed engineering

- Model reviews, mock-up, and simulations.

- Detailed task analysis, human reliability/error analysis.

## 6 HFE activities during procurement

- HFE requirements in inquiries/contracts.

## 7 HFE activities during construction

- Plan of action for construction, auditing of construction.

## 8 Design aids, additional requirements

- Design tools to review the layout; 3D CAD; application of "soft boxes".

## 9 References, codes and standards

## 10 Appendices

- PEFSs with coded valve categories.
