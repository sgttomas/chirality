# 5.12 EXPERT SUPPORT

## 5.12.1 Competence of contracted Human Factors Engineer

If use is to be made of the services of contracted ergonomists, they must be listed for example in the Register of European Ergonomists or the local equivalent. Also find information on competence in (Appendix 3), Competence requirements for Human Factors Engineers.

## 5.12.2 Expert support per project phase

Indicate for which activities the support of an HFE expert is required during the basic and detailed engineering, procurement and construction.

Expert support is important in:

- Co-ordination and execution of training.
- Execution of HFE bottlenecks analysis of a reference plant (benchmarking).
- Execution/follow-up of HFE analysis (e.g. HFE design analysis).
- Auditing of Engineering Contractors.
- Auditing of Vendors.
- Formulating and advising on Engineering Contractor's Implementation Plan.
- Mock-up studies.
- Witnessing 3D CAD block model, 30 %, 60% and 90 % reviews.
- Formulating and advising on HFE Plan for Construction.
- Audits during Construction phase.
- Ergonomic evaluation after start-up.
