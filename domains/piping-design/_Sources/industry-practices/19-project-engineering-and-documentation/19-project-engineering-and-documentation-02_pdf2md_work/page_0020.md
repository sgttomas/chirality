## 6.1.2 HFE Activities

From the Work Systems Analysis Tool, the PET should identify three levels of competence to carry out the three levels of HFE activity during the project (see Table 4.) Should a certain level of competence not exist within the organisation then appropriate external competences shall be consulted or engaged.

[TABLE: Table 4 Competences required for HFE activities]

| Activity | Competence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Definition of HFE scope and application of HFE Specifications | Working Knowledge |
| Human Factors Engineering Review | Working Knowledge |
| Human Factors Engineering Analysis and assurance | Skilled or Mastery |
