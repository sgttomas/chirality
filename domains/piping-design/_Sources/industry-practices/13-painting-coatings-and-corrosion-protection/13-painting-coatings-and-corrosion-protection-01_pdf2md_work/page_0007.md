1.7.6 Contractor shall protect and be responsible and liable for the clean-up of adjacent equipment, pavement, platforms, roadways, and other property not designated to be coated from overspray, droppings, splatterings, damage due to abrasive blasting, etc. (also see Sect. 1.5).

1.7.7 Contractor shall furnish all necessary equipment required such as ladders, scaffolds, brushes, spray equipment, drop cloths, warning signs, abrasive blast equipment, heating equipment, air compressors, exhaust fans, power cables, etc. Electrical equipment shall be suitable for the electrical area classification of the work area unless otherwise approved by BP CHP.

1.7.8 Contractor shall remove all excess materials, equipment, and debris remaining after completion of the work. The premises shall be left in a clean and orderly condition.

1.7.9 Exterior paint shall not be applied at temperatures below the manufacturer's recommendation for the products being applied, and in no case under the following conditions:

- a) If the surface temperature is below 40°F, unless written approval is obtained from BP CHP.

- b) In wet weather.

- c) When the humidity is above 85%.

- d) In any conditions where the surface is not thoroughly dry.

- e) When the temperature is expected to drop below 32°F before the paint is dry.

1.7.10 Interior paint may be applied in cold weather, providing the temperature is maintained above a minimum of 40°F, and the surface is dry.

1.7.11 In general, the manufacturer's instructions for minimum drying time for each coat of paint shall govern. However, there shall be a minimum of 24 hours between coats unless quick drying paints are used, or wet-on-wet systems are approved in writing from the coating manufacturer.
