2.3.2 Any steel surface not primed shall be reblasted if any rust develops before painting, regardless of length of time after abrasive blasting.

## SECTION 3 – PAINTING

**Refer to Table 1 in Section 5 for a detailed selection guide for the paint systems.**

### 3.1 BUILDINGS

3.1.1 Exterior brick and concrete walls do not require painting.

3.1.2 All exterior surfaces of pedestrian doors, sash, flashing, gutters, downspouts, and large swinging or rolling service doors shall be coated as specified in System #13.

3.1.3 The interior surfaces of all exterior doors shall receive the same treatment and color as the exterior surface unless otherwise specified.

3.1.4 All interior new ceilings, walls and woodwork to be painted shall be given one (1) coat of odorless undercoat and one (1) finish coat of odorless enamel with gloss, semi-gloss, or flat finish.

3.1.4.1 Block wall surfaces that are especially rough shall have one (1) filler coat applied prior to receiving finish coats.

3.1.4.2 Roof trusses, columns and exposed structural steel within buildings shall be primed in accordance with System #5 and finish painted to match the color treatment of the rest of the building interior.

3.1.4.3 Interior doors shall be finished in accordance with local guidelines. Window sash treatment shall match the wall above the Wainscot.

3.1.5 Special paints shall be indicated for areas requiring additional light reflection.

3.1.6 All control room instrument panels shall be coated as specified in System #13, or the manufacturer's standard finish.

### 3.2 STACKS, DUCTS AND SIMILAR PLATE WORK

3.2.1 Steel stacks, ducts and similar plate work shall be coated as specified in System #3 or #4 depending on the service temperature. Painting of these items may not be required when fabricated from COR-TEN steel, if approved by BP CHP.
