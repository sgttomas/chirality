## 1.8 INSPECTION

1.8.1 All surface preparation, materials, and application of materials shall be subject to inspection and approval by BP CHP or designated representatives.

1.8.2 The film thickness and film continuity shall be subject to inspection by BP CHP or designated representatives with the use of suitable measuring devices.

1.8.3 Materials found to have been damaged or improperly applied shall be removed and reapplied at the Contractor's expense unless the damage was caused by others.

1.8.4 Inspection and approval of materials and/or workmanship by BP CHP shall not be construed as relieving the contractor's responsibilities.

## 1.9 GUARANTEES AND REFERENCES

1.9.1 The contractor shall guarantee workmanship and materials for a period of one (1) year. This guarantee shall include, but not be limited to, failures due to improper surface preparation or application such as peeling, excessive chalking, flaking, fading (exterior surfaces), non-curing, loss of adhesion, and cracking.

1.9.2 The latest edition of the following documents from The Society for Protective Coatings (SSPC) shall be considered a part of this Standard:

Good Painting Practice: SSPC Painting Manual, Volume 1
Systems and Specifications: SSPC Painting Manual, Volume 2
