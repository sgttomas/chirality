1.5.3 Concrete floor surfaces and machinery bases shall generally be left unpainted. Where particular qualities such as non-skid surface coatings, high visibility, marking for safety or traffic lanes, etc. are required, special instructions will be issued by BP CHP.

## 1.6 QUALITY ASSURANCE

1.6.1 The following sub-section also includes procedures, approved by BP CHP, for vendor painted items.

1.6.2 Prior to painting, all surfaces shall be inspected as follows to assure that the specified surface conditions have been achieved.

   a) Confirm that the manufacturer's requirements for cleaning and surface preparation are met for the specific paint system to be applied.

   b) Verify surface profile.

   c) Verify that cleaned surfaces have been kept free of contamination.

1.6.3 Immediately prior to painting, all surfaces shall again be inspected for proper cleanliness and profile, and to assure that moisture, other contaminants, and surface imperfections are not present.

1.6.4 Storage, mixing, thinning, and application of all primers, intermediates, and finishes shall be verified to be in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions.

1.6.5 Surfaces not to be coated shall be verified to be masked off or otherwise protected prior to painting of adjacent surfaces.

1.6.6 The ambient temperature, humidity and substrate surface temperature shall be documented just prior to applying the coating.

1.6.7 The dry film thickness (DFT) of the primer, intermediate and finish coats shall be recorded.

1.6.8 Any repair work shall be documented and reinspected as outlined above.

1.6.9 Inspection instruments shall be appropriate to complete the inspection task. Test instruments shall be operated by trained and qualified personnel, and maintained and calibrated on a regular schedule as prescribed by the manufacturer. All test equipment maintenance and calibration shall be documented.
