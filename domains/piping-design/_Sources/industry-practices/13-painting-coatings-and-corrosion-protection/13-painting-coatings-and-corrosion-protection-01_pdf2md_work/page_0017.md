### SYSTEM #1

**PURPOSE:** New Work and Maintenance

**TYPE:** Amine Cured Novolac Epoxy, Nonreinforced Thin Film

**USE:** Carbon Steel and Low Alloy Steel Piping, Equipment, and Rotating Equipment – Insulated Service 0°F through 350°F and Fireproofed Service 0°F through 450°F – See Notes 1 & 2.

[TABLE: Coating system specification for System #1 — Amine Cured Novolac Epoxy]

| COATING MANUFACTURER | FIRST COAT | SECOND COAT | THIRD COAT | COMMENTS |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Sherwin-Williams | EPO-PHEN B62A55 | EPO-PHEN B62A55 |  | Max. Temp. 500°F |
| Carboline | Thermaline 450 | Thermaline 450 |  | Max. Temp. 450°F |

**Notes:** 1. For rotating equipment, remove manufacturer's standard coating before application of this coating system

2. In addition to normal usage temperatures shown above, use this system for insulated piping and equipment which normally operate at 350°F or below, but intermittently operate up to 450°F. Use System #9 for piping and equipment which normally operate at 350°F or below, but intermittently operate from 451°F through 1200°F.
