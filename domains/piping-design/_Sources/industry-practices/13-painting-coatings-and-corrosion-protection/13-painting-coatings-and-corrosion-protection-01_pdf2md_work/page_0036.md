**Notes:**

1. Consult with BP CHP Corrosion Engineer and coating manufacturer to confirm that the selected coating is appropriate for the fluid and temperature for each specific application.

2. For severe corrosive services a third coat may be recommended.

3. This coating is not recommended by manufacturer for tanks with diameters over 100 feet.

4. Between the first and second coats, apply Poly-Glass Polyester Putty 970-B-702/531-0-001 as needed to fill corrosion pits, to provide smooth transitions at weld seams and sharp edges.
