## SYSTEM #19

**PURPOSE:** New Work and Maintenance

**TYPE:** Polyester and Glass

**USE:** Tank and Vessel Linings for Immersion Services at Ambient Temperatures – See Note 1

[TABLE: Coating system table for System #19 – Polyester and Glass]

| COATING MANUFACTURER | FIRST COAT | SECOND COAT | THIRD COAT See Note 3 | COMMENTS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sherwin-Williams | Corobond Vinyl Ester Primer B88C10/B88V99 | Poly-Glass Polyester Resin w/Glass Mat 939/531-0-001/530-0-001 | Poly-Glass Polyester Resin w/Wax Solution 939/531-0-001/970C949 | See Note 2 |

**Notes:** 1. Consult with BP CHP Corrosion Engineer and coating manufacturer to confirm that the selected coating is appropriate for the fluid and temperature for each specific application.

2. Between the first and second coats, apply Poly-Glass Polyester Putty 970-B-703/531-0-001 as needed to fill corrosion pits, to provide smooth transitions at weld seams, and to provide a 3" radius at floor to shell joints. Do not apply thicker than 2" in a single application.

3. All exposed outer surfaces shall be lightly rolled to "lay" the fibers before the application has dried. Unsightly drippings on bottom flange of beams shall also be smoothed in this manner.
