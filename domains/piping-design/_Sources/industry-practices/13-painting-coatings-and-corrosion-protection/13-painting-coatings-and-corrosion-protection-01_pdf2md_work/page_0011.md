3.3.2 Uninsulated carbon steel piping with operating temperatures from 0°F through 200°F, shall be coated, as specified in System #3.

3.3.3 Uninsulated carbon steel piping with operating temperatures from 201°F through 750°F shall be coated as specified in System #4. 3.3.4 Insulated 300 series stainless steel piping operating from 0°F through 400°F shall be coated as specified in System #6, unless the piping has been stress relieved, in which case no coating is required.

3.3.5 Consult with the BP CHP corrosion engineer for any coating requirements for insulated high alloy piping.

3.3.6 Piping welds shall not be coated until after hydrotest. Shop or field fabricated piping welds may be coated only with an inorganic zinc primer, if the pipe coating will be done prior to hydrotesting.

## 3.4 PRESSURE VESSELS

3.4.1 Uninsulated carbon steel pressure vessels, including towers, heat exchangers, drums, accumulators, cooler boxes and similar equipment operating at temperatures from 0°F through 200°F shall be coated as specified in System #3.

3.4.2 Uninsulated carbon steel vessels as listed in paragraph 3.4.1 operating at temperatures from 201°F through 750°F shall be coated as specified in System #4. Vessels operating at 201°F through 450°F shall have a top coat applied as specified in System #12. Vessels operating at 451°F through 750°F shall have a top coat as specified in System #11.

3.4.3 Vessel surfaces to receive temperature indicating paint shall be prepared and coated as specified in System #7.

3.4.4 Insulated carbon steel and low alloy steel pressure vessels, including towers, heat exchangers, drums, accumulators, cooler boxes and similar equipment normally operating at temperatures from 0°F through 350°F, but also including intermittent operation up to 500°F, shall be coated as specified in System #1. Insulated vessels with continuous operating temperatures above 350°F, shall not be coated, except for any parts of the vessel which are not covered by insulation, such as supports or skirts, which shall be coated per paragraphs 3.4.1 or 3.4.2 according to the operating temperature.
