1.4.2 Paint and coating materials furnished by the contractor shall be delivered to the job site in the manufacturer's original sealed container.

1.4.3 A storage area will be assigned to the contractor. Contractor shall adequately protect all stored materials from theft or vandalism, and damage due to contamination, rain, high heat, and/or freezing temperatures. Replacement of damaged, stolen or vandalized material and equipment will be borne by the contractor.

1.4.4 Materials shall be prepared for use in accordance with manufacturer's instructions.

1.4.5 No thinners shall be used unless specified by the manufacturer. When such thinners are added, they must be those specifically named by the manufacturer. No extraneous materials are to be added.

## 1.5 ITEMS NOT TO BE PAINTED

1.5.1 The following surfaces, products or items are not to be painted:

a) Surfaces used for identification purposes such as nameplates, serial number plates, underwriter's approval plates, valve identification tags, and BP CHP pennants and signs.

b) Non-metallic products such as seal fabric on floating roof tanks.

c) Gauge glasses and boards, gauge faces, light fixtures, insulators, glass, ceramics, etc.

1.5.2 The following items generally will not require painting when located outdoors. The finish paint schedule will indicate whether finish painting for appearance is required for indoor locations.

a) Copper and finished brass products such as pipe, tubing, valves and fittings.

b) New galvanized products such as structural steel, grating, stair tread, etc.

c) Aluminum products such as siding and sheathing, roofing, doors, windows, ventilators, conduit and pipe.

d) Cast iron products such as manholes, grating and their frames.

e) Products such as thermostats, receptacles, instruments, stuffing boxes and glands, and moving parts of machines (shaft, coupling, piston rod, etc.).

f) 300 series stainless steel uninsulated surfaces operating at any temperature, or insulated surfaces operating above 400°F.

g) Insulated carbon steel and alloy steel surfaces operating above 350°F.
