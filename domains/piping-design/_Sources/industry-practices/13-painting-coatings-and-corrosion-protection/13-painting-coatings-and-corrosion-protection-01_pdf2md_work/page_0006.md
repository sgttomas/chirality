## 1.7 CONTRACTOR REQUIREMENTS

### 1.7.1

The contractor shall not commence work until permission to do so is received from BP CHP.

### 1.7.2

All equipment and materials used shall be subject to approval by BP CHP.

### 1.7.3

The contractor shall comply with all pertinent health, safety and environmental procedures and regulations of BP CHP.

### 1.7.4

If requested, the contractor shall make available their health, safety and environmental programs and procedures to BP CHP. As a minimum these programs and procedures shall address:

a) Procedures for handling spent abrasives and other debris generated during the work.

b) Storage of coatings and thinners while on-site.

c) Programs on respirators, hazard communication, and hearing conservation.

d) Ventilation in confined areas, if applicable.

e) Blasting safety.

f) Protection of workmen during blasting, mixing, and coating work.

g) Scaffolding safety.

h) Fire safety.

i) The use of life saving equipment for persons working above water, if applicable.

### 1.7.5

Contractor's work shall be performed by skilled mechanics in a professional manner. The contractor shall have a quality control program addressing the following:

a) The quality control of equipment used in the application of coating material.
