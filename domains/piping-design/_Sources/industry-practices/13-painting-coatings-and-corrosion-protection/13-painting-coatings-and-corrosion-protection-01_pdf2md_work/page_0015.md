## 5.2 COATING SYSTEMS

5.2.1 Refer to Section 3 of this standard for selection of the appropriate coating system from the following pages. All coating systems shall be applied in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions, including surface preparation, application methods, and coating thicknesses. Equivalent coating systems from other manufacturers not listed here may be used upon approval of the BP CHP corrosion engineer.

5.2.2 When coating systems on the following pages are found to be incorrect or not available, inform BP CHP Engineering Authority and provide the current equivalent name and number or indicate that the coating system is no longer available.
