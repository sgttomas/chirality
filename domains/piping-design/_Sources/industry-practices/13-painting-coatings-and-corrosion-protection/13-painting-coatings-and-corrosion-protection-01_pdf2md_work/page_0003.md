## SECTION 1: GENERAL

### 1.1 SCOPE

This Engineering Standard, together with other contract documents, shall constitute the minimum requirements of BP Cherry Point Refinery (referred to as BP CHP) for the preparation of surfaces for painting and coating, the application of paints and coatings, and the materials to be used. No changes or modifications will be permitted unless written approval is obtained from BP CHP.

### 1.2 BIDS AND PROPOSALS

1.2.1 The contractor's proposal must be in accordance with this Standard, and with additional requirements and standards provided with the bid package. Local environmental requirements also apply for both abrasive blasting and painting.

1.2.2 The contractor's basic proposal may be supplemented with an alternate proposal providing the following conditions are met.

- a) Significant price or completion date advantages are indicated, or a superior installation is offered at a justifiable higher price

- b) All deviations from this Standard are stated in detail

### 1.3 ACCEPTABLE MATERIALS

1.3.1 The contractor shall use products as specified in Section 5.4.

1.3.2 All paint (primer, intermediate, and finish coats) for each particular item painted must be furnished by the same manufacturer unless otherwise specified in this standard or approved by BP CHP.

1.3.3 Substitution of other suppliers or other paints is not permitted without the written approval of BP CHP.

1.3.4 Colors of paints used shall be in accordance with Section 5.1 unless otherwise specified by BP CHP.

### 1.4 MATERIAL HANDLING, STORAGE AND MIXING

1.4.1 For each coating, chemical, and other material subject to federal OSHA HCR requirements, which will be delivered to BP CHP, the vendor shall provide prior to delivery, a Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) FORM OSHA-20 or equivalent. Also, all equipment and parts containing or coated with chemicals or other materials, shall be prominently tagged at openings to indicate the nature of the contents, precautions for shipping, storage, and handling. Copies of the MSDS shall be affixed in a protective envelope to the outside of the shipment.
