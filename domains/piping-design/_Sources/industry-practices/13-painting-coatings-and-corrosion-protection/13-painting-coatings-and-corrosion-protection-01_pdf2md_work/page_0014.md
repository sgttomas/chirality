4.2.1 All concrete, steel, galvanized or other non-ferrous surfaces (painted or unpainted) designated to receive a non-skid coating shall be prepared and coated as specified in System #8.

## 4.3 INTERNAL PROTECTION OF IMMERSION SURFACES

4.3.2 Interior steel surfaces of tanks and vessels specified to be coated shall be prepared to SSPC SP.5 White Metal Blast Cleaning and shall have a minimum profile of 1.5 mils. The prepared steel shall be coated within the same day as the surface preparation.

4.3.3 The selection of the immersion coating material shall be done in concurrence with the BP CHP corrosion engineer, and be based on the operating temperature and fluid service.

4.3.4 The finished coating shall be thoroughly tested with a Holiday tester suitable for the coating used and set at the proper voltage.
