## SYSTEM #14

**PURPOSE:** New Work

**TYPE:** Zinc/Epoxy/Urethane

**USE:** Steel Plates for Storage Tanks to Avoid Blasting in the Field. Service 0°F through 200°F – See Note 1

[TABLE: System #14 Coating System — Zinc/Epoxy/Urethane for Steel Plates]

| COATING MANUFACTURER | FIRST COAT See Note 2 | SECOND COAT See Note 3 | THIRD COAT | COMMENTS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| PPG Amercoat | DSP 1M | Amercoat 383H | Amercoat 450H | |
| Carboline | Carboweld 11 | Carboguard 888 | Carbothane 134 HG | |
| Sherwin-Williams | ZC PCP Ultra B69A450/B69V455 | Macropoxy 646 B58-600 | HS Polyurethane B65-300 | |

**Notes:**

1. This system is designed to avoid exterior blasting in the field. Steel is coated in the flat after being automatically blasted using a wheelabrator.

2. The pre-construction zinc primer is applied in the shop prior to forming. The primer can be welded through if desired, however, in most cases the coating is held back 1" from the edge to be welded.

3. Where the primer has been damaged or where the steel is bare, apply this in two coats.
