## 7. COLOUR

The colour schemes shall be as specified by the Principal and may be governed by local regulations and customs.

If an intermediate coat has to be applied it should be applied a shade lighter than the top coat in order to increase the inspectability.

The Shell Standard Colours shall be used. If, for local reasons, this is not possible the following BS 4800 or RAL alternatives may be chosen:
