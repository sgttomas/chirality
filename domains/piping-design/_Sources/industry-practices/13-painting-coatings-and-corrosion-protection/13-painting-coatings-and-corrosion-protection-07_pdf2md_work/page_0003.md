### 1.3.2 Specific Definitions

**DFT** &emsp; Dry Film Thickness; the thickness of the dried or cured paint or coating film.

**Equipment** &emsp; The equipment to be painted is listed in Appendix 1 and includes piping, vessels, columns, exchangers, reactors, structural steel, fire-fighting systems, tanks, LPG storage vessels, furnaces, stacks, flare stacks, flue ducts, offshore structures and topside facilities

**Grit** &emsp; Particles that are predominantly angular, have fractured faces and sharp edges and are less than half round in shape.

**Operating Temperature** &emsp; See DEP 01.00.01.30-Gen.

**Shot** &emsp; Particles that are predominantly round, that have a length of less than twice the maximum particle width and that do not have edges, broken faces or other sharp surface defects.

**TDFT** &emsp; Total Dry Film Thickness; the thickness of the total number of coatings specified.

**WFT** &emsp; Wet Film Thickness; the thickness of the uncured or wet paint or coating film.
