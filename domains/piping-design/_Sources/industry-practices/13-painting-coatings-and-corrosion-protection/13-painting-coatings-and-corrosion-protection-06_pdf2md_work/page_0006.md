## 2.4 INFORMATION TO BE SUBMITTED BY THE CONTRACTOR

The Contractor shall submit information to the Principal on the coating system to be used. This information shall contain as a minimum:

- system identification (5.2);
- Manufacturer's data (5.3);
- system qualification information (5.4);
- coating application procedures;
- the name(s) of the proposed coating applicator(s);
- Applicator's qualification information (6);
- Quality Plan.

The Contractor shall confirm that the coating system is suitable for use under the specified conditions (2.3).
