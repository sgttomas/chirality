## SECTION 1: GENERAL

### 1.1 SCOPE

This standard specifies the requirements for hot-dip or mechanically deposited galvanizing of structural steel, fabricated steel assemblies, steel grating, piping, bolting, other hardware, and similar items for the BP Cherry Point Refinery (BP CHP).

### 1.2 DESIGN FOR GALVANIZING

1.2.1 Fabricated assemblies and finished products shall be designed where applicable in accordance with the design guide from the American Hot Dip Galvanizers Association (AGA) titled The Design of Products to be Hot Dip Galvanized After Fabrication.

1.2.2 Individual members or sections of fabricated structural assemblies shall be designed so that they can be galvanized in a single dip.

1.2.3 To minimize warpage during galvanizing of structural assemblies, the thickness of parts should be nearly the same, and sections should be made symmetrical where possible.

### 1.3 APPLICABLE STANDARDS AND SPECIFICATIONS

Unless otherwise specified, galvanizing and galvanized finished products shall conform to the latest edition of the following standards, where applicable.

- ASTM A90 - Standard Test Method for Weight of Coating on Iron and Steel Articles with Zinc or Zinc-Alloy Coatings
- ASTM A123 - Standard Specification for Zinc (Hot-Dip Galvanized) Coatings on Iron and Steel Products
- ASTM A143 - Standard Practice for Safeguarding Against Embrittlement of Hot-Dip Galvanized Structural Steel Products and Procedure for Detecting Embrittlement
- ASTM A153 - Standard Specification for Zinc Coating (Hot-Dip) on Iron and Steel Hardware
- ASTM A307 - Standard Specification for Carbon Steel Bolts, Studs, and Threaded Rod 60,000 PSI Tensile Strength
- ASTM A325 - Standard Specification for Structural Bolts, Steel, Heat Treated, 120/105 ksi Minimum Tensile Strength
- ASTM A384 - Standard Practice for Safeguarding Against Warpage and Distortion During Hot-Dip Galvanizing of Steel Assemblies

- ASTM A385 - Standard Practice for Providing High-Quality Zinc Coatings (Hot-Dip)

- ASTM A449 - Standard Specification for Hex Cap Screws, Bolts and Studs, Steel, Heat Treated, 120/105/90 ksi Minimum Tensile Strength, General Use

- ASTM A490 - Standard Specification for Structural Bolts, Alloy Steel, Heat Treated, 150 ksi Minimum Tensile Strength

- ASTM A563 - Standard Specification for Carbon and Alloy Steel Nuts

- ASTM A780 - Standard Practice for Repair of Damaged and Uncoated Areas of Hot-Dip Galvanized Coatings

- ASTM B695 - Standard Specification for Coatings of Zinc Mechanically Deposited on Iron and Steel

## 1.4 MATERIAL SAFETY DATA SHEET (MSDS)

1.4.1 A Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) is required for each chemical or other OSHA HCR regulated material received at BP CHP facilities.

1.4.2 Equipment or materials that contain or are coated with any regulated substances, such as the following, shall be prominently tagged at openings to indicate nature of contents and precautions for shipping, storage, and handling:

a) Insulating oils.

b) Corrosion inhibitors.

c) Antifreeze solutions.

d) Desiccants.

e) Chemical substances.

f) Hydrocarbon substances.

1.4.3 Federal OSHA HCR (Occupational Safety and Health Administration's Hazards Communication Rule) regulated substances shall have a current Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS).

1.4.4 MSDSs shall fully comply with Federal/State OSHA HCR regulations for MSDS preparation and shall include a statement that the substance is considered hazardous by regulation.

1.4.5 If any substance is exempt from regulation, a statement to that effect shall be included.

1.4.6 Before shipment, MSDSs shall be forwarded to BP CHP.

1.4.7 At shipment, MSDSs in protective envelopes shall be affixed to the outside of the shipment.

## SECTION 2: EXTENT OF GALVANIZING

### 2.1 PIPING

Refer to STP 205 to determine which piping is required to be galvanized. When specified, galvanizing shall be applied to both the inside and outside surfaces of the piping materials.

### 2.2 STRUCTURAL STEEL AND EQUIPMENT

2.2.1 Refer to STP 803 and STP 807 for additional direction on the coating requirements for structural steel, which includes but is not limited to pipe supports, equipment supports, platforms, walkways, floor plate, grating, stairs, handrails, guardrails, fixed ladders, ladder cages, and associated bolting hardware.

2.2.2 When galvanizing is specified, the items shall be thoroughly coated after all fabrication is completed.

2.2.3 Heater shells, convection section headers, breech sections, and stack sections shall not be galvanized.

2.2.4 Clips, brackets, and similar structural members which will be welded directly to pressure-containing equipment or piping shall not be galvanized.

2.2.5 Structural members for major supports for equipment and piping, including columns, beams, and bracing shall be hot dip galvanized in accordance with this standard, or painted in accordance with STP 500. Galvanizing is preferred where practical.

2.2.6 The following items shall be galvanized:

a. All elements of circular and rectangular platforms which will be bolted to pressure vessels;

b. Stairways, including stringers, guardrails, handrails, toe plates, landings, stair treads, grating, and checkered plate;

c. Fixed ladders and cages;

d. Flooring consisting of checkered plate or grating;

e. Platforms and walkways, including flooring, toe plates, handrails, and guardrails; and,

f. Air cooler plenums.

## SECTION 3: FABRICATION

### 3.1 WELDING

3.1.1 For fabricated assemblies that will be galvanized, the edges of tightly contacting surfaces should be completely sealed by welding. No pinholes in welds are permitted. All welds must be sandblasted by fabricator prior to galvanizing.

3.1.2 Before welding on galvanized materials, including piping, equipment, or structures, the zinc coating shall be mechanically removed from the weld area to prevent zinc penetration into the weld and subsequent cracking, and also to avoid exposure to hazardous welding fumes. The BP CHP Safety and Health Department will determine any requirements for PPE as part of the work permitting process.

3.1.3 After welding on areas where the galvanized coating was removed, the affected area shall be washed with water to remove salts and wire-brushed to remove welding slag and other foreign material, before repairing the galvanized coating per section 3.3.

### 3.2 GALVANIZING APPLICATIONS

3.2.1 Products fabricated from rolled, pressed, and forged steel shapes, plates, bars and strip (1/8" and heavier) shall be zinc coated in accordance with ASTM Specification A123.

3.2.2 Assembled steel products with thicknesses less than 1/8" shall be zinc coated in accordance with ASTM Specification A385.

3.2.3 Headed bolts (machine bolts), nuts and washers conforming to ASTM A307 shall be hot-dip galvanized in accordance with ASTM A153, Class C. Nuts shall be tapped oversize in accordance with ASTM A563, prior to galvanizing. Hot-dip galvanized nuts shall be retapped after galvanizing.

3.2.4 High strength bolts, nuts, and washers conforming to ASTM A325 or A449, and load indicator washers shall be mechanically zinc coated in accordance with ASTM B695, Type I, Class 50. Nuts shall be tapped oversize in accordance with ASTM A563 prior to zinc coating, and do not need to be retapped after coating.

3.2.5 High strength bolts, nuts, and washers conforming to ASTM A490 are not allowed to be galvanized per the Research Council on Structural Connections (RCSC), and shall be coated with Zinc/Aluminum inorganic coating in accordance with ASTM F1136, Grade 3 for bolts and washers, and Grade 5 for nuts.

## 3.3 REPAIR OF GALVANIZED COATINGS

Mechanically damaged portions of galvanized coatings shall be repaired as specified below.

a. Solvent wash the area to remove grease and other foreign material.

b. Repair the damaged areas of the galvanized coating in accordance with ASTM A780 with one of the three listed options: zinc-based solder, paint containing zinc dust, or sprayed zinc (molten). Surface preparation, application methods, and coating thickness shall be in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions for the specific repair coating used, and in accordance with ASTM A780. One application of the repair material shall provide a coating dry film thickness of at least 2.0 mils. Harris Gal-Vis galvanizing solder (available in the BP CHP General Shop, MSDS #PS02681) is an acceptable solder material. Sprayon WL 740 Zinc-Rich Galvanizing Compound (Storehouse stock #S2135010983) is an acceptable paint material. Requests to use other new or equivalent materials shall be submitted to the BP CHP Reliability Engineer for review and approval.

## SECTION 4: MATERIAL STORAGE

**4.1** Galvanized steel items shall be stacked clear of the ground and any loose objects, and shall be stored remote from chemical dust, fumes or splashing.

**4.2** Surfaces shall be adequately spaced to permit the free circulation of air.

**4.3** Protective treatments for shipping shall not be applied to galvanized materials and assemblies without prior approval from BP CHP.

## SECTION 5: INSPECTION

**5.1** BP CHP reserves the right to inspect the galvanized structural steel in the fabricator's plant or upon receipt at the job site to assure that the materials and workmanship are in accordance with this specification.

**5.2** The outer surface of the galvanizing shall be composed of relatively pure zinc and have a bright metallic finish. The appearance of the spangles and their size are not a part of this specification. The finished structure shall be free of bare spots, flux inclusions, ash inclusions, rust stains, and blisters.

**5.3** The zinc thickness shall be in accordance with the applicable ASTM Standards and shall be checked with a commercial magnetic thickness gauge of approved design (2 oz. per square foot equals 3.4 mils). Where thickness measurement is not feasible, the coating weight shall be determined in accordance with ASTM Standard A90.

**5.4** Materials with galvanized coatings will be rejected if they are warped, distorted or embrittled, or if the coating thickness or adherence to the base material is not in accordance with the applicable ASTM Standards.
