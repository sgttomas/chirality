7.2.3 Operators shall make necessary adjustments including tension adjustments to assure a continuously uniform, smooth, tight coating. Care shall be taken that no wrinkles, puckers, voids, or breaks are introduced.

7.2.4 Outer wraps are recommended to protect the tape during lowering in and backfill operations. Laminated kraft paper, felt, and some proprietary products have been used. Selected outer wrap must not interfere or shield the cathodic protection system.

7.2.5 In the coating of bends, wrinkling on the inner radius of the bend shall be minimized by increasing lap and/or wrapping tightly by hand with a narrow width tape (4" maximum).

7.2.6 Weld joints, fittings, bends, etc shall be wrapped with tape spirally applied and overlapped 50% ±15% of its width, resulting in double-wrap coverage.

## 7.3 FUSION BONDED EPOXY (FBE) COATINGS

FBE coatings shall be shop-applied to a clean, dry and oil-free pipe surface in accordance with coating manufacturer's instructions, including surface preparation requirements. The minimum coating thickness shall be 12 mils.

## 7.4 JOINT COATING AND REPAIRS

7.4.1 For piping with extruded coating, joints and minor defects shall be coated or repaired with heat shrink sleeves or tape wrap, unless otherwise approved.

7.4.2 For piping with tape coating, joints and minor defects shall be coated or repaired with tape wrap unless otherwise approved. A primer coat shall be applied in accordance with the coating manufacturer's instructions after the joint has been prepared or after any damaged coating has been trimmed back to sound coating.

7.4.3 For piping with FBE coating, joints and minor defects shall be coated or repaired per the coating manufacturer's instructions, or with a heat shrink sleeve, unless otherwise approved.

7.4.4 Repairs shall be made to all flaws and holidays in the coating, which are visually detected or identified by the holiday detector. Typical flaws include voids, pinholes, gouges, and areas of the coating damaged by installation or handling of the pipe. When non-bonded outer wrap is to be removed to make a repair, the outer wrap shall first be secured on both sides of the repair by one inch pressure-sensitive tape. Coating shall also be trimmed back to sound coating at damaged areas before applying more coating.
