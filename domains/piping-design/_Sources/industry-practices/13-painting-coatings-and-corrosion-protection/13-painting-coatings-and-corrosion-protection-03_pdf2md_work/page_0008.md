10.2  The maximum number of tiers in a stack should be governed by the pipe size, type of coating, and weight; however, in no event shall the number of tiers exceed the following:

| **PIPE SIZE** | **MAX NUMBER OF TIERS** |
| :---: | :---: |
| 4" | 7 |
| 6" | 6 |
| 8"-10" | 5 |
| 12"-16" | 4 |
| 18"-26" | 3 |
| 28"-36" | 2 |

10.3  All booms, hooks, forks, supports and skids used in handling or storing coated pipe shall be padded and handled in such a manner as to prevent damage to the coating.

10.4  Coated pipe shall not be stored for more than six months without providing ultraviolet ray protection.
