## SECTION 1: SCOPE

This standard establishes the minimum requirements for exterior protective coatings for underground steel piping at the BP Cherry Point Refinery (referred to as BP CHP). It includes application to both new and used pipe.

**Note:** Even galvanized pipe must be protected per this standard. Direct burial is not permitted.

## SECTION 2: CODES AND STANDARDS

Compliance with the following industry standards and specifications is required where applicable, except in case of conflict with this standard. The latest editions of the referenced documents shall apply unless otherwise noted.

- C210 - Liquid-Epoxy Coating Systems for the Interior and Exterior of Steel Water Pipelines
- C213 - Fusion-Bonded Epoxy Coating for the Interior and Exterior of Steel Water Pipelines
- C214 - Tape Coating Systems for the Exterior of Steel Water Pipelines
- C215 - Extruded Polyolefin Coatings for the Exterior of Steel Water Pipelines

NACE:

- SP0185 - Extruded Polyolefin Resin Coating Systems with Soft Adhesives for Underground or Submerged Pipe

- RP0399 - Plant-Applied External Coal Tar Enamel Pipe Coating Systems: Applicaton, Performance, and Quality Control

## SECTION 3: GENERAL

To ensure a reasonable life of buried steel pipe, it is necessary to provide both protective coating and cathodic protection. Coatings may develop holidays over time, and cathodic protection, either impressed current or sacrificial anodes, shall be installed to protect the pipe. Consult the BP CHP corrosion engineer to review cathodic protection design. Refer to standard drawing PA-0100-94 for sacrificial anodes.
