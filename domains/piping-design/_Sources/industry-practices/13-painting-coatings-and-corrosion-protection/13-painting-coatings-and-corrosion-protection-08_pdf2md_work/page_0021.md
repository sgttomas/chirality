#### 4.2.3 Planned shut-in < 24 hours

No additional corrosion control measures are required for planned shut-ins of less than 24 hours.

#### 4.2.4 Planned shut-in ≥ 24 hours and < 1 month (turn-around)

If a sour gas pipeline is temporarily taken out of service for a period longer than 24 h, the flow regime is equivalent to the worst stratified flow. Continued protection from corrosion by the residual inhibitors (continuous injection or batch) can only last for a certain time. The aggressiveness of the corrodents can be reduced by reducing their partial pressures (line depressurisation, fuel gas blanketing). In most circumstances, the line should also be pigged to remove water and to prevent solids from settling; see Table 4.4. The requirements in these tables only relate to mitigation measures to be carried out for corrosion control: there may other reasons to carry out similar mitigation measures, e.g., a fuel gas purge may be required to prevent hydrate formation or for safety reasons.
