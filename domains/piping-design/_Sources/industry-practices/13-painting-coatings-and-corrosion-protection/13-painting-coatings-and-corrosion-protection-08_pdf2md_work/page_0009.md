NOTES:

1. This velocity limit is for stripping inhibitors; 20 m/s should be achievable with all modern inhibitors. Inhibitors that work at higher velocities are available, but these require specific testing.

2. This velocity limit is for protective scale removal; if inhibitors are used, the same velocity limit exists as for sweet corrosion.

3. There is a galvanic element to the corrosion occurring on steel at areas of scale breakdown, since FeS is cathodic to bare steel; this is accounted for in the modelled corrosion rates.
