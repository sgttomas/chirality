## 3.3 COMPARATIVE CORROSION MECHANISMS

There are three different corrosion domains to consider:

| Condition | Dominant Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- |
| CO₂/H₂S < 20 | H₂S corrosion dominates; |
| 20 ≤ CO₂/H₂S ≤ 500 | Mixed CO₂/H₂S corrosion; |
| CO₂/H₂S > 500 | CO₂ corrosion dominates. |

The HYDROCOR corrosion model (6, [3]) has historically covered both the CO₂ corrosion domain and the mixed CO₂/H₂S domain, though it may be more conservative in the mixed domain. The SOGACOR (6, [4]) corrosion model has been recently incorporated into HYDROCOR to cover the H₂S corrosion dominated domain. Table 3.3 summarizes the main features of CO₂ and H₂S corrosion mechanisms. This DEP is primarily focused on the region where H₂S corrosion dominates; some parts of the mitigation strategy may apply to the mixed CO₂/H₂S corrosion regime.
