## 3.6 CORROSION MONITORING CONSIDERATIONS FOR WET SOUR GAS

To be effective and efficient in a wet sour gas environment, a corrosion-monitoring device shall be capable of detecting isolated pitting, which is the most dominant form of corrosion. A single point coverage device such as a coupon will often fail to detect pitting. Recommendations for corrosion monitoring are described in (6,[5]).
