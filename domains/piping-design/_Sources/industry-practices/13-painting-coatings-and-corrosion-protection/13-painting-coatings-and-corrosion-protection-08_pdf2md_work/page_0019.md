## 4.2.2 Normal steady state production

Based on the mitigation strategy outlined in Table 3.4, the mitigation methods (continuous injection, pigging, etc.) are recommended. The recommended frequency of pigging and batching has been determined based on current field experience and feedback from intelligent pigging. The overall corrosion mitigation methods and frequency are presented in Table 4.3.

Note: Batching frequency depends on the batch inhibitor persistency under specific operating conditions. Batch inhibitor persistency determined in the laboratory gives a lower boundary of the required batching frequency; it cannot be currently linked to the persistency achievable in the field. This depends on a combination of a number of factors, including the condition of the steel surface (bare steel, iron sulphide film), use of sulphur solvents, possibility of elemental sulphur deposition, and whether or not the inhibitor has been proven in continuous injection service in the field. If inhibitor chemicals are changed, it cannot be assumed that the batching frequency will remain the same; laboratory and field test programmes are needed to determine the required frequencies with the new chemical.

Other issues to be considered during normal steady state production include:

- defined operating window (flow regime, Cl⁻, S);
- regular sampling programme to check operating window. Change mitigation strategy if significant changes occur;
- long term trending of chlorides and water production rate to predict onset of formation water, which typically requires a change in mitigation strategy;
- compliance with batch inhibitor schedule (availability);
- compliance with inhibitor and sulphur solvent availability;
- inhibitor residual monitoring;
- intelligent pigging programme plus an inspection programme for all piping that cannot be inspected by means of intelligent pigging;
- corrosion monitoring as an indicator of potential problems;
- operational pigging and sampling of pig debris;
- impact of low flow and high flow;
- hydrates;
