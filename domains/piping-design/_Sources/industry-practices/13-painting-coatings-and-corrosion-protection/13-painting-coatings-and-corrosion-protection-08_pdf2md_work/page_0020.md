- requirement for sand detection;
- ensuring no oxygen gets into the system during normal operation (e.g. pigging operations).

6) To determine if sulphur solvent is required, modelling should be carried out. Modelling can only give an indication of quantities of elemental sulphur deposited, so a conservative approach should be taken on whether the solvent is required or not.
