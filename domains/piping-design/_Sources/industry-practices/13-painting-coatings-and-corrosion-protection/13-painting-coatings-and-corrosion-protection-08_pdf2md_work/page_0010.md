## 3.4 MITIGATION MEASURES

It is important to formulate mitigation measures to suit the expected flow regimes and corrosion mechanisms. For example, if stratified flow exists, continuous-injection inhibition will not mitigate top-of-line corrosion, as the inhibitors will not be transported to the vapour-phase portion of the pipeline. Corrosion might be severe enough to require alternatives such as corrosion allowance, pigging, batching, glycol injection, or even CRA. If the corrosion is dominated by H₂S, top-of-line corrosion is usually so low that no action is needed. However, bottom-of-line corrosion in H₂S might be so severe that continuous-injection inhibitors shall be formulated to disperse well in stagnant water pools to provide protection.
