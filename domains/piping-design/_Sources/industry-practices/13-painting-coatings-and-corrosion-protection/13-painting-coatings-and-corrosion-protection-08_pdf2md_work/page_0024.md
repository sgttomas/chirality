Where this has occurred, typical corrosion rates caused by methanol injection are from 1 mm/yr to 3 mm/yr, in addition to the sour gas corrosion rates. The additional mitigation measures in Table 4.6 are recommended where excess methanol is injected, to prevent this corrosion occurring.

| Type | Excessive Methanol Injection Duration | | |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| I | No actions | No actions | No actions |
| IA | No actions | Pigging once | Monthly pigging |
| II | No actions | Pigging once | Monthly pigging |
| III | No actions | Batching once | Monthly pigging + quarterly batching |

## 4.2.11 Pipelines with known existing corrosion defects

For a given type of sour gas class (I, IA, etc.), the corresponding corrosion mitigation measures might not be effective for deep corrosion pits (believed to be approximately 8 mm). An enhanced program might be needed until it is proven conclusively that the pits are dormant. The Principal's corrosion engineer shall be consulted on the formulation and subsequent discontinuation of the enhanced corrosion mitigation plan for all pipelines with known corrosion left in place.
