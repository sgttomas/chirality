## 4.0 APPLICATION

4.1  All coatings and wrapping products shall be applied in accordance with the manufacturer's application procedures. (See Table 1 for reference.)

4.2  For coverage of field welds, where primer and wrapping tape are applied, the pipe shall be heated to "hand warm" immediately prior to application of primer and tape. The tape roll shall be at a temperature greater than 50° F. The tape shall be applied uniformly and without wrinkles in a spiral fashion. The joint wrapping shall overlap at least 6 inches of the shop applied coating at each side.

4.3  In general, buried flanges are not permitted (refer to Suncor standards 0201, 0202, 0060, 0204, 1516, 1518 and 1520). However, where buried lines require flange joints, the flange connection shall be subject to the approval of the Owner's Engineer and shall be sealed as per the instructions given by the coating/wrapping manufacturer, subject to approval of the Owner's engineer.

4.4  For buried lines in high temperature service, containing flange joints shall be sealed for no leakage, taking into consideration the line thermal movement.

4.5  Pipe risers shall be coated to a minimum of 3 feet above grade. Where tape is used, it shall be applied such that the overlapped edge faces downwards. Where the height above ground is less than 3 feet, the entire section shall be coated and / or wrapped.

4.6  All coating procedures and requirements shall meet those of Suncor Standard 0214 unless otherwise specified in this standard.

## 5.0 HANDLING, MARKING, AND STORAGE

5.1  The lifting and handling of pipe shall be by means which ensures no damage occurs to the pipe or the coating systems.

5.2  The contractor shall stencil weather-resistant markings on the external coating, as specified by the Buyer. (i.e. EWR number, EWP number, etc.)

## 6.0 TESTING AND QUALITY CONTROL

6.1  In addition, to quality control requirements indicated in Suncor Std. 0214, prior to burial of underground piping, all coated pipe, joints, and fittings shall be inspected for holidays using a Purchaser-approved holiday tester. The test method shall depend on the Dry-Film Thickness (DFT), as per table below. No holidays shall be accepted.

Criteria for holiday testing

[TABLE: Criteria for holiday testing]

|  | DFT | Holiday Testing Method | Applicable Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Thin-film coatings | 20 mils < DFT | Low-Voltage Wet Sponge Testing | NACE RP0188 |
| Thick-film coatings | 20 mils ≥ DFT | High-Voltage Spark Testing | NACE RP0188 or NACE RP0274 |

Note: Coatings applied at thicknesses of less than 20 mils may be susceptible to damage if tested with a high voltage spark tester.
