## SECTION 3: FABRICATION

### 3.1 WELDING

3.1.1 For fabricated assemblies that will be galvanized, the edges of tightly contacting surfaces should be completely sealed by welding. No pinholes in welds are permitted. All welds must be sandblasted by fabricator prior to galvanizing.

3.1.2 Before welding on galvanized materials, including piping, equipment, or structures, the zinc coating shall be mechanically removed from the weld area to prevent zinc penetration into the weld and subsequent cracking, and also to avoid exposure to hazardous welding fumes. The BP CHP Safety and Health Department will determine any requirements for PPE as part of the work permitting process.

3.1.3 After welding on areas where the galvanized coating was removed, the affected area shall be washed with water to remove salts and wire-brushed to remove welding slag and other foreign material, before repairing the galvanized coating per section 3.3.

### 3.2 GALVANIZING APPLICATIONS

3.2.1 Products fabricated from rolled, pressed, and forged steel shapes, plates, bars and strip (1/8" and heavier) shall be zinc coated in accordance with ASTM Specification A123.

3.2.2 Assembled steel products with thicknesses less than 1/8" shall be zinc coated in accordance with ASTM Specification A385.

3.2.3 Headed bolts (machine bolts), nuts and washers conforming to ASTM A307 shall be hot-dip galvanized in accordance with ASTM A153, Class C. Nuts shall be tapped oversize in accordance with ASTM A563, prior to galvanizing. Hot-dip galvanized nuts shall be retapped after galvanizing.

3.2.4 High strength bolts, nuts, and washers conforming to ASTM A325 or A449, and load indicator washers shall be mechanically zinc coated in accordance with ASTM B695, Type I, Class 50. Nuts shall be tapped oversize in accordance with ASTM A563 prior to zinc coating, and do not need to be retapped after coating.

3.2.5 High strength bolts, nuts, and washers conforming to ASTM A490 are not allowed to be galvanized per the Research Council on Structural Connections (RCSC), and shall be coated with Zinc/Aluminum inorganic coating in accordance with ASTM F1136, Grade 3 for bolts and washers, and Grade 5 for nuts.
