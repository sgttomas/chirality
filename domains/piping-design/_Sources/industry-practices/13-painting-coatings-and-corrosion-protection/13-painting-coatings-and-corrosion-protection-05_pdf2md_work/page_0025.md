## 8. COATING REPAIR

The Contractor shall submit detailed procedures for coating repairs.

All coating repair procedures shall be qualified under both coating system qualification (5.4) and Applicator qualification (6). Minimum qualification requirements are given in Table 4.

These procedures shall contain as a minimum:

- repair of surface defects;
- repair of holidays, scratches and small defects (e.g. using a melt stick);
- repair of damages due to quality control testing (4.4.8), typically adhesion testing, film sampling and impact testing;
- removal of rejected coating and cleaning the pipe to the required standard for recoating;
- testing to prove the effectiveness of the repairs.

The maximum total surface area per pipe that may be repaired is 40 cm². Pipes requiring larger repairs shall be re-coated.

Pipes having insufficient coating thickness shall not be repaired by overcoating.

All pipes that have been repaired shall be fully re-examined in accordance with the quality control procedures (4.4). Minimum quality control requirements for repairs of small defects and damages are given in Table 3.

Records shall be kept of all repaired pipes and shall include the repair and re-test details.
