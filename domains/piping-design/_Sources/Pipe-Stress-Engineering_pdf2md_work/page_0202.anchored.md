$$y_p = \frac{pr^2}{4E} \tag{6.15}$$

The ring receives the internal pressure force over the width, *c*, and the radial reaction force, *H*, from the choking of the pipe. Because *H* is transmitted from both sides of the ring, a total of 2*H* is acting on the ring. The circumferential hoop tension over the ring is

$$N_\theta = (pc + 2H)r \tag{6.16}$$

This hoop tension generates a hoop stress of $S_\theta = N_\theta / A_r$, which results in an increase of the radius by

$$y_r = S_\theta \frac{r}{E} = (pc + 2H)\frac{r^2}{A_r E} \tag{6.17}$$

where $A_r$ is the ring cross-section area. Substituting Eqs. (6.15) and (6.17) to (6.14), we have

$$\frac{pr^2}{4E} - (pc + 2H)\frac{r^2}{A_r E} = \frac{2H\beta}{EI}$$

or

$$H = \frac{\beta(A_r - ct)}{B\left(A_r + 2\frac{1}{\beta}\right)} \cdot \frac{r}{1} \cdot \frac{(A_r - ct)}{...} \tag{6.18}$$

$$H = \frac{\beta(A_r - ct)}{\beta \cdot A_r + 2\frac{r}{\beta}} \cdot \frac{r^2}{1} \cdot \frac{p(A_r - ct)}{4E} \bigg/ \frac{r^2}{4E} \left(A_r + 2\frac{r}{\beta}\right)$$

In addition, from (6.13), we have

$$M_0 = \frac{H}{2\beta^2} \cdot \frac{(A_r - ct)}{\left(A_r + 2\frac{r}{\beta}\right)} \tag{6.19}$$

Substituting $\beta = 1.285/\sqrt{rt}$, (6.18) and (6.19) become

$$H = 0.78pr\frac{\sqrt{rt}}{(A_r + 1.56\sqrt{rt})} \cdot \frac{(A_r - ct)}{(A_r + 1.56\sqrt{rt})} \tag{6.20}$$

$$M_0 = 0.303pr \frac{(A_r - ct)}{(A_r + 1.56\sqrt{rt})} \tag{6.21}$$

The choking force and moment are determined at the edge of the ring defined by the *c* width. The ring area, $A_r$, is the total ring area combined with the shell located inside the *c* width. When the pipe is locally thickened or padded as in case (a), the *c* width is defined based on the length of the thickened pipe, $\ell_p$. If this length is equal to or longer than $\ell_e = 4.0\sqrt{r(t_r + t_p)}$ (see Eq. (5.28)), the locally thickened pipe or the padded pipe will be considered as the base pipe with ring width $c = \ell_e$, and the pipe thickness equal to $(t + t_p)$. If $\ell_p$ is less than $\ell_e$, then $c = \ell_p$ with the maximum *r* limited to $= 1.56\sqrt{r(t + t_p)} + t_p$. The pipe thickness in this latter case is the un-thickened base pipe thickness.

*Stresses at pipe shell.* The *H* and $M_0$ discussed above are for each unit circumferential width of the pipe shell. The longitudinal shell bending stress is equal to $M_0$ divided by the section modulus $t^2/6$ of the shell plate. That is,

$$S_L = \frac{M_0}{t^2/6} = 1.82 \frac{(A_r - ct)}{(A_r + 1.56\sqrt{rt})} \cdot \frac{pr}{t^2} \tag{6.22}$$
