Using the time-history approach, the above procedures, described for the static equivalent method, are mostly spared. The forces occurring at each end of the piping leg are applied directly as they are. The method will take the difference automatically through the calculation. The forces are all applied at the same time, but with proper sign and time-lag relation specified. This makes the method more accurate than the static equivalent method. Frequency calculation is required only for the modal integration method, but is not mandatory for the direct integration method. Nevertheless, having some idea about frequency distribution is also helpful for the direct integration method.
