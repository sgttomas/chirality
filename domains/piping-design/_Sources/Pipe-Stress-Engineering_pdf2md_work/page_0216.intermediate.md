The allowable loads per unit length of the lug are calculated separately for tension and compression loads by

$$L_a = K(L_t)S_{allow} \tag{6.42}$$

$L_t$ is the load factor. A tension load uses tension load factor, $L_{tt}$, and a compression load uses compression load factor, $L_{tc}$. The applied loads are then checked with the allowable loads. The allowable for the tension or pulling load is higher than that of the compression load.
