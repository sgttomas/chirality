If the pipe leg is very long with a $\Delta t$ greater than the effective valve opening time, the net force starts when the flow or wave front reaches the first elbow. It eventually reaches the maximum then maintains at a constant force the same as $F_1$. The net force starts to reduce when the initial flow or wave front reaches the second elbow. It eventually reduces to zero, and the flow becomes steady when the peak of the force reaches the second elbow.
