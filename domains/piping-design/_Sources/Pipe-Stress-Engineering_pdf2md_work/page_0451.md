$$a_1 = \frac{0.597\pi}{\ell}, \quad \omega = \frac{(0.597\pi)^2}{\ell^2}\sqrt{\frac{EI}{m}}$$ (13.36)

- Fixed ends beam (Fig. 13.11c)

$$y = 0.635[-0.9925(\sinh a_1 x - \sin a_1 x) + \cosh a_1 x - \cos a_1 x]$$

$$a_1 = \frac{3\pi}{2\ell}, \quad \omega = \left(\frac{3\pi}{2\ell}\right)^2\sqrt{\frac{EI}{m}}$$ (13.37)

- Fixed-supported beam (Fig. 13.11d)

$$y = 0.6624[-1.0007(\sinh a_1 x - \sin a_1 x) + \cosh a_1 x - \cos a_1 x]$$

$$a_1 = \frac{5\pi}{4\ell}, \quad \omega = \left(\frac{5\pi}{4\ell}\right)^2\sqrt{\frac{EI}{m}}$$ (13.38)

#### (b) Maximum bending moments

The stress before applying any stress intensification is called un-intensified stress. The un-intensified stress is calculated by dividing the bending moment by the section modulus. The maximum un-intensified stress does not occur at the same point that produces the maximum displacement, Δ. The maximum stress locations, however, are well known for these beams. By differentiating the displacement curve with respect to *x* twice, we will have the equation for the curvature, which is proportional to bending moment as given by Eq. (13.34). By simply taking the curvature value at the maximum bending stress location, multiplied with EI, we have the maximum bending moments of the first vibration mode for the beams as

- Simple supported beam, at center span point

$$M = 1.0EI\left(\frac{\pi}{\ell}\right)^2 \Delta$$ (13.39)

- Cantilever beam, at anchor point

$$M = 1.0EI\left(\frac{0.597\pi}{\ell}\right)^2 \Delta = 0.356EI\left(\frac{\pi}{\ell}\right)^2 \Delta$$ (13.40)

- Fixed ends beam, at fixed points

$$M = 1.26EI\left(\frac{3\pi}{2\ell}\right)^2 \Delta = 2.835EI\left(\frac{\pi}{\ell}\right)^2 \Delta$$ (13.41)

- Fixed-supported beam, at fixed points

$$M = 1.325EI\left(\frac{5\pi}{4\ell}\right)^2 \Delta = 2.07EI\left(\frac{\pi}{\ell}\right)^2 \Delta$$ (13.42)

The first mode moment for a fixed-supported beam is also applicable to the second mode moment of the fixed-ends beam. This will be used later as the basic moment in developing allowable velocity criterion.

The maximum bending moment can be expressed in term of the maximum vibration displacement in a general form as

$$M = C_M EI\left(\frac{\pi}{\ell}\right)^2 \Delta$$ (13.43)
