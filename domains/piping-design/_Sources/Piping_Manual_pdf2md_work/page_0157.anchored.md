This chapter has highlighted the principal layout requirements for the heat exchanger configurations most commonly used in process plants. A wide range of items, however, can be identified as heat exchange equipment for specific situations. Generally, these items have design features similar to those for common applications. Care should be taken to arrange the equipment so that the basic rules for heat exchanger layout are followed. Exhibits 6-53 and 6-54 show examples of more unusual heat exchanger equipment.
