**NOTES:**

- Spacing is based on radius of pipe to flange + 1" OD.
- Select line with largest flange diameter, find adjacent line size, and read across to find centerline-to-centerline dimensions, in inches.
- Adjust dimension to suit insulation thickness.
- Calculate spacing where pipe rack travel estate is limited.
- Adjustments to spacing for thermal expansion or contraction must be made.
