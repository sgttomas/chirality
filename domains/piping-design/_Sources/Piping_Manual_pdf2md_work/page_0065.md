Care should be taken to position air coolers to allow flexibility for interconnecting piping. A poorly positioned overhead condenser could result in additional large overall diameter piping and expensive supports. Unless furnished with fixed fire water sprays, pumps containing hydrocarbons and operating higher than autoignition conditions should not be located directly beneath air coolers. Space should be available on the plot plan for maintenance access by mobile cranes for removal of air cooler tube bundles.

Furnaces should be located at a safe distance and upwind from unrelated equipment containing hydrocarbons. Steam drums or deaerators can be located as required for operation and maintenance.
