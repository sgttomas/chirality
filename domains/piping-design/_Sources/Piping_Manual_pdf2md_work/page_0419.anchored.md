### Breaking Up a System into Its Spans

The designer can break up most piping systems into one of the three different span types. Exhibit 16-12 gives an example of this.

Once the designer has identified the system as a collection of one or more of these three types of routing, then it is a simple matter for him or her to apply the correct table and determine the location of the support points. Once the designer has the support points determined, he or she has the responsibility to make sure that there is a structure available for the support to use.

### Other Weight Stress Problems

So far when determining the span, we have only considered the pipe stress due to the pipe weight. There are two other points a designer also should consider: first, the significant effect concentrated weights such as valves and pipe risers can have on auxiliary steel locations; and, second, the impact of large weight loads on equipment nozzles.
