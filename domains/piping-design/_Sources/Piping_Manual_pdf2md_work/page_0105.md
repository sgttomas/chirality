### Line Branches

All branches should be located close to a line support whenever possible. Any such connections should be located on the top of the piping to minimize any potential liquid carry-over. Exhibit 4-52 shows typical branch connections.

### Compressor Pipe Supports

Exhibit 4-53 illustrates how to minimize the transmission of damaging vibrations by isolating the line supports from adjacent compressor or building foundations, operating floor steel, or building framing.
