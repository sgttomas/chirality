## Scanning and Translation

With graphics scanning, manually drafted documents may be converted to an electronic CAD file. Typical applications include the modification of existing client piping and instrumentation diagrams or piping drawings for plant enhancements. Generally, blueline prints are actually scanned, although microfilm aperture cards may be scanned as well. The edited image is converted to a CAD system for actual design work, as Exhibit 17-4 shows.
