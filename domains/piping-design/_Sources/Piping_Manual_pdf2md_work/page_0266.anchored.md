**EXHIBIT 10-53**
Overhead Arrangement

![Figure: Exhibit 10-53 — Overhead Arrangement. Two views (PLAN and elevation/isometric) showing the overhead piping arrangement from a tower to an overhead condenser and on to an air cooler.

PLAN view (top): Hand-drawn plan looking down at a pipe rack and an overhead condenser. A north arrow ("N") points to the right. Labels include: "PIPE RACK" (rack columns shown along the top), "OVERHEAD CONDENSER" (square plan with diagonals indicating finned/tube bundle area), "SUPPORT" (pointing to a support member at the rack near the tower), "OVERHEAD LINE" (the line leaving the tower vertically/upward), "TOWER" (circular plan section of the tower below the rack), and "HORIZONTAL LEG FOR FLEXIBILITY" (calling out the horizontal pipe run between the tower and the condenser). The view is annotated "PLAN".

Elevation/isometric view (bottom): Hand-drawn elevation showing the tower on the left with overhead piping rising, running across a horizontal leg, and dropping into an air cooler on the right. The tower is divided vertically into temperature/elevation zones with the following annotations stacked from top to bottom on the tower: top section labeled "180°" with a "MIN." callout at the tower top; middle section labeled "310°" (handwritten); lower section labeled "400°"; very bottom labeled "20' / 6.100" indicating the lowest segment height. Tower elevation marks on the left side read "120' / 36.570" for the upper segments. A "CALCULATED GROWTH (A)" arrow with a plus sign points upward at the tower top (representing tower thermal growth at the overhead nozzle). A "SUPPORT" is called out near the top of the tower where the overhead line leaves. A "GUIDE" is shown on the rising line just past the support. The horizontal pipe run between the tower and the air cooler is labeled "HORIZONTAL LEG (C)". The vertical drop from the tower toward the horizontal run is labeled "LINE GROWTH - (B)" with a downward arrow. Near the air cooler, the horizontal leg is annotated "LINE TEMPERATURE 180°". The air cooler is shown at lower right (labeled "AIR COOLER") with two vertical drops (with valves/flanges) connecting the horizontal leg to the cooler inlet header.

Handwritten note above the elevation view reads: "SUBTRACT GROWTH (B) FROM GROWTH (A). DIFFERENCE IS USED TO CALCULATE HORIZONTAL LEG (C)."]

Subtract growth (B) from growth (A). Difference is used to calculate horizontal leg (C).

Callouts:
- A — Calculated Growth (tower thermal growth at overhead nozzle, +)
- B — Line Growth (vertical line growth from tower side, −)
- C — Horizontal Leg (calculated horizontal pipe run for flexibility)
- Line Temperature: 180°
- Tower zone temperatures (top to bottom): 180°, 310°, 400°
- Tower elevations: 120' / 36.570 (upper segment), 20' / 6.100 (lower segment)
