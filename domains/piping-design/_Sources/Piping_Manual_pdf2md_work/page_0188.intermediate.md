The pilot gas valve is located below the furnace, providing operator access during ignition. It should be noted that although most new furnace installations use burner management systems, this design approach is acceptable if manual control and ignition is required.

The fuel gas, fuel gas, and pilot gas control valves are located at the pipe rack. The fuel gas is run symmetrically to provide an even gas flow to each burner. This system must be kept dry by the elimination of low points where condensate may collect. The header

should have a drip leg to remove any condensate build-up by piping it to the flare system. The fire steam line that atomizes steam must be run and insulated with the fuel oil line to decrease oil viscosity. Atomizing steam is injected into the burner with the fuel oil to obtain effective combustion.
