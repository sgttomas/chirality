## OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

Occasionally, a situation arises in which a flat-turn pipe rack may be employed. This often happens near a dead-end area where the potential for problems is minimal. As shown in Exhibit 11-28, the line sequence on the left side of the rack must remain constant as long as flat turns are used. A different elevation must be used at a 90° turn in the rack if the sequence must change, as shown on the right side of the diagram. This approach must be well thought out before it is used.
