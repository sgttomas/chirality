**EXHIBIT 4-32**
Elevated Centrifugal Compressor: No Shelter

![Exhibit 4-32 — Elevated Centrifugal Compressor: No Shelter](page_0090.png)

*Figure (hand-drawn). Two views of an elevated centrifugal compressor installation without an enclosing shelter.*

**Elevation view (top):**
- Compressor train mounted on an elevated concrete table-top foundation supported by piers, with grade hatching at base.
- Mobile handling equipment (boom/crane) shown at left, lifting a removable head/cover off the compressor casing (upward arrow indicating lift path).
- Steam inlet block valve labeled on the driver/turbine side of the train.
- Traveling gantry shown at right, depicted as a tapered tower structure adjacent to the table-top.
- Drop zone indicated at right beneath the gantry, with a load (cover/component) suspended above grade.

**Plan view (bottom):**
- Plan of the table-top showing compressor and driver casings on centerline.
- Rails (for the traveling gantry) annotated along the structure.
- Traveling gantry shown at the right end, spanning across the structure.
- Drop zone marked at the right end of the gantry travel.

**Callouts/labels visible on figure:**
- TRAVELING GANTRY (elevation and plan)
- STEAM INLET BLOCK VALVE
- MOBILE HANDLING EQUIPMENT
- DROP ZONE (elevation and plan)
- RAILS
- ELEVATION
- PLAN
