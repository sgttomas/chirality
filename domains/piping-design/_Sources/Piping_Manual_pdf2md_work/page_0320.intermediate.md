## STAIR STRUCTURES

When complex equipment (e.g., a converter and fractionator in a fluid catalytic cracking unit) is designed, a stair structure with a vertical pipe rack must be located between both vessels, as shown in Exhibit 12-26. Although elevators are often used, client approval must be obtained before they are included. The optimum layout includes arranging the vessel platforms for easy access from the structure. Clearance between the vessel and structure platforms must accommodate the growth of the vessels, which should be calculated to satisfy safety concerns. This structure eliminates the need to route lines and support them from their respective towers. The structure should be looked at as a vertical pipe rack and used to its maximum advantage to allow sufficient flexibility in the piping systems between the stationary structure and the movable towers.
