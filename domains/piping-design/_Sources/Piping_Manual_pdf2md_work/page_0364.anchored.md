## UNDERGROUND COMPOSITE

Exhibit 13-47 is a composite of the various underground piping systems discussed in previous sections of this chapter. The circled numbers refer to details shown in Exhibit 13-25. Shop-fabricated piping systems are the only underground lines assigned line numbers. All other piping is fabricated and installed from information supplied on this drawing. When preparing this drawing, the plant layout designer should double-check the following:

- All above-ground piping layouts to ensure that all drain points have been picked up.
- Coordination of the locating dimensions, interface point, flange size, rating and elevation, and bevel end schedules and elevations.
- All spread footing sizes and elevations to ensure that a foundation has not been undermined by entering the angle of repose.
- Spacing provided between lines and cover.
- The data transferred from the drawing to the sewer box schedule to be used by the construction contractor.
- All line-size calculations from the data recorded in Exhibit 13-14.
- All piping interface points between the new facility and any existing piping at the site.
- The issued construction piping and instrumentation diagrams to ensure that all lines have been accounted for on the underground piping plan.
