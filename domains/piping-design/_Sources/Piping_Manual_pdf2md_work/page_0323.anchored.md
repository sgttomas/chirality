The alternative arrangement is one in which the entire structure is supported from the elevated concrete deck. Although the vessel wall can be thinner than in the first arrangement, this design requires more steel and greater attention to pipe support and flexibility because the tower operation is cyclic as a result of decoking requirements. ## OPERATIONS PLATFORMS

Most large vertical vessels have platforms that are supported directly from the vessel shell. Some vessels have a great deal of associated piping and instrumentation. Exhibit 12-28 illustrates how much platform may be required for operation and maintenance. The top head platform is necessary for catalyst loading, and the monorail is used to hoist the catalyst from grade. At each level, the piping and instrumentation requirements need constant operator attention. Supporting large platforms from the vessels is often impractical, and a structure such as the one shown in this exhibit is best suited for support.
