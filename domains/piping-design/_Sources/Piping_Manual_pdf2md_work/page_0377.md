Nevertheless, the plant layout designer must consider pressure requirements, especially between components or at junctions, to allow for sufficient space to include pressure taps. An allowance of at least six pipe diameters should be made for the inclusion of the pressure tap after a control valve or a pipe intersection. Generally, pressure gauges should be accessible from grade or a platform or at least from a portable ladder. In both cases, the gauge must be readable from the operating level without a portable ladder. Pressure gauges in piping systems at such equipment as pump discharge lines must be located as close to the equipment as possible. Exhibit 14-23 shows examples of these locations.
