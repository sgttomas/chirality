Because many acceptable variations of a centrifugal compressor area are possible, the version presented in Exhibit 4-36 must be explained. Briefly, the area includes three centrifugal machines with separate lube oil consoles (driven by condensing steam turbines), two inter-coolers, three suction drums, a surface condenser, and two vertical condensate pumps. Because there are three condensing steam turbines operating at low steam pressure, minimizing the length of the exhaust line to the surface condenser should be one of the first goals a layout designer considers. The surface condenser is located just to the south of the compressor operating platform.
