**EXHIBIT 4-24**
Seal Oil Console Piping and Instrumentation Diagram

[FIGURE: Hand-drawn piping and instrumentation diagram of a Seal Oil Console serving a compressor. Upper portion shows a SEAL OIL TANK (small vessel at top right) connected via piping down into the main compressor enclosure. The COMPRESSOR is shown as a horizontal rectangular block with shaft extensions on both sides, enclosed in a larger boundary labeled with the demarcation "CONTRACTOR / VENDOR" along its left edge, indicating the split of supply responsibility. Lines from the compressor seal connections route downward into the SEAL OIL CONSOLE (lower dashed boundary). Within the console, from left to right: piping from the compressor passes through CONTINUOUS DRAINERS (two small drainer vessels with isolation valves), then to a control valve, into a COOLER (shell-and-tube heat exchanger symbol with relief valve on top), then through a FILTER, and back up to the compressor seal supply. Below the cooler/filter row sits the OIL RESERVOIR (large rectangular tank), which feeds two PUMPS (centrifugal pump symbols) in parallel, each with suction isolation valves; pump discharge ties into the cooler suction line. The cooler cooling-water inlet/outlet lines exit the bottom of the console to a "VENDOR / CONTRACTOR" interface point (two arrows down). Dashed lines indicate vent/drain or instrument signal returns from the seal oil tank back to the console.]
