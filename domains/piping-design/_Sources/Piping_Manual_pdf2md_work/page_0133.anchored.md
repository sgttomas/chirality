Support of the equipment (e.g., for air coolers or vertical reboilers) can also influence heat exchanger location. Exhibit 6-10 depicts a typical plot plan with several heat exchanger applications. Horizontal shell and tube exchangers should be positioned so that the channel end faces the auxiliary road or maintenance access way for tube bundle removal with adequate space provided at the front end of the exchanger for bonnet removal. This exchanger orientation is shown in Exhibit 6-11.

Exchangers can be located as single items, in pairs (this is the most common installation), or in larger groups when no intermediate control is required between the shell and tube streams. The single and paired installation is shown in Exhibit 6-12. Paired exchangers may operate in series, parallel, or dissimilar services; grouped exchangers operate only in series or in parallel. Exhibit 6-13 shows samples of parallel and series exchanger installations.
