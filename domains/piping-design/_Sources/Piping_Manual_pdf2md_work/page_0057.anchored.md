## Climate Conditions

Weather conditions could influence the location of equipment. In a severely cold climate, equipment should be housed; this can be done by encasing the whole unit, as depicted in Exhibit 3-18, or by individually housing groups of equipment (e.g., compressors or pumps), as illustrated in Exhibit 3-19. For individual housing, consideration must be given to locating equipment out of process sequence to minimize cost.
