**EXHIBIT 8-34**
Typical Pump and Turbine Support Considerations

[FIGURE: Isometric sketch showing a horizontal pump and steam turbine assembly mounted on an extended pump foundation, with associated piping and support details. Key callouts and labels:
- "PUMP DISCHARGE" — labeled at a flanged/valved connection at the top of the discharge piping.
- "SPRING" — a spring support on the discharge piping run.
- "SUPPORT STEEL" — overhead support steel beam at the top of the sketch.
- A detail at upper right shows an alternative spring support arrangement (spring can on a horizontal beam attached to support steel) connected by an arrow labeled "ALTERNATIVE MEANS OF SUPPORT".
- "SPRING" — a second spring support shown on a horizontal support beam in the alternative-support detail.
- Dimension note on the vertical discharge riser: "5φ OR LESS" (i.e., 5 pipe diameters or less between the pump nozzle and the first support / change of direction).
- "PUMP PIPING — API-610" — labeled on the pump suction/discharge piping connecting to the pump.
- "STEAM TURBINE PIPING — NEMA-SM-21" — labeled on the piping connecting to the steam turbine end of the assembly.
- "EXTENDED PUMP FOUNDATION" — labeled extension of the concrete pump foundation that carries an additional support post.
- "HARD, ADJUSTABLE OR SPRING SUPPORT" — labeled vertical support post landing on the extended pump foundation, supporting the pump piping.
- The pump and steam turbine are shown coupled on a common baseplate set on the concrete foundation, with suction piping coming in from the left and discharge piping rising vertically and turning at the top toward the support steel.]
