## TYPES OF PUMPS

Pumps are classified as centrifugal, positive displacement (reciprocating), or rotary.

### Centrifugal Pumps

The majority of pumps used in industry are centrifugal because of their flexibility in flow rates, pressure, and temperature. They are usually driven by electric motors or steam turbines. They may be single stage or multistage, depending on pressure requirements in the system, and can be horizontal or vertical. Centrifugal pumps, unlike reciprocating and rotary pumps, operate at a constant speed and can deliver any capacity from zero to maximum, depending on the head design and suction conditions. ### Positive Displacement (Reciprocating) Pumps

These pumps are commonly used to move viscous liquids, inject chemicals or additives into a system, or pump quantities too small for centrifugal pumps.
