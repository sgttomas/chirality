## Elevation and Support

Similar to towers, drum elevations are dictated by the NPSH, as illustrated in Exhibit 5-7, minimum clearance, common platforming, and maintenance and operator access. Within structures, especially chemical facilities, drums could be elevated above the minimum requirements of accommodate floor space availability. Drums for chemical collection systems are generally located below ground in concrete pits, as shown in Exhibit 5-8.
