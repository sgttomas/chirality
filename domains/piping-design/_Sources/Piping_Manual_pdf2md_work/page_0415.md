## Overstressing Nozzles

The second type of problem the stress engineer seeks to avoid is overstressing vessel nozzles. The nozzle itself typically is not the problem. It is the junction between the nozzle and the vessel wall. As a pipe cycles from cold to hot to cold again, it will push and pull on the nozzle. Many cycles of this force loading can cause cracking in the vessel wall. The reader can observe this type of failure when bending a paper clip.
