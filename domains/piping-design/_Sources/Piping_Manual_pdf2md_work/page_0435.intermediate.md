## LOWEST MECHANICAL NATURAL FREQUENCY OF GAS-FILLED, UNIFORM STEEL PIPING SPANS

[Nomograph diagram with the following scales and elements:]

- **L (Length of Leg, L-Feet)** scale on the left, ranging from approximately 1.0 to 50.
- **Reference Line** (vertical center axis).
- **K (Radius of Gyration, in.)** scale.
- **λ (Frequency Factor)** scale, with values ranging through 10,000; 8,000; 6,000; 4,000; 2,000; 1,000; 800; 600; 400; 200; 100; 80; 60; 40; 20; 10; 8; 6; 4; 2; 1.
- **f (Natural Frequency, Hz)** scale, with values approximately 20, 10, 5, 2, 1.
- **Pipe Size — I.D.** scale on the right, with various pipe size markings (e.g., 28", 26", 24", 22", 20", 18", 16", 14", 12", 10", 8", 6", 4") and corresponding numeric values (e.g., 17.250, 21.250, 22.626, 23.250, 24.376, 18.376, 16.876, 15.376, 14.000, 13.376, 12.500, 11.376, 10.020, 9.875, 8.125, 7.981, 7.625, 6.625, 5.761, 5.501, 4.825, 4.026, 3.825, 3.625).
- Various pipe configuration symbols/icons indicating different support and clamp arrangements.
- An "OUT" indicator near the bottom of the configuration column.
