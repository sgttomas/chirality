[FIGURE: EXHIBIT 6-43 — Overhead Piping Arrangements for a Condensing Air Cooler. Two elevation sketches stacked vertically.

Top sketch: Air cooler bundle (two-section box with X-bracing labeled "AIR COOLER") supported above a tower (circular plan symbol labeled "TOWER"). Overhead piping runs from the tower up into the bottom of the air cooler. A handwritten note on the left points to the support steel and reads "EXTEND AIR COOLER SUPPORT STEEL TO SUPPORT OVERHEAD LINE." A dashed leader on the right marks an alternative routing of piping running directly from the tower across to the air cooler nozzle, annotated "COST-EFFECTIVE ALTERNATIVE ROUTING IF PIPING IS FLEXIBLE AND CAN SPAN DISTANCE WITHOUT INTERMEDIATE SUPPORT."

Bottom sketch: Air cooler bundle (two-section box with X-bracing labeled "AIR COOLER") shown above a tower (circular plan symbol labeled "TOWER") with the inlet nozzles positioned on the far centerline of the pipe rack columns. Overhead piping drops from between the two air cooler sections down through the pipe rack and tees into the tower. Hatched pipe-rack steel is shown at the bottom. A handwritten note on the right reads "FOR FLEXIBILITY, THE AIR COOLER CAN BE POSITIONED WITH THE INLET NOZZLES ON THE FAR CENTERLINE OF THE PIPE RACK COLUMNS. THE AIR COOLER SECTIONS SHOULD BE SEGREGATED AND THE STEEL EXTENDED TO SUPPORT THE OVERHEAD PIPING."]
