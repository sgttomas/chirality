**Building elevation** The elevation of the building is further established by determining the size of the clear maintenance area (shown as X by Y'). **Hook centerline elevation** The layout designer determines the centerline elevation of the hook. The maximum lifted load must be supplied to the structural engineer or building contractor to furnish the correct traveling crane. The eave elevation is then set on the basis of the clearance of the crane selected.
