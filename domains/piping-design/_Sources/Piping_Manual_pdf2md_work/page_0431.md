## Checking the Piping Layout in Piperacks

This section will describe how to size a piperack loop. The first step is to determine the location of the anchors and loops in the line. Once the designer locates the anchors, he or she will design preliminary loop sizes and locations. Once he places the loops, the designer can check the stress in the pipe.

**Piperack allowable loads** There are different types of allowables for each phase of the piperack design. For the location of the anchors, the allowable movement is 6-in maximum pipe movement at the loops and 5-in maximum movement at changes of direction.

For the sizing of the pipe loops in a rack, the designer uses a maximum pipe stress allowable. The maximum primary stress in a loop is 20,000 psi for most temperatures encountered (reference ANSI B31.3, Appendix A for more detailed information).
