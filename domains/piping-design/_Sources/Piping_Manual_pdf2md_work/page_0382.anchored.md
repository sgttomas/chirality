**Cone-Roof Tank**

This low-pressure tank (see Exhibit 15-1) is used for countless products, including petroleum, chemicals, petrochemicals, food products, and water. Another low-pressure storage vessel is the flat-roof tank.

**Floating-Roof Tank**

The roof of this tank (see Exhibit 15-2) rises and lowers with the stored contents, thereby reducing va-

por loss and minimizing fire hazard. It is commonly found in oil refineries.

**Low-Temperature Storage Tank**

This tank (see Exhibit 15-3) stores liquefied gases at their boiling point. Products found in such tanks include ammonia (−28° F), propane (−43.7° F), and methane (−258° F).
