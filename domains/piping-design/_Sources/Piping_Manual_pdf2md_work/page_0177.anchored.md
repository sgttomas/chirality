## Auxiliary Equipment

A soot blower mechanically cleans soot build-up on convection section tubes in fuel-oil-fired heaters. A soot blower essentially consists of a lance (i.e., pipe) with nozzles specifically located for the tube section to be cleaned and a connection for the blowing medium, which is steam or air. There are two types of blowers: fixed and retractable.
