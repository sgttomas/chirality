**EXHIBIT 5-21**
Horizontal Drum Platform Floor Space Area Requirements

[FIGURE: Plan view of a horizontal drum showing platform floor-space layout. Top dimension annotation reads "2'-6" MIN. / .750" indicating the minimum clearance from the drum centerline area to the platform edge. Callouts on the drum identify, from left to right and top to bottom: STEAM OUT, LEVEL INSTRUMENTS, VENT, PRESSURE INSTRUMENT, TEMPERATURE INSTRUMENT, DAVIT, LADDER (along the right side), LADDER and MAINTENANCE ACCESS (along the bottom of the platform area). Hatched corner regions indicate platform extents around the drum.]

[FIGURE: Elevation view of a horizontal drum mounted on a concrete support, showing platform alignment. Top dimension annotation reads "3'-0" MIN. / .915" indicating the minimum platform projection from the drum nozzle face. Lower callout reads: "LINE UP PLATFORM WITH SHELL OF DRUM."]
