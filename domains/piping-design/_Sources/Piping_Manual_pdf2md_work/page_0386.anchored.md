When considering the overall plot plan for a process facility is developed, off-site storage should be set at a lower elevation than the process unit whenever possible. This approach (see Exhibit 15-12) provides an additional margin of safety in the event of a major rupture.

Containment of potentially dangerous liquefied natural gas spills is a significant concern to any operating facility. Therefore, extreme safety measures are employed, as shown in Exhibit 15-13. The inner tank is heavily insulated from the outer concrete containment and is buried in an earthen containment.
