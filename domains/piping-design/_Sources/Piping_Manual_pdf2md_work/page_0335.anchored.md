The piping designer should locate the oily water drain hubs using the above-ground piping studies, setting each invert elevation and routing sublaterals, laterals, and headers. Each fitting (e.g., Y branches, ⅛ bends, and ¼ bends) must be identified. Headers and laterals should be reduced, when possible, to 4 in before cleanouts are installed. All laterals entering sewer boxes are sealed.
