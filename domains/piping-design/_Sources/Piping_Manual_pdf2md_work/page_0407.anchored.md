The piping usually has quick-connect coupling located outside the dike. The line is routed to the tank wall. A foam maker—which essentially consists of a pressure gauge, an air strainer, and a check valve—is located in the vertical riser. The foam solution enters a foam chamber located at the edge of the tank roof and then discharges across the top of the liquid in the tank.

Another way of injecting foam into a tank is shown in Exhibit 15-50. Foam concentrate from a truck or tank is supplied to a foam maker located outside the dike wall. The foam may be injected into the regular product transfer piping or routed directly to the stor-
