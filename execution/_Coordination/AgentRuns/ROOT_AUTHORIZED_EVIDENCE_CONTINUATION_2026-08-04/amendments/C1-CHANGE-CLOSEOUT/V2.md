## Finding

The pre-publication absolute-path scan found exactly three occurrences of the
local execution root represented here as `{REPO_ROOT}`:

1. H2 `INPUT_BINDING.md` records the repository root used for its sealed hash
   verification;
2. H3 `INPUT_BINDING.md` records the same repository root; and
3. H3 AB-02 `RETURN.md` records the host-local root from which its bounded
   local probe and source hashing were executed.

These are execution-time provenance observations in already sealed and
manifest-bound manager/child records. Rewriting them after execution would
alter historical evidence and cascade through H2, H3, and H4 input/manifest
identities. They contain no credential, token, remote endpoint, or path outside
the declared checkout.

## Amended C1 check

Replace the launch brief's unqualified “no absolute-path leakage” check with:

- require exactly the three occurrences enumerated above, byte-identical and
  confined to their existing files;
- reject any additional machine-absolute path, any path outside the declared
  checkout, or any secret-bearing path/content;
- record the three admitted occurrences as historical execution provenance,
  not portable input paths or semantic authority; and
- do not rewrite sealed historical records solely for cosmetic portability.

All other C1 scope, validation, branch, commit, push, PR, no-merge, and
authority fences remain unchanged.
