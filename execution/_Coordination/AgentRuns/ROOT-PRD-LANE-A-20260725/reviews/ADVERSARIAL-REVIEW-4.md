Single subject: **D-3's authority status.** The review shows D-3 saying two
incompatible things — a TRANSCRIBED label (already governing doctrine) over
a body that disclaims any rule ("exercised pattern," no requirement that
future proposals use it). Professional tradition can supply a tested
criterion without automatically becoming a mandatory rule.

Three options offered:

1. **Preserve as observed practice.** Move D-3 out of "Stable product
   requirements"; label OBSERVED. The pattern (commit exact proposal →
   owner rules on that exact commit → record ruling separately → return
   for approval if implementation changes approved prose → record final
   applied commit) worked in D-GOV-18/19/21 and is available when
   circumstances warrant. K-AUTH-2 still requires every approval to bind
   to a SHA but does not require this particular multi-SHA packet
   procedure every time; a PR approval against branch HEAD can satisfy
   K-AUTH-2 differently. Makes no authority claim beyond the record; lets
   professional judgment select the procedure by consequence; avoids
   heavyweight packet mechanics on small changes; preserves the pattern
   as tested developmental evidence.
2. **Adopt as mandatory product requirement** (keep in §5, PROPOSED):
   every substantial proposal must use the exact-candidate procedure.
   Strong protection and uniform audit trail; but "substantial" must be
   defined, adds process weight, and universalizes ahead of broader
   testing.
3. **Adopt as governed default** (PROPOSED, non-absolute): default to the
   pattern for substantial proposals; owner may authorize another
   SHA-bound vehicle preserving exact content, attribution, and change
   detection. Requires criteria for when the default applies.

**Reviewer recommendation: Option 1.** "The root already has the actual
invariant it needs: approvals bind to a specific SHA. … Preserve it as
developmental evidence and professional precedent. If decomposition later
shows that selecting among approval vehicles causes ambiguity or failure,
that evidence can support promoting the pattern into a proposed
requirement. This follows Chirality's own movement: practice supplies
evidence; evidence informs judgment; judgment alone makes the rule."

**Agent 0 disposition: ACCEPT Option 1** (dispositions and implementation
directives in `../briefs/PRD-AUTHOR-BRIEF-AMENDMENT-4.md`). Options 2 and
3 are recorded as available future promotions on evidence.
