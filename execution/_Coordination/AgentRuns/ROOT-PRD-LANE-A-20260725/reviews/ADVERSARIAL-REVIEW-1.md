## Review text (verbatim, as routed by the owner)

Required revisions:

1. **Add product objectives and version-bounded success conditions.**
   Section 5 contains 78 labeled requirements, but 67 merely transcribe
   existing doctrine and only three introduce new commitments. The document
   lacks a concise statement of what successful Chirality Root v1 must
   achieve. The disclaimer at lines 652–658 — no completeness claim,
   acceptance criteria, verification methods, or priorities — is too broad
   for an adopted decomposition basis. The Decomposition Standard must
   derive a few meaningful, testable objectives from its source. Without
   explicit success conditions, decomposition will have to infer the
   product's direction from 78 mostly constitutional statements. Add a
   short objectives section covering at least: coherent and discoverable
   normative authority; governed production of professional knowledge work;
   the human iteration/evaluation loop; safe self-application without
   self-authorization; situated specialization and governed convergence
   back toward root; coordination that remains intelligible as concurrent
   Agent 0 activity grows; file-native continuity and recoverability.
   Detailed acceptance tests can remain downstream, but the PRD must define
   what success means. Claim completeness only for the stated v1 boundary —
   not for every future Chirality possibility.

2. **Express the relationships among the four categories.** The four
   categories are named, but the owner's central relational insight is
   missing: normative basis constrains operative work; operative work
   produces artifacts and evidence; evidence informs human evaluation;
   developmental machinery produces candidate changes and iteration
   evidence; human judgment accepts, rejects, or redirects; accepted acts
   change the normative or operative state. That is the generative loop.
   Presently the categories risk being read as four filesystem buckets.
   State explicitly that they classify functions and authority
   relationships, are non-exclusive, and do not prescribe four packages.
   The same artifact may participate in more than one category.

3. **Add the missing convergence relationship with variants.** Section 8
   describes one-way inheritance from root to situated working roots. It
   does not express how Chirality has actually grown. Add a proposed
   relationship stating that: variants specialize root patterns for
   particular forms of knowledge work; their observed friction and
   successful patterns may return as evidence-linked root candidates; no
   variant automatically changes or weakens root; promotion occurs only
   through the root's human-governed developmental machinery. This is the
   missing reciprocal half of "generative operating form."

4. **Correct the user model.** The current table treats agents and working
   roots as users. They are not users in the product sense. Separate:
   primary human users (accountable practitioners directing governed work);
   secondary human users (reviewers, auditors, maintainers, and people
   assessing reliance); system actors (Agent 0/1/2 instances); consuming
   contexts (projects, domains, desktop-selected folders, and other
   situated working roots). Also decide — or reserve explicitly — whether
   v1 is for the present owner-practitioner, generally for accountable
   professional practitioners, or for multi-practitioner organizations.
   RD-2 does not fully answer this product-user question.

5. **Prevent the PRD from becoming another drifting registry.** The
   candidate repeats dynamic catalogs, exact runtime arrangements, agent
   forms, capability enumerations, export membership, and invariant lists.
   This recreates the prose-index-rot risk already found in CONTRACT and
   README. The adopted PRD should contain stable product commitments and
   incorporate dynamic registries by reference. Move the exhaustive
   transcription inventory into a companion concordance artifact or annex
   that can be mechanically regenerated and checked. Expand R-D-16 beyond
   concordance with DIRECTIVE §1. Any adopted PRD containing transcribed
   requirements needs source-currency checks covering: referenced invariant
   IDs; live agent and skill registries; changed source anchors; repeated
   enumerations; provenance labels and counts. Otherwise every ordinary
   governance evolution risks silently making the adopted PRD stale.

Specific corrections:

- **R-O-20 is incorrect.** Line 311 says everything the product does is
  either an instruction artifact or deterministic tool and that no third
  execution substrate exists. Root `runtime/` is explicitly a generic
  executable substrate with daemon, clients, adapters, sessions, and
  transport. Rewrite this requirement around the actual operative layers
  and their authority boundaries.
- **R-N-17 overstates the dependency rule.** Line 273 should say no
  central dependency graph is authoritative. Derived full graphs are lawful
  coordination state and are necessary for FULL_GRAPH operation.
- **The applicability statement overnarrows Chirality.** Lines 235–239
  cite the activation boundary of the professional-responsibility model,
  not the total applicability boundary of the root product. Chirality is
  intended for governed professional knowledge work generally; the
  additional responsibility provisions become load-bearing in regulated,
  safety-significant, or reliance-bearing circumstances.
- **R-D-2 may be overclassified as TRANSCRIBED.** Line 322 generalizes a
  convention from particular decision records into an absolute rule
  covering all corrections. Either cite a root-wide rule that supports this
  exact formulation or label the generalization CLARIFIED/PROPOSED.
- **R-E-5 conflates SHA roles.** Line 350 should distinguish: the owner
  act binds approved content to its candidate SHA; the publication SHA
  identifies the durable ruling record; the EffectiveSHA identifies applied
  state.
- **Verify the OWNER_DECLARED quotations.** The provenance key says these
  are "not reworded." If ID-1, ID-2, or ID-3 are agent syntheses that the
  owner generally endorsed rather than the owner's verbatim words, do not
  present them as transcriptions in blockquotes. Either cite the exact
  utterance or label them as owner-endorsed synthesis pending exact
  confirmation.

Reserved-decision improvements:

- **RD-1:** offer an exact recommended RD-1-C formulation rather than an
  abstract option. Reviewer recommendation: "Chirality Root is the
  canonical human-governed application environment and generative operating
  form for governed professional knowledge work. It contains a
  filesystem-native agent operating system together with the normative
  basis, developmental machinery, evidence, and human judgment by which
  that operating system is formed and governed."
- **RD-2:** reframe as two axes rather than one binary choice: (1) who
  holds jurisdiction to govern the root? (2) who is accountable for each
  consequential professional or reliance act? `human_actors.md` identifies
  permitted authors of binding governance records; it need not be the
  registry of every person accountable for situated professional work.
- **RD-4-D:** candidates in `execution/` and an adopted PRD in `docs/` do
  not require two continuously concordant copies. The candidate can remain
  immutable historical evidence while a pointer identifies the adopted
  exact bytes. Its stated "two homes" cost is therefore overstated.

Release authority: add the owner's release posture explicitly — evidence,
validation, decomposition coverage, and guard state inform release
judgment; none releases Chirality Root mechanically. Release occurs when
the owner performs the applicable explicit release act.

Recommended document shape: keep the detailed governed-basis inventory,
but make it a companion concordance. The main PRD should be shorter and
organized around: 1 product identity and exact genus; 2 problem and human
users; 3 objectives and v1 success conditions; 4 four functional categories
and their human-governed loop; 5 stable product requirements; 6
self-application and concurrency direction; 7 variant specialization and
governed promotion; 8 non-goals, falsifiers, and release authority; 9
reserved decisions; 10 source concordance and document control.

Do not introduce packages or deliverables during this revision. The
required improvement is clearer direction for decomposition, not premature
decomposition itself.
