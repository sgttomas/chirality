Status: `ACTIVE`

## Direction

Migration-only metadata does not belong in the final production
`ScopeOfWork.md`. Preserve it externally as evidence and correct the Stage-2
method before project execution resumes.

## Required disposition

1. The converter output remains an evidence-rich derivative candidate. Its
   source markers, authority comment, and any `ISSUED` preparation bindings are
   retained for exact mapping and parity.
2. A registered deterministic finalizer creates a distinct clean production
   `ScopeOfWork.md` and an external finalization report that binds both hashes
   and all externalized metadata.
3. Claim maps and parity reports must bind and verify the clean production
   hash. Checklist derivation, HTML rendering, replacement manifests, and
   integration consume only that clean production artifact.
4. CHANGE must reject an evidence-rich candidate as integration input.
5. Every prepared but unintegrated App or pilot candidate is subject to this
   amendment. It must be finalized and its downstream evidence rebound before
   integration; historical evidence is not rewritten.
6. Piping execution remains stopped. PKG-00 remains excluded exactly as already
   directed; this amendment does not authorize PKG-01 or any other package.

## Gates unchanged

This amendment does not satisfy H1/H2, accept lifecycle content, authorize
integration or retirement, or change decomposition truth. It changes the
derivative-to-production boundary and the evidence required before those later
gates.
