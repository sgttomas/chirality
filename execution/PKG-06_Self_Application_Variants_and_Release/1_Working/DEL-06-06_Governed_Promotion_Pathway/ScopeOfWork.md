# Scope of Work — DEL-06-06

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This Scope of Work is the production contract for `DEL-06-06` — Governed
Promotion Pathway — in `PKG-06_Self_Application_Variants_and_Release`. It
covers project scope items SOW-070, SOW-071, SOW-072, and SOW-073, and supports
package objective OBJ-005, exactly as recorded in the deliverable register and
in `_CONTEXT.md` §Scope Traceability.

The deliverable's register description is: make the upward half work — variant
friction and successful patterns return as evidence-linked root candidates, no
variant automatically changes or weakens root, and promotion occurs only
through the root's human-governed developmental machinery, with at least one
candidate reaching a complete disposition.

OBJ-005 is stated in the adopted root PRD (`docs/PRD_ROOT.md` §3) as situated
specialization with governed convergence. Its v1 success condition has two
parts: (i) at least one candidate pattern originating in a situated working
root reaches a **complete promotion disposition — accepted, rejected, or
deferred with its evidence recorded** — with its evidence linkage intact, a
rejection or reasoned deferral being sufficient because the objective tests
that the pathway works rather than that any candidate deserves promotion; and
(ii) every root change that claims variant-derived provenance used the governed
promotion path. This deliverable produces the pathway and record surfaces that
condition is measured against.

This contract is authored in `MODE=INIT` from the accepted decomposition basis
bound in the frontmatter. Its acceptance criteria and verification methods are
**candidates for owner review**; they assert no acceptance, no reliance, and no
lifecycle transition. Lifecycle state is held solely by `_STATUS.md`, and
`ResponsibleParty` is `Ryan Tufts` under D-GOV-27 and the current deliverable register.

The register records `AnticipatedWriteLocus: execution-tree` for this
deliverable. That is a planning note, not authorization. A promotion that
actually lands on the shared instruction surface (`AGENTS.md`, `agents/`,
`skills/`, `tools/`, root `docs/`, `init/`, `.github/workflows/`) requires an
independently authorized M2 instruction-surface tranche; this Scope of Work
defines the pathway and grants no such authorization.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

The three outputs below are transcribed from the register field
`AnticipatedArtifacts` and from `_CONTEXT.md` §Anticipated Artifacts.

- **OUT-001** — Promotion pathway definition: a record in the deliverable
  folder describing the upward path end to end — where a candidate originates
  in a situated working root, what carries it to the root, which human-governed
  developmental instrument rules on it, and what an accepted tranche then
  changes.
- **OUT-002** — Candidate intake format: a stated record shape for a promotion
  candidate, with the fields required to make it evidence-linked — the
  originating working root, the observed friction or successful pattern, the
  evidence pointers, and the proposed root effect.
- **OUT-003** — Disposition record with evidence linkage: a stated record shape
  for a candidate's complete disposition — accepted, rejected, or deferred with
  its reason — bound to the ruling instrument and to the evidence that informed
  it, plus at least one instance carried to a complete disposition.

- **CLM-001** — All four covered scope items carry SourceRef `PRD §7.2 item
  <n> [PROPOSED]` and `InOutStatus: IN`, with the ledger `Notes` recording that
  the label is in effect per the D-GOV-22 adoption ruling and confirmed at the
  D-GOV-25 ruling, the source label being left unchanged under F6 discipline
  (no label change without an instrument). The obligations below are therefore
  adopted commitments of the root PRD, and this deliverable implements the
  upward half of §7.2 rather than proposing it.
- **CLM-002** — SOW-073's phrase "a candidate, a ruling, and an accepted
  tranche" names the three stages the outputs must be able to represent, which
  is why the output set is a pathway definition (the stages), an intake format
  (the candidate), and a disposition record (the ruling and its linkage to the
  accepted tranche).
- **CLM-003** — `_CONTEXT.md` records `ContextEnvelope: M` with the note "One

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

The requirements below restate the obligations carried by the covered scope
items, each cited to its ledger row and SourceRef.

- **REQ-001** — The pathway definition shall record that variants specialize
  root patterns for particular forms of knowledge work, so that the upward path
  begins from specialization rather than from divergence (SOW-070; PRD §7.2
  item 1).
- **REQ-002** — The intake format shall require that observed friction and
  successful patterns arrive as **evidence-linked** root candidates, with the
  evidence reachable from the candidate record (SOW-071; PRD §7.2 item 2).
- **REQ-003** — The pathway definition shall record that no variant
  automatically changes or weakens root, and that a pattern's success in a
  working root is evidence, not authority (SOW-072; PRD §7.2 item 3).
- **REQ-004** — The pathway definition shall record that promotion occurs only
  through the root's human-governed developmental machinery — a candidate, a
  ruling, and an accepted tranche — with no path that reaches root state
  without a human ruling (SOW-073; PRD §7.2 item 4).
- **REQ-005** — The disposition record shall be able to express a **complete**
  disposition in each of the three forms — accepted, rejected, or deferred with
  its reason recorded — and shall bind each to its ruling instrument and its
  evidence (SOW-071; SOW-073; OBJ-005 success condition (i)).
- **REQ-006** — At least one candidate shall be carried to a complete
  disposition through the defined pathway, that instance being the evidence
  that the pathway works (register description; OBJ-005 (i)).

- **AC-001** — The pathway definition (OUT-001) states, each cited to its scope
  item, all four §7.2 commitments: specialization downward, evidence upward,
  non-automatic and non-weakening effect on root, and promotion only through a
  candidate, a ruling, and an accepted tranche.
- **AC-002** — The intake format (OUT-002) and the disposition record
  (OUT-003) together make a candidate's evidence reachable from its record and
  admit all three complete dispositions, with the deferral form requiring a
  recorded reason.
- **AC-003** — At least one candidate has reached a complete disposition
  through the pathway, and its record resolves to both the ruling instrument
  and the evidence that informed it.
- **AC-004** — No output asserts that a variant result changes root state, and
  none claims acceptance, ruling authority, or an authorized instruction-surface
  change.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Production reads the four covered ledger rows, the adopted PRD §7.1–§7.2 and
§5.3 (developmental machinery), and the live governance-harness decision
surface under `docs/governance_harness/_DECISIONS/`. The pathway definition is
written against the machinery that exists, not an invented one: a candidate
record, a `D-GOV-*` ruling, and an accepted tranche.

- **VER-001** — For any promotion whose accepted tranche touches the shared
  instruction surface, run
  `python3 tools/validation/validate_instruction_tranche_manifest.py --base
  <REF> --head <REF> --tranche <ID>` (guard G4, registered in
  `execution/_harness/root_guards.yaml`) and require it to pass, evidencing
  that the change arrived as a manifested M2 tranche rather than an ungoverned
  edit. Deterministic.

No deterministic verification is defined for OUT-001. The pathway definition is
a prose record whose defect mode is *omitting or weakening one of the four §7.2
commitments*, and no tool in this repository reads a pathway description and
rules on whether it states them. Validating this contract would not do so
either: `tools/scope_of_work/validate_scope_of_work.py` resolves this
deliverable's production format and reports `SOW_V1`, a result invariant to
whether the pathway definition has been written at all. OUT-001 therefore
carries named human review.

The disposition judgment itself is not verifiable by any tool and must not be:
whether a candidate is accepted, rejected, or deferred is a human ruling
(K-AUTH-1, restated at PRD §5.1 N-3). The matrix therefore names human review
for the criterion that turns on it.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Evidence travels up; authority does not. The pathway's whole
  purpose is to carry what a variant learned into root consideration without
  carrying any variant's power to decide (SOW-072).
- **AX-002** — A rejection is a success of the pathway. Because OBJ-005 tests
  that the path works rather than that a candidate deserves promotion, the
  disposition record must make rejection and reasoned deferral first-class
  outcomes, not failure states.
- **AX-003** — The human hinge is not automatable. No amount of evidence
  quality shortens the path to a ruling; a candidate that appears obviously
  correct still requires a candidate, a ruling, and an accepted tranche
  (SOW-073).
- **AX-004** — Linkage is what makes the record usable. A disposition whose
  evidence cannot be reached from the record satisfies the form and defeats the
  purpose; the linkage requirement is the load-bearing part of OUT-003.
- **AX-005** — Defining a pathway authorizes no traversal of it. This contract
  produces the machinery's description and its first instance; any actual
  instruction-surface effect remains gated on an independently authorized M2
  tranche.
