# Type 0/1 instruction signal audit — candidate proposal

Basis: `781b478176db46ca66ebd38991607612e0ce9338`, with exact subject bytes pinned in `../SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`. All 19 assigned instruction files were read in full (9,663 lines). These are design hypotheses from static inspection, not measured claims about model behavior. No subject instructions were changed.

## Proposed direction

Shorten 17 roles around their distinctive judgment. Convert PDF2MD and DRAWING_EXTRACT into manager-consumed methods only after explicitly assigning their residual ownership; retain thin live managers during that transition. The four sections remain useful: PROTOCOL expresses the focused method, SPEC its warranted result, STRUCTURE the relationships the role needs to see, and RATIONALE the value that guides judgment. Their lengths need not match.

The dispositions are proposals about useful behavioral types, not deductions from the current qualification rule. That rule is itself under examination. A manager’s existing fan-out or human gates establish present migration obligations; they do not by themselves prove that its named persona should endure. The 17 SLIM recommendations favor the role-specific judgment described in each card, while leaving later role consolidation open to comparative behavioral evidence. PDF2MD and DRAWING_EXTRACT are stronger method candidates because their attention remains closely tied to one source-processing pipeline; that is a design interpretation, not a qualification theorem.

The strongest deletion candidates are revision stories, role-name tutorials, obsolete constraints, repeated policy statements, duplicated schemas and durable model-allocation claims. Remove these rather than creating an archive requirement for every deleted paragraph. Relocate only material with an identified ongoing consumer: methods, schema contracts, tool recipes and selected workflow profiles. The cards distinguish those actions.

## Shared treatment of surrounding blocks

Apply this treatment to each card's frontmatter, purpose, type table, references, invariants and runtime/I/O/compatibility blocks:

- **Purpose:** compress to the card's behavioral core, preferably leading PROTOCOL or RATIONALE. Delete duplicate purpose and naming statements.
- **Runtime metadata and Agent Type table:** propose one machine-owned contract for type, class, tools, permissions, callers and outputs, with a generated presentation. This is a migration candidate, not an existing replacement. Keep the current parsed representation until the consumer transition is adopted.
- **Governing references/precedence:** express the shared relationship once in root/runtime loading. Load role-specific authority only where it informs an actual judgment or selected method. Remove repetitive copied precedence ladders after the instruction-loading contract establishes the common one.
- **Invariants:** place distinctive role constraints once in SPEC or the relevant protocol decision. Literal duplicate formulations disappear. Changes to the underlying decision rights are separate owner choices.
- **Runtime inputs/defaults, output fields and compatibility:** put run choices in briefs, stable executable fields in schema/tool contracts, and recovery/compatibility rules in the selected method. Keep the small subset of conceptual relationships needed for the role's judgment in STRUCTURE. Remove obsolete compatibility narration after the compatible path is verified.

The current workflow-component standard §10 requires much of this surrounding form. Its adoption basis is D-GOV-14; proposed structural removal therefore includes changing that standard, the validator and consumers together. Parent inspection reports the app parser consumes type/class and six table rows (`agent-instruction.ts:47–73,252–280,367–431`); managed delegation uses caller/type/approval fields (`managed-delegation.ts:261–330`); the runtime successor parses type/class too (`runtime-service.ts:409–441`). These are observed migration couplings, not evidence of a behavioral benefit from repetition. Parent's baseline conformance check reports 34 files with zero errors and warnings. That proves current formal conformance, not consistency or role effectiveness.

Every adopted instruction tranche also needs the root agent-index coordination notices to affected corpus snapshots and SHA-pinned mirrors. D-GOV-21's accepted instruction basis and each owning loop's adoption remain applicable. Parent owns the consolidated consumer/notice plan. Proposed destinations marked **candidate** do not exist by virtue of being named here. All behavioral probes below are future comparisons against current instructions under comparable context, tools and authority; none was executed in this audit.
