## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

A *holding* includes instructions, tools, publications, reference material and
execution. A *selected basis* is the identified revision actually governing a
use or review. A *procedure set* is connected applicable guidance, potentially
already present in several maintained homes; it is not one workflow per operation.
A *maintenance case* is an authorized actual change and its available evidence.
A *recovery case* may be real retained work or an explicitly labelled case.
*Custody* is an exceptional reservation of an identified item and operation,
not an assumed global lock. Integration, publication, examination and adoption
remain different acts.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

Completion requires usable guidance and evidence for the claims actually made.
Schema validity and locality presence establish structure only. A walkthrough
can establish instructional usability; executable behavior needs observed checks;
named compatibility needs the actual maintained consumer contract/fixtures at a
specified revision. An unresolved requirement stays unfulfilled unless the
actual owner dispositions it with its consequences.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Read the selected holding and current method under the authorized undertaking.
Classify only affected means as usable, needing connection/repair or absent.
Select claim-specific existing evidence before commissioning new work. For a
reusable workflow revision, use the preceding `create-workflow` method and its
actual review/adoption route. For related contributions, use
`workflows/coordinated-knowledge-work/WORKFLOW.md`; TASK performs its bounded
assignment without delegation. The implementation owner retains ordinary repairs,
and the parent/integration owner retains integration and combined review.

Carry the change using the applicable maintenance route, invoking the maintained
bounded-reconciliation procedure in the same PR. Verify actual commands when
making behavior claims and bring necessary repairs to their source owner.
Preserve reusable means until verified adoption. Recover through the owning
locality and actual review record; keep only evidence needed for use, acceptance
or recovery. Return unsupported claims and reserved choices with consequences.

- **VER-001** — Inspect the selected instruction/resource revisions and walk a bounded brief through all eight R04 routes: add/revise; organize/relocate/retire; diagnose/reconcile; assess connected change; review/integrate; prepare publication/inspect updates; recover/continue; change maintenance means. Check inputs, destinations, method selection, actual owner connections and capability/failure limits. Record which parts are a walkthrough versus observed execution; this method alone proves no executable behavior.
- **VER-002** — Inspect the actual change and review/integration record, beginning with CLM-004 and accepted decomposition §6.1. Bind the changed holding, preceding/reviewed/final revision and applicable checks to each asserted A2 claim; inspect the reusable procedure at its maintained home. Identify any evidence that no longer applies and any missing step; obtain additional evidence only for the resulting gap under an authorized case.
- **VER-003** — Compare the maintenance-means revision with its preceding instruction/entry/tool basis and actual review/adoption record. Confirm the previous basis remains recoverable and was used to judge the replacement, and identify checks of both the holding and maintenance means. Reuse PR #1779 for supported A5 portions only; missing adoption or verification evidence stays unresolved.
- **VER-004** — Review the actual candidate's consequence classification against its focused content, behavior and interface evidence. Check candidate binding and independence of scrutiny for numerical, persistence, permission or destructive changes. Where evidence is reused, inspect relevance and unchanged assumptions; where behavior changed or remains uncertain, exercise the appropriate focused check. Do not change an expected result merely to silence failure.
- **VER-005** — Attempt continuation from real retained work or a clearly labelled recovery case, using the locality and actual review record. Establish the recoverable source/result, available checks, unresolved conditions, next action and current authorization; verify that an absent routine receipt is not required and any recovery note is necessary and replaceable. Report unperformed steps explicitly.
- **VER-006** — In the selected custody case, inspect the actual mechanism and exercise authorized release/recovery or a labelled safe simulation of the interruption. Observe the holder/item/operation binding, reader access, enforcement boundary and authorized-maintainer decision; test that timeout alone provides no authority to discard work. Distinguish procedural coordination from enforced locking and simulation from live operation; destructive or permission-affecting changes invoke REQ-004.
- **VER-007** — Review the procedure and case against REQ-008/REQ-009 and cited ownership claims, one excluded act at a time. Inspect same-PR reconciliation and retained authority/basis evidence; check Q-01/Q-02 and applicable Q-04/Q-05 limits. Screen public candidate material with maintained credential/private-term checks, keeping private evidence local and naming unavailable hosted checks. Expose any substantive conflict for the actual decision owner.
