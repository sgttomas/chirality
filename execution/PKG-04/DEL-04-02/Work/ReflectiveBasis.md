The bounded comparison read these existing methods and their relevant resources:

- **bounded-reconciliation:** usable for comparing actual result/evidence with
  commitments, Work and needs. Its question presumes the commitments are the
  current basis. Extending it to decide whether purpose or commitments should
  change would blur the very owner boundary it preserves. Retain it, and link
  from reflection when a finding instead needs ordinary factual reconciliation.
- **reconciliation:** its entry explicitly selects a human-activated corpus
  concordance program. Contract/method require frozen basis, claim ledgers,
  calibrated waves, decision packets and repair/backcheck tranches. Borrow its
  distinction between evidence and decision authority, and its treatment of
  ruled versus genuinely open choices. Do not silently select that program or
  loosen its adopted safeguards for a different use.
- **semantic-lensing:** entries are questions/candidates, not evidence; conflicts
  remain visible. That distinction informs reflection. The actual method requires
  _SEMANTIC matrices, production-format selection and Matrix.Row.Column proposal
  tags. Our bounded conversational use has none of those input conditions.
- **evaluation-protocol / evaluation-report:** purpose/stakes, source-bound
  evidence, unknowns and rubric limits are useful distinctions. Their actual
  protocol/report/handoff and scored-dimension machinery is not adopted here.
  A general reflective decision must not invent a score or require their files.
- **HELP_HUMAN / HELPS_HUMANS:** posture, continuity and inspectable interpretations
  are directly useful. Retain those mature roles. Coordinated knowledge work
  remains useful for contributors, waiting and integration; it is not the
  requested reflective method. No role or orchestration text needs revision.
